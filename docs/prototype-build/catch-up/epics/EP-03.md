# EP-03 · Booking and Procedure capture (anaesthetist) · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-03) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-03](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-03.md)

> Verification outcome: 10 of 11 gradings upheld; only US-03.3.8 corrected (Missing to Partial, because A1/A2 are removable chips and the seeded 72-year-old Ellison Booking opens with A1 selected). EP-03 is largely built on the July readings. Four clusters. (1) Events (FT-03.7, US-03.7.1/.2/.3): no Event entity; addPostOpAddendum is a Booking-on-a-List and its own comment says INTERIM until catch-up phases 38b/39b (ROADMAP D13). That work also removes BillingLine and the 'Stage post-op scenario' trigger; S4 beat 2 of the demo guide must be rewritten. (2) Price adjustment (FT-03.5 and US-03.5.1) is one fix: a per-Contract 'anaesthetist may change pricing' flag, fixed-price and prepaid locks, shown fixed discount, no reason, flag at office review; the validator's mandatory reason and negative-fee refusal plus office PriceOverrideSheet's mandatory reason go with it. (3) Modifiers and completion (US-03.3.1, 03.3.4, 03.3.8, 03.6.1) share domain/billing/fee.ts resolveBtm (ASA seeding), validateBookingForBilling (Contract + times-if-time-priced + modifier explanation replacing route + RVG-or-line + times), a new modifier explanation field on Procedure, an out-of-range base-units warning rule (US-13.7.1; only prepaymentUnpaid exists) and a DOB-based age rule. Closing 03.3.4/03.3.8 also needs the RVG table plus AA modifiers (US-05.1.5). (4) NHI mandatory (US-03.1.5) touches Patient type, nhiBadge, intake provisional patients and ManualBookingForm. Any of these changes persisted shape, so PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped, and canvas golden/fingerprint tests regenerated. Dependencies outside EP-03: EP-04 (Contract lines with default RVG code, US-04.2.4; anaesthetist changing Contract, US-03.4.1), EP-08 (additional invoice, credit note, traceability), EP-11 (billable party, insurer/approval), EP-13 warning routine. Open questions OQ-95, OQ-121, OQ-12, OQ-93 make the Verify-status grades (03.3.1, 03.3.4, 03.3.8, 03.6.1, 03.7.2) soft; none of them changes the direction of the work.
