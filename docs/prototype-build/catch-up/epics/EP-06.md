# EP-06 · Prepayment · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `60e2d1e` · [EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md)

> Prototype implements the July RFP model: selfFundedPrepayment payment category per procedure (retired per domain-model row 23), full-estimate or split-deposit, manual office raise, balance netted at billing run. The catalogue replaced all of it: profile prepaid set (FT-06.1; also US-12.1.3, US-05.1.3), first-party fixed-price Contracts per anaesthetist (US-04.2.14, US-04.3.4, OQ-91; no anaesthetist-held Contract exists), auto-generation at setup with admin approval and letters, trust account, refund on cancel, no automatic settlement. Verifier found one big miss: the prototype releases the prepayment payable before the procedure (payment received authorises the ACCPAY, payables run disburses with no gate, seeded paid prepayment disbursed Jul 16 for a Jul 24 procedure), which directly contradicts FT-06.5 and US-06.5.1, so those move from Missing to Contradicts. US-06.2.1 moved to Contradicts (retired category trigger) and US-06.5.3 to a low-confidence Partial. Dependencies: EP-04 (first-party Contracts), EP-05 (RVG groups), EP-08 (US-08.6.3 additional invoice, US-08.6.5 credit note; no credit-note kind on Invoice), EP-09 (draft pair timing, OQ-80), EP-12 (profile setting). Domain changes (drop PrepaymentDetail/split and prePaidByProcedure netting) need a PERSIST_VERSION bump and reseed of both Souter Fri 24 bookings (AM split unpaid, PM full paid; SEED_PREPAID_BOOKING_ID). Demo-guide S4 Beat 1 (prepayment warning on Souter Fri 24 AM) still works as a warning but its split-deposit wording and any 'Less pre-payment deposit' balance beat would break. Unpaid prepayment as a non-blocking warning (15a) is retained and fits. Open OQ-80, OQ-92, OQ-96, OQ-97 are not graded against; suggested designs only.
