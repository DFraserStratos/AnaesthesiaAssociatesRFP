Only genuine Missing items: US-06.3.6 (letters) and US-06.5.2 (cancel refund). (2) Outgoing-invoice approval screen does not exist (US-06.3.1, US-06.3.6; EP-08/EP-09 area). (3) Ad hoc additional invoice and credit note by hand (US-08.6.3, US-08.6.5) do not exist; needed by US-06.4.1 and US-06.5.2. (4) Internal ledger/holding-account concept shared with EP-08 and EP-10 (payableRelease, paymentActions release-on-receipt must skip prepayment cases). (5) The same no-calculation rule applies to all fixed-price Contracts (FT-05). (6) OQ-96/97/116 (editable prepaid price, part credit, mixed-Booking deduction) and OQ-104 (price held on issued invoice), OQ-115 (refund route) unresolved; build assumptions in the catalogue. (7) US-06.5.4's patient email touches notifications. Seed change plus PERSIST_VERSION bump needed (seeded split deposit, disbursed prepay). Stale 'Phase 41 seam' payee-repoint comments in listMoveActions/slotActions should be removed. Demo-script impact: docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md S4 Beat 1 (Annette Riley prepayment warning, Fri 24 Jul) and any S3 money beat mentioning the prepaid Booking's disbursement or a deposit-then-balance invoice change; the S1/S2 own-move line 'nothing about the patient's prepayment is recalculated' stays true. Warning-samples copy lists a 'prepaid procedure with no price' rule not yet built. Most gaps are demoable only via new buttons: approve-with-letter on admin invoices, a holding-account card, cancel-refund credit, authorise moving holding money to the anaesthetist, optional patient email on move.

- **Requirement:** Cancelling a Booking with a paid prepayment before the procedure always refunds it in full from the holding account, recorded as a credit against the prepayment invoice reversing receivable and holding payable; the office handles it.
- **Prototype today:** cancelBooking only stamps booking.cancellation; bookingRequiresPrepayment returns false for a cancelled Booking so the warning disappears; the paid invoice, case and Xero pair are untouched.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Refund/credit record linked to the prepayment invoice (AC2)
  - Reversal of receivable and holding payable (AC1)
  - Refund payout step, system vs Xero (OQ-115 open)
  - Visible refund in Accounts or Booking history
- **Demo trigger:** Existing Cancel Booking on the seeded paid-prepayment Booking (Souter Fri 24 PM) creates a credit note linked to the prepayment invoice; optional 'Mark refund paid' on the credit shows the payout.
