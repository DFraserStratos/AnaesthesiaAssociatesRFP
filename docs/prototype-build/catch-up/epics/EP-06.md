# EP-06 · Prepayment · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `a68f09b` · [EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md)

> The central missing structure is the AA Holding account. It is a creditor in the payables mirror, never paid, that receives prepayments and transfers them to the anaesthetist who did the List. That one change closes FT-06.3 and US-06.5.1 and removes the payee-repoint seam on a move (US-06.5.4). The second missing structure is a credit note and refund document kind: Invoice.kind is only standard\|prePayment, and cancelBooking and editProcedure touch no invoice. That closes US-06.5.2 and US-06.3.7 and is also needed by US-02.5.3 and the sent-or-paid half of US-06.3.5. Build order: holding account and transfer pair (with the US-06.4.1 / OQ-125 release trigger), then credit/refund engine plus cancel hook (US-06.5.2), then the Contract prepaid flag and change handler (US-06.2.1, US-06.3.5, US-06.3.7). The prototype keys prepayment on the Procedure's patientPaymentCategory (route billableParty plus selfFundedPrepayment) rather than a Contract-level prepaid flag, so US-06.3.5 and US-06.3.7 depend on the flag model from the prepayment-requirement and contract features. What already matches: a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation (US-06.3.5 AC2), and an unpaid prepayment only warns without blocking. Prepayment-related behaviour in the demo is stale under the current catalogue: the paid seed Booking (Fri 24 PM, INV0001/BC0001) pays through to the anaesthetist, and the balance run nets the deposit off the final invoice, where US-06.4.1 says a prepaid Procedure is left off the final invoice. Demo-script impact: S4 Beat 1 (Fri 24 prepayment warning) and S3 (payment received, then payable released) change, because a prepayment would land in the holding account and release only on List authorisation. S1, S2 and S5 are unaffected. The seed and PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped for the holding account and any seeded credit or refund. OQ-120 (does the prepayment invoice name the anaesthetist) is open and leans to not naming. The prototype's InvoiceDocument names the anaesthetist, which is minor to revisit once AA answers.

## US-06.5.2 · Cancellation of prepayment procedures · Missing

[US-06.5.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.5.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.5.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, admin, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Cancelling a Booking with an unpaid prepayment invoice automatically raises a credit note against it. Cancelling one with a paid prepayment always refunds in full from the AA Holding account, recorded as a credit linked to the original invoice that reverses its receivable and its payable to the holding account. A payment arriving after the credit note is refunded.
- **Prototype today:** cancelBooking validates a reason and edit rights, sets booking.cancellation and audits booking.cancel; it touches no invoice, case or Xero record. CancelBookingSheet has no prepayment or invoice text. The prepayment invoice and any paid case remain; bookingRequiresPrepayment returns false for a cancelled Booking, so only the warning disappears.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No automatic credit note when cancelling a Booking with an unpaid prepayment invoice
  - No full refund for a paid prepayment and no ledger credit linked to the original prepayment invoice
  - No reversal of the receivable and the payable to the holding account (no holding account exists)
  - No refund of a payment received after the credit note
  - No credit note or refund document kind in the ledger or Xero mirror (Invoice.kind only standard\|prePayment)
- **Demo trigger:** Cancelling the seeded Fri 24 AM (unpaid) or Fri 24 PM (paid) Booking in Admin Booking detail triggers the credit note or refund automatically, shown on the Booking and in the ledger. A Xero simulator Demo actions entry 'Pay after credit note' shows the late-payment refund.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:362-405 (cancelBooking)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:574-578`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:1112`, `searched: prepay\|invoice\|deposit in shared/flows/CancelBookingSheet.tsx -> none`, `searched: creditNote\|credit note\|refund in src -> none`

## US-06.3.7 · Contract changed after a prepayment is sent or paid · Missing

[US-06.3.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.3.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.3.7) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: domain, store, admin, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** If a prepaid Procedure's Contract changes to a non-prepaid one after the prepayment invoice is sent or paid, handle it as a cancellation of the prepayment: paid is refunded in full from the AA Holding account; sent-unpaid gets a credit note, and a late payment is refunded. The Procedure is then invoiced under its new Contract after the procedure.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing. Invoice.kind is only standard\|prePayment, so no credit note or refund type exists, and editProcedure does not look at invoices. The deposit stays on the original prepayment invoice and is netted off the later balance invoice. No Contract prepaid flag exists to detect the change.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Full refund of a paid prepayment, reversing its receivable and its payable to the holding account
  - Credit note cancelling a sent, unpaid prepayment invoice
  - Refund of a payment arriving after the credit note
  - Procedure invoiced under the new Contract after the procedure (the old deposit must not be netted off)
  - Contract prepaid flag to detect the change
- **Demo trigger:** On a Booking with a sent or paid prepayment, changing the Contract or payment category in Office billing setup (Admin Booking detail) automatically posts the credit note or refund to the ledger and Xero simulator and shows it on the Booking. If that is awkward to stage, a Demo actions entry on the Admin Booking detail screen, 'Change Contract after prepayment', applies the change to the seeded Fri 24 AM (sent, unpaid) or Fri 24 PM (paid) Booking.
- **Evidence:** `searched: creditNote\|credit note\|refund\|reversal in src (ts/tsx) -> none relevant`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:1112 (Invoice.kind only standard\|prePayment)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:452 (editProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:296-325 (deposit netting)`
