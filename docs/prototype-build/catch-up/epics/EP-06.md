# EP-06 · Prepayment · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `d436947` · [EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md)

> The prepayment flow is built around Invoice.kind 'prePayment' plus a post-AUTHORISE balance run. That run nets the deposit off a BTM-calculated fee (invoiceBuild.ts, selectors.prePaidByProcedure), and closing US-06.4.1 means replacing it with 'prepaid price = prepaid amount, nothing further invoiced'. Dependencies: US-06.5.1 (AA Holding account and transfer, absent), US-06.2.2 (prepaid amount is the price on the issued prepayment invoice, and the full prepayment currently uses the estimated BTM fee), US-08.2.2 (prepaid Procedure left off the final invoice), US-08.6.3 and US-08.6.5 (manual additional invoice and credit note, absent beyond the interim addPostOpAddendum), US-05.4.2 (office can change any price), and a Contract-level price-override flag that prepaid Contracts refuse (OQ-96). OQ-125 (release trigger) and OQ-97 (partial credit) may change the details. Demo beats: S4 uses the Fri 24 prepayment AM/PM Bookings (split $800 on $1,200 unpaid with warning; mixed and full seeded PAID, INV0001/BC0001), and S3 covers the money flow. Any change here alters the S4 prepayment beat and the 'balance invoice' wording in docs/demo-guide, and prePaymentInvoice.test.ts and the billing-run tests encode the old behaviour and need rewriting. PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped if the seed changes.
