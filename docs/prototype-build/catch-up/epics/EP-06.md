Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md)

> The prototype implements an older prepayment model (catch-up Phases 09 and 15a): trigger is a procedure's billingRoute 'billableParty' plus patientPaymentCategory 'selfFundedPrepayment' with full\|split detail; an office 'Raise pre-procedure invoice' button; amount is an estimate (feeFor with no contract) or a typed deposit; an ACCREC+ACCPAY pair whose payable is the List's anaesthetist and is released to them on receipt (xeroHandoff.ts:272, paymentActions.ts:99); and a balance invoice after authorise with a 'Less pre-payment deposit already invoiced' line (invoiceBuild.ts:385-391). Genuinely Missing items are the letter (US-06.3.6), cancellation reversal (US-06.5.2) and the open-question story US-06.3.7. No item grades as Matches; the prototype only matches on small points (warning is never a block; move code re-triggers nothing; per-procedure scan; prepayment status is derived).\n\nClosing the epic is one structural rework with a shared core: (1) a Contract prepaid flag and fixed-price line validation (US-04.2.14, US-04.3.2/3.4, US-05.4.2); (2) an AA Holding payee and a transfer entry in the ledger and Xero simulator (US-08.3.1, FT-09, FT-10.3, US-10.2.1); (3) credit note and refund primitives, which do not exist anywhere in the prototype (US-08.6.3, US-08.6.5, US-02.5.3); (4) an outgoing-invoice approval screen with a switch-off setting (does not exist; referenced by several other epics); (5) removal of the balance invoice and deposit deduction in the billing run (US-07.3.x, US-08.2.2, OQ-116 mixed Bookings); (6) the prepayment letter template (OQ-120) and the move email. Dropping the 'PHASE 41 SEAM' in listMoveActions.ts and slotActions.ts is part of it, since a move needs no payee repoint. Seed work: Fri 24 AM Riley ('prepayment', split $800 on $1,200, unpaid) and Fri 24 PM Nair ('prepaymentPaid', INV0001/BC0001 built in buildSeedBillingSlice) must be rebuilt on a prepaid Contract, with a PERSIST_VERSION bump. Demo guide beats S3 (money end to end) and S4 (prepayment warning, partial payment) change, a cancel-prepaid-booking beat fits S4, the S2 move line (03-demo-script.md:357) becomes true only after the rework, and the master demo guide HTML must follow. [US-06.3.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.3.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.3.5) · status Open (2) · surfaces: domain, store · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** When Procedures, Contract or payer change before the procedure, re-check prepayment (cleared if the Contract is no longer prepaid and no invoice is generated); a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation.
- **Prototype today:** prepaymentStatusFor is derived on every read, so editing the category to Self-funded clears the 'required' warning. Move code (listMoveActions, slotActions) does no prepayment work, only a 'PHASE 41 SEAM' comment. Once an invoice is raised a later change does nothing: no void, credit or refund; a changed payer makes the later balance run fail with prepaidCounterpartyChanged.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Re-check is driven by category, not by the Contract's prepaid flag (AC 1 cannot be shown by changing a Contract)
  - Nothing defined once the invoice has been generated (see US-06.3.7)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:574-580,639-644`, `aa-prototype/src/store/listMoveActions.ts:142 and slotActions.ts:459 (no prepayment work on a move)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:316-322 (prepaidCounterpartyChanged)`

## US-06.5.2 · Cancellation of prepayment procedures · Missing

[US-06.5.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.5.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.5.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: store, domain, admin, simulator, seed · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Cancelling a Booking with an unpaid prepayment invoice automatically raises a credit note; with a paid prepayment automatically refunds in full from the holding account, linked as a credit against the invoice (reversing receivable and holding payable); a payment after the credit note is sent back.
- **Prototype today:** cancelBooking only stamps booking.cancellation; buildPrePaymentInvoiceForBooking returns no invoices for a cancelled Booking and the warning stops. The existing invoice, case and Xero pair, and any received money, are left as they were.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Automatic credit note on cancel (unpaid)
  - Automatic full refund on cancel (paid), linked to the invoice
  - Reversal of the receivable and the holding payable
  - Refund of a payment received after the credit note
  - No credit note or refund entity at all
- **Demo trigger:** Existing cancel-booking flow on the seeded prepaid Bookings (Fri 24 AM unpaid, Fri 24 PM paid): the result appears as a credit note or refund row on the Booking's invoices and in the Xero simulator pair view. Add a simulator button 'Patient pays after credit note' to show the double transaction.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:362-405`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:462 (cancelled returns no invoices)`, `searched: refund\|creditNote\|credit note in store and domain -> none`

## US-06.3.6 · Prepayment letter template · Missing

## US-06.3.7 · Contract changed after a prepayment is sent or paid · Missing

[US-06.3.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.3.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.3.7) · status Open (2) · surfaces: store, domain, admin · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** The system must decide what happens to a sent or paid prepayment when its Contract is changed to non-prepaid (proposal: refund in full from the holding account for paid; sent-unpaid undecided).
- **Prototype today:** Nothing: once the pre-invoice is raised it stays, even if the category is later changed (status becomes 'none' but the invoice and its Xero pair remain); no void, credit or refund path exists.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Paid case: refund from holding then invoice normally
  - Sent-unpaid case: void or credit note, and a late payment
  - Any credit note or refund primitive in the prototype
- **Evidence:** `searched: refund\|creditNote\|credit note in src (non-test) -> none`, `aa-prototype/src/store/prepaymentActions.ts (no reversal)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:574-580 (requirement derived from category only, ignores existing invoice)`
