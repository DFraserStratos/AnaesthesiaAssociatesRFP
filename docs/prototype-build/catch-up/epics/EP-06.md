# EP-06 · Prepayment · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `501b0b8` · [EP-06](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-06.md)

> Verified from code; reviewer grading largely upheld, one correction (US-06.5.4 Partial to Contradicts). Structural shifts: (1) Trigger: prototype keys prepayment on a per-procedure patient payment category (selfFundedPrepayment on the billableParty route) with full/split detail; domain-model row 20 explicitly retires that category in favour of an anaesthetist-level prepaid RVG set (US-06.1.1/FT-06.1, not graded here), automatic at booking setup. Dropping category and split touches types, validateCardForBilling, EditBillingSetupSheet, ManualCardForm, OfficeBillingSetup, InvoiceDocument wording, seed cards, PERSIST_VERSION. (2) Money flow: trust hold needs paymentActions (pro-rata authorise on receipt), payablesActions (no procedure-done gate) and seed (INV0001 disbursed 2026-07-16, before its 24 Jul procedure) changes. (3) Refund and move: needs a credit/refund Invoice kind linked to the prepay invoice (shared with US-08.6.2), Xero credit note, and payable repoint in reassignCard/reassignList (depends on OQ-70 and the List-ownership epic). (4) Estimate: US-06.2.4/2.5 need estimated duration, a contingency-units setting and a visible breakdown (OQ-38, OQ-75). (5) Warning model: catalogue says prepayment warnings never block and should escalate toward the procedure date; prototype hard-gates completion (lifecycle.ts completionBlockersFor), with PrepaymentOverrideSheet, Card.prepaymentOverride and the review flag 'gate overridden' to rework into a soft warning with part-paid state and date-approach alert. (6) Overpaid prepayment must stop failing the billing run (negativeTotal). (7) Approve-then-send with letter templates (US-06.3.1, 06.3.6) is a new admin step. Matches: US-06.3.4 (re-check after receipt, Xero payment control Full/Half exists) and US-06.4.1 (balance netting; 'deposit' wording to fix). Demo-guide impact: S4 Beat 1 (pre-payment gate, Souter Fri 24 AM, Override pre-payment gate), cheat-sheet 'Pre-payment' section, 02-workflows 'full or split pre-payment', control-panel S4 blurb; seeded split $800 exemplar and the seeded paid prepay card (Fri 24 PM) need reworking. New beats suggested: approve-and-send with letter, cancel with refund, move with payee repoint, overpaid settlement, trust hold on Payables screen. Cross-cutting note: prototype map and the reviewer cite store/payablesActions.ts:146 for runPayables; eligibility is authorised minus disbursed greater than zero only, confirmed.
