# EP-06 · Prepayment · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-06) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md)

> Phase 27 (the prepayment lifecycle) is the planned rebuild, and the code is full of 'INTERIM until Phase 27' markers. These are the pieces it replaces: prepaymentDetail full/split, the PREPAYMENT_DEDUCTION netting line, procedureRequiresPrepayment, and the office-only raisePreProcedureInvoice with send:true. The AA Holding account is one shared new creditor that drives FT-06.5, US-06.3.1, US-06.4.1 and US-06.5.1 together, so the four L sizes overlap heavily: building the creditor, the pair re-pointing and the AUTHORISE transfer once closes most of them. Structural dependencies outside this set: EP-08 (US-08.2.2 final invoice leaves prepaid Procedures off, US-08.3.1 linked receivable and payable, US-08.6.3 and US-08.6.5 manual additional invoice and credit note), EP-09 (Xero creditor pair and transfer), US-06.5.2 and US-06.3.7 (cancel or Contract change after sent or paid: credit or full refund from holding; cancelBooking currently ignores billing), US-06.5.4 and US-06.3.6 (letter, OQ-120), and US-13.7.4 (approval on/off setting, no admin screen yet). Parts that already match and should be kept: per-payer prepayment invoices and the payer lock (22a), the derived requirement and unpaid warning that never blocks (15a), the read-only prepaid price after raise (A16), prepaid Contracts refused RVG pricing and so any override, and a move that re-triggers nothing. Open questions: OQ-125 (release trigger; use AUTHORISE, flagged), OQ-73 (already coded to the written reading), OQ-120, OQ-140. Demo guide: S4 Beat 1 (prepayment warning, Dr Souter Fri 24 Jul AM, Annette Riley) stays valid but gains approval and holding-account beats; the line about a move not recalculating the prepayment stays true. The seeded PAID prepayment case (seed/billing.ts, scenario.prepaymentPaid) and the Souter prepaid Booking need their pair re-pointed to the holding account, and PERSIST_VERSION bumped when the seed changes. S1 to S3 do not touch prepayment. Verification found all seven gradings sound; the only change was to US-06.3.2's demo_trigger (a partial-payment demo already exists) and extra evidence on cancelBooking.
