## EP-06 · Prepayment · Contradicts

[EP-06](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-06) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** A Procedure is prepaid by picking a prepaid Contract (or typing an amount for one Booking); the system invoices the payer, holds the money in the AA Holding account until the procedure is done, and transfers it to the anaesthetist who did the List. The prepaid amount is the price and nothing further is raised automatically.
- **Prototype today:** A Procedure is prepaid when billingRoute is billableParty and patientPaymentCategory is selfFundedPrepayment, with prepaymentDetail full (RVG estimate via feeFor) or split (deposit). The office clicks Raise pre-procedure invoice, which creates the invoice, case and Xero ACCREC plus draft ACCPAY to the anaesthetist at once. The post-authorisation run bills the BTM-based balance net of a visible deduction line. No holding account, transfer, credit note, refund, approval step or letter.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Trigger is a payment category on the Billable Party route, not a prepaid Contract (Contract has no prepaid flag)
  - Split deposit and RVG-estimated amounts exist (catalogue: full price only, no deposit)
  - Payable goes to the anaesthetist, not the AA Holding account
  - Run nets the prepayment and automatically bills a BTM balance instead of leaving the Procedure off and transferring the prepaid amount
  - No cancellation or Contract-change credit note or refund
  - No admin approval step or switch, no letter template, no typed Prepaid Fixed price, no manual additional invoice or credit note
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:794-806 (selfFundedPrepayment, PrepaymentDetail full\|split)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:574-580 bookingRequiresPrepayment`, `aa-prototype/src/store/prepaymentActions.ts:52-173 raisePreProcedureInvoice`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:296-330, 385-395, 456-490`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:190-273 (payee resolved from the List's anaesthetist)`, `searched: holding\|creditNote\|refund\|letter in aa-prototype/src -> none relevant`

- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: payable to the List's anaesthetist and released on receipt, before the procedure

  - Missing: AA Holding account and its balance (US-06.5.1)
  - Missing: automatic holding-to-anaesthetist transfer on List authorise
  - Missing: credit note on cancel (unpaid) and full refund from holding (paid), linked to the invoice, and refund of a payment after the credit note (US-06.5.2)
  - Missing: manual additional invoice or credit note traceable to the prepaid Procedure, and refusal of price override on a prepaid Contract
  - Missing: no payments out of the holding account except by the system
- **Demo trigger:** Cancel Booking sheet on a prepaid Booking: cancelling raises a credit note (unpaid) or a refund from the holding account (paid), shown on the Booking and in the Xero simulator pair view.
