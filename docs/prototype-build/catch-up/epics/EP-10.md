# EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-10) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-10.md)

> EP-10 (this set) is mostly built (catch-up Phase 16). Authorise-on-payment (US-10.2.1) and the AA fee settings (US-10.3.3) match; the fee run (FT-10.3) matches. Three gaps remain. (1) FT-10.2 Contradicts: the in-app 'Run payables' button on Admin Billing monitor and the 'Settle invoice' payout on the Xero pair page make the anaesthetist payment from the system, where OQ-111 (answered 4 Nov) says Xero's payables run pays and the system only detects and records it (US-09.2.4); ROADMAP D53 and Phase 37 already plan the move, with an Admin read-only list of payables awaiting Xero's run. (2) US-10.2.6 Partial: no payment period or approve-for-payment step; it overlaps US-10.2.7 (weekly ISO-week cycle, Proposed, OQ-47 open; ROADMAP assigns Phase 39a), so build them together. (3) US-10.3.1 corrected to Partial: the story says each fee invoice is tracked in the ledger; no internal ledger exists until Phase 36, which plans an aaFee pair, so the fee leg should be in Phase 36's brief. Open questions OQ-47, OQ-60 and OQ-112 do not change current behaviour: the paid-only BCTI count sits behind one switch (BCTI_COUNT_RULE) while the feature body says 'issued', and month end vs the 20th is not represented. Demo-guide impact: the beat at docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md (about lines 762 and 895-905) walks 'Billing monitor, Run payables' then 'Payment received · full' then 'Run payables' again; moving the payout to the Xero simulator changes that beat's button location and wording, and the Control Panel note at DemoControlPanel.tsx:488. Fee beats are unaffected unless OQ-112 moves the run to the 20th. Dependencies: Phase 36 ledger, Phase 37 Xero payables run, US-09.2.4 detect disbursement, US-05.2.7 GST presentation on the fee.

## FT-10.2 · Releasing payables · Contradicts

[FT-10.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-10.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-10.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, simulator, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Once a receivable is paid its matching payable is authorised for the next payables run, so the anaesthetist is paid only what AA received. The payment is made in Xero's payables run; the system records and tracks it (US-09.2.4) and does not make the payment itself.
- **Prototype today:** receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY for the cumulative amount received. Admin Billing monitor has a product 'Run payables' card and button (payablesDue, runPayables) that disburses authorised minus disbursed, writes Disbursement records and flips the case to disbursed. DemoXero's 'Settle invoice' calls receivePayment then disbursePayable, so a payout is also made from the simulator page. There is no Xero-side payables run and no detection of a payment made in Xero. The Control Panel explicitly says Run payables is the monitor's own product button.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - The in-app 'Run payables' payout (runPayables/disbursePayable, source 'system', office actor) makes the payment from the system; the requirement says Xero's payables run makes it and the system only detects and records it.
  - No Xero-simulator payables-run action (mark authorised ACCPAYs paid in Xero), and no webhook or poll detection of that payment feeding a disbursement record (US-09.2.4).
  - No Admin list of payables 'authorised, awaiting Xero's payables run' as a read-only status view in place of the payout button; the Billing monitor card wording ('to disburse to anaesthetists') presents AA as the payer.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing 'Payment received · full / half' triggers cover the authorisation. For the gap: on the Xero simulation pair page (and Admin Billing monitor) a bar button 'Xero payables run: pay authorised bills' that marks authorised ACCPAYs paid in Xero and fires the detection (webhook, or 'Run daily poll' for a missed one) so Admin then shows them as Disbursed; the Admin 'Run payables' button becomes a read-only 'Awaiting Xero payables run' list.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:1-60,147-175 (runPayables, disbursePayable; header 'UNBADGED office action ... proposed product')`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:55-146 (payablesDue, doRunPayables, 'Run payables' button)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:5,236-263 (disbursePayable called from the pair page 'Settle invoice')`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:90-145 (authorise exactly the cumulative amount received)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/payableRelease.ts:16-21`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/ROADMAP.md D53 and Phase 37 already plan this move`

## US-10.2.6 · Approve the period's BCTIs for payment · Partial

[US-10.2.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.6.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.6) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Once AA has generated the BCTIs for a payment period they are approved for payment before being paid out; BCTIs not yet approved are not in the payment run. Who approves and whether it is the same as releasing a payable is unknown (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** An ACCPAY enters the payables run only once authorised, which happens automatically when its receivable is paid; unauthorised (draft) payables are never in the run, so the acceptance criterion holds under the same-step reading. There is no payment period, week number, approve-for-payment action or state; the run is a free-running button.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No concept of a payment period or of approving that period's BCTIs as a step before payout.
  - No visible 'not yet approved, not in the run' state distinct from 'receivable unpaid'.
  - Weekly cycle and week number (US-10.2.7, Proposed) not built; the approval hangs on it.
- **Demo trigger:** Proposed: on Admin Billing monitor / payables list, a 'Close payment week and approve' action (demo bar button if it is not product UI) that approves the week's released BCTIs for payment; only approved ones appear in the Xero payables run.
- **Evidence:** `searched: approvedForPayment\|approve.*for payment\|payment period\|paymentWeek\|isoWeek in aa-prototype/src (non-test) -> none`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:36-44,147-155 (eligible = authorised minus disbursed > 0)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:129-142 (authorisation set on payment)`

## US-10.3.1 · Generate AA fee invoices · Partial

[US-10.3.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.3.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, domain · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** A monthly fee invoice run: one action generates every anaesthetist's fee invoice for the period from the fee settings and tracks each one in the ledger, kept separate from procedure receivables and payables. 40 BCTIs, $500 fixed, $5 per invoice gives $700.
- **Prototype today:** AaFeesScreen has a month stepper, preview table and 'Run monthly fee invoices'; aaFeeFor sums fixed items plus rate x BCTI count (500 + 5 x 40 = 700 ex GST, GST on top). Each invoice is stored in billing.aaFeeInvoices with snapshotted settings and counted BCTIs, plus an aaFee ACCREC with no payable; reruns are no-ops. Seeded fee history and 'Seed a month of BCTIs' show the example. Nothing records the fee invoice in an internal ledger (no ledger exists; Phase 36 plans an aaFee pair).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not tracked in the internal ledger as a receivable leg; the prototype has no ledger at all (planned Phase 36), only the aaFeeInvoices record and the Xero ACCREC.
  - Total shows GST on top of $700 (GST reading from US-05.2.7), not a flat $700 total; the story's example says the invoice is $700. Treated as a GST-presentation point, not a wrong rule.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Run scheduled month-end fee run' and 'Seed a month of BCTIs' on Admin AA fee invoices. Once the ledger exists, the Admin Ledger screen should show the fee invoice as a receivable leg after the run.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/aaFeeActions.ts:190-250`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/aaFee.ts:25-45`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/AaFeesScreen.tsx:87-136`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/aaFee.ts:25-31`, `searched: ledger in aa-prototype/src (non-test) -> only comments (bcti.ts:121 'Phase 36 ledger'), no ledger state`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/phases/phase-36-internal-ledger.md:33,54 (aaFee pair planned)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/aaFee.test.ts; aa-prototype/src/store/aaFeeActions.test.ts`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Matches. Corrected from Matches to Partial. The reviewer itself noted the missing ledger entry but still graded Matches; the story body says 'tracks each one in the ledger' and the domain model makes the internal ledger the system of record, with Phase 36 explicitly planning an aaFee pair. The $700 acceptance criterion, single action and separation from procedure payables all verified. Size M (the fee leg itself is small but depends on the Phase 36 ledger).

  - Depends on credit-after-invoicing flow US-08.6.2 (absent) as the source of the negative
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Raise credit on a paid invoice' on the admin invoice detail (creates a negative invoice to the anaesthetist), then 'Run payables' on Billing monitor pays the net and shows a Remittance advice panel (invoices, negative netted, net total) in Admin and Dr Souter's Web Accounts.

## US-10.2.7 · Weekly payment cycle · Missing

[US-10.2.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.7) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: admin, web, store, domain, seed, shell, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Payments run on a weekly cycle named by ISO week (Friday ledger close/snapshot/reconcile, Monday office working day, Tuesday payment schedule to the accountant, anomalies rolled into next cycle). A payment is shown against its cycle's week number; an anomaly not fixed by Tuesday is held out of this cycle's schedule and rolled forward. Status Proposed, still to confirm with accountant (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** Nothing for the cycle. runPayables creates Disbursements with payablesRunId (PR####) and clock time only (types.ts:824). ISO week number appears only as a label in the admin RightRail (RightRail.tsx:60,128), unrelated to payments. Demo clock is fixed at Tuesday 21 July 2026 8:00; no advance control found.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No payment cycle/week entity or ISO week label on runs, disbursements, or the web/admin payment lists
  - No Friday close/snapshot/bank reconciliation step
  - No Monday anomaly list or ability to hold a payable out of the run and roll it to next cycle
  - No Tuesday payment schedule (view/export for accountant)
  - Run payables not tied to a cycle day; seeded historical disbursements have no week
  - Payment lookup does not show week number (acceptance criterion 1 unmet)
- **Demo trigger:** Billing monitor 'Payment cycle' panel for week N with control-panel buttons shown only there: 'Close week (Friday)' snapshot+reconcile, 'Flag anomaly' on a payable (held), 'Build Tuesday schedule' (held items roll to week N+1), then Run payables pays the schedule and stamps the week number; web Payments and admin payment lists show 'Week N'.
- **Evidence:** `searched: isoWeek\|payment cycle\|accountant\|anomal\|friday\|roll(ed) forward in aa-prototype/src non-test -> only RightRail week label and unrelated canvas.rollForward`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:55-153`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:819-826`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-141`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/analysis/domain-model-delta.md DM-25 (same finding)`

## US-10.2.4 · Bulk remittance stays in Xero · Missing

[US-10.2.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.4) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: simulator, store, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Bulk hospital payments covering multiple invoices are matched by Xero's own bank reconciliation and remittance add-on, not the Billing/Invoice Engine; any item the add-on cannot match stays as an unmatched item in Xero's bank reconciliation for the office to handle there.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing that shows it. Payments reach the engine only as single-invoice webhook or poll events (full or half). The only related text is Xero-sim copy that the engine's InvoiceNumber is the key hospitals and insurers quote on remittances so remittance matching can key on it. Xero sim has Contacts and Invoices tabs only. The engine does no bulk matching (consistent with the requirement), but nothing shows Xero doing it or the unmatched leftovers.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No simulated bank feed or Xero bank reconciliation view
  - No bulk payment covering several invoices (one bank line, several ACCRECs) that Xero's add-on matches by reference and amount before the engine sees it
  - No unmatched line left in Xero for the office, and no 'what to do' hint for a receipt with no reference
- **Demo trigger:** On /demo/xero add a 'Bank reconciliation' tab and a badged (Demo simulation) button 'Receive bulk hospital payment': one bank line covering three open ACCRECs from the same hospital, matched by the remittance add-on (reference and amount) so the engine then receives three normal payment events, plus one bank line with no reference left 'Unmatched in Xero' for the office. Optional second button 'Office matches line'.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:66,548,718 (InvoiceNumber as remittance matching key; copy only)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/demoTriggers/registry.ts:548-578 (payment triggers are single-invoice only)`, `searched: remittance\|bank reconcil\|bank feed\|unmatched in src -> only DemoXero copy; 'unmatched appointment' integration wording is unrelated`
