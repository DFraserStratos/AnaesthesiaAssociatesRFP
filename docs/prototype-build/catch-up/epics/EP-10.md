# EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-10) · catalogue `efe123c` · [EP-10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-10.md)

> EP-10 and FT-10.1 match: the two-state model, release for exactly the amount received, separate AA fee invoice and the single payer-to-AA-to-anaesthetist path all exist, and no direct-payment pathway remains. The real gap in this slice is FT-10.2: the prototype's office 'Run payables' button and the Xero simulator 'settle' make the anaesthetist payment, where OQ-111 (answered 4 Nov, for the build, not yet seen by Greg) says the payment is made in Xero's payables run and the system only detects and records it. The fix overlaps US-09.2.4 (detect disbursement by webhook or daily poll), US-10.2.7 (weekly ISO-week cycle, Proposed, OQ-47 unresolved), US-10.2.6 (BCTI approval for payment) and US-10.2.5 (negative invoices netted in the run): plan them together so payablesActions is reworked once (keep the increment logic, move the trigger into the Xero simulator, add a poll catch-up for missed ACCPAY payments). The release rule (payableReleasedFor) and two-state chips need no change. The S3 money beat, the DemoControlPanel line 504 note, the DemoXero settle copy and the demo guide cheat sheet would change. PERSIST_VERSION bump only if the AccPay/Disbursement shape or seed changes (for example an ISO-week or Xero-run field). Unverified: this pass graded only EP-10, FT-10.1 and FT-10.2; the child stories (US-10.1.x, 10.2.x, 10.3.x) are graded elsewhere."

## FT-10.2 · Releasing payables · Contradicts

[FT-10.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-10.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-10.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, simulator, store, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Once a receivable is paid, its matching payable is authorised for inclusion in the next payables run so the anaesthetist is paid only what AA received. The payment to the anaesthetist is made in Xero's payables run; the system records and tracks it (US-09.2.4) and does not make the payment itself (OQ-111).
- **Prototype today:** receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY for exactly the received amount (full or part). The Billing monitor has an unbadged office 'Run payables' panel ('N payables authorised, $X to disburse to anaesthetists', button) that calls runPayables, which itself allocates a payablesRun id, writes Disbursement records, sets ACCPAY status paid and the case to disbursed, under a 'Payables run' system actor. DemoXero's 'settle' calls receivePayment then disbursePayable as OFFICE_ACTOR with copy 'has been paid to <anaesthetist>'. DemoControlPanel describes Run payables as product UI, not a demo action. There is no Xero-side payables run or ACCPAY-paid webhook/poll that the engine detects.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - The disbursement is performed by an AA office action ('Run payables', actor 'Payables run') in the product UI; the catalogue says the system must not make the payment but detect that Xero's payables run paid the ACCPAY (webhook or daily poll) and record it (US-09.2.4, OQ-111 answered)
  - No simulated Xero-side payables run or ACCPAY-paid event for the engine to detect; DemoXero settle calls the office disbursement action directly and presents it as AA paying
  - Admin panel wording ('to disburse to anaesthetists') and the DemoControlPanel note ('Run payables: product UI, not a demo action') frame it as AA's own run; no 'authorised, awaiting Xero payables run' framing
  - Authorised payables are not shown as 'included in the next payables run' with a week or ISO-week cycle (US-10.2.7, OQ-47); mainly belongs to child stories
- **Demo trigger:** Move the payment to the Xero simulator: a demo-bar button 'Run Xero payables run' on /demo/xero and the Billing monitor that marks every authorised ACCPAY paid in simulated Xero and fires a payment webhook (plus a 'missed webhook, daily poll catches it' variant) so the engine records the Disbursement and the Admin chips flip to Disbursed. The Admin 'Run payables' panel becomes a read-only 'Authorised, awaiting Xero payables run' list.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:1-20,56-170 (runPayables/disbursePayable write Disbursement records; PAYABLES_ACTOR)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:125-148 (Payables run panel and button)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:231-262 (settleInvoice calls disbursePayable as OFFICE_ACTOR)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:504 (Run payables described as product UI)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/payableRelease.ts (release rule correct)`, `searched: payables-run webhook or poll detection of ACCPAY paid -> none; reconciliation poll only covers ACCREC payments`

## US-10.2.6 · Approve the period's BCTIs for payment · Partial

[US-10.2.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.6.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.6) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, shell, demo-guide · confidence low

- **Requirement:** Once AA has generated the BCTIs for a payment period they are approved for payment before being paid out; BCTIs not yet approved are not in the payment run.
- **Prototype today:** Payables are draft until their receivable is paid, then authorised; runPayables disburses every authorised payable immediately with no further approval, period grouping or list of not-yet-approved items. BCTIs exist only as a count for the monthly AA fee.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No explicit approved-for-payment state or approve action for a period's BCTIs (Greg: an approved-for-payment list the office ticks through)
  - No payment period or weekly cycle grouping (US-10.2.7), so no 'BCTIs for a payment period'
  - Run payables takes everything authorised, with no approved/not-approved split or visible list of not-yet-approved items
  - No pre-run check that the critical numbers balance (Greg, 2026-10-28, under OQ-47/OQ-111)
- **Demo trigger:** Proposed: on Billing monitor, an 'Approve for payment' list on the Payables run card (per payment week) with an 'Approve all' button, and a demo-bar action 'Add unapproved BCTIs' (Billing monitor only) so unapproved ones visibly stay out of Run payables.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:147-160 (runPayables: eligible = every ACCPAY with authorised minus disbursed > 0)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:131-141 (authorisation only on payment)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/bcti.ts (BCTI count for the AA fee only)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-150 (single Run payables button)`, `searched: approved for payment\|approveFor\|paymentWeek\|isoWeek\|critical numbers\|reconcil in aa-prototype/src (non-test) -> none relevant`

## US-10.2.5 · Negative invoices netted in the payment run · Missing

[US-10.2.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.5) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** A negative invoice (refund or credit after invoicing) is netted against the anaesthetist's positive payables in the next payment run (19 positive + 1 negative = pay net); remittance advice shows the negative netted against the positives.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing. buildInvoicesForBooking refuses a negative group total as 'negativeTotal'; runPayables disburses only positive authorised increments, one Disbursement per ACCPAY; no credit note, refund, netting or remittance entity; anaesthetist apps have no remittance view.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No negative invoice / credit-driven negative payable entity
  - Payment run does not net negatives against positives; pays per payable, not a per-anaesthetist net total
  - No remittance advice (per run, per anaesthetist) in web, mobile or admin
  - No demo way to create a refund/credit after the anaesthetist is paid
  - Depends on credit-after-invoicing flow US-08.6.2 (absent) as the source of the negative
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Raise credit on a paid invoice' on the admin invoice detail (creates a negative invoice to the anaesthetist), then 'Run payables' on Billing monitor pays the net and shows a Remittance advice panel (invoices, negative netted, net total) in Admin and Dr Souter's Web Accounts.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:409-423 (negativeTotal refused)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:49-150 (per-ACCPAY increment, no netting)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/validateBookingForBilling.ts:233-241 (negative fee blocked)`, `searched (re-run): remittance\|refund\|credit note\|netting\|negative in src (non-test) -> only comments, negativeTotal guard, CSS margins, a DemoXero comment about remittance matching key`

## US-10.2.7 · Weekly payment cycle · Missing

[US-10.2.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.7) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: admin, web, store, domain, seed, shell, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Payments run on a weekly cycle named by ISO week (Friday ledger close/snapshot/reconcile, Monday office working day, Tuesday payment schedule to the accountant, anomalies rolled into next cycle). A payment is shown against its cycle's week number; an anomaly not fixed by Tuesday is held out of this cycle's schedule and rolled forward. Status Proposed, still to confirm with accountant (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** Nothing for the cycle. runPayables creates Disbursements with payablesRunId (PR####) and clock time only (types.ts:824). ISO week number appears only as a label in the admin RightRail (RightRail.tsx:60,128), unrelated to payments. Demo clock is fixed at Tuesday 21 July 2026 8:00; no advance control found.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No payment cycle/week entity or ISO week label on runs, disbursements, or the web/admin payment lists
  - No Friday close/snapshot/bank reconciliation step
  - No Monday anomaly list or ability to hold a payable out of the run and roll it to next cycle
  - No Tuesday payment schedule (view/export for accountant)
  - Run payables not tied to a cycle day; seeded historical disbursements have no week
  - Payment lookup does not show week number (acceptance criterion 1 unmet)
- **Demo trigger:** Billing monitor 'Payment cycle' panel for week N with control-panel buttons shown only there: 'Close week (Friday)' snapshot+reconcile, 'Flag anomaly' on a payable (held), 'Build Tuesday schedule' (held items roll to week N+1), then Run payables pays the schedule and stamps the week number; web Payments and admin payment lists show 'Week N'.
- **Evidence:** `searched: isoWeek\|payment cycle\|accountant\|anomal\|friday\|roll(ed) forward in aa-prototype/src non-test -> only RightRail week label and unrelated canvas.rollForward`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:55-153`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:819-826`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-141`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/analysis/domain-model-delta.md DM-25 (same finding)`
