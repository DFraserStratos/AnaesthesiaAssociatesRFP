# EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-10) · catalogue `1f067a8` · [EP-10](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-10.md)

> Core defect: AA fee is a 5% deduction on the ACCPAY (agencyFee.ts, xeroHandoff.ts:234, types serviceFee* fields, selectors paymentHistoryFor, Web Payments AA fee / Net to you columns, DemoXero fee box, seed history.ts and billing.ts, tests). Catalogue (EP-10, FT-10.3, US-10.2.1, US-10.3.1 all Confirmed) says fee is a separate AA-to-anaesthetist invoice and the payable equals the amount received. Plan as TWO work packages, no double counting: (1) Remove fee from ACCPAY (closes US-10.2.1; EP-10 and FT-10.3 partly; bump PERSIST_VERSION; reseed; reword DemoXero cards; update S3 Beat 2/3 and S4 Beat 5 in demo-guide 03-demo-script.md, 04-presenter-cheat-sheet.md lines 116-123, 02-workflows-and-handoffs.md lines 384-389, and master-demo-guide.html). (2) Add AA fee invoice entity + generation button + anaesthetist web view (closes US-10.3.1, FT-10.3, US-10.3.2). EP-10 is a roll-up of both. FT-10.2 regraded Matches (own text met; amount defect lives in US-10.2.1). US-10.2.4 needs a small Xero-simulator bulk remittance button and unmatched list (M). Already sound: single payment path, two independent money states, receipt reconciliation via shared receivePayment plus daily poll. Dependencies: EP-08 (ledger pair as first-class entity, system of record) since pair states live on BillingCase today; EP-09 (Xero mirror/ACCPAY authorise). Open questions needing labelled assumptions: OQ-02 (fee basis/frequency; also asks if fee nets against payables, so the 'separate invoice' reading is in the Confirmed text but not fully closed), OQ-47 (payment day/weekly cycle; no build needed now), OQ-19 (hospital dispute fallback; no behaviour). Existing demo controls cover payments: Control panel 'Payment received (webhook)' Full/Half, 'Run payables', 'Run reconciliation poll', Xero 'Simulate payment and payout'. New buttons proposed: 'Generate AA fee invoices' (Admin Billing Monitor + control panel scheduled jobs) and 'Simulate bulk hospital remittance' (Xero simulator Invoices tab). Catalogue screenshots for US-10.3.1 and US-10.3.2 show the current deduction UI and will be stale after the fix.

## EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · Contradicts

[EP-10](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-10.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-10) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, web, admin, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** All payments land in AA first, AA then disburses; two tracked states (paid into AA, disbursed) per ledger pair; full payment releases the full payable, part payment exactly the amount received; AA's fee is on its own invoice, distinct from any receivable/payable.
- **Prototype today:** ACCREC (payer to AA) plus DRAFT ACCPAY per billing case; receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY, runPayables disburses the increment; paid-in and disbursed tracked independently. But ACCPAY = gross less 5% (aaServiceFeeFor), no AA fee invoice exists, ledger pair is a BillingCase mirror, no payment cycle.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: AA fee netted off ACCPAY at handoff (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE 0.05) instead of a separate AA-to-anaesthetist invoice
  - WRONG: authorised/disbursed amounts are pro-rata of the fee-reduced net, not exactly the amount received
  - No AA fee invoice entity, numbering or ledger entry
  - Payment day/cycle (OQ-47) not modelled; payables run is an on-demand office button
  - Ledger pair not a first-class entity (EP-08 dependency)
  - OQ-19 (hospital dispute fallback): no behaviour required yet
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/agencyFee.ts:7,16`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:234 (payable = aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total)), :246-252 (ACCPAY spreads fee breakdown)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:41 proRataAuthorised, :95-104`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:96-130 disburse increment; fullyPaidOut compared to net amountPayable`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/InvoiceDocument.tsx:188-210 two-state chips`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:165-235 fee deduction columns`

[US-10.2.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-10.2.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.1) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, simulator, web, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** When a receivable is paid the matching payable is authorised. Full payment releases the whole payable; part payment releases a payable for exactly the amount received, remainder stays outstanding on the receivable. Payment day/cycle unsettled (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** receivePayment clamps to balance, updates ACCREC, sets ACCPAY amountAuthorised = ratio x amountPayable (gross less 5%), mirrors to BillingCase; second payment authorises the rest; runPayables pays only the increment; remainder stays on ACCREC. Payables run is an on-demand office button.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: authorised amount = received x 0.95 (fee deducted), not exactly the amount received; amountPayable set from aaServiceFeeFor at handoff
  - Selectors derive serviceFeeAmount = received - authorised and Web Payments shows AA fee / Net to you columns from it
  - Weekly/Tuesday-Wednesday cycle not modelled (only if OQ-47 resolves that way; open, so no build required)
  - Seed history and billing seeds embed fee-reduced payables (history.ts, billing.ts) and need reseed + PERSIST_VERSION bump
  - Demo guide S3 Beat 2/3 ($7.62 fee, $144.76 net), S4 Beat 5 ('proportional payable share'), cheat sheet lines 116-123 and workflows doc lines 384-389 need rewording
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Control panel 'Payment received (webhook)' Full/Half then 'Run payables'; Xero 'Simulate payment and payout'. Only if OQ-47 adopts a weekly cycle: add 'Close accounting week' on the Billing Monitor payables panel.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:41-49,95-104`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:234,246-252`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:35-60,96-130`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:751,804`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:581 Payment received Full/Half`, `docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:252-272,344`, `docs/demo-guide/04-presenter-cheat-sheet.md:116-123`
