# EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-10) · catalogue `a68f09b` · [EP-10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-10.md)

> Only FT-10.2 was graded here. Its authorise-on-receipt half (US-10.2.1, partial payments, no AA fee netting) matches. The gap is a structural change of who pays: disbursement must move out of the AA product (Admin Billing monitor 'Run payables', payablesActions.ts, and the Xero simulator's disbursePayable call) into the simulated Xero, with a new ACCPAY-paid detection handler (webhook plus poll, idempotent, audited with source system) that mirrors receivePayment. US-09.2.4 (Detect disbursement), US-10.2.7 (weekly payment cycle) and US-10.2.6 (BCTI approval) share this store and simulator surface, so they should reuse one 'Xero payables run' trigger and one Disbursement record rather than adding another payout path. Demo guide impact: S3 Beat 3 (narration 'AA pays' becomes 'Xero pays') and S4 Beat 5 (partial payment, two payables runs). Expected outcomes (payable authorises exactly what arrived, total paid equals invoice total, no double pay) are preserved. A PERSIST_VERSION bump is likely needed only if the Disbursement shape gains a source field. payablesActions, AccountsScreen and xeroNhi tests call disbursePayable directly and would need updating.

- **Requirement:** All payments land in AA first; two tracked states per ledger pair (paid in, disbursed); full payment releases full payable, part payment exactly the amount received; AA's fee is a separate invoice from AA to the anaesthetist, distinct from any receivable/payable.
- **Prototype today:** BillingCase tracks receivedAmount / authorisedAmount / disbursedAmount; receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY pro-rata; payables run disburses the increment. But ACCPAY = gross less an illustrative 5% 'AA service fee' (handoff computes aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total)), so payable != receivable and part payment releases 95% of the amount received. No separate fee invoice.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - AA fee modelled as 5% deducted from every payable (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE) instead of a separate fee invoice (wrong, superseded)
  - Payable value is 95% of receivable; catalogue says part payment releases exactly the amount received
  - Fee shown as a deduction in Xero simulator ACCPAY card ('Illustrative AA service fee' block) and in Web Payments history (Customer paid / AA fee / Net to you columns)
  - No AA fee invoice entity in the ledger
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/agencyFee.ts:7 (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE=0.05), :16 aaServiceFeeFor`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:234 (payable = aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total)), :245 (spread into XeroAccPay)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:53-58, :88-101 (authorised = ratio x amountPayable)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:96-100 (fully paid out vs amountPayable)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:697-719, :780-797`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:481-507`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:192`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:763-830`

## FT-10.2 · Releasing payables · Contradicts

[FT-10.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-10.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-10.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, simulator, store, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Once a receivable is paid, its matching payable is authorised so it can be included in the next payables run. The payment to the anaesthetist is made in Xero's payables run; the system records and tracks it and does not make the payment itself (OQ-111).
- **Prototype today:** receivePayment (webhook or poll, idempotent) bumps the ACCREC and flips the paired ACCPAY from DRAFT to AUTHORISED for exactly the cumulative amount received (partial payments included), audited as system. Disbursement is then done by AA: the Admin Billing monitor has a 'Run payables' button that calls runPayables, which pays amountAuthorised minus amountDisbursed on every ACCPAY and writes Disbursement records (office-only actor). The Xero simulator pair page's settle button calls receivePayment and then the AA-side disbursePayable as OFFICE_ACTOR. Nothing detects a payment made in Xero.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Contradicts OQ-111 and the new FT-10.2 text: the system makes the payout. The Admin 'Run payables' button (BillingMonitorScreen.tsx ~125-147, runPayables in payablesActions.ts ~147) is an AA product action that disburses, and its copy says 'to disburse to anaesthetists'. The payout should be a Xero-side simulated payables run, with the AA app only showing authorised payables awaiting it and then recording the result.
  - The Xero simulator's settle button (DemoXero.tsx ~229-262) calls the AA-side disbursePayable as OFFICE_ACTOR rather than raising a Xero payment event that the engine then detects.
  - No detection path for an ACCPAY paid in Xero: receivePayment and runReconciliationPoll handle ACCREC receipts only. No webhook or poll handler, idempotently keyed, records the Disbursement as a system-detected event (this is also what US-09.2.4 now requires).
  - No Admin view of the authorised payables queue ('included in the next payables run'): only a count and total on the Run payables card.
  - Demo guide S3 Beat 3 and S4 Beat 5 narrate AA 'running payables' as a product step, and the Control Panel note ('Run payables: the Billing monitor's own button, product UI') needs rewording.
- **Demo trigger:** Move the payout to the Xero simulation: on the Xero pair page and Xero invoices list add a 'Run Xero payables run' button (and a Control Panel entry on the Billing monitor and Xero screens) that marks every authorised ACCPAY paid in simulated Xero, then fires a payable-paid webhook (plus the daily reconciliation poll as the missed-event backstop) which the engine detects and records as the Disbursement, flipping the case and the anaesthetist's 'Paid to you' state. Replace the Admin 'Run payables' button with a read-only 'Authorised, awaiting the next Xero payables run' list.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:90-146 (authorise ACCPAY for cumulative received, DRAFT to AUTHORISED)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:36-60,147-170 (payablesDue, runPayables, disbursePayable, officeOnly)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-147 ('Run payables' product button)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:229-262 (settleInvoice calls receivePayment then disbursePayable under OFFICE_ACTOR)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:500-505 (note that Run payables is product UI)`, `searched: accpay paid/payment/webhook in src (non-test): no ACCPAY payment detection handler`, `requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-111.md (answered 4 Nov: Xero handles all payments, system tracks them)`
