## EP-10 · Payments, disbursement and AA fees · Contradicts

[EP-10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-10.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-10) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, simulator, web, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** All payments land in AA first; two tracked states per ledger pair (paid in, disbursed); full payment releases full payable, part payment exactly the amount received; AA's fee is a separate invoice from AA to the anaesthetist, distinct from any receivable/payable.
- **Prototype today:** BillingCase tracks receivedAmount / authorisedAmount / disbursedAmount; receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY pro-rata; payables run disburses the increment. But ACCPAY = gross less an illustrative 5% 'AA service fee' (handoff computes aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total)), so payable != receivable and part payment releases 95% of the amount received. No separate fee invoice.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - AA fee modelled as 5% deducted from every payable (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE) instead of a separate fee invoice (wrong, superseded)
  - Payable value is 95% of receivable; catalogue says part payment releases exactly the amount received
  - Fee shown as a deduction in Xero simulator ACCPAY card ('Illustrative AA service fee' block) and in Web Payments history (Customer paid / AA fee / Net to you columns)
  - No AA fee invoice entity in the ledger
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/agencyFee.ts:7 (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE=0.05), :16 aaServiceFeeFor`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:234 (payable = aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total)), :245 (spread into XeroAccPay)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:53-58, :88-101 (authorised = ratio x amountPayable)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:96-100 (fully paid out vs amountPayable)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:697-719, :780-797`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:481-507`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:192`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:763-830`

## US-10.2.6 · Approve the period's BCTIs for payment · Partial

[US-10.2.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.6.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.6) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, domain, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Once AA has generated the BCTIs for a payment period they are approved for payment before being paid out; unapproved BCTIs are not in the payment run. Who approves, and whether it is the same step as the US-10.2.1 authorisation, is unknown (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** ACCPAY is created DRAFT at Xero handoff and becomes authorised only when payment arrives; Run payables takes every authorised-minus-disbursed amount regardless of any period; no approve action, no payment period or ISO week, no approver, no awaiting-approval vs approved view. Unapproved (draft) payables do stay out of the run.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No explicit approval step or state for a payment period's BCTIs, distinct from payment-driven authorisation
  - No payment period (weekly cycle) to scope approval and the run (tied to US-10.2.7)
  - No UI separating BCTIs awaiting approval from approved
  - Admin role in approval undefined by the requirement; the prototype has none
- **Demo trigger:** Proposed: on Admin Billing monitor, an 'Approve week's BCTIs for payment' button (or Demo action) that moves the period's generated BCTIs from 'Awaiting approval' to 'Approved'; 'Run payables' then includes only approved ones. Design depends on OQ-47 and the weekly-cycle phase (US-10.2.7).
- **Evidence:** `searched: approv.*(payab\|bcti\|accpay\|payment\|period\|disburs) in aa-prototype/src (non-test) -> none`, `searched: isoWeek\|paymentWeek\|paymentCycle\|payment cycle\|weekly pay -> none`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:276 (ACCPAY status 'draft')`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:147-157 (runPayables eligibility = any authorised increment)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:~128-150 (Payables run card)`

## US-10.2.5 · Negative invoices netted in the payment run · Missing

[US-10.2.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.5) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** A negative invoice (refund or credit after invoicing) is netted against the anaesthetist's positive payables in the next payment run (19 positive + 1 negative = pay net); remittance advice shows the negative netted against the positives.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing. buildInvoicesForBooking refuses a negative group total as 'negativeTotal'; runPayables disburses only positive authorised increments, one Disbursement per ACCPAY; no credit note, refund, netting or remittance entity; anaesthetist apps have no remittance view.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No negative invoice / credit-driven negative payable entity
  - Payment run does not net negatives against positives; pays per payable, not a per-anaesthetist net total
  - No remittance advice (per run, per anaesthetist) in web, mobile or admin
  - No demo way to create a refund/credit after the anaesthetist is paid
  - Depends on credit-after-invoicing flow US-08.6.2 (absent) as the source of the negative
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Raise credit on a paid invoice' on the admin invoice detail (creates a negative invoice to the anaesthetist), then 'Run payables' on Billing monitor pays the net and shows a Remittance advice panel (invoices, negative netted, net total) in Admin and Dr Souter's Web Accounts.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:409-423 (negativeTotal refused)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:49-150 (per-ACCPAY increment, no netting)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/validateBookingForBilling.ts:233-241 (negative fee blocked)`, `searched (re-run): remittance\|refund\|credit note\|netting\|negative in src (non-test) -> only comments, negativeTotal guard, CSS margins, a DemoXero comment about remittance matching key`

## US-10.2.7 · Weekly payment cycle · Missing

[US-10.2.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.7) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: admin, web, store, domain, seed, shell, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Payments run on a weekly cycle named by ISO week (Friday ledger close/snapshot/reconcile, Monday office working day, Tuesday payment schedule to the accountant, anomalies rolled into next cycle). A payment is shown against its cycle's week number; an anomaly not fixed by Tuesday is held out of this cycle's schedule and rolled forward. Status Proposed, still to confirm with accountant (OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** Nothing for the cycle. runPayables creates Disbursements with payablesRunId (PR####) and clock time only (types.ts:824). ISO week number appears only as a label in the admin RightRail (RightRail.tsx:60,128), unrelated to payments. Demo clock is fixed at Tuesday 21 July 2026 8:00; no advance control found.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No payment cycle/week entity or ISO week label on runs, disbursements, or the web/admin payment lists
  - No Friday close/snapshot/bank reconciliation step
  - No Monday anomaly list or ability to hold a payable out of the run and roll it to next cycle
  - No Tuesday payment schedule (view/export for accountant)
  - Run payables not tied to a cycle day; seeded historical disbursements have no week
  - Payment lookup does not show week number (acceptance criterion 1 unmet)
- **Demo trigger:** Billing monitor 'Payment cycle' panel for week N with control-panel buttons shown only there: 'Close week (Friday)' snapshot+reconcile, 'Flag anomaly' on a payable (held), 'Build Tuesday schedule' (held items roll to week N+1), then Run payables pays the schedule and stamps the week number; web Payments and admin payment lists show 'Week N'.
- **Evidence:** `searched: isoWeek\|payment cycle\|accountant\|anomal\|friday\|roll(ed) forward in aa-prototype/src non-test -> only RightRail week label and unrelated canvas.rollForward`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:55-153`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:819-826`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-141`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/analysis/domain-model-delta.md DM-25 (same finding)`
