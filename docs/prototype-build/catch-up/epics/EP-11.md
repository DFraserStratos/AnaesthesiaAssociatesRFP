# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `fbdd555` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> Structural: the prototype's money model is route-first per Procedure (billingRoute hospital/billableParty/insurer, a BillableParty master, an Insurer master with acceptsDirectClaims, and contract holders of type hospital/insurer/surgeon/organisation/billableParty that bill the holder). EP-11 now assumes the flat Contract model: each Contract carries a 'bills its holder or the payer' setting, holders and insurers/funders are shared Parties (EP-04), and the Booking holds the payer (prefilled from the patient, with email or postal address), the insurer/funder and an approval number. FT-11.2, US-11.2.1, US-11.2.2 and US-11.4.3 therefore depend on the EP-04 contract rework (Party entity, bills-holder setting, No contract RVG and Fixed price fallbacks); the insurer acceptsDirectClaims master, setInsurerDirectClaims and direct-claim validation should be retired, and the Doyle bariatric surgeon-held Type 3 contract must bill the payer. Other dependencies: US-11.1.1 depends on US-11.1.4 and US-02.6.1 (NHI-less bookings wait as stubs; remove provisional patient creation in intake.ts, the PT0010 Noah Prescott seed and the provisionalNoNhi scenario). US-11.2.3 (payer email or postal address, NZ Post format; OQ-122 posted invoices) and US-11.2.4 are wholly absent. US-11.2.4, US-11.3.2 and US-11.4.3 depend on the warnings framework (US-13.7.1 and 13.7.2: one rule today, prepaymentUnpaid) and the dashboard to-do. US-11.3.2 and FT-11.3 also need a patient-level balance view in Admin (no Patients master/screen; only the Billing monitor 'Prior balance' pill), invoice-date aging against a fixed 90-day threshold, and a credit-balance concept (receivePayment clamps to balance). Anaesthetist-facing parts (payer edit, approval number, balance alert colour code) are absent on mobile and web: OfficeBillingSetup is office-only. Demo beats affected: S3 (Mitchell prior balance, nib insurer invoice, guardian Hana Park/Grace Park), S4 (prepayment, COS ACC billing failure, insuredReimbursement AIA) and S1 (NHI dedupe, NHI-less Noah Prescott). New beats needed for the booking-time balance alert and the under-18 payer warning. All ten reviewer gradings were upheld after re-checking the code; no verdict changes."
