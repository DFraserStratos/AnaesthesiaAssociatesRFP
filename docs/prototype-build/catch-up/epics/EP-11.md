## EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · Contradicts

[EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-11) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, web, mobile, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** One patient record per NHI (mandatory, no blank); each Booking names a payer (defaults to patient, guardian possible); insurers are master data with an accepts-direct-claims flag and the Booking records the approval number; the Contract decides who is billed (holder if it pays AA, else the Booking payer); admins see patient balances across anaesthetists and get mild/strong/credit alerts when the patient is booked again.
- **Prototype today:** upsertPatient dedupes on NHI but creates provisional NHI-less patients.   - NHI optional; provisional NHI-less patient created (contradicts mandatory, no blank)
  - Who is billed decided by route, not by Contract holder; no holder-pays-AA flag; no No contract (RVG) Contract
  - Payer lives on Procedure (billableParty route only), not on the Booking; no name+email auto-fill; no post-creation anaesthetist edit
  - No payer-email requirement; guardian form has no email field
  - Insurer flag flip auto-creates a protected default Type 1 Contract (US-04.3.3 now says no insurer defaults)
  - No insurance approval number on the Booking; insurer only on hospital/insurer routes
  - No cross-anaesthetist patient balance view; no mild/strong/credit alert at Booking create/match; no 90-day threshold
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:173 (nhi optional)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/intake.ts:97-118 (provisional patient)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:787,844 (BillingRoute, billablePartyId on Procedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:196-223 (counterpartyForProcedure by route)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/ReferenceViews.tsx:187-210 (Insurers view)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/mastersActions.ts:176-231 (auto default contract)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:206-210 (Prior balance pill)`
