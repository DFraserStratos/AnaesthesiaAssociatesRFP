# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `60e2d1e` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> Structural: the prototype decides who is billed by a hand-set per-Procedure billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer), with contract-holder billing implied by route 'hospital' (surgeon-, organisation- and billableParty-held Contracts bill the holder; seed Doyle bariatric bills the surgeon). The catalogue instead has the Contract decide (holder pays AA vs holder only sets price), a payer (name+email, defaulting to the patient) on the Booking, and a single No contract (RVG) default, with no per-hospital/insurer default Contracts. FT-11.2 and US-11.2.1/11.2.2 therefore need a domain change (Contract bills-holder flag, counterparty derivation, payer fields on Booking/Procedure) that cascades into invoiceBuild, validateBookingForBilling, seeds, Xero contact handoff (payer contact), the Insurers/Contract screens (remove protected defaults) and a PERSIST_VERSION bump. US-11.2.3 (payer email) and US-11.2.4 (under-18 warning) both depend on that payer model; US-11.2.4 also depends on a dashboard to-do list consuming the warning routine (US-13.7.2), which has no UI today (openWarnings etc. have no callers). NHI cluster: US-11.1.1 genuinely matches; US-11.1.4/11.1.5 need an Add NHI action, a missing-NHI anomaly/problem list and an authoriseList guard; Integration Monitor DataQualityCard is a reusable pattern. FT-11.3 (not graded here) is partially built: patientHasOutstandingPriorEpisode plus a 'Prior balance' badge in Billing Monitor, but no cross-anaesthetist balance view, no alert threshold or due date. No patient master or billable-party screen exists in Admin MasterData. Open questions OQ-49, OQ-93, OQ-98 leave NHI keying and insurer Contract shape uncertain. Demo guide: beats using the Grace Park / Hana Park guardian Booking, insured reimbursement, prepayment (billableParty route) and PDF-dedupe/NHI-validator stay valid until the route becomes Contract-derived; keep PT0010 Noah Prescott as the problem-list seed.
