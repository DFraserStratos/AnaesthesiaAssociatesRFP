# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `3d3a18c` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> Structural: explicit billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer) with per-Procedure billablePartyId, insurerId and patientPaymentCategory is the model the catalogue retired (OQ-67, OQ-55). FT-11.2, US-11.2.1, US-11.2.2 and US-11.4.2 close together with the Contract-defines-payer rework (catch-up phases on contract model, one contract per procedure, billable party and required inputs); plan once, not per story. OQ-78 (Greg: no default Contract, hospital holds every Contract) is open, so do not hard-code the default-Contract payer prompt beyond the catalogue recommendation. Dependencies: US-11.2.4 and US-11.3.2 need the Phase 15a warnings framework plus a UI that does not exist yet (dashboard to-do list, per-Booking warning display, clear action; EP-13 US-13.7.1/13.7.2) and a minimal admin threshold setting for the 90-day rule. US-11.2.4 also leans on US-11.2.3 (invoice email required, Confirmed, not built; no invoice email field exists anywhere) and the Contract-defined payer for its trigger. US-11.1.4 depends on US-11.1.5 (problem list), OQ-49, and touches authoriseList, ReviewScreen flags and US-07.3.1; attaching an NHI later also needs a patient edit field and duplicate handling. Seed changes: child as patient-direct payer, patient with unpaid invoice older than 90 days, patient with credit balance, patient without NHI on a List to authorise; PERSIST_VERSION bump. No standalone patient master or record screen exists though story screenshots for US-11.1.1 and US-11.3.1 imply one; consider a small admin patient view. US-11.1.1 is the only real Matches and the dedupe beat (S1 MSG-STG-1001, Sarah Mitchell) stays valid. Demo guide: S2 phone-advice booking and S3 money flow use billing-route and guardian wording and change with the rework; S4 exceptions would gain child-payer and prior-balance warnings. Story images for US-11.2.2 are known stale.

## FT-11.2 · Billable party and invoice contact · Contradicts

[FT-11.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-11.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-11.2) · status Verify · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, pwa, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each Procedure's Contract defines the billable party and where the invoice goes; picking a default Contract asks for the payer's name and email. No separate per-Booking billable party (OQ-67). A hospital taking the invoice at default RVG pricing is a Contract with that hospital as billable party.
- **Prototype today:** Each Procedure has an explicit billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer) and optional governingContractId. On the billableParty route the payer defaults to the patient, with an optional per-Procedure billablePartyId (guardian record). Hospital and insurer routes bill the contract holder. Picking a Contract never asks for a payer or email.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: separate billing-route step and per-Procedure billablePartyId override, the model OQ-67 retired
  - WRONG: Contract does not define the billable party; counterpartyForProcedure decides from route, not Contract (only one seed Contract has holderType billableParty)
  - MISSING: default Contract pick does not prompt for payer name and email; no invoice email captured on the billing path (BillableParty.email exists but no UI collects it)
  - MISSING: payer independent of pricing as an explicit Contract choice (hospital taking invoice at default RVG works only via hospital route and the default Type 1 fallback)
  - Uncertain: OQ-78 open; graded against the catalogue text as written
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:409 (BillingRoute), :444 (Procedure.billingRoute, billablePartyId, patientPaymentCategory), :216 (Contract.holderType)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:192-212 (counterpartyForProcedure: route decides payer)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx:127-169 (route Segmented, Payer select, New guardian: name, relationship, phone, no email)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/ManualBookingForm.tsx:235-250`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:44,68-71`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billablePartyActions.ts:16-42 (email accepted, never collected)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/contracts.ts:125 (only billableParty-held Contract)`
