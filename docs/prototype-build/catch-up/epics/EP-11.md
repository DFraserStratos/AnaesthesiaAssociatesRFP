# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `c9b65ad` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> All gaps in EP-11 trace to one structural change: the payer moves from Booking.payer (aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:812, written only by setBookingPayer in store/payerActions.ts:134) onto each Procedure. It is read by billablePartyForProcedure, payerDetails, childPayerOn, payerContactMissing, invoiceBuild (one invoice per Booking per distinct party), the Xero handoff, prepayment grouping and Review flags. Doing it needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump and seed changes (the guardian Hana Park payer and the payer-review Bookings). It also needs the 'apply to the other Procedures' ticked offer, later-added Procedure inheritance (addProcedure currently creates no payer), a payer line with relationship and the 'Email or postal address needed' and 'Under 18' warnings in each Procedure's 'Paid by' line (PaidByRow today shows the invoiced party or split for US-08.2.3), 'not billed yet' wording, removal of the Booking-level Payer section, and re-captured screenshots (US-11.2.x images show the per-Booking payer). US-11.2.1, 11.2.3 and 11.2.4 are small only once US-11.2.2 is built; size the move once, under US-11.2.2 or FT-11.2. What already matches: Contract-decides rule, billsTo, NZ Post address, Different payer tick, contact-required asking and Review flag, mild child warning with one to-do per Booking. Open questions: OQ-140 (per-Procedure payer; also affects US-08.2.3 split naming another person, US-06.3.1 prepayment grouping, US-08.2.1 shared invoices), OQ-122 (posted invoices; the prototype provisionally accepts post alone) and OQ-114 (delivery beyond email and post; prototype is email else post). Event billable party (US-03.7.1) and the balances alert are graded in other epics; no event billable-party code was found in src. No new demo triggers are needed, since all of this is user-driven. The guardian beat (Grace Park, Hana Park) and the child-warning beat in the demo guide would change to the per-Procedure line; S1 to S5 impact was not checked in detail.
