# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `1f067a8` · [EP-11](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-11.md)

> Structural: EP-11 gaps are dominated by the Booking-level billing-party change (DM-10 plus DM-06/DM-09 contract reshape). Payer and invoice email must move from Procedure/route-derived fields to Booking-level fields independent of pricing; US-11.2.2, 11.2.3, 11.2.4 and 11.4.2 all hang off it. Sequence after the FT-04 contract and Card-to-Booking rename work (DM-01, DM-06, DM-09, DM-10 are foundations). The catalogue screenshots for US-11.1.1, 11.2.1, 11.2.2, 11.3.2, 11.3.3, 11.4.1 and 11.4.2 are the CURRENT prototype UI (Card detail patient block, office billing setup, billing-monitor Prior balance pill, invoice Email button), so 'screen exists' does not mean the story is met; grade against the text and ACs. Missing patient surface: Admin has no patient record/search screen; one new Patient screen (NHI, demographics, ethnicity, invoices with paid state, follow-up log) would serve US-11.1.1 (ethnicity), 11.1.4 (attach NHI), 11.3.1 and 11.3.3. Missing-NHI problem list (US-11.1.5) can reuse the Integrations Data quality pattern (dataQualityItems) and needs an attachNhi/merge store action that matches an existing NHI record (also closes US-11.1.4). Balance alert (US-11.3.2) needs a booking-time evaluation on createCard/HL7/PDF plus an Admin threshold setting; reuse invoice raisedAtISO and bucketForAgingDays. Dependencies: US-11.1.5 links US-03.1.5 and US-07.2.2 (review flags, also under-18 flag, OQ-54); US-11.2.3 links US-04.2.7 and US-08.4.2; US-11.3.1 depends on the ledger work (DM-18); US-11.4.1 relates to OQ-20/OQ-23. Open questions: OQ-49 (Booking without NHI), OQ-54 (block or warn on child payer), OQ-41 (threshold basis), OQ-30 (NHI not in Xero; prototype already follows Appendix 2). PERSIST_VERSION bump needed for seed/shape changes (currently 13). Demo-script impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S1 beat 1 (MSG-STG-1001 creates Sarah Mitchell card) would raise the new unpaid-balance alert (Mitchell $610 raised 2026-04-14 is 98 days old at DEMO_TODAY) so that beat needs a line; S4 post-op addendum also uses Mitchell. New missing-NHI exception affects S1 PDF/HL7 no-NHI rows and Noah Prescott. Reworked billing setup UI changes beats using billableParty and reimbursement cards (Chen/Grace Park guardian, Rutherford AIA). Already aligned: NHI dual-format validation (mod-11 vs catalogue 'modulus 24' wording is a settled ruling; tell catalogue owners), hidden-ID keyed Xero contacts, guardian payer concept, insured-reimbursement invoice wording, NHI dedupe on all intake paths. No OutOfScope items in this epic.

## US-11.2.2 · Guardian or other override · Contradicts

[US-11.2.2](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-11.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.2.2) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, mobile, web, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins and anaesthetists can override billable party and invoice email on a Booking. Payer is independent of pricing: AC 'Procedure on default RVG Contract, admin sets a hospital as billable party -> price unchanged, invoice goes to the hospital'.
- **Prototype today:** Payer derived per Procedure: insurer route -> insurer; billableParty route -> BillableParty or patient; else resolved contract's holder (the List's hospital). Guardian picker appears only on the billableParty route; guardian sheet captures name, relationship, phone (createBillableParty supports email but the UI never sets it). Anaesthetist can pick an existing guardian only in ManualCardForm at creation; EditBillingSetupSheet is office-only (CardDetailBody renders it for office). A hospital payer is possible only via the Contract-holder route and only the List's own hospital, and then pricing follows that hospital's contract.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Billable party not independent of route/pricing (old RFP reading): cannot choose an arbitrary hospital or other organisation as payer while staying on the default RVG Contract
  - Invoice email not part of the override
  - Anaesthetists cannot change payer or add a new guardian after creation (mobile/web billing context is read-only)
  - Payer is per Procedure, not per Booking (DM-10)
  - AC (hospital as billable party, price unchanged) achievable only indirectly
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:192-210`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx:118-150`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/card/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:36-89`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/card/CardDetailBody.tsx:683-691`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/ManualCardForm.tsx:232-250`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billablePartyActions.ts:21-60`, `docs/prototype-build/catch-up/analysis/domain-model-delta.md (DM-10)`

## US-11.3.2 · Alert on booking a patient with unpaid bills · Contradicts

[US-11.3.2](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-11.3.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.3.2) · status Verify · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On Booking create/match, alert admin if the patient has an invoice unpaid longer than a configurable threshold (start 90 days; count basis OQ-41); a 30-day-old unpaid invoice raises no alert; a changed threshold applies to the next check.
- **Prototype today:** patientHasOutstandingPriorEpisode returns true for any other non-cancelled Card with money outstanding; used only to set MonitorCardRow.outstandingPriorBalance -> 'Prior balance' pill in BillingMonitorScreen (its tooltip admits open vs overdue is not distinguished). No threshold, no booking-time alert, nothing on manual/HL7/PDF create. The catalogue screenshot admin-prior-balance.png is this existing pill. Invoices carry raisedAtISO so aging maths exists.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No age test or threshold: a 30-day-old unpaid invoice would still flag (violates AC 2)
  - Evaluated only in the billing monitor post-billing, not on Booking create/match
  - No admin-editable threshold setting (AC 3)
  - No booking-time alert surface for the admin (banner, review flag or problem list)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: S1 beat 1 (replay MSG-STG-1001) matches Sarah Mitchell, whose $610 invoice raised 14 Apr is 98 days old at DEMO_TODAY, so the new booking-time alert would fire; the demo clock menu ages invoices. Add an 'Alert threshold (days)' field in Admin Master data. Seed a patient with a 30-day-old unpaid invoice for the negative case. No new button strictly needed.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:276-297`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:492`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:200-206`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/dateDays.ts (bucketForAgingDays)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/history.ts:107 (Mitchell unpaid raised 2026-04-14)`

## US-11.1.4 · Patient without NHI · Partial

[US-11.1.4](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-11.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.4) · status Open · surfaces: admin, store, web, mobile · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins can create a patient before the NHI is known and attach it later; shows NHI pending meanwhile. NHI is required; whether the Booking may exist pending is OQ-49 (Open).
- **Prototype today:** upsertPatient with no NHI creates a provisional Patient (createdProvisional); manual form does not require NHI; nhiBadge renders 'NHI pending'; Noah Prescott (PT0010) seeded via the PDF pathway.   - Manual form allows no-NHI silently, with no exception treatment (OQ-49 unresolved)
