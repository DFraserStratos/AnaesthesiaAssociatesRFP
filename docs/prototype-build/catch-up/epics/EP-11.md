# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> All 14 gradings upheld after re-opening the code. Structural: the prototype's EP-11 model is the old per-Procedure billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer) with a separate Insurer master and a shared BillableParty (guardian) master, resolved in invoiceBuild.counterpartyForProcedure. The catalogue replaces all three: the Contract (holder's is-billed setting, EP-04 / US-04.1.5) decides who is billed, insurers are plain contract holders, and the payer (name, email, postal address) lives on the Booking prefilled from the patient (or a 'different payer' tick), with no guardian record. This is the XL refactor behind FT-11.2, US-11.2.1 and US-11.2.2, touching domain/types.ts, invoiceBuild.ts, validateBookingForBilling.ts, billingRun, Xero contact creation (a payer without a record), seed, tests and PERSIST_VERSION; it depends on EP-04 (is-billed flag, No contract (RVG) default, own-Contract) and FT-03.7 (event billable party). Mandatory NHI (US-11.1.1/11.1.4/11.1.5) removes the provisional patient (PT0010 Noah Prescott) and needs the unmatched queue and List stubs from EP-02 (US-02.1.4, FT-02.6, OQ-118); ManualBookingForm, ingestPdfRow, Review and web List 'NHI pending' badges all change. Balance alerts (FT-11.3 / US-11.3.2) and child-payer / missing-approval warnings (US-11.2.4, US-11.4.3) share the warnings framework already built in Phase 15a (WARNING_RULES currently only prepaymentUnpaid; warningSamples.ts already earmarks the child-payer and balance samples) plus a patient-level balance incl. credit from the billing mirror. US-11.1.2 is the one Matches; the only wrinkle is the catalogue's 'modulus 24' label for the current format versus the prototype's deliberate, on-screen-flagged mod 11 (true Health NZ algorithm); switching would break real NHIs, so treat the catalogue wording as a label error to raise with the owner. Open questions OQ-114, OQ-93, OQ-118, OQ-122 do not change grades but limit how far work can be finalised. Demo-guide beats affected: S2 Beat 2 (phone-advice booking, billingRoute hospital, NHI DEM1239), S3 Beats 1 and 2 (explicit payer per Procedure, nib / St George's, insurer-route invoice payer nib), S4 Beat 1 prepayment (Billable Party route), S5 Beat 3 (BP0001 hidden ID guardian, no personal info in Xero); S5 Beat 2 (NHI validator) stays valid; the Grace Park guardian and Noah Prescott 'NHI pending' seeds would be replaced by a child-as-payer warning demo and an unmatched-queue demo.

  - WRONG: surgeon/organisation-held fixed-price Contracts bill the holder (counterpartyForProcedure uses holderType/holderId) instead of the payer
  - WRONG: payer is per Procedure and office-only, not on the Booking and editable by the anaesthetist
  - MISSING: contract holder 'is billed' setting and link to its billable party record (US-04.1.5)
  - MISSING: No contract (RVG) as the default Contract that bills the payer
  - MISSING: event billable party defaulting to the Procedure's (FT-03.7)
  - Acceptance 'new Booking, payer is the patient until changed' holds only implicitly (no billablePartyId means patient, on billableParty route only)
