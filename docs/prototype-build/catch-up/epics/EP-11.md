# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `d436947` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> EP-11 is largely un-built against the current reading. The prototype keeps the July 2026 route-first model (Procedure.billingRoute hospital/billableParty/insurer; Insurer master with acceptsDirectClaims; payer as an optional Procedure override; optional NHI with provisional patients) where the catalogue is now Contract-decides-who-is-billed with a holder 'is billed' setting, a Booking-level payer defaulting to the patient, insurers as ordinary contract holders, insurer + approval number as Booking fields and a mandatory NHI. One thing the first pass missed: a precursor of the debtor alert exists as the 'Prior balance' pill in the admin Billing Monitor (patientHasOutstandingPriorEpisode in store/selectors.ts:552, cross-anaesthetist, seeded for Sarah Mitchell, documented in the demo guide cheat sheet at line 369). It is boolean and mild-only, so the threshold, strong alert, credit alert, admin patient balance view, Booking/Day alert and anaesthetist List alert are all still to build; the work can derive from existing billing cases and receivables. Dependencies: US-04.1.5 (holder 'is billed' flag and delivery output, OQ-114), US-04.2.14 and US-04.3.3 (own Contract, No contract (RVG)), US-04.3.2 (insurance does not narrow the Contract list), FT-02.1 / US-02.1.4 (matching screen and unmatched queue, not built), FT-02.6 (stubs on the List, OQ-118), US-13.7.1 (warnings framework: only prepaymentUnpaid exists), US-07.2.2 (office review), FT-03.7 (event billable party), US-13.6.x (room contacts, OQ-127). Structural change: payer moves from Procedure to Booking, the 'insurer' billing route and Insurer master are retired, the NHI becomes required and the provisional-patient path goes, so PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped and the seed changed (provisional patient PT0010 Noah Prescott and its Booking, nib insurer-route bookings, surgeon-held Doyle and COS ACC organisation-held contracts, the billableParty-held contract). Scripted beats at risk: S1 billing beat (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md about lines 438-471; invoices AA-2026-0005 'payer nib' and Alan Prentice St George's) depends on the insurer route resolving to nib; S1 intake reuse by NHI (about lines 134-135) stays valid; the S4 COS ACC billing-failure beat and the bariatric surgeon-held contract change who is billed under the new rule; integration beats (about lines 559-575, replayed Losa Tuilagi / CPH PID-3) and the manual Add booking beat change once the NHI is mandatory; the cheat-sheet 'Prior balance' mention would be superseded by the new alert.

## EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · Contradicts

[EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-11) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, web, mobile, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** One patient record per NHI (NHI mandatory, no blank); each Booking names a payer defaulting to the patient; the selected Contract decides who is billed (holder's billable party if the holder pays AA, else the Booking payer); insurers are ordinary contract holders; an insured Booking holds insurer + approval number with a warning if the number is missing; admins see a patient's outstanding balances across all anaesthetists and get a mild/strong alert (by threshold) when a debtor is booked again and a mild alert for a credit; anaesthetists see the alert on their List.
- **Prototype today:** upsertPatient dedupes by NHI but accepts no NHI and creates a provisional patient, and a Booking then follows ('NHI pending'). Payer is an optional billablePartyId on the Procedure, meaningful only on the 'billableParty' route (defaults to the patient there). Who is billed is chosen by the per-Procedure billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer); on the hospital route the resolved Contract's holder is billed whatever its type (surgeon, organisation, hospital, insurer). Insurers are a separate master (acceptsDirectClaims) and an 'insurer' billing route. No approval number field or warning. The only balance check is patientHasOutstandingPriorEpisode (any other non-cancelled Booking of the same patient, across all anaesthetists, with an unpaid invoice), shown as a small 'Prior balance' pill beside the patient name in the admin Billing Monitor booking rows. There is no patient balance screen, no threshold, no mild/strong split, no credit alert, no alert on the Add booking or Day view, and nothing on the anaesthetist's List.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - NHI optional / provisional patient path contradicts 'NHI mandatory, no blank'
  - Payer is a Procedure field set only on the billableParty route, not a Booking-level payer defaulting to the patient for every Booking
  - Contract does not decide who is billed: no holder 'is billed' flag; surgeon- and organisation-held contracts bill the holder; route 'insurer' bills the insurer regardless of Contract
  - Insurers are a master list plus a billing route (acceptsDirectClaims), not ordinary contract holders
  - No insurer approval number field and no missing-approval warning
  - No admin view of a patient's outstanding balances across anaesthetists (only a boolean 'Prior balance' pill in the Billing Monitor, which has no amounts and no threshold)
  - No strong-vs-mild debtor alert (threshold), no credit-balance alert, and no alert when the patient is booked again on the Add booking / Day view; nothing on the anaesthetist's List
  - No alert threshold setting
  - Anaesthetist cannot change the payer (EditProcedureSheet has no payer field; only the office EditBillingSetupSheet does)
  - OQ-114 (invoice delivery flag on holder / billable party) unresolved: no output flag exists
- **Demo trigger:** Seed two returning patients (one owing AA from an unpaid prior invoice with a known amount, one holding a credit from an overpayment) and add a control-panel button on the Admin Day view and the Add booking sheet, 'Rebook returning patient who owes AA' / 'who is in credit', that opens Add booking pre-filled with that NHI so the mild/strong alert shows on the admin Booking and on the anaesthetist's List. Add an admin 'Alert threshold' setting to flip mild to strong.

## FT-11.2 · Who is billed: holder or the payer on the Booking · Contradicts

[FT-11.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-11.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-11.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, mobile, web, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** The Contract alone decides who is billed: if its holder pays AA (insurer, hospital) the invoice goes to the holder's billable party; otherwise (surgeon price-setter, anaesthetist's own Contract, No contract (RVG)) it goes to the payer on the Booking, who is the patient until the office or anaesthetist changes it. An event's billable party defaults to its Procedure's.
- **Prototype today:** Procedure.billingRoute (hospital / billableParty / insurer) is chosen explicitly. counterpartyForProcedure returns the insurer on route 'insurer', the patient or a BillableParty override on route 'billableParty', else the resolved contract's holder whatever its type. A surgeon-held bariatric or organisation-held COS ACC contract therefore bills the holder. The payer is only a Procedure billablePartyId override, editable by the office only (EditBillingSetupSheet) on the billableParty route; the anaesthetist's EditProcedureSheet has no payer. The Billable Party route with no contract roughly plays the role of 'No contract (RVG) bills the payer', but it is reached by route choice, not by the Contract. No event billable party.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No 'holder pays AA / is billed' setting on the Contract holder; surgeon- and organisation-held contracts bill the holder, contradicting AC2 (a holder that only sets the price bills the payer)
  - Route-first model: route 'insurer' and 'billableParty' override the Contract, contradicting 'the Contract alone decides'
  - Payer is not a Booking field defaulting to the patient for every Booking (AC3); it is an optional Procedure override meaningful only on the billableParty route
  - Anaesthetist cannot change the payer, only the office (EditBillingSetupSheet)
  - No holder-to-billable-party link on the Contract holder; payer record has name, relationship and an optional phone only in the form (BillableParty type has email and address but the sheet does not capture them)
  - Event billable party defaulting to the Procedure's absent (mostly FT-03.7)
  - Insurer treated as a master with acceptsDirectClaims, not an ordinary contract holder (OQ-114 open on the output/delivery flag)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:114-180 (resolveContractForProcedure falls back by route)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:193-212 (counterpartyForProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:367-374,787,831-846 (ContractHolderType; BillingRoute; billablePartyId override)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/contracts.ts:97 (surgeon-held), :110 (organisation-held), :125 (billableParty-held)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx:64-96,158-170 (payer only on billableParty route, office; guardian form relationship + phone)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditProcedureSheet.tsx (no payer field, grep payer -> none)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:56-72`, `searched: isBilled\|billsHolder\|holderPays\|holderIsBilled in src -> none`
