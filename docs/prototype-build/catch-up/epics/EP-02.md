Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-02) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-02](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-02.md)

> The matching screen (US-02.1.2 and its children, outside these four items) is the keystone. Every hospital-side gap in FT-02.1 and US-02.1.1 closes only when it exists. Today the feed in the Integrations monitor auto-applies messages (interim code marked 'until Phase 33'), and DraftOrigin 'hospitalRow' is defined but nothing creates it. Moving hospital rows behind the screen turns the Messages tab from an applying log into a producer of rows. The 'Hospital feed changes the primary' trigger (feed-changes-primary) is documented as applying directly until then. The Integrations Future-scope badges on Messages and Feed config also need rethinking, since the hospital import is in scope as a row source. Dependencies: build US-02.2.4 (List origin and sender address) before US-02.3.3, whose To-address rule reads it. US-02.3.3 also relies on the change history (US-02.5.5), Hospital.contactEmail (exists), surgeon office contacts (US-13.6.5, exist) and the List-move notification (US-13.8.2, exists). The older surgeon-room email helpers (roomEmailForSurgeon, otherRoomsForSurgeon, guarded by roomContactsGuard.test.ts) are superseded by the 18 November ruling (List sender, else first office contact) and need revisiting, including that guard test. OQ-13 (hospital format) and OQ-124 (whether emailed Lists stop) are open but do not block building the screen or the email draft. A new List field means PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped when US-02.2.4 is built. Grading note: FT-02.1 and US-02.1.1 are both marked Contradicts because the direct-apply feed is the replaced reading; US-02.1.1 is the more arguable of the two (Partial is defensible). Demo script impact: S1 (around line 140 of docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md) fires 'S12 · New booking · MSG-STG-1001' and the Booking appears straight away on Dr Souter's List. Once hospital rows go behind the matching screen that beat needs an office review and accept step. The S1 aside on the Tue 4 Aug 09:30 'right total knee' Booking and the S2 optional 'hospital's wording arrives' aside (around line 343) rely on direct application and would change. The S2 List-move notification beats (around lines 424 to 468) would gain a 'Draft update email' step with US-02.3.3. The surgeon PDF ingest beat would need the sender address shown or typed at upload.
