# EP-02 · Booking intake and change handling · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-02) · catalogue `501b0b8` · [EP-02](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-02.md)

> Structural: EP-02 intake rests on a staged matching screen (US-02.1.2 to 02.1.4, outside this batch) that does not exist. The prototype applies HL7/FHIR messages directly to Cards (processMessage) and ingests PDF rows straight into a chosen DRAFT List. US-02.1.1 and US-02.1.5 need one shared staging model: staged hospital rows, per-hospital sync state, unmatched queue. Build once, then re-point the HL7/FHIR simulator at staging so messages stop silently applying. That reworks demo beat S1. Catalogue now puts HL7/FHIR and further feeds in EP-14 Future Work, while the prototype's only automatic intake is that simulator; decide whether the simulator stays as a Future Work demo (labelled) or becomes the sync source for fixtures. Dependencies: US-02.3.3 needs hospital and surgeons'-room contact emails (US-13.4.1, US-13.6.1) plus change tracking from US-02.3.2. US-02.3.2 explicit save touches shared CardDetailBody (also used by anaesthetist apps): gate staged-save on the office actor (isOffice already exists there) so anaesthetist apps stay write-through. Related: US-02.5.5 (append-only history, audit exists), US-02.5.6 (concurrent edits caught at save). Open questions: OQ-69 (prompt vs on-demand button; recommended on-demand with remembered changes), OQ-65 (anaesthetist moves own List), OQ-13 (hospital download format; canned fixtures only). Matches (FT-02.2, US-02.4.1) are sound; only the Card to Booking copy lags (AddCardFlow, Card added, Photo of paper list), best handled as one terminology sweep. Demo-guide impact: S1 (MSG-STG-1001 shows Sarah Mitchell appearing on Souter's 28 Jul list) changes with a matching screen; S2 gains a draft-email step after the reassign; S4 dead-letter/locked-target messages need rethinking if auto-apply goes; S5 PDF and photo beats unaffected.
