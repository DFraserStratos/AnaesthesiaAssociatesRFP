Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-02) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-02](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-02.md)

> All eleven gradings upheld after re-reading the code. The central gap is an Admin hospital matching-and-creating screen (review and merge, unmatched queue, accept as new, approve or reject differences, save a Draft List or assign an anaesthetist), fed by a simulated day-before hospital list from St George's and Southern Cross. Code comments (integrationActions.ts:149,169; draftListActions.ts:74; NotificationsScreen.tsx:19) show the earlier plan already pointed at a Phase 33 matching screen and a Phase 35 update email, neither built. The existing HL7/FHIR simulator, feed config and Integrations monitor are badged Future scope and auto-apply to Bookings via applyEffect; they should be reframed or routed into the new screen. Contradictions to fix together: (1) NHI optional with provisional patients and 'NHI pending' Bookings (store/intake.ts, shared/format.ts nhiBadge, web ListDetailView.tsx:82, admin ReviewScreen.tsx:261, seeded provisional patient and Booking, ingestPdfRow blank NHI) versus NHI mandatory with no-NHI rows waiting at intake (also touches EP-11 US-11.1.4/11.1.5/11.1.1, seed, and PERSIST_VERSION, currently 25); (2) integration writes refused on SUBMITTED Lists (lifecycle.editRefusal) versus inbound changes accepted until authorised (US-02.5.4); (3) S13 move to another day is a reassign, not cancellation plus new Booking (US-02.5.2, whose swimlane is Future Work for the automatic part). Stubs (FT-02.6) depend on the matching screen and NHI rule and are open (OQ-118): sequence after intake and build only if OQ-118 is answered yes. US-02.3.3 draft update email is independent (history, Hospital.contactEmail, SurgeonRoom.contactEmail and the notification pool already exist) and links to EP-13 notifications and US-01.4.3. Demo script impact: S1 currently presents HL7/FHIR as illustration (DemoControlPanel.tsx:262) and would move to the matching-screen flow; S2 gains hospital rows landing in the matching screen and a Draft List created from a row; S4 dead-letter beat should become an unmatched or no-NHI row. Other children of EP-02 (US-02.5.x, US-02.2.x) were not in this batch.

## EP-02 · Booking intake and change handling · Contradicts

[EP-02](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-02.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-02) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, store, domain, simulator, seed, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Bookings come from four sources (hospital, surgeons' rooms, admin, anaesthetist ad hoc). Every change is recorded with its source in the change history. In release 1 nothing is applied automatically: hospital data lands on a review and merge screen where the office accepts new rows and approves or rejects differences. A day move is a cancellation plus a new Booking. Inbound changes (through review and merge) are accepted until the office authorises the List.
- **Prototype today:** Records Booking.source (hospitalDownload, surgeonPdf, admin, anaesthetistAdHoc) with labels, and audit entries carry actor, role and source shown in the Booking history. But canned HL7/FHIR messages (S12 new, S13 reschedule, S14 modify, S15 cancel) are applied immediately by applyEffect via createBooking/editBooking/reassignBooking/cancelBooking as the 'integration' actor. The surgeon PDF inbox (review rows then Ingest) is the only human review step. editRefusal refuses an integration actor on any non-ACTIVE List (so a SUBMITTED List parks the message). Bookings without an NHI are created on a provisional patient. The hospital pathway is badged Future scope and the S1 demo copy says the in-scope download 'arrives in a later build' (code comments say Phase 33 will add the matching screen).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No review and merge screen for hospital data; HL7/FHIR messages apply automatically (contradicts 'nothing is applied automatically')
  - Integration writes refused on a SUBMITTED List (lifecycle.editRefusal integration branch) but the catalogue says inbound changes are accepted until the office authorises (US-02.5.4)
  - A change moving to another day is applied as one reassignBooking (S13 cross-List) rather than a cancellation plus a new Booking (US-02.5.2)
  - Bookings with no NHI are created on a provisional patient ('NHI pending') instead of waiting at intake (US-02.1.4)
  - Hospital intake is labelled Future scope while the catalogue makes the manual download and match the priority pathway
  - No stub concept for a booking that cannot be posted (FT-02.6, open)
- **Demo trigger:** Admin matching screen needs a demo-bar button 'Receive tomorrow's hospital list' (see FT-02.1) so the day-before hospital list arrives as rows to review rather than being applied.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/integrationActions.ts:131-233 (applyEffect auto-applies S12 to S15; comments at :149 and :169 say interim until the Phase 33 matching screen)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/integrationActions.ts:190-205 (S13 cross-List reassignBooking then editBooking)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:56-63 (integration actor refused unless List ACTIVE)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/intake.ts:97-118 (provisional patient when no NHI)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/format.ts:207-212 (BOOKING_SOURCE_LABELS)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/HistoryTimeline.tsx:132-136 (who, role, source)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/IntegrationMonitorScreen.tsx:521-522 (Future scope wording)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:262`
