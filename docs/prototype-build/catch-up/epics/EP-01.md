# EP-01 · Schedule canvas, Slots and Lists · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-01) · catalogue `fbdd555` · [EP-01](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-01.md)

> EP-01's three in-scope items split cleanly. US-01.1.1 is correctly Matches: the Slot canvas (AM and PM per active anaesthetist, free default, leave then series then recurring, clash to Draft List, one List per Slot, 4-month roll-forward) is implemented and visible in the Admin Day grid and both anaesthetist list screens. The two gaps, US-01.1.5 (split a session into A and B Lists, Proposed) and US-01.1.6 (out-of-hours List, Confirmed), are both new from the 5 November meeting and both break the prototype's core assumptions that Session is 'AM' \| 'PM', that a day has exactly two Slots, and that a Slot holds at most one List (AssignedList.slotId, SlotView.list, listInSlot, slotOccupied guards, Admin ruler 07:00 to 18:00, List times derived from the Slot via timesForList). Build order: do US-01.1.6 first (Confirmed). It is likely smaller as a standalone after-PM List kind with its own start time than as a widened Session union, but it needs a design decision first (third Slot kind or a List outside Slots; who creates it; finder treatment). US-01.1.5 is only Proposed and should wait for agreement with Donald and Greg or be built behind a clearly marked option. It changes the Slot to List relation to 0..2 and the domain-model ERD line must change with it. Dependencies: US-01.1.4 (default and per-half times), US-01.4.2 (availability finder treatment of evening and splits), US-01.3.1 (pairing rule: one surgeon and hospital per List), US-01.3.2 (recurring bookings stay unsplit), FT-01.6 (can a Draft List be one half), US-13.6.6 (surgeon specialties narrow Contracts per List), and the billing and submit flows (Bookings billed as on any other List). A PERSIST_VERSION bump and a seed example are needed for either. Demo script S1 to S5 beats are unaffected if the features are additive, but S1 and S2 read the Admin Day grid and the anaesthetist's day, so their screenshots and data-shot hooks (DayGrid, SlotDrawer) must be rechecked; both features need new presenter-guide beats.

## US-01.4.6 · The List's anaesthetist did its procedures · Contradicts

[US-01.4.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.4.6.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-01.4.6) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: store, domain, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Whoever submits a List did all its procedures; a Booking done by another anaesthetist is moved to that anaesthetist's List (even a single-Booking List). Unprepaid: payable follows the Booking to the doer. Prepaid: payable is the AA Holding account and does not move, the doer is paid from it at settlement; nothing is recalculated or re-triggered on the move.
- **Prototype today:** submitList is owner-only and needs every non-cancelled Booking completed; anaesthetists cannot edit another's List. The office can move one Booking to any non-AUTHORISED List (MoveBookingFlow to reassignBooking), which touches no prepayment or invoice. The Xero handoff derives the ACCPAY payee from the Booking's List's anaesthetist at handoff time, so an unprepaid Booking moved before handoff is paid to the new anaesthetist. A prepayment invoice also hands off at once with a payable to the List's anaesthetist (raisePreProcedureInvoice then handoffCasesForBooking); there is no AA Holding creditor and no settlement transfer, and an already-created accPay keeps its anaesthetistId after a move (code comments mark a 'Phase 41 seam').
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No AA Holding payable: a prepaid Booking's ACCPAY is created for the anaesthetist on the List at handoff and never repointed, so after a move A stays the payee and B, who did the work, is not paid from it (contradicts AC 3 and AC 4). The prepayment itself is still the old deposit/self-funded model rather than the Contract-flagged full-amount prepayment of EP-06.
  - An already-handed-off unprepaid ACCPAY is not repointed when the Booking later moves (payee fixed at handoff).
  - An anaesthetist cannot move a Booking to a colleague as a new List: reassignBooking refuses anaesthetists unless both Lists are their own (notOwnList), the older reading. Only the office path produces 'Booking ends up on B's List' (see US-01.4.7).
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Admin Xero simulator and billing case views show the payable. Proposal: on the Booking move result and the Xero ACCPAY row, show the payable's payee (AA Holding for a prepaid Booking, the List's anaesthetist for an unprepaid one) so a move visibly leaves the prepaid payee unchanged.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:574-630 (reassignBooking; anaesthetist own-Lists-only guard at :599-604)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:221 (submitList owner-only) and :50 (editRefusal)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/MoveBookingFlow.tsx:28-60 (any List that day, same day)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:194-198,265-278 (payee = List's anaesthetist at handoff; accPay.anaesthetistId fixed)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/prepaymentActions.ts:46,170 (handoffCasesForBooking after prepayment)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/slotActions.ts:458-461 and aa-prototype/src/store/listMoveActions.ts:141-142 ('PHASE 41 SEAM' payee repoint not built)`, `searched: holding\|AA Holding in aa-prototype/src non-test -> none relevant`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Contradicts. Contradicts upheld (the prepaid payee is implemented, and wrongly). Dropped the reviewer's point that the office Booking move may drop into an existing List: US-01.4.6 only says the Booking ends up on the doer's List, and the no-existing-List rule is stated for the anaesthetist's own move in US-01.4.7. Added that an already-handed-off ACCPAY is not repointed. Narrowed surfaces (mobile, web, pwa removed: the work is the payable model and the admin billing and Xero views) and added a demo trigger proposal. Depends on EP-06 (US-06.5.1, US-06.5.4).
