Demo-guide beats at risk: S3 billing beats (split-billing time-units-only additional procedure, two-funder Booking), Health NZ agreed-rate beat, S5 compliance tour (ASA edit seeding modifier units), 'demo-plausible modifier values' discovery point. ## FT-05.2 · Unit and fee calculation · Contradicts

[FT-05.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-05.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-05.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, mobile, web, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Procedure holds its own price/rate/discount values filled from Contract line and RVG code; a held price is the price (BTM kept for reference), else (B+T+M) x rate x (1-discount) with discount as its own invoice line; Contract version in force on the procedure date (prepaid keeps its issued prepayment price); price 0 gives a no-charge invoice; submission rejected if no price can be worked out.
- **Prototype today:** feeFor computes units x (anaesthetist unitValue \| Type 2 agreed rate \| rounded discounted rate) or looks up a Type 3 ContractPrice at pricing time, then a typed priceOverride. Discount is folded into a rounded per-unit rate. Zero-total invoice groups are skipped. Prepaid: fee is recomputed and the deposit netted off as a deduction line.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Price, rate and discount are not values held on the Procedure; rate/discount are derived from the Contract at calculation time and the fixed price is a lookup (no 'fill from Contract line, last change stands' model)
  - Discount is folded into the unit rate (round(unitValue x (1-pct))) and is either/or with a Contract rate (ContractType2Detail union); no separate discount invoice line, not held as a fraction
  - A price of $0 / 100% discount gives no invoice: invoiceBuild skips zero-subtotal groups (opposite of 'no-charge invoice'); fixedFee UI needs >0
  - No Contract version in force on the procedure date (single Contract with effectiveFrom/To; selectContract by List date); no refresh from revised Contract line and no 'original values plus warning' path
  - Prepaid Procedure does not keep its issued prepayment price: fee is recalculated and the deposit netted off
  - Type 3 with no matching row silently falls back to BTM; submission is not rejected when no price can be worked out beyond 'RVG code or a billing line'
  - Mandatory override reason is still enforced (the reshaped model has none)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/fee.ts:180-274`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:420-428 (zero subtotal continue), 365-387 (override and deposit lines)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/validateBookingForBilling.ts:129,235-243`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:384-413 (ContractType2Detail either/or)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contracts.ts:34 selectContract`
