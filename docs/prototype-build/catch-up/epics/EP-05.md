# EP-05 · RVG master data and fee calculation rules · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-05) · catalogue `60e2d1e` · [EP-05](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-05.md)

> Prototype still implements the original July RFP pricing model for EP-05. Verification found the reviewer's grades largely sound; one regrade (US-05.2.5 Partial to Contradicts: additional-procedure and no-match fallbacks to BTM, and non-RVG lines added on top of a fixed price) and small evidence corrections (FT-05.2 validator point, US-05.4.3 visibility). Gaps cluster in three structural changes. (1) Missing procedure master and RVG hierarchy: no PROCEDURE entity, body section, subgroup, system code or general procedure per group; RVG and modifier masters are read-only demo data with invented codes (not NZSA H/S codes); hip and shoulder codes wrongly absorb P1 while real Neuro/Spine codes are absent. Needs new domain types, seed (illustrative ~400-row list), audited admin CRUD, picker rework (FT-04.3), and base-unit precedence contract line > procedure > group (touches US-04.2.x). (2) Pricing precedence rewrite: single priceOverride slot must split into anaesthetist-typed price vs office override, gated to No contract and first-party Contracts; Type 2 either/or basis must become independent fixed rate and fixed discount (US-04.2.2) shown locked to the anaesthetist; $0 / 100% must yield a no-charge invoice (invoiceBuild skips $0 groups, UI blocks fixed fee 0); Type 3 must always price from its own Contract line, including additional procedures. (3) Multi-procedure rule: replace 'additional = time only' with modifier split (<=4 on primary, else equal split, remainder to primary); needs a Booking-level pass in feeContext; OQ-90 may retire it. Modifier master also needs ASA pre-fill removed (fee.ts:79-85, AsaCard), age modifiers auto-applied from DOB (OQ-95), and non-numeric unit values. Items that already match: unit value per anaesthetist (US-05.2.1), Type 2 fixed rate (US-05.2.6), GST exclusive (US-05.2.7), per-procedure attribution (US-05.3.5), office override (US-05.4.2), and the tiered time maths (US-05.2.2, apart from hard-coded tiers and the stale 'assumption' caption). Dependencies: EP-03 (US-03.3.4/3.3.6/3.3.8 capture; the legacy Method 3 rate x time billing line is graded there), EP-04 (Contracts, picker, Contract lines), EP-06 (prepaid by RVG group), EP-08 (no-charge invoice, ad hoc/credit note). Demo-guide impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S3 Beat 1 pins invoice figures (AA-2026-0002 Forte $396.18; Prentice $152.38/$91.43) which change with base/modifier/seed-code/modifier-value changes; S3 Beat 4 uses the Type 2 contract; S1/S2 capture beats use the current picker and ASA pre-fill. Bump PERSIST_VERSION on any seed/shape change. Several catalogue screenshots for these stories (hip replacement P1 struck through, rate x time line, anatomical-site picker) show the current prototype, not the new rules, so they are not evidence of conformance.

## FT-05.2 · Unit and fee calculation · Contradicts

[FT-05.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-05.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-05.2) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, admin, web, mobile, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Price = first of: office override, anaesthetist typed price (No contract and first-party only), Contract fixed price, BTM x Contract rate (else anaesthetist's unit value) less Contract fixed discount or anaesthetist's own. A price of 0 gives a no-charge invoice; BTM still recorded.
- **Prototype today:** feeFor: Type 3 matched fixed price (else BTM) or BTM x (Type 2 agreed rate OR unitValue less %, rounded to cents), then ONE typed priceOverride (fixedFee / $ / %) replaces or adjusts the total. A $0 group raises no invoice.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - One priceOverride slot: no office-vs-anaesthetist distinction or precedence; office edit overwrites the anaesthetist's price
  - No gating of anaesthetist price/discount to No contract and first-party Contracts (OverrideCard shown on any Contract)
  - Type 2 is rate OR discount (either/or basis), not a Contract rate plus a separate fixed discount; discount folded into a cents-rounded unit rate, not applied to the calculated price; anaesthetist's own discount cannot stack
  - Total of $0 raises no invoice (invoiceBuild skips group netting to 0) instead of a no-charge invoice; UI also blocks a fixed fee of 0 (OverrideCard/PriceOverrideSheet require > 0)
  - Additional procedure on a fixed-price Contract falls back to time-units BTM unless the price row carries an ordinal; no-match Type 3 also falls back to BTM (price should always follow the procedure's own Contract line)
  - No resolver snapshot of which layer the price came from (only override before/after recorded)
  - Validator is not Contract-aware: demands start/handover times for any RVG code even when a fixed price resolves, and never rejects a price/discount entered where the Contract disallows it
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/fee.ts:180-267`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/fee.ts:188-196 (Type 2 either/or)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/fee.ts:212 (additional + fixed price fallback)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:423 ($0 group skipped)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/capture/OverrideCard.tsx:56`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/validateBookingForBilling.ts:130-150,232-243`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Contradicts. Verdict upheld (re-read fee.ts end to end, OverrideCard, PriceOverrideSheet, invoiceBuild, validator). Corrected one bullet: reviewer's 'missing BTM units with no fixed price not rejected' is not accurate - the validator rejects missing times on every RVG path (stricter, and not fixed-price-aware); replaced with that nuance and added the fallback on no-match Type 3 and stacking of anaesthetist discount.
