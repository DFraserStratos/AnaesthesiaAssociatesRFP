# EP-05 · RVG master data and fee calculation rules · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-05) · catalogue `fbdd555` · [EP-05](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-05.md)

> EP-05 is largely built on the OLD RFP reading: Contract Type 1/2/3, Method 3 rate x time lines, override with mandatory reason in three shapes, additional procedure = time units only, Type 3 BTM fallback. Verification regraded two items (US-05.2.6 and US-05.2.8, both Partial to Contradicts) and added concrete wrong behaviours: a $0 total raises no invoice (requirement: a no-charge invoice), OverrideCard refuses a $0 charge, a prepaid deposit is netted off a recomputed fee so a difference invoice is raised automatically, a Contract change does not clear a typed price, and price changes are never flagged at review (reviewFlags.ts has no such flag). Matches stand only for US-05.2.1 (per-anaesthetist unit value, Contract rate and discount) and US-05.2.7 (GST held ex and added at the foot). Structural dependencies: pricing kinds, price ranges, open price and 'Needs a price' depend on the EP-04 flat Contract rework (and the OQ-133 backdating answer); the ACC funding tag on US-04.2.17; the primary-procedure flag on US-03.2.1; held-price rules on prepayment (EP-06) and the AUTHORISED snapshot (US-04.3.5). The domain-model delta already lists DM-13 (RVG group), DM-14 (single typed price), DM-15 (multi-procedure) and DM-50; suggested order is DM-13, then DM-50 with DM-14, then DM-15. Admin RVG and Modifier screens are view only; RVG codes are keyed by code, so the duplicate-code type-ahead cannot be shown without a hidden system key. Pure billing code in domain/billing/fee.ts means many changes are one-place edits, with a PERSIST_VERSION bump for seed or shape changes. Demo-guide impact: S4 Beat 1 invoice figures (split Booking totals) and the bariatric Type 3 Booking change if the modifier split, 'Needs a price' or optional times alter totals; S4 Beat 4 contract effective-dating conflicts with the undated-rate rule; S1/S2 override narration ('mandatory reason') changes. Date-driven behaviours (backdated price range, held prices) need demo buttons. No OutOfScope items.

## FT-05.2 · Unit and fee calculation · Contradicts

[FT-05.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-05.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-05.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** The engine prices each Procedure by its Contract's pricing kind: fixed price (range in force on the procedure date), open price (typed amount), or RVG units x the anaesthetist's or Contract's rate less a Contract discount shown as its own invoice line. A typed price, a prepaid amount and AUTHORISED hold the price; a price of 0 gives a no-charge invoice.
- **Prototype today:** Pure `feeFor` with Contract Type 1/2/3: units x anaesthetist unitValue; Type 2 agreed unit rate or percent discount folded into a derived unit rate; Type 3 undated ContractPrice row lookup that FALLS BACK to B+T+M when no row matches. Fee is recomputed live from the current Contract (no hold). No open price, no price ranges, no 'Needs a price'. Typed price (fixedFee/$/%) applied on top. A group netting to $0 raises no invoice at all.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: Type 3 with no matching row falls back to BTM units instead of 'Needs a price'
  - WRONG: discount folded into a derived unit rate, not shown as its own invoice line
  - WRONG: RVG code, start and handover times required for billing whatever the Contract (a fixed price should not ask)
  - WRONG: a $0 price/total raises NO invoice (invoiceBuild skips zero-subtotal groups; OverrideCard also rejects a $0 charge) instead of a no-charge invoice
  - Missing: open price kind (No contract · Fixed price amount typed on the Procedure)
  - Missing: dated price ranges in force on the procedure date (OQ-133 backdating open)
  - Missing: price held by a typed price, a prepayment invoice or AUTHORISED (fee is always live; prepaid balance is re-netted against a recomputed fee)
  - Missing: explicit pricing kind on the Contract (Type 1/2/3 only; EP-04 flat Contract)
- **Demo trigger:** Admin > Master data > Contracts (fixed-price Contract): 'Add backdated price range' button adding a $550 range from 1 Dec, then show an unedited Procedure on 10 Dec re-pricing while a typed/prepaid/AUTHORISED one holds.
