# EP-05 · RVG master data and fee calculation rules · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-05) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-05](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-05.md)

> Verified against code. Main structural findings. (1) Master data: the RVG and modifier masters are demo, view-only and keyed by code; the catalogue wants NZSA-published data, a hidden system key (codes repeat), base-unit range, modifier units, AA-added groups, a 30-code modifier table plus AA's own with non-single values, and an audit of changes. One slice (editable, audited RVG and modifier masters with a re-key of rvgBaseCode across ~33 files) closes FT-05.1, US-05.1.1, 05.1.3 and most of 05.1.5; it is the biggest EP-05 piece. (2) Modifier model contradicts the catalogue: ASA class seeds an AS modifier (AsaCard, resolveBtm), age modifiers are not applied from patient age (bands still OQ-95), absorbed modifiers are inert and struck through rather than selected at 0 and removable, and one-code-per-band exclusivity is the prototype's own rule. (3) Multi-procedure: additional Procedures take time units only; the catalogue splits modifier units equally above 4 across all Procedures and ranks prepaid primaries. (4) Price adjustment: the anaesthetist override is offered everywhere, reason mandatory (also on the office sheet, contrary to 'no reason needed'), no per-Contract allow flag, allowed on fixed prices, no percentage or rate option for the anaesthetist, $0 or 100% discount raises no invoice (zero invoice group skipped), no review flag for price adjustments. (5) Fixed price is still the Type 3 price-list with a BTM fallback, and the validator still demands times and range base units for a fixed-price Procedure with an RVG code; EP-04's Contract-line model and No contract (RVG) will force US-05.2.1, 05.2.5 and 05.4.x to be re-graded and reworked together. Matches: FT-05.5, US-05.5.1 and US-05.2.1 (the engine has no ACC path; unit value per anaesthetist). Verifier correction: US-05.2.2 moved from Matches to Partial (S) for stale 'assumption' wording in the UI, code and demo guide, plus tiers not defined as data; time-unit arithmetic itself is right. Verifier also dropped two non-gaps (range not shown in the picker row, ASA seeding under US-05.1.4) and added the validator-on-fixed-price gap and the case-sensitive picker search defect. Dependencies: EP-04 (Contract model, flags, prepaid), FT-03.7 (events for ACC pre-op), US-03.3.4/03.3.8 (ASA, age modifiers), US-08.6.x (ad hoc invoice or credit), US-13.5.2 (audit). Demo-guide beats affected: S1 capture beat (rounding discovery point, ASA seeding wording), any beat showing positioning struck through or an override with a reason, and cheat sheet section 10 on modifier values and rounding. No demo trigger is needed for any EP-05 item; all are directly operable in the UI.

## FT-05.2 · Unit and fee calculation · Contradicts

[FT-05.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-05.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-05.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, mobile, web, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Procedure holds its own price/rate/discount values filled from Contract line and RVG code; a held price is the price (BTM kept for reference), else (B+T+M) x rate x (1-discount) with discount as its own invoice line; Contract version in force on the procedure date (prepaid keeps its issued prepayment price); price 0 gives a no-charge invoice; submission rejected if no price can be worked out.
- **Prototype today:** feeFor computes units x (anaesthetist unitValue \| Type 2 agreed rate \| rounded discounted rate) or looks up a Type 3 ContractPrice at pricing time, then a typed priceOverride. Discount is folded into a rounded per-unit rate. Zero-total invoice groups are skipped. Prepaid: fee is recomputed and the deposit netted off as a deduction line.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Price, rate and discount are not values held on the Procedure; rate/discount are derived from the Contract at calculation time and the fixed price is a lookup (no 'fill from Contract line, last change stands' model)
  - Discount is folded into the unit rate (round(unitValue x (1-pct))) and is either/or with a Contract rate (ContractType2Detail union); no separate discount invoice line, not held as a fraction
  - A price of $0 / 100% discount gives no invoice: invoiceBuild skips zero-subtotal groups (opposite of 'no-charge invoice'); fixedFee UI needs >0
  - No Contract version in force on the procedure date (single Contract with effectiveFrom/To; selectContract by List date); no refresh from revised Contract line and no 'original values plus warning' path
  - Prepaid Procedure does not keep its issued prepayment price: fee is recalculated and the deposit netted off
  - Type 3 with no matching row silently falls back to BTM; submission is not rejected when no price can be worked out beyond 'RVG code or a billing line'
  - Mandatory override reason is still enforced (the reshaped model has none)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/fee.ts:180-274`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:420-428 (zero subtotal continue), 365-387 (override and deposit lines)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/validateBookingForBilling.ts:129,235-243`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:384-413 (ContractType2Detail either/or)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contracts.ts:34 selectContract`
