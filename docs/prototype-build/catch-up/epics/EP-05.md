# EP-05 · RVG master data and fee calculation rules · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-05) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-05](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-05.md)

> EP-05 is largely built (catch-up phases 19, 19a, 19b, 23, 24 cover the RVG masters, the modifier master, the price ladder and the multi-procedure rule). I re-verified FT-05.2, US-05.1.5, US-05.2.5 and US-05.3.1 against the code and the text as written, with no gaps found. One real gap: US-05.5.2, which is Contradicts rather than only Partial. The prototype still models the ACC pre-op as the superseded BillingLine stand-in. That line rolls into the Procedure's price, and a typed, prepaid or authorised price silently drops it. Its caption also hardcodes CS250/CS260/CS70 as a settled code set although OQ-12 is open. It depends wholly on the pre-op/post-op event model (FT-03.7, US-03.7.1, OQ-121; phases 38b and 39b, where 39b already lists US-05.5.2), so close it there: keep the ACC codes as preconfigured-event data, not copy, and retire BillingLine, AddBillingLineSheet and the fee.ts add-on path together. OQ-132 (base units on a mixed Booking with a fixed or open primary) is handled by the provisional option 1 in baseOn(), and a seeded mixed Booking already exists (seed/bookings.ts:717), so a different answer changes baseOn(), its tests and the UnitsCard caption only. OQ-95 age bands (answered 18 Nov as the recommendation) are implemented as written, but the Modifiers tab still carries a 'Provisional' badge, which is acceptable until Vanessa confirms. Small unspecified edges are logged in code as our reading: with a fixed primary and modifiers of 4 or fewer they are billed nowhere, and above 4 the remainder goes to the first RVG participant. The mixed-Booking fixed primary still shows the Booking's modifiers section, which is needed for US-05.3.1 but is the only place a fixed price asks for modifiers. Demo beats S3 (multi-procedure) and S5 (ASA III modifier) are unaffected. Moving the ACC pre-op to an event will change docs/demo-guide/04-presenter-cheat-sheet.md (the billing-line section) and 02-workflows-and-handoffs.md, but no S1 to S5 beat uses it.

## US-05.2.8 · A Procedure's price follows its Contract until held · Contradicts

- **Requirement:** An unedited Procedure's price is worked out from its Contract whenever needed (range in force on the procedure date, Contract rate/discount as they stand, anaesthetist's unit value). A typed price, a raised prepaid amount and AUTHORISED hold it; changing the Contract clears a typed price; no price shows 'Needs a price' and blocks complete and AUTHORISED; no automatic difference invoice.
- **Prototype today:** `feeFor`/`bookingFee` compute the fee live from the stored governing contract at each render and at invoice build; invoices are snapshotted at the billing run. No snapshot at AUTHORISED, no range dates.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: prepaid amount does not hold; deposit is netted off a recomputed fee, so a rate change creates an automatic balance/difference invoice (or a 'negativeTotal' exception)
  - WRONG: changing a Procedure's Contract does not clear its typed override
  - Missing: price ranges with procedure-date lookup (backdated range scenario, OQ-133)
  - Missing: AUTHORISED freeze of price (an AUTHORISED List still recomputes from the current Contract until invoices are built)
  - Missing: 'Needs a price' state blocking complete and AUTHORISED
- **Demo trigger:** Admin > Contracts price-range screen: 'Add $550 range from 1 Dec' demo button, then open a Booking dated 10 Dec to show it re-price while a typed-price, prepaid and AUTHORISED Procedure stay put.
