# EP-12 · Anaesthetist profile and reporting · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-12) · catalogue `3d3a18c` · [EP-12](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-12.md)

> EP-12 and FT-12.1 are Grouping; FT-12.2 is Contradicts only through its children (size S is roll-up glue, do not double count). Real gaps: (1) US-12.2.1 (Confirmed) flat list is contradicted by age chips, bucket columns, Overdue tab/route and the dashboard Receivables aging panel (OQ-59 answered, D8, Phase 38). Removing ageing also touches US-12.2.3 (dashboard panel, receivablesAgingFor) and US-08.3.5. Open ambiguity: whether 'unpaid payable' means unpaid by the payer (current: invoice total minus received) or not yet disbursed to the anaesthetist (US-10.1.2); today a row disappears when the payer pays and shows the gross invoice, not the net payable. (2) US-12.2.2 GST schedule is receipts-based and rolling; needs payout-date cash basis, a balance check against what was paid, and period-aligned windows; depends on US-10.1.2, US-05.2.7, and the AA fee treatment (OQ-60). (3) US-12.1.4 bank details wholly missing from type, seed, forms, table, audit labels and the payables run; HPI should be 'HPI CPN' and editable. Adding fields needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 13). (4) US-12.1.2: no anaesthetist profile or settings screen anywhere (mobile More tab is static); catalogue text says the anaesthetist sets it but the captions say the office; mobile peek ignores the period; label drift Bi-monthly vs Two-monthly and raw 'biMonthly' in the Master data table. Demo-guide impact: 03-demo-script.md:57,273 (/web/accounts/overdue, 'remain under Overdue') and 01-personas-and-responsibilities.md:114 ('receivables aging'); GST beat becomes payout-based. Tests to update: AccountsScreen.test.tsx and aging selector tests. The phase plan docs (phase-26, phase-38) already anticipate all four gaps. Out of this assignment but adjacent: US-12.1.1, US-12.1.3, US-12.2.3.

## US-12.1.3 · Prepaid procedures · Contradicts

[US-12.1.3](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.1.3) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, mobile, web, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each anaesthetist's profile holds their own list of prepaid RVG codes and groups; a Procedure using one of those codes must be prepaid before the anaesthetist carries it out, checked across the whole Booking.
- **Prototype today:** cardRequiresPrepayment is true when any procedure has billingRoute billableParty and patientPaymentCategory selfFundedPrepayment, chosen manually per procedure in ManualCardForm / EditProcedureSheet / EditBillingSetupSheet. The gate itself (Pre-payment required banner, raise pre-payment invoice, office override, cleared when paid) works. No Anaesthetist prepaid field, no RVG group master, no profile screen on mobile/web/admin.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Anaesthetist prepaid set (rvgCodes[], rvgGroups[]) and no RVG group master
  - Trigger is a manually chosen 'Pre-payment' payment category, not derived from RVG code/group membership in the anaesthetist's set (contradicts US-06.2.1 note: prepaid is a property of the procedure choice)
  - No tick-list UI for codes/groups on any surface (mobile/web profile or admin on behalf)
  - Seed has no prepaid sets; seeded prepay cards (SEED_PREPAID_CARD_ID, prepaymentUnpaidList) use the category flag
  - Depends on EP-06 (US-06.1.1, US-06.2.1) and RVG groups (US-05.1.3)

## US-12.2.1 · Outstanding balances list · Contradicts

[US-12.2.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.2.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.2.1) · status Confirmed · surfaces: mobile, web, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each anaesthetist sees a flat list of their own unpaid payables (~100 rows), oldest first, with no rollup or grouping: no ageing buckets, no age chips, no Overdue view; line queries go to office staff.
- **Prototype today:** Mobile Balances: header total plus one card per invoice with AgeChip (Nd, amber over 60 days) and ACC chip. Web Accounts tab named 'Overdue' (route /web/accounts/overdue): one row per invoice but amount placed in Current / 31 to 60 / 61 to 90 / 90+ columns, per-bucket footer totals, helper copy 'ordered by date raised'. Dashboard 'Receivables aging' panel with bars and '(N) accounts over 60 days, view overdue accounts' link. Rows oldest first, billing-mirror sourced, next-day visibility.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Age chips on every mobile row (AgeChip, amber >60d) must go
  - Web table ageing bucket columns and per-bucket footer totals must become one amount column and a plain total
  - 'Overdue' tab, route, and copy ('/web/accounts/overdue', 'accounts over 60 days', 'view overdue accounts', screen docstring) must become Outstanding; Overdue has no basis since invoices have no due date
  - receivablesAgingFor bucketing and the dashboard 'Receivables aging' panel contradict no-ageing (panel itself owned by US-12.2.3/US-08.3.5)
  - Row semantics: outstanding = invoice.total (gross receivable incl. GST) minus receivedAmount from the payer, so the row vanishes once the payer pays even though the payable to the anaesthetist is not yet disbursed, and the amount is the gross invoice not the payable net of AA fee; requirement says unpaid payables. Ambiguous, confirm with AA (US-10.1.2 two payment states)
  - No hint that line queries go to office staff; only 7 seeded unpaid rows, no ~100-row scale or paging narration
  - ACC chip is extra row classification (minor)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:36-46,102,140-147 (AgeChip, receivablesAgingFor total)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:28-34,41-60,114-160 (AGING_COLS, bucket tfoot, 'Overdue' tab)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/DashboardScreen.tsx:185-214 (Receivables aging panel)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:625-690 (outstanding = total - received; bucketForAgingDays; oldest-first sort)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/routes.tsx:28,57,130 (overdue sub-tab and navigate)`, `docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:57,273; docs/demo-guide/01-personas-and-responsibilities.md:114`

## US-12.2.2 · GST schedule · Contradicts

[US-12.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.2.2) · status Verify · surfaces: web, mobile, store, domain, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Per-anaesthetist GST-return schedule aligned to their GST period: sales AA made for them, GST component of each, and a check that it balances with what they were paid. Cash basis: list only payables AA actually paid them in the period; outstanding falls into a later period.
- **Prototype today:** Web Accounts 'GST activity' and mobile 'GST this month' list billing.receipts (payments received from payers) in a date window, each with gross and GST (gross*0.15/1.15), plus period totals. Reads the billing ledger not Xero. Image captions in the catalogue describe the same 'Amounts received' screens.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Basis is wrong: rows are receipts into AA (date received), not payables paid out (disbursement date); an invoice paid in by the customer but not yet disbursed already counts in the period
  - No 'sales made for them' view (invoice ex-GST amounts) and no balance check or reconciliation against what was actually paid to them (net of AA fee / disbursed amount)
  - GST derived from the gross receipt, not from the paid-out payable portion (partial payments and payouts)
  - Period is a rolling N months to today, not the anaesthetist's aligned period; mobile hardcoded to current month; web period toggle unsaved
  - Disbursement records with atISO exist (state.xero.disbursements / Disbursement type) but the report never reads them; any switch must stay on the billing mirror, not Xero (add disbursement dates to BillingCase/ledger if absent)
  - Outstanding falling into a later period is not demonstrated
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Demo Xero 'Simulate payment and payout' / 'Pay <anaesthetist> now' and Control Panel 'Run payables' / 'Record payment' move money in and out; the schedule should react on the payout date (receipt alone should not add a row). Optionally add a 'Reconciles to payouts' tick on the GST activity screen.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:709-745 (gstActivityFor over billing.receipts)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts (receipt GST = gross*0.15/1.15 via gstComponentOf)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:228-243,265-350`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:42-46,108-131`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:809-815 (Disbursement.atISO)`

## FT-12.2 · Reporting for anaesthetists · Contradicts

[FT-12.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-12.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-12.2) · status Proposed · surfaces: web, mobile, store · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Two anaesthetist reports: a flat list of what is owed to them and a GST schedule of what AA paid them in their own GST period; both read from the Billing Engine's own ledger, not Xero.
- **Prototype today:** Web Accounts (Overdue / Payments / GST activity) and mobile Balances (Outstanding / GST this month) read only billing.cases/invoices/receipts, never state.xero (ledger-source rule met). Outstanding view is ageing-bucketed and 'Overdue' named; GST view lists receipts into AA.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Outstanding report carries ageing buckets, age chips and an Overdue tab (see US-12.2.1)
  - GST report is receipts-based, rolling window, no balance check (see US-12.2.2)
  - No separate work beyond the two child stories (screen copy and demo-guide edits only)

## US-12.1.2 · GST period · Partial

[US-12.1.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.1.2) · status Proposed · surfaces: web, mobile, store, domain · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Each anaesthetist sets their own GST period (monthly, two-monthly, six-monthly) so their GST schedule aligns to it. Screenshot captions say it is set by the office on the record and the web GST view defaults to it.
- **Prototype today:** Anaesthetist.gstPeriod is edited by the office only (Add flow and EditAnaesthetistSheet, labels Monthly/Two-monthly/Six-monthly). Web GST activity initialises its Segmented from the record (labels Monthly/Bi-monthly/Six-monthly) but a change is local useState and never saved. Window is start of month (N-1) to today. Mobile GST tab is fixed to the current month and ignores gstPeriod. More tab is a static persona card with no settings.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Anaesthetist has no way to set or save their own GST period (web toggle is local state only; no profile/settings screen; MoreScreen static). Caveat: image captions say 'set by the office', so who sets it is ambiguous in the catalogue; the office-edit half is already met
  - Web toggle can show a period that differs from the saved record, with no persistence
  - Window is rolling N months to today (AccountsScreen periodWindow), not aligned to the period's boundaries
  - Mobile GST peek always 'this month', ignoring the saved period
  - Label drift: 'Bi-monthly' (web) vs 'Two-monthly' (admin); Master data table shows raw enum 'biMonthly'
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:141-152`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/EditAnaesthetistSheet.tsx:22,65`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/fieldChrome.ts:25-29`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/MasterData.tsx:180 (renders a.gstPeriod raw)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:31,228-243,265-300`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:42-46,77`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/MoreScreen.tsx:18-80`
