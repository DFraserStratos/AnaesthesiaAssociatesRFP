## US-09.1.4 · ACCPAY as buyer-created tax invoice · Contradicts

[US-09.1.4](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-09.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.1.4) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, admin, domain, store, seed, web, mobile, pwa, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** ACCPAY to the anaesthetist carries buyer-created tax invoice wording (plus extra details that treatment needs) because invoices are raised in the anaesthetist's name with AA as agent. One per billable-party invoice, same value as its receivable (OQ-42). Exact wording awaits accountant / new IRD name (OQ-29, open). Whether BCTI is the same record as the ACCPAY is ambiguous.
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase builds a DRAFT ACCPAY per ACCREC, bill number '<invoice>-P', amountPayable = gross less AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE 5% (aaServiceFeeFor). Xero sim AccPayCard shows 'Illustrative AA service fee' panel (gross, -fee, net payable) and net payable/authorised/disbursed stats. No 'buyer-created' / BCTI text anywhere in src (grep empty; only 'TAX INVOICE' on the ACCREC InvoiceDocument with 'Billed by Anaesthesia Associates as agent for ...'). The catalogue's own screenshot (simulator-accpay-record.png) shows exactly this fee panel with no BCTI wording.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No BCTI wording or tax-invoice details on the ACCPAY card or any payable document view (ACCPAY has no document view; InvoiceDocument is ACCREC only)
  - WRONG: ACCPAY value is net of an invented 5% service fee; requirement/OQ-42 says the BCTI is the same value as its receivable. Fee panel, 'Net payable to anaesthetist' and fee-based authorise/disburse maths all rest on this
  - Fee ripples into pro-rata authorisation (authorised = ratio x net, not 'exactly the amount received' per US-10.2.1), seed history disbursed amounts, web Accounts screen and selectors
  - No agent/GST-agency detail on the ACCPAY (OQ-29 open, so placeholder wording at best, flagged as pending the accountant)
  - Per-billable-party BCTI (one per procedure, own invoice per billable party) only implicit via one ACCPAY per case; no statement of whether BCTI is the ACCPAY record or a separate document
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:199-232 (payable = aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total), accPays status draft)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/agencyFee.ts:1-27 (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE 0.05)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:456-516 (AccPayCard, data-testid aa-service-fee, 'Illustrative AA service fee')`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/InvoiceDocument.tsx:106,142 (TAX INVOICE + agent line, ACCREC only)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:78-130 (accPayTotal = amountPayable, pro-rata authorise)`, `files referencing serviceFee/amountPayable/totalPayable: DemoXero, xeroPairView, web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx, seed/billing.ts, seed/history.ts, types.ts, selectors.ts, payablesActions.ts, paymentActions.ts, plus tests`, `searched: buyer\|bcti\|tax invoice\|buyerCreated in src -> only 'TAX INVOICE' heading`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Contradicts. Verdict upheld; re-read handoff, agencyFee, DemoXero and the catalogue screenshot. Corrections: surfaces add pwa (mobile/shared ships to PWA); size raised M to L because removing the fee touches seed history, web Accounts, selectors, ~6 test files, PERSIST_VERSION and demo-guide figures, plus a new BCTI document/wording view. Added ripple into US-10.2.1 exact-amount authorisation.

## US-09.1.1 · Invoice pair creation and identification · Contradicts

[US-09.1.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-09.1.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.1.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: domain, store, simulator, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Per ledger pair create ACCREC to billable party and DRAFT ACCPAY to anaesthetist in one transaction, store both Xero IDs on the ledger record; each carries the engine's unique invoice number in InvoiceNumber and the internal case reference in Reference (InvoiceNumber is the remittance-match key, Reference is tracing only).
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase creates the pair in one mutate and stores accRecId/accPayId on BillingCase. Sim shows invoice number as the ACCREC title and '<no>-P' for the ACCPAY (this suffix is fine per US-08.4.3). Case reference appears only as 'Pair reference' on the ACCPAY card and in the engine-link callout, not on the ACCREC. ACCPAY amount = invoice total less AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE 5% (illustrative), with an 'Illustrative AA service fee' panel. Admin invoice rail shows ACCREC/ACCPAY as invoice numbers, not the Xero IDs.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - ACCPAY amountPayable is net of an invented 5% service fee (agencyFee.ts); domain-model and US-10.3.1/OQ-02 say AA's fee is a separate invoice and the payable matches the receivable. Shared root cause with EP-10 (US-10.2.1, US-10.3.1); fix once
  - XeroAccRec/XeroAccPay carry no invoiceNumber or reference fields; both are derived at view time in xeroPairView
  - ACCREC card shows no Reference (case reference) field and the sim does not label InvoiceNumber vs Reference or explain remittance-match vs tracing roles
  - Admin invoice rail shows invoice numbers rather than the two returned Xero IDs (catalogue image shows IDs)
  - 'Ledger record' is the BillingCase; no internal ledger pair exists yet (EP-08 dependency)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:234 (aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total))`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/agencyFee.ts:7 (AA_SERVICE_FEE_RATE 0.05)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:769-790 (no InvoiceNumber/Reference on Xero shapes)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/xeroPairView.ts:133 (billNumber `${invoiceNumber}-P`)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:483 (Pair reference on ACCPAY only)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/InvoiceDocument.tsx:319-320 (numbers not IDs)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/history.ts:36 (seed also uses aaServiceFeeFor)`

## EP-09 · Xero integration · Partial

[EP-09](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-09.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-09) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, store, domain, admin, shell, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Dedicated Xero org under the engine (not system of record) mirrors an ACCREC and draft ACCPAY per ledger pair, detects payment by webhook plus daily poll, uses hidden-ID contacts. Proposed resilience: async processing, durable queue with exponential-backoff retry, idempotency, handling of engine/Xero divergence (voided/amended in Xero, payment against a credited invoice), failure/recovery testing.
- **Prototype today:** Handoff is synchronous inside one mutate after the billing run. A single armed fault (settings.failNextHandoff) records handoffFailure on the case, shown in Billing Monitor with a manual 'Resolve & retry'. Webhook and poll are simulated. No queue, no automatic retry/backoff, no outage or alerting, no drift detection. XeroAccRec 'voided' is typed and rendered but nothing ever sets it. Idempotency is present (accRecId no-op, per-payment key).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero outage / slow-API simulation showing the user flow unblocked while events queue
  - No automatic retry with exponential backoff, retry count or next-attempt visibility (manual Resolve & retry only)
  - No alerting of persistent handoff failure beyond the Billing Monitor chip
  - No divergence handling: an invoice voided/amended directly in Xero is never detected; no payment-against-credited-invoice ordering case
  - 'voided' ACCREC status exists in types/UI but is never driven
  - Epic frames the engine ledger as system of record (US-08.3.2); prototype has no internal ledger, BillingCase + XeroAccRec/AccPay hold the money (dependency on EP-08)
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Simulate Xero outage' on the Xero sim (/demo/xero) and on /demo/control: while on, new handoffs and payment syncs go into a visible pending queue showing attempt count and backoff timer, while the user flow (authorise a List) continues unblocked. 'Restore Xero' drains the queue and pairs appear with no duplicates. Plus 'Void this invoice in Xero' on the pair detail so the next poll flags the mismatch for office resolution. Shell has no screen-contextual slot for these today, so they live on the Xero sim pair detail / control panel.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:153-286 (synchronous idempotent handoffCase)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:174-190 (single-shot fault, manual retry only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:777 (status 'voided' never set)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:647 (renders Voided, nothing produces it)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:736 (HandoffFaultCard 'Arm handoff failure')`, `searched: backoff\|outage\|queue\|nextAttempt in src/store and src/apps/demo -> only integration (HL7) retry, none for Xero`

## US-09.2.4 · Detect disbursement · Partial

[US-09.2.4](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-09.2.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.2.4) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, store, domain, admin, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** When an ACCPAY is paid in Xero's payables run, learned via webhook or daily poll, the engine records the disbursement against the ledger payable, so 'paid into AA' and 'disbursed to the anaesthetist' stay two explicit tracked states.
- **Prototype today:** Two states exist (XeroAccPay.amountAuthorised/amountDisbursed, Disbursement rows, case status disbursed, two-state chips on invoice docs). 'Run payables' (Admin Billing Monitor, Control Panel) and Xero sim 'Simulate payment and payout' call runPayables/disbursePayable, which write Disbursement rows and mark the ACCPAY paid directly (unbadged office action). Poll and webhook only handle PaymentIn on ACCREC. Nothing models Xero paying the batch and the engine learning of it.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero-side 'ACCPAY paid in payables run' event and no webhook path that records disbursement from it
  - Reconciliation poll only re-detects ACCREC PaymentIn, never missed ACCPAY payments
  - No demo trigger that marks the ACCPAY paid on the Xero side for the engine to pick up
  - Disbursement recorded by the office action itself, not 'when the engine learns of payment'; disbursed amounts are net of the invented 5% fee (see US-09.1.4)
- **Demo trigger:** Control Panel card 'Payables paid in Xero (webhook / poll)', shown on the Control Panel Xero payments section: button 'Xero pays payables run' marks authorised ACCPAYs paid on the Xero side, with 'Fire webhook' (engine records Disbursement immediately) or 'Skip webhook' (caught by the daily poll on next day advance). Admin Billing Monitor then shows Disbursed.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:1-140 (disbursePayables writes Disbursement and accPay status paid directly)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/reconciliationPoll.ts:20-40 (PaymentIn only)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:236-262 (settleInvoice calls disbursePayable as OFFICE)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-140`, `searched: webhook\|poll for accPay paid in src -> none`
