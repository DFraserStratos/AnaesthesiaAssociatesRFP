# EP-09 · Xero integration · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-09) · catalogue `d4db277` · [EP-09](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-09.md)

> Main gap is prepayment: no prepay holding account (US-06.5.1) and no second settlement pair (FT-06.4). FT-09.1, US-09.1.1 and US-09.1.3 all state the holding-account clause and all contradict; it is one body of work (US-09.1.3, L), not three. Paid prepayments currently authorise and disburse to the anaesthetist before the procedure. Cross-epic dependency on EP-06 (prepayment, settlement, refund on cancellation) and EP-08 (US-08.3.1 pair spec, US-08.4.5 GST agency). Touches handoffCase, receivePayment, payablesDue/disbursePayables (hold holding-account payables back), billingRun prepaid-balance logic (billingRun.ts:97), Xero sim and web Accounts mirror; PERSIST_VERSION bump if seed changes. US-09.2.4: depends on OQ-111, avoid building Xero payables-run detection until Greg answers. US-09.1.4 waits on OQ-29 (labelled placeholder only). Demo-guide beats affected (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): Beat 2 Xero collection and payable pair, part-then-balance payables-run beat, any prepayment beat showing payment reaching the anaesthetist. Statuses not used to scope work.

[FT-09.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-09.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-09.1) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, simulator, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** For each ledger pair the engine creates an ACCREC to the billable party and a draft ACCPAY to the anaesthetist (to the prepay holding account for a prepayment invoice) together in one transaction, linking them via returned Xero IDs. Billing state is tracked per invoice so partial runs are resumable and visible.
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase creates ACCREC + DRAFT ACCPAY in one mutate and stores accRecId/accPayId on the BillingCase. A fault creates neither and is recorded per case; the Billing monitor shows per-Booking status with Resolve & retry.   - No separate 'ledger pair' entity: pair hangs off BillingCase (acceptable for demo, not a gap)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Arm handoff failure + Resolve & retry (Billing monitor) cover partial runs. For the prepayment pair no new button; Xero sim Invoices tab should show payee 'Prepay holding account' (see US-09.1.3).

## US-09.1.3 · Prepayment invoices also paired · Contradicts

[US-09.1.3](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-09.1.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.1.3) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, simulator, admin, web, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** A prepayment invoice creates the same pair as any invoice but the ACCPAY goes to the prepay holding account. A second pair, holding account to the anaesthetist who did the List, is created when the office authorises the List after the procedure, so the trust account balances without amendment if the anaesthetist changes.
- **Prototype today:** raisePreProcedureInvoice builds Invoice{kind:'prePayment'} + case and calls handoffCasesForBooking, creating the pair via the same handoffCase with the anaesthetist as payee. On payment receivePayment authorises that ACCPAY (payableReleasedFor = min(received, payable)) and Run payables disburses it. At List authorisation the billing run bills only the remaining balance and creates no holding-to-anaesthetist pair.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - ACCPAY payee for a prepayment must be the prepay holding account (xeroHandoff.ts:194-221)
  - A paid prepayment is authorised and disbursed to the anaesthetist before the procedure (payablesDue/disbursePayables have no hold-back for prepayments)
  - No second pair (holding to anaesthetist) at List authorisation; if the anaesthetist changes, the ACCPAY payee is wrong
  - No holding-account entity or balance view (prerequisite US-06.5.1)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: raise a pre-procedure invoice on a self-funded Booking, then Payment received full on its invoice. Needed: Xero sim pair shows payee 'Prepay holding account' and Run payables does not disburse it; Authorise List then creates the second pair (holding account to anaesthetist), visible on the Xero Invoices tab.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:194`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:221`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:315-321`, `aa-prototype/src/store/prepaymentActions.ts:162-168`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:97-98`, `aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:131-146`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:36-45`, `searched: holding account\|prepay holding\|trust account in src -> none`

## US-09.3.1 · Contact identification without NHI · Contradicts

[US-09.3.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-09.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.3.1) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: store, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Identify each billing contact to Xero by a hidden internal ID in ContactNumber, rather than by name or NHI. Cache the returned ContactID so later invoices look the contact up directly. NHI never goes to Xero in any field; per OQ-30 answer, no personal information goes into Xero, only a unique linking ID. Status Confirmed.
- **Prototype today:** payerContactSpec sets contactNumber to the patient's hiddenInternalId (or the billable party's own hidden id). resolveContactInto tries the cached ContactID (billing.contactIdCache), then looks up by ContactNumber, then creates, and unarchives an archived match. NHI is never sent and there is no NHI column. But the created XeroContact has name = masters.patients[id].name (and billable party name); the Contacts tab, pair list 'Payer' column, ACCREC card 'Payer', and money-flow card all show that name. Seeded contacts also carry patient names. DemoXero warns the NHI policy is an unresolved Appendix 1 vs 2 contradiction.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Patient (and individual billable party) real names are written to the Xero contact (store/xeroHandoff.ts:57-68) and displayed as Xero data in the sim (Contacts Name column, ACCREC Payer, money-flow). Contradicts 'rather than by name' and 'No PII in Xero'. The contact should carry only the hidden ID (or a neutral label); the patient name should stay in the engine-link presenter callout only.
  - Seed history builds patient and billable-party Xero contacts with real names (domain/seed/history.ts:140-175); needs the same change plus a PERSIST_VERSION bump.
  - DemoXero warning callout 'NHI never resides in Xero (Appendix 2 vs Appendix 1) ... unresolved contradiction needing an AA ruling' (DemoXero.tsx:56-62) is stale: OQ-30 is Answered and the item is Confirmed.
  - Contacts-tab help text (DemoXero.tsx:~101) says only 'No NHI column exists'; should say no personal information is held in Xero.
  - Organisation contacts (hospitals, insurers, surgeon groups) and the anaesthetist payee name are not patient PII and can stay.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:57-68 (contactNumber = hidden id; name = patient name)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:84-133 (cache then ContactNumber lookup then create)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:758-766 (XeroContact.name)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:56-62 (stale callout), :101 (help text), :117,189,373,407 (name display)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/history.ts:140-175 (seeded contacts named)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/xeroPairView.ts:59-69,103-140 (contactView passes name through; patientName kept under engine)`, `requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-30.md answer: 'No PII in Xero'`

## EP-09 · Xero integration · Partial

[EP-09](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-09.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-09) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: simulator, store, domain, admin, shell, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Dedicated Xero org under the engine (not system of record) mirrors an ACCREC and draft ACCPAY per ledger pair, detects payment by webhook plus daily poll, uses hidden-ID contacts. Proposed resilience: async processing, durable queue with exponential-backoff retry, idempotency, handling of engine/Xero divergence (voided/amended in Xero, payment against a credited invoice), failure/recovery testing.
- **Prototype today:** Handoff is synchronous inside one mutate after the billing run. A single armed fault (settings.failNextHandoff) records handoffFailure on the case, shown in Billing Monitor with a manual 'Resolve & retry'. Webhook and poll are simulated. No queue, no automatic retry/backoff, no outage or alerting, no drift detection. XeroAccRec 'voided' is typed and rendered but nothing ever sets it. Idempotency is present (accRecId no-op, per-payment key).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero outage / slow-API simulation showing the user flow unblocked while events queue
  - No automatic retry with exponential backoff, retry count or next-attempt visibility (manual Resolve & retry only)
  - No alerting of persistent handoff failure beyond the Billing Monitor chip
  - No divergence handling: an invoice voided/amended directly in Xero is never detected; no payment-against-credited-invoice ordering case
  - 'voided' ACCREC status exists in types/UI but is never driven
  - Epic frames the engine ledger as system of record (US-08.3.2); prototype has no internal ledger, BillingCase + XeroAccRec/AccPay hold the money (dependency on EP-08)
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Simulate Xero outage' on the Xero sim (/demo/xero) and on /demo/control: while on, new handoffs and payment syncs go into a visible pending queue showing attempt count and backoff timer, while the user flow (authorise a List) continues unblocked. 'Restore Xero' drains the queue and pairs appear with no duplicates. Plus 'Void this invoice in Xero' on the pair detail so the next poll flags the mismatch for office resolution. Shell has no screen-contextual slot for these today, so they live on the Xero sim pair detail / control panel.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:153-286 (synchronous idempotent handoffCase)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:174-190 (single-shot fault, manual retry only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:777 (status 'voided' never set)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:647 (renders Voided, nothing produces it)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:736 (HandoffFaultCard 'Arm handoff failure')`, `searched: backoff\|outage\|queue\|nextAttempt in src/store and src/apps/demo -> only integration (HL7) retry, none for Xero`

- **Requirement:** When an ACCPAY is paid in Xero's payables run, whether the engine learns by webhook or daily poll, it records the disbursement against the ledger payable, so 'paid into AA' and 'disbursed' are two tracked states.

- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero-side ACCPAY payment event detected by webhook or the daily poll

- **Demo trigger:** Add 'ACCPAY paid in Xero' on /demo/xero/invoices/:id and Billing monitor: marks the ACCPAY paid in the Xero sim payables run, then engine detects it via webhook (or Run reconciliation poll if marked missed) and records the disbursement (paid-in/disbursed chips update). Hold until OQ-111 answered.
