## US-09.1.4 · ACCPAY as buyer-created tax invoice · Contradicts

One per procedure, same value as its receivable (OQ-42, Notes #18/#41). Final wording waits on the accountant and IRD's new name (OQ-29, still Open).
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase creates a draft ACCPAY beside each ACCREC (bill number <invoice>-P, payee = anaesthetist contact) with ...aaServiceFeeFor(invoice.total), i.e. gross less 5% = amountPayable. Agent wording exists only on the ACCREC-side InvoiceDocument.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: ACCPAY nets a 5% AA service fee (XeroAccPay.serviceFeeRate/serviceFeeAmount/amountPayable), so ACCPAY is not the same value as its receivable. Catalogue: same value (OQ-42 answer, US-09.1.4 notes); AA fees are a separate monthly fee invoice run (US-10.3.1, fee settings US-10.3.3), and the existing 5% screenshot is marked superseded there.
  - WRONG: card copy 'The RFP does not specify AA's fee rate or GST treatment' and 'Net payable to anaesthetist' line are stale against the agent model.
  - MISSING: no buyer-created tax invoice wording/heading on the ACCPAY record (simulator ACCPAY card or any admin view).
  - MISSING: no extra buyer-created tax invoice details (anaesthetist name and GST number, AA as agent/buyer, GST treatment) on the ACCPAY.
  - MISSING: no framing of one buyer-created tax invoice per procedure / per billable party (structurally one ACCPAY per ACCREC, so probably consistent, just not presented as such).
  - Wording is unresolved (OQ-29 Open), so a placeholder label is needed and the requirement is Proposed.

## US-09.3.1 · Contact identification without NHI · Contradicts

[US-09.3.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-09.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.3.1) · status Confirmed · surfaces: store, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Identify each billing contact to Xero by a hidden internal ID in ContactNumber, rather than by name or NHI. Cache the returned ContactID so later invoices look the contact up directly. NHI never goes to Xero in any field; per OQ-30 answer, no personal information goes into Xero, only a unique linking ID. Status Confirmed.
- **Prototype today:** payerContactSpec sets contactNumber to the patient's hiddenInternalId (or the billable party's own hidden id). resolveContactInto tries the cached ContactID (billing.contactIdCache), then looks up by ContactNumber, then creates, and unarchives an archived match. NHI is never sent and there is no NHI column. But the created XeroContact has name = masters.patients[id].name (and billable party name); the Contacts tab, pair list 'Payer' column, ACCREC card 'Payer', and money-flow card all show that name. Seeded contacts also carry patient names. DemoXero warns the NHI policy is an unresolved Appendix 1 vs 2 contradiction.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Patient (and individual billable party) real names are written to the Xero contact (store/xeroHandoff.ts:57-68) and displayed as Xero data in the sim (Contacts Name column, ACCREC Payer, money-flow). Contradicts 'rather than by name' and 'No PII in Xero'. The contact should carry only the hidden ID (or a neutral label); the patient name should stay in the engine-link presenter callout only.
  - Seed history builds patient and billable-party Xero contacts with real names (domain/seed/history.ts:140-175); needs the same change plus a PERSIST_VERSION bump.
  - DemoXero warning callout 'NHI never resides in Xero (Appendix 2 vs Appendix 1) ... unresolved contradiction needing an AA ruling' (DemoXero.tsx:56-62) is stale: OQ-30 is Answered and the item is Confirmed.
  - Contacts-tab help text (DemoXero.tsx:~101) says only 'No NHI column exists'; should say no personal information is held in Xero.
  - Organisation contacts (hospitals, insurers, surgeon groups) and the anaesthetist payee name are not patient PII and can stay.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:57-68 (contactNumber = hidden id; name = patient name)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:84-133 (cache then ContactNumber lookup then create)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:758-766 (XeroContact.name)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:56-62 (stale callout), :101 (help text), :117,189,373,407 (name display)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/history.ts:140-175 (seeded contacts named)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/xeroPairView.ts:59-69,103-140 (contactView passes name through; patientName kept under engine)`, `requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-30.md answer: 'No PII in Xero'`

## EP-09 · Xero integration · Partial

[EP-09](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-09.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-09) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, store, domain, admin, shell, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Dedicated Xero org under the engine (not system of record) mirrors an ACCREC and draft ACCPAY per ledger pair, detects payment by webhook plus daily poll, uses hidden-ID contacts. Proposed resilience: async processing, durable queue with exponential-backoff retry, idempotency, handling of engine/Xero divergence (voided/amended in Xero, payment against a credited invoice), failure/recovery testing.
- **Prototype today:** Handoff is synchronous inside one mutate after the billing run. A single armed fault (settings.failNextHandoff) records handoffFailure on the case, shown in Billing Monitor with a manual 'Resolve & retry'. Webhook and poll are simulated. No queue, no automatic retry/backoff, no outage or alerting, no drift detection. XeroAccRec 'voided' is typed and rendered but nothing ever sets it. Idempotency is present (accRecId no-op, per-payment key).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero outage / slow-API simulation showing the user flow unblocked while events queue
  - No automatic retry with exponential backoff, retry count or next-attempt visibility (manual Resolve & retry only)
  - No alerting of persistent handoff failure beyond the Billing Monitor chip
  - No divergence handling: an invoice voided/amended directly in Xero is never detected; no payment-against-credited-invoice ordering case
  - 'voided' ACCREC status exists in types/UI but is never driven
  - Epic frames the engine ledger as system of record (US-08.3.2); prototype has no internal ledger, BillingCase + XeroAccRec/AccPay hold the money (dependency on EP-08)
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Simulate Xero outage' on the Xero sim (/demo/xero) and on /demo/control: while on, new handoffs and payment syncs go into a visible pending queue showing attempt count and backoff timer, while the user flow (authorise a List) continues unblocked. 'Restore Xero' drains the queue and pairs appear with no duplicates. Plus 'Void this invoice in Xero' on the pair detail so the next poll flags the mismatch for office resolution. Shell has no screen-contextual slot for these today, so they live on the Xero sim pair detail / control panel.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:153-286 (synchronous idempotent handoffCase)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:174-190 (single-shot fault, manual retry only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:777 (status 'voided' never set)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:647 (renders Voided, nothing produces it)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoControlPanel.tsx:736 (HandoffFaultCard 'Arm handoff failure')`, `searched: backoff\|outage\|queue\|nextAttempt in src/store and src/apps/demo -> only integration (HL7) retry, none for Xero`

## US-09.2.4 · Detect disbursement · Partial

[US-09.2.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-09.2.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.2.4) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, store, domain, admin, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** When an ACCPAY is paid in Xero's payables run, learned via webhook or daily poll, the engine records the disbursement against the ledger payable, so 'paid into AA' and 'disbursed to the anaesthetist' stay two explicit tracked states.
- **Prototype today:** Two states exist (XeroAccPay.amountAuthorised/amountDisbursed, Disbursement rows, case status disbursed, two-state chips on invoice docs). 'Run payables' (Admin Billing Monitor, Control Panel) and Xero sim 'Simulate payment and payout' call runPayables/disbursePayable, which write Disbursement rows and mark the ACCPAY paid directly (unbadged office action). Poll and webhook only handle PaymentIn on ACCREC. Nothing models Xero paying the batch and the engine learning of it.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Xero-side 'ACCPAY paid in payables run' event and no webhook path that records disbursement from it
  - Reconciliation poll only re-detects ACCREC PaymentIn, never missed ACCPAY payments
  - No demo trigger that marks the ACCPAY paid on the Xero side for the engine to pick up
  - Disbursement recorded by the office action itself, not 'when the engine learns of payment'; disbursed amounts are net of the invented 5% fee (see US-09.1.4)
- **Demo trigger:** Control Panel card 'Payables paid in Xero (webhook / poll)', shown on the Control Panel Xero payments section: button 'Xero pays payables run' marks authorised ACCPAYs paid on the Xero side, with 'Fire webhook' (engine records Disbursement immediately) or 'Skip webhook' (caught by the daily poll on next day advance). Admin Billing Monitor then shows Disbursed.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:1-140 (disbursePayables writes Disbursement and accPay status paid directly)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/reconciliationPoll.ts:20-40 (PaymentIn only)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:236-262 (settleInvoice calls disbursePayable as OFFICE)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:121-140`, `searched: webhook\|poll for accPay paid in src -> none`

## US-09.1.1 · Invoice pair creation and identification · Partial

[US-09.1.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-09.1.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-09.1.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: simulator, domain, store, seed · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Per ledger pair, create an ACCREC to the payer and a DRAFT ACCPAY to the anaesthetist in the same transaction and store both Xero IDs on the ledger record. Each invoice carries the engine's unique invoice number in Xero InvoiceNumber and the internal case reference in Xero Reference (InvoiceNumber = remittance matching key; Reference = tracing only).
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase creates ACCREC + DRAFT ACCPAY in one mutate, stores accRecId/accPayId on the BillingCase, is idempotent, and a simulated fault creates no pair (completes or backs out together). Xero sim titles the ACCREC with the engine invoice number (derived from billing.invoices at view time) and the ACCPAY '<number>-P'. Case reference appears only in the 'engine link' callout and as 'Pair reference' on the ACCPAY card. Admin invoice rail shows ACCREC = invoice number, ACCPAY = '<number>-P'.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - XeroAccRec/XeroAccPay (domain/types.ts:769-792) have no InvoiceNumber or Reference fields; invoiceNumber and caseReference are derived in xeroPairView.ts, so the sim never shows InvoiceNumber and Reference as distinct Xero fields on each invoice
  - Case reference is not on the ACCREC card as a Xero Reference; it is a presenter callout ('not stored on the Xero contact') and a 'Pair reference' item on the ACCPAY card only
  - ACCPAY carries '<number>-P' rather than the engine's own unique number as the criterion words it (reading uncertain; a suffix may be a deliberate way to keep Xero numbers distinct)
  - DemoXero duplicate-invoice-number callout still says the setting is an open item (presenter note, minor)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Arm Xero handoff fault' (billing monitor) and 'Resolve & retry' cover the pair backout. No new button. Add explicit InvoiceNumber and Reference items to both Xero sim cards.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:153-300 (one mutate, ids stored on case, idempotent, fault path)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:769-792 (no InvoiceNumber/Reference on XeroAccRec/XeroAccPay)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/xeroPairView.ts:103-160 (invoiceNumber and caseReference derived; billNumber = `${invoiceNumber}-P`)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoXero.tsx:353,401-407,458,481 (case reference placement, ACCREC card has no Reference item)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/InvoiceDocument.tsx:297-320 (Xero handoff card shows numbers; matches catalogue image admin-invoice-xero-ids.png)`
