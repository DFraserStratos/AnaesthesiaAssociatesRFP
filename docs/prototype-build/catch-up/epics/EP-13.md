# EP-13 · Admin oversight and master data · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-13) · catalogue `c9b65ad` · [EP-13](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-13.md)

> Only US-13.1.1 and US-13.7.1 are in scope. US-13.1.1 holds: the Day grid, the Draft List band and the drill-in all exist, and OQ-139 (public Slot display) could later change only the Public text or colour. The current List-kind treatment matches the question's own recommendation. US-13.7.1 has two real gaps. (1) The child warning must go from one per Booking to one per Procedure billed to the payer, with the to-do list collapsing to one row per Booking. This touches the warning key, openWarnings, clearWarning and clearances, the triangle counts, the ProcedureStack display and the Day-block outline, and it is linked to OQ-140 and US-11.2.4. (2) There is no patient-balance warning. It needs a per-patient balance and invoice-age fact from the US-11.3.2 work, a fixed 90-day threshold until the US-13.7.4 settings page exists, strong over it, mild under it, and mild for a credit. Prepayment strength escalation (US-06.3.2) is not required here because strength is 'not yet set'. The extra splitDoesNotFit rule is outside the story's list but harmless. Demo guide beats affected: S2 Beat 3 (Ella Moana's child-payer warning and Grace Park's 'Raise sample warnings', around lines 555 to 564 of docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md) would change wording. S4 Beat 1 (prepayment) is unaffected. No scripted beat shows a balance warning, so one would be added.

## US-13.7.1 · Warning routine · Contradicts

[US-13.7.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.7.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-13.7.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, simulator, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** One standard routine raises a warning for each condition found on a Booking or its billable parties, each with its own text and several per Booking. Kinds are before-procedure and after-procedure, strengths are mild and strong, and a warning never blocks. The current warnings are: unpaid prepayment; billable patient with a balance (owing mild under the alert threshold and strong over it, days counted from the invoice date, credit balance mild); child left as a Procedure's payer (mild, shown on each Procedure billed to the payer, one to-do per Booking); base units outside the RVG range (after-procedure, office); and insured Booking with no approval number (shown to the office and the anaesthetist). An insurer Booking with no insurer Contract raises no warning.
- **Prototype today:** evaluateWarnings runs five registered rules (prepaymentUnpaid, baseUnitsOutsideGuide, childPayer, approvalNumberMissing, splitDoesNotFit) over WarningFacts and returns several warnings sorted strong first. Each carries kind, strength and text, and nothing in lifecycle or authorise reads them, so they never block. prepaymentUnpaid is before-procedure and strong. baseUnitsOutsideGuide is after-procedure and mild, one per Procedure, skipping additional Procedures. approvalNumberMissing is before-procedure and mild, and a missing insurer Contract raises nothing. The office can clear a warning, and the anaesthetist sees them in the panel. childPayer returns one finding with no procedureId, so there is one warning key per Booking, and childPayerOn tests the Booking's payer, the patient's age and whether any Procedure bills the payer. There is no balance rule: the only trace is a boolean outstandingPriorBalance flag on the Billing monitor row, and the demo-trigger text in the registry says a balance sample is a later rule.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Child-payer warning is per Booking, not per Procedure billed to the payer. childPayer.ts:20-25 returns a single finding with no procedureId and the key has no procedure part. The story (Donald, 17 November) and acceptance criterion 'Child warning per Procedure' want one warning on each such Procedure, with one dashboard to-do per Booking. This is the older reading. The change needs per-Procedure evaluation, per-Procedure keys, Procedure-level display and openWarnings collapsing to one child row per Booking. OQ-140 (AA to confirm) is open and also questions a payer per Procedure.
  - Patient-balance warning is missing. There is no rule, no WarningRuleId, no per-patient balance or invoice-age fact, no alert threshold (fixed 90 days until US-13.7.4), no mild-under or strong-over escalation counted from the invoice date, and no mild credit-balance warning. Searched balance\|owing across src with no rule found. outstandingPriorBalance is a yes or no flag that is not a warning, has no strength, and does not reach the to-do list or the anaesthetist's Booking.
  - The routine reads only the Booking's own facts (payer, funder, Procedures). It has no billable-party fact, which the balance check needs.
  - splitDoesNotFit is a registered rule that the story's list does not name. It is harmless but extra.
  - Unpaid prepayment is always strong, with no date-driven strength. The story leaves strength 'not yet set', so this is a provisional reading and not a defect.
- **Demo trigger:** Extend the existing 'Raise sample warnings' Demo action (Admin Day, Booking detail and Mobile Booking, backed by store/warningSamples.ts) to also stage patient-balance samples: an invoice dated 40 days ago (mild), one dated 120 days ago (strong), and a credit balance (mild). Also make its child-payer sample put one warning on each of two Procedures billed to the payer, with one to-do row for the Booking. 'Clear sample warnings' already reverses them.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/routine.ts:35-58 (evaluateWarnings: several per Booking, keyed by booking, rule and finding.procedureId)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/index.ts:19 (five rules, no balance rule)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/types.ts:23 (WarningRuleId union has no balance rule)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/childPayer.ts:20-25 (single finding, no procedureId)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/whoIsBilled.ts:229-239 (childPayerOn: Booking-level, anyPayerBilled)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/baseUnitsOutsideGuide.ts (per Procedure, mild, afterProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/prepaymentUnpaid.ts (strong, beforeProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/approvalNumberMissing.ts (mild, beforeProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/demoTriggers/registry.ts:1197 (raise-sample-warnings text says 'Later rules add... a paying patient with a balance')`, `searched: balance\|owing in aa-prototype/src (non-test) with no warning rule or threshold found`
