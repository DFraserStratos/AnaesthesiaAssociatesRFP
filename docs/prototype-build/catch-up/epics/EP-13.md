# EP-13 · Admin oversight and master data · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-13) · catalogue `3d3a18c` · [EP-13](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-13.md)

> Verification outcome: 26 of 27 gradings upheld; one correction (US-13.2.1 Missing to Partial, because the Billing monitor already shows a whole-ledger payables-due figure). Structural needs: new domain entities (SurgeonRoom, blacklist entries, notification, ledger view model), a Surgeon profile route, a Ledger screen, a notification pool panel, a spreadsheet loader on Master data, and a PWA sign-in screen. Phase 15a built the warning plumbing (domain/warnings routine; store openWarnings, clearWarning, warningSummaryByList) but no UI consumes it and the rule registry holds only prepaymentUnpaid. Wire it into the admin RightRail (to-do list with Clear), the DayGrid outline (replace the direct prepayment '$' flag), and Booking rows in mobile/web lists, rather than rebuilding. A 'Stage sample warnings' demo button is needed because the other three rules depend on EP-11 patient balance and child payer and US-03.3.1 base-unit range data. Two parallel flag mechanisms exist (reviewFlags.ts on the Review screen versus the warning routine); US-13.7.1 says one routine, so fold them together or keep review notes deliberately separate. Dependencies: US-13.8.2 needs US-01.4.3 (anaesthetist moves own List), FT-01.6 Draft Lists (also US-13.1.1 unassigned Drafts) and US-02.3.3 update email; US-13.6.3 needs US-13.6.2 profile, US-01.3.5 and US-01.4.5; US-13.6.1 rooms feed US-02.3.3 To address, US-11.1.5 and US-06.2.5; US-13.2.2 depends on the EP-11 patient profile; US-13.5.2 credit notes depend on US-08.6.2. Open questions OQ-43, OQ-79, OQ-83 and OQ-85 leave blacklist visibility, pool expiry and actioned state, sign-in platform and single-Booking move undecided; build the simple recommendations. Demo guide impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S2 beat 1 read-the-day and beat 3 reassign change if the grid shows booking counts or unassigned Drafts; S4 beat 1 prepayment warning (Souter Fri 24 Jul, Annette Riley) gains the to-do entry and Booking-row triangle; S5 audit and 'Simulate sign-in attempts' change if a real sign-in screen lands; S3 billing monitor beats change with the sort/filter/group restyle. Note for owner: SubmitListSheet's generic submit confirmation is not warning-related and was read as compatible with the 'no confirm step' criterion in US-13.7.3.
