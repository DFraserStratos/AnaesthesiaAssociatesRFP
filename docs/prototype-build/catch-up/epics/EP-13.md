# EP-13 · Admin oversight and master data · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-13) · catalogue `d436947` · [EP-13](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-13.md)

> All six gradings upheld after re-opening the catalogue and the code. Surgeon and room master data (Phase 17) is solid: the profile, pairings, room card and the derived room-to-surgeons link match FT-13.6 and US-13.6.2. The real gaps are the 3 November changes. (1) Room contact email must become optional (US-13.6.1 notes): the type, cleanRoomFields validation and the seed's invented reception@ placeholders all assume it is mandatory, and the profile and later email To-address logic should show 'Not recorded' and leave To empty. (2) PA and practice manager contacts (US-13.6.5) are absent, and ownership is open (OQ-127), so pick the simplest reading (contacts on the surgeon, or on the room and shown on the profile) and keep it easy to move. (3) The cutover load has no visible step or rejection report (US-13.4.3); a demo load button would also expose the OQ-129 tension, because the prototype requires an HPI CPN and the seed invents fictional ones. Open questions OQ-126 (address book, street address), OQ-128 (surgeon at two rooms) and OQ-129 are not represented and not graded as gaps. US-13.7.1 sits in EP-13 but four of its five rules depend on other epics' data (US-03.3.1 base-unit range, US-11.3.2 patient balance, US-11.2.4 child payer, US-11.4.3 approval number); the rule registry and sample hooks are ready, so each rule is a small add once its data exists. The dropped insurer-will-pay warning still appears in sample-warning descriptions and the warningSamples header. Seed changes (empty room emails, contacts, load demo) are master data only and move no List, so scripted beats S1 to S5 are barely affected (S2 office pairing and S4 warnings are the ones to re-check); any reseed needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump.
