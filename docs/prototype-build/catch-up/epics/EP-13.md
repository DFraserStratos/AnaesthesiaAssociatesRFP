The prototype's Surgeon.roomIds, SurgeonRoom (email, phone, RoomContact[]), the "Surgeons' rooms" master view, RoomContactsList and room sheets, store roomContactActions and domain/roomContacts.ts (roomEmailForSurgeon) are all superseded. OTHER GAPS: (1) US-13.4.1 names a master (public holiday) calendar that the prototype lacks; only per-hospital closure calendars exist (the catalogue is slightly ambiguous because FT-01.5 describes hospital calendars only). (2) US-13.1.1 belongs with US-01.2.1/US-01.2.2/OQ-139: Public should be a Slot status with a note and no List detail, whereas the seed makes public a List kind with surgeon and hospital in recurring bookings; removing it touches recurring bookings, Assign List kinds and the day grid. US-13.5.3 sign-in is the one wholly Missing item and needs OQ-83 answers only for details; a simulated login gate plus Show sign-in demo trigger is enough. (5) US-13.4.3 depends on the same HPI-CPN-optional change and OQ-143. MATCHES: pairing stories US-13.6.3 (Agreed, re-checked against every AC), US-13.6.4 and surgeon specialties US-13.6.6 are fully built (catch-up Phases 17 and 18a). DEMO SCRIPT IMPACT (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): lines ~85, 92, 93 name /admin/masters?view=surgeonRooms and the surgeon profile and will change; the S2 beat near line 266 (adds a Public hospital status by hand) changes if Public becomes a seeded status; S2 assignment beats using pairings and tiers are unaffected; S3 money beats produce the payment audit rows; a sign-in trigger would be a new optional beat in S5 or the mobile part of S1.
