# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `d436947` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> US-07.2.2 depends on several other epics and shares most of its gaps with them: the Booking-level payer (US-11.2.2), the insurer and approval number on the Booking (US-11.4.3), the child-payer warning (US-11.2.4), the office-check flag (US-03.6.3), and the three-part Procedure display (US-03.1.9, which needs a stored source text). Build these in their own phases first; the review screen then renders them. Moving insurer and payer from Procedure to Booking changes the data model, so it needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump, seed edits, and changes to the billing run's reads of insurerId and billablePartyId. The warning engine (domain/warnings) already exists with only the prepaymentUnpaid rule, and reviewFlags.ts defers to it. Adding the two new warning rules is S to M. The review screen also has no approve-or-correct step and no link to Booking detail. It needs per-Procedure rows or an expandable Booking panel that embeds the office billing setup, plus an explicit approve-Contracts control. Demo beats S3 (Money end to end), S4 (exceptions) and S5 stage or authorise SUBMITTED Lists through the Review screen, so layout and warning changes mean updating the demo guide's Review screenshots and wording. The seed would also need an insured Booking with no approval number on a SUBMITTED List, without disturbing the pinned SEED_LIST_IDS.

## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Partial

[US-07.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** At the SUBMITTED review the admin sees each Booking's Procedures (text as received, Contract, RVG code), insurer need and insurance status, the payer on the Booking (shown and changeable), insurer and approval number, references, addresses and invoice emails, Procedures flagged for the office, and anything missed. The admin approves or corrects the Contract selections. It warns, without blocking, for a child payer and for an insured Booking with no approval number.
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen builds one row per non-cancelled Booking from the primary Procedure only (patient, NHI, primary op, +n more, route, Contract, base code, times, B/T/M, units, fee, flags, History). The warning triangle covers the single existing rule (unpaid prepayment). The action bar has only Log phone note and Authorise for billing, and Authorise is never gated. The per-Procedure OfficeBillingSetup (route, insurer, payer, Contract, reference, override, funders) lives in BookingDetailBody, reached from the Day view. The Review screen itself has no link to it.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Review table shows only the primary Procedure's Contract and RVG code, with no per-Procedure rows or expandable panel.
  - No 'text as received, then Contract, then RVG code' presentation (US-03.1.9). No source-text field exists.
  - No insurer need or insurance status, and no insurer or approval number on the Booking (US-11.4.3). The Booking type has neither, and insurerId is per-Procedure and only on the insurer or hospital routes.
  - Payer is held per Procedure (billablePartyId), not on the Booking (US-11.2.2), and is not shown on the review table. It can only be changed in Booking detail's Edit billing setup.
  - No anaesthetist 'flag for the office to check' (US-03.6.3), so nothing shows at review.
  - Addresses and invoice email addresses are not surfaced on the review.
  - No under-18 payer warning (US-11.2.4): WarningRuleId is only 'prepaymentUnpaid'.
  - No warning for an insured Booking with no approval number (US-11.4.3).
  - No explicit approve-or-correct step for the Contract selections. Authorise is the only gate, and the Review screen has no row link or drill-down to Booking detail (no onOpenBooking or navigate), so the office has to leave Review and go via the Day view to correct a Contract.
  - Review flags cover completeness, missing billing reference, ACC on the billable-party route and manual B/T/M only, with nothing for operations not completed properly beyond 'Not marked completed'.
- **Demo trigger:** The review itself needs no new trigger, because the Review screen is interactive. Extend the existing 'Raise sample warnings' action (registry.ts:924) on the Review route to stage a child-payer Booking and an insured Booking with no approval number on a SUBMITTED List.
