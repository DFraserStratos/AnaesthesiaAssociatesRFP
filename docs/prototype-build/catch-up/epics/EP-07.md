# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `efe123c` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> US-07.2.1 is the one hard contradiction. The prototype treats SUBMITTED as read-only for the anaesthetist in the store (editRefusal), shared Booking detail (canEdit), both anaesthetist apps, the submit confirm copy, the web 'Submitted to office' state and the demo-guide persona text, and the tests assert it. Reversing it touches all of these, plus a 'changed since submit' hint at Admin review. The Done filter also keeps a List until billed rather than until authorised. List moves on SUBMITTED Lists (US-07.2.3, ownMoveRefusal listSubmitted) are a separate story. US-07.1.1 is a clean match, and its only visible blemish is the stale confirm copy, which is fixed under US-07.2.1. US-07.2.2 and the snapshot part of US-07.3.1 depend on other epics. They need the flat Contract model and step rail (EP-04: US-04.3.5 snapshot, US-04.3.8 invoice destination, US-04.3.11 move-fit), a typed price on a non-open-price Contract and 'Needs a price' (EP-05, US-05.2.8), the payer on the Booking, insurer and approval number, and the under-18 warning (EP-11: US-11.2.2, 11.2.4, 11.4.3), and the flagged-for-office marker (EP-03, US-03.6.3). Rebuild the Review screen after those fields exist; the Route column and the ACC-on-patient and 'Not marked completed' flags in reviewFlags.ts are the old reading. OQ-135 (Disabled Contract flag) is open, so leave it unbuilt until answered. Demo script: S2 Beat 4 (authorise Morrison from the Review queue) and S3 Beat 1 (authorise Forte AM and St George's PM) use the current Review screen and 'Authorise for billing', so they survive, but the table columns, flags and the 'locked on submit / no return' talking points change. S1 and S2 lines saying the anaesthetist cannot edit after submit would change. The S4 contract-failure beat (retry un-end-dates the live Contract) would sit awkwardly beside a snapshot-at-lock rule.

## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Contradicts

[US-07.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Per Booking on a submitted List the admin approves or corrects each Procedure's Contract, seeing text as received, Contract and RVG code, insurer and approval number, the payer named on the Booking (changeable), references and invoice emails, Procedures the anaesthetist flagged for the office, review flags (Contract changed by the anaesthetist; typed price on a non-open-price Contract beside the computed price; Contract no longer fits after a move), 'Needs an invoice destination' and 'Needs a price', and a non-blocking warning when a child under 18 is the payer. The insurer never narrows the choice and the Contract decides who is billed.
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen lists non-cancelled Bookings in a table (Time, Patient, Route, Contract, Code, Times, B-T-M, Units, Fee, Flags). Route is Hospital/Patient/Insurer/Mixed/Not set. Contract name and RVG code are those of the first Procedure only. Flags come from reviewFlags.ts: 'Not marked completed', 'No billing reference' (hospital route), 'ACC should not bill the patient directly', and manual B/T/M override. A warning triangle shows the unpaid-prepayment warning, plus History and phone notes. The office corrects billing setup on Booking detail (OfficeBillingSetup: route, insurer, category, governing contract, reference, guardian payer, price override). Authorise is never blocked by flags.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Route-based billing model (billingRoute hospital/billableParty/insurer) conflicts with the flat Contract model where the selected Contract decides the payer and the insurer only suggests a path on the step rail
  - Review shows only the first Procedure's Contract and code, not each Procedure, and not text as received, then Contract, then RVG code
  - No insurer or insurance approval number on the Booking or in review (no approval field in domain types); no 'insured Booking with no approval number' warning (US-11.4.3)
  - Payer is a per-Procedure billablePartyId guardian override, not 'the payer named on the Booking' shown in review with default patient; no change-payer control in the review itself
  - No 'flagged for the office to check' Procedure marker (US-03.6.3) and none surfaced at review
  - Missing review flags: Contract changed by the anaesthetist; typed price on a non-open-price Contract beside the computed price; Contract no longer fits after a List move
  - No 'Needs an invoice destination' (step rail) or 'Needs a price' states
  - No under-18 payer warning (US-11.2.4); invoice email addresses and addresses are not reviewed
  - Old flags 'ACC should not bill the patient directly' and 'Not marked completed' are prototype-era readings not in the story and are implemented behaviour that differs from it
  - No Contract approve-or-correct action in the Review table itself (correction is only via Booking detail OfficeBillingSetup)
  - OQ-135 (flag for a Disabled Contract) is unresolved; it is not built and there is nothing to contradict, so it is not a gap yet
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:60-100 (rows: routeText, primary only), :244-282 (table columns, Route and Contract cells)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/reviewFlags.ts:50-110 (flags a to d)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:780-870 (BillingRoute, governingContractId, insurerId, billablePartyId, billingReference; no approval number)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx`, `searched: approvalNumber\|approval number\|insuranceApproval\|flaggedFor\|flagForOffice\|Needs an invoice\|Needs a price\|under 18\|under-18\|snapshot in src -> no hits relevant (only unrelated 'children'/'snapshot' comments)`

## US-07.3.1 · Authorise the List · Partial

[US-07.3.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.3.1) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: store, domain, admin · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** The admin marks a submitted List AUTHORISED, locking its Bookings, Procedures and the Contracts selected for them (copied into each Procedure's pricing snapshot), and passes the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit. There is no partial release, and a failing Booking is held for a manual fix while the rest invoices.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList (office only, SUBMITTED only) sets AUTHORISED, audits list.authorise and emits listAuthorised. editRefusal then locks every Booking edit for all actors. The billing run invoices the whole List at once, with per-Booking failure held as a BillingCase while the others invoice, and the Billing monitor offers Resolve and retry. The Admin Review screen has a confirm overlay, an 'Authorised · locked' banner, lock icons and an 'N invoices raised' line. There is no return to the anaesthetist and no partial release.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No pricing snapshot is copied into each Procedure at authorisation: authoriseList only flips state, and the billing run reads live Contracts and rates at run time (the Billing monitor retry even un-end-dates the live Contract and re-rates), so 'invoice stays tied to what was true at lock' is not demonstrated
  - Confirm text and review do not mention the Contract snapshot, and no snapshot is visible on an authorised Procedure
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Authorise for billing' on the Review screen already shows the lock and whole-List handoff. For the snapshot, add a read-only 'Pricing snapshot (locked at authorisation)' panel per Procedure on an authorised Booking, and a Master data Contract-edit demo showing the authorised Booking's price does not change.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:275-309 (authoriseList; state flip only)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:50-56 (AUTHORISED locked for all actors)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:55-110 (runBillingForList reads live state per Booking)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:162-175,300-352 (authorised banner, confirm overlay)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:68 (retry edits live Contract)`, `searched: pricingSnapshot\|snapshot in src -> no per-Procedure Contract snapshot`
