# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `a68f09b` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> US-07.2.2 depends on stories in other epics, so its closure is largely a consequence of them: US-03.1.9 (source text, then Contract, then RVG code), US-03.6.3 (a Procedure flagged for the office; no field or UI exists), US-11.2.2 and US-11.2.4 (a Booking-level payer and the under-18 payer warning; today the payer is a per-Procedure billablePartyId), and US-11.4.3 / US-13.7.1 (insurer and approval number on the Booking plus the insured-without-approval warning; absent from the domain model). The review table (ReviewScreen.tsx) needs restructuring to Booking rows with nested per-Procedure lines, a way to open or edit a Booking from the review (none today), and an approve/correct affordance. The warning mechanism (useListWarnings and WarningTriangle on each row) is the natural home for the child-payer and missing-approval warnings. Authorise is never gated, which matches 'warns, without blocking'. Seed data and PERSIST_VERSION would change with new Booking fields, and the S1 to S5 demo beats that include the office review would change. The seeded guardian-pays-for-a-minor Booking (Grace Park) is the natural demo case for the child-payer warning.

  - No non-blocking warning for a patient under 18 who is the payer (US-11.2.4); no age check in reviewFlags.ts or the warning rules
  - No explicit 'approve the Contract selections or correct them' step or per-Booking reviewed state; Authorise for the whole List is the only action
