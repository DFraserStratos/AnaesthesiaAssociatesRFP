# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `1f067a8` · [EP-07](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-07.md)

> EP-07 is mostly built; gaps are small and cluster in four places. (1) US-07.2.2 (Confirmed) is the largest: needs an under-18 billable-party flag with warn/block (OQ-54 recommends flag at setup and review, block authorise; plan together with US-11.2.4, Patient.dobISO and ageYears already exist), explicit per-Procedure Contract approval, insurer/address/invoice-email visibility in review, and a Review-row link to the Card and its OfficeBillingSetup. (2) US-07.3.1 needs Contract versioning/snapshot onto Procedures at AUTHORISED; there is no Contract version concept (only effectiveFrom/To, in-place edits), so plan once with US-04.3.5 and the EP-08 snapshot work, not twice. (3) US-07.2.1 needs a web 'completed, unbilled' marker on the Lists table/WeekStrip and past unbilled Lists visible by default (ListsScreen defaults From to today). (4) US-07.4.1 matches today; if OQ-31 resolves to 'unbilled to billed and processed, not vanish' it becomes an S mobile/web change (billed marker or Billed filter) and possibly a deferred-invoice-generation demo toggle since the run is synchronous inside authoriseList. Terminology: catalogue says Booking, prototype UI says Card (submit sheet, review tiles, list rows); one cross-epic rename decision. Demo script impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S2 Beat 4 (authorise Kate Morrison's Mon 20 Jul List) and S3 Beat 1 (authorise Melanie Forte AM/St George's PM) must stay non-blocking, so any child-billable-party seed must go on a separate List or scenario; the DemoControlPanel contract-snapshot compliance tour (DemoControlPanel.tsx:443) relies on invoice-level snapshot; the PWA office simulation (pwa/officeSimulation.ts) also drives authorise and must follow any authorise gating. Guard console (DemoData.tsx:245) already serves the 'edit refused' screenshots for US-07.2.1 and US-07.3.2. Feature/epic Grouping calls are safe: their bodies only restate children.

## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Partial

[US-07.2.2](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Confirmed · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List (Procedures, selected Contract per Procedure, insurance needed and insurer details, references, addresses and invoice emails, anything incomplete), approves or corrects Contract selections, and checks no patient under 18 is the billable party (block vs warn is OQ-54, Open; recommendation: flag at setup and review, block authorise).
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen table columns: Time, Patient, Route, Contract (first procedure only), Code, Times, B/T/M, Units, Fee, Flags + per-Card History. reviewFlags: not completed, pre-payment outstanding/overridden, missing billing reference, ACC billed to patient, manual B/T/M override. Office can correct via Day view > Card detail > OfficeBillingSetup (route, insurer on insurer route, payer, contract, reference, price override, funder allocation) on SUBMITTED Lists. 'Authorise for billing' is never gated by flags. Approval of Contracts is implicit in authorising.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No under-18 / child-as-billable-party flag anywhere (Patient.dobISO and ageYears exist but are only displayed on card detail); no warn or block on authorise or at setup (OQ-54, shares work with US-11.2.4)
  - No explicit approve/confirm of the Contract selected for each Procedure; Review shows only the first Procedure's Contract (procCount > 1 shows '+N more' on patient line only)
  - Review does not surface 'insurance needs adding' or insurer details, addresses or invoice email addresses (BillableParty/Insurer email and address fields exist in types but are not shown in review or OfficeBillingSetup); Route column only
  - Review rows have no link to the Card or its billing setup, so 'approve or correct' is reachable only by leaving Review for Day view
  - Flags do not cover a missing/None Contract or an unset insurer on the insurer route as review flags (only shown as 'None'/'Not set' text in card detail)
- **Demo trigger:** Propose: a Demo Data scenario 'Child billed directly' (or seed on a spare SUBMITTED List, not the S2/S3 Lists) staging a Card with a patient under 18 as billable party, so Review shows the flag and authorise warns or blocks per OQ-54.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:244-275 (columns, no links)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/reviewFlags.ts:1-100`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/card/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:36-90`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/card/CardDetailBody.tsx:314,682-692 (canEdit, office-only setup)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:277-306 (authoriseList, no flag gate)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:109-110,132-133 (email/address fields)`, `searched: under 18\|under-18\|minor\|isChild\|age < 18\|childBill in src -> none (only a comment at domain/types.ts:123 and ageYears display at shared/card/CardDetailBody.tsx:580)`

## US-07.3.1 · Authorise the List · Partial

[US-07.3.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.3.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Admin marks a submitted List AUTHORISED; this locks Bookings, Procedures and the Contract versions selected, and passes the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit; reference data (contract terms, rates, RVG values, unit value, party details) is snapshotted at lock.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList (SUBMITTED only, office only, audited) emits listAuthorised; runBillingForList processes the whole List atomically with per-card isolation and stamps billedAtISO. Confirm overlay, then 'List authorised · locked for billing' banner with invoice count; rows lock. Procedure holds only governingContractId (live reference, no version); invoice lines snapshot rate detail into descriptions at run time, and the run is synchronous so effective freeze is at authorise.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Contract versioning or snapshot of the selected Contract version onto the Procedure at AUTHORISED (Contract has only effectiveFromISO/effectiveToISO; editContract mutates in place)
  - Authorised Card fee views recompute from live master data, so a later Contract edit changes displayed fees on locked Cards though issued invoices stay fixed
  - Reference-data snapshot (rates, RVG values, unit value, party details) into the billing record is only partial (invoice line descriptions), not a stored snapshot
  - Owned jointly with US-04.3.5 (Confirmed): plan the snapshot once, there
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Demo Control Panel compliance tour (end-date the Health NZ Contract after authorising, reopen the invoice to show it is unchanged) already shows invoice-level freeze; extend it to show the locked Procedure still names the original Contract version once snapshotting exists.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:277-306`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:68-120,262`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:130,211,318-336,349-357`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:454 (governingContractId only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:225-240 (Contract, no version)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:106-140`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:243-258 (line-description snapshot)`, `searched: snapshot in src -> only invoiceBuild comments and DemoControlPanel tour text`

## US-07.2.1 · Anaesthetist loses edit access · Partial

[US-07.2.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.2.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: web · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Once SUBMITTED the anaesthetist cannot edit Bookings; the List stays visible, marked 'completed, unbilled', while the office reviews it.
- **Prototype today:** editRefusal returns listSubmitted for anaesthetist edits; CardDetailBody canEdit=false hides edit/capture controls on both apps. Mobile Forward Lists 'Done' filter (no date limit) shows SUBMITTED/AUTHORISED-unbilled Lists with 'Done · unbilled'. Web List detail shows a 'Submitted to office' pill. Demo Data guard console can attempt editCard as Dr Souter on a submitted List and shows the refusal.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Web Lists table (ListsScreen) rows show only the session StatusChip; no 'completed, unbilled' or submitted marker
  - Web Lists default From = today, so a submitted List from a past day (e.g. Monday when demo today is Tue 21 Jul) is hidden until the user moves From
  - Web WeekStrip and dashboard have no submitted/unbilled marking; web wording is 'Submitted to office', never 'unbilled'
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Demo Data guard console (editCard as Dr Souter on a submitted List) shows the refusal.
