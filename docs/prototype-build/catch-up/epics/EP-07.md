# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `c9b65ad` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> Only US-07.2.2 was in scope. Its gap comes almost entirely from the 17 November change to a payer on each Procedure (US-11.2.2, OQ-140 open, AA to confirm), together with US-11.2.4 (child warning per Procedure) and the delivery and prepayment grouping stories. The prototype built Phase 21 on one BookingPayer per Booking, with a single writer (setBookingPayer) kept deliberately contained. Moving to a per-Procedure payer touches the Procedure type, the payer actions, childPayerOn and payerContactMissing, whoIsBilled and invoice addressee resolution, the Booking and Review payer UI, the prepayment grouping per payer, the seed (a Booking with two Procedures billed to payers, each leaving a child as payer) and a PERSIST_VERSION bump from 44. Once the payer is per Procedure, the Review change is small: move the Paid by payer and its Change into each Procedure stack and emit per-Procedure warning rows. OQ-135 (Disabled Contract flag) is unresolved. The no-flag default (D81) is a one-rule addition if answered 'flag'. No new demo control is needed, since Review, the flags and the Contract-change Approve are all manually reachable. Demo guide beats that name 'the payer' per row or the optional payer-review List (S2 Beat 4: Chen Fri 17 Jul, Ella Moana, one warning per Booking) would need rewording.

## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Contradicts

[US-07.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, web, mobile, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admin reviews each submitted Booking: per Procedure the text received, Contract and RVG code, insurer and approval number, each Procedure's payer in its 'Paid by' line (patient by default, changeable to a parent or guardian, only where the Contract bills the payer), flags for a changed Contract, a typed price beside the computed one and a Contract no longer fitting the List, Needs an invoice destination and Needs a price. A mild non-blocking child-payer warning appears on each Procedure billed to the payer (two Procedures give two warnings), and the admin can still approve.
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen shows the shared ProcedureStack per Procedure with the flags (contractChanged with Approve and Correct, contractNoLongerFits, needsInvoiceDestination, needsPrice, typedPrice beside computed, notCompleted, payerContactMissing, btmOverride), the insurer and approval-number warning, the office-flagged alert and the open warnings with Clear. The payer is one BookingPayer on the Booking: Review's PayerAndInsurer shows one 'Patient pays' or 'Payer: name' per Booking with a Change link to the Admin Booking. The Billing card's 'Paid by' line (PaidByRow) shows the invoiced party or a fee split with a Split action, not an editable payer. The child-payer warning is derived once per Booking (childPayerOn takes booking.payer) and yields one warning naming the patient. A Disabled Contract raises no flag (D81, matching OQ-135 option 1). Flags never block.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - CONTRADICTS: the payer is stored on the Booking (Booking.payer, types.ts:811-812; BookingPayer type, types.ts:237-258, whose own comment says 'one payer per Booking'; setBookingPayer in store/payerActions.ts), not on each Procedure, so one Booking cannot have two Procedures billed to different payers, which the story now states
  - CONTRADICTS: the child-payer warning is derived once per Booking (childPayerOn in domain/billing/whoIsBilled.ts:229-239 takes booking.payer; rules/childPayer.ts:21-27 returns a single warning), where the acceptance criterion wants one warning per Procedure billed to the payer, so two child-payer Procedures show two warnings
  - Review shows the payer in a per-Booking PayerAndInsurer cell (ReviewScreen.tsx:363, :569-585) rather than in each Procedure's 'Paid by' line, and its Change opens the Booking, not a per-Procedure change
  - The 'Paid by' line (PaidByRow.tsx) is the invoiced party or fee split with a Split action; there is no 'change the payer to a guardian' action per Procedure, and the 'Under 18' and contact warnings sit in the Booking-level payer view rather than the Procedure's line
  - The 'change offered to the other Procedures that bill the same payer, ticked' behaviour (US-11.2.2, reached from Review's change) does not exist
  - No seed Booking has two Procedures billed to payers that each leave a child as payer (payerReviewBookings.ts has one child-payer Booking, Ella Moana)
  - payerContactMissing is evaluated per Booking (BookingDetailBody.tsx:632) and would need to follow the payer per Procedure
  - OQ-135 is open: no flag for a Disabled Contract on an open Procedure. The prototype's no-flag default is consistent with the text as written and is a one-rule addition to REVIEW_FLAG_RULES if answered 'flag'. This is not a contradiction.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:237-258 (BookingPayer, 'one payer per Booking'), :811-812 (Booking.payer)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/whoIsBilled.ts:229-239 (childPayerOn per Booking)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/childPayer.ts:21-27 (single warning)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:363, :569-585 (PayerAndInsurer per Booking; Change opens the Booking), :373 (ProcedureStack per Procedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/PaidByRow.tsx:1-80 (invoiced party or split)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/BookingDetailBody.tsx:632-633 (payerContactMissing and childPayerOn per Booking)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/reviewFlags.ts:18-20 (D81, OQ-135 no flag), :188-197 (REVIEW_FLAG_RULES)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payerActions.ts (setBookingPayer)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/appStore.ts:266 (PERSIST_VERSION 44)`

- **Requirement:** The admin marks a submitted List AUTHORISED, locking its Bookings, Procedures and the Contracts selected for them (copied into each Procedure's pricing snapshot), and passes the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit. - **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Authorise for billing' on the Review screen already shows the lock and whole-List handoff. For the snapshot, add a read-only 'Pricing snapshot (locked at authorisation)' panel per Procedure on an authorised Booking, and a Master data Contract-edit demo showing the authorised Booking's price does not change.
