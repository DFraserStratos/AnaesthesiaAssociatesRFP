# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> US-07.2.2 is the only item graded. The warnings rail (domain/warnings with one rule, prepaymentUnpaid; store/warnings.ts with an office-only stored clearance; WarningsPanel with Clear) is the natural home for both new warnings, and reviewFlags.ts already notes that an insurer-will-pay rule belongs there. Correction to the first grader: the clearance mechanism exists. The gap is that the review row's triangle is display-only, so review needs a way to open or clear the warning in place. The story depends on US-11.4.3 (insurer and approval number on the Booking, today on the Procedure, which needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump), US-11.2.2 and US-11.2.4 (Booking payer and child-payer warning), US-03.1.9 (text as received, Contract, RVG code per Procedure) and US-03.6.3 (anaesthetist's flag for office check, new mobile flow). OQ-93 is still open on whether insurance narrows the Contract list and where the approval-number warning shows (office review, anaesthetist, or both), so build the review warnings as written and leave Contract-list narrowing alone. Demo guide: seeded Bookings on S1 to S5 lists that get authorised could newly show warnings, so tune the seed or script. docs/demo-guide was not checked line by line.

## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Partial

[US-07.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** At the SUBMITTED review the admin sees per Booking each Procedure as text received, Contract, RVG code, insurer need and status, the payer (patient by default, changeable), insurer and approval number, references, addresses and invoice emails, and any anaesthetist 'flagged for office' Procedure; approves or corrects Contracts. Two non-blocking, clearable warnings: a child (under 18) as payer, and an insurer named with no insurer Contract chosen; the List can still be authorised.
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen shows one row per non-cancelled Booking: patient/NHI, primary operation (+n more), Route, the primary Procedure's governing Contract name, base RVG code, times, B/T/M, units, fee, and flags (not completed, no billing reference, ACC billed to patient, manual B/T/M override). The only registered warning rule is prepaymentUnpaid, shown as a read-only triangle beside the patient name. Authorise is never gated. Insurer, payer and Contract can be edited only on the admin Booking detail (OfficeBillingSetup / EditBillingSetupSheet), not from review rows, which have no row navigation (only History).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No warning for 'Booking names an insurer but no Procedure has an insurer's Contract' (rule not registered in WARNING_RULES; reviewFlags.ts:66-68 comment says it is a later rule).
  - No warning when a patient under 18 is the payer on a Booking for a Procedure billed to the payer (US-11.2.4); ageYears exists in shared/format.ts but no warning rule uses it.
  - No insurer or insurance approval number shown on the review, and no field for the approval number anywhere (OQ-93 update 2026-10-28: its absence is a warning). The insurer is held per Procedure (domain/types.ts:838), not on the Booking, and is read only on the insurer route.
  - Review shows only the primary Procedure's Contract and RVG code, with '+n more'; no per-Procedure 'text as received, then Contract, then RVG code' (US-03.1.9).
  - No indication of whether a Procedure needs an insurer's Contract, nor the insurance status that came with the Booking.
  - The payer on the Booking is not shown in the review table, and cannot be changed from it (AC 2); payer editing exists only on the Booking detail.
  - No display of addresses or invoice email addresses on the review.
  - No 'flagged for the office to check' marker (US-03.6.3 has no prototype field).
  - No explicit 'approve Contract selections' action or per-Booking approved state, and no inline correct-Contract route from a review row.
  - The warning triangle on review rows is display-only: the 'Clear' action lives in WarningsPanel (Booking detail, WarningsToDo), so the AC 'warning the admin can clear' would not be satisfiable from the review screen itself without wiring a clear/panel there.
- **Demo trigger:** The harness already has 'Raise/Clear sample warnings' (registry :924, Day and Booking detail), which could be extended to the Review route. Add on /admin/review: 'Insurer cover falls through', which switches a Booking that names an insurer to a patient-billed Contract so the insurer-without-Contract warning appears on its row; and 'Make payer a child', which sets a under-18 patient as their own payer. Authorise stays enabled and each warning has a Clear action.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:228-233,250-285,322-323`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/reviewFlags.ts:57-98 (comment :66-68)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/index.ts:12 (WARNING_RULES = [prepaymentUnpaidRule])`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/warnings/WarningTriangle.tsx (no Clear; Clear is in WarningsPanel, warnings.test.tsx:79-82)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/warnings.ts:5-7,169 (office clearance mechanism exists)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:42-71; aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:834-838 (insurerId on Procedure)`, `searched: approvalNumber\|insuranceApproval\|acceptanceRef\|flaggedFor\|flagForOffice\|officeCheck\|under 18\|isChild in aa-prototype/src -> none; ageYears only used for display in shared/booking/BookingDetailBody.tsx:526 and apps/web/screens/BookingDetailView.tsx:73`
