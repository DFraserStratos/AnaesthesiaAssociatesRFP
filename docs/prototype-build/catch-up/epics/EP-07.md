## US-07.2.2 · Office review of Contracts and references · Partial

[US-07.2.2](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.2) · status Confirmed · surfaces: admin, domain, seed, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** At SUBMITTED review the admin checks each Booking's Procedures, the Contract selected for each, whether an insurer's Contract is needed and the insurer details, references, addresses, invoice emails, and anything missed or wrong. The admin approves or corrects the Contract selections, and gets a mild non-blocking warning for a patient under 18 who is the billable party (US-11.2.4).
- **Prototype today:** ReviewScreen shows a row per non-cancelled card (Time, Patient, Route, Contract, Code, Times, B/T/M, Units, Fee, Flags). The Contract column is the first procedure's governing Contract only, and Route shows 'Mixed' when procedures differ. Flags are not-completed, pre-payment outstanding or overridden, no billing reference, ACC billed to patient, and manual B/T/M override. The office can open a card on a SUBMITTED List and use OfficeBillingSetup to edit route, insurer, category, guardian payer, Contract, reference, price override and funder split. Log phone note exists. 'Authorise for billing' is the only approval and is not gated by flags. Review happens after submit, as OQ-26 says.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No under-18 check anywhere: ageYears exists in shared/format.ts and is only displayed on card detail. No flag, warning or to-do when a patient under 18 is the billable party, and nothing distinguishes a hospital billable party (US-11.2.4 ACs: raised, not raised for a hospital, clearable by admin).
  - No invoice email on any entity: grep for invoiceEmail and 'invoice email' in non-test, non-seed src finds nothing. The review screen also shows no billable-party or patient address or email.
  - Insurer details are not on the review table. Insurer is held on the Procedure (procedure.insurerId, shown only inside OfficeBillingSetup), whereas OQ-55 says neither Patient nor Booking holds it and each Procedure's Contract says who pays.
  - Contract column shows only the first Procedure's Contract (ReviewScreen primaryView), so a Booking whose Procedures sit on different Contracts cannot be reviewed at a glance.
  - No explicit 'approve Contract selections' action or per-Booking approved marker. Approval is implicit in Authorise.
  - Review is framed by billing Route (hospital / billable party / insurer), while domain-model says there is no separate route step and the Contract defines the billable party. The guardian is a separate BillableParty entity (billablePartyActions), whereas US-11.2.4 says there is no separate guardian record. Both belong to EP-04/EP-11 but shape this screen.
  - No dashboard to-do or warning model for the under-18 warning to appear in or be cleared from. It is built elsewhere (US-11.2.4, US-13.7.2) and surfaced here.
- **Demo trigger:** Seed a patient under 18 who is their own billable party on a SUBMITTED List in the Review queue. The review row shows a mild 'Patient under 18 is the billable party' pill and the same warning appears in the dashboard to-do (once built), where it can be cleared. No new button needed. The row also needs a hospital-billable minor on another List to show the no-warning case.

## US-07.3.1 · Authorise the List · Partial

[US-07.3.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.3.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Admin marks a submitted List AUTHORISED; this locks Bookings, Procedures and the Contract versions selected, and passes the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit; reference data (contract terms, rates, RVG values, unit value, party details) is snapshotted at lock.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList (SUBMITTED only, office only, audited) emits listAuthorised; runBillingForList processes the whole List atomically with per-card isolation and stamps billedAtISO. Confirm overlay, then 'List authorised · locked for billing' banner with invoice count; rows lock. Procedure holds only governingContractId (live reference, no version); invoice lines snapshot rate detail into descriptions at run time, and the run is synchronous so effective freeze is at authorise.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Contract versioning or snapshot of the selected Contract version onto the Procedure at AUTHORISED (Contract has only effectiveFromISO/effectiveToISO; editContract mutates in place)
  - Authorised Card fee views recompute from live master data, so a later Contract edit changes displayed fees on locked Cards though issued invoices stay fixed
  - Reference-data snapshot (rates, RVG values, unit value, party details) into the billing record is only partial (invoice line descriptions), not a stored snapshot
  - Owned jointly with US-04.3.5 (Confirmed): plan the snapshot once, there
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Demo Control Panel compliance tour (end-date the Health NZ Contract after authorising, reopen the invoice to show it is unchanged) already shows invoice-level freeze; extend it to show the locked Procedure still names the original Contract version once snapshotting exists.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/lifecycle.ts:277-306`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:68-120,262`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:130,211,318-336,349-357`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:454 (governingContractId only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:225-240 (Contract, no version)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:106-140`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:243-258 (line-description snapshot)`, `searched: snapshot in src -> only invoiceBuild comments and DemoControlPanel tour text`

## US-07.2.1 · Anaesthetist loses edit access · Partial

[US-07.2.1](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-07.2.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-07.2.1) · status Proposed · surfaces: web · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Once SUBMITTED the anaesthetist cannot edit Bookings; the List stays visible, marked 'completed, unbilled', while the office reviews it.
- **Prototype today:** editRefusal returns listSubmitted for anaesthetist edits; CardDetailBody canEdit=false hides edit/capture controls on both apps. Mobile Forward Lists 'Done' filter (no date limit) shows SUBMITTED/AUTHORISED-unbilled Lists with 'Done · unbilled'. Web List detail shows a 'Submitted to office' pill. Demo Data guard console can attempt editCard as Dr Souter on a submitted List and shows the refusal.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Web Lists table (ListsScreen) rows show only the session StatusChip; no 'completed, unbilled' or submitted marker
  - Web Lists default From = today, so a submitted List from a past day (e.g. Monday when demo today is Tue 21 Jul) is hidden until the user moves From
  - Web WeekStrip and dashboard have no submitted/unbilled marking; web wording is 'Submitted to office', never 'unbilled'
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Demo Data guard console (editCard as Dr Souter on a submitted List) shows the refusal.
