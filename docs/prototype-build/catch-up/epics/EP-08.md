Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-08) · catalogue `d436947` · [EP-08](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-08.md)

> Both items touch invoiceBuild.ts and billingRun.ts, so they can be built together. US-08.2.2 replaces the deposit/net model (invoiceBuild.ts, selectors.prePaidByProcedure) with an omit-prepaid rule. That removes the prepaidFunderOverride and prepaidCounterpartyChanged exceptions, the 'Less pre-payment' line and the split-deposit type. It must rank Procedures across all of them (US-05.3.1) before omitting the prepaid ones. It depends on EP-06 (US-06.2.2 prepaid amount as the price, US-06.3.1 the prepayment invoice, US-06.4.1 no automatic invoice or credit, US-06.5.1 AA Holding transfer) and on US-04.2.14 (a prepaid Contract line holds the price). US-08.4.6 depends on US-02.5.7 (received descriptions kept as received), US-03.1.9, US-04.2.4 (a Contract line invoice description) and US-08.4.1. It needs new Procedure fields (invoice description, separate rooms and hospital received texts) and a Contract line field, so PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped. The seeded prepaid Booking (SEED_PREPAID_BOOKING_ID) and its tests must change with the omit rule. Demo script impact: S1 Beat 1 (prepayment warning, raise the pre-procedure invoice) and S3 money beats that show a balance invoice less a prepayment would change to show the prepaid Procedure absent; any S3 beat printing invoice lines would read differently once the invoice description replaces generic 'Anaesthesia (B + T + M units)' wording. OQ-130 is open (status 1), so edit-versus-later-default is an assumption to flag in the demo guide.

## US-08.2.2 · Prepaid Procedures left off the final invoice · Contradicts

[US-08.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-08.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-08.2.2) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** When invoices are generated for an AUTHORISED List, every prepaid Procedure is left off them (no deduction, no prepayment line). A mixed Booking's final invoice carries only the not-prepaid Procedures; an all-prepaid Booking has no final invoice; a prepaid primary does not change the additional Procedure's multi-procedure price (ranking is across all Procedures). The prepaid price is the issued prepayment invoice amount, BTM units are reference only, and no automatic difference invoice or credit is raised.
- **Prototype today:** billingRun threads prePaidByProcedure (summed from the Booking's prePayment invoice lines) into buildInvoicesForBooking. That function rates every Procedure (prepaid ones included) via feeFor, tags the fee lines, then for a prepaid Procedure pushes a negative line 'Less pre-payment deposit already invoiced'. A group that nets to $0 is dropped, so an all-prepaid Booking raises no invoice only by arithmetic. If the rated fee exceeds the prepaid amount the surplus is billed to the patient; if lower, the Booking fails with a negativeTotal exception ('an overpaid pre-payment needs a manual credit'). Split (flat deposit) prepayments bill the remaining balance. Guards prepaidFunderOverride and prepaidCounterpartyChanged exist only for the netting model. Ranking is index+1 over all Procedures, so a prepaid primary would still rank the additional correctly, but only because the prepaid primary stays in the build. No UI or demo control shows a prepaid Procedure left off.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Prepaid Procedures are priced and netted via a 'Less pre-payment deposit already invoiced' line instead of being omitted from the final invoice (patient sees a prepayment line; requirement says no prepayment is deducted from any invoice)
  - Prepaid price should be the issued prepayment invoice amount with BTM units reference only; the prototype re-rates the Procedure and bills the surplus or raises a negativeTotal exception on a shortfall, which breaks 'never invoices or credits a difference by itself'
  - Split/deposit prepayment type (flat deposit then balance) is the old model; prepayments are all-or-nothing, so partial netting should go
  - All-prepaid Booking should explicitly yield no final invoice, not depend on a $0 net
  - Mixed Booking: the final invoice should carry only the not-prepaid Procedure; currently the prepaid Procedure's fee lines plus the deduction appear
  - Prepaid primary: the additional must stay ranked as additional while the primary is omitted after ranking; needs explicit omit-after-ranking logic and a test
  - Anaesthetist payment for a prepaid Procedure should come from the AA Holding account transfer (US-06.5.1), not a patient invoice; no such path in the invoice engine
  - prepaidFunderOverride and prepaidCounterpartyChanged exceptions, and the 'Less pre-payment' wording, are obsolete
  - Seed (SEED_PREPAID_BOOKING_ID), tests and demo guide Beat 1 / S3 beats assume deposit-then-balance netting
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Run billing on an AUTHORISED List (Billing monitor). Suggest seeding one Booking with a prepaid and a not-prepaid Procedure, and one all-prepaid Booking, so the run visibly leaves the prepaid Procedure off (final invoice shows only the not-prepaid line; the all-prepaid Booking raises none).
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:52-59 (prePaidByProcedure doc: appends one visible deduction line per prepaid procedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:298-330 (prepaidFunderOverride and prepaidCounterpartyChanged exceptions)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:385-395 ('Less pre-payment deposit already invoiced', negative amount)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:400-418 (negativeTotal exception; $0 group raises no invoice)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:456-510 (buildPrePaymentInvoiceForBooking: split deposit line vs full estimated fee via feeFor with no contract)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:99-105 and :302-308 (threads prePaidByProcedure into the build)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:612-628 (prePaidByProcedure sums prePayment invoice lines)`
