# EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-08) · catalogue `1f067a8` · [EP-08](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-08.md)

> Structural: (1) Biggest change is US-08.1.1. Removing engine-side route/contract resolution means billingRoute and the hospital/insurer default-contract fallback in invoiceBuild.ts:114-180 go, and a per-Procedure locked Contract snapshot is taken at booking/authorise. This is EP-04 work (US-04.3.5), cascading into validateCardForBilling, OfficeBillingSetup, seed cards and contracts. Bump PERSIST_VERSION (13). (2) No 'internal ledger' as such: BillingCase + invoices + receipts play that role, the receivable/payable pair only exists after the Xero handoff, and the admin payables-due panel and payables run read Xero-sim ACCPAY (payablesDue reads state.xero.accPays). Code, comments and Xero-sim copy still say 'billing mirror'. Fix terminology, consider explicit ledger receivable/payable records created with the invoice (also serves US-13.2.1, 08.3.4, 08.3.5 admin position). (3) Credit-note and additional-invoice entities are new domain types (linked to Procedure and original invoice, own number/pair/Xero pair/payable); Xero sim needs credit/void plus reversed ACCPAY (XeroAccRec 'voided' exists, unused). The post-op addendum Card (cardActions.ts:270) should be replaced by the admin additional-invoice flow (US-08.6.1, 6.3 both graded Contradicts against it); 08.6.1 and 08.6.3 are one build. (4) Missing fields: Anaesthetist.gstNumber and an invoice email on Booking (US-08.4.5, 08.4.2). (5) Product-owner rule: shell has no slot for screen-contextual demo buttons; AppShell.tsx:103-131 is the extension point (matchPath on routes); a shared trigger registry modelled on demoClockShortcuts.ts in src/shared (pwaPurity forbids shell/demo imports). Note most 08.6.x 'buttons' are product admin actions, not demo triggers; genuine demo-only candidates are 'Regenerate from locked data' (US-08.4.4) and an optional parallel-run variance panel (EP-08). Automatic engine sends (US-08.4.2) should show on the existing authorise action. (6) Verification changes: FT-08.6 -> Contradicts, US-08.3.2 -> Partial (admin payables from Xero sim), US-08.3.5 -> Partial (no admin/single-place view), US-08.6.3 -> Contradicts (addendum workaround). Minor: a fully prepaid Card that nets to $0 has no BillingCase and reads 'Pending' in the monitor (US-08.5.1 edge). Catalogue tension: FT-08.1 says no engine action before AUTHORISED while US-06.3.1 has the engine raising a prepayment invoice at booking setup. Dependencies: EP-04 (contract per Procedure/lock, US-04.3.5, US-04.2.8), EP-06 (prepayment, negative balance OQ-03), EP-05 (combined procedures OQ-53), EP-09/10 (Xero direction, payment matching, payables offset after credit OQ-42), EP-11 (billable party, invoice email), EP-13 (ledger balance US-13.2.1, monitoring, audit US-13.5.2). Demo script impacts (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S3 Beat 1 (authorise, invoice counts, 'resolves the explicit payer' wording, Email invoice step) if route/fallback/email change; S3 optional funder-reallocation aside if split moves to Contract cover; S4 Beat 2 post-op addendum (lines 303-310) replaced by the admin additional-invoice flow; S4 billing-failure beat (~line 320) breaks under a locked contract (COS ACC dating-out no longer fails at run time) so a new failure trigger is needed and Resolve & retry must stop editing contracts; S5 contract effective-dating beat (391-406) stays valid and is strengthened by versioning. A credit-and-rebill beat would fit S4 or S3. Open questions OQ-23/29/05/42/45/51/53 mean fixes for US-08.2.3, 08.4.5, 08.6.2 to 08.6.4 should follow recommended readings and be flagged provisional. Epic and feature sizes (EP-08, FT-08.3, FT-08.4, FT-08.6) are rollups of the child stories, do not add them.

## EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · Contradicts

- **Requirement:** On AUTHORISED the engine reads each locked Procedure and its Contract, applies multi-procedure rules, nets prepayment, creates linked receivable/payable in its own ledger (system of record), generates and sends invoices, mirrors to Xero. Parallel-run reconciliation is a delivery constraint.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList emits listAuthorised; wireBillingRun rates every Card from live masters, falls back to hospital/insurer default Type 1 or resolves by billingRoute, nets prepayment, raises invoices per counterparty, then hands each to the Xero sim as ACCREC + draft ACCPAY. No ledger entity or ledger screen, no contract snapshot at AUTHORISED, no credit notes or additional invoices; UI copy calls the engine store a 'mirror' of Xero.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Contract not locked at authorise; engine re-resolves route/contract and reprices from current masters (US-08.1.1)
  - No distinct internal ledger receivable/payable records or ledger balance view (US-08.3.1, 3.4, 3.5)
  - Invoice not presented in the anaesthetist's name with GST number (US-08.4.5)
  - No credit-and-rebill or additional-invoice flows; post-op addendum Card used instead (FT-08.6)
  - Engine store described as a 'billing mirror' of Xero in code and Xero-sim copy (old direction)
  - Parallel-run reconciliation not represented (delivery constraint, presenter-explainable only)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Admin Review 'Authorise for billing' runs the whole engine. Parallel-run reconciliation: narrate-only; optional contextual demo button 'Parallel run variance' on Admin Billing monitor showing legacy vs new totals per anaesthetist/hospital/contract with one seeded variance.
