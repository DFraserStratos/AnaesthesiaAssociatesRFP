## US-08.3.1 · Linked receivable and payable pair · Contradicts

- **Requirement:** Every procedure invoice yields a receivable from the billable party and a matching payable to the anaesthetist, linked to each other and to the Booking/Procedures; for a prepayment invoice the payable is to the AA Holding account until the procedure is done. Neither side can be created, paid or reversed without the other; Xero posts the pair through a clearing account that should net to zero.
- **Prototype today:** handoffCase creates the ACCREC and a draft ACCPAY in one mutate (idempotent; a failed handoff leaves no pair), with the case/booking links held on the BillingCase and invoice. The ACCPAY payee is always the List's anaesthetist and amountPayable = invoice.total, with no prepayment branch, so a prepayment invoice follows the same handoff. receivePayment authorises the ACCPAY up to the amount received (payableReleasedFor) and a payables run disburses it, so prepayment money reaches the anaesthetist as soon as it is paid. The Xero simulator shows the pair and its links. There is no Holding account, no clearing account, and no reversal path.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Prepayment invoice's payable goes to the anaesthetist and is released on payment; it should be to the AA Holding account (US-06.5.1) until the procedure is done (contradiction)
  - No Holding account (a creditor in the payables sub-ledger) and no transfer to the anaesthetist on completion
  - No clearing account in the simulated Xero posting and no zero-balance 'pair out of step' indicator
  - 'Reversed together' is not representable: no reversal of either side exists (see US-08.6.2)
  - ACCPAY itself carries only accRecId and the case reference; the Booking/Procedure link is via the BillingCase and invoice lines (minor)
