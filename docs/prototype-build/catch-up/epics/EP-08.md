The prototype has only the Invoice kinds 'standard' and 'prePayment'; no credit, additional or event kind. The interim post-op addendum Booking (bookingActions.addPostOpAddendum) is meant to be replaced by US-08.6.3 (catch-up Phase 38b per the code comment). Verification result: 8 of 9 grades upheld; US-08.4.2 corrected from Partial to Contradicts because insurer invoices are hard-wired to a portal and refused for email (the dropped 'accepts direct claims' reading, replaced by delivery on the Party). US-08.2.1 stays Matches for the grouping behaviour, with the route-derived party noted as an EP-04 dependency. The only Matches item is therefore a Verify-status story with no acceptance criteria. Dependencies on other epics: EP-04 (locked Contract at AUTHORISED US-04.3.5, flat Contract billable-party model, Contract invoice description, holder reference code US-04.2.18, delivery on Party US-04.1.5/US-04.2.8, fixed-price combined Contract US-04.2.11, prepaid Contract US-04.2.14); EP-06 (prepayment invoice US-06.3.1, prepaid amount US-06.2.2, no automatic invoice or credit US-06.4.1, AA Holding account transfer US-06.5.1; this also removes split deposits); US-08.6.5 (credit note option) and US-08.3.1 (ledger pair and payable per invoice) are prerequisites for US-08.6.4 and the 'own pair' criterion of US-08.6.3; EP-03 (bill an event US-03.7.2, OQ-138 pre-op consults open); EP-11 (payer and invoice email on the Booking, US-11.2.2); EP-07 (immutable after AUTHORISED US-07.3.2); US-02.5.7 and the integration feeds (hospital description arrival for US-08.4.6). US-08.1.1, US-08.4.4 and US-08.4.1 all hinge on the same locked-Contract snapshot, so building US-04.3.5 first shrinks them. Demo script impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S3 Beat 1 (authorise and generate invoices, grouping, Brian Holt and Alan Prentice two-funder) still works.
