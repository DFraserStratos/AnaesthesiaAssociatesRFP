# EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-08) · catalogue `d4db277` · [EP-08](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-08.md)

> Structural: no internal ledger (BillingCase + simulated Xero ACCREC/ACCPAY slices stand in; pair created in the Xero handoff after the run, and an armed handoff fault leaves an invoice with no pair), no prepay holding account, no credit-note/additional-invoice/event entities, no invoice description, no anaesthetist GST number. The old prepayment model (patientPaymentCategory=selfFundedPrepayment, deposit/estimated-fee pre-invoice raised by an office button, balance run deducts it, negativeTotal exception) conflicts with FT-08.1, US-08.2.2, FT-08.3 and US-08.3.1; rework with EP-06 (prepaid Contracts, FT-06.4 settlement, FT-06.5 holding account) and bump PERSIST_VERSION (now 25). Payer/route model (Procedure.billingRoute, default-contract fallback, no authorisation snapshot) overlaps EP-04 (US-04.3.5 snapshot, No contract (RVG)) and EP-11 (billable party); it is the root of the US-08.1.1 contradiction and a soft qualifier on US-08.2.1. Credit/additional-invoice/split stories (US-08.6.2 to 6.5) depend on each other, on events (FT-03.7, OQ-63) and on payables netting (US-10.2.5): build order 08.6.3 (additional invoice + event + next-run) then 08.6.5/08.6.2 (credit, negative payable) then 08.6.4. Matches: US-08.2.1 (grouping) and US-08.5.1 (per-Booking status and roll-up). Verifier change: FT-08.1 Partial to Contradicts (exception implemented on superseded model, no admin approve step); all other verdicts upheld. Demo guide beats affected (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S3 Beat 1 (~427-445) and Beat 2 (~452-459) if pair/ledger or invoice wording change; S4 Beat 1 prepayment warning (~522) and S4 Beat 2 post-op addendum (~543-553, explicitly interim) replaced by the additional-invoice flow; S4 billing-failure beat (~561) if the expired-contract fallback is removed; payables beats (~584-589) if prepay payable goes to the holding account.

## EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · Contradicts

[EP-08](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-08.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-08) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On AUTHORISED the engine reads each locked Procedure and its Contract, applies multi-procedure rules, nets prepayment, creates linked receivable/payable in its own ledger (system of record), generates and sends invoices, mirrors to Xero. Parallel-run reconciliation is a delivery constraint.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList emits listAuthorised; wireBillingRun rates every Card from live masters, falls back to hospital/insurer default Type 1 or resolves by billingRoute, nets prepayment, raises invoices per counterparty, then hands each to the Xero sim as ACCREC + draft ACCPAY. Anaesthetist views read BillingCase/invoice/receipt records. No ledger entity or ledger screen, no contract snapshot at AUTHORISED, no credit notes or additional invoices; UI copy calls the engine store a 'mirror' of Xero.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Contract not locked at authorise; engine re-resolves route/contract and reprices from current masters (US-08.1.1)
  - No distinct internal ledger receivable/payable records or ledger balance view (US-08.3.1, 3.4, 3.5)
  - Invoice not presented in the anaesthetist's name with GST number (US-08.4.5)
  - No credit-and-rebill or additional-invoice flows; post-op addendum Card used instead (FT-08.6)
  - Engine store described as a 'billing mirror' of Xero in code and Xero-sim copy (old direction)
  - Parallel-run reconciliation not represented (delivery constraint, presenter-explainable only)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Admin Review 'Authorise for billing' runs the whole engine. Parallel-run reconciliation: narrate-only; optional contextual demo button 'Parallel run variance' on Admin Billing monitor showing legacy vs new totals per anaesthetist/hospital/contract with one seeded variance.

## FT-08.3 · Internal ledger · Contradicts

  - No per-patient ledger view; history is not independent of the Xero contact
