Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-08) · catalogue `501b0b8` · [EP-08](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-08.md)

> 14 of 15 gradings upheld; one corrected: US-08.3.2 Matches to Partial (admin payables-due and pay run read state.xero ACCPAYs, no admin balance view). Biggest cluster remains FT-08.6 (US-08.6.1 to 08.6.4): credit-in-full-then-rebill, admin free-form additional invoices and combined-procedure split are all absent. 08.6.1 and 08.6.3 are effectively one build (additional invoice); 08.6.4 sits on top and needs combination Contracts (US-04.2.11). Needs Invoice fields (kind additional/credit, parent link), a payable reversal path (negative invoice netted in runPayables, overlapping US-10.2.5 and OQ-71) and Xero sim credit-note support. Structural gap: no engine-side ledger. Invoice plus BillingCase is the record and the payable exists only as a Xero-sim ACCPAY created at handoff (authorised/disbursed truth for the pay run sits in state.xero); a derived Admin ledger view plus moving payablesDue/disbursePayables onto billing.cases would close FT-08.3 and US-08.3.2 cheaply. Cross-epic dependencies: US-06.1.1 prepaid list (FT-08.1), US-04.2.12 Contract payment setting and US-04.2.11 combination Contract (08.2.3, 08.6.4), US-04.3.5 contract snapshot at AUTHORISED (08.4.4), US-04.2.1/US-11.2.2 billable party coupling (billing route currently bundles pricing and payer; OQ-67), US-10.2.5 (08.6.2), US-09.1.4 and a new Anaesthetist GST number field (08.4.5). Terminology: Booking = Card. Shell has no screen-contextual demo-trigger slot, so contextual buttons need a new DemoContextMenu or placement on /demo/control. Demo script beats affected: S3 Beat 1 (only if grouping or invoice header changes), S4 Beat 1 (pre-payment gate narration if auto-generated held prepayment is built), S4 Beat 2 (post-op addendum joined by additional invoice; free-session-today constraint may drop), S5 Beat 4 (Regenerate check). Any seed or type change needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 13).

## EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · Contradicts

[EP-08](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-08.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-08) · status Confirmed · surfaces: domain, store, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On AUTHORISED the engine reads each locked Procedure and its Contract, applies multi-procedure rules, nets prepayment, creates linked receivable/payable in its own ledger (system of record), generates and sends invoices, mirrors to Xero. Parallel-run reconciliation is a delivery constraint.
- **Prototype today:** authoriseList emits listAuthorised; wireBillingRun rates every Card from live masters, falls back to hospital/insurer default Type 1 or resolves by billingRoute, nets prepayment, raises invoices per counterparty, then hands each to the Xero sim as ACCREC + draft ACCPAY. No ledger entity or ledger screen, no contract snapshot at AUTHORISED, no credit notes or additional invoices; UI copy calls the engine store a 'mirror' of Xero.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Contract not locked at authorise; engine re-resolves route/contract and reprices from current masters (US-08.1.1)
  - No distinct internal ledger receivable/payable records or ledger balance view (US-08.3.1, 3.4, 3.5)
  - Invoice not presented in the anaesthetist's name with GST number (US-08.4.5)
  - No credit-and-rebill or additional-invoice flows; post-op addendum Card used instead (FT-08.6)
  - Engine store described as a 'billing mirror' of Xero in code and Xero-sim copy (old direction)
  - Parallel-run reconciliation not represented (delivery constraint, presenter-explainable only)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Admin Review 'Authorise for billing' runs the whole engine. Parallel-run reconciliation: narrate-only; optional contextual demo button 'Parallel run variance' on Admin Billing monitor showing legacy vs new totals per anaesthetist/hospital/contract with one seeded variance.

[US-08.6.3](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-08.6.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-08.6.3) · status Verify · surfaces: domain, store, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admin creates a free-form additional invoice (description, quantity, amount) on a Procedure of an invoiced Booking from a button; linked both ways to the original; own number, ledger pair, Xero pair and payable; no approval; audited; original not unlocked; not a correction. OQ-72 open on discount wording and different billable party.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing equivalent. Billing lines are priced or fixed and editable only before AUTHORISED; Invoice has no parent/additional link.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No 'Create additional invoice' button on invoiced Procedures
  - No free-form invoice entry; no Invoice kind 'additional' or link to original Procedure/invoice
  - No own ledger pair/Xero pair/payable for an ad hoc invoice
  - Original invoice does not list its additional invoices
  - No audit entry for it
- **Demo trigger:** Existing control panel 'Stage post-op scenario' leaves a billed List; the 'Create additional invoice' button appears on that Procedure in Admin Card detail and on the invoice document, with its own pair visible in the Xero simulator.
- **Evidence:** `searched: additional invoice\|additionalInvoice\|next account in src -> none`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:~662 Invoice kind standard\|prePayment only`, `aa-prototype/src/store/billingLineActions.ts:46 addBillingLine (blocked once AUTHORISED via editRefusal)`

## US-08.6.4 · Split a combined Procedure into additional invoices · Missing

[US-08.6.4](../../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/US-08.6.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-08.6.4) · status Verify · surfaces: domain, store, admin, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** For a combined (cosmetic, prepaid or fixed price) Procedure, admin creates one free-form additional invoice per component, attached to the Procedure, component prices not forced to sum to the bundle. OQ-72 open on crediting the original.
- **Prototype today:** Nothing. Only a per-line funder allocation across payers exists (different concept: splitting between payers, not components). No combination Contract, no admin split-invoice action.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No combination Contract (one Contract set against each parent procedure, US-04.2.11)
  - No several-additional-invoices-per-Procedure action
  - No seeded combined procedure (abdominoplasty, breast lift, liposuction) for a Southern Cross three-way split
  - No decision on crediting the original combined invoice (OQ-72)
- **Demo trigger:** Control panel button 'Stage combined procedure' (Admin invoices screen): seeds a billed prepaid combined cosmetic Card whose invoice is then split via 'Create additional invoice' once per component, totals not forced to match.
- **Evidence:** `searched: combination\|combined\|breast lift\|liposuction in src -> none (abdominoplasty only as a single RVG code and one Aria Skin hourly card, seed/cards.ts:708)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/FunderAllocationSheet.tsx (payer split only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:216 Contract types 1\|2\|3 only`
