# EP-04 · Contracts · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-04) · catalogue `c9b65ad` · [EP-04](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md)

> EP-04 is largely built: Party master with refusals, flat Contract editor, five pricing kinds, price ranges, derived destinations, the system backstops (open-price per destination, plus RVG per hospital tag and Special holder), and the shared BillingCard step rail mirrored for the anaesthetist. The gaps are narrow and cluster in three places. 1. Anaesthetist price override (US-04.2.2 and US-04.2.14, and now US-04.1.1; also US-03.5.1 outside this epic). Contract.anaesthetistMayOverridePrice is stored and editable (editor refuses it on non-RVG kinds) but never read when the anaesthetist prices a Procedure. OverrideCard (shared/capture/BtmCaptureBlock.tsx:229) is always shown, demands a reason (OverrideCard and validateBookingForBilling), and a fixedFee override also clears 'Needs a price' (contracts.ts:494, fee.ts:321), so it can replace a fixed price or stand in for a typed amount on a hospital backstop (a further hole in US-04.3.9). One fix, sized M: gate the card by the governing Contract's flag (read-only otherwise, nothing at all on fixed or prepaid), drop the mandatory reason, reseed typed-price review beats (reviewFlagBookings typedPriceBooking, S3/S4), bump PERSIST_VERSION, and fix demo-guide line 773 ('There is no override any more'). I upgraded US-04.1.1 from Partial to Contradicts because its table and rules state the same override rule. 2. Add-booking rail variant (US-04.3.9). shared/flows/ContractRail.tsx (ManualBookingForm, ProcedureContractRow) keeps the retired 'Other price' switch and lacks the 'No contract' heading and RVG backstop that BillingCard has. Point it at the BillingCard's ContractCombo and backstop pair, or reuse BillingSteps in the sheet. Affects only a presenter who adds a booking by hand; the S1 beat uses the BillingCard and is correct. 3. Seed realism. The five hospital plain RVG Contracts carry no funding tag and so show under Special; hospital > tag RVG-kind Contracts exist only at the Contract's rate (Southern Cross SXAP, CPH HNZ, St George's ACC). No Forte SXAP Contract or rvgDiscount Contract is seeded, so the Forte backstop AC and the 'Fixed discount' AC need hand-made Contracts. Any retag must keep the S4 single-tag fill-in (St George's has ACC only). Needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump. 4. Payer on each Procedure (Donald, 17 November, OQ-140, US-11.2.2, AA to confirm). EP-04's stories (04.1.1, 04.1.5, 04.2.1, 04.2.14, 04.3.3) now say 'the Procedure's payer'; the prototype holds the payer on Booking.payer and billablePartyForProcedure reads it. I graded these stories on their own rules and left the move to the US-11.2.2 phase, since Contract-to-invoice behaviour is identical for single-payer Bookings. When that phase lands, billablePartyForProcedure's signature, Booking.payer, and the demo-guide wording (03-demo-script.md around line 636) change with it. 5. Open questions OQ-114 (delivery beyond email and post) and OQ-136 (holder references the Booking must collect) are unresolved; the prototype follows the interim readings, no work needed. Story screenshots (US-04.1.1, 04.2.1, 04.2.2) are noted as showing the retired model and need recapture after the build. 6. Scripted beats. S1 (Contract choice on the rail), S3 (invoices to holder or payer) and S4 (prepayment on Souter's list, billing failure with COS fixed fee) depend on the model as built; gaps 1, 3 and 4 are the ones that could alter them when closed. No beat is broken by current state.
