# EP-04 · Contracts · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-04) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-04](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md)

> EP-04 is largely built: the flat Contract model (Phases 18 to 25) matches nine of the eleven in-scope stories on re-verification. Two stories carry gaps, both from the 18 November decisions (OQ-133, OQ-135, taken by Donald for the build, not yet discussed with AA), which reverse the prototype's earlier readings D79 (no list of affected Procedures) and D81 (a Disabled Contract bills as normal and raises no flag). (1) US-04.1.2 Contradicts, size M: a dated-change warning that lists Procedures dated on or after the change still on the old Contract (needs a 'replaces Contract' and change-date input when creating), a matching warning when disabling a Contract, and a new review-flag rule for an un-authorised Procedure dated on or after the day its Contract was disabled (the day comes from the contract.disable audit row). The editor's own text and three demo-guide files (03-demo-script.md:568, 04-presenter-cheat-sheet.md:185, 02-workflows-and-handoffs.md:419) state the retired behaviour. (2) US-04.2.15 Partial, size S: after adding a price range, warn how many authorised Procedures kept their old price and list them. Correction to the first review: the seed already has an AUTHORISED, invoiced Face lift (Thu 16 Jul, $3,100) inside the existing 'Demo: add a backdated price range' window, so no seed change or PERSIST_VERSION bump is needed and the existing trigger demonstrates the warning. The 04.1.2 demo also needs no new seed: the unedited Mon 10 Aug Face lift Booking is un-authorised and later than the demo clock's 21 Jul. Dependencies: the new flag reuses the review-flag machinery (US-07.2.2) and the lock; US-04.2.14's prepayment lifecycle is interim (US-06.2.2, phase 27); US-04.3.2 search is scoped inside the rail path (US-04.3.8). OQ-114, OQ-122 and OQ-142 are all unresolved and the prototype is consistent with the text as written. Retired-model screenshots cited by the stories need recapture after the build, not a prototype gap.

## US-04.1.2 · Create, edit and disable Contracts · Contradicts

[US-04.1.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-04.1.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Office creates, edits and disables Contracts (Active/Disabled, hidden when disabled, never deleted); list filters by status and holder; no dates; prepaid and kind fixed. When the office makes a new Contract for a dated change, the app warns and lists Procedures dated on or after the change still on the old Contract (nothing moves); the same warning on disabling the old Contract. A Procedure keeps a disabled Contract; one not yet authorised and dated on or after the disable day is flagged at office review; earlier ones bill as normal.
- **Prototype today:** ContractsView has Active/Disabled/All and holder filters; ContractEditSheet creates, edits and disables (button toggles status); disableContract only sets status and audits contract.disable; prepaid, kind and holder fixed by contractRefusal; ContractSelect shows '(disabled)'. The editor text says 'Procedures already on it keep it and bill as normal'. contractNoLongerFits uses anyStatus so a disabled Contract never flags. Demo guide states 'A Disabled Contract raises no flag'.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Dated-change warning missing: creating a Contract takes no 'replaces which Contract' or change date, and nothing warns or lists Procedures dated on or after the change still on the old Contract (store/contractActions.ts header: D79 no list)
  - Warned on disable missing: disableContract warns and lists nothing
  - Review flag contradicted: a not-yet-authorised Procedure dated on or after the disable day raises no flag (reviewFlags.ts header: D81 'bills as normal and raises no flag'; no disabled rule in REVIEW_FLAG_RULES)
  - Date test absent: nothing reads the contract.disable audit row's day to separate flagged Procedures from earlier unflagged ones
  - Disabled-Contract editor note and demo guide text assert the retired behaviour and must change
- **Demo trigger:** No new control needed: with the demo clock at 21 Jul 2026, disabling Merivale's Face lift on Admin > Master data > Contracts (seeded unedited Face lift Booking dated Mon 10 Aug is un-authorised and later than the disable day) shows the disable warning, and the Review flag then appears on that Booking. For the dated-change warning, create a new Contract with 'replaces: Merivale Face lift, from <date>' and the same Booking is listed.
