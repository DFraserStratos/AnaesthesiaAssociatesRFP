# EP-04 · Contracts · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-04) · catalogue `7cd7802` · [EP-04](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md)

> All of EP-04 sits on the July model: Contract.type 1\|2\|3, holderType/holderId/scope, protected per-hospital and per-insurer default Type 1s, an Insurer master with acceptsDirectClaims, and a required Procedure.billingRoute (hospital/billableParty/insurer) with an optional governingContractId resolved at billing. The catalogue replaces this with one Contract per Procedure that decides pricing and who is billed. Verification changed one item (US-04.2.2 Partial to Contradicts) because OQ-89 and US-05.2.6 make the Contract rate a unit rate, not rate x time, and the ordinal/Method 3 machinery is an older reading. Only US-04.2.8 stays Partial and US-04.2.11 Missing. Suggested order: (1) domain types and seed (Contract settings, contract holder record with billable party, practitioner links and delivery flag, Contract lines with system keys, one No contract (RVG), dated versions, retire Insurer master and defaults), (2) billing engine (resolveContractForProcedure, counterpartyForProcedure, feeFor driven by Contract and line), (3) filtered Contract picker and admin Contracts master with stage-and-submit versions. Tightly coupled outside this batch: US-03.4.1 (anaesthetist changes Contract), US-04.1.2/04.1.3/04.1.4/04.1.6, EP-05 (price override, discount, GST), FT-06.2 (prepaid via the Contract flag), EP-08 (invoice description, layout, additional invoices), EP-11 (Booking payer, insurer and approval number; OQ-93, OQ-122). Persisted state needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 13). Demo guide impact: S4 beat 2 (dating out COS ACC with no default fallback), S5 beat 4 (Health NZ effective-dating, docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:643-652), prepayment beats, Doyle bariatric ordinal pricing, Aria hourly scenario and any beat showing hospital default Type 1 or Type labels. contract-picker-mock/ is a separate throwaway and was not graded.

## EP-04 · Contracts · Contradicts

[EP-04](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=EP-04) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, seed, admin, web, mobile, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Prototype today:** Contract has type 1\|2\|3, holderType, scope, permitsIndividualArrangement, isDefault. - **Missing or wrong:**
  - governingContractId is optional and billingRoute is a separate required concept, so there is no 'exactly one Contract per Procedure'
  - Who is billed comes from billingRoute, insurer or billable party, not from the Contract holder's 'is billed' setting or the Booking payer
  - Type 1/2/3 are categories; the catalogue says there are no kinds of Contract
  - Per-hospital and per-insurer protected default Type 1s exist; the catalogue has one No contract (RVG) held by AA and no hospital, insurer or procedure defaults
  - No 'anaesthetist may change pricing' or 'prepaid' Contract settings
  - Contracts are edited in place, can be backdated and can be deleted, with no dated versions
  - Insurer master and acceptsDirectClaims still drive routing
  - No stage-and-submit of Contract changes (US-04.1.6, outside this batch)
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:365-432 (Insurer, ContractHolderType, Contract, ContractPrice)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:80-195 (defaultContractFor, resolveContractForProcedure)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/invoiceBuild.ts:196-225 (counterpartyForProcedure by billingRoute)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx:174-186`, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:36-215`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/contracts.ts:43-62`
