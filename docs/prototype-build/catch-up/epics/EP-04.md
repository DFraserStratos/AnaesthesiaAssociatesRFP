# EP-04 · Contracts · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-04) · catalogue `efe123c` · [EP-04](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md)

> EP-04 is the largest structural gap between catalogue and prototype. The prototype is entirely on the July Type 1/2/3 Contract model and the catalogue replaces it with the flat Contract model (AR-58). Because the old model is implemented, most graded stories are Contradicts, not Missing. Verification corrected three gradings: US-04.2.14 Partial to Contradicts (override is allowed on fixed-price items and individual scope is the older precedence reading), US-04.3.3 Contradicts to Partial (price, billed-to-payer and override outcomes already match for a self-funded Booking with no contract), and US-04.3.8 Missing to Contradicts (the office route control plus unfiltered Contract select is an older equivalent, with the route stored on the Procedure). Structural changes: - Contract shape: remove type 1/2/3, holderType/holderId, scope, isDefault, permitsIndividualArrangement, effectiveFrom/To and type2Detail. Add a Party reference, a bills-holder-or-payer setting, linked surgeons and at most one linked anaesthetist, a funding tag, specialties, a pricing kind (5), a per-Contract anaesthetist-override flag (RVG-priced only), an AA code, a holder reference code, an invoice description, a default RVG code, a prepaid flag and an Active/Disabled status; kind and prepaid fixed after creation. - New masters: Party (merging insurers, organisations and the hospital pointer, adding funders, AA and anaesthetists), funding tag list (US-13.4.5), specialty list (US-13.4.4), price ranges on fixed prices. - Retire: the protected per-hospital and per-insurer default Type 1 contracts (auto-created by createHospital and setInsurerDirectClaims), Insurer.acceptsDirectClaims, deleteContract, the individual-anaesthetist scope precedence and the rate x time Method 3 gate. Replace with the AA-held 'No contract · RVG' plus system-made open-price fallbacks. - Procedure: governingContractId becomes mandatory with no 'None' option. billingRoute, insurerId, patientPaymentCategory and billablePartyId stop being set directly; the destination is derived from the Contract and the payer is on the Booking (EP-11). This ripples through validateBookingForBilling, invoiceBuild.resolveContractForProcedure and counterpartyForProcedure, OfficeBillingSetup, EditBillingSetupSheet, ManualBookingForm, EditProcedureSheet, the funder allocation sheet, all seeded Bookings (bookings.ts references the default Type 1s directly) and scenario markers. - Contract picker: a new office step rail (working design in contract-picker-mock/) mirrored on mobile, web and PWA for the anaesthetist, with 'Needs an invoice destination', Suggested path, typed search and the 'Other price' toggle. - Override: the OverrideCard has no contract gating and a mandatory reason; it must follow the per-Contract setting, refuse fixed and prepaid, and drop the reason. - A Procedure-level pricing snapshot at AUTHORISED must be stored (today only invoice-line rate detail exists), and a missing price must block AUTHORISED. - PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped with the seed change. Dependencies on other epics: EP-03 (US-03.4.1 anaesthetist changes Contract, US-03.5.1 adjustment, US-03.3.9 RVG detail), EP-05 (US-05.2.8 Needs a price, US-05.4.2 office price change, US-05.2.6 and US-05.4.3 rate and discount lines, US-05.5.1 funding tag), EP-06 (prepaid Contract starts the prepayment), EP-07 (US-07.2.2 review flag for a Contract not offered; OQ-135 for a Disabled Contract), EP-08 (invoice layout and delivery, credit note and re-issue, additional invoices and events), EP-11 (US-11.2.2 payer on the Booking, US-11.4.3 funder and approval number), EP-13 (US-13.4.3 cutover load, US-13.4.4 specialties, US-13.4.5 funding tags, US-13.6.6 surgeon specialty). Open questions OQ-133, OQ-135, OQ-114, OQ-136, OQ-122 do not block the build: the text says no affected-Procedures list and delivery is email or post. Scripted demo beats affected: S3 Beat 1 (invoice numbers and amounts depend on seeded Contracts and routes); S4 Beat 3 (billing-failure trigger end-dates the COS ACC Type 2 contract, registry.ts:364, which cannot exist under Active/Disabled, so a new failure cause such as a fixed price with no range on the List date, shown as 'Needs a price', is needed); S5 Beat 4 (contract end-dating of Health NZ agreed rate, also narrated in the DemoControlPanel S5 scenario text, becomes 'a rate change means a new Contract; raised invoices are unchanged'); S4 Beats 1 and 2 (prepaid becomes a Contract setting); S1 Beat 2 and S2 Beat 2 (office billing setup uses the step rail). Sequence the XL and L items first: model rewrite (US-04.1.1), Party (US-04.1.5), who-billed and offered-on-List (US-04.2.1, US-04.3.2) and the step rail (US-04.3.8); AA code, reference code, specialty and funding-tag items are mostly seed and admin-form work (S to M) once the Contract shape exists.
