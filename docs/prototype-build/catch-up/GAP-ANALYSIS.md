## Summary

### Headline

The prototype is the July RFP model with Phases 15 to 32 layered on. Calendar, Slots, Lists, Draft Lists, pairings, tiers, warnings engine, the AA fee run and the Xero simulation are close to the catalogue. Money and intake are not. Of roughly 235 graded items, about 40 Match, about 25 are Partial, about 15 are Missing, and the rest are Contradicts or Grouping. Contradicts dominates EP-02 to EP-08, EP-11 and EP-12: the prototype implements a different, superseded design there, not a smaller version of the new one. EP-01, EP-10 and most of EP-13 are near done. The core is one coordinated rework: Contract (holder, dated versions, lines, flags) replaces billing route plus Type 1/2/3. Pricing, completion, prepayment, invoicing, payer, review and events all hang off it. Statuses were not used to scope work; they appear below only where an open question (OQ) makes a gap unsafe.
