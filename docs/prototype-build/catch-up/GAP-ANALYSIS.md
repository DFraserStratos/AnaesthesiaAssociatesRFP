## Summary

### Headline

The prototype is the July RFP model with Phases 15 to 32 layered on. Calendar, Slots, Lists, Draft Lists, pairings, tiers, warnings engine, the AA fee run and the Xero simulation are close to the catalogue. Money and intake are not. Of roughly 235 graded items, about 40 Match, about 25 are Partial, about 15 are Missing, and the rest are Contradicts or Grouping. Contradicts dominates EP-02 to EP-08, EP-11 and EP-12: the prototype implements a different, superseded design there, not a smaller version of the new one. EP-01, EP-10 and most of EP-13 are near done. The core is one coordinated rework: Contract (holder, dated versions, lines, flags) replaces billing route plus Type 1/2/3. Pricing, completion, prepayment, invoicing, payer, review and events all hang off it. Statuses were not used to scope work; they appear below only where an open question (OQ) makes a gap unsafe.

### EP-06 · Prepayment

[EP-06](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-06.md) · epic status Agreed (4) · 11 Contradicts, 2 Partial, 3 Missing, 1 Matches

> The central missing structure is the AA Holding account. It is a creditor in the payables mirror, never paid, that receives prepayments and transfers them to the anaesthetist who did the List. That one change closes FT-06.3 and US-06.5.1 and removes the payee-repoint seam on a move (US-06.5.4). The second missing structure is a credit note and refund document kind: Invoice.kind is only standard\|prePayment, and cancelBooking and editProcedure touch no invoice. That closes US-06.5.2 and US-06.3.7 and is also needed by US-02.5.3 and the sent-or-paid half of US-06.3.5. Build order: holding account and transfer pair (with the US-06.4.1 / OQ-125 release trigger), then credit/refund engine plus cancel hook (US-06.5.2), then the Contract prepaid flag and change handler (US-06.2.1, US-06.3.5, US-06.3.7). The prototype keys prepayment on the Procedure's patientPaymentCategory (route billableParty plus selfFundedPrepayment) rather than a Contract-level prepaid flag, so US-06.3.5 and US-06.3.7 depend on the flag model from the prepayment-requirement and contract features. What already matches: a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation (US-06.3.5 AC2), and an unpaid prepayment only warns without blocking. Prepayment-related behaviour in the demo is stale under the current catalogue: the paid seed Booking (Fri 24 PM, INV0001/BC0001) pays through to the anaesthetist, and the balance run nets the deposit off the final invoice, where US-06.4.1 says a prepaid Procedure is left off the final invoice. Demo-script impact: S4 Beat 1 (Fri 24 prepayment warning) and S3 (payment received, then payable released) change, because a prepayment would land in the holding account and release only on List authorisation. S1, S2 and S5 are unaffected. The seed and PERSIST_VERSION must be bumped for the holding account and any seeded credit or refund. OQ-120 (does the prepayment invoice name the anaesthetist) is open and leans to not naming. The prototype's InvoiceDocument names the anaesthetist, which is minor to revisit once AA answers.
