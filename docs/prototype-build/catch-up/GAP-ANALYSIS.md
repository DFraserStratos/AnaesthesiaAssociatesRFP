## Summary

### Headline

The prototype was built against the July 2026 RFP. The catalogue has since moved on in ways that change the core model, not only the screens. Roughly a third of the in-scope stories match, about half are partial or missing, and around 30 items contradict the catalogue outright. The contradictions cluster on four foundations: the Slot/List/Draft List split, the Contract-as-whole-pricing-decision model, the money side (AA fee, ledger, credit/rebill), and the auto-applying hospital feed. Most of EP-05, EP-09, EP-12, EP-13 and EP-14 is sound or small. EP-01, EP-02, EP-04, EP-06 and EP-08 carry most of the work.

Sizing: Contract model (DM-06, DM-09, EP-04) is XL, intake/matching (DM-28, EP-02) is XL, Slot/List split (DM-02) is XL, Booking rename plus booking-level fields (DM-01) is L, and the ledger promotion (DM-18) is L. Parent epic and feature sizes are roll-ups of their children; do not add them.

### Demo-trigger buttons

Build one shared contextual-trigger registry first, modelled on demoClockShortcuts and living in src/shared so the PWA import closure stays pure; route matching picks which buttons show. This unblocks every trigger below.
