## Summary

### Headline

The prototype is a sound shell on the old RFP model; the catalogue has moved the model underneath it. Of the roughly 330 graded items across EP-01 to EP-15, most gaps are structural, not cosmetic. About 15 "Matches" are all in places the catalogue did not change (canvas generation, tiered time rule, Xero pair and webhook, audit viewer, AUTHORISED lock, NHI lookup stub). The rest cluster in four places: the schedule (Slot/List/Draft List), the Contract-replaces-route pricing model, the prepayment and money flow, and a missing warning model. Several rulings the prototype built as hard rules are now reversed: prepayment blocks completion, AA fee is a 5% deduction, cover is requested and accepted. EP-04, EP-06 and EP-01 are the heaviest (XL, XL, L). Estimate: the first four themes below are most of the work. A reverse check (things the prototype has that the catalogue no longer wants) was not supplied to this summary; the removal list below is derived from the item gradings and DM deltas and should be confirmed against the RV- list when it exists.

### Themes

**1. Schedule rebuilt on Slot, List and Draft List (XL).** Slot and List are one record today and List.anaesthetistId is mandatory. The catalogue wants a Slot per half-day, a List created on assignment, and Draft Lists with no anaesthetist. Availability becomes a status on the Slot, kept from one calendar with series, not a second master reconciled into Lists. Office-booked Free slots still read Free to anaesthetists (parked P5). Assigned Lists may lack surgeon or hospital, against "exactly one of each". Items: EP-01, FT-01.2, FT-01.3, FT-01.6, US-01.1.3, US-01.2.1 to US-01.2.3, US-01.3.3, US-01.5.2, US-01.5.3, US-01.6.1 to US-01.6.4, US-13.1.1. DM-02, DM-03, DM-04. OQ-64 (logical model) is Open, so build the shape minimally.

**2. Items: US-01.4.3, US-01.4.5, US-01.4.6, US-01.4.1 (email offer after), US-06.5.4. DM-05, DM-06, DM-32. OQ-65, OQ-70, OQ-43 open.

### Demo-trigger buttons

The shell has no such slot today, so build one shared contextual strip first (EP-08 notes; DemoContextMenu), then add buttons per phase. ### EP-01 · Schedule canvas, Slots and Lists

[EP-01](../../discovery-reference/Updated%20Requirements/catalogue/requirements/EP-01.md) · epic status Confirmed · 8 Contradicts, 10 Partial, 7 Missing, 6 Matches
