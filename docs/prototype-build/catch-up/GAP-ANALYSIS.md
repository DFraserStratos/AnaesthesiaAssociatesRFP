## Summary

### Headline

The prototype is a sound July-2026 build of the original RFP, but the catalogue has moved well past it. Of roughly 190 graded items, about 40 Match (mostly mobile/web capture, audit, billing-monitor and the per-counterparty invoice grouping), about 70 are Partial, about 40 Contradict and about 35 are Missing. Seven epics (EP-01, EP-04, EP-06, EP-08 late billing, EP-10 fee, EP-11, EP-02 intake) are Contradicts or XL at epic level. The gap is mostly structural, not cosmetic. Four model rewrites carry most of it: Slot/List/Draft List (DM-02, DM-03), Contract-defines-everything (DM-07, DM-10, DM-11), Events plus credit/rebill (DM-17, DM-18, DM-24) and the internal ledger with fee/payable changes (DM-22, DM-26). Two of these (Contract model, intake matching) sit on items still at Verify or Proposed, so build thin.
