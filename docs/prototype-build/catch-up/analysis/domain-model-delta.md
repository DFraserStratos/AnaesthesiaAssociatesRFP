# Data-model delta: catalogue vs prototype

Read-only comparison of the entity, relationship and lifecycle model the requirements catalogue now describes (see [domain-model.md](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/domain-model.md), updated through 5 November with the meeting with Ben and Vanessa and Donald's Contract model decisions) with what `aa-prototype/` implements at HEAD (PERSIST_VERSION 26, after catch-up Phases 14 to 17 and 28 to 32 and the specialist directory seed). The code is treated as the truth about the prototype; the old build docs were used only as leads. Retired items and items in the Future Work lane are excluded except as boundary references that explain a removal; item statuses were otherwise ignored, because the status scheme was recoded since the first pass.

This pass (catalogue commit efe123c, from a68f09b) updates the earlier one. **No commit has touched `aa-prototype/` since the last pass** (`git diff a68f09b..HEAD -- aa-prototype` is empty; the four commits since change the catalogue, the plan pages and the Requirements Board only), so every "Prototype has" paragraph still holds and only the catalogue side moved, but it moved a long way. On 5 November Donald met Ben and Vanessa over the contract picker mock (`contract-picker-mock/`) and then made eleven decisions (D1 to D11) that **rebuild the Contract model as a flat one**: [the meeting](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/notes/2026-11-05-aa-meeting-with-ben-and-vanessa.md), [Donald's decisions](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/notes/2026-11-05-donald-contract-model-decisions.md) and [the change log](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/changes/2026-11-05-flat-contract-model-and-ben-vanessa-meeting.md). They are Donald's, for the build, and not yet confirmed by AA or Greg, so every item whose substance changed went to Verify. The working copy of the design is [docs/prototype-build/contract-model-proposal.md](../../contract-model-proposal.md) (catalogue artifact AR-58). The effect on the model:

- **One Contract per priced item; no lines, no versions, no staged changes.** A holder's schedule becomes many Contracts, each edited in place with an audit trail, and only a fixed price is dated, through backdatable price ranges. Retired: [US-04.1.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.6.md), [US-04.2.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.4.md) and [US-04.2.10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.10.md). [OQ-131](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-131.md) and [OQ-100](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-100.md) are answered. DM-07 is rewritten (a flat Contract; the dated-version series goes), DM-09 shrinks from Contract lines to price ranges and three optional fields, **DM-57 (the staged change) is dropped** and **DM-16 is merged** into DM-48 and DM-09. The prototype already edits Contracts in place with an audit entry, so the version machinery the old reading needed is not needed at all.
- **A Party replaces the contract holder** ([US-04.1.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.5.md)), and each Contract carries its own bills-holder-or-payer setting. The is-billed flag on the holder and the separate billable party record are gone (DM-48, DM-11); the Booking's insurer or funder is a Party that only suggests a path (DM-12).
- **Five pricing kinds** replace Type 1, 2 and 3 (RVG at the anaesthetist's rate, at the Contract's rate, less a discount, fixed price, open price). A Procedure's price follows its Contract until a typed price, a prepayment invoice or AUTHORISED holds it, and there is one typed price on the Procedure instead of price, rate and discount (DM-50, DM-14, DM-43, DM-08). RVG code, times and modifiers are required only on the RVG kinds (DM-59).
- **Specialties and funding tags** are two new masters (DM-63, DM-64). A Contract is offered on a List only where its hospital, links and specialties fit, and the Booking's funder never narrows.
- **The step rail is decided** (where the invoice goes, then Payment type or the hospital's funding tag, then the Contract), "Needs an invoice destination" replaces "Needs a contract", and every invoice destination has a system-made open-price fallback picked by "Other price" (DM-10, DM-49). A moved Booking keeps its Contracts.
- **Prepaid** is fixed for a Contract's life, always bills the payer, is never priced from RVG units and never carries the override, and the amount is the Procedure's price when the prepayment invoice is raised, read-only after (DM-20). [OQ-73](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-73.md) is back at Confirm.
- **Procedure text and invoice description** (DM-51 reworked): the hospital's text now replaces the rooms' instead of sitting beside it, with the replaced text in the Booking's change history, and an admin's edit to the invoice description always wins ([OQ-130](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-130.md) answered). A per-Booking change history screen is [US-02.5.8](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-02.5.8.md) (DM-35: the prototype already has one).
- **Two scheduling items are new and open:** a session split into an A and a B List ([US-01.1.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.5.md), Proposed, DM-65) and out-of-hours Lists ([US-01.1.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.6.md), DM-66).
- **No model change:** the Booking total for the office ([US-03.1.10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-03.1.10.md)), the price shown to the anaesthetist ([US-03.3.10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-03.3.10.md), folded into DM-14), changes showing without signing out ([US-15.0.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-15.0.7.md)) and the help desk ([US-15.0.8](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-15.0.8.md), OQ-137). AA's pre-op consult in its own room ([US-03.7.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-03.7.4.md), Proposed) is open on [OQ-138](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-138.md) and is noted under DM-17, not built.

## Suggested order

Two tracks share only Booking and Procedure and join at DM-34's matching screen and DM-42's notice. The Schedule track already ran ahead of Contracts, so what is left of it is mostly independent. The flat Contract model removes the version series, the staged change and the relink from the Pricing track, and adds the Party, specialties and funding tags in front of the picker.

- **Pricing track.** DM-13 (RVG groups) and DM-48 (Party) first, then DM-63 (specialties) and DM-64 (funding tags), then DM-07, DM-09 and DM-49 together (the flat Contract with its flags and status, price ranges and the three optional fields, No contract · RVG and the open-price fallbacks), then DM-10 (one Contract per Procedure, the candidate query and the step rail, no route). DM-12 (insurer or funder on the Booking) follows DM-48. DM-43, DM-14, DM-44, DM-15 and DM-50 follow DM-10 (DM-50 needs DM-09 and DM-14; DM-15 needs DM-44); DM-59 (the completion gate) follows DM-10, DM-44, DM-50 and DM-13 and is built with DM-46; DM-51 pairs with DM-13 and takes its Contract-description layer from DM-09; DM-11 (who is billed) needs DM-48, DM-07 and DM-10, then DM-12 and DM-28; DM-08 (snapshot at AUTHORISED) closes the track. DM-46 (BillingLine removal) waits for DM-17. The open items (OQ-133, OQ-135, OQ-136) change screens, not structure, so nothing in the track waits on them; DM-57 is gone.
- **Money track.** DM-22 (payable in the ledger, with control accounts and sub-ledgers) first; DM-20 (prepaid Contract trigger, no deduction) needs DM-07, DM-09, DM-49 and DM-11; DM-21 (AA Holding account) needs DM-20 and DM-22; DM-17 then DM-18 then DM-24 (DM-21's automatic cancel credit and DM-20's refund on a Contract change both need DM-24); DM-23, DM-25 and DM-29 need DM-22 (DM-25 also DM-24; its payment is made in Xero and only detected, so it needs the Xero simulator's pay action, not a new in-app one).

DM-65 (the A and B split, Proposed) and DM-66 (out-of-hours Lists, not designed) wait for the owner and AA and should not start.

## Catalogue points that contradict each other (for the owner; the deltas follow the more specific item)

### Prepayment: started by picking a prepaid Contract, amount is the Procedure's price when the invoice is raised, invoice generated when an admin approves it (an optional step), prepaid Procedures left off the final invoice with nothing deducted, and a Contract changed off prepaid cancels the prepayment
