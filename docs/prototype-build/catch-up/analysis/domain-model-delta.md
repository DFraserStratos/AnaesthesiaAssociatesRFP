## DM-13

### The RVG group becomes the one master list (hidden key, type-ahead rows, modifier units, AA groups); there is no procedure list

## DM-07

### The Contract becomes a series of dated versions with an AA code and behaviour flags; Type 1/2/3, scope, the default per holder and the Method 3 gate go

**Kind:** ChangedEntity · **Size:** XL

**Catalogue says.** A Contract is a structure that sets how a procedure is paid for and who pays; there are no kinds or categories. Each Contract is a series of dated versions (start, optional end, a review date, a forward link to the version that revised it) and all its lines share the dates. Every change, a correction or a renewal, creates a new version from a date the office chooses (never past, never backdated, nothing deleted); the previous version ends the day before; only one future-dated version at a time, freely corrected or withdrawn until it starts; the version in force on the procedure date prices the Procedure. On submit, un-invoiced Procedures dated on or after the new start are relinked to the new version's line with the same line key, unmatched ones are listed, and a prepaid Procedure keeps its invoiced price (OQ-104). Every Contract has a unique short AA code (format OQ-66), searchable with holder codes. Behaviour comes from flags AA sets: the holder is billed (on the holder), editable (may the anaesthetist change price, rate or discount; OQ-107), prepaid, and default (No contract (RVG)), plus invoice layout, delivery method and GST treatment (US-04.1.1 to US-04.1.4, US-04.2.2, US-04.2.8, US-04.2.10). Anaesthetists never edit Contracts. A prepaid Contract is never editable and its line price must be above zero (OQ-96, 28 October); a fixed price never allows the anaesthetist's override whatever the flag says (OQ-117, 29 October). How invoices are delivered is no longer a Contract setting (see DM-16, DM-48). Open: how the office keeps a Contract current day to day (whole-Contract dated versions as above, a date on each line, or editing in place with an audit trail) is [OQ-131](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-131.md), for Vanessa; [US-04.2.10](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.10.md) and [US-04.2.14](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.14.md) went from Agreed to Open on 4 November, though both still describe dated versions. **Prototype has.** Contract (types.ts:388): type 1 | 2 | 3, holderType and holderId, scope, permitsIndividualArrangement, isDefault, effectiveFromISO and effectiveToISO, type2Detail. No version link, review date, AA code, editable flag or prepaid flag; contractActions.ts edits in place with audit entries only. Effectiveness is judged on the List date at billing time (contracts.ts:22) and selection ranks individual over organisational over default (contracts.ts:34). Needs DM-48 and DM-09 in the same step. The rank-based selectContract is replaced by the filtered picker of DM-10. The editable flag drives DM-14 and the prepaid flag drives DM-20. A new version needs a copy-lines action, an overlap guard and the relink on submit; staged review is DM-57. Seed Contracts are re-authored. The editable and prepaid flags are mutually exclusive (a save-time guard), and the prepaid flag also forces a price above zero on every line (see DM-09). Until OQ-131 is answered, build the header, holder link, flags and a version row (start, end, review date, forward link) so a Procedure's Contract resolves on its date, and hold the copy-lines action, overlap guard and relink on submit (and DM-57) behind the answer; DM-08's snapshot keeps an issued invoice independent of whichever editing model wins.

**Kind:** ChangedEntity · **Size:** L
