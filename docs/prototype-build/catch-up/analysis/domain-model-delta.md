# Data-model delta: catalogue vs prototype

Read-only comparison of the entity, relationship and lifecycle model the requirements catalogue now describes (see [domain-model.md](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/domain-model.md), updated through 17 November) with what `aa-prototype/` implements at HEAD (PERSIST_VERSION 44, after catch-up Phases 18 to 22, 32a to 32c and 38a). The code is treated as the truth about the prototype; the old build docs were used only as leads. Retired items and items in the Future Work lane are excluded except as boundary references; item statuses were ignored.

This pass (catalogue commit c9b65ad, from efe123c) updates the earlier one. **The prototype moved a long way since the last pass** (PERSIST_VERSION 26 to 44): the whole Contracts track up to the price and completion rules is built, and so are Parties, specialties, funding tags, RVG groups, modifiers, procedure text, the payer and funder on the Booking, invoice presentation, the fee split, the single-Booking move, unavailability without conflicts, the notification pool's states and the out-of-hours session. Nineteen deltas are therefore closed as built (list below), and what remains open is mostly the Money track and the intake track, which no phase has touched, plus the price ladder (Phase 24) and the primary Procedure (Phase 23). The catalogue side moved too, in four ways:

- **The payer moves to each Procedure** (17 November; [Donald's note](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/notes/2026-11-17-donald-payer-per-procedure.md), [the change log](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/changes/2026-11-17-donald-payer-per-procedure.md), [OQ-140](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-140.md) for AA to confirm). Two Procedures on one Booking can bill two different people; a changed payer is offered to the Procedures billing the same payer; invoices and prepayment invoices group by payer; a split can name another person; the contact rule and the under-18 warning apply per Procedure. The prototype built the payer as a Booking field in Phase 21, so this is rework of just-built code (DM-11, with notes on DM-28, DM-20, DM-31, DM-23, DM-08 and DM-17).
- **Out-of-hours becomes a third Slot** (10 November, [US-01.1.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.6.md) rewritten): not a List held outside the Slots. Phase 32c built exactly that, so DM-66 is closed. [US-01.1.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.5.md) (the A and B split) is unchanged and still Proposed (DM-65).
- **Two No contract backstops and the payment type always asked** (16 November, [US-04.3.8](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.3.8.md), [US-04.3.9](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.3.9.md)): every destination's Contract list starts with a "No contract" heading holding an RVG backstop and a Fixed price backstop, and the "Other price" toggle is gone. The Phase 20 owner review built the same thing (v39), so DM-49 and DM-10 stay closed.
- **The meeting with AA's accountant** (10 November, [the change log](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/changes/2026-11-10-aa-meeting-with-accountant.md)): Greg and Sam's proposed posting design routes each invoice and its bill through a clearing account that reads zero (DM-22, not agreed; their process maps will settle it), the move from the AA Holding account is "an accounting move, not a payment" and how Xero posts it is open (DM-21), the weekly cycle's days are not agreed (DM-25), and one new Proposed story, [US-13.2.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.2.4.md), asks for a reconciliation status on the dashboard (**new DM-67**).

Also noted, no model change: [OQ-139](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-139.md) (what a public-hospital Slot shows; it bears on the Slot status list, not the model), and OQ-130's invoice description rule, already built.

## Suggested order

Two tracks share only Booking and Procedure and join at DM-34's matching screen. The Pricing track's foundation (Parties, flat Contracts, the rail, the price ranges) is built; the next work in it follows the plan's phase order.

- **Pricing track.** DM-11 (the payer on each Procedure) first, since the prototype's Booking payer is the thing that moved; DM-28's 'another person' share follows it. Then DM-14 and DM-50 together (one typed price and the price ladder, Phase 24), DM-15 (the primary Procedure, Phase 23, independent of those two), then DM-08 (the snapshot at AUTHORISED, which needs DM-50, DM-14 and DM-11). DM-46 (BillingLine removal) waits for DM-17; its Method 3 gate goes with Phase 24's reshaping of the Contract.
- **Money track.** DM-22 (payable in the ledger, with control accounts and sub-ledgers; the clearing account waits on Greg and Sam's maps) first; DM-20 (prepaid Contract trigger, one invoice per payer, no deduction) needs DM-14 and DM-11 and can start before the ledger; DM-21 (AA Holding account) needs DM-20 and DM-22; DM-17 then DM-18 then DM-24 (DM-21's refund and DM-20's cancel-on-change both need DM-24); DM-23, DM-25 and DM-29 need DM-22 (DM-25 also DM-24; its payment is made in Xero and only detected); DM-67 (the reconciliation status) needs DM-22 and DM-23 and is Proposed.
- **Schedule, remaining.** DM-30 (NHI mandatory) then DM-34 (the matching screen) with DM-61 (the PDF inbox, built together); DM-58 (stubs) needs DM-30 and DM-34's unmatched queue; DM-35 (explicit save and the update email) is free-standing now that DM-41 is built. DM-65 (the A and B split, Proposed) waits for AA and should not start.
- **Free-standing, small.** DM-32, DM-38, DM-45 and DM-60 can go at any time (DM-60's contact list waits on OQ-127); DM-68 (the Procedure's billing reference) is held until OQ-136 is answered. DM-31 absorbs the two remaining rules as DM-23 and DM-11 land.

Every model change that alters the seed shape needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 44; CLAUDE.md still says 13).

## Catalogue points that contradict each other (for the owner; the deltas follow the more specific item)

- **Payer on the Procedure against the Booking.** Greg said on 28 October that every Booking names a payer ("I think that's right, it's pretty simple"); Donald chose a payer on each Procedure on 17 November, following Greg's design v6, and asks AA to confirm ([OQ-140](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-140.md)). Older questions (OQ-54, OQ-67, OQ-73, OQ-78, OQ-105, OQ-114) and diagrams (AR-01, AR-02, AR-44) still say 'payer on the Booking'. [US-08.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-08.2.2.md) speaks of 'the final invoice' in the singular although a Booking can now have one per payer. DM-11 follows the Procedure.
- **Specialties per surgeon.** [US-13.6.6](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.6.6.md) and [US-13.6.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.6.2.md) hold a list of one or more (Donald's D3, built), but Ben answered 'No' on 5 November to a surgeon having several. The item stays at Verify for AA to confirm.
- **Backdated price ranges.** [US-04.2.15](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.15.md) lets a range start in the past (built, D6) while Greg said on 23 October that a Contract cannot be backdated; the story records the clash.
- **A prepaid price after the invoice.** Greg's 28 October wording (and OQ-96) lets the office change any price including a prepaid one; the 5 November reading ([US-05.4.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-05.4.2.md), AR-58 A16) makes a prepaid price read-only once its invoice is raised. DM-14 and DM-20 follow 5 November.
- **A holder-billed prepaid Contract.** The 28 October revision of [OQ-73](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-73.md) allowed one, while a prepaid Contract now always bills the payer (our reading A2, built), so OQ-73 sits at Confirm.
- **Included modifiers.** [US-03.3.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-03.3.4.md) still says in one bullet that an included modifier is 'a pill selected at 0 units that cannot be unselected', while its own criteria, [US-05.1.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-05.1.4.md) and [US-05.1.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-05.1.5.md) say none is locked, and the domain model's modifier paragraph still says 'locked'. The code follows not locked (Phase 19b, removedModifierIds).
- **Stubs against the intake ruling.** Settled on 4 November ([OQ-118](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-118.md)): a stub is not a Booking, so no Booking is saved without an NHI; that the Booking then replaces the stub is still our reading. DM-30 builds the rule and DM-58 the stub.
- **Cash refunds and payments.** Settled on 4 November ([OQ-111](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-111.md)): Xero handles all payments and the system tracks them. How Xero posts the holding-account move and keeps its bill out of the payment run was left open on 10 November ([US-06.5.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.5.1.md)). DM-21 and DM-25 build the ledger side, which is the same either way.
- **The weekly payment cycle.** [US-10.2.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.7.md) has a Friday close and a Tuesday payment and rolls a Monday anomaly into the next cycle; Greg now wants nothing carried forward and the accountant wants more time before payment ([OQ-47](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-47.md), not agreed). DM-25 keeps the days as settings.
- **PDF ingest against its siblings.** [US-02.2.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-02.2.1.md) left Future Work on 4 November, but [US-02.2.3](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-02.2.3.md) and [US-02.5.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-02.5.4.md) stay in Future Work although the rooms re-send the whole List on every change. DM-61 and DM-34 build the review-and-merge step and leave removal detection out.
- **Bank details and who pays.** [US-12.1.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.4.md) says the anaesthetist's bank details 'are used for the payables run', the disbursement from AA's account; [OQ-111](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-111.md) and [US-10.2.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.1.md) have Xero make every payment and the system only track it. DM-32 holds the details as master data and DM-25 does not pay from them.
- **A post-op addendum as a Procedure or as an event.** [US-11.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.2.2.md) and the domain model list 'a post-op addendum' among the Procedures added to a Booking later, while [FT-03.7](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-03.7.md) makes post-op work an event on the original Procedure, 'not a Procedure added to the Booking'. DM-17 follows the event reading.
- **GST number on the invoice.** [US-08.4.5](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-08.4.5.md) (Proposed) puts the anaesthetist's GST number on the receivable invoice, built as an optional Admin-only field, while the profile stories name no GST number field. Both wait on [OQ-29](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-29.md).
