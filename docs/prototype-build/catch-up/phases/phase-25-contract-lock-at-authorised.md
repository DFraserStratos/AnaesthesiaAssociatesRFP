## Goal

**The lock** (US-04.3.5, US-07.3.1, DM-08, RV-11; [AR-58 section 2.8](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-58.md), the Procedure in [AR-61](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-61.md)). Contracts are edited in place, so the snapshot **copies** what it needs and points at no version. `authoriseList` writes a **pricing snapshot** for every Procedure on every non-cancelled Booking, in the same commit that flips the List to AUTHORISED, holding:
- the Contract's settings as they then stood (AA code, name, holder Party, pricing kind, rate, discount, bills holder or payer, prepaid, adjustable), **only the price range in force** on the procedure date, and `pricingDateISO`;
- the Procedure's values (name, invoice description, RVG code) and what was recorded: base, time and modifier units (19b's items with explanations), the units after the multi-procedure rule and this Procedure's share (23, D78), and `outOfGuideRange`;
- the **unit rate** used (the Contract's fixed rate, or the anaesthetist's unit value in force, US-12.1.1), the discount, and the **price** with 24's figures (the typed price, the office change, the discount line, ex GST);
- who is billed: the holder Party, or the payer on the Booking, with the details the invoice prints, the split shares (22) and the presentation (layout, delivery);
- once per Booking, the **payee** (the List's anaesthetist at authorise, DM-42) with the supplier details 22 prints, the patient details, the GST rate and any pre-payment already invoiced.

That is the invoice's recipe (US-08.4.4). **A Procedure that still shows "Needs a price" blocks AUTHORISED** (our reading, A48; the first and only authorise blocker, through 21a's `authoriseBlockersFor` seam; 15a's warnings still never block). The billing run then prices **only** from the snapshot (US-08.1.1, RV-11): no effective-date fallback, no route or payer resolution, and no resolve-and-retry that edits a master. Later Contract edits, new or backdated price ranges and unit-value edits never alter an issued invoice, and a screen-contextual **Regenerate from locked data** rebuilds one exactly (US-08.4.4). 24's price function reads the snapshot as its first rung. A Booking the run cannot build (no snapshot, or one it cannot price) is held back whole while the rest of the List bills (OQ-05); the List never fails.

**Contract history** (US-04.1.3). Each Contract's audited edits (create, edit, disable, its price ranges, specialties and tag), with who, when and the values before and after, shown on a **History tab** on 18's Contract screen, beside the Procedures locked against it (and their invoices). Nothing is deleted: a corrected price range keeps its earlier value in the audit. Parties and RVG groups are audited the same way and shown in their own views by one shared component.

**The billing-failure demo is re-based.** The old cause (ending a Contract and un-ending it in "Resolve & retry") cannot work, and no holder reference exists to go missing (D82). The cause that survives the lock is an outside one: the Xero hand-off of Losa Tuilagi's Booking faults. She fails and is held back, Hemi Walker's Booking bills, and the office retries once without touching any Contract.

**The pricing model in one place.** The snapshot type, builder, lock-only pricing, comparison and `lockViewFor` live in one new `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/lock.ts` (re-exported from the billing index, plus the seed); the History rows in `contractHistory.ts` beside it. The UI reads `lockViewFor` and the store's selectors, never raw snapshot fields, so a change to what the snapshot holds (US-04.3.5 still says the exact fields are "to be determined in discovery") is an edit to `lock.ts`, its tests and the seed, never to a screen. Build AR-58 2.8's list plus what the printed invoice and the payee need, and log it for the owner.
