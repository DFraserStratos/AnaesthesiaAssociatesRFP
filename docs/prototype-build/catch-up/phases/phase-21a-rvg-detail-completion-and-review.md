## Goal

**What a Procedure asks for follows its Contract's pricing kind** (US-03.3.9, US-03.3.3; Donald's D9; [AR-58](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-58.md) 5.7 step 4, 5.8). On an RVG kind (No contract · RVG and the three rate and discount kinds) the RVG code, start and finish times and a completed modifiers step are asked and required: an explanation per claimed modifier, and choosing none is a valid answer (A20). On a fixed or open price the form collapses on mobile, web and Admin: no times, no modifiers, and an RVG code only for reference. Ben's two routes to the same invoice both work: RVG detail with a price override on an RVG Contract that allows it (the detail stays recorded and visible), or No contract · Fixed price with a typed amount and an optional code. **One predicate decides it**: the form, the completion rule and the capture hooks all read it.

**Completion is exactly three conditions** (US-03.6.1, DM-59, RV-21): every Procedure has a Contract; on RVG kinds, the RVG detail; and a price, not "Needs a price" (our reading, A48). The rule is its own pure function, split from the billing validator, which keeps only the engine's pre-flight. Out-of-range base units and a missing approval number only warn; the holder's references are not built (D82). Times are start and finish, never "handover". A Procedure the anaesthetist cannot finish goes to the office through 32b's alert.

**Office review shows what the office must check** (US-07.2.2): each Procedure's procedure text, Contract and RVG code (20a's stack), the payer, the insurer or funder and approval number (21's), 32b's alerts and 38a's late edits, and **review flags that never block** (our reading, A49): a Contract the anaesthetist changed; a typed price on a Contract that is not open price, shown beside the computed price (24 supplies it); a Contract that no longer fits its List after a move; and Procedures still needing an invoice destination or a price. A Disabled Contract raises none (D81). The office approves or corrects each Contract change.

**A moved Booking keeps its Contracts** (US-04.3.11, our reading, A41): no move path ever resets a Procedure's Contract; a price not held works out again on the new date; a Contract that is not a candidate on the new List is flagged at review.

**One place each.** The pricing-kind predicate (`rvgDetail.ts`), the price status (`priceStatus.ts`, which 24 re-points), the completion rule (`completion.ts`) and the review-flag list (`reviewFlags.ts`) live in `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing`, pure and tested; the UI only reads them. Where a rule is our reading (the price condition, the flags, the moved-Booking flag, the modifiers-step marker) say so in a doc comment citing AR-58 A20, A41, A48 or A49, and log it.
