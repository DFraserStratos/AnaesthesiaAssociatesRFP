## Goal

Per OQ-63 (answered), "event" is the one name for everything recorded against a Procedure after it is set up: a pre-op or post-op recording, an additional invoice, a credit. It is its own element attached to the Procedure, never a Procedure added to the Booking and never a Booking. Today the prototype has nothing of the kind: a post-op charge is a whole new addendum Booking (`bookingType: 'postOpAddendum'`, `addendumOfBookingId`, `addPostOpAddendum` in `src/store/bookingActions.ts`) that the anaesthetist starts from "Add post-op event" on a locked Booking, which lands on a free session today and runs its own capture, submit, authorise and bill cycle (RV-10). This phase replaces it:

- **The event element** (DM-17). A thin `ProcedureEvent` record on the Procedure: its kind, its date (and a start time for the kinds that need one), the invoice tick, the billable party as "same as" or a named party, its kind's detail, its review state, who raised it and, once invoiced, its invoice. One store action per step (add, update, remove, approve, decline, invoice), so Phase 39 (credits) and Phase 39b (pre-op and post-op) add kinds without reshaping it.
- **One standard review step and next-run invoicing** (D13). Every billable event, whoever raised it, goes to an **Events** tab in Admin Review. The office approves it (or declines it, with a reason). An approved event is invoiced in **the next billing run**. One pure rule decides whether an event travels with the Procedure's own invoice (recorded before that invoice is approved, same party, a kind that may bundle) or is invoiced on its own in the next run; the additional invoice is always its own invoice, and the bundling branch is wired into the List billing run by 39b, whose pre-op and post-op kinds use it.
- **The events list on every Procedure** (US-03.7.3), on mobile, web and Admin, from one shared component under each Procedure in `BookingDetailBody`. Everyone sees each event's kind, date, description and status. On their own Procedure the anaesthetist also sees its total ex GST and party (they raise these invoices themselves, in their own name), never a Booking total and no review action; the office also sees the invoice link and the review actions. 39's credits and 39b's pre-op and post-op events join the same list.
- **The additional invoice, the first kind** (DM-18, US-08.6.1, US-08.6.3; D10, D22). A button against the Procedure's Contract line (the Contract part of 20a's stack, beside 22's Split) on a Procedure of an invoiced Booking: **Create additional invoice** in Admin on any Procedure, and **Raise additional invoice** on mobile and web on the anaesthetist's **own** Procedure, which they reach through 38a's archive or search. Both open the same free-form form: lines (description, quantity, amount; no Contract, unit, modifier or RVG input) and a "Same as the original invoice" billable-party tick (Greg's courier-style tick, #24) that unticks to any billable party. A bottom sheet on the phone, a side sheet in Admin and a dialog on the web. Saving records an event in review. Once approved by the office and run, it is its own invoice with its own `AA-2026-` number, ledger pair, Xero pair and anaesthetist payable, linked both ways to the original Procedure and invoice, which stays unchanged (US-07.3.2). It is its own receivable with its own ACCPAY, so it is one more BCTI through Phase 16's one count.
- **The by-hand route after a fixed price** (US-08.6.3 "After a fixed price", US-06.4.1). Nothing in any run creates an additional invoice or an event: after a prepaid or fixed-price procedure whose work turned out more than expected, an additional invoice exists only once the anaesthetist or the office raises one with this button. On a prepaid Procedure (Phase 27: priced at the prepaid amount, nothing left to bill) the original it links to is the prepayment invoice.
- **The addendum Booking goes** (RV-10). `addPostOpAddendum`, the addendum fields, its banner, its "Add post-op event" button, its Booking source mapping and 28's addendum List helper are removed, not hidden. The anaesthetist's late-charge route for money is now Raise additional invoice; Phase 39b brings their pre-op and post-op events back on this element.

One seeded event, an additional invoice that Dr Souter raised on a billed Procedure of hers and that is awaiting the office's review, makes the events list, the anaesthetist's view, the review step and the run demoable straight from a reset, on a handset too, without moving any seeded figure. S4 Beat 2 (the post-op addendum) is rewritten as an additional invoice, with the anaesthetist's own route as an aside. The state gains a `procedureEvents` slice, an invoice kind and loses two Booking fields, so `PERSIST_VERSION` is bumped.

- fan out three independent Opus review subagents, one each for **quality**, **bugs/correctness** and **plan adherence** (scale to four with **money integrity**, as the money phases do, given the new invoices, pairs and BCTIs), each given the covered catalogue files, this doc and the diff;
- this session then independently verifies every finding against the catalogue, this doc and the code, fixes the confirmed ones (with a test wherever a bug had none), re-greens, and records the pass in the phase entry;
- do not re-raise anything already settled in the Decisions log (as amended by this phase).

**Steer this phase's reviewers at:**

- **Immutability.** No action writes the original Booking, its Procedures, its lock or the original invoice and its lines. Every back-link is a derived selector. Hunt for a spread-and-overwrite of `billing.invoices[originalId]` or a lock, and for any loosening of 38a's read-only rule beyond the event.
- **One element, thin.** One `ProcedureEvent` record and one store action per step; the kind's rules only in `EVENT_KINDS`; status derived, never stored; nothing kind-specific outside the `detail` union and `additionalInvoice.ts`, so 39 and 39b add kinds without reshaping. No modifier, ASA, base-unit, RVG or Contract field on the record.
- **Own Procedure only.** An anaesthetist raises, edits or removes only on a Procedure whose `procedureOwner` is them, and only events they raised; the button never renders on a colleague's Procedure and the store refuses it anyway; the ownership rule lives only in `mayRaise`, `mayEditEvent` and `procedureOwner`.
- **One review step.** Every billable event, whoever raised it, is approved or declined by the office in the Events tab before it is invoiced; nothing is invoiced at approval; approval re-validates; a decline needs a reason; an approved or invoiced event cannot be edited or removed.
- **Next-run invoicing.** Only `runEventInvoicing` raises an event's invoice; it invoices each approved event exactly once (idempotent across runs, retries and the PWA stand-in's narrowed run); `runBillingForList` is unchanged, never sweeps events and never creates one, so S3 and S5 numbers do not shift and nothing is raised automatically after a fixed price.
- **Free form means free form.** `additionalInvoice.ts` imports no fee, Contract, line, resolver, precedence, unit, modifier or RVG module; the form shows no Contract picker; the amount is quantity x amount per line and nothing else.
- **Own pair, own BCTI.** Each additional invoice has its own number from the ordinary sequence, its own pair with payable equal to receivable and the original's payee, its own Xero pair with no NHI or patient name, and exactly one BCTI record through `bctiRecordsOf`, counted by 16's `bctisFor` under its paid-only rule like any other; events awaiting review, approved but not run, or declined add none. On a prepaid Procedure the original is the prepayment invoice and the payee is its pair's.
- **Party.** "Same as" resolves to the original invoice's party and email; any other party is allowed and audited; the resolution lives only in `resolveEventParty`.
- **What the anaesthetist sees.** Totals and party on their own Procedure's events only; no review action, invoice link or Booking total; the PWA build has no admin import (`pwaPurity`), and the shared form and list live in `src/shared`.
- **Retired behaviour gone.** No `addPostOpAddendum`, `bookingType`, `addendumOfBookingId`, addendum banner, "Add post-op event", addendum source mapping or addendum List helper remains; `stage-post-op` only authorises.
- **One label.** The word "event" and every kind, status and button label come only from `procedureEventCopy.ts`.
- **Determinism and persistence.** No `Date.now()`, `new Date()` or `Math.random()`; the seeded event's instant is fixed; `PERSIST_VERSION` bumped with migrate tests; two fresh seeds deep-equal; no seeded figure moved.
- **Design and copy.** Teal the only action colour; status pills on semantic tints; mono for dates, numbers and amounts; a bottom sheet on the phone, not a modal; no en or em dashes; "slot" never in app copy.
