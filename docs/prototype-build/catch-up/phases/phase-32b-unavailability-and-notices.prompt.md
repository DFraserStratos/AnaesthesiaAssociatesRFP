Phase goal: unavailability is never a conflict. When the office marks a booked session unavailable, sickness included, the List must go back to the office as a Draft List or be handed to an available colleague there and then, as for the anaesthetist, and the office never assigns onto an unavailable session; whoever moves a List chooses whether the vacated session is free or unavailable, on every path.
