## Goal

What the step rail reads beyond the Contract record, built so 19a and 20 only consume it.

1. **Specialties** (US-13.4.4, DM-63). A master list of broad specialties, no subspecialties, kept by the office in Master data (add, rename, remove), every change audited. Anaesthetists hold none. Starting list: Ophthalmology, General surgery, Plastic surgery, ENT, Gynaecology, Orthopaedics, Urology, Gastroenterology, Cardiothoracic surgery (AR-58 2.5, our reading A11), plus Neurosurgery, Vascular surgery and Oral and maxillofacial surgery, added here because the directory seed holds surgeons in them with no broad home in the nine (our reading; for AA to confirm with its spreadsheet).
2. **Surgeon specialties** (US-13.6.6; follow-up of 17's FT-13.6 and US-13.6.2). `Surgeon.specialty?: string` becomes `specialtyIds: SpecialtyId[]`, one or more, usually one, **required**: the surgeon form refuses a save with none. Shown on the profile and everywhere the free text showed. Several per surgeon is Donald's D3 against Ben's "No", at Verify: build the list, log it. The free text is mapped onto the list for the cast and the 131 directory surgeons, with a test.
3. **Funding tags** (US-13.4.5, DM-64). A master list in step-rail order: SXAP, HNZ, ACC, each optionally naming its funder Party (our reading A25: Southern Cross Health Society for SXAP, ACC, Health NZ), office-maintained and audited. Nothing here reads the funder; 21's "Suggested" will.
4. **On the Contract** (US-04.2.16, US-04.2.17). `specialtyIds` (none or more; none means every specialty) and `fundingTagId` (at most one). Where a Contract sits on the rail is derived from its settings and never stored: bills the payer is **Patient** (Standard, or Prepaid), bills a hospital holder and carries a tag is **that hospital and tag**, anything else is **Special** under its holder (AR-58 5.2). One pure predicate says whether a Contract's specialties fit a surgeon's (none, or any overlap, our reading A10); 20's filter calls it.
5. **ACC is a funding tag, not a kind of Contract** (FT-05.5, US-05.5.1). The seeded ACC Contracts (St George's ACC, Canterbury Orthopaedic Surgeons' ACC) carry the ACC tag and stay priced from RVG units at their Contract's rate, so no fee moves. The ACC marker on a Procedure is read from its Contract's tag: the stored `Procedure.accRelated` goes.
6. **The open-price fallbacks** (DM-49, AR-58 5.5). Exactly one system-made Contract of pricing kind `open` per invoice destination: **No contract · Fixed price** and **Prepaid · Fixed price** (held by AA, seeded), `<Hospital> · <TAG> · Fixed price` per hospital and tag, `<Holder> · Fixed price` per Special holder. No specialties, no links, never disabled or deleted; the office may rename them and edit their AA code (and, from 19a, invoice description), nothing else. One idempotent `ensureFallbacks()` keeps them in step after seeding and after every Contract create or edit. They are named "Fixed price" because a person types a fixed amount on the Procedure, but they are kind `open`, never `fixed`: in code `openPriceFallback`, never `fixedFallback` (AR-58 1.1).
