## Goal

A prepayment is not paid to the anaesthetist. Its payable is the **AA Holding account**: a creditor in the payables sub-ledger, kept like an anaesthetist's account (Greg's "fake anaesthetist"), that receives prepayments and transfers them to the anaesthetist who did the procedure but is never paid itself. It is not a bank account and not a Xero ledger account: the money sits in AA's trust account, the bank account. Its balance is every prepayment received for procedures not yet done (US-06.5.1, FT-06.5; D38). Today the prepayment's draft ACCPAY is to the anaesthetist and released as the patient pays (`payableRelease.ts:16`, `paymentActions.ts:84`), and the seeded paid prepayment (Souter Fri 24 PM, Nair) was disbursed on 2026-07-16 for a 24 Jul procedure (`seed/billing.ts:68`).

Step one (Phase 27) is the collect pair: a receivable from the payer on the Booking and a payable to the account, plus its draft Xero pair. Step two is built here: once the procedure is done the prepaid amount moves **automatically**, with no office step, from the account to the anaesthetist who did the List (the payee in 25's snapshot) by a **journal transfer**, an accounting move, not a new invoice and not a payment: the account's payable for the Procedure is reduced and a payable to the anaesthetist created, recorded as one transfer entry with both legs, linked to the prepayment invoice and mirrored to Xero as a journal. The anaesthetist's payable is then released for the payables run like any payable. **Only the system pays out of the account** (this transfer, or 41's cancellation refund): a payables run skips it and a manual payout against it is refused (US-06.5.1's new criterion).

**Trigger (OQ-125, D72, open).** The catalogue's trigger, the office authorising the List (US-06.4.1, US-06.5.1), is built and labelled provisional in one place, so Greg's "confirmed and submitted" is a small change.

A moved prepaid Booking needs no change: the payable is the account, so no code repoints a payee anywhere and nothing is recalculated on a move (D20 superseded, D38; OQ-03 confirmed by Ben on 27 October); the transfer simply pays whoever did the List.

**Design in one place.** Everything lives in `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/prepayHolding.ts` (pure, tested) behind types the UI reads (`HOLDING_ACCOUNT`, `holdingBalance`, `holdingEntries`, `isHeldPayable`, `transferFor`, `canPayOutOfHolding`), with the store actions in `store/prepaymentSettlement.ts`. Phase 36 later promotes the legs and the transfer into the internal ledger and reads this module's results for a parity test; keep the leg tags below stable. Link [AR-52](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-52.md) (`#holding-account`, `#pp-transfer`, `#release-trigger`, `#trust-bank`, `#creditors`), [AR-24#prepayment-settlement](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-24.md), [#prepayment-move](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-24.md) and [AR-29#prepayment](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-29.md) in the header comments.
