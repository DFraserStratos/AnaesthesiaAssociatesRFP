## Goal

A prepayment is not paid to the anaesthetist. Its payable is the **prepay holding account**: a ledger account that AA treats like an anaesthetist's account, with a balance that is every deposit received for procedures not yet done (US-06.5.1, FT-06.5; OQ-80 answered 19 October, D38). Today it is the opposite: the prepayment's draft ACCPAY is to the anaesthetist and is released as the patient pays, so Dr Souter can be paid for a procedure that has not happened (`payableRelease.ts:16`, `paymentActions.ts:84`), and the seeded paid prepayment (Souter Fri 24 PM, Nair) was disbursed on 2026-07-16 for a 24 Jul procedure (`seed/billing.ts:68`).

This phase builds the account and the second step. Step one (Phase 27) is the collect pair: a receivable from the payer on the Booking and a payable to the account, plus its draft Xero pair. Step two is built here: when the office authorises the List, a **second pair** moves each prepaid invoice's amount from the account to the anaesthetist who did the List (the payee in 25's snapshot), a receivable from the account and a payable to the anaesthetist with its own draft Xero pair, in place of another invoice to the patient (US-09.1.3, FT-09.1). The second payable is then released for the payables run exactly as any payable is. Nothing is paid out of the account before then: `payablesDue` and the run skip it.

A moved prepaid Booking needs no change: the payable is the account, so no code repoints a payee anywhere and nothing is recalculated on a move (D20 superseded by OQ-80); the second pair simply pays whoever did the List.

**Design in one place.** Everything lives in `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/prepayHolding.ts` (pure, tested) behind types the UI reads (`holdingBalance`, `holdingEntries`, `isHeldPayable`, `transferAmountsFor`), with the store actions in `store/prepaymentSettlement.ts`. Phase 36 later promotes the legs into the internal ledger and reads this module's results for a parity test; keep the leg tags below stable. Link [AR-24#prepayment-settlement](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-24.md), [#prepayment-move](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-24.md) and [AR-29#prepayment](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-29.md) in the header comments.

2. **Model (new).**
   - One account record, `HOLDING_ACCOUNT = { id: 'HOLD', name: 'Prepay holding account' }`, held in `masters` (read-only, seeded, not editable) and a Xero contact for it through a new `holdingAccountContactSpec` beside `anaesthetistContactSpec` (key `holdingAccount:HOLD`, an organisation-type, archive-exempt contact, contact number `HOLD-PREPAY`).    - `XeroAccPay.payee: 'anaesthetist' | 'holdingAccount'` (reuse 27's flag if it shipped one); `anaesthetistId` becomes optional and set only for an anaesthetist payee. `XeroAccRec.kind` gains `'prepayTransfer'`. `BillingCase.prepayLeg?: 'collect' | 'transfer'` and, on a transfer, `transferOf: caseId` (the collect case it settles). 3. **Pure rules, `prepayHolding.ts`, with `prepayHolding.test.ts`.**
   - `isHeldPayable(accPay)`: true for a `holdingAccount` payee.
   - `holdingEntries(legs)`: the account's entries, oldest first, each `{ atISO, kind: 'received' | 'moved', amount, bookingId, invoiceNumber, anaesthetistId? }`: a `received` entry per receipt on a collect pair, a `moved` entry per settlement on a transfer ACCREC.
   - `holdingBalance(legs)` = received minus moved, in cents (`money.ts`); it equals all deposits received for procedures not yet done.
   - `transferAmountsFor(collectCases, snapshot)`: per prepaid invoice on an authorised List, the transfer's amount (the invoice total, gross, so the payable equals the receivable: FT-10.3), the amount released now (`payableReleasedFor(received, total)`, the existing rule, not a copy) and the payee (25's snapshot payee, else the List's anaesthetist). Tests: part paid, paid, unpaid, over-received clamp, two prepaid Procedures on one Booking, a cancelled Booking skipped (41 refunds it), a withdrawn or unsent invoice skipped, and a moved Booking paying whoever did the List.
4. **Hold the collect payable.** `payablesDue`, `runPayables` and `disbursePayable` skip any ACCPAY where `isHeldPayable` (the payables-run panel's count and total never include it). `receivePayment` on a collect case credits the account as today (the receipt, the ACCREC, the ACCPAY authorised pro rata by `payableReleasedFor`), and nothing reaches an anaesthetist. The collect ACCPAY never reaches `paid` through a run; it stays authorised, and the account's balance reads it. `handoffCase` takes the payee spec from the case's leg: `holdingAccountContactSpec` for a collect case, the List's anaesthetist otherwise. A prepaid Booking that moves keeps its collect payee untouched; delete the "Phase 41 seam" comments in `listMoveActions.ts` and `slotActions.ts` and replace them with one line naming D38 (no repoint).
5. **The second pair (new `store/prepaymentSettlement.ts`).**

Audited as the system (`prepayment.transfer`, with the amount and payee), one `mutate`, idempotent per collect case, never throws.

   - The transfer ACCREC is settled from the account up to the amount released, with no `PaymentIn` and no `BillingReceipt` (a transfer is not money from a payer, so GST receipts, the GST report and web Accounts' received totals cannot change; test it).
