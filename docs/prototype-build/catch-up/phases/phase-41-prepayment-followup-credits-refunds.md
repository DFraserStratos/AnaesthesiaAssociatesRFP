## Goal

Finish the prepayment story on 27 and 27a. Keep it thin and build what the catalogue now says: nothing about a prepayment is calculated or raised automatically after it is sent, except that **cancelling settles it**; nothing in the ledger changes when the Booking moves.

- **The letter (US-06.3.6).** Whenever a prepayment invoice is sent (27's **Approve and send**, or automatically with approval switched off), it goes with a letter filled in from **one standard template** that admins maintain (more may exist; the default is used). The admin sees a preview before sending. The template states the prepaid amount as **the price payable before the procedure, never an estimate**, and may say a further invoice or credit is possible. Merge fields: patient, procedure, date, prepaid amount, anaesthetist (D68), invoice number and, for a reminder, the amount outstanding. **Send prepayment reminder** follows up an unpaid or part-paid one.
- **Nothing further after a prepaid procedure (US-06.4.1).** 27 prices the prepaid Procedure at the issued invoice, locked, and leaves it off the final invoice; 27a transfers the amount from the AA Holding account to whoever did the List. This phase pins that end to end and offers the routes by hand: a settlement block (all three apps) showing the prepaid amount as the price, the recorded BTM for reference only and "Nothing is invoiced or credited automatically", with 38b's **Create additional invoice** and 39's **Credit note**, which now accepts a prepayment invoice, in full or (D31) for a stated part. No threshold, no balance invoice, no deduction line.
- **Cancellation settles the prepayment (US-06.5.2, D56).** Cancelling a prepaid Booking, by anyone, credits each issued prepayment invoice in full through 39's credit path (reversing the receivable and the holding-account payable; nothing against an anaesthetist) and, for money received, refunds it in full, paid out by the system from the AA Holding account and mirrored to Xero, in the same commit. A payment that arrives after the credit note is refunded the same way.
- **Contract changed off prepaid (US-06.3.7, D67).** After the prepayment invoice is sent or paid, changing the Procedure's Contract to one not prepaid settles that prepayment as a cancellation does (one shared function, replacing 27's interim warning), and the Procedure is then billed after the procedure under its new Contract.
- **Moved prepaid Booking (US-06.5.4, D38).** No code runs on a move. Tests prove it; the office's move steps carry the honour line; the office may send **Email patient: anaesthetist changed**, never sent by itself.
- **Rebook after a true cancellation (US-06.5.3).** "Rebook with another anaesthetist" creates a new Booking on the replacement's own price list; 27's engine derives a fresh prepayment at their fixed price, awaiting Approve and send. Both amounts are visible.
- **No pricing structure and no ledger account is built here.** The prepaid amount and the left-off rule are 27's; the account, its entries and the transfer are 27a's (extended here with credited and refunded entries, never copied). [AR-28](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-28.md) and [AR-29](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-29.md) `#prepayment` hold, except the v6 editable prepaid price and "a refund may be issued" for a lowered one (not adopted, OQ-96). [AR-52](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-52.md) `#cancellation` and `#pp-transfer` are the money picture.

## Out of scope

- Anything calculated or raised automatically after a prepaid procedure, either way: no balance invoice, automatic credit or refund, threshold, "overpaid" state or deduction line. A partial prepayment.
- A dollar figure computed from the recorded BTM against the prepaid amount.
- The AA Holding account itself, its hold, transfer, trigger, card and ledger legs (27a, 36); only the credited and refunded entries are added.
- Any payee repoint, re-check or recalculation on a move (D38); a patient email sent by a move.
- More than one by-hand credit per prepayment invoice; a stated amount on any other invoice.
- A refund of a by-hand credit (39's option, part or full): that refund happens outside the system (US-08.6.5). Only cancellation, a late payment after it and the Contract change pay out.
- A refund after the List is authorised (the money is with the anaesthetist; 39's credit is the route).
- A cancelled Procedure on a live Booking settled automatically (27's warning, by hand; logged).
- Lifting `canSplitCombined`'s prepaid refusal (US-08.6.4): Phase 44 records it as open.
- Real patient bank details and a bank-feed match for refunds.
- Templates beyond the request, reminder and changed-anaesthetist email; rich text, attachments, per-anaesthetist letters, an outbox, scheduled reminders.
