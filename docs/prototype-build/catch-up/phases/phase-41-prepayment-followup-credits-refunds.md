## Goal

Finish the prepayment story on 27 and 27a. Most of it is Verify, so keep it thin, and build what the catalogue now says: nothing about a prepayment is calculated or raised automatically after it is sent, and nothing in the ledger changes when the Booking moves.

- **Letters (US-06.3.6).** When an admin approves a prepayment to send (27's "Approve and send"), they pick one of a small set of standard letter templates and see a preview. The letter goes out with the invoice. Every template states the prepaid amount as **the price payable before the procedure, never as an estimate** (no "estimate", "deposit" or "may be higher or lower"), and may say that if the procedure turns out very different from what was planned, the anaesthetist may raise a further invoice or a credit. Merge fields: patient, procedure, the prepaid amount, anaesthetist (plus the date, the invoice number and, for a reminder, the amount still owed). Admins maintain the templates in Master data. "Send prepayment reminder" on the Booking sends a reminder letter for an unpaid or part-paid prepayment.
- **Settlement with nothing automatic (FT-06.4, US-06.4.1; OQ-61 and OQ-76 answered).** Phase 27 prices a prepaid Procedure at the prepaid amount, locked, and 27a pays the anaesthetist from the holding account at authorisation. This phase makes that visible and offers the routes by hand. On a prepaid Procedure the Booking (all three apps) shows the prepaid amount as the price, the BTM units recorded on the day for reference only, and "Nothing is invoiced or credited automatically", with two by-hand actions for the anaesthetist (own Procedure) and the office, each recorded as an event on the Procedure and traceable to it: Phase 38b's **Create additional invoice** and Phase 39's **Credit note**, which now accepts a prepayment invoice. Per OQ-97's default (D31) that credit note may be for a stated part of the prepayment invoice, reversing the same amount of the linked payable. There is no threshold, no balance invoice and no "overpaid" state; a mixed Booking follows 27's deduction line (D57). The last July wording ("deposit", "estimate", "balance", "remaining", "top-up", "manual credit") goes from the prepayment surfaces.
- **Refund on cancellation (US-06.5.2).** Cancelling a Booking with a paid prepayment, before its List is authorised, refunds it in full from the holding account: a credit against the prepayment invoice through 39's credit path that reverses the receivable and the payable to the holding account (nothing is reversed against the anaesthetist, who was never paid), a refund due, and the payout recorded from the system (D56, provisional). This is not 39's credit note option, which pays no refund (US-08.6.5: "One's cash, one's not").
- **Moved prepaid Booking (US-06.5.4, D38).** A moved prepaid Booking, as a whole List (32, or the office's reassign) or a single Booking (32a, or the office's move), changes nothing: no code runs on the move. This phase proves it with tests, shows the honour-system line on the office's move steps if 32 and 32a did not, and adds an office action **Email patient: anaesthetist changed**, an optional informational email from a template, never sent by itself.
- **Rebook after a true cancellation (US-06.5.3).** "Rebook with another anaesthetist" on a cancelled Booking creates a new Booking under the replacement. Phase 27's engine derives a fresh prepayment at **their own price list's fixed price**, awaiting Approve and send. The original is refunded, never transferred, and both amounts are visible.
- **No pricing structure and no ledger account is built here.** The prepaid amount, the price step and the deduction live in Phase 27's `domain/billing/prepayment.ts` and 24's price rule; the holding account, its entries and the second pair are 27a's (this phase extends `prepayHolding.ts` with refund entries, never a second copy). The plain-language guide [AR-28](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-28.md) `#prepayment` and [AR-29](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/artifacts/AR-29.md) `#prepayment` hold as written, except that the v6 design's editable prepaid price and "a refund may be issued" for a lowered one were not adopted (OQ-96, OQ-97).

## Out of scope

- Anything calculated or raised automatically after a prepaid procedure, in either direction: no balance invoice, no automatic credit or refund, no threshold, no "overpaid" state (OQ-61 and OQ-76 answered). A partial prepayment (OQ-76: all or nothing).
- A dollar figure computed from the recorded BTM against the prepaid amount: the BTM is units for reference only.
- The prepay holding account itself, its hold until authorisation, its second pair, its balance card and its ledger legs: Phases 27a and 36. This phase only adds the credited and refunded entries and the refunds card.
- Any payee repoint, re-check, recalculation or detection on a move (D38), and crediting and prepaying again when a prepaid Booking moves (OQ-21's reading, superseded): it survives only as the rebook after a true cancellation (item 15). A patient email sent by itself on a move.
- More than one credit per prepayment invoice, and a stated-amount credit on any invoice but a prepayment invoice (OQ-97 is about the prepayment; 39 credits other invoices in full).
- Recovering money from an anaesthetist for a prepayment before authorise: the hold means none was paid. After authorise, a correction is 39's credit, with its negative invoice netted by 39a; with no later payment it is handled outside the system (D21).
- Refunding a credit raised through 39's credit note option, including a part credit of a prepayment: a credit note is not a refund, and that refund happens outside the system (US-08.6.5, note #33). Only the cancellation refund is paid out here.
- A refund of a prepayment after its List is authorised (the money is with the anaesthetist; 39's credit is the route).
- Lifting `canSplitCombined`'s refusal for a prepaid combination (39's handoff, US-08.6.4): not this phase's scope; Phase 44 records it as an open gap against US-08.6.4 unless the owner adds it.
- Real bank details for patients and a bank-feed match for refunds.
- Letter templates for anything but prepayment requests and reminders and the changed-anaesthetist email; rich text, attachments, per-anaesthetist letters, and an outbox. Phase 35's update email and its templates stay separate.
- Automatic or scheduled reminders or patient emails. Both are office actions; 27's escalating warning stays the control.
- Patient-screen views of refunds beyond what Phase 40's patient position already reads from the ledger.
