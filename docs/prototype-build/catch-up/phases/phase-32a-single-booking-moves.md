## Goal

Let an anaesthetist move one Booking as well as a whole List (US-01.4.7, DM-42; Greg: "at the list level or at the booking level"), and build the doer rule on the same move (US-01.4.6).

Today only the office can move a Booking to another anaesthetist (Admin `MoveBookingFlow` over `reassignBooking`, any day, any existing List). For an anaesthetist actor, `reassignBooking` refuses any move to someone else's List (`notOwnList`), and no anaesthetist screen calls it. Nothing finds the receiver and nothing creates a List for them.

This phase:

- adds **Move to a colleague** on a Booking in the mobile and web apps, for the List's owner while the List is ACTIVE and its day is today or later. The sheet finds the receiver two ways: **the list of colleagues free in that session** (shown at once) or **the shared type-ahead box** (names filter as each letter is typed). Colleagues are sorted by name only: availability alone, never ordered or marked by a pairing preference or tier (US-01.4.5, D44, D54). A colleague on leave or unavailable, or who already holds a List in that session, shows when searched for, but cannot be picked. Someone on leave first sets that session available in their own app, which is their acceptance (the office does not hand work to someone unavailable without them knowing);
- **creates a List** in the receiver's free session holding the Booking, even a one-Booking List, copying the source List's hospital, surgeon and kind. A Booking is **never moved into an existing List**, so a colleague whose session holds a List is not offered (two Lists in a session are not supported, and the rule may change later, OQ-85). Whether others may then add Bookings to the new List was not discussed; nothing stops it;
- makes the move at once, as one audited from, to, by, when event (D7). **No preference warning** is shown to anyone, and the move path never reads the office-only preference slice (D44);
- lets the mover **tick "Tell the office"** (optional, off by default), which posts one row to Phase 32's notification pool. The update email is also optional and is Phase 35's on-demand button; nothing here drafts it. The colleague's "Moved to you" notice does not depend on the tick;
- **the payable follows the Booking by derivation** for an unprepaid one (the List's owner, until Phase 25's snapshot fixes it at authorise). For a **prepaid Booking the payable is the prepay holding account**: nothing moves, nothing is recalculated, no prepayment or invoice logic runs, there is **no payee-repoint hook** (D38; D20 superseded), and the second pair at authorisation (27a) pays whoever did the List;
- **is the doer rule.** Whoever submits a List did every procedure on it, so a Booking a colleague will do is moved to the colleague first. The submit sheet says so in one sentence and adds no step;
- replaces the anaesthetist guard in `reassignBooking` (one sanctioned path, the new action);
- reworks the office's `MoveBookingFlow` to the same-day reading (RV-27): targets are an existing List on the Booking's day, or a free colleague's session as a new List, found with the same type-ahead; another day is a cancellation plus a new Booking;
- registers one Admin Day bar trigger and one PWA stand-in, so the office's side and the receiving side can be shown without switching persona.

FT-01.4 then holds in full: a List (Phases 28 and 32) or a single Booking from it (this phase) moves between anaesthetists at short notice with its Bookings, status history and audit trail intact.
