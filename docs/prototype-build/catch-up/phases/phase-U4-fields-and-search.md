One field chrome, one fill rule, one focus ring (PLAN.md U4). Today the same job is built several ways: 35 product selects in about 9 style variants at 48, 44, 36 and about 30px, grey or white; three search boxes in five looks; 20 native date inputs in seven variants; four of seven checkboxes in browser blue; four focus looks, with the browser's own blue ring on every `TextField`, `TextArea` and select; and the Contract chooser's answer options, "No contract" backstops and step markers built twice, once in `ContractRail` and once in `BillingCard`.

1. **One field chrome.** `TextField`, `TextArea`, a new `SelectField`, `NameSearchBox`, `Checkbox` and `Switch` share the chrome `TextField` already has, at 48px in sheets and one compact 36px size for toolbars (question 5), each control carrying one class, `aa-field`, for the focus ring.
2. **One fill rule** (question 4): `fill="well"` (grey) inside white sheets, cards and drawers, `fill="surface"` (white) on the page or the phone canvas. Each component's default keeps its look today; call sites that the rule changes pass the prop and are listed under deliberate changes.
3. **One focus ring** (question 6): `.aa-field:focus-visible` gives a teal border and `--focus-ring` on every field. The admin calendar's crimson ring stays; buttons keep their outline until U5.
4. **One part per job** for date and time entry, number stepping, single choice, chip multi-select, calendar keys and the Contract chooser, so 23, 24, 27, 36, 37 and 39b build on them instead of adding copies.

No redesign: the visible changes are the ones listed in drift-check step 5, each with the README question or bug that approved it.
