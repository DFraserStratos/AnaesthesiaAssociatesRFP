## Findings

### 02.1 - "for every active Anaesthetist" is unenforced: the `active` flag is inert  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** the structure is "created by the system for every active Anaesthetist, for every Day in the rolling horizon, with exactly two Lists per Anaesthetist-Day (AM and PM)" (line 506-508).
- **Built:** `Anaesthetist.active: boolean` is typed (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:150`), seeded `true` for all 14 cast members (`src/domain/seed/cast.ts:45-58`), rendered as an "Active" column and made editable through `editAnaesthetist`'s `AnaesthetistPatch.active` (`src/store/mastersActions.ts:176`, surfaced at `src/apps/admin/screens/MasterData.tsx:175-184`, whose subheading reads "Unit value, contact, GST period and active flag are editable").
- **Gap:** nothing reads the flag for behaviour. The canvas generator is handed every anaesthetist id unfiltered, both at seed time (`src/domain/seed/index.ts:333`) and on every roll-forward (`src/store/clockActions.ts:42`), and the admin day grid roster (`src/apps/admin/AdminApp.tsx:53-59`) does not filter on it. Setting an anaesthetist inactive changes one table cell and nothing else: they keep a full day-grid row and keep receiving two new Lists per day forever.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** only if someone toggles the flag. It is visible and labelled as editable on the Master data screen the demo does show (`visual/shots/a7-04-masters.png`), so an audience question ("what happens when someone leaves?") lands directly on it, and the honest answer today is "nothing".
- **Severity:** cosmetic

### 02.2 - A newly added anaesthetist gets a canvas but never gets a day-grid row  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** the canvas is "created by the system for every active Anaesthetist" and is "a stable grid that exists independently of activity" (line 506-512); REQUIREMENTS D1 makes the growing canvas explicit.
- **Built:** `addAnaesthetist` (`aa-prototype/src/store/mastersActions.ts:241-300`) correctly adds the master row and generates their Lists from today to the horizon end in one audited commit (`anaesthetist.create` + a `canvas.generate` summary), tested at `src/store/mastersActions.test.ts:85-130`. The admin flow exists (`src/apps/admin/flows/AddAnaesthetistFlow.tsx`) and tells the user so: "Adding an anaesthetist extends the canvas forward for them, two list slots a day", then reports "Added. N forward list slots generated (2 per day)."
- **Gap:** the Day view's roster is built by mapping over the **static seed cast array**, not the live master: `const all = ANAESTHETISTS.map((a) => masters.anaesthetists[a.registrationNumber])` (`src/apps/admin/AdminApp.tsx:53-56`, `ANAESTHETISTS` imported from `src/domain/seed` at line 6). Both sort modes ("Roster order" and "A to Z") map from that same array, so a runtime-added anaesthetist has no row in either. Meanwhile the header summary counts distinct anaesthetists from the day's Lists (`AdminApp.tsx:82-86`), so after adding one the strip reads "15 anaesthetists · ..." above 14 rows. The mobile Availability screen reads the live master (`src/apps/mobile/screens/AvailabilityScreen.tsx:35,59`) and does show them, as does Master data, which makes the Day view the odd one out. The flow's own docblock states the opposite of what happens: "so the day grid immediately gains their [rows]" (`AddAnaesthetistFlow.tsx:17`).
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** yes, if the presenter demonstrates growing the canvas, which the Add flow's copy invites. The visible symptom is a count that disagrees with the rows plus a new person who is absent from the office's headline screen.
- **Severity:** notable

### 02.3 - `lastModifiedBy` / `lastModifiedAt` are captured but never shown anywhere  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "lastModifiedBy / lastModifiedAt track the most recent change, given that Cards are mutable from multiple sources (surgeon integration, hospital integration, anaesthetist mobile app) right up to the time of the procedure" (line 557-560).
- **Built:** both fields are required on `Card` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:396-397`) and stamped by the single mutation wrapper in lockstep with each audit entry, including on procedure and billing-line edits which stamp the parent Card (`src/store/mutate.ts:199`). Seed values are deliberate (PROGRESS Phase 01/02 review item 4: not-yet-completed cards stamp the seed clock so nothing reads "modified in the future").
- **Gap:** no user-visible surface renders them. `grep -rn "lastModified" src/apps src/shared` returns nothing, and neither does `grep -rni "modified|updated by|last change" src/apps src/shared`. The mobile card screen, the web card detail, the admin card detail, the review table and the day-view drawer all omit it. The deeper ask (full audit) is answered by the audit viewer and the per-card History modal, but the RFP's own at-a-glance "who touched this last, and from where" summary has no display.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** plausibly yes. The multi-source mutability point is one the RFP presses hard, and "who changed this card, and when" is the natural question on a card screen; today it takes a detour into the audit viewer.
- **Severity:** notable

### 02.4 - The List Status master is a closed six-value enum; "available for emergency" has no row  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** status "is sourced from a master ListStatus table", illustrated as "available, available for emergency, unavailable, on leave (etc)" (line 540-542).
- **Built:** a real master exists (`ListStatus`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:606-610`), seeded with six rows (`src/domain/seed/cast.ts:142-149`) and surfaced on its own Master data tab (`src/apps/admin/screens/MasterData.tsx:451-467`). The keys map onto the RFP's examples reasonably: `free` ~ available, `unavailable` ~ unavailable, `holiday` ~ on leave.
- **Gap:** two parts. (a) There is no status for "available for emergency" / on-call; the demo conveys it as free text in a List note ("Not available / ICU on call", "keep free, on call for ICU tonight" in `visual/shots/a-01-day.png`) and in a day note, so it cannot be filtered, counted or coloured. (b) The master is explicitly "A fixed enumerated set (view only)" (`MasterData.tsx:456`) and the key union is compile-time (`LIST_STATUS_KEYS`, `types.ts:53-61`), so the screen demonstrates a reference table that cannot actually take a new row, unlike the other master screens which all have Add actions.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** an RFP-attentive reviewer might, since the six-status legend is on screen constantly and the RFP names a status the legend lacks. The status vocabulary choice is itself a logged decision (see tensions below), so this reads as a talking point rather than a defect.
- **Severity:** cosmetic

### 02.5 - An informational insurer on the Hospital route cannot be recorded, and is actively cleared  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "insurer (→ Insurer, nullable) is populated when billingRoute = Insurer, and may otherwise be recorded informationally when noted by the hospital under the Hospital route" (line 581-582).
- **Built:** the type is route-agnostic (`Procedure.insurerId?`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:456`) and the read path displays whatever is set, on any route: the capture block folds the insurer name into the procedure context line (`src/shared/capture/BtmCaptureBlock.tsx:94,98`) and the office billing panel shows it (`src/shared/card/OfficeBillingSetup.tsx:42`).
- **Gap:** every write path forbids it. Both route editors set `patch.insurerId = route === 'insurer' && insurerId !== '' ? insurerId : undefined` (`src/shared/flows/EditBillingSetupSheet.tsx:109`, `src/shared/flows/EditProcedureSheet.tsx:52`), so choosing the Hospital route silently deletes an insurer that was recorded for information. The manual card form behaves the same way (`src/shared/flows/ManualCardForm.tsx:105`), the insurer select is only rendered under the insurer route, no seeded procedure pairs a hospital route with an insurer (`src/domain/seed/cards.ts:1116-1124` sets `insurerId` only on the insurer branch), and the integration paths never set an insurer at all.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** unlikely unaided; it needs someone to have read the entity description and ask "the hospital told us the patient is with nib, where does that go?" The answer today is a free-text note.
- **Severity:** cosmetic

### 02.6 - The admin day-view drawer lists Cards by id, not in time order, and shows no times  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "A List will contain multiple Cards, displayed in time order" (line 553-554).
- **Built:** the four views where the list is worked all sort explicitly on `scheduledTime` with an id tiebreak: mobile list detail (`aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/ListDetailScreen.tsx:65`), web list detail (`src/apps/web/screens/ListDetailView.tsx:52`), admin review (`src/apps/admin/screens/ReviewScreen.tsx:61`) and the submit sheet (`src/shared/flows/SubmitListSheet.tsx:40`). All four render the time in the row.
- **Gap:** the shared selector `cardsForList` sorts by `c.id` (`src/store/selectors.ts:71-75`) and the admin day-view List drawer repeats that local sort (`src/apps/admin/components/ListDrawer.tsx:31-33`) while showing only patient name plus completion state, with no scheduled time at all (`visual/shots/a-03-drawer.png`, "CARDS (5)"). Because runtime ids are allocated sequentially (`allocateId` in `src/store/mutate.ts`), a Card added out of chronological order (phone-advice booking, integration S12, or a card moved in via `reassignCard`) lands at the bottom of the drawer regardless of its time.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** the mis-order is hard to see because the drawer prints no times, but that is itself the weaker half of the finding: the office's first click into a List shows a card list with no clock on it, which sits oddly against a section whose Card entity is defined by its time.
- **Severity:** cosmetic

## Deliberate exclusions in this section

- **No `Schedule` or `Day` entity type.** The canvas is one flat `schedule.lists` record keyed by the deterministic slot id `L-{anaesthetistId}-{dateISO}-{session}` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/canvas.ts:45`), with day-level data attached to the date (`dayNotes: Record<IsoDate, DayNote[]>`, `src/domain/types.ts:621`). This is a faithful implementation of the RFP's own framing ("conceptually a single ongoing structure rather than a series of discrete, separately generated calendars", line 519-521) rather than a gap, and the RFP explicitly invites the structure to be challenged (line 491-494). Not treated as a finding.
- **Seeded audit is minimal by decision.** PROGRESS decisions log, 2026-07-23, "Seed audit is minimal": seeding is initial state, not mutation, so only staged lifecycle facts are audited (33 to 37 entries) rather than one entry per seeded row. Consequence for the demo: on a cold load the audit viewer is a wall of `card.complete` plus two `list.submit` rows (`visual/shots/a7-07-audit.png`), and a seeded Card's History modal shows a single line (`visual/shots/a7-08-history.png`), so the RFP's "every create, update, and reassignment" claim (line 567-570) is demonstrated by *doing* something live first, not by opening the viewer cold. Cited, not raised as a gap.
- **Hospital-holiday and availability conflicts are advisory, never hard blocks** (PROGRESS Phase 06 decision 1). The RFP leaves hard-vs-soft open.
- **No hard anaesthetist/surgeon pairing guard on card or list moves** (PROGRESS Phase 06 decisions 8 and 9; 5th review #5). The pairing rule governs booking availability, which is structurally satisfied because the pairing lives on the List; a hard reassignment block would prevent legitimate moves.
- **Scale is narrated, not simulated** (N4; PROGRESS 6th review #9). The demo runs 14 anaesthetists, not 85; the day grid carries a footer note naming the production paging answer (`visual/shots/a-01-day.png`), and the 85 x full-horizon case is proved by test instead (`src/store/canvasRoll.test.ts:198`, ~23,000 Lists inside a 2-second budget).
- **The mobile forward view hides Unavailable sessions** (`src/apps/mobile/screens/ForwardListsScreen.tsx:120`). The anaesthetist's own view is a to-do list; the structural "presence on every Day" claim is demonstrated in the admin day grid and the data inspector.

## Beyond the RFP

- **Deterministic slot-hashed generation.** Each slot derives its own RNG stream from `hash(SEED, anaesthetistId, dateISO, session)` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/slotHash.ts`), so generation is order-independent and rolling the horizon forward day by day is provably byte-identical to seeding the whole horizon at once. This is what lets the roll-forward be demonstrated as a real behaviour rather than asserted (`src/store/canvasRoll.test.ts:62`).
- **A canvas-invariant self-check on screen.** The demo Data inspector states "Canvas invariant holds: exactly 2 Lists per anaesthetist today" and prints today's whole canvas with status, hospital, surgeon, times, card count and state (`visual/shots/demo-data.png`) - an inspectable answer to the RFP's structural claim rather than a slide about it.
- **`ListConflict` as a first-class reconciliation artefact.** The RFP does not model the collision between an approved-leave or hospital-holiday calendar and an already-booked List; the prototype adds a typed advisory conflict (`src/domain/types.ts:265-268`) rendered as an amber badge and border in the day grid.
- **`CoverRequest` on a Free List** (`src/domain/types.ts:277-284`) and the mobile offer-cover flow, adopted from the design mockups (PROGRESS decision 2026-07-21 "New interactions"), giving the Free status an action rather than only a colour.
- **`ListPhoneNote`** (`src/domain/types.ts:295-299`): the office's logged phone call about a List, added because the prototype has no Returned state, so a SUBMITTED List needs a forward-only way to record "we rang the hospital about this".
- **`DayNote`** (`src/domain/types.ts:621-629`): per-date internal office notes, giving the RFP's Day level real data of its own beyond being a key.
