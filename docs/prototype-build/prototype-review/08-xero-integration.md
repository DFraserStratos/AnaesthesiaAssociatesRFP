## Findings

### 08.1 - The ACCPAY's "Buyer Generated Tax Invoice" framing is absent  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "An ACCPAY, owed to the surgeon/anaesthetist ... This is effectively a form of a Buyer Generated Tax Invoice." (line 1400)
- **Built:** the ACCPAY behaviour is complete and correct - drafted at handoff, authorised on payment, disbursed by the payables run - `aa-prototype/src/store/xeroHandoff.ts:242`, `aa-prototype/src/store/payablesActions.ts:84-108`. The Xero sim explains the payable in its own words ("a DRAFT then AUTHORISED ACCPAY (owed to the anaesthetist)", `DemoXero.tsx:110-112`) and the invoice document carries the agency wording ("Billed by Anaesthesia Associates as agent for ...", `InvoiceDocument.tsx:129-131`).
- **Gap:** the RFP's own accounting term for the record never appears. `grep -rni "buyer generated\|buyer-generated\|BGTI" aa-prototype/src docs/demo-guide` returns nothing, so neither the app nor the presenter script uses it. Binding convention 10 asks for RFP vocabulary exactly as the RFP uses it.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** only an accountant in the room, and only if they asked "so is that a buyer-created invoice?" - the concept is demonstrated, the label is not.
- **Severity:** cosmetic

### 08.2 - The InvoiceNumber vs Reference split is not shown on the Xero side  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "Two distinct reference needs were identified, and are kept deliberately separate", with an explicit field table: Xero `InvoiceNumber` holds the engine-generated unique invoice number (the remittance matching key), Xero `Reference` holds the internal CaseReference for tracing and staff display only. (lines 1404-1425)
- **Built:** both values exist and are genuinely separate in the model - `Invoice.invoiceNumber` and `Invoice.caseReference` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:659-661`), allocated separately in the run (`aa-prototype/src/store/billingRun.ts:186-194`, whose comment states the case reference is "display-only, never the remittance key"). The invoice document prints both, with "internal reference only" under the case reference (`InvoiceDocument.tsx:109-113`), and the Xero sim's callout names InvoiceNumber as the reliable matching key (`DemoXero.tsx:65-69`). The demo script walks the audience through both on the invoice (`docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:149-150`).
- **Gap:** the simulated Xero records carry neither field. `XeroAccRec` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:765-774`) has `invoiceId`, `contactId`, `amountDue`, `amountReceived`, `status` and no `invoiceNumber` or `reference`; the sim's Invoices tab renders a number borrowed from the linked billing invoice (`DemoXero.tsx:43`) and has no Reference column at all (headers at `DemoXero.tsx:122`). The repo's own candidate data model puts both fields on the Xero record (`docs/rfp-reference/Data-Model-and-Flow.md:228-234`, `invoiceNumber UK "duplicate-prevention on"` and `reference "internal CaseReference"`), so this is a divergence from our own reading as well as from the RFP table.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** plausibly yes. The Xero sim is exactly the surface an AA reader of that table would point at to ask "so what goes in Xero's Reference field?", and the answer on screen is currently nothing.
- **Severity:** notable

### 08.3 - The ACCPAY's DRAFT to AUTHORISED flip is never printed as a status  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** the ACCPAY is "created in DRAFT status and not yet part of any payables run" (line 1400), and "When the ACCREC (collection invoice) is confirmed paid, the corresponding ACCPAY is set to AUTHORISED so that it is included in the next payables run" (line 1427).
- **Built:** `XeroAccPay.status` is a real `'draft' | 'authorised' | 'paid'` field (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:776-786`), set to `draft` at handoff (`xeroHandoff.ts:242-249`) and flipped on first receipt (`paymentActions.ts:147-155`), pinned by `paymentActions.test.ts` "a full payment marks the ACCREC paid, authorises the ACCPAY, and mirrors the case". The Xero sim's Invoices tab explains the lifecycle in prose and exposes it indirectly through the "ACCPAY authorised" money column, which reads $0.00 while the payable is draft (`DemoXero.tsx:110-137`).
- **Gap:** no surface renders the ACCPAY's own status word. The sim's single per-row `StatusChip` describes the pair's collection state instead, and maps a draft ACCPAY to "Awaiting payment" and an authorised one to "Part paid" (`DemoXero.tsx:201-210`); the billing monitor's pill uses the case's derived label (Handed off / Part paid / Paid / Disbursed, `BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:280-300`); the invoice footer shows only the pair's numbers (`InvoiceDocument.tsx:241`). The demo script's beat 2 asks the presenter to "show ... its draft ACCPAY to the anaesthetist" (`docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:161-165`), and there is no cell on screen saying Draft.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** yes, if the presenter follows the script - the DRAFT-then-AUTHORISED gate is the RFP's mechanism for controlling when AA pays out, so it is one of the things the audience is most likely to want to see change on screen. The money column does move, so the point can be made, but it takes narration rather than being self-evident.
- **Severity:** notable

### 08.4 - The mobile GST peek ignores the anaesthetist's own GST period  [PARTIAL]
- **RFP says:** "A periodic activity summary - aligned to each anaesthetist's individually-set GST period (monthly, bi-monthly, or six-monthly)" (lines 1450-1452)
- **Built:** the period is a real master field (`Anaesthetist.gstPeriod`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:147`, editable in Master data at `MasterData.tsx:182`), and the web Accounts screen defaults the report window to the logged-in anaesthetist's own period and lets all three be selected (`aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:146-166`, screenshot `visual/shots/w-10-gst.png`). The underlying selector is period-agnostic and takes an explicit range (`selectors.ts:704-731`).
- **Gap:** the mobile Balances tab's GST section is hardcoded to the current calendar month - `monthStart = ${todayISO.slice(0, 7)}-01` (`aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:41`) - and never reads `gstPeriod`. For Dr Ben Whitaker, the one seeded `sixMonthly` anaesthetist (`domain/seed/cast.ts:56`), the mobile figure would not match his return period.
- **Would a workshop audience notice:** unlikely in the scripted demo. The persona is Dr Melanie Souter, whose seeded period is `monthly` (`domain/seed/cast.ts:45`), so the mobile window coincides with her period, and the section is honestly labelled "GST this month" rather than claiming to be her return. The mobile is described in the source docblock as a deliberate peek with the web screen owning the period selector (`BalancesScreen.tsx:40`).
- **Severity:** cosmetic

## Deliberate exclusions in this section

- **Bulk hospital remittance allocation and bank reconciliation.** Excluded by the RFP itself (lines 1435-1442: local to Xero's bank reconciliation and the Remittance add-on) and fenced in `docs/prototype-build/phases/phase-10-xero-and-payments.md:90`. The engine-side obligation the RFP does keep, a reliably populated unique InvoiceNumber, is built and narrated.
- **Real Xero API shapes, OAuth, webhook endpoints.** REQUIREMENTS 10 excludes real Xero/API connections; X1 scopes the Xero surface to a clearly badged simulator. Judged accordingly throughout.
- **Idempotency keyed literally by InvoiceID.** The prototype keys the receipts ledger by a per-payment idempotency key alongside an invoice-level fully-paid no-op (`paymentActions.ts:87-98`). This is a recorded refinement, not an omission: X4 specifies "keyed by payment id, alongside InvoiceID idempotency" because InvoiceID-only keying cannot represent successive partial payments, and the 2026-07-24 Phase 10 review-fix entry lists it as considered and not a defect.
- **Illustrative AA service fee.** The 2026-07-29 user decision supersedes `D-payee-amount`: the ACCREC remains the gross customer amount, while the ACCPAY snapshots a clearly labelled illustrative 5% AA service fee and the net amount payable to the anaesthetist. Partial receipts authorise that net payable proportionally and payables disburse the net amount. The rate and GST treatment remain discovery items because the RFP does not specify either.
- **Display-only ACCPAY number.** The `-P` suffixed number satisfying AA's similarity preference is derived at render, not stored on the `XeroAccPay` record. Logged as a simulation convention in the same review-fix entry.
- **Overpayment / credit notes.** An over-receipt is clamped to the balance due (`paymentActions.ts:97`); credit notes are out of scope per the Phase 10 handoff notes.

Not a gap but worth stating for the record: the simulated `XeroAccPay` carries an `accRecId` back-pointer (`types.ts:776-779`) purely so the payables run can read the paired ACCREC's full payable. That is a mock-backend convenience; every user-facing surface pairs the two records through the Billing Engine case, and the Xero sim copy says so explicitly, so the RFP's "not natively linked within Xero itself" story is intact where the audience can see it.

## Beyond the RFP

Delivered on this section's surfaces but not asked for in lines 1388-1557:

- **A triggerable Xero handoff fault with idempotent retry.** "Fail the next Xero handoff" records a readable failure on the case, creates neither half of the pair, and the monitor's Resolve and retry re-invokes the idempotent handoff (`store/xeroHandoff.ts:172-191`, `BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:65-98`). The RFP never asks what happens when the handoff itself fails.
- **A "Xero handoff" pipeline stage with partial/failed states** in the office monitor (`selectors.ts:525-544`), giving the office the completion-and-errors view the RFP asks for in more detail than it specifies.
- **Receivables aging buckets and an over-60-days count** on the anaesthetist dashboard and Overdue table (`selectors.ts:667-682`), above the RFP's flat list.
- **A manual "Run reconciliation poll" presenter button** with a result count, so the safety net can be demonstrated without advancing the clock (`DemoControlPanel.tsx:779-822`).
- **An end-to-end automated audit trail** for the run, handoff, webhook, ACCPAY flip and payables run, all `source:'system'`, reconstructable in the audit viewer (pinned by `payablesActions.test.ts` "reconstructs one invoice automated trail end-to-end, all source=system").
- **A "Prior balance" intake badge** on the monitor row when a returning patient has an unpaid prior episode (`BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:199-203`), which comes from the RFP's contacts appendix rather than this section.
