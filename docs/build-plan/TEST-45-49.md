# Test 45 to 49: the Contracts section

One walk through the Admin app on **5173** that covers Phases 45 to 49 together. Start with **Reset** in
the harness bar, sign in as the office (Kirsty W.), and go in order: later steps build on earlier ones.
The tests already prove the rules (guards, audit, change sets, counts); this list is for how it looks
and feels.

1. **The section.** Side nav, **Contracts** (between Ledger and Master data). Three tabs: Contracts,
   Parties, Funding tags. The catalogue opens grouped by Kind, with the group index on the left and
   counts beside each group.
2. **Grouping.** Switch Group by through Holder, Funding tag, Surgeon and Invoice goes to. Funding tag,
   SXAP: only SXAP Contracts, the index count equal to the rows. Surgeon: Aria Skin and Laser Clinic
   shows under Ms Lim and under Mr Okafor. Search "CAT": matches across groups. Status Disabled shows
   Christchurch Eye Surgery 2025 rate.
3. **Old links.** Paste `/admin/masters?view=contracts&holder=PY-SOUTER`: it lands on the holder
   grouping at Mr Souter. `/admin/masters?view=parties` lands on Parties. Master data shows its "now
   in Contracts" line and no Contracts, Parties or Funding tags views.
4. **A Contract's page.** Open Merivale's **Face lift** (`/admin/contracts/CT-MER-FACELIFT`). Header,
   four tabs with counts, the price ranges table newest first with Change and Procedures. Add a range
   from a past date: it is the top row and the kept-price notice appears. Back returns to the same
   grouping and group you came from.
5. **Cards save one at a time.** On the Face lift, Edit Where it is offered, change a specialty, Save.
   History shows one change with only that field. Cancel on another card discards.
6. **System Contract.** No contract · RVG: its notice, only name, AA code and invoice description
   editable, no Disable and no Replace from a date.
7. **Disable.** Christchurch Eye Surgery RVG: Disable lists the Procedures from today; Keep it active
   changes nothing; Disable disables and the note shows the day; Make active restores it.
8. **New contract.** From the catalogue grouped by Holder at Merivale, New contract: the page opens
   "For Merivale" with four numbered steps. Pick an anaesthetist as holder: Bills is fixed to The
   payer. Prepaid Yes moves the kind to Fixed price. Name it "Abdominoplasty, simple", create it: you
   land on its page. Start another named "Abdominoplasty with liposuction": Already held shows the
   first with "Similar name", and Create still works.
9. **Parties.** Parties tab: grouped by kind; an insurer with no Contracts reads "None". Open
   Christchurch Eye Surgery: edit its billing name and Save (History shows before and after); its
   Contracts are counted by funding tag, and each View opens the catalogue with exactly that many.
   New contract from the Party opens the New contract page for it.
10. **Funding tags.** Funding tags tab: SXAP, HNZ, ACC in order; move one Up and Down. Pick SXAP: each
    hospital's count, and View Contracts opens the catalogue with that many rows.
11. **Set up Replace.** Day view, Tue 1 Dec, Dr Souter's St George's AM List (Mr Hale): open two or
    three Bookings and set each one's Contract to **Unisports Ortho** on the step rail.
12. **Replace from a date.** Open Unisports Ortho (`/admin/contracts/CT-UNISPORTS`), **Replace from a
    date**. Change date 1 Dec 2026, New Contract 97. Read the AA code (UNI-002), the rows (all ticked),
    and the notice "A rate has no dates, so UNI-002 is offered from today". Untick one row, leave the
    old one active, Create UNI-002 and move. You land on UNI-002 with the result line and "Replaces
    UNI-001 from 1 Dec 2026"; the unticked Booking is still on UNI-001; History on both shows the
    replacement. The catalogue grouped by Holder at Unisports shows both.
13. **The to-do.** On UNI-001's page, Demo actions, **Demo: reach the change date**: the clock moves to
    Tue 1 Dec. UNI-001's notice turns amber: "Disable it so it is no longer offered". Day view, rail,
    To-do (Show all if needed): "Disable Unisports Ortho (UNI-001)"; Open Contract lands on its page.
14. **Late disable.** Put another of Mr Hale's later Bookings on UNI-001 by hand, move the clock a few
    days on, then Disable UNI-001: the confirm says "Listed from Tue 1 Dec, the date it was replaced"
    and lists it. Disable: the to-do goes. Make active: it comes back.
15. **Fixed price.** Face lift, Replace from a date: "Only the price changes from a date? Add a price
    range instead." Its link closes the sheet and opens the add-range row.

## Decisions to look at

- **OQ-144 (D90), open:** a replacement priced at a rate is offered from the day it is saved, so both
  Contracts are offered until the old one is disabled; the to-do on the date stands in for a
  scheduled disable.
- **The Disable confirm's red button** (the second `danger` use); several cards may be in edit at once;
  no unsaved-changes prompt anywhere in the section.
- **"Procedures priced"** in the ranges table and **"On this Contract"** count every Procedure not yet
  authorised, past Lists awaiting review included.
- **Similar name:** a match on the first word or half the shorter name's words; Disabled matches shown
  and marked.
- **Prepaid Yes** moves the kind to Fixed price; Already held rows and See all open in a new tab.
- **OQ-145 (D91), open:** a hospital's untagged Contract sits under Special.
- **Catalogue:** Kind is the default grouping, remembered per office user and cleared by Reset;
  system Contracts sit in AA's group; 50 rows a page; Group by is a select.
- **Parties and tags:** counts are Active office Contracts with a Disabled row; surgeons and
  anaesthetists share one group; New party stays a panel; "Not under a hospital" opens the whole tag.
- **Replace from a date:** event fees are copied; every movable row is ticked; leave active is the
  default; a past change date is allowed; a fixed-price copy keeps its ranges; a re-enabled old
  Contract brings its to-do back.
- **The to-do sits in date order,** so on a busy day it can be under "Show all".
