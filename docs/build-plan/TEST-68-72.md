One walk through the app on **5173** that covers Phases 68 to 72 together (one step uses the PWA on
**5174**). Start with **Reset** in the harness bar, sign in as the office (Kirsty W.), and go in order:
later steps build on earlier ones. Steps 5 to 13 play the six Intake demo actions in order: the Okafor
story, Mr Okafor's St George's List for **Wed 25 Nov PM** with **Dr Rutherford**. The tests already
prove the rules (guards, change sets, audit, counts, the badge walk); this list is for how it looks and
feels.

1. **The section.** Side nav, **Intake**: it opens on Arrivals, the feed beside the docked workbench,

## Decisions to look at

- **The badge** counts each waiting arrival once and equals To decide; Needs NHI rows are their own
  items in a Waiting group at To decide's foot; the Patients badge never counts them (D125).
- **Left out** is its own kind; removals come only from a rooms' re-send; a left-out removal is offered
  again by a later re-send; identical re-arrivals stage as Lists that match exactly.
- **No per-row Contract:** placed and added Bookings land with none and wait on the Contracts tab.
- **Update bench:** a tick per Booking, not per field; Apply is all or nothing (a row that cannot apply
  shows why, tick off); the old panel's row corrections, surgeon picker, RVG pick and procedure stack
  are gone (BACKLOG: correcting a row on an Update).
- **Reject** needs a reason (quick reasons as chips) and rejects the whole List; no correction after
  placing (a placed List is edited on its Bookings); OQ-124 open.
- **Placing:** the named anaesthetist's session is suggested only when free (a quiet line otherwise);
  only the same day and session's free sessions are offered; a Draft List takes the PDF's surgeon,
  asked for when not recognised.
- **The Okafor story is Wed 25 Nov PM**, not AM: Rutherford's AM is S2's sickness beat.
- **Unreadable PDF:** an editable draft email recorded in the audit, nothing sent; Dismiss needs a
  reason. The scan lands as an Update with three values to check.
- **Data quality** is a flag only on the arrival; Apply code lives on the patient's page.
- **Sources:** OQ-109 part 1 as seeded (St George's and Southern Cross receiving, Forte not connected);
  last sent is the later of the newest arrival and the stored time; no quiet-source alert; Became and
  State wording as built; the held raw view still reads "Raw hospital row" and "View raw row".
- **Confirm unchanged** stamps each Booking's last-modified, as every hospital match does; no content
  changes.
- **Needs NHI:** Urgent within three days; the chase card's one address (the List's sender, else the
  surgeon's first office contact), the `mailto:` draft and "Requested"; a row with no List reads None;
  Add NHI through the Hub lookup is the one manual path.
- **The stub guard:** the reason's wording and its one home; "1 waiting at intake" in place of "0 of 0
  complete"; the greyed dock with "Tap to see why"; a submitted List left holding only a stub keeps
  "Submitted to office" on the phone while Review refuses it.
- **Help:** the NHI and ethnicity format explanation for Admin only, the try-it boxes dropped, the
  Modulus 24 label for AA to confirm.
- **Old labels:** four new actions keep the run sheet's labels until the sweep ("Send update for a
  matched Booking" sits on the Southern Cross cancellation); the feed row is a styled `role="option"`
  row, not a shared variant.
- **For the sweep:** every Intake recipe, ATLAS row and guide beat naming Matching, the import dialog,
  PDF lists, Waiting for NHI, Data quality or Validators; the new benches, Sources and the stub-only
  reason on mobile and in Review; S1 beat 2's text; S5's stub beat and the "never blocks submit" lines.
