## Goal

Rows waiting for an NHI are worked from the feed with one way to add the NHI, and a List holding only a stub can be neither submitted nor authorised.

- **The Needs NHI bench** (68's stub, in the docked Workbench): the patient as received; the List, its date and how close it is (urgent within three days, as built); a chase card with the one address to chase and the request as a draft the office sends; **Add NHI**, the one path, the Hub lookup sheet (`AddNhiSheet`), which matches the patient's single record. The row then leaves Needs NHI and becomes a Booking in its stub's place, and Intake's count drops by one (68's badge, unchanged here). `WaitingNhiView` folds into the bench and goes.
- **The stub guard** (DM-18) in `store/lifecycle.ts`: `submitList` and `authoriseList` refuse, and `authoriseBlockersFor` gives the blocker, when a List has a standing stub and no Booking that is not cancelled, with one reason from one place; the Review queue shows it with no edit to `ReviewScreen`. `SubmitListSheet` gains a List-level reason line. A stub-only List reads "1 waiting at intake", never complete, in the mobile app, the PWA, the web app and Admin; Mark list completed waits. Resolving or rejecting the stub lifts it; a List with Bookings and a stub is never held up.
- **Validators to Help:** the NHI and ethnicity format explanation moves into the Help panel (`shared/support`), add-only.
