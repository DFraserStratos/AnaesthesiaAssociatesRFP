## Goal

Every surgeon has their own page in People, edited one card at a time, with what they are on beside it; and the surgeon group record is gone from the domain, the store and the seed.

- **The page** (`/admin/people/surgeons/:surgeonId`, the surgeon's id): cards for name and practice, registration number, HPI CPN, specialties and contacts, each with Edit, Save and Cancel, each save one audited change through `editSurgeon`, a refusal shown on its card. No Groups card.
- **Side panel:** the surgeon's private pairings in both directions, the Contracts linked to them (each naming its holder, opening its Contract page) and their next Lists, a few of each with See all.
- **The cut (DM-08):** the `SurgeonGroup` type, `masters.surgeonGroups` and its two seeded groups, the group actions and their retire and rename plumbing, the selectors, the sheet, audit labels, catalogue rows and tests, with a `PERSIST_VERSION` bump. The Party of kind surgeon group (COS, Unisports, Aria, Merivale) and every Contract it holds do not change.
