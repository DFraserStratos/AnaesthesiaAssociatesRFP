## Goal

A dated change that is not a price is one action from the Contract's page. "Replace from a date" opens a sheet that copies the Contract with any setting changed (the ones fixed at creation included) under a new AA code, lists the Procedures dated on or after the change date still on the old one, moves only the ones the office ticks, and either leaves the old Contract Active with a to-do on the date or disables it now. Every step lands in one change set. The link and the date are read back both ways: the to-do appears in the Day view and on the old Contract's page once the date is reached, and a late disable lists the Procedures from the change date. A replacement not priced as a fixed price says it is offered from today; a fixed price changing from a date points to adding a price range; a system Contract offers no Replace.

## Out of scope

- A future-dated or scheduled disable, or any date on a Contract (OQ-144, D90).
- Replacing many Contracts at once (D95); seeding a replacement; moving authorised or unticked Procedures; any automatic move.
- The review flag for Disabled Contracts (D81, `disabledDaysByContract`): it still dates from the disable day; DM-04 keeps the two apart on purpose.
- The missing-NHI list (the other half of the backlog line).
- Capture recipes, ATLAS and the demo guide (owner's sweep).
