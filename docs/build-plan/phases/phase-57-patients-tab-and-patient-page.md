## Goal

The Patients tab of People becomes one list with three filters, and the patient's page moves onto the shared record pattern.

- **The list** (`/admin/people/patients`): searched by name, NHI or date of birth and paged by the shared `Pager` (the 50 cap goes), with the filters **Coming up** (the default: a Booking in the upcoming window), **Needs follow-up** (one row per patient with its reasons, from a new pure `patientsNeedingOffice`) and **All**. A row opens the patient's page; no row carries an action.
- **The count**: the Needs follow-up filter's figure and the People entry's attention count are the same number, the patients on Needs follow-up (`patientAttentionCount` re-based on the new read). The three follow-up tables are retired and `/admin/patients/follow-up` lands on the filter.
- **The patient's page** (`/admin/people/patients/:nhi`), on 53's pattern: the NHI beside the name and no internal id; Details in the side panel with their own Edit, one audited change; the main column holds the balance, the alerts with their actions (Clear warning, Done on a due reminder, keep or use differing details), the invoices across every anaesthetist and the follow-up log. No tabs: the catalogue wins over the design page.
