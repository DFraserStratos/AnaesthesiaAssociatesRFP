## Goal

Each anaesthetist has a page in People, on 53's record pattern, where everything about them is on one screen.

- **Route and frame:** `/admin/people/anaesthetists/:anaesthetistId` (the registration number, which never changes; the old Master data record link already redirects, D100), on `ProfilePage` fluid with the 412px side panel. Back link "Anaesthetists"; the HPI CPN chip stays in the header.
- **Cards, each edited and saved on its own** (53's shared editable card; Edit, Save, Cancel; several may be open at once; a refusal shows on its own card and nothing changes): **Identity** (name and registration number, read-only, and the HPI CPN, mandatory and unique), **Contact** (phone, email), **Bank and GST** (bank account, mandatory, masked at rest; GST number), then **50's Rate and tier card** and **52's Status card** and its **History card**, placed as they are. Each save is `editAnaesthetist` with that card's fields only, so one audited `anaesthetist.update`.
- **Side panel** (53's): their preferred and not-preferred pairings (office-private, add and end as today), the Contracts linked to them (each opens its Contract page) and their next Lists (each opens the List), three of each with See all.
- **Gone:** `EditAnaesthetistSheet` and the Anaesthetists list's row Edit that opened it; Add anaesthetist keeps its flow.
