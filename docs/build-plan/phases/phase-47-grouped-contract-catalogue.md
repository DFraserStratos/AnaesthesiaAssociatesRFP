## Goal

The office finds a Contract by looking at the catalogue the way they think about it. The Contract list becomes a two-pane catalogue: an index of groups with their counts on the left, the chosen group's Contracts on the right under sub-headings, searched by name, holder reference or AA code and filtered Active, Disabled or All. A **Group by** control picks one of six groupings:

- **Holder**, sub-headed by funding tag.
- **Kind** of holder (AA, hospitals, insurers, funders, surgeon groups, surgeons, anaesthetists), sub-headed by holder. The default.
- **Funding tag**, with No tag last, sub-headed by holder, so every SXAP Contract sits together across hospitals.
- **Surgeon**: a Contract linked to two surgeons shows under both, and the ones linked to none sit together at the end.
- **Invoice goes to** (the step rail's first two steps, read through `destinationOf`).
- **Pricing kind**, sub-headed by holder.

The grouping and the picked group live in the URL (`?group=`, `?in=`), so a link can say "Funding tag, ACC". The last grouping is remembered for each office user. Search runs across every group and the index counts show where the matches are. The holder and funding tag selects go: they are the holder and tag groupings now. A row opens the Contract's page; there are no checkboxes and no bulk action.

## Work items

1. **Pure grouping** (new `domain/billing/contractGroups.ts`, exported from `domain/billing/index.ts`; no React, store, clock or RNG).
   - `type ContractGrouping = 'kind' | 'holder' | 'tag' | 'surgeon' | 'destination' | 'pricing'` and `CONTRACT_GROUPINGS` (value, label "Kind", "Holder", "Funding tag", "Surgeon", "Invoice goes to", "Pricing", and the default `'kind'`).
   - `groupContracts(contracts, by, masters)` with `masters = { parties, fundingTags, surgeons }`. It takes the list already searched and status-filtered, so the index counts reflect the search. It returns `ContractGroup[]`: `{ key, label, section?, entries: { contract, subKey, subLabel }[] }`, groups and sub-groups in the order below, entries keeping `contractSearch`'s order within a sub-group. The `key` is an id, never a name, because it goes in `?in=`.

     | Grouping | Group (key) | Sub-heading | Group order |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | `kind` | holder's `PartyKind` | holder | AA, hospital, insurer, funder, surgeon group, surgeon, anaesthetist |
     | `holder` | the holder (`PartyId`); `section` is its kind's plural, for the index | funding tag code, No tag last | by kind as above, AA first, then name |
     | `tag` | `FundingTagId`, or `none` for No tag | holder | `fundingTagsInOrder`, No tag last |
     | `surgeon` | `SurgeonId`, or `none` for "Not linked to a surgeon" | holder | surname (`surnameOf`), `none` last |
     | `destination` | `patient`, `hospital:<PartyId>`, `special` | Patient: Standard or Prepaid; hospital: its tag code; Special: the holder | Patient, hospitals by name, Special |
     | `pricing` | `PricingKind` | holder | rvgAnaesthetistRate, rvgContractRate, rvgDiscount, fixed, open |
