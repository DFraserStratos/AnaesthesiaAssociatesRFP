## Goal

A new Contract is entered on its own page, `/admin/contracts/new` (`?holder=<PartyId>` prefills the holder), filled in the order its rules depend on: **1 Holder and who it bills**, **2 Pricing** (the kind, the rate or first price range, prepaid, whether the anaesthetist may change the price), **3 Where it is offered** (linked surgeons or anaesthetist, specialties, funding tag), **4 Name, AA code, holder reference, invoice description and default RVG code**. A rail beside it shows two things live: **Where it will show** on the step rail (read from `destinationOf`, D91) and **Already held**, the holder's Contracts with a similar name, as a hint and never a block. Create runs the existing save guard and lands on the new Contract's page (Phase 45). `ContractEditSheet`, its warning-only "Replaces" picker and its catalogue rows are deleted.

## Work items

1. **Similar-name helper** (new `src/domain/billing/contractSimilarity.ts` with `contractSimilarity.test.ts`; pure, no React or store; exported through `domain/billing/index.ts` if its neighbours are). `similarContracts(name, held)` takes the typed name and the holder's Contracts (`id`, `name`, `status`, `systemRole`) and returns the matches, same name first, then Active before Disabled, then by name. System Contracts and other holders' never match; a name under three letters matches nothing. Words are lowercased, punctuation dropped, a trailing "s" and the filler words (with, and, the, of, for, a, to, in, on, plus, only) ignored. Two names are similar when they share their first remaining word, or at least half of the shorter name's words. Tests: the criterion ("Abdominoplasty with liposuction" matches "Abdominoplasty, simple"), "Face lift" does not match either, case and punctuation, plural, an identical name, a blank or two-letter name, a Disabled match marked, another holder's name ignored, a system Contract ignored, ordering.
2. **Draft and rules** (new `src/apps/admin/flows/contractDraft.ts`, with tests; Phase 49's Replace sheet reuses it, so it takes an initial value and holds no page code).
