## Goal

An arriving List AA does not hold is read and placed, and a rooms' PDF is shown beside the values read from it. Layout: [intake-ideas.html](../../explorations/intake-ideas.html) `#idea1` (B, a new List from the rooms' PDF), `#reco` (the Slot picker), `#journey` (the rooms email the List).

- **The new List bench, any source** (`workbench/NewListBench.tsx`, 68's stub): the List's title line, a PDF page or the hospital's rows beside what to place, the sender, the placement section, Reject.
- **A PDF's page beside its values** (`workbench/newList/PdfPage.tsx`, `PdfValuesTable.tsx`): each value with how sure the reader was (D120: no score is shown; a value below 68's check threshold reads "to check" and lights its box where it sits on the page); each row's fields corrected before ingest with the from, to, who and when kept (ethnicity included: the data-quality fix for a new List's rows, DM-15, D125); the procedure text kept as received.
- **Placing the whole List as one change set** (new `store/intakePlace.ts`, `workbench/newList/PlaceListCard.tsx`): the named anaesthetist's free Slot suggested first (DM-19; only when that Slot is free for the day and session, otherwise no suggestion), another anaesthetist's free Slot, an existing List to add the Bookings to, or a Draft List holding all the Bookings for its hospital, surgeon, day and session with no Slot changed. A row with no NHI becomes a stub on the placed List (`stubSettle.ts`). The new Bookings land with no Contract, chosen on the Booking afterwards (D125).
- **The unreadable PDF bench** (`workbench/UnreadableBench.tsx`): the page, the reason it could not be made into a List, a draft email to the sender that the office sends, and Dismiss with a reason; it stays in the feed until dismissed.
- **Add a PDF by hand** (`workbench/newList/AddPdfByHand.tsx`, 68's stub that `IntakeSection.tsx` already opens, filled from `PdfUploadDialog`; it sets `ADD_PDF_BY_HAND_READY` to true, which enables 68's action): choose the file, type the sender, and it is read and lands in the feed through 68's mailbox ingest.
