## US-06.3.1 · Raise the prepayment invoice · Partial

[US-06.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-06.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-06.3.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** An admin approves each prepayment invoice under Awaiting approval on the Finances section's Invoices tab, and the system generates and sends it with the prepayment letter. One invoice per payer, for the Procedures' price when raised, addressed to the payer. A switch beside that list turns approval off. The pair is a receivable from the payer and a payable to the AA Holding account. The payer is locked once their own invoice is raised, while a payer whose own invoice is not yet raised can still be changed. A List submit never invoices it again.
- **Prototype today:** Admin Invoices screen has an 'Awaiting approval' tab (?view=approval) holding PrepaymentApprovalStrip: the 'Prepayment approval' Switch and a row per Booking and payer with Approve and send. approvePrepayment (office only) calls generatePrepaymentInvoice, the single place the invoice (kind prePayment, send true, letter rendered), the ledger pair (payee HOLDING_PAYEE) and the Xero ACCREC plus draft ACCPAY to the AA Holding account are made. Groups are per payer via refKey, and each line is the Procedure's price when raised, which becomes its prepaid amount. Nothing is generated until approved. With approval off, autoSendPrepayments sends ready groups at once, on switching off and after each action that can make a group ready. payerLockedByPrepayment is per payer, read from issued invoice lines, and enforced in payerActions, bookingSaveActions and PaidBySheet. billingRun leaves prepaid Procedures off the final invoice (settledByPrepayment), so a List submit does not invoice them again.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Finances section: approval sits on the top-level Invoices nav item (/admin/invoices), not on an Invoices tab inside a Finances section (US-13.3.3, built under its own story). InvoicesScreen.tsx tabs are only 'All invoices' and 'Awaiting approval'.
  - Awaiting approval is a separate tab on the Invoices screen, not one of the filters on a list (US-08.4.7, built under its own story). The switch does sit beside the approval list, as required.
  - Entry links need re-pointing once the section exists: routes.tsx:178 and :228 navigate to /admin/invoices?view=approval (right-rail Pre-payments tile and to-do), and the demo guide S4 Beat 1 click path changes.
  - Dropped from the reviewer's list: OQ-120 (naming the anaesthetist) is open and the story states no rule on it, so the prototype's provisional default (PREPAYMENT_NAMES_ANAESTHETIST = true) is a watch item, not a gap. OQ-73 and OQ-140 are already followed.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Add prepaid Booking' (ADMIN_DAY and ADMIN_BOOKING) stages a prepaid Booking; the Prepayment approval switch and Approve and send are live controls; 'Move to 2 days before procedure' covers timing. Nothing new needed.
