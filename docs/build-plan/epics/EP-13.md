## US-13.4.1 · Maintain reference tables · Contradicts

[US-13.4.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.4.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-13.4.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Reference data is kept in two places. Master data holds hospitals, surgeons and groups, anaesthetists, availability statuses, recurring bookings, the public and hospital holiday calendars, RVG codes and groups, modifier codes and specialties. A Contracts section holds Contracts (with price ranges), Parties and funding tags, each on its own tab. Billing exception: Contract changes are audited edits, fixed prices change by price range, and Procedures keep their stored pricing so issued invoices never change.
- **Prototype today:** Master data (/admin/masters?view=) has a grouped sub-nav registry of 20 views. Contracts, Parties and Funding tags are three views under its Billing group, and masterViews.test.ts maps the catalogue rows for Contracts, Parties and funding tags to them. There is no Contracts nav item, section or route. Contract edits are audited (contract.update, price-range audits) and locked prices protect issued invoices, so the Billing exception behaves correctly. Every other listed master can be added or edited, and retired where it applies.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Contracts, Parties and Funding tags sit in Master data, contradicting the two-places split.
  - No Contracts section: no /admin/contracts route, no side-nav item, no tabs. masterViews.ts still lists parties, fundingTags and contracts in group Billing with their catalogueRows, and masterViews.test.ts maps those rows to Master data.
  - Old ?view=contracts, ?view=parties and ?view=fundingTags links would need redirecting. The same goes for the Party 'active' link (?view=contracts&holder=), the demo-trigger contexts adminMasters.view and adminContracts.openContractId, and the Seed load and Go-live entity sheets that present contracts, priceRanges, parties and fundingTags as Master data.
  - Master data would keep the Billing group only for RVG groups and Modifiers once the three views move out.
  - The title still says reference tables. That is an owner decision recorded in the story Notes, not a prototype gap.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/masterViews.ts:41-43 (parties, fundingTags, contracts under group Billing in MASTER_VIEWS)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/masterViews.ts:14 (MASTER_VIEW_GROUPS includes Billing)`, `searched: contracts in aa-prototype/src/router.tsx and apps/admin/components/SideNav.tsx: no Contracts route or nav item (router only imports admin routes; masters view carries contracts)`, `docs/build-plan/analysis/prototype-map-admin.md:3 and :121 (no Contracts section, route or tabs exist)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/FundingTagsView.tsx:1-30 (Funding tags as a Master data view)`

## US-13.4.5 · Funding tags · Partial

[US-13.4.5](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.4.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-13.4.5) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins keep AA's ordered list of funding tags (SXAP, HNZ, ACC) in step-rail order. A Contract may carry one, and every change is audited. A tag may name its funder, which lets the Booking's funder suggest a path. The list is its own tab of the Contracts section with each tag's funder, and picking a tag shows the Contracts carrying it grouped by hospital with their count. ACs: Order, Contracts under a tag (each hospital with its SXAP count), Funder named (path marked Suggested).
- **Prototype today:** FundingTagsView (Master data, Billing group) lists Tag, Funder (or 'No funder named') and Contracts (one total count per tag) with Up/Down reorder, History, Edit and Remove (refused while any Contract carries the tag). The add/edit sheet has Code and a Funder select grouped by Party kind. Order drives the step rail (fundingTagsInOrder). Funder naming feeds the rail's Suggested marker (shared/contracts/railParts.tsx, domain/billing/contractRail.ts funderPartyId). Changes go through mutate() and are audited, with a History sheet.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not on its own tab of a Contracts section. The section does not exist, and the view is still Master data's fundingTags.
  - No pick-a-tag interaction: selecting SXAP does not show its Contracts grouped by hospital with each hospital's count (AC Contracts under a tag). Only a total count column exists (FundingTagsView.tsx:41, fundingTagUsage).
  - The count does not link into the Contracts list, and that list has a Funding tag select but no hospital grouping or per-tag hospital summary (ContractsView groups by holder only).
  - Status Verify: Donald's 25 Nov decision is not yet discussed with AA (a note, not a prototype gap).
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/FundingTagsView.tsx:41-61 (columns Tag, Funder, total Contracts count; Up, Down, History, Edit, Remove; no selection state or per-hospital breakdown)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/FundingTagsView.tsx:35 (sub copy: tags show in this order when choosing a Contract)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/masterViews.ts:42 (fundingTags under Billing group)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractRail.ts (funderPartyId) and aa-prototype/src/shared/contracts/railParts.tsx (funder-based Suggested)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/masters/ContractsView.tsx:56,94,151 (Funding tag filter only, results grouped by holder)`
