# EP-13 · Admin oversight and master data · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-13) · catalogue `8637b65` · [EP-13](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-13.md)

> All six items graded on code re-read. The prototype has every underlying screen (Billing monitor, Invoices, Ledger, payment cycle, AA fees, Xero queue and divergence panels, reconciliation status) but no Finances container: grep for 'finances' in aa-prototype/src finds nothing, and the side nav has Invoices, Billing monitor and Ledger as three items. The work is structural. First, a Finances section with six routed tabs (Billing, Invoices, Payments, Ledger, Xero, AA fees), tab counts and one warning badge, keeping the old /admin/invoices, /admin/billing and /admin/ledger addresses resolving. Second, the monolithic BillingMonitorScreen must be split: the Xero queue and divergence panels go to Xero, PaymentCyclePanel and the payment-run pages to Payments, AaFeePanel and AaFeesScreen to AA fees, and HoldingAccountPanel to the Ledger list. Third, the Billing tab gets rebuilt as the Problems-first option C table (List heading with Bookings as rows) with Problems/In progress/Done/All filters, search and a fix-the-payer action, which needs a new failure path (payer with no billing email) in the domain, store and seed. Fourth, the Ledger loses the Whole ledger / One anaesthetist switch and gains the four-figure header, a three-way list and per-anaesthetist rows that open account pages. Two items are Contradicts (US-13.2.1 and, corrected from Partial, US-13.3.1) because the prototype implements designs the catalogue now says were considered and not kept. OQ-149 (where an unplaceable receipt is placed) is open and touches the Ledger place/refund actions, the receipts-to-place figure and the reconciliation unresolved items, so build those provisionally. US-13.2.4 is Proposed and waits on the posting design (clearing account, sub-ledger against control account), which the code header says is deliberately not modelled. Demo triggers: billing-failure and arm-handoff-fault (screen BILLING_MONITOR_SCREEN), xero-outage and inject-unmatched-receipt (screen 'Admin · Ledger') are keyed by screen and must be re-pointed at the new tab routes; add 'Payer loses billing email' for the fix-the-payer beat, and optionally 'Leave money in clearing account' on the Day screen if the extra reconciliation checks are built. Demo-guide impact (docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md): S3 Money end to end (Billing monitor, AA fee invoices and Ledger beats, plus the direct-URL table) and S4 Exceptions (Holding account card, billing failure, Xero outage, changed-in-Xero, refund after payout, payment week) change navigation and wording; the Ledger beat changes most. The demoGuideSync test, capture recipes and ATLAS belong to the owner's sweep, not this work. Read-only; no files modified.

- **Requirement:** Admins keep AA's ordered list of funding tags (SXAP, HNZ, ACC) in step-rail order. A Contract may carry one, and every change is audited. A tag may name its funder, which lets the Booking's funder suggest a path. The list is its own tab of the Contracts section with each tag's funder, and picking a tag shows the Contracts carrying it grouped by hospital with their count. ACs: Order, Contracts under a tag (each hospital with its SXAP count), Funder named (path marked Suggested).
- **Prototype today:** FundingTagsView (Master data, Billing group) lists Tag, Funder (or 'No funder named') and Contracts (one total count per tag) with Up/Down reorder, History, Edit and Remove (refused while any Contract carries the tag). The add/edit sheet has Code and a Funder select grouped by Party kind. Order drives the step rail (fundingTagsInOrder). Funder naming feeds the rail's Suggested marker (shared/contracts/railParts.tsx, domain/billing/contractRail.ts funderPartyId). Changes go through mutate() and are audited, with a History sheet.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not on its own tab of a Contracts section. The section does not exist, and the view is still Master data's fundingTags.
  - No pick-a-tag interaction: selecting SXAP does not show its Contracts grouped by hospital with each hospital's count (AC Contracts under a tag). Only a total count column exists (FundingTagsView.tsx:41, fundingTagUsage).
  - The count does not link into the Contracts list, and that list has a Funding tag select but no hospital grouping or per-tag hospital summary (ContractsView groups by holder only).
  - Status Verify: Donald's 25 Nov decision is not yet discussed with AA (a note, not a prototype gap).

## US-13.3.3 · The Finances section · Missing

- **Requirement:** One Finances side-nav entry with six routed tabs (Billing, Invoices, Payments, Ledger, Xero, AA fees), opening on Billing, each tab at its own address. Tab numbers count what needs doing (Invoices: prepayment invoices awaiting approval; Ledger: failed checks; Xero: alerts and divergences; Payments and AA fees none until due). One warning badge on Finances sums billing problems and failed ledger checks. Records (invoice, credit note, payment run and remittance advice, anaesthetist account) open on pages under their tab with a way back. Earlier addresses still resolve.

Routes /admin/invoices, /admin/billing (aa-fees and payment-runs beneath it) and /admin/ledger exist; the payment cycle is a panel on the Billing monitor and AA fees a sub-screen. Record pages exist for invoice, credit note, payment run, remittance advice and the anaesthetist ledger, but not under Finances tabs.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Finances nav entry, no /admin/finances routes, no tab strip, no opens-on-Billing default

  - Record pages are not nested under Finances tabs (credit notes map to Invoices via sectionForPath; an unknown payment run redirects to Billing)
  - Earlier addresses (/admin/invoices, /admin/billing, /admin/ledger) must keep resolving to the same content after the move
