## US-01.1.1 · Two Slots per anaesthetist per day · Partial

[US-01.1.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-01.1.1) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: domain, store, admin, seed · confidence high

- **Requirement:** The Scheduling Engine writes an AM and a PM Slot, free, for every active anaesthetist for every day across the rolling horizon even with nothing booked, then paints the anaesthetist's calendar and recurring bookings on top; the third OOH Slot is office-opened only; an anaesthetist made inactive has no Slots after their last day and Slots again from the day made active (US-12.1.6).
- **Prototype today:** domain/seed/canvas.ts generates AM+PM for active anaesthetists only, all Free, paints leave and series, then recurring bookings, with clashes becoming Draft Lists. The same path serves seed, clock roll-forward, new anaesthetist, reactivation and horizon extension. OOH is never generated. The Active/Inactive toggle in EditAnaesthetistSheet calls editAnaesthetist, which uses anaesthetistCanvasDelta from state.clock.todayISO. Inactive removes Slots from today and is refused if Lists exist from today; reactivation regenerates from today. There is no last-day date, reason, clearable or scheduled inactivity, and no date-driven trimming.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No 'last day': Slots are removed from today the moment Inactive is saved, rather than kept until a chosen last day
  - The refusal ('N Lists from today. Reassign them first.') is keyed to today, not to Lists after the last day, and the office is not shown which Lists to reassign
  - No way to clear a future last day, and no reason captured (US-12.1.6)
  - Reactivation always regenerates from today, not from a chosen 'active again' date
  - Inactive status is not date-driven, so rolling the demo clock past a last day would not trim Slots
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/canvas.ts:116 (active filter), :122-137 (AM/PM, default Free), :140-175 (leave, series, recurring painted, clash handling)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/slotActions.ts:970-1001 (anaesthetistCanvasDelta: active flag, removes Slots from fromISO)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/mastersActions.ts:369-410 (canvas follows active from todayISO; 'listsAhead' refusal; canvas.trim and canvas.generate audit)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/EditAnaesthetistSheet.tsx:128-130 (Active/Inactive Segmented, 'Their sessions from today are removed.')`, `searched: lastDay\|inactiveFrom in aa-prototype/src -> only unrelated AdminApp date ref and a mobile calendar state`

## US-01.6.1 · Create a Draft List · Partial

[US-01.6.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.6.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-01.6.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** AA staff create a Draft List (phone, email, PDF) or make one on the matching screen from an arriving List with no anaesthetist; hospital, surgeon, day and session required; no anaesthetist and no Slot taken; Bookings can be added before assignment. AC: on the matching screen, a Draft List is saved with the arrival's hospital, surgeon, day and session and its Bookings, and no Slot changes.
- **Prototype today:** createDraftList (office only) requires hospital, surgeon, day within horizon and not past, session, kind and how it came in; inserts a DRAFT List with no anaesthetist or slot and touches no Slot; audited draftList.create. The Draft List drawer has Add Booking. On Intake Matching, 'Create a Draft List' (row without a match) runs createDraftList with the row's hospital, surgeon, date and session, then creates only that row's Booking on it. The sample PDFs hold three rows each, so the Draft List holds one Booking after the decision and the other two rows are decided one by one.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - From Intake, the 'Create a Draft List' decision is per row: it saves the Draft List with one Booking, not 'its Bookings' (the arriving List's whole set) in one act. The matching screen has no arriving-List grouping (no whole-List decision, matchingActions.ts createDraft branch handles one row).
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Intake Matching 'Create a Draft List' on a row with no match (from the Okafor or Whitford PDF upload, 'matching-receive-theatre-list', 'simulate-incoming-request'). After the fix, one decision on the PDF should leave a Draft List holding all of its rows' Bookings; no new control.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/matchingActions.ts:523-540 (createDraft branch: createDraftList then createOnList for the single row)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/matching/RowPanel.tsx:203-208,246-248,777-782 (option title 'Holds this Booking until the office assigns an anaesthetist'; surgeon picker only when none recognised)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/intake/pdfLists.ts:67-98 (each sample PDF has three rows, each decided separately)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/draftListActions.ts:97-161 (createDraftList: required fields, no slot, audit)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:934-939 (DraftList has no anaesthetistId or slotId)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/DraftListDrawer.tsx (Add Booking before assignment)`, `searched: arrivingList\|ArrivingList\|groupBy in apps/admin/matching, domain/intake, store -> no whole-List decision`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Matches. The reviewer graded Matches while admitting the gap. The rubric for Matches is ALL acceptance criteria; the last AC is new (26 Nov) and says the Draft List holds 'its Bookings' from the matching-screen act. Confirmed in code that the act saves one row's Booking and that the sample PDFs carry three rows, so the AC is not demonstrated as written. The manual-creation parts (required fields, no Slot, Bookings addable, audit) all check out. Corrected to Partial; fix largely rides on the US-02.1.2 whole-List rebuild, standalone M (loop the arriving List's rows, with stub and wrong-NHI rows still handled).
