# EP-04 · Contracts · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-04) · catalogue `8637b65` · [EP-04](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-04.md)

> US-04.1.2 is split across phases: 45 (Disable confirmed, built), 46 (New contract page, order, similar-name hint, deletes the Replaces section), 47 (grouping, index, facets; closes the story). The remaining gap, the inactive anaesthetist Contract cascade and offer-back, is covered by none of 46 to 49. It depends on US-12.1.6 (anaesthetist last day and inactive state) and so belongs with the People work (Lane C domain and store half). It reuses disableContract and enableContract with a system actor, and adds a Contract-restore sheet on the anaesthetist record. The sheet can wait until Contracts files settle. OQ-144 (future-dated disable) is unresolved; the story says a Contract has no dates, so disableFromISO stays 'today', and any answer allowing a future-dated disable would contradict the No dates criterion. Shared files (ContractsView, ContractEditSheet, screens/contracts) are Lane A/B territory. The recipes using contract-holder-filter and contract-tag-filter change in Phase 47 and are left to the owner's sweep.

## US-04.1.2 · Create, edit and disable Contracts · Partial

[US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-04.1.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins create, edit and disable Contracts in the Contracts section. The list is searched (name, holder ref, AA code), shows Active by default (Disabled/all on request), is groupable six ways (holder, kind of holder, funding tag, surgeon with a two-surgeon Contract under both, where the invoice goes, pricing kind) with a group index. A new Contract is a page in a set order (holder and who it bills, pricing, where offered, then name/codes/invoice wording) with a similar-name hint. Disable is confirmed and lists Procedures from today; nothing moves; the Contract has no dates; holder, prepaid and pricing kind are fixed; an anaesthetist made inactive has their own Contracts disabled after their last day and offered back ticked on reactivation.
- **Prototype today:** Contracts section at /admin/contracts with search (name, holder ref, AA code, default RVG), Active (default)/Disabled/All pills, a Holder select and a Funding tag select, rows under holder headings only; a row opens the Contract page. New contract is a create-only side panel (ContractPanel) whose sections run Contract (name), Invoice and codes, Holder and who it bills, Where it is offered, Pricing, then a warning-only 'Replaces' section; no similar-name hint. Who it bills is forced to payer for AA or anaesthetist holders and prepaid. Disable opens a ConfirmSheet listing Procedures still on it from today; nothing moves; Make active is immediate. Disabled Contracts leave the step rail (contractRail.ts:112). Contract has status only, no dates. contractDisabled review flag uses the disable day from audit, never a block. holderFixed, prepaidFixed and pricingKindFixed refusals exist. An anaesthetist's active flag only affects sessions/canvas (mastersActions.ts:369-375); no Contract cascade, no last day field.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Group-by control: only holder headings. No grouping by kind of holder, funding tag, surgeon (two-surgeon Contract under both), where the invoice goes or pricing kind, and no group index with counts (Grouped and Two surgeons criteria unmet; Phase 47)
  - Only Holder and Funding tag filters; no surgeon, destination or pricing-kind facets (Phase 47)
  - New Contract is a side panel, not its own page, and has no rail showing where it will appear in step-rail terms beyond a one-line placement hint, and no similar-name hint listing the holder's like-named Contracts (Similar name criterion unmet; Phase 46)
  - Field order differs from the story: the panel starts with name, codes and invoice wording, then holder and who it bills, then Where it is offered BEFORE Pricing; the story says holder and who it bills, then pricing, then where offered, then name, codes and invoice wording (New contract order; the forced-payer rule itself works; Phase 46)
  - The create panel still carries the older warning-only 'Replaces' section, which stores nothing (Phase 46 deletes it; replacement is US-04.1.8 in Phase 49)
  - Inactive anaesthetist: nothing disables their own Party Contracts linked to them once their last day passes, the anaesthetist has no last day, and reactivating offers nothing back ticked with prices (Inactive anaesthetist criterion unmet; not in Phases 46 to 49; belongs to the new People work and US-12.1.6)
- **Demo trigger:** Inactive-anaesthetist cascade: on the Admin anaesthetist record (People section once built), a 'Make last day passed' control that sets their last day to today and disables the Contracts held by their own Party and linked to them, so the Contracts list then shows them under Disabled; making them active again opens an offer-back sheet with each Contract ticked and showing its price, and Confirm makes them Active.

## US-04.1.7 · A page for each Contract · Partial

  - Refusal reasons appear in a form-level error, not on the card; the reason text itself exists (holderBillingDetails)
  - Replace is not offered (and so not blocked for system Contracts), pending US-04.1.8

## US-04.1.8 · Replace a Contract from a date · Partial

[US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-04.1.8) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, domain, seed, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Replacing a Contract from a date creates a copy with changed settings (fixed-at-creation ones included), lists on-or-after-date Procedures still on the old Contract (not those on an authorised List), moves only the ticked ones as audited Procedure changes, then leaves the old Contract active with a to-do on that date or disables it now; a later disable lists Procedures from the change date; a non-fixed-price replacement is offered from the save day and the action says so; a system Contract cannot be replaced.
- **Prototype today:** On create only, an optional Replaces select and From date feed proceduresStillOnContract to show a warning table with Open links ('Nothing moves by itself'); nothing is stored, no copy is made, nothing moves. A disabled Contract lists Procedures from its audit disable day (or today), not from a change date.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Replace action on an existing Contract; no copy-with-changes (new AA code, every other setting carried, holder, pricing kind and prepaid changeable)
  - No tick-to-move Procedures and no audited per-Procedure move from the action (a move is manual per Booking)
  - No leave-active option with a to-do on the date (no rule in domain/warnings/rules, no store field) and no disable-now option
  - The change date is stored nowhere, so a late disable cannot list Procedures from it (stillOnDisabled uses the audit disable day, which for a disable on 8 Jan would miss a 5 Jan Procedure)
  - No 'offered from today, not from 1 January' message for a rate or discount replacement
  - Replace is not blocked for system Contracts (no action exists)
  - Locked-left-out is only partly met: proceduresStillOnContract skips AUTHORISED Lists, which is correct, but nothing uses it for a move
- **Demo trigger:** Reuse the existing clock-advance store action (advanceClockToDate, as used by the prepayment trigger) in a new 'Demo: reach the change date' button on the Contracts section, shown only when a Contract has a pending replacement: it moves the demo clock to the change date so the 'Disable [old Contract]' to-do appears in the Day view To-do and on the Contract page while the old Contract is still offered until disabled.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/ContractEditSheet.tsx:136-144 (stillOnReplaced, stillOnDisabled from audit day), ~507-540 (Replaces section, create only)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractChanges.ts:43-60 (proceduresStillOnContract skips cancelled, AUTHORISED, earlier-dated), :143 (NOTHING_MOVES)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractHistory.ts:81 (contractDisabledOn)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/rules/ (no contract-disable reminder rule)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/clockActions.ts (advanceClockToDate exists, imported at shared/demoTriggers/registry.ts:227)`, `searched: replacedBy\|replaceContract\|replacedFrom\|effectiveFrom in src -> none relevant`
