## US-10.2.7 · Weekly payment cycle · Partial

[US-10.2.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.2.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.2.7) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: admin, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Payments run on a weekly ISO-week cycle (Friday close and bank reconciliation, Monday sort-out, Tuesday schedule to the accountant, anomalies rolled to the next cycle). The office runs it from the Payments tab of the Finances section (steps, what is waiting, Close the week and after, events awaiting the run, every earlier run); each run has its own page under the Payments tab with each anaesthetist's remittance advice. Criteria: week number, anomaly rolled over, run page.
- **Prototype today:** PaymentCyclePanel (a card on the Billing monitor) shows 'Payment cycle · Week N', the steps, what is waiting, anomalies with fix or 'Hold out of this run' (rolled into next week with a 'Rolled from Week N' pill), 'Run payables in Xero', and RunsList of earlier runs; the events-awaiting-run notice is on the Billing monitor too. A payment shows its week ('Paid out in Week N' on the invoice document; Ledger rows). A run opens at /admin/billing/payment-runs/:runId with the run, week number, ledger at close, totals by anaesthetist and a 'Remittance advice' link each (after approval) to /payment-runs/:runId/remittance/:anaesthetistId. All three acceptance criteria are behaviourally met. The side nav has separate Invoices, Billing monitor and Ledger entries; there is no /admin/finances route or Payments tab.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Finances section or Payments tab: the cycle, events awaiting the run and earlier runs sit on the Billing monitor (/admin/billing), not on a Payments tab with its own address (depends on US-13.3.3).
  - The run page's back link reads 'Billing monitor' and goes to /admin/billing; it should return to the Payments tab, and the run and remittance addresses should sit under Payments (old /admin/billing/payment-runs/* addresses must still open, per US-13.3.3).
  - Not in the 10.2.7 text itself (it is US-13.3.3's rule): the Payments tab shows no number when nothing is due; the cycle has no tab count today.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Stage a payment week' (stage-payment-week), 'Stage a bank mismatch' (stage-bank-mismatch), Close the week, 'Hold out of this run' and 'Run payables in Xero' on the Billing monitor; after the move they appear on the Payments tab (their route lists in registry.ts need updating). No new trigger needed.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/PaymentCyclePanel.tsx:101-232 (steps, close, approve, send, run in Xero, RunsList at :223), :290 and :307 ('Rolled from' pills), :370 and :408 ('Hold out of this run')`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/BillingMonitorScreen.tsx:810 (events-awaiting-run notice on the Billing monitor)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/PaymentRunScreen.tsx:46-52 (back link 'Billing monitor' to /admin/billing), :75-103 (totals and Remittance advice links)`, `aa-prototype/src/router.tsx:190-196 (billing/aa-fees and billing/payment-runs routes under billing)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/InvoiceDocument.tsx:494 ('Paid out in Week N') and LedgerScreen.tsx:406`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/SideNav.tsx:81-85 (Invoices, Billing monitor, Ledger separate; no Finances)`, `searched: finances in aa-prototype/src (ts, tsx) -> none`

## US-10.3.1 · Generate AA fee invoices · Partial

[US-10.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.3.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, shell, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** An admin raises every anaesthetist's AA fee invoice for the month in one run from the AA fees tab of the Finances section, which lists fee invoices opening on the unpaid ones with the month's run and preview beside them; AA fee invoices are not on the Invoices tab. Criteria: $700 fee total, preview raises nothing, opens on unpaid first.
- **Prototype today:** AaFeesScreen (/admin/billing/aa-fees, 'Fee invoices' sub-tab) has a month stepper, a preview table per anaesthetist (BCTIs paid, fixed, per-BCTI, fee ex GST, Invoiced or 'Not yet'; nothing is raised until 'Run monthly fee invoices') and, below, 'Every fee invoice' with Paid/Unpaid pills and expandable detail. Each raised invoice is an aaFee ledger pair with no payable. aaFee.test.ts reproduces $700 (and $805 with GST). The list is sorted newest month first (allAaFeeInvoices), mixing paid and unpaid. Fee invoices are not on InvoicesScreen. Provisional note cites OQ-29/OQ-60.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not an 'AA fees' tab of a Finances section (no Finances section): it sits at /admin/billing/aa-fees, reached from the Billing monitor, with no own side-nav tab.
  - 'Opens on unpaid' criterion not met: allAaFeeInvoices sorts by month descending then raised date, with no unpaid-first ordering or default unpaid filter.
  - Layout: the run and preview sit above the list on the 'Fee invoices' sub-tab rather than the list leading with the run and preview beside it.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Seed a month of BCTIs' (seed-month-of-bctis) and 'Run scheduled month-end fee run' (run-scheduled-fee-run) on the AA fees screen, and 'Record fee payment' (record-fee-payment) on a fee pair in the Xero simulation to make some fee invoices paid.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/AaFeesScreen.tsx:46-62 (AdminPage title and two sub-tabs), :64-175 (month stepper, preview table, run button), :188-250 (FeeInvoiceList, 'Every fee invoice')`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:1639-1654 (newestFirst, allAaFeeInvoices)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/aaFee.test.ts:15-21 ($700 example)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/ledger.ts:166 (newAaFeePair)`, `aa-prototype/src/router.tsx:192-193 (billing/aa-fees routes)`, `searched: aaFee\|aa-fee\|aaFees in apps/admin/screens/InvoicesScreen.tsx -> none`

## US-10.3.3 · AA fee settings · Partial

[US-10.3.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-10.3.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-10.3.3) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins maintain the fixed monthly fee items (several allowed) and the per-invoice charge on the AA fees tab of the Finances section, beside the fee invoices and the month's run. The run uses these settings so a rate change needs no code change. Criteria: fee total = fixed items plus rate times BCTIs; a new rate applies at the next run.
- **Prototype today:** FeeSettingsTab (/admin/billing/aa-fees/settings) lets an admin add, edit and remove fixed items and edit the charge per BCTI paid (ex GST), with a live worked example, validation and save via saveAaFeeSettings; changes apply from the next monthly run and raised invoices keep their own settings. The run reads appSettings.aaFee through aaFeeFor, not code. aaFee.test.ts covers the sum and a changed rate ($740). Seed sample: $350 + $150 fixed plus $5 per BCTI.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Placement: settings are a second sub-tab ('Fee settings', its own URL) of the Billing-section AA fees screen, not on the same AA fees tab beside the fee invoices and the run, and not within a Finances section.
  - Whether fixed items vary by anaesthetist is open (OQ-60); the prototype keeps one practice-wide schedule, consistent with the story text, so not a gap.
