> All three gradings are upheld after re-opening the code. FT-12.1 is a pure Grouping. US-12.1.4 and US-12.1.6 both Contradict, because two older readings are implemented: the tier saves on click, and active is an immediate boolean that trims Slots from today. Each is large (size L) on its own. Lane placement. The domain, store and sign-in half of US-12.1.6 fits Lane C in Wave 1, because it touches none of the Contracts-owned files. That half covers: - a last-day date and reason on the Anaesthetist type; - audited make-inactive, clear and make-active actions; - Slots trimmed only after the last day; - sign-in checked against the master record; - own-Party Contracts disabled and offered back. The new Anaesthetist fields need a PERSIST_VERSION bump and a seed review. Moving the tier into a card with the rate is also Lane C-safe as store and domain work. The per-card Edit/Save, the side panel and the People move itself need the record pattern lifted out of ContractCards.tsx and ProfileChrome.tsx, plus router/SideNav/masters redirect changes, so they wait for Wave 1 to join. Interactions to coordinate: - Disabling and re-enabling an anaesthetist's Contracts uses disableContract and enableContract from Phase 45. Phase 49 (Replace a Contract from a date) also changes Contract status, and the Contract identity there is linkedAnaesthetistId plus the holder Party. - The shared anaesthetistSession.ts is in the PWA closure and must stay pure (pwaPurity.test.ts). Reading active from the master record therefore needs a small injected check or a store-side hook, not an import from the store. - OQ-147 and OQ-83 are open, so departed-anaesthetist remittance access stays narrated. - The Anaesthetists list also needs search, tier filter and paging, which are absent today. Demo guide: S1 to S5 do not use make-inactive or the tier control, so the impact is additive (a new beat on the demo clock). The Dr Sharma record path in the guide changes with the People move, which is for the sweep.

## US-12.1.4 · Anaesthetist identity, contact and bank details · Contradicts

[US-12.1.4](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.1.4) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, domain, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each anaesthetist has a People-section page keyed by registration number, with cards for name/registration/HPI CPN, contact, bank, and rate plus tier together. Each card is edited and saved on its own as one audited change, and the tier is seen and set only by the office. A side panel shows pairings, linked Contracts and the next Lists (a few, with see all), and the page offers make-inactive. The Anaesthetists list is searched, filtered (by tier among others) and paged. HPI CPN and bank details are mandatory.
- **Prototype today:** AnaesthetistRecord sits under Master data, with a back link to the Anaesthetists master view. Its header has an HPI CPN chip and an 'Edit details' action. A separate Priority tier card uses a SlidingSegmentedControl whose onSelect calls setPriorityTier immediately, with its own audit entry. One Profile card (rate, status, phone, email, HPI CPN, masked bank) is edited through the single EditAnaesthetistSheet, which saves every changed field as one anaesthetist.update. Below are the office-private pairing preferences. HPI CPN uniqueness and presence, and bank details, are enforced in editAnaesthetist. AnaesthetistsView is a plain sorted table with a Tier column, no search, no filter and no pager.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: the tier saves immediately on a click (changeTier -> setPriorityTier), but the criterion says picking a tier without Save changes nothing, and the tier is edited with the rate on one Edit/Save card.
  - WRONG: rate per unit and tier sit on different cards written by different actions and audit entries (anaesthetist.update vs anaesthetist.tierSet), where the requirement puts them on one card as one audited change.
  - WRONG: there is no per-card Edit/Save. One EditAnaesthetistSheet edits rate, phone, email, HPI CPN, bank, GST, start date and status together, so 'one card at a time, one audited change per card' is not met. The per-card pattern exists only on the Contract page (ContractCards.tsx).
  - Missing: separate cards for name/registration/HPI CPN, contact and bank. Today there is one Profile card, with the HPI CPN also in the header chip.
  - Missing: the side panel. The page shows no pairings-plus-Contracts-plus-next-Lists panel and no 'see all'. Pairings are a section below the cards, and nothing on the page shows linked Contracts (Contract.linkedAnaesthetistId) or Lists.
  - Missing: the page is in Master data (/admin/masters/anaesthetists/:id, back to the Anaesthetists master view), not a People section. This waits for the later wave.
  - Missing: a make-inactive action on the page (US-12.1.6). Status is a segmented control inside the edit sheet.
  - Missing: Anaesthetists list search, filter (tier and others) and paging. The view renders every row.
  - Matches already: HPI CPN and bank details mandatory, bank details held in the system and masked on the page, the tier shown only in Admin, and audited changed fields.

## US-12.1.6 · Make an anaesthetist inactive · Contradicts

[US-12.1.6](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.6.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.1.6) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, mobile, pwa, domain, store, seed, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** From the anaesthetist's page the office makes them inactive by choosing a last day and a reason. Until that day nothing changes. After it they have no Slots, are not offered or picked (assignment, availability finder), cannot sign in, and their own-Party Contracts linked to them are disabled. History stays readable. It is refused while they hold a List after the last day (those Lists are shown). The last day can be cleared before it arrives. After the last day they can be made active again (Slots from that date, sign-in restored, disabled Contracts offered ticked with price and re-activated). Make inactive, clear and make active are each audited.
- **Prototype today:** Anaesthetist.active is a boolean with no date or reason field (searched lastDay\|inactiveFrom\|inactiveReason\|inactiveSince in src, no domain hits). EditAnaesthetistSheet has a Status Active/Inactive segmented control, and editAnaesthetist on an active change immediately removes Slots from today (canvas.trim) or regenerates them (canvas.generate). It refuses with listsAhead ('N Lists from today. Reassign them first.') when any List is from today onward. Inactive anaesthetists are skipped in the roster, finder, draft-list candidates, move targets and the pairing add sheet. disableContract and enableContract exist but are not linked to the anaesthetist. The anaesthetist app sign-in is a per-browser localStorage flag (shared/session/anaesthetistSession.ts) that never consults masters.anaesthetists[].active. The record page shows only profile, tier and pairings, with no Lists, Bookings, invoices or payments (only the audit History sheet).
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: inactivity takes effect immediately (Slots trimmed from today). The requirement keeps Slots, offering and sign-in until a chosen last day.
  - WRONG: the refusal is 'Lists from today'. The requirement refuses only for Lists after the chosen last day and shows the office those Lists.
  - Missing: last-day date and reason fields, and a dedicated Make inactive action on the page. Today it is a Status toggle in the edit sheet with no reason.
  - Missing: clearing the last day before it arrives.
  - Missing: sign-in refused after the last day (anaesthetistSession is a browser flag with no link to the master record).
  - Missing: disabling the anaesthetist's own Contracts (own Party, linkedAnaesthetistId) on the last day, so they are not offered on Lists.
  - Missing: make active again from a date, with an offer of the disabled Contracts each ticked and showing its price, re-enabling the ticked ones.
  - Missing: separate audited entries for make inactive, clear last day and make active. Today it is one anaesthetist.update field change plus canvas.trim/generate.
  - Missing: history readable on an inactive anaesthetist's page (past Lists, Bookings, invoices, payments). The page shows none of them.
  - Missing: any time-driven effect (the demo clock crossing a last day does nothing, as there is no date). OQ-147 (a departed anaesthetist's remittance access) is open, so that part is narrated only.
  - Matches in part: inactive anaesthetists are excluded from assignment, the finder and Draft List candidates, and no List is ever deleted.
- **Demo trigger:** Use the existing Control Panel Clock & reset (advanceClockToDate). Make Dr X inactive with a last day 7 days out, advance the clock past it, and watch her Slots disappear, her Contracts show Disabled and the finder skip her. Add a presenter control on the mobile sign-in screen, 'Sign in as this anaesthetist', that is refused once the last day has passed, so the sign-in effect is visible. Optionally add a demo-sheet note on the Remittances screen for OQ-147 showing the 'sent by email from the office' reading.
