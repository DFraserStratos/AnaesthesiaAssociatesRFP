# EP-08 · Billing/Invoice Engine and internal ledger · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-08) · catalogue `8637b65` · [EP-08](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-08.md)

> US-08.4.7 depends on the Finances section (US-13.3.3), which exists only as requirements and an exploration. The Invoices screen is still a standalone nav section with its own /admin/invoices routes, so moving it into a Finances tab and renaming routes is a structural change shared with the other Finances stories (Payments, AA fees, Billing, Ledger tabs). Amount received and still owed already live in the ledger (receivables, receipts, receivableOutstanding); the list needs a selector joining them to each invoice and credit note, an age from the demo clock (DEMO_TODAY 2026-11-24), a state filter (Unpaid default) and a broader search (payable number, case reference, patient, anaesthetist). Changing the default to Unpaid affects any demo beat that lands on Invoices to find a paid invoice, and InvoicesSearch.test.tsx and demoGuideSync may need updating. AA fee invoices are already excluded, matching the story. Awaiting approval is currently a tab and the story wants it as a filter beside the state filters. Status is Verify (3) and the notes say the change has not yet been seen by AA, so the grading is against the text as written.

## US-08.4.7 · Find any invoice · Contradicts

[US-08.4.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-08.4.7.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-08.4.7) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On the Finances section's Invoices tab, one searchable list of every invoice, prepayment invoice and credit note (search by invoice number, payable number, case reference, patient, payer, anaesthetist). Rows show kind, payer, patient and Booking, anaesthetist, date raised, total, still owed, state (Unpaid / Part paid / Paid, with age of unpaid) and delivery. Opens filtered to Unpaid; Awaiting approval is a filter beside the approval switch; each invoice and credit note has its own page under Invoices; AA fee invoices are excluded.
- **Prototype today:** /admin/invoices is its own side-nav section; no Finances section or route exists. Two LineTabs: All invoices (default, unfiltered) and Awaiting approval (?view=approval, PrepaymentApprovalStrip with the approval switch and count badge). The table lists invoices (kind as a pill beside the number for Prepayment/Additional/credit note), with credit notes as rows above the invoice credited. Columns: Number, Patient / booking, Anaesthetist, List, List date, Counterparty, Total, Delivery (+ Raised date). Search 'Find by invoice number or payer' matches only invoice or credit-note number and addressee name. Invoices open at /admin/invoices/:id and credit notes at /admin/credit-notes/:id. AA fee invoices are on /admin/billing/aa-fees and not in the list. Seeded backdrop history is excluded.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: the screen opens on All invoices, not Unpaid; there is no Unpaid / Part paid / Paid / All state filter
  - MISSING: no 'still owed' amount and no Unpaid / Part paid / Paid state on any row, and no age of an unpaid invoice (InvoicesScreen.tsx has no reference to paid, owed or receipts; the ledger holds receipts and receivableOutstanding but this screen does not read them)
  - MISSING: search by payable number (e.g. AA-2026-0002-P), case reference, patient name and anaesthetist name (search is number plus addressee only)
  - WRONG (minor): kind is a pill beside the number rather than its own Kind column; date raised is a sub-line inside the Delivery cell, not its own column; no distinct state column
  - STRUCTURAL: not a tab of the Finances section (no /admin/finances route or nav item; Invoices is its own nav section); depends on US-13.3.3
  - PARTIAL: Awaiting approval is a sibling tab, not a filter alongside the state filters; the approval switch and approve-and-send are present
  - PARTIAL: invoices and credit notes each have their own page, but under /admin/invoices and /admin/credit-notes, not both under the Invoices tab path
- **Demo trigger:** Existing triggers cover the money states: payment-full, payment-half and payment-replay on the Invoices screen give Paid and Part paid rows; inject-unmatched-receipt is on Ledger. A seeded unpaid invoice about 40 days old (or a demo-clock advance) is needed to show age; no new button, but a seed or clock case is required.
