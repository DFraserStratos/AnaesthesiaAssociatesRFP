Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `f885f67` · [EP-11](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> EP-11 is largely built and the gaps are structural, all from the 25 Nov People-section decision (note AR-70). I confirmed each reviewer finding against the code and found no false Matches; the one correction is the shared-note evidence in US-11.3.3. (1) Waiting for NHI moves to a tab on Intake (US-11.1.4 and US-11.1.5). The change is IntakeScreen.tsx INTAKE_TABS, a new Intake route entry, a redirect from /admin/patients/waiting, and WaitingNhiView moving out of patients/. It does not need the People section, so it fits a People lane in Wave 1. It touches shared files: router.tsx (one route) and AdminApp.tsx (the badge). Flag those against the Contracts lanes. (2) The Patients badge double-counts waiting rows today (patientAttentionCount adds waitingNhiRows, and the Intake badge matchingAttentionCount already counts every open row). Remove them from the patient count in the same change. (3) Needs follow-up (US-11.3.3) and the list filters, paging and defaults (US-11.3.1) are one piece of work. patientSearch needs Coming up (default), Needs follow-up and All filters with paging. Needs follow-up needs a one-row-per-patient selector with reasons, a People badge counting distinct patients (strong and mild warnings alike, no waiting rows), and the Clear/Done actions moved onto the patient's page. The selector and domain part could run in the People lane before the section move, but the screen work lands on the Patients screen that moves. Doing both together after the move is simpler. (4) The section move (US-11.1.1, US-11.3.1): the People nav entry, routes and redirects for /admin/patients/*, and the Patients tab of People. PatientRecord's content is already the intended pattern (NHI pill beside the name, Details rail with own audited Edit, Details differ card, invoices, alerts and follow-up log), so adopting the shared record page lifted from the Contract page is a refactor. It depends on that lift and on router.tsx, SideNav.tsx, AdminApp.tsx, apps/admin/routes.tsx and masterViews.ts, so it waits for Wave 1 to join. (5) Already matching: dedupe and dedupe-never-overwrites with held details, mandatory NHI, stubs on Lists in all three apps, one-address chase, Urgent marking within 3 days, follow-up log kinds with audited resend, shared notes (addSharedNote, all three apps), and invoices across anaesthetists with status and balance. Demo guide impact (left to the owner's sweep): S2 patient beat paths (/admin/patients/CQY9304), the Waiting for NHI beats ('Patients → Waiting for NHI', Receive list counts) and the NHI register refresh beat ('Patients → Search') change their paths and labels. Cross-epic links: US-13.4.1 (People section), US-02.6.1 (stubs), US-02.1.4 (unmatched rows), US-13.6.5 (surgeon office contacts), US-11.3.2 (balance warning).

## US-11.1.5 · Intake rows waiting for an NHI · Contradicts

[US-11.1.5](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.5) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Rows waiting for an NHI show on their own tab on Intake beside the unmatched rows, and Intake's attention count includes them and drops when the NHI is added. Each row shows the patient as received, the List or date, and one address to chase (the List's sender, else the surgeon's first office contact, with others offered to switch to). Close rows are marked urgent. Adding the NHI matches the row to the single record and it leaves the view. A stub stands on the List.
- **Prototype today:** WaitingNhiView at /admin/patients/waiting lists open rows with no NHI (waitingNhiRows) with patient as received, List, stub chip, one address (officeAddressFor: List sender first, else the surgeon's office contacts; others offered), days to go with an Urgent pill within 3 days, last requested, an audited mailto request, and AddNhiSheet -> addNhiToWaitingRow. The Intake badge is matchingAttentionCount (all open rows, which includes waiting rows). The Patients badge (patientAttentionCount) adds waitingNhiRows again.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Waiting for NHI tab on Intake (INTAKE_TABS lacks it). The view and its tab count live under Patients.
  - Waiting rows are counted in both the Intake badge (all open rows) and the Patients badge (waiting + warnings + reminders + pending details). Once the view moves, the patient count must stop including them.
  - The old /admin/patients/waiting route needs a redirect to the Intake tab, and WaitingNhiView's caption and tab copy need re-pointing.
  - Minor edge: with no stub standing (a row with no List yet), addNhiToWaitingRow only corrects the row and leaves a decision on Matching ('Choose a decision for this row'), so the row does not become a Booking and the Intake count does not drop until it is decided. The acceptance criterion assumes the row is matched when the NHI is added.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: Receive list (Forte Health, two no-NHI bookings) and Add NHI, moved to the new Intake tab. For urgency, the existing demo clock control (advance to within 3 days of the row's date).

## US-11.3.1 · Patient outstanding bills view · Contradicts

[US-11.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.3.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On the Patients tab of People, a searchable, paged list with three filters: Coming up (the default, patients with a Booking coming up), Needs follow-up (every patient needing the office, with the reason per row) and All. A row opens the patient's page with all invoices across every anaesthetist, each paid, part paid or unpaid, with the balance.
- **Prototype today:** patientSearch: an empty query lists patients with a Booking within 14 days, then anyone with a balance warning or pending details (sliced to PATIENT_SEARCH_LIMIT 50). A typed query searches name, NHI or DOB (also capped at 50). The table shows name, NHI, DOB, next Booking, balance and warning. A row opens PatientRecord, whose Invoices card lists invoices across every payer with status pills, plus balance tiles. Patients is a top-level nav item with Search / Waiting for NHI / Follow-up tabs.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Coming up / Needs follow-up / All filters. The default list silently mixes upcoming patients with those needing follow-up, so it is not 'patients with a Booking coming up'.
  - No All list and no paging: results are sliced to 50 and there is no pager.
  - Not a tab of the People section; the nav entry and its badge (patientAttentionCount) are separate from People.
  - The Needs follow-up filter with a reason per row does not exist on the list; follow-up is a separate tab of three tables (see US-11.3.3).

## US-11.3.3 · Follow-up tools · Contradicts

[US-11.3.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.3.3) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** The follow-up log on the patient's page records call, note, reminder and resend with who, when and invoice. Needs follow-up is one list of every patient needing the office for a balance warning, a follow-up due or differing details, one row per patient with its reason. The row opens the patient's page, where the action is taken. The patient leaves when nothing is left and the People entry's count drops. Anyone can write a shared note on a List or Booking.
- **Prototype today:** logPatientFollowUp (office only, audited) handles kinds call, note, reminder and resend (resendInvoiceFromRecord logs 'Resent <number> to <address>'), and FollowUpLogCard is the composer. addSharedNote (shared/notes SharedNotes, used by web, mobile and admin) lets the office and the anaesthetist write a note on a List or Booking with author and time. FollowUpView on /admin/patients/follow-up has three tables (Balance warnings, one row per warning/Booking; Follow-ups due; Details to review). Clear warning and Done are inline on the rows. Items leave when resolved (derived). The Patients badge is waiting rows + strong balance warnings + due reminders + pending details.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not one list with one row per patient and a reason: a patient with two reasons appears in two sections, and one with several warnings appears once per warning.
  - Actions (Clear warning, mark reminder Done) are taken directly on the list rows. The story says the row opens the patient's page, where the action is taken.
  - The badge is not 'the number of patients on Needs follow-up': it counts waiting-for-NHI rows (not patients), counts only strong warnings (mild credit warnings are listed but not counted), and counts each warning, reminder and details item rather than distinct patients.
  - Follow-up lives on /admin/patients/follow-up as a tab, not as a Needs follow-up filter on the Patients tab of People, and the count belongs on the People entry.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: the S2 patient balance beat (open patient record, Resend, Log call, Clear). A due reminder can be staged with the existing demo clock control.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/patients/PatientsScreen.tsx:188-336`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientSelectors.ts:48-73,383-390`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientActions.ts:354-456`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/patients/PatientRecord.tsx:525-600`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/notes/SharedNotes.tsx:34-50`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/BookingDetailBody.tsx:762-764`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Contradicts. Verdict and gaps upheld after re-reading FollowUpView and patientAttentionCount. Corrected the evidence: the reviewer cited logListNote (lifecycle.ts:438), which is office-only. The 'anyone can write a shared note' rule is actually met by addSharedNote / SharedNotes (shared/notes), so the shared-note criterion holds. Also added that the badge counts items, not distinct patients.

## US-11.1.4 · Patient without NHI · Partial

[US-11.1.4](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.4) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Every patient has an NHI and a Booking cannot be saved without one. An incoming booking with no NHI waits at intake, shown with the rows waiting for an NHI beside the unmatched rows. No Booking or patient is created and a stub stands in its place on its List. Adding the NHI matches it into a Booking.
- **Prototype today:** Booking save, patient create and patient edit refuse a blank NHI. No-NHI rows are assessed waitingForNhi, stay open and visible in Intake > Matching with the reason 'Waiting for an NHI', create no Booking or patient, and stand as stubs on Lists. addNhiToWaitingRow resolves the stub (matched or new record, Booking made). The dedicated waiting list is Patients > Waiting for NHI at /admin/patients/waiting.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - The rows waiting for an NHI are not shown on Intake as their own tab beside the unmatched rows. INTAKE_TABS is Matching, PDF lists, Data quality and Validators, and the dedicated view sits under Patients. They do also appear inside the Matching queue with a 'Waiting for an NHI' reason, so the gap is the missing tab, not missing visibility.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: the Receive list / Forte Health demo control on Waiting for NHI (adds two no-NHI rows). It moves with the view to the new Intake tab.
