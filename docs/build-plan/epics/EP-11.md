The prototype built the 25 Nov tab reading (Waiting for NHI tab, /admin/intake/waiting), and the catalogue now wants a Needs NHI filter in the one matching feed (US-02.1.4). It depends on the EP-02 front-door rework (US-02.1.4, US-02.2.4, stubs FT-02.6) and on how hospital updates and PDF resends are staged. Decide whether the Matching RowPanel Add NHI stays (one hand route rule). Chase-address logic (US-02.2.4, US-13.6.5) already matches. Demo guide: docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md names the Waiting for NHI tab (S1 optional check, Intake section, the Losa Tuilagi beat) and needs rewording, plus a beat for the later-arrival update. Trigger screen strings are read by demoGuideSync.test.ts, so renaming them must keep it green. Capture recipes and screenshots (admin-waiting-for-nhi-*.png) are left to the owner's sweep.

## US-11.1.5 · Intake rows waiting for an NHI · Contradicts

[US-11.1.5](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.5.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.5) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, simulator, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Rows waiting for an NHI show in the matching feed under a Needs NHI filter (no view of their own), counted once in Intake's attention count, with the one address to chase (List sender, else the surgeon's first office contact) and an urgent mark when the date is close. Adding the NHI by hand from the row, or applying a later arrival that carries the NHI, matches the patient's single record, creates the Booking and removes the stub.
- **Prototype today:** A dedicated Waiting for NHI tab (WaitingNhiTab and WaitingNhiView) lists the rows with patient, List, stub link, one chase address (alternatives in a select), days to go with an Urgent pill (3 days or fewer, or passed), Email for the NHI (mailto, audited, one address) and an Add NHI sheet. Intake's matchingAttentionCount counts each open row once, and the tab carries its own count badge. Add NHI (tab sheet, or RowPanel Add NHI via resolveStub) creates the Booking on the single patient record and settles the stub. Stubs show on Mobile, Web and Admin Lists with no amount. A later arrival with an NHI is a separate row: findMatch needs an existing Booking, so it is not matched to the waiting row, and the waiting row and stub stay.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Needs NHI filter on Matching is missing; rows are on a separate tab with their own table, which the story now says they must not have
  - Later arrival carrying the NHI does not resolve the waiting row: no proposed update showing the NHI, no apply that turns the row into a Booking and removes the stub
  - 'The one way to add an NHI by hand' is not true: there are two Add NHI entry points (the tab's AddNhiSheet and the Matching RowPanel's stub-add-nhi), and a presenter beat uses the tab
  - Tab count badge duplicates the count; the count should sit on the filter while Intake's attention count stays once per row (that part already holds)
  - Urgent threshold (3 days) and no-List-yet behaviour are our readings, unconfirmed; story is Verify
- **Demo trigger:** Add 'Receive update with the NHI' on Admin Intake (Matching feed, Needs NHI filter): stands in for the hospital's later update for a List holding a waiting row (e.g. Forte Health Mon 30 Nov, Losa Tuilagi with the NHI). It stages a proposed update showing the NHI; Apply makes the row a Booking on her single record, drops it from Needs NHI, drops Intake's count by one and removes the stub. Keep 'Receive list with a missing NHI' and 'Office adds the NHI to this stub'.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/IntakeScreen.tsx:23-30,75-90`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/intake/WaitingNhiTab.tsx`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/intake/WaitingNhiView.tsx`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientActions.ts:204-243 (nhiRequestAddressFor, requestNhiFromOffice)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientSelectors.ts:515-552 (waitingNhiRows)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/matchingSelectors.ts:169-171 (matchingAttentionCount)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/patients/waitingNhi.ts:13-19 (urgent within 3 days)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/intake/matching.ts:439-449,512-537 (no-NHI row waits; later row goes to findMatch)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/matching/RowPanel.tsx:333-349,509-517 (second Add NHI route)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/stubActions.ts:47-100`, `searched: 'later arrival\|carries the nhi\|nhi.*(update\|resend)' in src -> no implementation`

## US-11.3.1 · Patient outstanding bills view · Contradicts

[US-11.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.3.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** On the Patients tab of People, a searchable, paged list with three filters: Coming up (the default, patients with a Booking coming up), Needs follow-up (every patient needing the office, with the reason per row) and All. A row opens the patient's page with all invoices across every anaesthetist, each paid, part paid or unpaid, with the balance.
- **Prototype today:** patientSearch: an empty query lists patients with a Booking within 14 days, then anyone with a balance warning or pending details (sliced to PATIENT_SEARCH_LIMIT 50). A typed query searches name, NHI or DOB (also capped at 50). The table shows name, NHI, DOB, next Booking, balance and warning. A row opens PatientRecord, whose Invoices card lists invoices across every payer with status pills, plus balance tiles. Patients is a top-level nav item with Search / Waiting for NHI / Follow-up tabs.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No Coming up / Needs follow-up / All filters. The default list silently mixes upcoming patients with those needing follow-up, so it is not 'patients with a Booking coming up'.
  - No All list and no paging: results are sliced to 50 and there is no pager.
  - Not a tab of the People section; the nav entry and its badge (patientAttentionCount) are separate from People.
  - The Needs follow-up filter with a reason per row does not exist on the list; follow-up is a separate tab of three tables (see US-11.3.3).

## US-11.3.3 · Follow-up tools · Contradicts

[US-11.3.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.3.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.3.3) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, store, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** The follow-up log on the patient's page records call, note, reminder and resend with who, when and invoice. Needs follow-up is one list of every patient needing the office for a balance warning, a follow-up due or differing details, one row per patient with its reason. The row opens the patient's page, where the action is taken. The patient leaves when nothing is left and the People entry's count drops. Anyone can write a shared note on a List or Booking.
- **Prototype today:** logPatientFollowUp (office only, audited) handles kinds call, note, reminder and resend (resendInvoiceFromRecord logs 'Resent <number> to <address>'), and FollowUpLogCard is the composer. addSharedNote (shared/notes SharedNotes, used by web, mobile and admin) lets the office and the anaesthetist write a note on a List or Booking with author and time. FollowUpView on /admin/patients/follow-up has three tables (Balance warnings, one row per warning/Booking; Follow-ups due; Details to review). Clear warning and Done are inline on the rows. Items leave when resolved (derived). The Patients badge is waiting rows + strong balance warnings + due reminders + pending details.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Not one list with one row per patient and a reason: a patient with two reasons appears in two sections, and one with several warnings appears once per warning.
  - Actions (Clear warning, mark reminder Done) are taken directly on the list rows. The story says the row opens the patient's page, where the action is taken.
  - The badge is not 'the number of patients on Needs follow-up': it counts waiting-for-NHI rows (not patients), counts only strong warnings (mild credit warnings are listed but not counted), and counts each warning, reminder and details item rather than distinct patients.
  - Follow-up lives on /admin/patients/follow-up as a tab, not as a Needs follow-up filter on the Patients tab of People, and the count belongs on the People entry.
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: the S2 patient balance beat (open patient record, Resend, Log call, Clear). A due reminder can be staged with the existing demo clock control.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/patients/PatientsScreen.tsx:188-336`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientSelectors.ts:48-73,383-390`, `aa-prototype/src/store/patientActions.ts:354-456`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/patients/PatientRecord.tsx:525-600`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/notes/SharedNotes.tsx:34-50`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/booking/BookingDetailBody.tsx:762-764`
- **Verifier:** corrected from Contradicts. Verdict and gaps upheld after re-reading FollowUpView and patientAttentionCount. Corrected the evidence: the reviewer cited logListNote (lifecycle.ts:438), which is office-only. The 'anyone can write a shared note' rule is actually met by addSharedNote / SharedNotes (shared/notes), so the shared-note criterion holds. Also added that the badge counts items, not distinct patients.

## US-11.1.4 · Patient without NHI · Contradicts

[US-11.1.4](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.4) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide, simulator · confidence high

- **Requirement:** The NHI is mandatory and a Booking cannot be saved without one. An incoming booking with no NHI waits at intake in the matching screen's feed under the Needs NHI filter with the other waiting rows, creates no Booking or patient record, shows a stub on its List, and becomes a Booking once the office adds the NHI.
- **Prototype today:** ManualBookingForm blocks Save without an NHI. A feed or PDF row with no NHI is flagged noNhi and waitingForNhi, creates nothing (only Reject or Add NHI allowed), and stages a stub on its target List. Add NHI (the Waiting tab sheet, or the Matching RowPanel through resolveStub) creates the Booking and settles the stub. Waiting rows are listed on a separate Intake tab 'Waiting for NHI' (/admin/intake/waiting) with a count badge. Matching has only Open, Unmatched and Decided filters, with no Needs NHI filter.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Waiting rows sit on a separate Waiting for NHI tab (the 25 Nov reading), not under a Needs NHI filter in the one matching feed (the 26 Nov reading)
  - Matching's filter set (Open, Unmatched, Decided) has no Needs NHI option
  - The tab, its route /admin/intake/waiting and the legacy redirect /admin/patients/waiting need folding into the feed filter
- **Demo trigger:** Existing: 'Receive list with a missing NHI' (Admin Intake; its screen label would move from the Waiting for NHI tab to the Matching feed) and 'Office adds the NHI to this stub'. No new trigger needed.
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/IntakeScreen.tsx:23-30 (INTAKE_TABS holds 'Waiting for NHI')`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/IntakeScreen.tsx:75-90 (tab count badge)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/MatchingScreen.tsx:185-187 (filters: Open, Unmatched, Decided)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/intake/matching.ts:512-537 (noNhi flag, waitingForNhi, queue unmatched, creates nothing)`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/intake/stubs.ts:61-85 (stubFor, reason noNhi)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/stubActions.ts:47-100 (resolveStub addNhi)`, `aa-prototype/src/shared/flows/nhiCopy.ts:11 and aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/matching/RowPanel.tsx:169-175 (blocked without NHI)`

## US-11.1.1 · Patient record keyed on NHI · Partial

[US-11.1.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.1) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: admin, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** One record per NHI matched on NHI even when name or address changed. The patient page is opened from the Patients tab and keyed by NHI. The NHI sits beside the name and no internal id shows. Details are in a side panel with their own Edit, saved as one audited change. The main column holds invoices, alerts and the follow-up log. Differing incoming details are held, never written over the record, and the office keeps the record or uses the new details.
- **Prototype today:** upsertPatient matches on the normalised NHI and holds differences as pendingDetails. PatientRecord shows an 'NHI <nhi>' pill beside the name in the AdminPage aside slot and uses no internal id for display. The rail has a Details differ card (keep/use incoming, audited resolvePatientDetails) and a Details card (Edit via audited editPatient, 'patient.update'). The main column holds balance tiles, warnings, invoices, bookings and the follow-up log. Back link reads 'Patients' to /admin/patients.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - The page is not opened from a Patients tab of a People section. Patients is its own side-nav section (SideNav 'patients', /admin/patients) and the back link says 'Patients'.
  - The 'same pattern as every other record's page' is not a shared component yet: PatientRecord is its own AdminPage composition (aside pill, rail), because the shared record pattern lifted from the Contract page does not exist. Its content is already the intended shape, so this is a refactor, not new behaviour.
  - Routes and redirects for /admin/patients/:nhi will need to move under People (the section-move wave).
