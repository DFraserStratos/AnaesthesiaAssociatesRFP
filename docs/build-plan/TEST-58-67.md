## Decisions to look at

- **Billing rows lost four lines (Phase 60):** the old monitor's Booking rows showed "prepaid left off",
  "+N additional / event invoices" and the patient-balance warning triangle; the Billing tab does not.
  Keep them gone, or bring any back?
- **Fix the payer edits an authorised List's Procedures** (the billed party's email and address)
  without the lifecycle guard that refuses edits on an authorised List; it is audited. Keep, or route
  it through a guarded action?
- **Run payables in Xero commits once:** each bill is still an audited step, but on a working copy
  written to the store in one go (that is what ends the freeze). Keep this as the one exception to
  "every write is its own `mutate()`"?
- **No address fails the Booking (D116):** a Booking whose payer has no billing email or postal address
  fails whole at the run, with no invoice; the run sends to the details locked at authorise, a retry to
  the fresh ones. The only seeded effect is Kevin Doherty's BK0171; old `noAddress` invoices read as before.
- **The badge and the numbers (D109):** the badge is problem Lists plus failed Ledger checks; a hand-off
  that only waits already makes its List a problem, so Billing and the badge count it before the Xero
  tab does (which counts only alerts and changes in Xero). Invoices counts Booking and payer groups
  awaiting approval; Payments and AA fees show none.
- **Fix the payer:** a sheet from the row editing the billed party's email and postal address (Party,
  patient or typed payer), Save and retry in one click; a payer locked by a prepayment, or none found,
  offers Mark resolved only; a patient's address left blank keeps the one held.
- **Mark resolved** needs a one-line note; the Booking reads Done, its List stays In progress while
  others are.
- **What Done means:** sent, in Xero, paid in and disbursed, or resolved by hand; a $0 invoice needs no
  disbursement; a cancelled Booking is Done and left out of the counts.
- **Billing:** 20 Lists a page; URL `q`, `show`, `anaesthetist`, `page`; the rail's five oldest awaiting
  Lists with "See all N" to the Review queue; Booking rows open the Booking on its Day. The Review
  banner's "Add one and send it from the invoice" is now misleading, left as it was.
- **Xero tab:** opens on Needs action (waiting included); Sent after waiting folded below; an alert
  after 5 tries, not the mock's 3; Organisation dropped from the connection card; retry results end
  their group.
- **Invoices order and words:** Unpaid (with part-paid) oldest first, so a fresh invoice is on the last
  page; "Unpaid · N days" amber from 30 days; no due date on prepayments; a credited invoice reads Paid.
- **Credit notes** ride above their invoice under every filter and count as rows; one whose invoice is
  seeded history lists under All only.
- **Awaiting approval** lists pending prepayments only (not under All); no approving from an invoice
  page; the Switch reads "On · each prepayment invoice waits under Awaiting approval".
- **Seed:** AA-2026-S2402 added for the 40-day example (`PERSIST_VERSION` 81); audit ids after mid
  October move by 6, no booking, invoice or ledger id.
- **Invoice page:** a credited invoice shows its standing in the header in place of the money state;
  History is the new seventh card; the rail's Delivery buttons are the shared compact size (36 px, were 40); the credit note page keeps Delivery and Xero; "New invoices" is now
  "Replaced by". No core-seed record reads No address.
- **Ledger receipts (D106):** placed or refunded in the system only; placed and refunded receipts leave
  the rail, and their history rows are gone from the tab.
- **Ledger sentence and tiles:** square only when the Day view says so, else "out by $X" or "not square"
  with one line per kind; tiles stack in one column at 1280; the list has no avatars and no Last run
  column. The Invoice pairs and Holding tables still scroll sideways inside their cards (BACKLOG).
- **Account page:** the pill reads "Out by $X"; payment runs' See all expands in place (no Payments
  filter yet); one credit trail row per credit note, opening its page; no remittance column; Still to net
  as a Notice above the cards.
- **Payments:** the runs table's columns as the mock (Run ID, Closed, Approved and Scheduled on the run
  page); every table 20 a page; Download schedule is the Print; "Open the ledger" dropped; the waiting
  table has no "net $X"; the cycle card's headings are plain Card titles.
- **Run payables:** 19.5 s to 2.0 s at practice scale by committing the recordings once; about 2 s
  remains in `disbursementDetection.ts` (BACKLOG).
- **AA fees:** URL `q`, `show`, `month`, `page`; the month select lists every month from January 2026;
  Runs on is the month's last day (OQ-112); the preview replaces the list; settings as a summary with
  Change; BCTIs column, not the mock's Lists; paid reads "Paid 9 Oct 2026".
- **The frame:** one Finances title with each tab's own sub line; record back links "Invoices",
  "Payments", "Ledger", the remittance's to its run; trigger headings "Admin · Finances · ...".
- **For the sweep:** about 76 capture recipes to the new addresses, the guide's "Billing monitor"
  mentions, S3 Beats 4, 6 and 7, S4 Beat 1, a recipe for Payer loses billing email, and ATLAS's
  finance rows.
