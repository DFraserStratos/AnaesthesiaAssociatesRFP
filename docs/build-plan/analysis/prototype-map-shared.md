# Prototype map: aa-prototype/src/shared and src/theme

All paths are relative to `aa-prototype/src/` unless stated. Written read-only from the code at HEAD f885f67c (refreshed 26 Nov 2026 after build plan Phases 45 to 57 landed); line numbers are approximate leads.

## Orientation

`shared/` is the cross-app layer used by all three apps (Mobile, Web, Admin) and the PWA build. It has NO routes of its own: the only route strings here are the route patterns in the demo-trigger registry (`shared/demoTriggers/registry.ts`) and the legacy `/cards/` redirect helper. What it holds: (1) the shared **Booking detail body** and everything under a Procedure (Billing card, step rail, Paid by, split, prepayment, events, credit, additional invoice); (2) the **BTM capture** cards (RVG code, times, units, modifiers, price, total, complete bar); (3) **flows** (add booking, cancel, move list/booking, funder, patient, price, submit list, alert office); (4) the **surface seam** (`useSurface()` gives a bottom sheet on the phone, a dialog or side panel on web and Admin); (5) **UI kit, table kit, calendar**; (6) audit reading layer, warnings, NHI Hub simulator, support panel, help topics, availability editor; (7) **demo trigger registry** (88 entries). The Contract words and the Contract chooser parts live in `shared/contracts/` and `shared/flows/ContractRail.tsx`; the Contracts section screens (list, Contract page, New contract, Replace from a date, Parties and Funding tags tabs) and the People section (Anaesthetists, Surgeons, Specialties, Patients pages) are NOT here: they live in `apps/admin/` (find them from the admin map). Shared holds only their words (`contracts/labels.ts`), kit pieces (`ui/Card` step/footer/editing/bare, `ui/EditableCard`, `ui/RailSection`, `table` GroupRow sub level, `ConfirmSheet` danger/wide, `OptionRow selected`), audit labels, demo triggers, and `flows/PatientFormFields`. There is no replace-from-a-date domain logic here (it is `domain/billing/contractHistory`, `store/replacedContractSelectors`). `theme/` is tokens only (listed briefly at the end).

Conventions that bind this area (CLAUDE.md, enforced by tests): one component per job (`apps/oneComponentPerJob.test.ts`), no dashes or "slot" in copy (`appCopy.test.ts`, `apps/noSlotCopy.test.ts`), PWA purity (`shared/` may not import `apps/` or `shell/`; `pwaPurity.test.ts`), anaesthetist sees no Contract complexity (`shared/viewer.ts`, `apps/anaesthetistSimplicity.test.tsx`), Admin Booking draft discipline (`shared/booking/draft/draftDiscipline.test.ts`), money only via `formatMoney`.
