# Prototype map: store and seed

Paths are under `aa-prototype/src/` unless stated. Refreshed at HEAD f885f67c (26 Nov 2026), after the Contracts section (Phases 45 to 49), the People section (50 to 57) and the Nov 26 polish commits. This area is the fake backend: one persisted Zustand store (`store/`) over a deterministic seed (`domain/seed/`). It has no routes or screens; screens call store actions and selectors (consumers listed in section 8). Every write goes through `mutate()` (audit entry per change), guards return `Outcome` data (`ok()` / `refuse(code,msg)`, never throw), and time comes only from the demo clock (`DEMO_TODAY` 2026-11-24, 08:00; `domain/clock.ts:36`). Billing maths lives in `domain/billing/`, not here. Key facts for current plans: a Contract has NO date fields (D90, OQ-144 open); Replace from a date exists (`contractReplaceActions.ts`) with the link and date held only in the new Contract's `contract.create` audit entry (D94); the surgeon group record is gone (a group is a Party of kind surgeonGroup); an anaesthetist's active state is derived from `startDateISO`/`lastDayISO`/`activeAgainISO` (the `active` boolean is gone).
