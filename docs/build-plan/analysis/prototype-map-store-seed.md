# Prototype map: store and seed

Paths are under `aa-prototype/src/` unless stated. This area is the fake backend: one persisted Zustand store (`store/`) over a deterministic seed (`domain/seed/`). It has no routes or screens of its own; screens call store actions and selectors, and section 8 lists the Contracts, Parties and Funding tags consumers, since the Admin Contracts section change (US-04.1.7, US-04.1.8) lands here. Every write goes through `mutate()` (audit entry per change), guards return `Outcome` data (never throw), and time comes only from the demo clock (`DEMO_TODAY` 2026-11-24, 08:00). Billing maths lives in `domain/billing/`, not here. Headline for the Contracts-section plan: there is no Contract replace action, no effective-from date on a Contract, no Party/Contract enable-disable beyond Contract `status`, and the "Replaces" and disable warnings are UI-side, warning-only reads (section 8).
