A verifier pass re-read every catalogue and code reference: all four deltas stand (DM-01 and DM-02 corrected, DM-03 and DM-04 upheld); no delta was added.

Order of work: DM-01 first, then DM-02, DM-03 and DM-04 on top of it.

## DM-01 · A Contract remembers which Contract it replaces, and from when

**Kind:** NewRelationship · **Size:** M · **Must precede:** DM-02, DM-03, DM-04

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) (the late disable "lists the Procedures dated from the date of the change"; the old Contract gets "a to-do on that date"), [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) ("No dates": a Contract has no start, end or review date), [US-04.1.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.3.md) (a replacement is audited).

**Prototype:** the link exists only as two `useState` values in the New contract panel (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/ContractEditSheet.tsx:125-126`, `replacesId`, `replacesFrom`), used to show a warning list once and then lost. `Contract` has no replaces or replaced-by field (`aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contracts.ts:77`); `createContract` takes no replaces input (`aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:135`); the audit entry for `contract.create` does not name the old Contract. After the panel closes nothing records that the old Contract was replaced or from what day.

**Impact:** the late disable (DM-04) and the to-do on the date (DM-03) both need the change date after the fact, so it must be stored. The catalogue gives two constraints. The "no dates" rule ([US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md), and `domain-model.md` "None of these settings is dated") says a Contract has no start, end or review date of its own, and [OQ-144](../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-144.md) is still asking whether it may get one; a `{ fromISO }` field on the old Contract would be a date on the Contract. So the audit route is the one that fits: the `contract.create` entry of the new Contract carries `replaces: { contractId, fromISO }` in its `after`. The date has to be an explicit payload field: it is the office's chosen change date, which can differ from the day the replacement is saved, whereas `disabledDaysByContract` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractHistory.ts:68`) reads the entry's own timestamp, so it is a pattern to follow, not a reuse. The other route, a replaced-by field on the old Contract, means a `PERSIST_VERSION` bump (now 78) and a seed scenario; the audit route needs neither (the audit is uncapped and persisted whole, `aa-prototype/src/store/persistDelta.ts`), though the demo may want a seeded replacement in the audit. The phase picks one and records it as a build choice. Either way one derived read, "the Contract this one replaced, and from which day" (and the reverse, "which Contract replaced this one"), feeds DM-02, DM-03 and DM-04. `domain-model.md` does not mention the link (it says only that a dated change means a new Contract), so the catalogue's authority for it is US-04.1.8 alone.

## DM-02 · Replace a Contract from a date, as one action

**Kind:** NewLifecycle · **Size:** L

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) (new), [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) (the dated change is a replacement), [US-04.1.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.3.md) (what a replacement audits), [US-04.2.15](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.15.md) (a fixed price changing from a date is a price range, not a replacement), [US-07.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.3.1.md) (an authorised List locks its Procedures).

**Prototype:** a replacement is two separate acts. The office creates a new Contract by hand, optionally naming the one it replaces and a date, and the panel then lists the Procedures still on the old one as a read-only warning (`ContractEditSheet.tsx:139`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractChanges.ts:43`, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractChangeSelectors.ts:14`). Nothing copies the old Contract, nothing moves a Procedure from the panel (each move is a separate `setProcedureContract` on its own Booking, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractSelectionActions.ts:99`), and the old Contract is not left or disabled as part of it. The list of Procedures already excludes authorised Lists and cancelled Bookings (`contractChanges.ts:49-52`), which is the "locked left out" rule.

**Impact:** new store action (office only) that, in one audited step, creates a Contract copied from the old one with any setting overridden, the settings fixed at creation (holder, pricing kind, prepaid) included, a new AA code from `nextAaCode`, and the DM-01 link; moves exactly the ticked Procedures through the existing Contract change path, one audited Procedure change each; then either leaves the old Contract Active or disables it in the same action. `planContractCreate` (`contractActions.ts:169`) is the create half and already runs `contractRefusal`. A system Contract cannot be replaced (the same guard as `systemNeverDisabled`). Depends on DM-01; carries the New contract page's replace entry and replaces the warn-only list on create. Points the stories leave open for the phase:

- **Moves are not one commit today.** `setProcedureContract` (`contractSelectionActions.ts:99`) has no planner: it calls `mutate()` itself and, per Procedure, clears the typed price and rail answers, can re-open a completed Booking, and can settle a prepayment (credit note, refund) after its commit. So "one action" is either a sequence of per-Procedure commits after the create, or a refactor of that function into a planner. Per-Procedure audit and side effects stay as they are either way.
- **Per-row refusal.** A moved Procedure must still pass `contractPickRefusal` (`contractSelectionActions.ts:52`: the candidate guard `contractAllowedOn`, `editRefusal`), which can refuse it when the copy changes holder, links or specialties, or when its List is a removed Draft (`proceduresStillOnContract` does not leave removed Lists out, `contractChanges.ts:47-52`). The action needs a per-row outcome, and when it disables the old Contract it must move first, then disable.
- **Copy scope.** A Contract's own fields (including a fixed price's ranges, links, specialties, funding tag) copy with it. A Contract's event fees are separate records (`masters.contractEventFees`, `contractEventFeeActions.ts`), which `planContractCreate` does not touch; whether the copy carries them is not stated ([US-04.1.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.7.md) lists them on the page, US-04.1.8 says "every other setting") and is a decision for the phase.
- **Offered from the day saved.** A replacement not priced as a fixed price needs no date on the Contract (no dates), so the action only has to say so.

## DM-03 · A Contract still Active after its replacement date is a to-do item

**Kind:** NewEntity (derived, not stored) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** DM-01

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) ("on 1 January a to-do reminds the office to disable it"), [FT-13.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-13.7.md) and [US-13.7.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.7.2.md) (the dashboard to-do list).

**Prototype:** the to-do list holds two row kinds, both keyed to a Booking: open warnings and Bookings with no Contract (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/WarningsToDo.tsx:23`, `ToDoItem`). Warnings are derived per Booking by registered rules and `Warning` carries a `bookingId` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/types.ts:114`); the rules cannot take a Contract as subject (`WarningFacts` is one Booking). No to-do row concerns a Contract, and none depends on a date passing.

**Impact:** a third `ToDoItem` kind whose subject is a Contract: derived from the DM-01 link when the change date has been reached (demo clock, `DEMO_TODAY` and clock events) and the old Contract is still Active, leaving the list when the office disables it. Because it is not a Booking warning, it should not be forced through the warning routine or its clearance store; it follows the `noContract` row's pattern (a work list, no Clear). Row ordering by date and session (`toDoItems`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/WarningsToDo.tsx:36`) needs a rule for a row with no session, and the row opens the old Contract's page: the rail's work card (`RailWorkCard.tsx`) only passes `onOpenBooking` today, so it needs a way to open a Contract. The `noContract` rows show on that rail card, not on the dashboard's warnings card, so the new row follows them there. Whether a re-enabled old Contract brings the to-do back is not stated (a derived row would). No stored state.

## DM-04 · Disabling is confirmed and lists the Procedures first

**Kind:** ChangedLifecycle · **Size:** S · **Needs:** DM-01 for the replaced case

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) (criterion "Disable confirmed"), [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) ("Late disable"; "Old one disabled"). The "Disabled flag" rule at office review ([US-07.2.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.2.2.md)) is unchanged.

**Prototype:** Disable is one click that sets `status: 'disabled'` at once (`ContractEditSheet.tsx:259-263`, `disableContract`, `contractActions.ts:320`). The Procedures still on it are listed only after the disable, on the disabled Contract's panel, from the disable day (`ContractEditSheet.tsx:143`, `stillOnContract(..., disabledDay ?? today)`). There is no confirmation step and no list before the change.

**Impact:** the list moves ahead of the change, from today by default, and, when the Contract has been replaced (DM-01) and is disabled after the change date, from that earlier change date instead of from the day of disabling. No store invariant changes: a Procedure already on a Disabled Contract keeps it and the review flag still keys on the disable day from the audit (`disabledDaysByContract`), so the confirm step is a read (`proceduresStillOnContract`) with a different `fromISO`, plus the "disable it as it is replaced" path that DM-02 calls. The post-disable listing on the panel can go. The confirm list is the same read the replace action shows (DM-02), so the two share the one list component and the `fromISO` choice.
