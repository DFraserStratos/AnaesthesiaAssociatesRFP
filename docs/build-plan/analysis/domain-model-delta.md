## DM-01 · A Contract remembers which Contract it replaces, and from when

**Kind:** NewRelationship · **Size:** M · **Must precede:** DM-02, DM-03, DM-04 (replaced case) · **Status:** open, Phase 49 (Lane A)

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) (the late disable "lists the Procedures dated from the date of the change"; the old Contract gets "a to-do on that date"), [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) ("No dates": a Contract has no start, end or review date), [US-04.1.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.3.md) (a replacement is audited).

**Prototype:** the link exists only as two `useState` values in the New contract panel (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/ContractEditSheet.tsx:85-86`, `replacesId`, `replacesFrom`), used to show a warning list once and then lost (`:90-94`). `Contract` has no replaces or replaced-by field (`aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contracts.ts:67`); `createContract` takes no replaces input (`aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:158`); the `contract.create` audit entry (`:201`) does not name the old Contract. After the panel closes nothing records that the old Contract was replaced or from what day. Phase 46 removes the picker; Phase 49 builds the link.

**Impact:** the late disable (DM-04) and the to-do on the date (DM-03) both need the change date after the fact, so it must be stored. A `{ fromISO }` field on the old Contract would be a date on the Contract, which the "no dates" rule and [OQ-144](../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-144.md) rule out, so the audit route fits: the new Contract's `contract.create` entry carries `replaces: { contractId, fromISO }` in its `after` (Phase 49's D94 chooses this). The date is an explicit payload field, the office's chosen change date, which can differ from the day the replacement is saved; `disabledDaysByContract` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractHistory.ts:68`) reads the entry's own timestamp, so it is a pattern to follow, not a reuse. One derived read, "the Contract this one replaced, and from which day" (and the reverse), feeds DM-02, DM-03 and DM-04. No `PERSIST_VERSION` bump. `domain-model.md` does not mention the link, so the catalogue's authority for it is US-04.1.8 alone.

## DM-02 · Replace a Contract from a date, as one action

**Kind:** NewLifecycle · **Size:** L · **Needs:** DM-01 · **Status:** open, Phase 49 (Lane A)

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md), [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md), [US-04.1.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.3.md), [US-04.2.15](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.2.15.md) (a fixed price changing from a date is a price range, not a replacement), [US-07.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-07.3.1.md) (an authorised List locks its Procedures).

**Prototype:** a replacement is two separate acts. The office creates a new Contract by hand, optionally naming the one it replaces and a date, and the panel lists the Procedures still on the old one as a read-only warning (`ContractEditSheet.tsx:90-94`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/contractChanges.ts:43`, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractChangeSelectors.ts:14`). Nothing copies the old Contract, nothing moves a Procedure from the panel (each move is a separate `setProcedureContract` on its own Booking, `aa-prototype/src/store/contractSelectionActions.ts:99`), and the old Contract is not left or disabled as part of it. The list already excludes authorised Lists and cancelled Bookings (`contractChanges.ts:49-52`).

**Impact:** a new store action (office only) that, in one audited step, creates a Contract copied from the old one with any setting overridden (the settings fixed at creation included), a new AA code from `nextAaCode`, and the DM-01 link; moves exactly the ticked Procedures through the existing Contract change path, one audited Procedure change each; then either leaves the old Contract Active or disables it in the same action. `planContractCreate` (`contractActions.ts:169`) is the create half and already runs `contractRefusal`. A system Contract cannot be replaced. Points the stories leave open for the phase:

- **Moves are not one commit today.** `setProcedureContract` has no planner: it calls `mutate()` itself and per Procedure clears the typed price and rail answers, can re-open a completed Booking, and can settle a prepayment after its commit. "One action" is a sequence of per-Procedure commits inside one change set, or a refactor into a planner.
- **Per-row refusal.** A moved Procedure must still pass `contractPickRefusal` (`contractSelectionActions.ts:52`), which can refuse it when the copy changes holder, links or specialties, or when its List is a removed Draft. The action needs a per-row outcome, and when it disables the old Contract it must move first, then disable.
- **Copy scope.** A Contract's own fields copy with it. Its event fees are separate records (`masters.contractEventFees`, `contractEventFeeActions.ts`) that `planContractCreate` does not touch; whether the copy carries them is not stated ([US-04.1.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.7.md) lists them on the page, US-04.1.8 says "every other setting").
- **Offered from the day saved.** A replacement not priced as a fixed price needs no date on the Contract (no dates), so the action only has to say so.

## DM-03 · A Contract still Active after its replacement date is a to-do item

**Kind:** NewEntity (derived, not stored) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** DM-01 · **Status:** open, Phase 49 (Lane A)

**Catalogue:** [US-04.1.8](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.8.md) ("on 1 January a to-do reminds the office to disable it"), [FT-13.7](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-13.7.md) and [US-13.7.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.7.2.md) (the dashboard to-do list).

**Prototype:** the to-do list holds two row kinds, both keyed to a Booking: open warnings and Bookings with no Contract (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/WarningsToDo.tsx:26`, `ToDoItem`; `toDoItems` at `:36`). `Warning` carries a `bookingId` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/warnings/types.ts:114`) and the rules cannot take a Contract as subject. No to-do row concerns a Contract, and none depends on a date passing.

**Impact:** a third `ToDoItem` kind whose subject is a Contract, derived from the DM-01 link when the change date has been reached (demo clock) and the old Contract is still Active, leaving the list when the office disables it. Not a Booking warning, so it follows the `noContract` row's pattern (a work list, no Clear), not the warning routine or its clearance store. Row ordering by date and session needs a rule for a row with no session, and the row opens the old Contract's page: `RailWorkCard.tsx` only passes `onOpenBooking` today. Whether a re-enabled old Contract brings the to-do back is not stated (a derived row would). No stored state. Verifier note: Phase 49's D94 words the to-do as "a rule in `domain/warnings/rules/`"; but a `Warning` takes a Booking as subject, so either the finding type is widened to a Contract subject or (as above) it is a derived `ToDoItem` kind. The phase must pick one; the rule file's pure-routine form fits either.

## DM-05 · An anaesthetist has a last day, from which they are inactive

**Kind:** ChangedLifecycle · **Size:** L · **Must precede:** DM-06, DM-07 · **Status:** open, new at `67cba2c`

**Catalogue:** [US-12.1.6](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.6.md) (new), [US-01.1.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-01.1.1.md) (Slots for every active anaesthetist), [US-12.1.4](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.4.md), [FT-12.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-12.1.md) (kept from joining to leaving), `domain-model.md` "Active and inactive anaesthetists". Note #10 and #11.

**Prototype:** an anaesthetist has one boolean, `active` (`aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:452`), flipped by the editor's Status control (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/flows/EditAnaesthetistSheet.tsx:46,128`). It acts at once: turning it off removes the Slots from today (`aa-prototype/src/store/mastersActions.ts:368-390`, `anaesthetistCanvasDelta`, `aa-prototype/src/store/slotActions.ts:972`) and is refused while the anaesthetist holds Lists from today (`mastersActions.ts:375`, "from today"). There is no last day, no reason, no way to choose a future date or to clear it before it comes, and the change is audited as an ordinary `anaesthetist.update` of `active`. The flag is read in about twenty places as "is this person usable now": the open-slot picker (`aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:201`), the AA fee run preview (`selectors.ts:1587`), List moves (`aa-prototype/src/domain/listMoves.ts:85`), Booking moves (`aa-prototype/src/domain/bookingMoves.ts:149`), draft assignment (`aa-prototype/src/store/draftListActions.ts:209`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/draftLists.ts:147`), the canvas generator (`aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/canvas.ts:116,186`), the clock roll (`aa-prototype/src/store/clockActions.ts:36`), the pairing picker (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/components/pairing/PairingAddSheet.tsx:49`) the demo invariant (`aa-prototype/src/apps/demo/DemoData.tsx:170`), the matching screen's open-slot read (`aa-prototype/src/store/matchingSelectors.ts:98`), rebook candidates (`aa-prototype/src/store/rebookActions.ts:191`), the List-move demo (`aa-prototype/src/store/listMoveDemo.ts:60,252`) and the unmatched-receipt check, which refuses a payment naming an inactive anaesthetist (`aa-prototype/src/store/paymentActions.ts:294`). Recurring bookings do not read the flag: they read Slot existence (`aa-prototype/src/domain/recurringBookingProjection.ts:233`, "no Slot: the anaesthetist is inactive or not started"), so they follow the canvas for free. The start date already exists (`startDateISO`, `types.ts:446`), so "active from their start date" needs no change. No seeded anaesthetist is inactive.

**Impact:** the model needs a **last day** (and the reason) on the anaesthetist, set, cleared and reversed by three audited actions, with "inactive" meaning "after the last day" rather than "flag off". That makes activity a function of a date, so every reader above becomes "active on this date" (a pure `isActiveOn(anaesthetist, dateISO)` in the domain, one home) instead of reading a boolean; whether `active` stays as a stored mirror or is derived is the phase's choice, but a stored flag that must be flipped when the clock passes the last day would need a clock hook (`dayAdvanced` exists, `store/events.ts`; the archive job is the precedent) while a derived one needs none. Consequences to build: Slots stop after the last day (the generator, the roll-forward and `anaesthetistCanvasDelta` take the last day as an end, not only a start); the guard becomes "refused while they hold a List after that day" and returns the Lists to reassign (today's guard returns a count from today); clearing the last day before it comes changes nothing; making active again after it gives Slots from that day (the generator already creates missing Slots from a date forward, and nothing is back-filled); every action is audited with its reason. Open points for the phase: a payment received after the last day for work done before it (`paymentActions.ts:294` would refuse it as "no active anaesthetist"; "everything recorded before stays" says it should be accepted, so that guard must key on the date of the work or on existence, not on the flag); the AA fee run for the month someone leaves (the preview filters on `active`, so an anaesthetist inactive at month end would drop out of a run for work they did earlier in the month; "everything recorded before stays" argues the run should key on the month, not the flag); a demo needs one inactive anaesthetist or a trigger. Contracts lanes: touches none of their files (the editor and record are `EditAnaesthetistSheet.tsx` and `AnaesthetistRecord.tsx`; the page rebuild and nav entry belong to the later wave). A seeded inactive anaesthetist or a changed shape bumps `PERSIST_VERSION`; a purely derived last-day field optional on the record does not.

## DM-06 · An inactive anaesthetist's own Contracts are disabled, and offered back

**Kind:** NewRelationship (a cross-entity rule between the anaesthetist and the Contract lifecycle) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** DM-05 · **Status:** open, new at `67cba2c`

**Catalogue:** [US-12.1.6](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.6.md), [US-04.1.2](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-04.1.2.md) (criterion "Inactive anaesthetist"), `domain-model.md` "Active and inactive anaesthetists" (note #10: "Disable them with her"; #11: "Yes, restore").

**Prototype:** nothing connects an anaesthetist's status to a Contract's. `disableContract` and `enableContract` exist (`aa-prototype/src/store/contractActions.ts:321,326`) and each is one audited `contract.disable` or `contract.enable`; the seed has the story's own example (Dr Beaumont's Rhinoplasty Contract, held by `PARTY.beaumont` and linked to her, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/pricedItems.ts:186-192`). A Contract's links to an anaesthetist are `linkedAnaesthetistId` (at most one) and its holder; there is **no link from a Party of kind anaesthetist to the anaesthetist record** (`Party` has `hospitalId` for hospitals and nothing for anaesthetists; the seed's Party is matched to the person by name only, `aa-prototype/src/domain/seed/parties.ts:71-73`). "Held by their own Party and linked to them" therefore has no stored meaning today.

**Impact:** a rule, not a new record: when the last day has passed, the Contracts where the holder is an anaesthetist-kind Party **and** `linkedAnaesthetistId` is the person are disabled (system, audited, through the existing planner so `contractRefusal` and the audit stay one home); on making them active again the office is shown those Contracts ticked with their price and confirms which come back (`enableContract` per ticked one). Choices for the phase: (1) how "own Party" is found, either derived (holder kind anaesthetist and `linkedAnaesthetistId` = her, no new field, matches the domain model's "tied by each Contract's own links, not through a party") or a stored `Party.anaesthetistId` like `hospitalId` (a model change, a bump, and every anaesthetist's Party needs backfilling: `addAnaesthetist` creates no Party today); (2) which Contracts are "offered back": all the person's disabled ones, or only those disabled by the inactivity, which the audit can tell apart (a `contract.disable` whose entry names the cause) without a stored mark; (3) when the disable happens: at the clock passing the last day (a `dayAdvanced` subscriber, like the archive job) or at the moment the last day is set when it is already past. The date lives on the anaesthetist, never on the Contract, so US-04.1.2's "No dates" and [OQ-144](../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-144.md) are untouched. A Contract disabled this way is flagged at review from its disable day like any other (`disabledDaysByContract`, `contractHistory.ts:68`, which reads the entry's timestamp, so the system entry must be written on the day the last day passes). Contracts lanes: new file (for example `store/anaesthetistContractActions.ts`) calling the existing actions; do not edit `contractActions.ts` or `contractHistory.ts` while Lane A holds them.

## DM-07 · No sign-in after the last day

**Kind:** RuleChange · **Size:** S · **Needs:** DM-05 · **Status:** open, new at `67cba2c`

**Catalogue:** [US-13.5.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.5.3.md) (criterion "Inactive"), [US-12.1.6](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.6.md); [OQ-147](../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-147.md) (open: how a departed anaesthetist sees later remittances; built to the catalogue's default, sign-in stops entirely).

**Prototype:** sign-in is a per-device browser session, never `AppState` (`aa-prototype/src/shared/session/anaesthetistSession.ts`: `{ signedIn, account }` in `localStorage`, which imports nothing from the store so it stays in the PWA closure). The one demo account is Dr Souter's (`aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/SignInScreen.tsx:37,57`: the typed email must equal her record's, anything else is `refused` and audited as `auth.signInFailed`, `aa-prototype/src/store/authDemoActions.ts:88-105`). Nothing looks at the anaesthetist's status, and a stored `signedIn` survives reloads and clock changes.

**Impact:** the check is "the account's anaesthetist is active on today's date" (DM-05's one reader), applied at two places: the sign-in attempt (refused with its own message and audit reason, `auth.signInFailed`) and the gate that keeps a stored session (`MobileApp.tsx:138`) so a handset already signed in is signed out once the demo clock passes the last day. The session store stays pure; the screen and gate read the store. A model consequence: the account belongs to the anaesthetist record by email, so a refused-because-inactive attempt needs the email to resolve to a person; only Dr Souter's demo account exists, so the rule is visible in the demo only when she is the one made inactive, or when the demo account becomes selectable (phase choice). No stored state.

## DM-09 · The rate and the priority tier are one card and one audited change

The rate is `Anaesthetist.unitValue`, saved by `editAnaesthetist` as one `anaesthetist.update` (`aa-prototype/src/store/mastersActions.ts:299`); the tier is a row of the office-private slice (`officePrivate.priorityTiers`), saved by `setPriorityTier` as its own `anaesthetist.tierSet` (`aa-prototype/src/store/officePrivate.ts:134`). **Impact:** no new data. Touches `AnaesthetistRecord.tsx` and `EditAnaesthetistSheet.tsx` (neither is a Contracts-lane file; the page rebuild in the later wave uses `ProfileChrome.tsx`).

## DM-10 · A patient needing the office is one row with its reasons

**Kind:** NewEntity (derived, not stored) · **Size:** S · **Status:** open, new at `67cba2c`

**Catalogue:** [US-11.3.3](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.3.md) (Needs follow-up is one list; "a patient with more than one reason appears once"; the count is People's attention count), [US-11.3.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.3.1.md), [US-11.1.5](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.5.md) ("Counted on Intake"), [US-13.4.1](../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.4.1.md).

**Prototype:** the Patients follow-up view is three separate tables, balance warnings, follow-ups due and details to review (`aa-prototype/src/apps/admin/screens/patients/PatientsScreen.tsx:185-331`), plus a Waiting NHI tab, and the nav count adds the four lists' rows: waiting rows, strong balance warnings, due reminders and patients with pending details (`aa-prototype/src/store/patientSelectors.ts:383-390`). The count is of rows, not patients: a patient with two reasons counts twice, and so does one with two open balance warnings (one per Booking) or two due reminders (`openBalanceWarnings` and `followUpsDue` are per warning and per reminder, `patientSelectors.ts:48,63`; only `patientsWithPendingDetails` is per patient). A waiting NHI row (which is an intake row with no patient yet) counts in both the Patients badge and Intake's (`matchingAttentionCount` counts every open intake row, `aa-prototype/src/store/matchingSelectors.ts:161`, which already includes the waiting ones).

**Impact:** a derived read `patientsNeedingOffice(state)`: one row per patient with the set of reasons (balance warning, follow-up due, details that differ) and the count of those patients, replacing the three-table split and the four-way sum. The waiting NHI rows leave the patient count and stay in Intake's, which already counts them, so the Intake tab needs no new count rule. No stored change. Screens: the Needs follow-up filter on the Patients tab and the People badge (`AdminApp.tsx:221-226`) are in the section-move wave. The new Intake tab for waiting rows is Lane C work, but Intake's tabs are routes (`IntakeScreen.tsx:22`, `routes.tsx`, `router.tsx`), so it adds a route in files the Contracts lanes edit: keep that to the one added entry and expect a small merge at the join.
