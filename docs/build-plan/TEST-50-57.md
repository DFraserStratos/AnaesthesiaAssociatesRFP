## Decisions to look at

- **Rate and tier:** a sliding tier control that moves a draft only; Save with no change writes nothing;
  a refused rate leaves the tier unwritten; the list's tier filter is local, then in the URL as All tiers.
- **Make inactive (D103, D97):** sign-in stops entirely after the last day; later remittances go by email
  (OQ-147, nothing built); no Change action (Clear, then set again); the AA fee run bills the month
  someone leaves; Lists after the last day refuse it rather than being removed.
- **Reasons and start date:** Left the practice, Long leave, Other; the start date is on the Status card
  ("Active from"); Dr Oscar Reeves is seeded inactive from 20 Nov 2026.
- **Clear last day** acts at once; the make-inactive sheet opens on today, so it refuses straight away
  for anyone with Lists. Dr Beaumont holds Lists to the horizon's end (BACKLOG).
- **Intake's count:** a no-List row leaves Waiting for NHI but stays counted once on Matching; the tab
  carries a count, Intake's other tabs carry none; the People badge counts one row per patient.
- **People lists:** 50 a page; state in the URL with replace; Active default (Anaesthetists by
  surname); Retired and All on Surgeons; search by name, registration or HPI CPN; no pager on the
  specialty's lists.
- **Anaesthetist page:** name read-only; each card's fields as listed; three rows a panel section, See
  all expands in place; History a header button; Save held back while a mandatory field is blank.
- **Surgeon page:** five cards plus Status; five rows a section but three pairings; Contracts' See all goes
  to the catalogue, pairings and Lists expand in place; OQ-148 at its default (no surgeon group list) and
  OQ-143 as built.
- **Specialty page:** Remove stays enabled and refuses in the rail; History on the rail; the seed keeps
  twelve specialties against the story's nine, for AA's spreadsheet to replace.
- **Patients:** every open balance warning counts, mild included; one reason per kind per patient; a
  follow-up is due on its date or after; Coming up is 14 days and no longer appends follow-up patients;
  a search narrows the chosen filter; the Needs the office card shows only while there is a reason.
- **Master data** opens on Hospitals with a pointer line naming both sections.
- **For the sweep:** the capture recipes, ATLAS rows and demo guide lines that name Master data's
  Anaesthetists, Surgeons, Specialties or Surgeon groups, the old Patients routes, the Priority tier card,
  S5 Beat 5 and the "Patients, Waiting for NHI" beats; the make-inactive beat and its two triggers still
  to script; the 48 known Playwright failures from before 45 (BACKLOG).
