How to run a phase

Each phase is a fresh session that plans before it builds and is reviewed before it closes.

1 · ReadCLAUDE.md, PROGRESS.md in full, PLAN.md, the owned findings and the SPEC.
2 · DecideSettle the phase's decision gates and open questions with you; record them in PROGRESS.md.
3 · PlanSave IMPLEMENTATION.md beside the SPEC: choices, bites, checks, recovery.
4 · BuildSmall verified bites, a failing-then-passing test per finding, verify:board green.
5 · ReviewTwo fresh Sonnet reviewers (one for a small diff): completeness and correctness; quality plus the phase's lens. The session verifies and fixes.
6 · RecordPROGRESS entry last: every finding's disposition, doc facts, what later phases need. Commit on board-fixes; never push.
Plan mode or auto mode. Open a phase in plan mode when it has an open decision for you: the session reads, rechecks its findings, asks you the questions and shows its plan, and nothing changes until you approve. The other phases can open in auto mode; their prompt still brings any product question to you. Each phase's detail, the order strip and the status table say which. D-05 is the exception: after B04, open B05 in plan mode to settle D-05 only, then run B08, then come back to B05.
