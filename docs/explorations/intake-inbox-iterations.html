Inbox, then open: the details

You picked idea 3 from the last round: Arrivals as a full-width inbox, and each arrival opened onto the whole screen. This round settles three things: where the decision lives once an arrival is open, how the inbox filters and sorts, and how the date of surgery sits beside the date received so the soonest Lists get dealt with first.

First, a look at where the rest of the Admin app puts its next action, because round one's mock put the choice of anaesthetist at the bottom of the screen and the button that confirms it at the top right.

What this round answers

  • Choose and confirmThe anaesthetist was picked bottom middle and confirmed top right. They should sit together, where the rest of the app puts them.
  • Filter and sortThe inbox needs both: drop-downs that narrow it, and column headers that order it.
  • Two datesWhen it arrived, and when the surgery is. Soonest surgery first is the order the office works in.
  • Every kindThe open frame has to hold an update or a cancellation as well as a new List.

The inbox, two ways

Both have the same toolbar: search, then drop-downs for what to show, kind, source and date of surgery, and a checkbox for "Needs a look". Every column header sorts. Both open sorted by date of surgery, soonest first.

A · One sorted table. "Surgery" and "Received" are separate columns. Surgery dates inside two days are red, inside a week amber. Shown with the date drop-down open.

B · Grouped by date of surgery. The same table with a heading per day, so tomorrow's Lists stand apart. Sorting by any other column drops the headings.

Both

  • Columns: Surgery, List, Kind, Patients, Needs, From, Received. The List is surgeon and hospital only; the date has its own column.
  • "Needs" is blank when nothing does, so a scan down it is the to-do list. The checkbox hides the blank ones.
  • "Add a PDF by hand" is a secondary button at the end of the toolbar: it makes something new.
  • Waiting for an NHI stays a choice in Show, not a separate list.

A or B

  • A is the plainer table and sorts any way you like; urgency shows only through colour.
  • B makes "what's tomorrow" impossible to miss and costs one row per day. It is the Conflicts screen's own pattern (a group row per day).

Opened, three ways

All three keep the header for moving: back to Arrivals, the List's name, and Previous and Next with the count, as Review has them. They differ in where you choose the anaesthetist and confirm.

O1 · Decide at the foot of the column. The page on the left, the List and the choice on the right; the right column's footer holds Reject and Place directly below the options. The sheet pattern, on a page.

O2 · Decide first. One row at the top of the right column: where it goes and Place, side by side. The other choices are text actions under it, and the patients follow.

O3 · One bar across the foot. The page and the patients get all the height. The choice is a drop-down in a bar across the bottom, next to the buttons that act on it. Shown with the drop-down open.

How they compare

  • O1 shows every kind of choice at once (named anaesthetist, someone else, a List we hold, Draft). Choice and button stay together however many options there are.
  • O2 is the fastest when the suggestion is right, but asks you to decide before your eye has reached the patients.
  • O3 gives the PDF and patients the most room. The choice is hidden in a drop-down until opened.

In all three

  • The page sits on the grey desk; everything you work with is on white. No grey on grey.
  • J K step to the next and previous arrival, Esc goes back to the inbox with its filter, sort and place kept.
  • After Place, the next arrival opens, with a line at the top saying what was placed and an Undo.

The same frame for an update

O1 with Mr Whitford's re-sent PDF: one time changed and one patient missing from the new page. The right column shows what would change; its footer applies it. A cancellation, a held message and an unreadable scan fit the same frame with their own footer buttons.

Recommendation

Inbox B, opened as O1

Group the inbox by date of surgery, soonest first, with every column still sortable and the four drop-downs and "Needs a look" above it. Tomorrow's Lists sit at the top under their own heading, which is the order the office actually works in, and Received is there whenever you want "what came in this morning".

Open each arrival as O1. It is the pattern the app already uses for choosing and confirming (the sheets, the drawers, Review's Authorise): choose in the right-hand column, confirm in the footer directly below, and keep the header for Back, Previous and Next. It also carries over to every other kind of arrival unchanged, as the update shows.
