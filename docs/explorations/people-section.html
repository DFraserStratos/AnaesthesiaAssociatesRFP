Cutting surgeon groups

You're right that it's a hangover. I checked the app code, the seed, the tests, the catalogue and the RFP. Nothing in the app reads a surgeon group except its own screens. The idea that matters, a group that holds Contracts and is billed for them, is the Party of kind "surgeon group" (COS, Unisports, Aria, Merivale), and that stays.

Why the card

  • No accidental change: a stray click on a page you're reading can't reorder the practice.
  • One pattern: every fact on a record is edited the same way, as on a Contract.
  • Rate and tier sit together: the two settings the office sets about an anaesthetist's work.

What it costs

  • Two clicks more when the office does mean to change it.
  • The web app's own rate editor (the anaesthetist changes her rate) is unaffected.

Catalogue: US-01.3.6 says the tier is set on the anaesthetist's record; nothing about how. No change needed beyond the record story's "each card edited on its own". The Anaesthetists list gains a Tier filter, which US-01.3.6 already asks for.

Making an anaesthetist inactive Decided

A new story under FT-12.1, as you noted. The app already has an Active switch that removes Slots from today and refuses while Lists remain; the story adds a chosen last day, stops sign-in, and disables her own Contracts.

Story sketch: Make an anaesthetist inactive

The office makes an anaesthetist inactive from their page, choosing their last day, when they leave the practice or stop taking Lists for a long time. Until that day nothing changes. After it they have no Slots, are not offered for any List, can't be picked in assignment, the availability finder or a Contract, can no longer sign in, and the Contracts held by their own Party and linked to them are disabled. Everything already recorded stays readable from their page: Lists, invoices, payments, Contracts and pairings.

  • Lists first. Refused while they hold a List after the last day; the dialog lists those Lists, each a link to reassign.
  • A reason (left the practice, long leave, other), kept in the audit.
  • Shown. "Inactive from 31 Dec" on their page until the day; after it, an Inactive pill, and hidden from the list unless Inactive or All is chosen.
  • Contracts. On the last day each of their own Contracts is disabled, one audited change each, by the system.
  • Change of mind. Before the day, the office can clear the last day. After it, "Make active" gives Slots from today and sign-in back.

Example from the seed: Dr Sarah Beaumont leaves at the end of the year. The office sets her last day to Thu 31 Dec. Her one Contract, Rhinoplasty at $1,350.00 (prepaid, held by her own Party), is disabled on 1 Jan. Her past invoices still show her as the anaesthetist.
