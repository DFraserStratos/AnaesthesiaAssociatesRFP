Say what the arrival does, first, in the rail

Today the right-hand rail opens with the List's owner and state, then the match boxes. You have to read down to work out whether you are placing a new List, applying changes, clearing a re-send or cancelling. Each idea below puts one shared header at the very top of the rail. It has the same place and the same shape for every kind, and only the words and the mark change.

Every idea is shown for the four kinds on the same List (Mr Rochford, St George's, Wed 25 Nov AM). The faded part below each header is the rail as it is now, so you can judge the header in context. The rails are drawn at the app's real width.

Recommendation: A, with D's numbers in its second line

The sentence heading answers "what is this?" in words the office already uses, in the same spot and at the same size for every kind. Its second line carries the tally ("1 patient added, 1 removed, 1 time moved") as plain text rather than big numbers, so it stays calm. Colour is kept for the one kind that destroys something, the cancellation. New, update and matches stay neutral and differ by icon and words, so amber and green keep meaning "check this" and "found".

The owner and state line ("Active, open to changes until it is authorised") becomes the heading's small third line, so nothing is lost. "This List so far" stays where it is for updates, matches and cancellations. E is the alternative if you would rather the track did the job itself, but it leaves a New List with an almost empty header.
