Day notes options

Six ways the Admin Day notes card could look now that the To-do list is flat, plus how it looks today. Every card works: add a note, flag it, and watch where it lands. All use the three notes on 24 November.

What makes the card look out of place

  • The left rule is the same device we just took off the To-do rows: a grey bar, turning amber for a flagged note.
  • The time and initials are 10px mono in mist: the smallest type in the rail, and too pale to read comfortably. Every other row in the app puts its details in 11.5px slate under the main text.
  • Flagging only shows as colour, and the checkbox names the colour, "Flag (amber)", instead of what the flag means. Every option here shows a flag icon and uses the label "Flag as important".
  • Initials stand in for a name. "RT" means little to someone covering for Rachel. Most options spell out the name, which DayNote.by already holds.
