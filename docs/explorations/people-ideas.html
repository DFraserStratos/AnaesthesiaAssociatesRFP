Admin Web App, People. Brainstorm for the office, 25 November 2026.

Second pass: People section design, with your first decisions.

85 anaesthetists, 165 surgeons. They live in a sub-nav under Master data.

The office keeps records about five kinds of thing that are people or groups of people: anaesthetists, surgeons, surgeon groups, the specialties surgeons practise, and patients. Patients have a section of their own (catch-up Phase 40). The other four are views in Master data's "People and places" group, beside Hospitals. Only anaesthetists and surgeons open into a page, and neither page shows what the person is on.

Below: who the office keeps records of and how they connect, what is there today, four ways to give people a section of their own, one domain question underneath (surgeon groups and Parties), how the whole Admin app could share one frame, and what I would do.

What the office has today

Built in catch-up phases 17, 17b, 18a, 26, 40 and 42 (AnaesthetistsView, SurgeonsView, SpecialtiesView, SurgeonProfile, AnaesthetistRecord, PatientRecord). The pieces work. They are hard to reach, can't be searched, and the records stop at the person's own details.

Four ideas

Each moves the four people masters out of Master data. They differ in what the office lands on, whether Patients joins, and how deep a record goes. Mocks are drawn at the app's real sizes (216px nav, 28px inset, 412px rail) with seed data; scroll them sideways on a small screen.

1

People section: list and record

The plain promotion, the same shape as Contracts. A People entry after Contracts, with routed tabs Anaesthetists, Surgeons, Surgeon groups and Specialties. Each tab is an AdminPage fit list with one search box, filter pills and the pager in the card footer. Every row opens a record page keyed by its fixed id. Groups and specialties get pages for the first time. Records keep today's content on cards, each card with its own Edit, plus a rail answering "who or what does this touch".

What it fixes

  • One click to any person; the nav lights People.
  • Search and paging on the two long lists (closes the BACKLOG item for them).
  • Groups and specialties become pages: a specialty lists its surgeons and its Contracts.
  • A record answers "what is this person on?"
  • Edit per card: one audited change per card, as on a Contract.

What it costs

  • Four list views move onto the Contracts list shape; two new record pages (group, specialty).
  • Both record pages swap their one sheet for EditableCards.
  • A redirect for each old ?view= and the two profile routes.
  • Hospitals stays alone in "People and places", which becomes "Places".

Catalogue: US-13.4.1 moves four rows to People (as it did for Contracts). US-13.6.2 and US-12.1.4 say each surgeon and anaesthetist has a page, cards edited one at a time, and a "what they're on" rail. Two new stories: a surgeon group's page, a specialty's page. US-13.6.3/4 unchanged (pairings already show on both records).

Design: reuses ProfilePage, LineTabs, PageToolbar, TableShell fit, Pager. No new pattern.

  • The side nav stays at 13 entries with People added.

What it costs

  • Patients isn't master data. Nobody adds a patient by hand; records come from Bookings. Its tabs are daily work queues with a badge.

The rail on Overview shows five of each with "See all". Works with idea 1 or idea 2.

One question underneath: surgeon groups and Parties

a. Keep them apart

As now. The group page links "Holds Contracts as" to a Party chosen by hand, at most one. Membership stays on the group; Contracts still link surgeons by name.

Cost: one optional field. Matches the domain model's reading.

b. A group may be a Party

A surgeon group points at its Party (or none), as a hospital Party points at its hospital. The group page shows the Party's Contracts; the Party page shows the members. New Contracts for the Party offer its members first.

Cost: one link and two rails. Replaces the name-matching rename.

c. One record

Fold groups into Parties: a surgeon group Party gains members, and the Master data group goes.

Cost: a group that holds nothing (Christchurch Eye Surgeons) becomes a Party that bills nobody. Muddles the address book with billing.

One frame for the whole Admin app

The page frame already exists (AdminPage fit, fluid and document; the 412 rail; one toolbar row). What is missing is one record pattern and one list pattern that every section builds from, so a Contract, a Party, a surgeon and a patient read the same. Four patterns cover the app:

List page

  • AdminPage fit, count beside the title, one primary "New …" / "Add …"
  • Routed LineTabs for sibling lists
  • One PageToolbar: search, FilterPills, selects, the count at the right
  • Optional index pane (GroupIndex, Phase 47)
  • TableShell fit, rows open the record, Pager in the footer
  • No Edit cell, no checkboxes

Record page

  • RecordPage (today's ProfilePage, renamed): back link, name, identifier chip, status pill, one or two header actions
  • Routed tabs: Overview first, History last
  • Overview: EditableCards in the profile grid, each its own audited save
  • Rail: "who or what this touches", five rows and "See all" into a tab
  • URL keyed by the fixed id

New page

  • A full page at …/new, fluid with a rail
  • Fields in the order their rules depend on
  • The rail previews where it lands
  • On create, replace the URL with the record's

Work queue and document

  • Work queues (Review, Intake, Draft Lists, Patients' Waiting for NHI) are list pages with a nav badge; their rows open the thing to act on
  • Invoices, credit notes, remittances stay document
  • Day view and the dashboards stay their own

What I'd do

Start with idea 1: it is the Contracts move again and needs only the stories rewritten. Keep Patients where it is, and move its record onto the shared record pattern. Then add in this order:

  1. People with four tabs, search and paging on Anaesthetists and Surgeons, a page for every anaesthetist, surgeon, group and specialty, cards edited one at a time, and old URLs redirected.
  2. The rail on the anaesthetist and surgeon pages: pairings, groups, Contracts linked, next Lists. This is the "who does this touch" part.
  3. Option b for groups: a surgeon group may point at its Party. Small, and it ends the rename-by-name matching.
  4. Lists and Contracts tabs from idea 4, once the rail shows they're wanted.
  5. Grouping the surgeon list from idea 2, once Phase 47's index pane exists. Start with "by specialty".
  6. One record pattern: lift EditableCard, the rail list and RecordPage into the shared kit as the first People phase, then move the Patient record onto it.

Questions for you before the design page

  • Patients: stay a separate section (my pick), or a fifth People tab?
  • Specialties: a People tab, or stay in Master data with surgeons linking to it? The Contracts note left them in Master data.
  • Hospitals: stays in Master data on its own ("Places"), or moves with People?
  • Groups and Parties: keep apart (a), may be a Party (b), or one record (c)?
  • Tier: keep the one-click control, or edit it in a card like the rest?
  • An anaesthetist leaving: a "Make inactive" action, and a story to say what it does to their Lists?
  • Nav order: People after Contracts, or next to Patients?
