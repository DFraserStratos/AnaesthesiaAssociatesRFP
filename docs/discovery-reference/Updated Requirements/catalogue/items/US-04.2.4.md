As an admin, I enter fee schedule lines with the holder's own code, description, price (GST excl and incl), optional RVG mapping, optional time band, add-on flag and quantity rule.

## Notes

Real examples: SXAP AP codes, CES HNZ codes, ACC OPT codes, Merivale set fees, vitrectomy time bands, GA add-on fees, liposuction per area.
