Greg: "I think we need to be clear about what the logical model is, and I don't think we are." The working model is a day holding an AM and a PM Slot per active anaesthetist (default free), a status on the Slot, and a List that overrides the status once put in it. Open: (1) is the day the real parent with Lists as its children and Slots only a view, and is an unavailable half-day a status or ("ironically") a List? (2) is every Slot stored, or are empty ones inferred? (3) when an anaesthetist with Lists marks themselves unavailable, do the Lists become Draft Lists (OQ-27 answer) or stay theirs with a conflict flag (US-01.5.2)? (4) the final status values and their colours; (5) user-facing names for "slot" and "draft list" (or "unassigned list").

Recommendation: keep the working model and Draft Lists for unavailability, and settle names with AA's users.

## Answer

1. Claude and the final devs can decide how these are handled behind the scenes. In our view, Slots exist in a day. They are a box or container. They can have a status. Lists go into that box. 

2. Every one is created and stored on the 4 rolling month thing.

3. When an anaesthetist marks a AM or PM slot on a day as unavailable, while that slot has a list. They are presented with a choice to return the list to the office to resolve or assign it to another anaesthetist's. 

4. You decide for now.

5. Admin staff will see draft lists yes. This is a major feature for AA staff. As for the word slot, we will keep that out of the UI as text. But it is represented much as it is today in the prototype.

The meeting adds: (1) Greg: slots and Lists are both children of the day ("a slot by definition belongs to a day"); a slot is "the same as a list with no children... just another entity in the hierarchy". Greg argued for no slot at all ("just a list with a different status"); settled as logical versus implementation model, with "slot" kept out of user land. (2) Greg: "we should create all the slots... the foundation on which everything else is painted": the generation run writes AM and PM as free, then paints the anaesthetist's calendar and the surgeon's (priority not settled). (3) Greg: the List "reverts to a draft list"; Donald agreed ("does the list become a draft list? And we think yes"), and unavailability "can initiate the flow or ask the question: return to office or assign to an available anaesthetist." (4) The "you decide" was qualified: statuses should be a user-maintained list (internal ID, editable label and colour, rules keyed on the ID) rather than an enum, and Greg wants a clear definition of the values from AA's users, who have "a lot of redundant statuses". (5) Greg: "I like the idea of the draft list." (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #5 #27 #29 #30 #45)
