Greg: "I think we need to be clear about what the logical model is, and I don't think we are." The working model is a day holding an AM and a PM Slot per active anaesthetist (default free), a status on the Slot, and a List that overrides the status once put in it. Open: (1) is the day the real parent with Lists as its children and Slots only a view, and is an unavailable half-day a status or ("ironically") a List? (2) is every Slot stored, or are empty ones inferred? (3) when an anaesthetist with Lists marks themselves unavailable, do the Lists become Draft Lists (OQ-27 answer) or stay theirs with a conflict flag (US-01.5.2)? (4) the final status values and their colours; (5) user-facing names for "slot" and "draft list" (or "unassigned list").

Recommendation: keep the working model and Draft Lists for unavailability, and settle names with AA's users.
