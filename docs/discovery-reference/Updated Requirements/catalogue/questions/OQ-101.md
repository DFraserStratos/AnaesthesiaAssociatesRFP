Some [RVG groups](US-05.1.1) print a range of base units (for example H4 complex procedures, 10 to 12) and the anaesthetist [chooses the exact value](US-03.3.1). The draft design holds one required base units figure on each RVG group.

Is the range kept on the group (with a starting figure) or only the starting figure, with the anaesthetist free to change it?

Recommendation: Hold a starting figure and the published range on the group; the anaesthetist starts from the figure and may pick any value, with an out-of-range warning for the office.
