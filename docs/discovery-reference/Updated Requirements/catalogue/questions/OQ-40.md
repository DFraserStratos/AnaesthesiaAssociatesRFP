Greg proposed, and Vanessa welcomed, handling a cancelled prepaid Booking centrally: always refund the original prepayment from AA's [trust account](US-06.5.1), then raise a new prepayment for the replacement anaesthetist (the patient may see two estimates because rates differ). To confirm with AA's financial authority:

- Is this process acceptable to AA and its accountants?
- When does prepayment money leave the trust account for the anaesthetist: is it held until the procedure is done, or paid out on the normal payment run once received (US-10.2.1)?
- Does reassigning a List with prepaid Bookings to another anaesthetist follow the same refund and re-prepay path?
- Does the same refund path serve a prepayment that exceeds the final amount (OQ-03)?
- How is a refund handled when the anaesthetist has already been paid (OQ-42)?

## Answer

Answered in Oct 1st Transcript

From the transcripts:

- Prepayment money is held in the trust account as a pending payment and is not paid to the anaesthetist until the procedure is done. If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment is credited; if it moves, the anaesthetist who does the procedure becomes the payee.
- The payable is tied to the Booking. Whoever submits the List did the procedure; if someone else does it, the Booking is moved to their List. Any move of a List or a procedure re-checks prepayments.
- Reassignment: the weight of the transcripts is that the new anaesthetist keeps the amount already agreed and wears or benefits from the difference, so the patient is not billed again (Ben, OQ-03's answer). Transcript B restates it as established (the prepayment stays based on the first anaesthetist) and says reassignment does not follow the refund path. The 2026-09-29 answer to OQ-21 (credit the whole prepayment and raise a new one) is the reading Ben disagreed with. Also heard: the draft payable pair is repointed to the new anaesthetist. To confirm with Ben.
- A prepayment above the final amount is not refunded (OQ-03).
- A refund after the anaesthetist has been paid is a credit note to the billable party and a negative invoice to the anaesthetist, netted in the next payment run (OQ-42).
