When only half the prepaid work is done, the anaesthetist may raise a credit note by hand. The [credit note option](US-08.6.5) today credits the original invoice in full, and corrections are [credit in full then rebill](US-08.6.2).

Can a credit note be raised for a stated part of a prepayment invoice in this case, or must it be a full credit and a new invoice for the lower amount?

Recommendation: Allow a credit note for a stated amount against the prepayment invoice for this case, reversing the same amount of the linked payable; keep credit in full and rebill for corrections.
