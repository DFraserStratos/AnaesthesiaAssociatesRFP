The RFP gives two different-looking sets of values for `ListStatus`, and doesn't say which is final:

- p.14 (List entity): *available, available for emergency, unavailable, on leave (etc)*
- p.17 (Supporting/Master Data): *private, public, pre-op, holiday, unavailable, free* — itself marked "(to be confirmed)"

Only "unavailable" appears in both. Need AA to confirm: is one of these the real list and the other
stale, do they merge into one combined vocabulary, or are they actually two separate fields that got
listed under the same "List Status" heading (see OQ-27, which is about that split)? Also need the
colour assigned to each final value, per RFP's "description, colour" field pair.
