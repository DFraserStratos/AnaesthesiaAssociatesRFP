The [blacklist](US-13.6.3) is kept by admin staff. When an anaesthetist hands their own List to a colleague who is blacklisted with the List's surgeon, the app shows a soft warning. How much should it say? Options: a generic prompt to check with the office; that the surgeon has asked not to work with this colleague; or the recorded reason. Also, does the anaesthetist see the warning when the blacklisted surgeon is on the other side (the anaesthetist does not want that surgeon)?

Recommendation: a short prompt without the reason, so the pairing is flagged without disclosing who asked.

Do we need two black lists or 1?

Meeting 2026-10-01: deferred to Ben. Greg pictures anaesthetists keeping their own blacklist on their own screen, alongside holidays, and admins keeping one on the surgeon's profile. Open: one list or two, and does each side learn of the other's? The name may change ("block list or something").
