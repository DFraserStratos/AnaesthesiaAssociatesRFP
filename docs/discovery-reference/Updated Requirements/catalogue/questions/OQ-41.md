AA suggested alerting on an unpaid patient invoice after 90 days: patients are allowed 90 days to pay and around 100 invoices are raised a day. Some invoices are six months to two years old when the patient returns. Confirm 90 days as the starting threshold, and what the 90 days count from.

Recommendation: 90 days, configurable, counted from the invoice date (our suggestion, not stated at the meeting).

## Answer

This is a threshold which may move from 90 to X0 days.

In question 40 we talked in the transcript about the system always alerting AA staff about patients who have + or - balances. In this case, there is a mild alert when under the threshold, and a strong alert when above. And in all cases, AA staff can still wave it though.

The threshold is only for - balances.
