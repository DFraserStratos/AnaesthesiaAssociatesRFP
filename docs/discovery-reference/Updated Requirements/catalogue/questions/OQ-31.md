RFP proposes invoice generation but flags it as a build detail to confirm.

Raised with AA on 2026-09-29: Vanessa says it is the anaesthetists' call, though they need to see their own work. Greg's reason for keeping a List visible until approval is that it should move from unbilled to billed and processed, not simply vanish.

## Answer

From the 7 October client meeting: a List leaves the anaesthetist's view "once the invoicing has been done, it's been finalised, and there are no more questions. So probably once the office staff have finalised it and sent it through to invoicing." Anaesthetists still need to look back: "we'd still like the ability to go back and look at old invoices. Sometimes there's a query about an invoice you did three months ago: what did I enter?"

Donald's proposed screen, with no objection: the main view starts at today and shows upcoming work and any Lists not yet invoiced (scrolling back to earlier un-invoiced Lists), with a deeper search or archive elsewhere in the app for past Bookings and Lists. Donald: "the transcript will answer it for question 31." The room's exit point is close to, but not word for word, US-07.4.1's "once its invoices are generated".
