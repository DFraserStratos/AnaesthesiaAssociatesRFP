The [filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2) narrows by the patient's insurer or funding source (private, SXAP, HNZ, ACC), and the [office review](US-07.2.2) checks whether insurance needs adding. The catalogue never says where that value is held. Is the patient's insurer and funding source recorded per Booking (it can differ from one procedure to the next), or once on the Patient and reused?

Recommendation: on the Booking, defaulted from the patient's most recent Booking. This is the catch-up plan's build default (data-model delta DM-35, Phase 20) if no answer comes in.

## Answer

The contract defines the billable party. And there can be many contracts for any mix and match needed.

From the transcript: neither the Patient nor the Booking holds it, so the question falls away. Each procedure's Contract says who pays, and one Booking can have procedures on different Contracts with different billable parties. A default Contract bills the patient. Every hospital gets a default RVG Contract plus its fixed and other Contracts, and RVG variants with different base units are Contracts too. Greg's tentative view: contracts are "always owned by the hospital" ("I think"), like a price book where the procedure is the product and the hospital is the customer. In tension with that, he later said "the contract is always by the funding source. So there are contracts for each funding source." Many insurance jobs are plain RVG, paid by the insurer, rather than fixed fee. Not settled: Greg thinks there is a fall-through order for who pays (the hospital applies to the insurer as agent, and theatre lists name the insurer), to draw out with Vanessa.
