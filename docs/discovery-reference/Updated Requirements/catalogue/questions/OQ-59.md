The prototype shows anaesthetists receivables in ageing buckets (Current, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 90+) and an Overdue tab. The [outstanding balances list](US-12.2.1) says a flat list with no rollup or grouping, and invoices have no due date, so "overdue" has no basis. Against that, [reporting for anaesthetists](FT-12.2) cites the legacy "Overdue" screen (RFP p.49) and a billing screenshot is captioned "aged". Do anaesthetists want ageing buckets or an Overdue view, or just a flat outstanding list?

Recommendation: a flat outstanding list, oldest first, with no buckets and no age chips. This is the catch-up plan's build default (reverse finding RV-19, Phase 38) if no answer comes in.

## Answer

Yea, lets go with your recommendation for now.
