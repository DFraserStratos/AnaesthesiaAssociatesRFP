At the client meeting AA asked that, when the office cannot confidently match the room's text to a procedure or [RVG code](US-03.3.1), it can leave it blank for the anaesthetist, as long as the billable party and billing approach are settled first ([booking setup](US-04.3.4)). Greg wants every booked procedure assigned to a procedure, even on a fixed price, and the [Contract list](US-04.3.2) is narrowed by the picked procedure.

With the procedure blank, how is the Contract chosen (holder first, or No contract (RVG) only), and what must be filled in before the anaesthetist can [mark the Booking complete](US-03.6.1)?

Recommendation: Allow the procedure to be blank at setup, with the Contract chosen by holder (or No contract (RVG)); require a procedure on every Procedure before the anaesthetist can mark the Booking complete, and show unmatched Bookings to the office.

Update 2026-10-08: the picker's RVG codes tab bears on this. The office can now pick an RVG code without a specific procedure; with No contract (RVG) the Procedure takes the group's general procedure, and with a Contract line it takes that line's procedure ([Contract list from an RVG code](US-04.3.2)). Whether that replaces leaving the procedure blank is not decided. (See "Notes 2026-10-08 · Procedure picker and source text #4 #5")
