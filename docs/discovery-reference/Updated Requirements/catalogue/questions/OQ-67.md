OQ-55: the Contract defines the billable party, with as many Contracts as needed. Greg said both that contracts are "always owned by the hospital" (a price book) and that "the contract is always by the funding source". The catalogue (2026-09-29 #24) also lets an admin set a billable party on the Booking independently of pricing. Which holds? Is there a fall-through order for who pays (the hospital applying to the insurer as agent; the theatre list naming the insurer)? Do guardian details sit with the Contract?

Recommendation: draw the fall-through with Vanessa; until then keep the per-Booking override alongside Contract-defined billable parties.

## Answer

I don't fully understand the question but for now, the billable party is always defined by the contract. And there can be as many as needed to fit what AA needs.

The meeting adds: not settled. Greg deferred it to Monday and floated a different model: "whether the assumption that we should create a default contract is actually" the problem. Today staff pick "RVG" as a contract type meaning standard pricing; without a default contract per hospital, "the billable party is always the hospital that holds the contract and there is no contract with individuals at which point you're using the RVG." This sits against Donald's default contract with a payer name and email field, against prepayments always billed to a person (OQ-73), and against the per-Booking override. Donald asked Greg to think it through and talk again. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #8 #41)

The meeting adds: AA is the party in the centre and everyone else is a contract holder. Greg wants two fields on a Contract for the billable party: "contract holder is billable party, yes or no", and a reference to a billable party table "including hospitals, surgeons and patients or patient guardians"; "If the contract holder is not the billable party, then by default it is the patient", and no invoice email on the Contract. Donald left the detail to Greg's technical document. Most insurer and hospital Contracts are plain RVG Contracts, invoiced to their billable party. The guide (taken as true for now): "The contract decides who is billed. Some contract holders pay AA themselves... Others only set the price and the patient pays", and when the patient pays "the invoice goes to the payer named on the booking", editable to a parent or guardian; the draft design has the same (bills_holder on the holder, otherwise the payer on the booking). This keeps the Contract deciding who is billed, with the payer for patient-pays Contracts named on the Booking.
