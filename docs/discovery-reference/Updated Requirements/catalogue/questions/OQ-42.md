Credit in full, then rebill (OQ-28) is settled. When the anaesthetist has already been paid for the invoice being credited, or for a prepayment being refunded, how is the money recovered? Today the anaesthetist refunds the patient themselves and both AA's and the anaesthetist's books need a note for the accountants. Greg's proposal, which Vanessa welcomed: bring it into the office, so the office refunds the patient and offsets the anaesthetist in their next payment run.

- Is offsetting against the next payment run the rule?
- What if the next run is smaller than the offset, or there is no later payment, so the anaesthetist owes AA?
- What does the anaesthetist's statement show for the credit and the rebill?
- How is it recorded in AA's books and the anaesthetist's, so the accountants can follow it?

Recommendation: the office refunds the patient and the amount is offset against the anaesthetist's next payment run, shown as its own lines in the anaesthetist's ledger position, with any remainder carried forward.
