OQ-50 settled that time units come only from the RVG rules. The check it asked for is still to do: compare US-05.2.2 (1 unit per 15 minutes or part for the first two hours, then 1 per 10 minutes or part) against the NZSA RVG 2021 text, including how a part-interval is rounded.

## Answer

Time is always rounded up. and is always using the RVG teired rules

The meeting adds: Donald read the tiers aloud (1 unit per 15 minutes for the first two hours, then 1 per 10 minutes) and Greg said a part interval is "always rounded up". The comparison with the NZSA RVG 2021 text itself was not done in the meeting. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #16)
