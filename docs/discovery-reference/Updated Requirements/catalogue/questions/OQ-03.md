When the prepaid estimate exceeds the final calculated fee, is the difference refunded, held as credit against future work, or written off? Who actions it and where (Xero credit note vs engine)?

## Answer

When a booking, with a fixed fee moves from one anaesthetist to another, anaesthetist B still receives the fixed fee that anaesthetist A set. To make it stable for the patient.

Sometimes this will result in more money for anaesthetist B, orther times less. It's just accepted.

Transcript adds: the answer above is about reassignment. On the question asked: when the prepaid amount is more than the final fee, the difference is not refunded; it is ignored. Greg indicated the reverse case (final higher than prepaid) is also often ignored, and he would only go back to the patient if it were really significant (no threshold given). In tension with ignoring a shortfall, transcript B says of a prepayment that exceeds the final: "There may be a subsequent bill, but it's not a refund" (what the subsequent bill would be for is not said). Either way, a prepayment above the final is not a refund. See Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #2 #16.
