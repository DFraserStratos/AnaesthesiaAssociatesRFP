When the prepaid estimate exceeds the final calculated fee, is the difference refunded, held as credit against future work, or written off? Who actions it and where (Xero credit note vs engine)?

## Answer

When a booking, with a fixed fee moves from one anaesthetist to another, anaesthetist B still receives the fixed fee that anaesthetist A set. To make it stable for the patient.

Sometimes this will result in more money for anaesthetist B, orther times less. It's just accepted.
