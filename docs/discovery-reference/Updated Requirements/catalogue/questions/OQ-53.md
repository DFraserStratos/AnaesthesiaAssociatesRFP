Cosmetic combinations (for example abdominoplasty, breast lift and liposuction) are generally prepaid or fixed price and billed as one combined operation. They are split only when a billable party asks, for example Southern Cross asking for three invoices, and the component prices may not add up to the bundle price because the bundle can carry a discount. Should the system model a combined procedure as (a) a bundle master procedure with a fixed price, (b) several Procedures in one Booking priced by a Contract, or (c) one Procedure that is only split into components on invoices? When it is [split](US-08.6.4), is the original combined invoice credited?

Recommendation: (a) for the bundle, with the split done by additional invoices (component prices independent of the bundle price). This shapes how the multi-procedure rule and additional invoices interact.
