A patient under 18 (Miss or Master) must be billed to an adult (Mr or Mrs), never the child; the office catches this by hand at review today. When the system finds [a child set as billable party](US-11.2.4), should it only flag it, or stop the List being authorised until an adult is set? Should it also stop the Booking being saved at setup?

Recommendation: flag at setup and at review, and block authorising the List until an adult is set.

## Answer

Much like the system will warn AA admin staff of billable parties with a - balance. (Strong / mild warning), the system should also give AA staff a mild warning when a patient on a booking is under 18 and is the billable party (EG, if the billable party is a hospital, then no warning needed)
