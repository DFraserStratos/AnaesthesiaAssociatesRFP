RFP Split Billing situation 2: one Procedure partly covered by a contract holder or insurer with the patient paying the gap (NZSA RVG also describes insurer gap and top-up). Under one-Contract-per-Procedure, how is this expressed: a covered amount or percent on the Contract, a second billing line, or a second Contract on the same Procedure?

## Answer

Contracts have a new "setting" for full payment to the assigned billable party. Or spliting the contact's line item to divide the payment between parties. 
ONLY percentage split

From the transcript and Donald's notes, the mechanism (full payment or a split) was not challenged, but the split basis is not settled:

- The board answer says percentage split only.
- Donald's notes after the meeting say percentage split or a free field. (Both his answer and his notes say "contact's line item", read here as the contract's.)
- In the meeting Greg said he would have gone for a value: the office has a calculator and the case is uncommon ("you could type in a dollar sign in 48, or you could type in 48%"). He asked for a question mark after "only percentage split", and Donald suggested percentage or free-form, to be confirmed and pressure tested by AA.

Also not said: whether percentages must total 100, who sets them (on the Contract or on the Booking), and whether full payment or split is a per-Contract default. See Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #12 #37 #58.
