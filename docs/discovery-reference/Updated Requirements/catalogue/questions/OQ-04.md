Is the default prepaid amount the Contract's fixed fee, an RVG estimate at the anaesthetist's unit value, or always entered by hand? Can it be a deposit percentage?

## Answer

Base + time + 2 mod * anaesthetist's unit value

The meeting adds, and replaces the answer above: the prepaid amount is no longer an estimate. Guide §10 question 2 was answered at the directors meeting: "Anaesthetist will create and manage their own fixed price contracts." Donald: "These are understood as first party contracts." At the 7 October client meeting, Donald: an anaesthetist who wants a procedure prepaid needs "to define a fixed prepaid amount that is selected and used for that prepayment, rather than an estimate based on BTM"; the room: "every anaesthetist always has a defined amount: this is how much the prepayment is." The amounts are kept as a list per anaesthetist ("a catalogue of prepayment amounts that you can use for that anaesthetist until he changes them"), maintained by the office; an anaesthetist "should probably just send an email". Greg, the same morning: the admin "just selects that contract", with a contract line for each procedure, "like a price list", and the office stays in control of Contracts in version one. No deposit percentage: "The full expected fee is prepaid. There is no part payment or deposit." (OQ-76)

Still open: who maintains the amounts (the directors' "Anaesthetist will create and manage" against the 7 October position that the office keeps them); what the "approval step" Donald mentioned approves; and what amount applies when a procedure the anaesthetist has marked prepaid has no price in their own contract. (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #4 #9", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #34 #36", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #3 #17 #18 #35", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #1 #15")

Update 2026-10-08: who maintains the amounts is answered by OQ-91: the office creates and maintains each anaesthetist's first-party Contract from the price list the anaesthetist supplies. (See Typed decision 2026-10-08 · Donald (product owner))
