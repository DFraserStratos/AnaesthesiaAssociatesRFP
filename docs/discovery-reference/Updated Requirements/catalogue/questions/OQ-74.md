OQ-41: alert AA staff about patients with + or - balances; for amounts owing, mild under the threshold and strong above it (90 days, may move). Not answered: what the days count from, and how a credit (+) balance is shown.

Recommendation: count from the invoice date; show a credit balance as a mild warning.

## Answer

count from the invoice date; show a credit balance as a mild warning.

The meeting adds: mild versus strong is "basically the difference between a yellow and a red warning"; an amount owing younger than 90 days still gets the mild warning. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #15)
