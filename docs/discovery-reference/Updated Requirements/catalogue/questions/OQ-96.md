The guide lets the anaesthetist [change the price (with a reason)](US-03.5.1) on their own first-party Contracts, and a prepaid procedure is priced on one. But the directors said [nothing further is calculated](US-06.4.1) on a prepaid or fixed-price procedure: when the work differs, the anaesthetist raises an ad hoc invoice or credit note by hand, and the guide's 'raise the price and have the difference invoiced' was not adopted. If the anaesthetist types a different price on a prepaid procedure, [netting the prepayment](US-08.2.2) would leave a balance the system would then invoice or credit.

Is the price on a prepaid procedure editable, or is the only route an ad hoc invoice or credit note?

Recommendation: On a prepaid procedure the price stays the prepaid amount and the price field is locked; any difference is handled only by an ad hoc invoice or credit note raised by hand.
