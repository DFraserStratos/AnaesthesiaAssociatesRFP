Settled: a prepayment above the final fee is not refunded. In the other direction the catalogue invoices any positive remaining balance, but Greg said a shortfall is also often let go unless it is really significant (no threshold), while transcript B says "there may be a subsequent bill". Is every positive balance invoiced, or only above a threshold, and what is that threshold?

Recommendation: always invoice the balance (current rule) unless AA names a threshold.

## Meeting update

Read out on 2026-10-02 but not answered: "we've determined that question 61 we need to ask Ben." No threshold was named. The open prepayment questions in OQ-76 (all or nothing, and a procedure that runs over its estimate) bear on it. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #2 #42)

## Answer

Ben: "By default, if the outcome of the procedure is that the pre-paid amount is too low or too high, then the system will not automatically invoice. Anaesthetists can / must create any ad-hoc invoices or credit notes as needed."

Donald, clarifying: "There is no automatic credit or additional invoice. The system will allow users to manually create them if needed."

So the system raises nothing by itself in either direction: no automatic invoice for a balance owed, no automatic credit or refund for an overpayment, and no threshold. Anaesthetists create any ad-hoc invoice or credit note by hand. This replaces both "any positive remaining balance is invoiced automatically" and "a prepayment above the final fee is never refunded". (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #1 #2")

The meeting adds: Donald's 7 October expansion widens this from prepaid procedures to every fixed-cost contract: "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract." The exception is by hand: where a prepaid procedure runs much shorter or longer, "the anaesthetist can optionally choose to generate any ad hoc invoices or credit notes as they need to", through "the already established and defined ability to create additional invoices on a procedure". This stands in place of the guide's proposal that the anaesthetist raises the price and the difference is invoiced as an extra invoice (Donald did not refer to that proposal directly). (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #10", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #15")
