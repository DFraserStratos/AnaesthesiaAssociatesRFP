Contract prices carry an [effective-from date](US-04.2.10), and hospitals and funders usually give an effective date, sometimes with a few weeks' notice. When a Procedure is priced, which date picks the price: the date of the procedure, the date the Booking was made, or the date the invoice is raised?

Recommendation: the date of the procedure, because that is when the service was given; the Contract version used is then locked at AUTHORISED as already agreed (US-04.3.5).
