## Answer

"No contract (RVG)" is the one default. It is offered first for every procedure ([No contract (RVG) always offered first](US-04.3.3)), and there are no hospital, insurer or procedure default Contracts (FT-04.4 and US-04.4.2 are retired). The guide, which Donald asked be taken as true for now: "When nobody has a special arrangement, the business says there is 'no contract' and the procedure is priced on the RVG... It is available for every procedure and appears first in the list of choices. Behind the scenes the system stores this as a contract like any other". In the room Greg agreed the per-hospital and per-procedure defaults do not exist: "the RVG is the default starting place for everything. And then you change it."

It bills the payer named on the Booking: the patient by default, editable to a parent or guardian ([payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2)). The guide: with No contract (RVG) "The patient, or their parent or guardian" is billed, and "When the patient pays, the invoice goes to the payer named on the booking. The patient's details are filled in automatically, and the office or anaesthetist can change them to a parent or guardian." Any other Contract decides who is billed through its holder ([contract holder and who is billed](US-04.2.1)).

Greg said he would think about who the default bills ("I'd like to have a think about that... I'll come back to you on that"). If he comes back differently, reopen this question. (See "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #7 #54 #59 #60", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #2 #10 #11")
