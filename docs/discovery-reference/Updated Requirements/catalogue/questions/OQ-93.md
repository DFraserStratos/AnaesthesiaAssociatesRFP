Greg: "the booking does hold the funding source. That's where it's meant to be defined." The guide: the surgeon's booking often shows the patient's insurance status (for example SXAP), which guides which Contract is chosen, but the Contract decides who is billed; when the Booking says an insurer will pay but no insurer Contract is chosen, the office is warned before billing. The draft design leaves where the indication is stored open, and OQ-55's answer said neither the Patient nor the Booking holds it.

Do we store an insurance or funding indication on the Booking (or Procedure), from what source, and does it narrow the [Contract list](US-04.3.2) or only drive the [review warning](US-07.2.2)? The guide's question 7 (is a warning the right response) is also unanswered.

Recommendation: Store a simple insurance or funding indication on the Booking, taken from the source data and editable by the office; use it to suggest the [insurer's](US-11.4.1) Contracts and to raise a soft warning at office review when it says an insurer pays but no insurer Contract is chosen. It never decides who is billed.
