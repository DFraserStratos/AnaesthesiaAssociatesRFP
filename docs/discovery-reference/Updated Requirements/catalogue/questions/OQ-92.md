Prepayment is triggered by the anaesthetist's [prepaid setting](US-12.1.3) (procedures or whole RVG groups), and [the amount](US-06.2.2) now comes from their own first-party fixed-price Contract.

What happens when a Booking has a procedure in the prepaid set but the anaesthetist's Contract has no line or price for it (for example a whole group is marked prepaid)?

Recommendation: Flag it to the office as a warning and hold the [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) until a price is added to the anaesthetist's Contract; never fall back to an estimate.
