[Surgeon profiles](US-13.6.2) hold a CPN number alongside the NZ medical registration number and HPI number. Vanessa linked CPN to prescribing and the practitioner's practising certificate, but the full name was not established. What does CPN stand for, and is it a value the system validates or only stores?

In the NZ Health Provider Index, a practitioner's identifier is the HPI Common Person Number (HPI-CPN, for example 12ABCD), so "CPN" is likely that number. If so, the surgeon's "HPI number" and "CPN number" are one identifier, not two fields.

Recommendation: confirm with AA that CPN is the HPI Common Person Number and hold it as the surgeon's single HPI identifier; otherwise store it as a free-text identifier until AA confirms its format.

## Answer

for an individual health practitioner, the CPN and their HPI practitioner number are the same identifier. Health NZ itself writes it as “HPI CPN (Common Person Number)”

So lets call it HPI CPN

Transcript adds: the HPI is the practitioner's unique index (the equivalent of the NHI for a patient); the other numbers on the profile are for information. Agreed: there is one identifier, referred to as the HPI CPN. A minor aside, not taken further: Greg noted the help text says it identifies a person within an organisation ("It's bizarre. Anyway, doesn't matter"). See Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #26 #63.
