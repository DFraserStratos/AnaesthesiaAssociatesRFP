OQ-45: an additional invoice is free-form ("Desc, Qnt, $"). Open: (1) does "Desc" mean description or discount (Greg: "I think you're talking about discount")? (2) can it go to a different billable party from the original? (3) when a combined procedure is split into additional invoices, is the original combined invoice credited?

Recommendation: description, any billable party, and credit the original in full when it is split.

## Answer

1. Desc is description

2. Yes

3. The additional invoice function needs a credit note option. So any party can receive the credit. Then new additional invoices are created.

The meeting adds: in the case described (Vanessa via Donald, and Greg: "It's always the insurance company will ring up and say, Can you split those out") the split comes after the invoice is sent, so it is a credit and rebill; the before-invoicing case was not discussed. The credit and rebill is one credit event against the original (to any party, the original included), then the new invoices; Greg: "it should automatically include a reversal". A credit note is not a refund ("One's cash, one's not"); refunds happen outside the system. Additional invoices and credits are recorded as events on the procedure. Nice to have (Greg): after the credit note, copy the original's line items into a draft invoice as the starting point for the rebuild. The practical tooling is partly a question for Vanessa.
