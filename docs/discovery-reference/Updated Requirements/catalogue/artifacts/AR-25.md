How an AUTHORISED List becomes invoices, Xero records, collected payments and payouts to anaesthetists. The Billing/Invoice Engine works through each Booking, takes each Procedure's billable party from its Contract, groups one invoice per billable party per Booking, creates a linked receivable and payable in the internal ledger (the source for the office's monitoring and the anaesthetist's balance views), and generates and sends the invoices itself. It then creates an ACCREC to the billable party and a linked draft ACCPAY (a buyer-created tax invoice) to the anaesthetist in Xero, storing both Xero IDs on the ledger record, since Xero does not link the pair. A payment recorded against the ACCREC is detected by the Xero INVOICE webhook, or by a daily poll as a safety net, and both write through one idempotent handler keyed by InvoiceID that reconciles the receipt to the ledger; bulk hospital remittances are matched in Xero's own bank reconciliation. A full payment authorises the whole ACCPAY; a part payment authorises a payable for exactly the amount received, with the rest outstanding. Authorised payables join the next payables run and are paid from Xero, and the engine records the disbursement. Whether payables need a separate approval before the run, and the payment cycle, are not yet decided (OQ-47).
