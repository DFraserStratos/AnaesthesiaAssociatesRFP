The end-to-end AA process on one line: the Scheduling Engine creates rolling Slots, booking information comes in and is ingested as Lists and Bookings, the anaesthetist does the work and completes the Procedure details, the office reviews and authorises the List, and the Billing/Invoice Engine sends the invoice to the billable party while the financial records go to Xero, payment is collected into AA and the anaesthetist is paid.
