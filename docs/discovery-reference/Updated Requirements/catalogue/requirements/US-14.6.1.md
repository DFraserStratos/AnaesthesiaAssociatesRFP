The system takes hospital data from the Christchurch providers that are not integrated today, into the same [matching screen](FT-02.1):

- Forte Health
- Christchurch Eye
- Burwood
- Southern Endo
- McMurray Centre

Forte has an export, but it never worked with Solutions Plus. Christchurch Eye and Forte send daily sheets. Burwood is public, but AA handles outsourced private theatre work there (plastics, orthopaedics). McMurray has only a couple of Lists.

This is so less of the office's hand entry is needed, once the incoming data shapes are understood.
