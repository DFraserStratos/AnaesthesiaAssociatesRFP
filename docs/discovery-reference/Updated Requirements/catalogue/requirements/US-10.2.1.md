When a receivable is paid, the system authorises the matching payable so it enters the next payables run.

A receivable paid in full releases the whole payable. A receivable that is only part paid releases a payable for exactly the amount received, and the remainder stays outstanding on the receivable.

This keeps what the anaesthetist is paid in step with what AA has actually received, whether that arrives in one payment or several partial ones.
