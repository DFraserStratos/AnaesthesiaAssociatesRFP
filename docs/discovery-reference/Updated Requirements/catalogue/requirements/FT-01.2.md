Availability is set at the half-day Slot level: an anaesthetist's AM and PM Slots on the same day can carry different statuses, independent of whether either Slot holds a List or bookings.

Whether this is the same concept as the RFP's separate [Anaesthetist Availability calendar](US-01.5.3), or a second one to reconcile against it, is OQ-27.
