Availability is set at the half-day List level: an anaesthetist's AM and PM sessions on the same day can carry different statuses, independent of whether either List holds bookings.

Whether this is the same concept as the RFP's separate Anaesthetist Availability calendar, or a second one to reconcile against it, is OQ-27.
