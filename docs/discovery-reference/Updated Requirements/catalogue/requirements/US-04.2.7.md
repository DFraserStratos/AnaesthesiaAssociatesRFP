Admins declare which per-Booking inputs a Contract requires, from:

- an invoice email
- [a billable party](US-11.2.2)
- [a prepaid amount](US-06.2.2)
- an insurer member number or claim reference

Once a Contract declares an input as required, the app prompts for it, and blocks the Booking from being [marked complete](US-03.6.1) until it is present.
