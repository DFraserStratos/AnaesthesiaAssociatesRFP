Cosmetic combinations (for example an abdominoplasty, breast lift and liposuction) are usually prepaid or fixed price and billed as one combined operation. When a billable party asks for it to be split, for example the patient receives the invoice and Southern Cross asks for it split three ways, an admin creates one [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) per component, attached to the combined Procedure.

The component prices need not add up to the bundle price, because the bundle can carry a discount. The system does not force them to match.

This is so each party can account for its share in the pieces it needs.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One per component.** An admin can create several additional invoices from one combined Procedure, each with its own amount.
- **No forced total.** The components are not required to sum to the bundle price.
- **Traceable.** Every split invoice links back to the combined Procedure.

## Notes

How combined procedures are modelled (a bundle, distinct Procedures, or an invoice-only split), and whether the original combined invoice is then credited, are open: see OQ-53.
