Cosmetic combinations (for example an abdominoplasty, breast lift and liposuction) are usually prepaid or fixed price and billed as one combined procedure through a [combination Contract](US-04.2.11) (one Contract set against each of its parent procedures). When a billable party asks for it to be split, for example the patient receives the invoice and Southern Cross asks for it split three ways, an admin creates one free-form [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) per component, attached to the Procedure.

The usual case is a split asked for after the combined invoice was sent (the insurer rings and asks for it split out). That is a credit and rebill (OQ-72):

- first a credit note against the original, from the [credit note option](US-08.6.5), to any party, the original billable party included;
- then one additional invoice per component.

The component prices need not add up to the bundle price, because the bundle can carry a discount. The system does not force them to match.

This is so each party can account for its share in the pieces it needs.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One per component.** An admin can create several additional invoices from one combined Procedure, each with its own amount.
- **No forced total.** The components are not required to sum to the bundle price.
- **Traceable.** Every split invoice links back to the Procedure.
