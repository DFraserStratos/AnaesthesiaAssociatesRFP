The anaesthetist adds pre-op and post-op events to a Procedure to capture time recordings or fixed fees for future invoices. An event may or may not be billable.

This is so anaesthetists can add post-care themselves instead of ringing or emailing the office. It covers:

- pain management after the procedure, the common case
- an [ACC pre-op assessment](US-05.5.2), a fixed-fee service recorded against the real Procedure

## Notes

Not settled, and asked in OQ-63: whether an event is a Procedure added to the Booking (Greg's lean) or its own element attached to the original Procedure (Donald), what it is called, and whether it shows on an invoice as a line item or a procedure. The OQ-12 board answer says events are "recorded and displayed as distinct line items", but the transcript (A L525-531) leaves it open. The ACC codes are still with AA's accountant (OQ-12).
