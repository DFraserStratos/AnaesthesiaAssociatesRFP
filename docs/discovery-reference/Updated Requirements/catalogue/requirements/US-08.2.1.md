The Billing/Invoice Engine groups a Booking's Procedures by the billable party each one has, which is its [Contract's holder](US-04.2.1) unless an admin has set a different [billable party](US-11.2.2), then generates one invoice per group.

This is why a single Booking can produce several invoices: each group of Procedures billed to a different party gets its own invoice.

## Notes

Who a Contract belongs to, and whether a per-Booking billable party stays, is OQ-67.
