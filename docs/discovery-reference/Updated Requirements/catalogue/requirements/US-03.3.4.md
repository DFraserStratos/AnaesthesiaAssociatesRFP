The anaesthetist records every modifier that applies to a Procedure as its own itemised code, rather than entering only a combined total, and the ASA score, the standard anaesthetic risk classification, seeds this itemisation automatically.

This is because some Contracts require the itemised breakdown, not just the total units.

## Acceptance criteria

- **ASA seed.** The anaesthetist enters the ASA score in its own field, separate from the modifiers total. Entering it seeds the modifier (M) units with that score's value, which the anaesthetist can then adjust to add any other applicable modifiers.
- **Itemised modifiers.** The anaesthetist itemises each further modifier code that applies to a Procedure, rather than entering only a combined total. This covers:
  - age extremes
  - BMI banding
  - emergency status
  - non-standard positioning
  - awake intubation
  - pre-assessment
