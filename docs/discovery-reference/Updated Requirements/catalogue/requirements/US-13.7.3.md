A Booking with a warning from the [warning routine](US-13.7.1) shows a small warning triangle, like Excel's, in both the anaesthetist's app and the Admin App. Tapping the triangle shows the warning text. The outline the [admin day view](US-13.1.1) already gives Lists with warnings extends down to the Booking.

When the anaesthetist submits a Booking that carries a warning, a short confirm step shows before it goes through.

This is so the anaesthetist sees a problem, such as an [unpaid prepayment](US-06.3.2), before surgery starts, while the surgeon can still be told.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Flag.** Given a Booking with a warning, when it shows in either app, then it carries the triangle, and tapping the triangle shows the warning text.
- **Confirm step.** Given a Booking with a warning, when the anaesthetist submits it, then a confirm step shows, and the Booking is submitted once they confirm.
