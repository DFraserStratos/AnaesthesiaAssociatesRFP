The NZSA RVG codes, AA's additional codes, and the pure calculation rules (base, time, modifiers, multi-procedure split, pricing bases, adjustments). All calculation is deterministic and testable.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Open policy decisions, such as a Contract resolving rates differently or an override that carries a reason, are treated as rules added within the existing domain model, proven by automated test coverage, and resolved during Scoping and Design.
- Automated testing is the safety net that confirms nothing broke when fixing bugs, adding features or responding to regulatory updates; it covers core business logic and critical workflows, supported by targeted manual end-to-end testing.
- The Test environment checks billing and invoicing calculations in detail.
- At cutover a small group of AA users validates contract, rate and billing reference data and the billing calculation logic, including tiered time and split billing, and a sample set of invoices is calculated end to end and reconciled against expected values.
