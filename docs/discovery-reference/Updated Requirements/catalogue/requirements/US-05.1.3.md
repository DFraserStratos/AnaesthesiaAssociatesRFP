[RVG groups](US-05.1.1) sit under body sections, for example Head, as the NZSA guide prints them. The body sections are the headings of both tabs of the [procedure picker](FT-04.3): procedures under body section and subgroup, and RVG codes under body section and the guide's sub-headings.

AA can add its own groups beside the published ones.

An anaesthetist can [mark a whole RVG group as prepaid](US-06.1.1), so every procedure in it is prepaid.

This is so the list stays short at each step of the picker, and a set of procedures can be chosen in one go.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design holds the body section as a field on each RVG group, not as a separate tagging ([AR-29 p3](AR-29#p3)). It is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
