Bookings reach the Scheduling Engine from four sources: a hospital download, a surgeon PDF list, admin entry, and an anaesthetist's own ad hoc entry.

Every change, whether it's a new Booking, a modification, a reschedule or a cancellation, is applied to the schedule with its source recorded, right up to the procedure itself.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change is validated against the system's internal data model rather than the incoming message, so there is one consistent validation gate however a booking change arrived.
