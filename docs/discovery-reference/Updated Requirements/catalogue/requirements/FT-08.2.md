The Billing/Invoice Engine raises one invoice per distinct [billable party](FT-11.2) within a Booking, not one invoice per Booking. Where every Procedure in a Booking resolves to the same party, that produces a single invoice; where they resolve to different parties, the Booking produces one invoice for each.
