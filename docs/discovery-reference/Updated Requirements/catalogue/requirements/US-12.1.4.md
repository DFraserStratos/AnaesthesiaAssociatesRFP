Admins hold each anaesthetist's master record: their contact details, registration number, Health Provider Index (HPI) number where available, and bank account details.

This is so the anaesthetist's identity can be referenced consistently across the system, in line with the NZ Health Provider Index standard, and so AA has what it needs to pay them.

The bank account details are used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Bank details.** Admins hold each anaesthetist's bank account details, used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid. Bank details field, location (system vs Xero only, per OQ-14) still pending.
