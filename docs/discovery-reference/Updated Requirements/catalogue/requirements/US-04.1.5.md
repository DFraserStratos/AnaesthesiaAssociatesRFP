The office keeps a record for every contract holder, the party a Contract belongs to:

- an insurer, a hospital, a surgeon or a surgeon's rooms (a third party)
- an individual anaesthetist, for their own price list (a first party)

For each holder it records:

- whether the holder pays AA itself, in which case the holder's billable party record is billed and one is required, or only sets the price, in which case the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is billed
- for a first-party holder, which anaesthetist it is

A holder can hold many Contracts.

This is because staff identify the holder early when narrowing the Contract list, and the holder decides who receives the invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a holder that pays AA, when it is saved, then it must have a billable party.
- Given a holder linked to an anaesthetist, then it is a first-party holder.
- Given a first-party holder, then its Contracts are offered only on that anaesthetist's Bookings.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design calls this the contract holder ([data model](AR-29#p3), [ERD](AR-30)): a party type (third or first party), a "bills holder" setting, a billable party and, for a first-party holder, the anaesthetist. It is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
