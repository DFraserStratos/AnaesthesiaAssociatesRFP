When an anaesthetist hands one of their own Lists to a colleague by [asking for a swap](US-01.4.3), and the colleague they choose is on the [blacklist](US-13.6.3) for the List's surgeon, the app shows the same soft warning as when the office assigns. The anaesthetist can still go ahead.

This is so a bad pairing is caught at the point the anaesthetist arranges cover, not only when the office assigns. See [the assignment warning](US-01.3.5) for the mechanism.

## Notes

What the warning reveals to the anaesthetist is OQ-43.
