When an anaesthetist [moves](US-01.4.3) one of their own Lists into a colleague's free Slot, and that colleague is on the [blacklist](US-13.6.3) for the List's surgeon, the app shows the same soft warning as when the office assigns. The anaesthetist can still go ahead.

This is so a bad pairing is caught at the point the anaesthetist arranges cover, not only when the office assigns. See [the assignment warning](US-01.3.5) for the mechanism.

## Notes

What the warning reveals to the anaesthetist is OQ-43. Who else is told of the move is OQ-65.
