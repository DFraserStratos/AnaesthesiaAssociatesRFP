The anaesthetist sees their Lists and Bookings, records the clinical billing data for each Procedure, sees and can change the procedure and the Contract until the List is submitted, applies any permitted adjustment, and marks Bookings complete.

This is so every Booking carries a complete billing record before it goes to the office for [review and authorisation](EP-07).
