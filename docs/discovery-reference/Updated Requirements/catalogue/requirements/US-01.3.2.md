Admins maintain recurring bookings: the standing intersection of a hospital, an anaesthetist and a surgeon, on a day of the week and an AM or PM session, painted onto Lists.

This is so roughly 80% of List assignments populate automatically from the pattern, rather than being assigned by hand each time.

## Notes

"Recurring booking" replaces "template" and "permanent booking" (and this catalogue's earlier "Permanent List"). A recurring booking is a pattern of Lists, not a Booking for one patient.
