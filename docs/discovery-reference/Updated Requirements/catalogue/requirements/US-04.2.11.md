The [master procedure list](US-05.1.6) holds standard single procedures. A combination, for example an abdominoplasty, breast lift and liposuction, is a Contract, not a procedure. One combination Contract sits against each of its parent procedures, so picking any of them offers it.

When a billable party wants the combined price broken into parts, the admin raises [additional invoices](US-08.6.4).

This keeps the master procedure list clean while still pricing combined work the way it is agreed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a combination Contract set against three procedures, when any of them is picked, then the Contract is offered.

## Notes

Once a combination Contract is chosen, the Booking records only the Contract, not its component procedures (a trade-off raised in the meeting). Greg's "operation" container (a level holding several procedures tied to one Contract) was not adopted; the simpler model is to be tried in the next prototype. Contracts are expected to number in the thousands; they are kept navigable by the picker's procedure and hospital filters and AA code search (OQ-66). A split after invoicing is a credit note against the original combined invoice, then new additional invoices ([split a combined Procedure](US-08.6.4), OQ-72).
