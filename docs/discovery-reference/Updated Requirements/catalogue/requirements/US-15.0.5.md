As the team, all fee, unit, split and pricing logic is pure, deterministic and covered by automated tests with worked examples.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Domain-Driven Design: the system models the business domain (the concepts, events and rules of scheduling and billing) rather than database tables and data flows, so processes can change without unpicking unrelated parts. Open policy decisions (a Contract that resolves rates differently, an override that carries a reason, a billing route that can fall back) become rules within existing concepts, proven by automated tests, and are resolved during Scoping and Design.
- Automated testing is the safety net for change (bug fixes, new features, regulatory updates). It covers core business logic and critical workflows for regression protection, supported by targeted manual end-to-end testing. Named tooling: xUnit and Postman.
- At cutover, a small group of AA users validates the billing calculation logic, including tiered time and split billing, and a sample set of invoices calculated end to end and reconciled against expected values.
- During the parallel run, AA reconciles billing calculations, invoice totals and revenue by anaesthetist, hospital and contract against the legacy system. Variances are investigated with Hypercare priority and corrected or explained before the parallel run closes.
