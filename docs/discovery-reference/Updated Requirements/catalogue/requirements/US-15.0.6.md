As AA, NHI is confined to the clinical and ledger systems and never propagates to finance platforms.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the NHI is treated as the most restricted element in the system and is held in the core platform only. Three design constraints stop health identifiers leaking through infrastructure rather than functionality:

- Logs and telemetry: the NHI is excluded from application logs, error messages and monitoring telemetry. Diagnostic data references internal record identifiers, not patient identity.
- Failed message handling: a dead-lettered HL7 message carries a full PID segment. Where failures are surfaced to office staff, the working view shows only what is needed to resolve the problem, and the raw payload is held under restricted access rather than displayed by default.
- Non-production environments: Development, Test and UAT hold no production patient data, only synthetic or de-identified data, so real NHIs exist in one environment only.

Separately, no client data (patient identifiers, NHI numbers, clinical detail, billing records, production extracts) enters AI coding tools; synthetic data is generated where realistic data is needed.
