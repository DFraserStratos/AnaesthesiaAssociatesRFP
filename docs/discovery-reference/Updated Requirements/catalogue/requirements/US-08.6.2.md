As an admin, when an invoiced Booking is found to be wrong, I raise a credit note against the receivable, reverse the linked payable, and re-issue a corrected invoice, all audited.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: an authorised invoice is never rewritten; correction is by credit note, and it must move both sides. A credit against the ACCREC without a matching reversal of the ACCPAY leaves the anaesthetist's ledger position out of step with what AA actually collected. The policy and who resolves it are to be worked through with AA in Scoping and Design (OQ-28).
