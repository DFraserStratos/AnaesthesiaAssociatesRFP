When an invoiced Booking turns out to be wrong, an admin credits the invoice in full and then rebills: a credit note against the receivable for the whole amount, a reversal of the [linked payable](US-08.3.1), and a new corrected invoice with its own number. Every step of this is [audited](US-13.5.2).

The reversal of the payable is a negative invoice to the anaesthetist (it is not called a BCTI credit). It is [netted in their next payment run](US-10.2.5) and shown on their remittance advice; the anaesthetist funds the refund.

Because an authorised invoice is never rewritten directly, correcting it always works this way, through a credit note and a fresh invoice, not by editing the original.

The original invoice is never edited, retracted or partly adjusted. A correction is also not an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), which is reserved for supplementary charges and splits. The trail reads debit, credit, contra, new debit, so AA's books and the anaesthetist's can both be explained to the accountants. Typical causes are an insurer paying twice, or both the patient and the insurer paying.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Credit in full.** The credit note reverses the original invoice's whole amount, even where only one line was wrong; the corrected amount is billed on the new invoice.
- **Traceable.** The credit note and the new invoice are each linked to the original invoice.
- **Both sides move.** The ledger payable and the Xero ACCREC and ACCPAY are reversed and re-created together. The payable is reversed by a negative invoice to the anaesthetist, netted in their next payment run.
- **Reaches the billable party.** The credit note reaches the billable party; Xero raises credit notes but does not send them, so the mechanism is to be designed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: an authorised invoice is never rewritten; correction is by credit note, and it must move both sides. A credit against the ACCREC without a matching reversal of the ACCPAY leaves the anaesthetist's ledger position out of step with what AA actually collected. AA's answer (OQ-28) settles the policy: credit in full, then rebill. Greg's direction is to do the business logic in the app and tell Xero the answer. Xero's credit notes are a weak spot (it raises one but does not send it to the customer), so getting the credit note to the billable party needs a workaround.
