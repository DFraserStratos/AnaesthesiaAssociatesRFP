The engine archives a Xero contact once every one of its invoices is fully paid and a defined inactivity window, for example 90 days, has passed with no further activity.

This keeps Xero's active contact count low even though most of AA's patients are one-time clients: AA raises around [28,000 invoices a year](US-15.0.4), and archiving means only a small number of contacts need to stay active at once. An archived contact keeps its full transaction history in Xero, so it can still be reported on or reused later.
