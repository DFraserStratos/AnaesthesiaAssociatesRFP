As an admin, I reassign a List (with all its bookings) to another anaesthetist, and the bookings, status history and audit trail are preserved.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response (answer to the RFP's Open Question 2, see OQ-08):

- Manual re-keying of Cards for illness cover is avoided, because it loses the Cards' audit history.
- The owning anaesthetist is a reference held on the List aggregate, the root to which its Cards, statuses, attachments and history are anchored. Reassignment changes that owner reference, so the whole List moves intact with its full change history.
- The move is captured as a single temporal-table event (from, to, by, when) that adds to history without rewriting it.
- Two-slots-per-day invariant: the swap consumes the covering anaesthetist's existing half-day slot rather than adding a third List, and the vacated slot returns to available (or is marked unavailable if more appropriate), so the calendar has no gaps or duplicates.

## Notes

Mechanism is an RFP open question; see OQ-08.
