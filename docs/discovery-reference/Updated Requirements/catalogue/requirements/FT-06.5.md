Prepayment money is held in AA's trust account as a pending payment and is not paid to the anaesthetist until the procedure is done. It is handled centrally by the office rather than by the anaesthetist.

- A cancelled Booking's prepayment is always refunded in full from the trust account.
- A Booking that moves to another anaesthetist keeps its prepayment under an honour system: the new anaesthetist claims no more than was prepaid, so the patient sees a stable amount (OQ-70). The system holds no logic that detects the move or recalculates anything. Whether the payable is still updated to the new anaesthetist on a move is OQ-80.

Today, if the anaesthetist has already been paid, they refund the patient themselves or pay the replacement anaesthetist directly, with a lot of back and forth.
