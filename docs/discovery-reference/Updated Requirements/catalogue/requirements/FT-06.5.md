Prepayment money that has to go back to the patient, most often because the Booking is cancelled, is handled centrally through AA's trust account rather than by the anaesthetist. The original prepayment is always refunded, and the next anaesthetist starts a new prepayment process.

This is a proposed process change (Greg's proposal, welcomed by Vanessa), to be confirmed with AA's financial authority (OQ-40).

Today, if the anaesthetist has already been paid, they refund the patient themselves or pay the replacement anaesthetist directly, with a lot of back and forth.
