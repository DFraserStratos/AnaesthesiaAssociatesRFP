The system tracks 'paid into AA' and 'disbursed to anaesthetist' as two separate states on every [ledger pair](US-08.3.1), the receivable and its matching payable.

This is so the ledger always shows whether money has arrived, been passed on, or both, rather than treating a payment as a single event.
