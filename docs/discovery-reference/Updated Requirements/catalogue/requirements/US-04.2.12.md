A Contract has a payment setting:

- full payment by the assigned billable party, or
- the Contract's line item split to divide the payment between parties, for example an insurer and the patient's gap

A Contract may be a single line item, so the split must be able to divide it in any way the parties need. Each party's share is a typed value in $ or % (for example $48 or 48%). The split is set on the Booking and defaults from the Contract.

With a split, the engine raises an invoice to each party ([split one Procedure's fee](US-08.2.3)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Contract set to full payment, when it is billed, then one party is invoiced for the whole line.
- Given a split Contract, when a Booking uses it, then the split defaults from the Contract and can be changed on the Booking.
- Given shares typed as a $ amount or a %, when billed, then each party is invoiced its share.

## Notes

Retired 2026-10-05 by Donald: neither a Contract nor a Procedure holds a "full payment or split" setting. Splitting a fee is a button on the Booking ([split one Procedure's fee](US-08.2.3)).
