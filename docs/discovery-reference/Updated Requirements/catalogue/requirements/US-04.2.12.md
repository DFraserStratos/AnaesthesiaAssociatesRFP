A Contract has a payment setting:

- full payment by the assigned billable party, or
- the Contract's line item split to divide the payment between parties, for example an insurer and the patient's gap

With a split, the engine raises an invoice to each party ([split one Procedure's fee](US-08.2.3)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Contract set to full payment, when it is billed, then one party is invoiced for the whole line.
- Given a Contract set to split, when it is billed, then each party is invoiced its share.

## Notes

The split basis (percentage only, percentage or a free field, or a typed value in $ or %), whether percentages must total 100, who sets the split (the Contract or the Booking) and whether the setting is a per-Contract default are still open (OQ-68).
