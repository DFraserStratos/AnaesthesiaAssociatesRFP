The engine puts its own unique invoice number in Xero's InvoiceNumber field, and the internal case reference in Xero's Reference field.

InvoiceNumber is the field hospitals and insurers quote back on remittance advices, so it is what an automated remittance matching process keys its lookups on. Reference is for internal tracing and staff-facing display only, since Xero does not enforce uniqueness on it.
