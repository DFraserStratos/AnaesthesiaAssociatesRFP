When [the selected Contract allows an adjustment](US-04.2.2), the anaesthetist sees an extra field where they can enter either a percentage discount or a fixed final price. Before the adjustment can be saved, the anaesthetist must record a reason for it.

This is so the office, and the audit trail, always show why a fee was discounted or fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Adjustment field.** When the selected Contract allows an adjustment, the anaesthetist sees an extra field where they can enter either a percentage discount or a fixed final price.
- **Reason required.** The anaesthetist must record a reason with any adjustment before it can be saved.
