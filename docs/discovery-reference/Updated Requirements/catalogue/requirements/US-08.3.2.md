As the system, the internal ledger, not Xero, is the source of truth for balances shown to anaesthetists and admins.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the system of record for what is owed. A dedicated Xero instance sits behind it as an accounts receivable and banking add-on only, not the system of record and not AA's general ledger.
