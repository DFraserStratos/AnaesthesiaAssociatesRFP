Flat outstanding list and GST-aligned activity summary.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the system of record for what is owed, with Xero operating behind it as the accounts-receivable, banking and payment service. Payments are collected into AA's account, and amounts due to anaesthetists are tracked separately through linked receivable and payable records.
