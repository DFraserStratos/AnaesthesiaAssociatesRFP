Anaesthetists get two reports of their own: a flat list of what is owed to them, and a periodic summary of their activity aligned to their own GST period.

Both read from the Billing/Invoice Engine's [own ledger](US-08.3.2), not from Xero directly, since that ledger is the system of record for what is owed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the system of record for what is owed, with Xero operating behind it as the accounts-receivable, banking and payment service. Payments are collected into AA's account, and amounts due to anaesthetists are tracked separately through linked receivable and payable records.
