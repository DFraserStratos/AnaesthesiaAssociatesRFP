Whenever a Booking's Procedures or Contract change before the procedure takes place, or whenever a List or a Booking is moved, the Billing/Invoice Engine re-checks the prepayment requirement and amount. When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist, the re-check does not change the agreed amount ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

This is so a Booking that no longer needs prepayment, for example because its payment category changed, has that cleared automatically rather than left stale on the Booking.

## Notes

No RFP passage supports this. It comes from a diagram Donald created and Greg (a subject matter expert) checked. Until AA says otherwise, treat it as more correct than the RFP (OQ-35).

The re-check on a move comes from the 2026-10-01 meeting: any time a List or a procedure is moved, prepayments are re-checked. Keeping the agreed amount on a move to another anaesthetist follows Ben's rule (OQ-70). Whether the new anaesthetist's own prepaid list can change whether prepayment is required at all was not discussed.
