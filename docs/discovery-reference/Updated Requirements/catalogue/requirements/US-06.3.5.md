Whenever a Booking's Procedures or Contract change before the procedure takes place, the Billing/Invoice Engine re-checks the prepayment requirement and amount.

This is so a Booking that no longer needs prepayment, for example because its payment category changed, has that cleared automatically rather than left stale on the Booking.
