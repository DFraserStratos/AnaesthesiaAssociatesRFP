The billable party is always defined by the Contract, and there can be as many Contracts as AA needs. When an admin or anaesthetist picks a default Contract for a Procedure, they enter the name and email of whoever pays, for example a guardian paying for a child.

The billable party is independent of how the Procedure is priced. A hospital taking the invoice for work priced under the [default RVG Contract](US-04.3.3), for example Christchurch Eye taking the invoice instead of the patient, or, for a Southern Cross-insured patient, Christchurch Eye taking the invoice and being reimbursed by the insurer, is a Contract with that hospital as billable party.

This covers cases where the person responsible for payment is not the patient themselves.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a default RVG Contract picked for a Procedure, when the payer's name and email (a guardian) are entered, then the invoice goes to that person and the price is unchanged.

## Notes

This replaces a per-Booking override of the billable party (OQ-67): every payer arrangement is a Contract.

A guardian's details are more a Contract matter than master data: they last for the life of the debt and are then archived (Greg).

The screenshots predate OQ-67 and show the retired per-Booking payer override; re-capture once the prototype follows the Contract-defined payer.
