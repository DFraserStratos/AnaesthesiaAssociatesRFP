Admins and anaesthetists can override the default and set a different billable party and invoice email on a Booking, for example a guardian paying for a child.

The billable party is independent of how the Procedure is priced. An admin can send the invoice to a hospital while the Procedure stays priced under the [default RVG Contract](US-04.3.3), for example Christchurch Eye taking the invoice instead of the patient, or, for a Southern Cross-insured patient, Christchurch Eye taking the invoice and being reimbursed by the insurer.

This covers cases where the person responsible for payment is not the patient themselves.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure on the default RVG Contract, when an admin sets a hospital as billable party, then the price is unchanged and the invoice goes to the hospital.

## Notes

OQ-55's answer says the Contract defines the billable party, and there can be many Contracts for any mix needed. Whether the per-Booking override here stays, or is replaced by a Contract for each arrangement, is OQ-67.

A guardian's details are more a Contract matter than master data: they last for the life of the debt and are then archived (Greg).
