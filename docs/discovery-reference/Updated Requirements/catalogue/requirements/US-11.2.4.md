When a patient under 18 is the billable party on a Booking, the system gives AA staff a mild warning. There is no warning when the billable party is someone else, for example a hospital.

The warning does not block saving the Booking or authorising the List. It shows on the [dashboard to-do list](US-13.7.2), where an admin can clear it, and reminds the office to double check the [invoice email](US-11.2.3). Where the Contract makes the patient the default billable party, that is where a parent's or guardian's email goes. There is no separate guardian record.

This is because an invoice for a child normally goes to a parent or guardian, and today the office catches this by hand at review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient under 18 as billable party on a Booking, then a mild warning is raised.
- Given a hospital as billable party for a patient under 18, then no warning is raised.
- Given the warning, when an admin clears it, then it leaves the to-do list.

## Notes

Age comes from the patient's date of birth on the [patient record](US-11.1.1).
