Where the patient is under 18, the patient cannot be the billable party. An adult, such as a parent or guardian, has to be set as the [billable party and invoice email](US-11.2.2) instead.

The system flags a Booking whose billable party is a patient under 18, at booking setup and again at the [office review](US-07.2.2). Whether the flag also stops the List being authorised is OQ-54.

This is because an invoice must never go to a child, and today the office catches it by hand at review: a Miss or Master under 18 has to be moved under Mr or Mrs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient under 18 with themselves as billable party, when the Booking is set up or reviewed, then it is flagged.
- Given an adult is set as billable party, when the Booking is saved, then the flag clears.

## Notes

Age comes from the patient's date of birth on the [patient record](US-11.1.1).
