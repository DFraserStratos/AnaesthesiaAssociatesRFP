Every price a Contract holds carries an effective-from date: each [fixed fee schedule line](US-04.2.4), and a Contract's rate or discount percentage. Admins can enter a new price ahead of time, so a change a hospital announces with a few weeks' notice is already in place when it takes effect.

Hospitals and funders send an annual schedule and then email additions during the year, especially as outsourced DHB Lists change, usually with an effective date. The same procedure can have different prices under different funding arrangements. Each arrangement is its own Contract, offered through the [filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2).

This is so one Contract can take a mid-year price change without a new Contract being made, and older prices stay for [invoices that have to be reproduced](US-08.4.4).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a schedule line with a price effective 1 April and another effective 1 July, when a Procedure is priced, then the price in force on the deciding date is used (which date decides is OQ-48).
- Given a price entered with a future effective-from date, when admins view the Contract before that date, then both the current and the upcoming price are visible.

## Notes

The Contract already carries its own effective-from, effective-to and review dates ([create, edit, retire Contracts](US-04.1.2)); this story puts the date on the price lines inside it.
