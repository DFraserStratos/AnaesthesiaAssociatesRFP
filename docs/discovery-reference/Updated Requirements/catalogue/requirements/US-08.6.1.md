An admin creates an additional invoice for a billing line dated after the Booking's List has already been invoiced, for example a post-operative review. The anaesthetist tells the office (today by email, for instance a 15 minute post-op visit) and the admin raises the invoice from the original.

The charge is built from time units and/or modifiers. It is created as an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) attached to the original Booking and Procedure, so it has its own [receivable and payable pair](US-08.3.1) and its own [invoice number](US-08.4.3), [without unlocking or changing the original Booking](US-07.3.2). No extra approval is needed.

## Notes

Flow settled by OQ-24: additional invoices attached to the Booking's Procedure. Solutions Plus does this today with a "next account", another invoice with its own number, attached back to the original (see OQ-51). What pricing rules the lines follow is OQ-45.
