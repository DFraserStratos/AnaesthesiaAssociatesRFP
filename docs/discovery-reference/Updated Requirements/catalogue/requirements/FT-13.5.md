Role-based access and full audit of manual and automated actions.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Authentication and access management across the web and mobile apps through an Identity-as-a-Service product, either Auth0 or Microsoft Entra External ID. Both hold ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certification, which underpin HISO 10029 compliance. Best fit to be confirmed in discovery.
- MFA recommended as enforced for Admin accounts, given the sensitivity of the financial and patient information.
- Anaesthetists reset their own passwords securely; admins provision accounts without ever having access to user credentials.
- Every login attempt is logged and auditable, supporting HISO 10029 and access reviews.
- Anaesthetists on mobile additionally get social login via Google and Apple for one-tap login.
