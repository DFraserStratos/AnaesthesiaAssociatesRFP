The [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) function has a credit note option. An admin credits the original invoice, and any party can receive the credit, the original billable party included. New additional invoices are then raised.

The credit and the new invoices are recorded as [events on the Procedure](FT-03.7).

This is so the office can credit and rebill an invoice, for example when an insurer asks for a combined Procedure to be split out, without editing the original (OQ-72).

A credit note is not a refund ("One's cash, one's not"). Refunding a credit that results happens outside the system.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Credit to a chosen party.** Given an invoiced Procedure, when the admin uses the credit note option, then a credit against the original invoice is raised to the party they pick.
- **Recorded as an event.** Given a credit, then it shows as an event on the Procedure.
- **No refund.** The credit note option does not pay a refund.

## Notes

The practical tooling for credit and rebill is partly a question for Vanessa. Greg's view is that the credit "should automatically include a reversal"; whether that means it reverses the [linked payable](US-08.3.1) as in [credit note and re-issue](US-08.6.2) is open, with the rebill total (OQ-77).
