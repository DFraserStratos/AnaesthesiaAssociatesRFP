Admins see the ledger's position at two scopes: the whole ledger, and a single anaesthetist.

At the whole-ledger scope, this covers:

- receivables outstanding
- receipts held
- payables due
- amounts disbursed
- any imbalance between these

At the anaesthetist scope, admins see that anaesthetist's own ledger position: what is owing to them, and what they owe. This is the same balance view as the whole ledger, scoped to a single anaesthetist.

This gives the office one place to check whether the ledger is in balance, both overall and for a single anaesthetist.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Per anaesthetist.** Admins see each anaesthetist's own ledger position: what is owing to them, and what they owe, using the same balance view as the whole ledger, scoped to a single anaesthetist.
