AA uses a separate Xero organisation solely for receivables, payables to anaesthetists and banking. It is never used for AA's general accounting.

This is because the Billing/Invoice Engine, not Xero, is the system of record for what is owed. Xero sits beneath it as an accounts receivable, payment collection and banking add-on, one of the consumption-based platform services already reflected in the hosting and support cost.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the system of record for what is owed. A dedicated Xero instance sits beneath it as an accounts receivable, payment collection and banking add-on only, not AA's general ledger. The dedicated Xero organisation is one of the consumption-based platform services already reflected in the hosting and support cost.
