Each anaesthetist gets a schedule to help them prepare their GST return, aligned to their [own GST period](US-12.1.2). It shows the sales AA made for them, the [GST component](US-05.2.7) of each, and a check that it balances with what they were paid.

GST is on a cash basis, so the schedule lists only the payables AA actually paid them in the period. Anything still outstanding falls into a later period.
