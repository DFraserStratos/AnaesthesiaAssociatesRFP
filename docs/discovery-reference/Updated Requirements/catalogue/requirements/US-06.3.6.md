When an admin approves a [generated prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) to send, they pick one of a small set of standard letter templates instead of writing each letter by hand. Admins maintain the templates.

Every template states the prepaid amount as the price payable before the procedure, not as an estimate. A template may say that if the procedure turns out very different from what was planned, the anaesthetist may raise a further invoice or a credit. The letter is filled in from the Booking (patient, procedure, prepaid amount, anaesthetist) and goes out with the prepayment invoice.

This is because the office writes every prepayment letter by hand today.
