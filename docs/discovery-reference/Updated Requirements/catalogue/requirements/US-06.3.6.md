When an admin approves a [generated prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) to send, they pick one of a small set of standard letter templates instead of writing each letter by hand. Admins maintain the templates.

Every template words the amount as an estimate that is payable before the procedure, and says the final amount may be higher or lower ([prepayment is an estimate](US-06.2.3)). The letter is filled in from the Booking (patient, procedure, estimated amount, anaesthetist) and goes out with the prepayment invoice.

This is because patients query a final amount that comes out higher than the prepayment, and because the office writes every prepayment letter by hand today.
