As the system, I handle the same Booking being edited by two sources close together without losing either change silently.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response (answer to the RFP's Open Question 1, see OQ-07): optimistic concurrency, not locking. Cards and Procedures are editable concurrently from hospital messages, the anaesthetist app and the admin web app up to the day of the procedure; every source edits freely and conflicts are caught at save. Locking was rejected for a worse user experience and the risk of records being left locked.

- Row-version backstop in Azure SQL: each Card and Procedure carries a row version, read with the data and checked on update, so silent data loss is impossible even for simultaneous edits.
- Resolved in the domain first: non-conflicting edits (different fields) are merged and both kept; only genuine same-field contention is escalated to a person as a clear, actionable message, and a raw database rejection never reaches a user as a technical error.
- Which fields may auto-apply and which must stop for a person is a business policy, to be mapped field-by-field with AA in discovery.
