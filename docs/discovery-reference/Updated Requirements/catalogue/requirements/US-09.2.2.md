As the system, I poll Xero daily for paid invoices to catch missed webhooks.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: extend the daily reconciliation poll beyond payment detection so it also confirms that authorised invoices in Xero still match what the engine issued (for example, catching an invoice voided or amended directly in Xero). The policy for each mismatch, and who resolves it, is to be confirmed during Scoping and Design.
