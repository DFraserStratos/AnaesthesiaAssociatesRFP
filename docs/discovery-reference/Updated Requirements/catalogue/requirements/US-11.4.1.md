As an admin, I maintain insurers with an acceptsDirectClaims flag; direct insurers hold Insurance Contracts.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: insurer terms tend to be schedule-based (a maximum payable per procedure) rather than a discount applied to a counterparty. The response leans toward extending how the Contract model resolves rates rather than building a parallel rate structure.

- AA to advise how likely direct insurer billing is to grow, and whether cover split between an insurer and the patient is a real case (see OQ-20, OQ-23).
- A Contract that resolves rates differently becomes a rule within the existing model, proven by automated tests, and is resolved during Scoping and Design.
