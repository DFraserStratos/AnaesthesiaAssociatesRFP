Admins maintain the insurer master list, each with an accepts direct claims flag.

Where an insurer accepts direct claims, it holds one or more Insurance Contracts governing how procedures billed to it are priced.

## Technical discussion

AA confirmed (OQ-20) that an insurer needs no rate table of its own. Special rates, for example for nib, are held as insurer Contracts in the existing Contract model, not a parallel rate structure.

- Insurer terms tend to be schedule-based (a maximum payable per procedure) rather than a discount applied to a counterparty. A Contract that resolves rates differently becomes a rule within the existing model, proven by automated tests, and is resolved during Scoping and Design.
- Whether cover split between an insurer and the patient is a real case is still open (OQ-23).
