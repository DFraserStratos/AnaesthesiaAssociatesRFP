As the system, a failure on one Booking does not silently block or discard the rest of the List.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: one authorised List can produce several invoice pairs across counterparties, and if one fails the earlier ones already in Xero cannot be unwound. The expected handling is to track billing state per invoice rather than per List, so partial runs are resumable and visible. The specific policy for each case, and who resolves it, is to be confirmed during Scoping and Design (OQ-05 remains open).

## Notes

Whether it blocks the whole List is open; see OQ-05.
