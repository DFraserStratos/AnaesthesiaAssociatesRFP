The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Booking as requiring prepayment whenever any of its Procedures carries an RVG code in the [anaesthetist's prepaid set](US-06.1.1), and only where the patient (the person paying for them) is the billable party.

This is checked across the whole Booking, not just its primary Procedure, since the typical case is a hospital Booking with an additional, elective cosmetic Procedure added on.

## Notes

Q&A #5: prepaid is a property of the procedure choice, not the contract. There is no [Pre-paid Contract category](US-04.1.1), so the Contract never triggers it (OQ-25).

The patient-only limit comes from the OQ-58 answer. Whether "patient" means the person paying for the patient, and what happens when the patient is not the billable party, is OQ-73.
