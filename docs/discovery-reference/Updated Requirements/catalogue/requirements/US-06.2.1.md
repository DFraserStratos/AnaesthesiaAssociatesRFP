The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Booking as requiring prepayment whenever any of its Procedures carries an RVG code in the anaesthetist's prepaid set.

This is checked across the whole Booking, not just its primary Procedure, since the typical case is a hospital Booking with an additional, elective cosmetic Procedure added on.

## Notes

Q&A #5: prepaid is a property of the procedure choice, not the contract. Whether a Pre-paid Contract category also triggers it is OQ-25.
