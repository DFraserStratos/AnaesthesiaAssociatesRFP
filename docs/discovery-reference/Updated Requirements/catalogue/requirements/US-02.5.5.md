As the system, I keep an append-only change log for Bookings and Procedures (including contract selection and adjustments), so an invoice can be reproduced against what was true when raised.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: full change history and invoice reproducibility across roughly 28,000 invoices a year.

- Azure SQL system-versioned (temporal) tables at the data layer act as the audit log, retaining who, what and when for every change to key scheduling and billing data, not a single last-modified stamp.
- Because the database records history automatically, no write path can bypass it, and point-in-time reconstruction (what a Card looked like at an instant) is a direct query rather than a log replay.
- Alternatives considered: trigger-based audit tables were rejected as more bespoke and weaker at point-in-time reconstruction; a domain-event log may be used where the reason for a change matters, but is not the primary history mechanism.
