The Scheduling Engine keeps an append-only change log for Bookings and Procedures, including every Contract selection and adjustment.

Every entry records its source, whether a person or an [integration](US-14.6.2) that applied the change automatically.

This is so an invoice can always be [reproduced exactly](US-08.4.4) as it was when it was raised, even after the Booking, Procedure or Contract has since changed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: full change history and invoice reproducibility across roughly 28,000 invoices a year.

- Azure SQL system-versioned (temporal) tables at the data layer act as the audit log, retaining who, what and when for every change to key scheduling and billing data, not a single last-modified stamp.
- Because the database records history automatically, no write path can bypass it, and point-in-time reconstruction (what a Booking looked like at an instant) is a direct query rather than a log replay.
- Alternatives considered: trigger-based audit tables were rejected as more bespoke and weaker at point-in-time reconstruction; a domain-event log may be used where the reason for a change matters, but is not the primary history mechanism.
