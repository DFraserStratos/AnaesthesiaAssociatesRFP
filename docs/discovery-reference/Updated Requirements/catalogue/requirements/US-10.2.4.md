Bulk hospital payments that cover multiple invoices at once are matched by Xero's own bank reconciliation and its remittance add-on, not by the Billing/Invoice Engine.

Any item the remittance add-on cannot match automatically is left as an unmatched item in Xero's bank reconciliation, for office staff to handle there.
