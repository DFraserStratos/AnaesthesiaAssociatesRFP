The Scheduling Engine updates an existing Booking whenever a modification arrives, from any source.

Every update records who or what changed it, when, and what changed.

## Notes

A change that arrives from a hospital still goes through the admin's review before it is applied (see [automatic sync](US-02.1.5)).
