When a Contract defines a [Contract arranged rate](US-04.2.2), the system prices that [procedure](US-03.3.6) at the specified rate.

It is a unit rate, not an hourly rate: an alternative to the [anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1), defined on the Contract.

## Notes

Renamed from "Rate x time pricing" (2026-10-02). Donald: "it's not an hourly rate, because it's not hourly, it's unit rate"; "contract-defined rate, because alternative rates don't exist on anything other than a contract." Whether it is the same basis as the agreed contract rate in US-05.2.1 and [Contract pricing](US-04.2.2) is OQ-89.
