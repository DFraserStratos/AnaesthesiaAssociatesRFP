Admin normally sets the Contract when the Booking is set up. It is visible to the anaesthetist, who may change it; admin then approves the selection during the SUBMITTED review, after the anaesthetist submits the List (OQ-26). The anaesthetist can therefore change the Contract freely until they submit.
