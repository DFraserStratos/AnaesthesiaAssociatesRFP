Some Contracts carry a fixed discount, for example 10%. The system prices the Procedure as usual, BTM units multiplied by [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1), and applies the Contract's discount as a price override already in place.

- Where the app shows the price override, the anaesthetist sees the discount applied, and cannot edit or remove it.
- The office can still [override the price](US-05.4.2) at review.

This is so a discount agreed with a Contract holder is applied every time, without the anaesthetist having to remember it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Discount applied.** Given a Contract with a fixed discount of 10% and a calculated price of $1,000, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is $900.
- **Shown and locked.** Given that Procedure, when the anaesthetist opens its price override, then the 10% discount shows as applied and cannot be edited or removed.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design keeps a contract-level discount as dormant: stored, with no logic or screens, and its calculated price step has a term for the anaesthetist's entered discount but none for the Contract's ([AR-29 p7](AR-29#p7), [p10](AR-29#p10)). It now needs logic and screens. The design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
