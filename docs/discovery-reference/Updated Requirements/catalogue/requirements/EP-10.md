Every payment, whoever pays it, lands in the AA account first. AA then disburses to the anaesthetist, so the system tracks two separate states for every [ledger pair](US-08.3.1): paid into AA, and disbursed to the anaesthetist. Paying a receivable in full releases the matching payable in full; a part payment releases exactly the amount received.

AA's own fee is billed separately, on its own invoice from AA to the anaesthetist, distinct from any procedure's receivable and payable.

This replaces the old arrangement where some hospital contract holders paid anaesthetists directly and AA reconciled the payment afterwards. AA is now always the intermediary, so disbursement is a first-class, trackable process in its own right.
