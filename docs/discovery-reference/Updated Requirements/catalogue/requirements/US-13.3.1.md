Admins see, for every AUTHORISED List, whether it has been invoiced, sent to the billable party, and mirrored to Xero.

Where any of that has failed, admins see why it failed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: AA staff have clear visibility of Xero sync status for each record. Failed items are surfaced for review rather than silently dropped.
