Today AA staff take bookings from the hospitals' daily sheets and downloads and match them to Lists and Bookings on a matching screen. Only St George's and Southern Cross are integrated with the current system; everything from the other providers is entered by hand.

This manual download and match is the real pathway the system supports, and today it carries the most bookings (OQ-34), so it is the priority intake pathway.

The first release keeps matching a manual admin review, and adds an automatic sync from St George's and Southern Cross into the screen. More hospital feeds and automatic matching are Future Work ([feeds from the other Christchurch providers](US-14.6.1)).
