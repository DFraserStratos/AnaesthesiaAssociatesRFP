A billable [event](US-03.7.1) produces a new invoice, traceable to the original Procedure, priced from its recorded time or its fixed fee. The Procedure's Contract can replace a recorded time with a fixed fee: the anaesthetist may see the patient for a time, but only the fee is charged.

Provisionally, pending OQ-63, the invoice goes through the admin's approval before it is issued (Donald: "probably the same approval flows"), unlike an admin's [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), which needs none.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a billable event with a time, when it is approved, then a new invoice is raised, traceable to the original Procedure.
- Given a Contract with a fixed fee for the event, then the fee is charged instead of the time.
- Given an event not yet approved, then no invoice is issued (provisional, pending OQ-63).

## Notes

The approval step, and whether the event shows on the invoice as a line item or a procedure, are OQ-63.
