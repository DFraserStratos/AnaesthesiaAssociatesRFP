Admins maintain all of the system's reference data, in one place:

- hospitals, each with a contact email for booking updates
- [surgeons](US-13.6.2) and surgeon groups
- [surgeons' rooms](US-13.6.1)
- [insurers](US-11.4.1)
- anaesthetists
- [Slot availability statuses](US-01.2.2)
- [recurring bookings](US-01.3.2)
- the master (public holiday) calendar, and [each hospital's own holiday calendar](US-01.5.1)
- RVG codes and groups
- modifier codes
- Contracts

This is master data: the rest of the system refers to it by reference rather than copying it, so a change here is reflected everywhere it's used.

## Notes

Greg: "I wouldn't call those reference tables. Some of those are definitely master data", and he is happy for the developers to sort out what is master data. The story already treats them as master data, referred to rather than copied. The title still says reference tables, for the owner to decide.
