Admins maintain all of the system's reference data, in one place:

- hospitals
- surgeons and surgeon groups
- [insurers](US-11.4.1)
- anaesthetists
- [list statuses](US-01.2.2)
- [Permanent Lists](US-01.3.2)
- the master (public holiday) calendar, and [each hospital's own holiday calendar](US-01.5.1)
- RVG codes and groups
- modifier codes
- Contracts

This is master data: the rest of the system refers to it by reference rather than copying it, so a change here is reflected everywhere it's used.
