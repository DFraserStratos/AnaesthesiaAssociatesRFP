An admin can view and edit an anaesthetist's [prepaid settings](US-06.1.1) on their behalf.

The prepaid amounts themselves sit on the anaesthetist's [own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14), which the office always creates and maintains from the price list the anaesthetist supplies (OQ-91).
