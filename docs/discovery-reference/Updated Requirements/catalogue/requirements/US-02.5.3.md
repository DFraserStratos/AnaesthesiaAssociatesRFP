The Scheduling Engine records a cancellation and updates the schedule accordingly, including a cancellation that arrives automatically from an integration.

The cancelled Booking is never removed: it stays visible on the List, marked as cancelled, and in the List's history.

If the Booking had a paid prepayment, its refund is covered by [refund a prepayment on cancellation](US-06.5.2) (OQ-40).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Automated cancellation.** Given a cancellation arrives from an integration, when it is applied, then the Booking is marked cancelled and stays visible on its List, and its change history records the integration as the source.

## Notes

Donald's reading of why it stays visible: "an anaesthetist can turn up to the hospital and they don't have a mismatch where one system might say it's there and another one doesn't."

AA charges no cancellation fees: a cancelled Booking's loss is simply taken (OQ-01).

Automated changes from an integration come after MVP, with [automatic matching and updates](US-14.6.2); until then an admin records a cancellation through [manual booking entry](FT-02.3) or the [matching review](US-02.1.5).
