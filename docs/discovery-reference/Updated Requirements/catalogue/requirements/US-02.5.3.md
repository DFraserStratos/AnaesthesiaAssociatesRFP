The Scheduling Engine records a cancellation and updates the schedule accordingly.

The cancelled Booking is never removed: it stays visible in the List's history.

If the Booking had a paid prepayment, its refund is covered by [refund a prepayment on cancellation](US-06.5.2) (OQ-40).

## Notes

AA charges no cancellation fees: a cancelled Booking's loss is simply taken (OQ-01).
