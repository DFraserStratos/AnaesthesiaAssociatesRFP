On every booking screen, in the admin app and the anaesthetist app, each Procedure is shown as a stack of three, top to bottom:

1. **As received**: the exact procedure text [kept from the system it came from](US-02.5.7), such as the surgeon's rooms or the hospital, with fuller wording from a later update shown too
2. **Procedure**: the procedure chosen in the system and its RVG code
3. **Contract**: the Contract's name and holder, who is invoiced, and its pricing basis with the figure, for example "Fixed $2,400", "10% off RVG" or "RVG"

Our reading of "every booking screen": the Booking's views in both apps, the hospital matching screen and the office review.

This is because the source wording is not plain English: rooms and hospitals send short codes and snippets, in endless variety, that someone who knows the work can read. Room text is often abbreviated ("WLE MM", "RTK"), while hospital lists are often fuller ("wide local excision", "right total knee"), and the person checking the match needs to see both. The invoice uses the procedure's own wording, which is "worded as it should appear on the invoice", since the patient would not understand the source text.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Both apps.** Given a Booking from any source, then each Procedure shows its source text, then its procedure and RVG code, then its Contract, in that order, in the admin app and the anaesthetist app.
- **Contract summary.** Given a Procedure on a fixed-price Contract held by Southern Cross, then the Contract part shows the Contract's name, Southern Cross as holder, who is invoiced, and the fixed price.
- **Not matched yet.** Given a Procedure with no procedure or Contract chosen yet, then the source text still shows, with the empty parts marked as still to choose (a blank procedure at setup is OQ-99).
- **Fuller wording kept.** Given a later hospital update with fuller wording for the same procedure, then that wording is kept and shown too.
