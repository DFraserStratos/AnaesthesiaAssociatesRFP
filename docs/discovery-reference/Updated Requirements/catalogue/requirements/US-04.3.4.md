Admins apply the Contract to each Procedure at booking setup, when the Booking is created or matched, ahead of the day of the procedure. Setting it is mandatory: a Booking without one stays on the admin's list until it is sorted. The billable party and the billing approach (the Contract) are the critical things to settle before the anaesthetist fills in the Booking.

Every Procedure on a Booking is matched to a procedure on the [master list](US-05.1.6), even when it is priced at a fixed price.

This is different from the RFP's original design, where the billing route and its Contract were only resolved by the engine once [the List was authorised](US-07.3.1). Setting the Contract at booking setup instead means office staff can see and check it well before the procedure happens.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure with no Contract, then its Booking stays on the admin's list until a Contract is set.

## Notes

Today the office sets the Contract from the hospital's daily sheets. In the future state the hospital defines it through its inbound data: see [hospital data sets the Contract](US-04.3.6) (OQ-22).

At the 7 October client meeting AA asked that the RVG code or procedure can be left blank at setup when the office cannot match it confidently, for the anaesthetist to fill in. What that allows is OQ-99.
