The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the [base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation](FT-05.2).

A Contract with its own fixed rate is not one of them: it [prices the whole procedure](US-05.2.6) at the Contract's unit rate, so it needs no billing line.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Other billing lines.** The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation, including:
  - post-op ward or HDU reviews
  - nerve catheters
  - pain consults
  - medical transport

  Some of these happen days after the original procedure, so a billing line can carry its own later date.

## Notes

Merges the former US-03.3.7 (Rate x time capture), now Retired. Its rate x time charge was folded in as one more billing line, reworded on 2026-10-02 to a unit x rate line, and is now removed (below). Both items were Proposed, so status is unchanged.

Post-care the anaesthetist adds after the procedure, such as post-op reviews and pain consults, may instead be captured as [events](FT-03.7). OQ-63 settled an event as its own element on the Procedure and its own invoice line; whether post-op reviews and pain consults leave this billing-line list entirely for events is not stated.

Resolved (OQ-89, questions 1 and 2): the directors said "Some contracts DO have a fixed rate contract", and Donald, expanding it: the fixed rate "will obviously still use the RVG BTM units, but it would be multiplying it by a fixed rate as opposed to the anaesthetist rate". So a fixed-rate Contract prices the whole procedure, not a billing line, and the unit x rate billing line (titled "UNIT X RATE" on 2026-10-02) is removed. Contract add-on fees are also removed: the add-on flag on fee schedule lines was dropped on 7 October (Greg: "No, I don't think so").
