The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the [base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation](FT-05.2). This includes a [Contract defined rate](US-05.2.6), captured as a unit x rate billing line through the same path as any other billing line, so it needs no separate screen or workflow of its own.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Other billing lines.** The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation, including:
  - post-op ward or HDU reviews
  - nerve catheters
  - pain consults
  - medical transport
  - Contract add-on fees

  Some of these happen days after the original procedure, so a billing line can carry its own later date.
- **Unit x rate.** Where a Procedure's [Contract defines its own rate](US-05.2.6), the anaesthetist captures that charge as a unit x rate billing line, through the same billing line capture path used for any other billing line. This means it needs no separate screen or workflow of its own.

## Notes

Merges the former US-03.3.7 (Rate x time capture), now Retired. US-03.3.7's own description states the rate x time charge goes through the same billing line capture path used for any other billing line, so it is one instance of the list this story already covers, not a separate capability. Both items were Proposed, so status is unchanged.

Post-care the anaesthetist adds after the procedure, such as post-op reviews and pain consults, may instead be captured as [events](FT-03.7). OQ-63 settled an event as its own element on the Procedure and its own invoice line; whether post-op reviews and pain consults leave this billing-line list entirely for events is not stated.

Retitled to "UNIT X RATE" on 2026-10-02, as the Contract defined rate is a unit rate, not an hourly rate x time. Donald: "We'll come back to that when we get there." In tension: US-05.2.6 now prices the whole Procedure at the Contract's unit rate, while this story treats it as a billing line outside the BTM calculation (OQ-89).
