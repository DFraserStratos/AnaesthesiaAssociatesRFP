The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the [base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation](FT-05.2). This includes an individually arranged hourly rate, captured as a [rate x time billing line](US-05.2.6) through the same path as any other billing line, so an hourly arrangement needs no separate screen or workflow of its own.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Other billing lines.** The anaesthetist can add billing lines to a Procedure that sit outside the base, time and modifier (BTM) calculation, including:
  - post-op ward or HDU reviews
  - nerve catheters
  - pain consults
  - medical transport
  - Contract add-on fees

  Some of these happen days after the original procedure, so a billing line can carry its own later date.
- **Rate x time.** Where a Procedure's Contract permits an individually arranged hourly rate, the anaesthetist captures that charge as a rate x time billing line, through the same billing line capture path used for any other billing line. This means an hourly arrangement needs no separate screen or workflow of its own.

## Notes

Merges the former US-03.3.7 (Rate x time capture), now Retired. US-03.3.7's own description states the rate x time charge goes through the same billing line capture path used for any other billing line, so it is one instance of the list this story already covers, not a separate capability. Both items were Proposed, so status is unchanged.

Post-care the anaesthetist adds after the procedure, such as post-op reviews and pain consults, may instead be captured as [pre-op and post-op events](FT-03.7). How those events relate to billing lines is not settled (OQ-63).
