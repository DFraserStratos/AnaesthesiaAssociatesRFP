A negative invoice to an anaesthetist, from a refund or a [credit after invoicing](US-08.6.2), is netted against their positive payables in the next payment run, for example 19 positive invoices and one negative.

The anaesthetist sees the run washed up in their remittance advice. Netting this way means the anaesthetist bears the refund.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given 19 positive payables and one negative invoice in a payment run, when the run is paid, then the payment to the anaesthetist is the net total.
- Given a negative invoice in a payment run, when the remittance advice is produced, then it shows the negative invoice netted against the positive ones.

## Notes

When there is no later positive payment to net against, for example a refund after the month's last payment run with no later work, AA settles it with the anaesthetist outside the system (OQ-71). Nothing is built for this case. A cancelled prepayment is not this case: that money is still in trust and the anaesthetist has not been paid.
