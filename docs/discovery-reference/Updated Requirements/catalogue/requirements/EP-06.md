Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, must be paid for before they go ahead. Which ones is a setting each anaesthetist keeps on their own profile, keyed on RVG codes or [whole groups of codes](US-05.1.3).

Once a Booking needs prepayment, AA invoices it at booking setup, tracks it up to the day of the procedure, and settles it against the final calculated amount afterwards.

Prepayment money is held in AA's trust account until the procedure is done (see [trust account and cancellation refunds](FT-06.5)). If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment is refunded in full. If the Booking moves to another anaesthetist, the agreed amount stays, and the anaesthetist who does the procedure is paid and wears or benefits from the difference (OQ-70).
