As the system, for every invoice I create a receivable from the billable party and a matching payable to the anaesthetist, linked to each other and to the Booking and Procedures.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Payments are collected into AA's account, and amounts due to anaesthetists are tracked separately through linked receivable and payable records.
- The ACCREC and ACCPAY are expected to be treated as a unit that completes or backs out together, since a pair created only on the ACCPAY side is money owed to an anaesthetist with no receivable behind it.
