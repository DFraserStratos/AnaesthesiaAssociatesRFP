When the procedure is rebooked with a different anaesthetist after a cancelled prepaid Booking, that anaesthetist's own prepaid process starts afresh: the system raises a new prepayment for them, estimated at their [own unit value](US-12.1.1) ([prepayment estimate](US-06.2.4)).

The original prepayment is not moved between anaesthetists, because their rates differ. The patient may therefore see two different estimates.

This is so each anaesthetist is prepaid at their own rate and nothing has to be transferred between them.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a refunded prepayment and a replacement anaesthetist with a different unit value, when the Booking is made under them, then a new prepayment is raised at their rate.
