Every surgeon has a profile page in the Admin App, held on the surgeon master list. It records:

- the surgeon's name and the [room](US-13.6.1) they belong to
- their New Zealand medical registration number
- their HPI number (Health Provider Index)
- their CPN number
- the [blacklist](US-13.6.3) of anaesthetists they do not work with

This is so a surgeon is one identified record rather than free text, the same way an anaesthetist has [a profile](US-12.1.4). Solutions Plus already keeps these fields on its surgeon profile page.

## Notes

HPI identifies practitioners and facilities; it is not the patient's NHI. The full name of CPN was not established in the meeting (Vanessa linked it to prescribing and the practising certificate); see OQ-52. It may be the HPI Common Person Number, the HPI's identifier for a practitioner, in which case the HPI number and the CPN are one identifier.
