An admin or the anaesthetist sets the prepaid amount on the Booking. It is always the [estimated full fee](US-06.2.3), [calculated automatically](US-06.2.4) at [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-12.1.1) (with two contingency modifier units, OQ-38); there is no partial prepaid amount or deposit.

The amount is stored on the Booking.

## Notes

Vanessa pushed back on a partial prepaid amount: prepayment is all or nothing (relayed by Donald).

Greg thinks a prepaid amount might sometimes be less than the full estimate (for example 20%) and finds "all or nothing" unhelpful. Whether partial prepayment happens is open with Ben (OQ-76).
