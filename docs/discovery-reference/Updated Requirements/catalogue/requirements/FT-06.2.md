When one of a Booking's Procedures matches the anaesthetist's [prepaid setting](US-06.1.1) and [the payer](US-11.2.2) is a person, the Booking needs prepayment.

The amount is the fixed price for that procedure in the [anaesthetist's own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14).
