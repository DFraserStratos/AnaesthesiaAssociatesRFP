A List moves through three states: DRAFT, SUBMITTED and AUTHORISED.

While a List is DRAFT, the anaesthetist can edit its Bookings. Submitting the List removes the anaesthetist's edit access and sends it to the office for review. Authorising the List locks its Bookings, Procedures and selected Contracts, and [hands the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine](US-08.1.1).
