Time units are tiered, not linear. For the first two hours of anaesthetic time, the system charges 1 unit per 15 minutes or part thereof. From the start of the third hour, it charges 1 unit per 10 minutes or part thereof.

## Notes

How the rule works after two hours is to be confirmed against the NZSA RVG (OQ-50).
