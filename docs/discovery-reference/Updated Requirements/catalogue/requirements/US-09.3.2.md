As the system, I never send the NHI to Xero in any field.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the response notes the RFP's inconsistency (its NHI design-policy section proposes the NHI as a searchable cross-reference field on the Xero contact, while Appendix 2 says the NHI never leaves the PMS) and follows Appendix 2, the stricter reading. Nothing clinical crosses into Xero and the NHI is not used as a billing key. The cost is losing direct NHI lookup in Xero; that lookup happens in the billing system instead. The choice is to be confirmed with AA in discovery (see OQ-30).
