An admin creates an additional invoice attached to a Procedure in a Booking, on a List, from a button in the Admin App. The invoice links to, and is traceable back to, the original Procedure and its invoice, and it is recorded as an event on the Procedure. The admin can send it to any billable party, not only the original invoice's.

It is a free-form invoice: the admin sets each line's description, quantity and amount. No Contract pricing or unit rules apply, so AA staff can bill whatever the case needs. It is not to be confused with splitting a Contract's line item between parties ([payment setting](US-04.2.12)). It goes through the same standard review step as every event (OQ-63) and is invoiced in the next run. It is audited like any admin action.

Each additional invoice gets its own unique invoice number, its own [receivable and payable pair](US-08.3.1), its own Xero pair and its own payable to the anaesthetist. The original Booking is not unlocked or changed ([immutable after AUTHORISED](US-07.3.2)).

This is so the office can bill anything that turns up after invoicing without editing the original. It covers:

- post-op charges after the original invoice was sent
- a combined Procedure split at a billable party's request

The balance after a prepayment, when surgery runs longer than estimated, is invoiced by the [remaining balance invoice](US-06.4.1), not by an additional invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Button.** A Procedure on an invoiced Booking shows a button to create an additional invoice.
- **Linked.** The additional invoice shows and links to the original Procedure and its invoice, and the original shows its additional invoices.
- **Own pair.** It creates its own ledger pair, Xero pair and anaesthetist payable.
- **Review step.** Given an additional invoice, then it is issued only after the standard review step.
- **Any billable party.** The admin can pick any billable party for it.
- **Not a correction.** Correcting a wrong invoice uses [credit in full, then rebill](US-08.6.2), not an additional invoice.

## Notes

Solutions Plus does this with its 'next account' (another invoice number attached back to the original); we keep the idea, not the numbering. Pricing settled by OQ-45: free form. OQ-72 confirms "Desc" means description, not discount, and that it can go to any billable party.
