An admin creates an additional invoice attached to a Procedure in a Booking, on a List, from a button in the Admin App. The Procedure already has a Contract; the additional invoice is created against a Contract on that Procedure, chosen from the [filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2). The invoice links to, and is traceable back to, the original Procedure and its invoice.

The admin can set its lines freely (time units, modifiers, a fixed amount, the billable party): AA staff can do whatever the case needs. Because admin staff generate it, it needs no extra approval, and it is audited like any admin action.

Each additional invoice gets its own unique invoice number, its own [receivable and payable pair](US-08.3.1), its own Xero pair and its own payable to the anaesthetist. The original Booking is not unlocked or changed ([immutable after AUTHORISED](US-07.3.2)).

This is so the office can bill anything that turns up after invoicing without editing the original. It covers:

- post-op charges after the original invoice was sent
- a combined Procedure split at a billable party's request

The balance after a prepayment, when surgery runs longer than estimated, is invoiced by the [remaining balance invoice](US-06.4.1), not by an additional invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Button.** A Procedure on an invoiced Booking shows a button to create an additional invoice.
- **Linked.** The additional invoice shows and links to the original Procedure and its invoice, and the original shows its additional invoices.
- **Own pair.** It creates its own ledger pair, Xero pair and anaesthetist payable.
- **No approval step.** It is issued without a second person approving it.
- **Not a correction.** Correcting a wrong invoice uses [credit in full, then rebill](US-08.6.2), not an additional invoice.

## Notes

Solutions Plus does this with its 'next account' (another invoice number attached back to the original); we keep the idea, not the numbering (OQ-51). What pricing rules the lines follow is OQ-45.
