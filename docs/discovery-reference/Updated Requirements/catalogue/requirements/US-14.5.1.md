As an admin, I see integration failures and can intervene manually.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: a zero-tolerance dead-letter queue. A message still unprocessable after retries is set aside with its failure context and raises a critical alert to office staff, so none is lost silently and integration fails safely and visibly.

Proposed privacy constraint: a dead-lettered HL7 message holds a full PID segment, so the working view shown to office staff contains only what is needed to resolve the problem, and the raw payload is held under restricted access rather than displayed by default.
