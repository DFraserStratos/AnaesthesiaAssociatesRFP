When a List becomes AUTHORISED, the Billing/Invoice Engine processes it as a whole: it works through each Booking on the List, and each Procedure within it, reading [the Contract already locked to that Procedure](US-04.3.5).

The engine does not resolve a billing route of its own. The recipient, the pricing rules and the price itself all come from that locked Contract, which was chosen earlier, at [booking setup](US-04.3.4) and [office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so every Procedure is priced and routed from data that was fixed at authorisation, not recalculated later against Contract terms, or a payer, that may since have changed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Contract already resolved.** The engine does not resolve a billing route or determine a payer when it processes a List: the recipient, the pricing rules and the price itself all come from [the Contract already locked to each Procedure](US-04.3.5). This is a confirmed departure (Q&A 2026-09-24 #2) from the RFP response's design, where the engine determines the payer at authorisation; the engine only reads what was already decided at booking setup and office review.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: authorisation passes the completed List to the Billing Engine, which applies procedure, contract and billing rules, determines the appropriate payer, and generates the required invoices.

Note: the response describes the engine as determining the payer at this point, whereas this story (per Q&A #2) takes the recipient from the locked Contract on each Procedure with no route resolution in the engine.
