The anaesthetist, like the office when it [sets up the Booking](US-04.3.4), finds the procedure in the same two-tab picker, each tab one list under body-area headings with one search:

- **Procedures**: by body area, then subgroup, then procedure
- **RVG codes**: by body area (and the guide's sub-heading), then [RVG code](US-05.1.1), browsing the codes as reference or typing one they already know

They then pick the Contract from those [offered](US-04.3.2), with [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3) first. After an RVG code, picking a Contract line also sets the procedure, and No contract (RVG) sets the group's general procedure.

Base and modifier units start from the first of these that sets them:

- the Contract's line for that procedure
- the procedure, where AA has set its own figure
- the procedure's RVG group

Changing the procedure or the Contract refreshes the starting units and any fixed price. The anaesthetist may then change base and modifier units to any value they see fit, including one outside the published RVG range. An out-of-range value warns the office and is never blocked.

This is because the range of procedures actually performed is too varied for the system to derive the right code, or the right figure within a ranged code, on its own. The anaesthetist's own selection and professional judgement, from a curated list, is the reliable source.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Two routes.** The anaesthetist can find a procedure on the Procedures tab (body area, subgroup, procedure), or browse or search the RVG codes tab (body area, RVG code) and reach the procedure through the Contract chosen.
- **Contract.** After the procedure, the anaesthetist picks a Contract from those offered for it, with No contract (RVG) first.
- **Starting units.** Given a Contract whose line for the procedure sets base or modifier units, then those are the starting units; otherwise the procedure's own figure; otherwise its RVG group's.
- **Refresh.** Given a Procedure, when the anaesthetist changes its procedure or its Contract, then the starting units and any fixed price refresh.
- **Ranged base code.** When the procedure's RVG code has a unit range, for example 6 to 8 rather than one fixed number, the anaesthetist chooses the exact base unit value.
- **Out of range.** Given base units outside the code's published range, when saved, then the value is accepted and an after-procedure warning is raised for the office ([warning routine](US-13.7.1)).

## Technical discussion

[The draft technical design](AR-29#p7) resolves each starting value by looking down a stack of layers, highest first: the selected Contract's line for the procedure, the procedure, then the RVG group (it also allows a Contract line for a whole RVG group, which is not needed: no Contract sets one price for a group), with the first layer holding a value winning (a 0 counts as a value). It runs again whenever the procedure or the Contract changes. At time entry the resolved base and modifier units are shown as starting values, the anaesthetist enters the time units, and the record keeps what the anaesthetist recorded. The design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
