The anaesthetist picks the Procedure from the [procedure list](US-05.1.6), grouped and filtered by RVG body headings ([group codes](US-05.1.3)), then a Contract from those set against that procedure ([filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2)). Base units are seeded from the Contract picked for the procedure (its default RVG Contract unless another applies), not from the procedure itself, and where the procedure's [RVG code](US-05.1.1) carries a unit range rather than one fixed number, the anaesthetist then chooses the exact value. They may set any value they see fit, including one outside the published range.

This is because the range of procedures actually performed is too varied for the system to derive the right code, or the right figure within a ranged code, on its own. The anaesthetist's own selection and professional judgement, from a curated list, is the reliable source.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Procedure.** The anaesthetist picks the Procedure from a curated, searchable list, grouped and filtered by RVG body headings.
- **Contract.** After the procedure, the anaesthetist picks a Contract from those set against that procedure. Picking it seeds the base units from that Contract.
- **Ranged base code.** When the procedure's RVG code has a unit range, for example 6 to 8 rather than one fixed number, the anaesthetist chooses the exact base unit value.
- **Out of range.** Given base units outside the code's published range, when saved, then the value is accepted and an after-procedure warning is raised for the office ([warning routine](US-13.7.1)).
