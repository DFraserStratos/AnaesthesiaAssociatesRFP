The system supports uploading a Contract's [schedule of lines](US-04.2.4) in one go, as well as editing Contracts on screen. The upload is for Contracts only, not for other master data.

Price lists arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets in each holder's own form, and holders will not fill in AA's template. So the office transposes each one into a single AA-approved format, and a second person checks the transposed schedule against the original before it is loaded. Greg wants the system to support that office discipline.

## Notes

The details are to be designed. Greg's ideas:

- one pre-approved upload format for every Contract schedule
- a tabular draft that a second person checks, with control totals such as a column total compared with the schedule's
- a full overwrite that closes the old lines, rather than a delta

The draft technical design has both delta and full uploads, with a preview of new, changed and removed lines before anything is applied ([AR-29 p9](AR-29#p9), [p10](AR-29#p10)).

Donald and Nick hold that schedules change rarely and can be keyed by hand. Whether the upload is needed for the first release is not settled (OQ-100).
