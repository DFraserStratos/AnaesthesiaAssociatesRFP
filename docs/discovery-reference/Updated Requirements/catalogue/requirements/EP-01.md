Maintain the rolling four-month schedule: every active anaesthetist has an AM and PM List on every day, whether or not anything is booked. Lists carry availability, and once assigned carry exactly one surgeon and one hospital.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: adopt AA's candidate architecture (the schedule canvas grid, the List/Card hierarchy, half-day availability and permanent-list templates) as the starting point, with the Schedule Management candidate architecture verified during discovery.

- Reference data by identity: hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and the like are referenced into the schedule, not copied into it, so a change is made once and reflected everywhere.
- The single proposed exception sits in the billing model, where reference data is frozen at authorisation; exactly which fields is determined in discovery.
