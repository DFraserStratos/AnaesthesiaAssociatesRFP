AA runs a rolling schedule, four months ahead in current practice. Every active anaesthetist has an AM Slot and a PM Slot on every day of that window, whether or not anything is booked. A Slot is a box, or container: it has a status and can be empty. Every new Slot starts as free, and a List is put into a Slot.

A List is assigned to an anaesthetist, sits in one of their Slots and holds Bookings. Once a List is assigned, it belongs to exactly one surgeon, one anaesthetist and one hospital. A Draft List is a List created with no anaesthetist: its surgeon, hospital, day and session are known, only the anaesthetist is missing.

A day holds its Slots and Lists (both belong to the day), a List is put into a Slot and holds Bookings, a Booking holds Procedures, each Procedure has a Contract, and the Contract may be split.

Each Slot carries its own availability. This keeps the calendar complete and gapless, whatever is or isn't booked yet.

This is the settled logical model (OQ-64): a day holds an AM and a PM Slot for every active anaesthetist, a Slot is a box with a status, and a List is put into it. Slots and Lists are both children of the day (Greg: "a slot by definition belongs to a day").

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: adopt AA's candidate architecture (the schedule canvas grid, the List/Booking hierarchy, half-day availability and permanent-list templates) as the starting point, with the Schedule Management candidate architecture verified during discovery.

- Reference data by identity: hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and the like are referenced into the schedule, not copied into it, so a change is made once and reflected everywhere.
- The single proposed exception sits in the billing model, where reference data is [frozen at authorisation](US-07.3.1); exactly which fields is determined in discovery.
- Days, Slots and Lists above are the logical model. How they are stored is left to the developers: the logical model and the implementation model are separate questions.

## Notes

The word "slot" is never shown in the UI: users see Lists, and free or unavailable Slots are shown much as in the prototype today (OQ-64). Admin staff seeing [Draft Lists](FT-01.6) is a major feature for AA staff.
