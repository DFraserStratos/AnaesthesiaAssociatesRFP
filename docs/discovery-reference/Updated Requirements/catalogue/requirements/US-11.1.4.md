Admins can create a patient record before the NHI is known, and attach the NHI once it becomes available.

This covers a Booking that needs to go in before the hospital or surgeon has supplied the patient's NHI. Until the NHI is attached, the patient shows as NHI pending.

The NHI is required. A patient without one is an exception that [has to be resolved](US-11.1.5). Whether the Booking can exist in this pending state, or cannot be created without an NHI at all, is OQ-49.

Until OQ-49 is answered, the working assumption is:

- the Booking, its Procedures and their Contracts can go ahead with the NHI pending, flagged as an anomaly to fix
- [authorising the List](US-07.3.1) is blocked until the NHI is added

This is because Bookings arrive from feeds and theatre lists that sometimes omit the NHI, and "you don't want things rejected out of feeds" (Greg). A procedure never goes ahead without an NHI in the room (Donald), so an NHI exists even when the office does not have it yet.

## Notes

What happens to a duplicate patient record when the NHI is later found (for example a second record under a married name), and whether duplicates matter once the patient has had their operation, are part of OQ-49.

On 7 October Vanessa said "make NHI mandatory"; the room added that this makes the NHI the patient identifier throughout the system. In the public health sector a patient without one is assigned a temporary NHI, and even a self-funding patient from overseas has one if they are in the hospital's records. Whether "mandatory" means a Booking cannot be created without an NHI, or can be held pending with later steps blocked, was not said and is still OQ-49.
