Admins can create a patient record before the NHI is known, and attach the NHI once it becomes available.

This covers a Booking that needs to go in before the hospital or surgeon has supplied the patient's NHI. Until the NHI is attached, the patient shows as NHI pending.

The NHI is required. A patient without one is an exception that [has to be resolved](US-11.1.5). Whether the Booking can exist in this pending state, or cannot be created without an NHI at all, is OQ-49.
