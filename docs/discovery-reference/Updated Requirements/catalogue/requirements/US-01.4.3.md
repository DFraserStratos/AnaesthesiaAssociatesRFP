From the [availability view](US-01.4.2), an anaesthetist can request that one of their Lists be swapped to a colleague.

The office confirms the reassignment before it takes effect.

## Notes

Whether an anaesthetist can hand a List to a colleague without the office confirming it, for short-notice cover, is OQ-39.
