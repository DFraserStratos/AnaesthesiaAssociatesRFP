In their app, an anaesthetist selects one of their Lists and starts a move, then either:

- moves it to the AA office, where it becomes a [Draft List](FT-01.6), or
- uses the [availability view](US-01.4.2) to push it into a free Slot of a colleague

The colleague does not need to accept, and the office does not confirm the move first: this is a high-trust system. An anaesthetist who withdraws from a List also returns it to a Draft List, so it can be assigned to someone else.

Who is told of the move (the office, the colleague, the hospital) is OQ-65; Ben to confirm.
