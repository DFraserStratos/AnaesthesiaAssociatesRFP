When a surgeon's rooms call with an ad hoc request, an admin assigns a surgeon and a hospital to an available Slot directly, which creates its List.

This is so sessions that fall outside any [recurring booking](US-01.3.2) are still captured on the schedule.

## Notes

An admin turns a free Slot into an ad hoc List; anaesthetists are not expected to (Greg: "I can't see an anaesthetist needing to do that"), though they still [add a Booking on the fly](US-02.4.1) in theatre.
