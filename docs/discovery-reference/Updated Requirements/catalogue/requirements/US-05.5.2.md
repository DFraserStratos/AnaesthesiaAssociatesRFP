An ACC pre-operative assessment is a fixed-price service ACC pays for when the anaesthetist sees the patient before the procedure. The anaesthetist records it against the real Procedure, as a [pre-op event](US-03.7.1) (whether this is an event or an added procedure is OQ-63):

- it is priced by a [fixed-fee Contract](US-05.2.5)
- it is never the primary procedure
- it shows as its own line on the invoice

AA does little of this work, but practices in other regions do, so it must be supported.

The three codes are to be confirmed: CS250, CS260 or CS70.

## Notes

The screenshots show the earlier model, a flat fee billing line.

OQ-12 stays Open for AA's accountant to confirm the codes and the fixed-fee Contract. How pre-op events are modelled and named on an invoice is OQ-63.
