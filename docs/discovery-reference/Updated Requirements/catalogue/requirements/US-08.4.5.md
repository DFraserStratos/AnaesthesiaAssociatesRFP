The Billing/Invoice Engine presents each receivable invoice in the anaesthetist's own name, with the anaesthetist's GST number, and identifies AA on the invoice as agent rather than as the supplier.

This is so the invoice reflects the correct GST agency treatment: it is the anaesthetist supplying the service, and AA billing on their behalf.
