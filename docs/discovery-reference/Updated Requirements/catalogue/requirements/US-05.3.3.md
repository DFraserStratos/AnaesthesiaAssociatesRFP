As the system, when total modifier units are 4 or fewer they all sit on the primary; when more than 4 I divide them equally across all Procedures in the Booking and allocate any remainder to the primary.

## Notes

Worked example in domain-model.md: 7 modifier units over 3 procedures = 3 / 2 / 2.
