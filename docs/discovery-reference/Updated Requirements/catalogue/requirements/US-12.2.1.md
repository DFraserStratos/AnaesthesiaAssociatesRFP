Each anaesthetist sees a flat list of their own [unpaid payables](US-08.3.1), typically around 100 rows, oldest first.

The list carries no rollup or grouping: no ageing buckets and no age chips. If an anaesthetist has a query about a specific line, they contact office staff for detail rather than getting an answer from the app.

## Notes

AA accepted the recommendation "for now" (OQ-59): a flat outstanding list, oldest first, with no ageing buckets, no age chips and no Overdue view. Greg backed the flat list: "We just want an age list." Oldest first and no age chips come from the recommendation, not from AA directly.
