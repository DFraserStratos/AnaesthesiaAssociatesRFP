The system supports a trust account for prepayment money. A cancelled prepayment returns to it and is refunded to the patient from it ([refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2)), and payments out of the trust account are made from the system, not by hand outside it.

When prepayment money enters and leaves the trust account, for example whether it is held until the procedure is done, is still to be settled (OQ-40).

This is so refunds and prepayments are reconciled in one place, instead of loosely across AA's books and each anaesthetist's.
