The system supports a trust account for prepayment money. Prepayment money is held in it as a pending payment and is paid to the anaesthetist only after the procedure is done. A cancelled prepayment is refunded to the patient from it ([refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2)), and payments out of the trust account are made from the system, not by hand outside it.

This is so refunds and prepayments are reconciled in one place, instead of loosely across AA's books and each anaesthetist's.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a paid prepayment, when the procedure has not happened, then nothing is paid to the anaesthetist.
