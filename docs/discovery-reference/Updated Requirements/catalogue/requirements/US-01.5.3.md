From their app, an anaesthetist maintains their own availability calendar, independent of any List assignment.

The system reconciles that calendar against their Lists and flags any conflict, rather than merging the two.

## Notes

RFP keeps Anaesthetist Availability separate from List Status; the catalogue's FT-01.2 folds them together. Which reading is intended is OQ-27.
