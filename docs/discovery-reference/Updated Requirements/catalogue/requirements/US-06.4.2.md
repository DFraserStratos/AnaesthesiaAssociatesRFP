When the prepaid amount turns out to be more than the final calculated amount, the difference is not refunded or credited. It is accepted, and the Billing/Invoice Engine raises no credit for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid amount above the final calculated amount, when the List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), then no credit or refund is created for the difference.
