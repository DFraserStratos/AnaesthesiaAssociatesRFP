When the prepaid amount turns out to be more than the final calculated amount, the Billing/Invoice Engine raises no credit or refund for the difference automatically. A user can raise a [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand if one is needed. There is no threshold.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid amount above the final calculated amount, when the List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), then no credit or refund is created for the difference.
- Given that difference, when a user raises a credit note by hand, then it is created and traceable to the Procedure.

## Notes

Earlier ruling (OQ-03, then OQ-61): a prepayment above the final is ignored and never refunded. Ben's answer of 2026-10-06 replaces "never refunded": nothing is automatic, but a credit note can now be raised by hand. The board answer to OQ-03 itself is about a Booking that moves to another anaesthetist, now [prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4). Whether an under-run needs anything more is still part of OQ-76 (Ben).

Retired 2026-10-07: merged into US-06.4.1 (No automatic invoice or credit after a prepaid procedure), which now covers both directions.
