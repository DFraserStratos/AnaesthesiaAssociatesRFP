Once a List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine calculates the final amount, [deducts the prepaid amount already invoiced](US-08.2.2), and invoices the remaining balance if it is positive.

## Notes

This is also the top-up when surgery runs longer than the estimate. It attaches to the Procedure and is traceable to the prepayment invoice, the same way an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) is traceable to the original, but the balance after a prepayment is invoiced here, not as an additional invoice.

Greg said a final above the prepayment is also often let go, and he would only go back to the patient if it were really significant (no threshold given); transcript B says "there may be a subsequent bill". Whether every positive balance is invoiced is OQ-61. Whether an overrun raises another invoice is also in OQ-76 (Ben); a fixed fee cannot be billed more (Donald).
