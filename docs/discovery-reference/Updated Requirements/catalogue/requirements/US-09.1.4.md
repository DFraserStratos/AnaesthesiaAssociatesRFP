The ACCPAY to the anaesthetist carries buyer-created tax invoice wording, plus whatever additional details that treatment requires.

This is because invoices are raised in the [anaesthetist's name with AA acting as agent](US-08.4.5), so the ACCPAY itself has to stand as a buyer-created tax invoice rather than an ordinary payable.
