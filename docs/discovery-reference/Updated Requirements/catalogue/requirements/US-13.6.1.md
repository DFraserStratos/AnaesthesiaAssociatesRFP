Admins maintain a master list of surgeons' rooms. Each room holds its name, contact details including a contact email, and the surgeons who belong to it.

This is so a surgeon is always tied to a room, and so the office has one place to find the room's email. That email is the To address for a [drafted booking update email](US-02.3.3), and the room is who the office emails for a [missing NHI](US-11.1.5) and who sends [estimated durations](US-06.2.5) for prepayment.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Room record.** A room has a name, contact email and phone, and a list of surgeons.
- **Surgeons belong to rooms.** A surgeon is linked to a room; the room's contacts are shown on the surgeon's profile.

## Notes

The initial list comes from Vanessa. Recommendation: one room per surgeon unless AA says otherwise. The meeting did not settle whether a surgeon can belong to more than one room.

Greg's picture of the room record: it holds the office's contact details ("a short contact list") and, as children, its surgeons; or a more generic relationship, an actor with a role of admin or surgeon. Donald: "Whichever the relationship may look like."
