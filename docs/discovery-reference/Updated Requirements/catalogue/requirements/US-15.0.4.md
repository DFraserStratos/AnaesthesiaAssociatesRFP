The system must handle about 85 anaesthetists, 20,000 List records within the [four-month scheduling horizon](FT-01.1), and 28,000 invoices a year, without any drop in performance.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: at AA's current and projected scale (about 85 anaesthetists plus office staff), user numbers have no material effect on hosting cost. Performance testing and optimisation are part of the formal Testing milestone before go-live.
