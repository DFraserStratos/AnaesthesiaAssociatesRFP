Before the Billing/Invoice Engine works out the remaining amount to invoice for a Booking, it deducts any [prepayment already invoiced](US-06.3.1) against that Booking.

This is so a patient who has already paid a prepayment (the full estimate) is only billed for what is left owing, not for the full fee again.
