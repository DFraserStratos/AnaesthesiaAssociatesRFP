When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist before the procedure, the patient is not billed again. The prepayment agreed with the first anaesthetist stands, and the anaesthetist who does the procedure becomes the payee, wearing or benefiting from any difference in rate. Only the payable half of the prepayment's [draft pair](US-09.1.3) is updated to the new anaesthetist; the receivable from the patient is unchanged. The payable has to be amended because the [trust account](US-06.5.1) in Xero always has to balance. The move still [re-checks](US-06.3.5) the prepayment, but the re-check does not change the agreed amount.

This keeps the amount stable for the patient.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Booking for anaesthetist A, when it is moved to anaesthetist B before the procedure, then no new prepayment invoice is raised and the patient is not billed again.
- Given that move, when it is saved, then the payable half of the prepayment's draft pair is to B and the receivable is unchanged.
- Given the procedure is done by B, then the payable is to B for the prepaid amount.
