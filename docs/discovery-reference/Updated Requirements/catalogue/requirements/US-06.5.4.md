When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist before the procedure, the patient is not billed again. The prepayment agreed with the first anaesthetist stands, and the anaesthetist who does the procedure becomes the payee, wearing or benefiting from any difference in rate. The prepayment's draft payable is repointed to the new anaesthetist. The move still [re-checks](US-06.3.5) the prepayment, but the re-check does not change the agreed amount.

This keeps the amount stable for the patient.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Booking for anaesthetist A, when it is moved to anaesthetist B before the procedure, then no new prepayment invoice is raised and the patient is not billed again.
- Given that move, when it is saved, then the prepayment's draft payable points to B.
- Given the procedure is done by B, then the payable is to B for the prepaid amount.
