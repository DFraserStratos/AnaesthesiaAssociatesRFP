In their app, an anaesthetist can move a single Booking from one of their Lists to another anaesthetist, as well as [move a whole List](US-01.4.3) (Greg: "They need to be able to move at the list level or at the booking level").

The app cannot show every List, so the anaesthetist finds the receiver by searching by anaesthetist (Donald's suggestion). A receiver who is on leave or unavailable does not show as available: they first set their own Slot to available, which is their declaration that they accept responsibility for the work. The office does not assign work to someone marked unavailable without them knowing.

This is so an anaesthetist can hand on one case and keep the rest, for example to a colleague on leave who agrees to do one procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking on their List, when the anaesthetist moves it to a colleague, then it leaves their List and is on a List of the colleague's.
- Given a colleague marked unavailable, then they are not offered as a receiver until they set themselves available.
