The Billing/Invoice Engine treats a 'full' prepayment as an estimate, not a fixed final price.

The final amount is only calculated after the procedure, and may come out higher or lower than the prepaid estimate, since the actual duration and any complications aren't known beforehand. Either way the system raises no invoice or credit automatically; a user raises one by hand if needed ([no automatic balance invoice](US-06.4.1)).

The prepayment request is always worded as an estimate, because patients query a final amount that comes out higher; see [prepayment letter templates](US-06.3.6).

## Notes

Greg: "It's a prepayment, it's our estimate." A discretionary case would use RVG pricing rather than a fixed fee (Donald). Ben answered on 2026-10-06 that an estimate that proves too high or too low is not adjusted automatically (OQ-61); anything further about overruns is still OQ-76.

Retired 2026-10-07: the prepaid amount is no longer an estimate. It now works like the anaesthetist's own fixed price: by default it is the final price, and the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only. Replaced by US-06.2.2 (Set the prepaid amount) and US-06.4.1 (No automatic invoice or credit after a prepaid procedure).
