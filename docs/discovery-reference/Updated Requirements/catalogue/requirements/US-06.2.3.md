The Billing/Invoice Engine treats a 'full' prepayment as an estimate, not a fixed final price.

The final amount is only calculated after the procedure, and may come out higher or lower than the prepaid estimate, since the actual duration and any complications aren't known beforehand.

The prepayment request is always worded as an estimate, because patients query a final amount that comes out higher; see [prepayment letter templates](US-06.3.6).
