The engine identifies each billing contact to Xero by a hidden internal ID, stored in Xero's ContactNumber field, rather than by name or NHI.

Once Xero returns a ContactID for that contact, the engine caches it, so a later invoice for the same patient can look the contact up directly instead of searching Xero again.

The patient's NHI itself [never crosses to Xero](US-15.0.6), in any field. This is because the NHI is subject to Health Information Privacy Code obligations and stays inside the practice management system. NHI-based lookup happens in the billing system rather than in Xero, so nothing clinical reaches Xero, only the hidden internal ID.

## Acceptance criteria

- **NHI never sent.** The engine [never sends a patient's NHI to Xero](US-15.0.6), in any field. Only the hidden internal ID crosses to Xero, so nothing clinical reaches it, and NHI-based lookup happens in the billing system rather than in Xero.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the response notes the RFP's inconsistency (its NHI design-policy section proposes the NHI as a searchable cross-reference field on the Xero contact, while Appendix 2 says the NHI never leaves the PMS) and follows Appendix 2, the stricter reading. Nothing clinical crosses into Xero and the NHI is not used as a billing key. The cost is losing direct NHI lookup in Xero; that lookup happens in the billing system instead. The choice is to be confirmed with AA in discovery (see OQ-30).
