The anaesthetist opens a calendar and jumps to any day, including days before the rolling four-month view. From that day they drill into its List, a Booking on it and that Booking's Procedures, as the [drill-down](US-03.1.1) does, without scrolling back day by day.

This is so they can look back at past procedures, for example to add a [post-op event](US-03.7.1).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a day months in the past, when the anaesthetist picks it on the calendar, then that day's Lists show.
- From there they reach a Booking on the List and its Procedures.

## Notes

Discussed for the anaesthetist app, with its four-month rolling view. The admin app was not mentioned.
