The anaesthetist can find their past work in two ways:

- **Calendar.** They open a calendar and jump to any day, including days before the rolling four-month view. From that day they drill into its List, a Booking on it and that Booking's Procedures, as the [drill-down](US-03.1.1) does, without scrolling back day by day.
- **Archive.** In an archive or deeper search they find previously created Bookings and Lists, including ones already invoiced, and see what they entered on them.

This is so they can look back at past procedures, for example to add a [post-op event](US-03.7.1), or to answer a query about an invoice from three months ago: "what did I enter?"

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a day months in the past, when the anaesthetist picks it on the calendar, then that day's Lists show.
- From there they reach a Booking on the List and its Procedures.
- Given a Booking already invoiced, when the anaesthetist looks it up in the archive or search, then they see what they entered on it.

## Notes

Discussed for the anaesthetist app, with its four-month rolling view. The admin app was not mentioned.

On 7 October the room asked to "go back and look at old invoices", and Donald proposed that the main screen show Lists not yet invoiced and upcoming work, with "a deeper search or archive where you can find previously created bookings and lists" elsewhere in the app; the room moved on without objection.
