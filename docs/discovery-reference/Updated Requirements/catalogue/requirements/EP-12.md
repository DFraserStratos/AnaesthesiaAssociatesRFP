Each anaesthetist has their own profile of settings that drive how their work is billed: a dollar value per RVG unit, a GST period, and the [RVG codes they treat as prepaid](US-06.1.1).

Anaesthetists also have two reporting needs of their own: a list of the balances still owing to them, and a periodic summary of their activity for their GST return.
