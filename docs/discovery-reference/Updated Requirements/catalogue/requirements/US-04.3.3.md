Where no other Contract applies to a Procedure, the Scheduling Engine defaults it to the [RVG Default Hospital Contract](US-04.1.1) for the List's hospital.

This Contract is derived automatically from the List's location, so a Procedure is never left without pricing rules to fall back on.

## Notes

The default Contract is the "no special contract" Contract: normal RVG base, time and modifier pricing, with no agreed fixed price or modifier rules. Choosing it does not decide who is invoiced; that is the [billable party](US-11.2.2). Where a fixed-rate Contract applies, the anaesthetist still records base, time and modifiers and the charge is the fixed rate; prepayment overlays either as a separate lifecycle.
