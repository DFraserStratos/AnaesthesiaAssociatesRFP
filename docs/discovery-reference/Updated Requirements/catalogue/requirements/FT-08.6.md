Some billing lines are dated after the List containing them has already been invoiced, for example:

- a ward or HDU review
- a nerve catheter day
- a pain consult

Separately, an already-invoiced Booking can turn out to need correcting.

Both cases have to be handled without unlocking the original, [already-immutable](US-07.3.2) Booking.
