Some billing lines are dated after the List containing them has already been invoiced, for example:

- a ward or HDU review
- a nerve catheter day
- a pain consult

Separately, an already-invoiced Booking can turn out to need correcting.

Both cases have to be handled without unlocking the original, [already-immutable](US-07.3.2) Booking.

Late billing lines are billed either as an admin's [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), or added by the anaesthetist as a [post-op event](FT-03.7), which is expected to go through admin approval before it is invoiced. A wrong invoice is credited in full, then rebilled.

## Notes

Whether the anaesthetist's post-op events need admin approval is still to be checked with AA (Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #61).
