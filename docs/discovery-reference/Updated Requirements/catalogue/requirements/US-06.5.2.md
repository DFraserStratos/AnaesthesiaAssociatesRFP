When a Booking with a paid prepayment is cancelled before the procedure ([record a cancellation](US-02.5.3)), the system always refunds the original prepayment in full: the money is held in the [trust account](US-06.5.1) until the procedure, so it has not reached the anaesthetist, and it is refunded to the patient from there. The refund is recorded in the ledger as a credit against the prepayment invoice, so the trail stays visible ([credit and rebill](US-08.6.2)).

The office handles the refund; the anaesthetist is no longer left to refund the patient or pay a replacement anaesthetist directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a paid prepayment on a Booking, when the Booking is cancelled before the procedure, then the full prepayment is refunded, whichever anaesthetist held it.
- Given the refund, when the ledger is viewed, then the credit is linked to the original prepayment invoice.
