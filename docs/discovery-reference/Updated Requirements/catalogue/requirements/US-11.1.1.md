The system keeps exactly one patient record per NHI, holding demographics, ethnicity coded to NZHIS Level 4, and contact details. This information can be supplied by the hospital, the surgeon, or AA itself.

When a patient returns, the system matches them to this existing record by their NHI, even when their name or address has changed since their last visit, rather than creating a new one.

This is so every clinical episode for the same patient links back to a single record, whoever supplied it, and the same patient is never duplicated just because their details were recorded differently at check-in.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Dedupe on NHI.** When a returning patient is entered, the system matches them to their existing record by NHI, even when their name or address has changed since their last visit, so no duplicate record is created.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the NHI is treated as the most restricted element in the system and is held in the core platform only, with lookups against the [NHI FHIR API via the Health NZ Digital Services Hub](US-14.4.1). It is not used as a billing key and [does not cross into Xero](US-15.0.6).

The response follows RFP Appendix 2 (NHI never leaves the core system) and will confirm this with AA in discovery; see OQ-30.

Record keeping and dedupe matching are the create-if-absent and match-if-present sides of the same NHI lookup, so the merge carries both without dropping anything.

NHI is the clinical identifier and dedupe key. Billing and ledger records key on the hidden internal patient ID, per RFP Appendix 1 policy; NHI is a cross-reference there. See OQ-30 on Appendix 1 vs 2.
