Where a Procedure's Contract is set to split rather than full payment (its [payment setting](US-04.2.12)), the Billing/Invoice Engine divides the Contract's line item between the parties and invoices each party its share as a separate invoice, for example the insurer for its portion and the patient for the gap. Each party's share is a typed value in $ or % (for example $48 or 48%), set on the Booking and defaulting from the Contract.

This lets a Procedure that is only partly covered still be billed correctly on both sides, without the patient being charged for the covered portion or the insurer being charged for the gap.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: AA is asked to advise whether cover split between an insurer and the patient is a real case, alongside the leaning to extend how the Contract model resolves insurer rates rather than add a parallel structure. AA's answer (OQ-23) settles the mechanism: a payment setting on the Contract, full payment or split. The basis of the split follows OQ-68, where AA left the choice to us ("You pick what's best. We can edit after."): a typed value in $ or %, set on the Booking, defaulting from the Contract.

## Notes

Mechanism settled by OQ-23. The split basis is our pick under OQ-68, to pressure test with AA, which can change it later.
