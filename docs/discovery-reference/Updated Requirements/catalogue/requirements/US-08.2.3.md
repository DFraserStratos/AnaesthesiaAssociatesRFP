Where a Procedure's Contract defines a covered amount or a covered percentage, the Billing/Invoice Engine invoices the contract holder or insurer for that covered portion, and invoices the patient (the billable party) for the remaining gap, as two separate invoices.

This lets a Procedure that is only partly covered still be billed correctly on both sides, without the patient being charged for the covered portion or the insurer being charged for the gap.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: AA is asked to advise whether cover split between an insurer and the patient is a real case, alongside the leaning to extend how the Contract model resolves insurer rates rather than add a parallel structure. The mechanism remains open (OQ-23).

## Notes

The one-Contract-per-Procedure model does not naturally express partial cover; mechanism is OQ-23.
