As the system, where a Contract defines a covered amount or percentage, I invoice the contract holder or insurer for the covered portion and the patient (billable party) for the gap, as two invoices.

## Notes

The one-Contract-per-Procedure model does not naturally express partial cover; mechanism is OQ-23.
