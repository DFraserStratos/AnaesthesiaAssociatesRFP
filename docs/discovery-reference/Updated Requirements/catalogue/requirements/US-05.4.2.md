During [SUBMITTED review](FT-07.2), admins can apply a discretionary price override to a Procedure, each with a reason:

- a fixed final fee
- a dollar adjustment
- a percentage adjustment

The office can always override. The Contract's permission for an [anaesthetist adjustment](US-05.4.1) gates only the anaesthetist, never the office. This includes a fixed-fee Contract price: normally a fixed fee stays fixed, but it is not hard-coded as unchangeable, so an authorised admin can override it.

## Notes

RFP says priceOverride is independent of any Contract; Diagram 7 lists 'whether discount / price override is allowed' on the Contract. Gating is settled by OQ-16: the office can always override.
