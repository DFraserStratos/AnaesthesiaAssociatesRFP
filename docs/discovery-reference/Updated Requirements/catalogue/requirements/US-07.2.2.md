The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, covering:

- its Procedures
- the Contract selected for each one
- whether a Procedure needs an insurer's Contract, and the insurer details that came with the Booking
- other references
- addresses and invoice email addresses
- anything missed or filled in incorrectly, such as an operation not completed properly

The admin then approves the Contract selections made earlier, or corrects them. The admin also checks for any patient under 18 who is the billable party. This raises a mild warning, not a block (see [warn when a child is the billable party](US-11.2.4)).

## Notes

Approval happens here, at the SUBMITTED review after the anaesthetist submits, not before the procedure (OQ-26; Q&A #4).

The insurer is not held on the Patient or the Booking: each Procedure's Contract says who pays, so one Booking can have Procedures billed to different parties (OQ-55; Notes 2026-10-01 #29). The order for who pays (the insurer named on the hospital's theatre list, the hospital applying to the insurer as agent) is still to be drawn out with Vanessa (Notes 2026-10-01 #29 #55). The under-18 check is a mild warning that the admin can clear, not a block (OQ-54; Notes 2026-10-01 #28 #47).
