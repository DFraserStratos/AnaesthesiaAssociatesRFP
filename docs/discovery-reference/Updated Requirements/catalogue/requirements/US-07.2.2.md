The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, covering:

- its Procedures
- the Contract selected for each one
- insurer details
- other references

The admin then approves the Contract selections made earlier, or corrects them.

## Notes

Approval happens here, at the SUBMITTED review after the anaesthetist submits, not before the procedure (OQ-26; Q&A #4).
