The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, covering:

- its Procedures
- the Contract selected for each one
- whether insurance needs adding, and the insurer details already there
- other references
- addresses and invoice email addresses
- anything missed or filled in incorrectly, such as an operation not completed properly

The admin then approves the Contract selections made earlier, or corrects them. The admin also checks that no patient under 18 is the billable party (see [a child is never the billable party](US-11.2.4); whether it blocks or only warns is OQ-54).

## Notes

Approval happens here, at the SUBMITTED review after the anaesthetist submits, not before the procedure (OQ-26; Q&A #4).
