The anaesthetist can change the procedure or the Contract on a Procedure until they submit the List, choosing the Contract from a list already [filtered to the Contracts relevant to that Procedure](US-04.3.2). No explanation is needed.

When either one changes:

- the [starting units](US-03.3.1) and any fixed price refresh
- if the new Contract does not let the anaesthetist [change the price](US-03.5.1), any price or discount they had entered is cleared

[Every change is audited](US-02.5.5), and [flagged for office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so the anaesthetist can bill for what actually happened. The common case: the hospital cannot get insurance pre-approval on the day and the patient pays, so the anaesthetist changes the Contract to No contract (RVG) and the patient is billed. This happens regularly.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No reason needed.** Given a Procedure on a List not yet submitted, when the anaesthetist changes its procedure or its Contract, then the change saves without an explanation.
- **Refresh.** Given a changed procedure or Contract, then the starting units and any fixed price refresh.
- **Entered price cleared.** Given a price or discount the anaesthetist entered, when they change to a Contract that does not let them change the price, then the entered price or discount is cleared.
- **Audited and flagged.** Every change is audited and flagged at office review.
- **Pre-approval falls through.** Given a Procedure on an insurer's Contract, when the anaesthetist changes it to No contract (RVG), then the patient, or [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), is billed.

## Technical discussion

[The draft technical design](AR-29#p6) re-runs the resolver on any change of procedure or Contract, and clears the entered price and discount when the new Contract is not adjustable (third-party Contracts are not). The design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
