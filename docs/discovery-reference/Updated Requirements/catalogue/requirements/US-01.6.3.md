An admin assigns a Draft List to an anaesthetist by choosing the anaesthetist and the AM or PM Slot it falls in. The Draft List becomes a List in that Slot, keeping anything already recorded on it, and stops being shown as unassigned. The [availability finder](US-01.4.2) helps find who is free.

An assignment onto an unavailable Slot, or to a [blacklisted pairing](US-01.3.5), shows a soft warning and can still go ahead, the same way [conflict flagging](US-01.5.2) works.

This is so every Draft List ends up with an anaesthetist, without the office re-keying it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Office assigns.** A Draft List is never put to anaesthetists as an offer; only the office assigns it.
- **Becomes a List.** After assignment the item is an assigned List in the chosen Slot with exactly one surgeon and one hospital ([one surgeon, one hospital](US-01.3.1)).
- **Slot must be free.** A Slot holds one List; an occupied Slot is not offered without the admin resolving it.
- **Audited.** The assignment is recorded in the audit trail (US-13.5.2).
