Admins can create a new Booking on a List, or amend an existing one, changing details such as the patient, time, procedures or Contract.

Every such change is recorded against the admin who made it.

## Notes

Every field of a Booking is editable by an admin until AA says otherwise (Donald: "I think everything can be edited until we're told otherwise").
