Admins see every anaesthetist's [AM and PM Slots](US-01.1.1) for a single day together, each with its availability status and, where a List sits in it, its hospital, surgeon and booking count. [Draft Lists](US-01.6.2) for that day appear too, flagged as being prepared.

From there, an admin can drill into any List to see its detail.
