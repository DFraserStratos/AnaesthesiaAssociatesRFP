The Billing/Invoice Engine issues a unique number for every invoice; this is the key an automated remittance match relies on to find the right invoice.

The receivable and its matching payable also carry visibly similar numbers, such as the payable number carrying the receivable's number with a "-P" suffix, so staff reviewing a pair by eye can see at once that they belong together.
