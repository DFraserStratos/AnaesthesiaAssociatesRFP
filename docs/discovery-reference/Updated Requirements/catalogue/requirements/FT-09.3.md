Each real patient has exactly one Xero contact, kept for life and identified by a hidden internal ID rather than by the patient's NHI, which [never reaches Xero](US-15.0.6). A contact is archived once its invoices are fully paid and it has sat inactive for a defined window, keeping Xero's active contact count low no matter how many patients AA has billed over time.
