As an anaesthetist, the mobile app is my primary tool and is designed mobile-first, with the web app as a full alternative.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: a Progressive Web App (PWA) is recommended over native apps for about 85 anaesthetists. It installs to the home screen and runs in the browser engine with no app store, giving effectively the same day-to-day experience at materially lower upfront and ongoing cost. Store discoverability is not a factor for a small user base, and no on-device services (such as location) are required.

Native apps would need App Store and Play builds, separate pipelines, store compliance and platform updates (Stratos would share a single codebase across iOS and Android to reduce this, though some extra cost is unavoidable). In return they give seamless notifications, store install and device services; the Commercials section describes native as a richer offline and notification experience at significantly higher cost.

PWA versus native is to be evaluated more thoroughly in Scoping and Design, including the cost implications with AA and user validation.
