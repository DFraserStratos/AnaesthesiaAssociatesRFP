A monthly fee invoice run: one action generates every anaesthetist's fee invoice for the period, from the [fee settings](US-10.3.3), and tracks each one in the ledger.

This invoice is kept separate from procedure receivables and payables, since it is AA charging the anaesthetist, not a payment for a procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a month with 40 BCTIs for an anaesthetist, fixed charges of $500 and a charge of $5 per invoice, when the monthly run is made, then their fee invoice is $700.

## Notes

The basis is answered (OQ-02): fixed charges plus a charge per BCTI. Whether only paid invoices count, and whether the fee nets against payables, are OQ-60.

Greg believes these invoices are made by hand today.
