Every patient has one record in the system, keyed on their NHI, with their contact details.

Each Booking carries its own billable party and invoice email. These default to the patient, but can differ, for example a guardian paying and receiving the invoice for a child.

Admins can see a patient's outstanding balances across all anaesthetists, and are alerted when a patient with unpaid bills is booked again.
