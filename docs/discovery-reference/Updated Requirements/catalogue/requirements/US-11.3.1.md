Admins can open a patient's record and see all their invoices, each showing whether it is paid, part paid or unpaid, across every anaesthetist the patient has been booked with.

This gives AA a single view of a patient's billing history, since each anaesthetist otherwise bills separately.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the [system of record](US-08.3.2), with a dedicated Xero instance as an accounts-receivable and banking add-on only. Because the response follows RFP Appendix 2 and keeps the NHI out of Xero, looking a patient up by NHI happens in the billing system rather than directly in Xero. This is settled: there is no patient data in Xero, only a unique ID that links each transaction back to its invoice in our system (OQ-30).
