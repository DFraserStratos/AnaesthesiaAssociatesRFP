As the system, I flag Lists that conflict with a hospital closure or the anaesthetist's own unavailability rather than silently merging the data.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: this is treated as a business policy question for AA (OQ-09). Because bookings must be accepted up to the start of a session and theatres open for emergency work, a rule that cannot be overridden would be worked around outside the system. The response leans toward interrupting the booking and requiring an authorised administrator to confirm with a captured reason. AA to define how much friction each case warrants and where the line for "authorised" sits.
