# Data-model delta: catalogue vs prototype

Read-only comparison of the entity, relationship and lifecycle model the requirements catalogue now describes (see [domain-model.md](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/domain-model.md), updated through 18 November) with what `aa-prototype/` implements at HEAD (PERSIST_VERSION 50, after catch-up Phases 22a, 17a, 23, 24 and 25 and the UI unification track to U6). The code is treated as the truth about the prototype; the old build docs were used only as leads. Retired items and items in the Future Work lane are excluded except as boundary references; item statuses were ignored.

This pass (catalogue commit 63a4490, from c9b65ad) updates the earlier one. **The prototype built most of the pricing track since the last pass**: the payer on each Procedure (22a), the primary Procedure and the multi-procedure rule (23), one typed price, the price ladder and who may change a price (24), and the pricing snapshot written at AUTHORISED with the Contract's history (25). Six deltas are therefore closed as built (DM-11, DM-28, DM-14, DM-50, DM-15, DM-08), and DM-46 shrinks to the BillingLine entity. What remains open is the Money track and the intake track, which no phase has touched, plus surgeon contacts and a handful of small rules. The catalogue side moved in four ways, all on 17 and 18 November:

- **Surgeon contacts on the surgeon, no surgeons' room record** (18 November, [Donald's note](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/notes/2026-11-18-donald-surgeon-contacts-no-rooms.md), [the change log](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/changes/2026-11-18-donald-surgeon-contacts-no-rooms.md); [OQ-127](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-127.md), [OQ-128](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-128.md) superseded, [US-13.6.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-13.6.1.md) Retired). The prototype built rooms, more than one room per surgeon and room contacts in Phases 17 and 17a, days earlier, so DM-60 is now an unwind and rewrite, and a new DM-70 holds the List's origin ([US-02.2.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-02.2.4.md)).
- **Thirteen board answers** ([the change log](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/changes/2026-11-18-board-answers.md)). Those that change the model: a payer on each Procedure confirmed ([OQ-140](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-140.md), the code already follows it), a credit note always for the whole invoice, a prepayment invoice included ([OQ-97](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-97.md): DM-20 and DM-24), no automatic hospital sync in the first release ([OQ-109](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-109.md): DM-34), a warning listing Procedures on an old Contract, a warning listing authorised Procedures a backdated range left at their old price, and a review flag on a Disabled Contract ([OQ-133](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-133.md), [OQ-135](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-135.md): new DM-69), no per-Booking reference other than the insurer approval number ([OQ-136](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-136.md): DM-68 is now decided), Public as a Slot status ([OQ-139](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-139.md): new DM-71), the surgeon list as an address book and a surgeon saved without an HPI CPN ([OQ-126](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-126.md), [OQ-129](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-129.md): DM-60), and the two money alerts kept separate ([OQ-134](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/questions/OQ-134.md): DM-23, DM-31).

## Suggested order

Two tracks share only Booking and Procedure and join at DM-34's matching screen. The Pricing track is almost built; what is left of it is DM-20's prepayment lifecycle and DM-46's entity removal.

- **Pricing track.** Nothing blocks DM-20 now (DM-14 and DM-11 are built): it takes the prepaid rung of the price ladder, the approval step, one invoice per payer's lifecycle and the removal of PrepaymentDetail and the lock's deduction line. DM-46 (the BillingLine entity) waits for DM-17. DM-69 (the Contract change warnings and the Disabled Contract flag) is free-standing and builds on Phase 25's Contract history.
- **Money track.** DM-22 (payable in the ledger, with control accounts and sub-ledgers; the clearing account waits on Greg and Sam's maps) first; DM-20 can start before the ledger; DM-21 (AA Holding account) needs DM-20 and DM-22; DM-17 then DM-18 then DM-24 (DM-21's refund and DM-20's cancel-on-change both need DM-24); DM-23, DM-25 and DM-29 need DM-22 (DM-25 also DM-24; its payment is made in Xero and only detected); DM-67 (the reconciliation status) needs DM-22 and DM-23 and is Proposed; DM-31's balance rule needs DM-23.
- **Surgeons and intake.** DM-60 first (unwind the rooms, put contacts and the practice name on the surgeon), then DM-70 (the List's origin) with DM-61 (the PDF record carries the sender), then DM-35 (explicit save and the update email: its To address reads DM-70 and DM-60); DM-30 (NHI mandatory) then DM-34 (the matching screen) with DM-61 (the PDF inbox, built together); DM-58 (stubs) needs DM-30 and DM-34's unmatched queue. DM-65 (the A and B split, Proposed) waits for AA and should not start.
- **Free-standing, small.** DM-71 (Public as a Slot status), DM-68 (the Procedure's billing reference, now decided), DM-32, DM-38 and DM-45 can go at any time.

Every model change that alters the seed shape needs a PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 50; CLAUDE.md still says 13).
