# EP-11 · Patients and billable parties · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-11) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-11](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-11.md)

> Built and correct: the payer-per-Procedure cluster (US-11.2.1 to 11.2.4) and insurer or funder plus approval number (US-11.4.3, apart from the matching-screen display) were built by Phases 21 and 22a and read as correct against the current text. Gaps are NHI and balance related. (1) NHI is not mandatory in the prototype: upsertPatient (store/intake.ts:98-128) still creates a provisional no-NHI Patient, ManualBookingForm and ingestPdfRow accept a blank NHI, a provisional Booking is seeded, and Booking, Review and the web List render 'NHI pending'. That contradicts US-11.1.5 and the epic summary's 'mandatory NHI, no blank'. It also affects US-11.1.4 and US-11.1.1, graded elsewhere. Closing it needs Phase 33 (unmatched queue, Matching screen), Phase 33a (List stubs, US-02.6.1 to 02.6.3) and Phase 40, plus a held chase address: the List's sender, else the surgeon's first office contact (US-13.6.5, OQ-127), one address only. The feeds carry neither. (2) US-11.3.2 needs a new FT-13.7 warning rule (WarningRuleId, patient balance and invoice-age facts from the billing mirror, fixed 90-day threshold, credit balance, evaluated on Booking create or match, beside the prepayment alert). The only trace today is the Billing monitor's 'Prior balance' pill, and a credit-balance concept does not exist in the mirror. The epic's 'admins see a patient's balances across all anaesthetists' belongs to US-11.3.1 (no patient record or master view exists). The unmatched queue and matching screen are the single dependency behind US-11.1.5 and US-11.4.3. Corrections from verification: EP-11 and FT-11.2 are Grouping (summaries with no ACs; their gaps sit in children); US-11.2.2 downgraded to Partial/S for stale 'Booking's Payer row' copy and a Booking-level Payer block on the add-Booking form (ManualBookingForm.tsx:311-329); US-11.4.3 downgraded to Partial/S for the matching-screen display. Open questions: OQ-114 (non-email, non-post delivery) and OQ-122 (posted invoices) leave the prototype on the default email or post with 'To post' labelled provisional; OQ-142 (approval number on the holder's invoice) is not modelled; OQ-140 is settled for the build and carries one provisional label on the payer offer. Demo guide impact: S1 and the 'add a Booking manually, try both NHI formats' beat change when NHI becomes mandatory; S1's Sarah Mitchell repeat-patient beat and the S4 Sarah Mitchell beat are where a balance alert would first appear; the payer beats (S3 split, S4 prepayment, Grace Park guardian) are unaffected.

## US-11.1.4 · Patient without NHI · Contradicts

[US-11.1.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-11.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-11.1.4) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: store, domain, admin, web, mobile, simulator, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** NHI mandatory; a Booking cannot be saved without one. An incoming booking without NHI is not a Booking: it waits on the unmatched queue (US-02.1.4) until the office adds the NHI, then matches and becomes a Booking; a stub shows on its List meanwhile; the 'NHI pending Booking' idea is dropped.
- **Prototype today:** A Booking can be created and saved with a blank NHI via manual form, PDF row ingest and feed, with a provisional patient record. It shows 'NHI pending' on the web List detail and admin Review screen. No unmatched queue, no waiting intake row, no stub on the List.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - WRONG: a Booking can be created or edited with no NHI (no save refusal)
  - WRONG: an incoming PDF row with blank NHI creates a Booking and provisional patient (ingestPdfRow) instead of waiting at intake
  - WRONG: 'NHI pending' badge on Bookings (concept dropped)
  - MISSING: unmatched queue holding NHI-less intake rows (US-02.1.4)
  - MISSING: office adds NHI to a waiting row, which then matches and becomes a Booking
  - MISSING: stub on the List while a row waits (FT-02.6)
- **Demo trigger:** Button 'Receive booking without NHI' on the Integration monitor (Surgeon PDF inbox and feed replay): feeds a row with a blank NHI so it lands on the unmatched queue and a stub appears on its List; then the office types the NHI on the queue row and it becomes a Booking on the List.
