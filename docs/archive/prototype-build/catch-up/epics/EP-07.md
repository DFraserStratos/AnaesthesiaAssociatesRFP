# EP-07 · List approval workflow · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-07) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-07](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-07.md)

> Only US-07.2.2 was in scope. The review screen is largely aligned (Phases 21a, 22a, 24, 25 landed). The flag rules are one pure list, flags never block, and the only authorise blocker is needsPrice. The one gap is the OQ-135 answer of 18 Nov: flag a Disabled Contract on Procedures dated on or after its disable day, never block. Per the answer the date comes from the contract.disable audit entry, since a Contract has no dates of its own. Closing it needs these changes. (1) Pass the disable dates, derived from the audit, into the review-flag input. (2) Add one REVIEW_FLAG_RULES rule, or extend contractNoLongerFits to a date-aware status test. The story notes say it is the same check as 'not offered on the List'. (3) Seed a disabled Contract with its audit entry and a post-disable Booking on a reviewable List, with a PERSIST_VERSION bump. (4) Update the D81 comments and the disableContract docstring. The rail's own candidate test uses status only, which is unaffected. No scripted S1 to S5 beat should break, but a seeded disabled Contract on a reviewable List would change Review flag counts, so the Flags StatTile beats need a re-check. The story is status Verify. The insurer and child-payer text (OQ-140) was confirmed by Donald on 18 Nov but not yet discussed with AA, and OQ-135 is likewise not yet discussed with AA, so wording could still move.
