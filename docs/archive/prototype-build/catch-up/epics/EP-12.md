# EP-12 · Anaesthetist profile and reporting · gap details

Part of the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md#ep-12) · catalogue `63a4490` · [EP-12](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/EP-12.md)

> Only FT-12.2 and US-12.2.1 were graded (siblings US-12.2.2/3 and FT-12.1 are other agents' items). Main structural change: the ageing concept (AgingBuckets, bucketForAgingDays, receivablesAgingFor, dashboard Receivables aging panel, web 'Overdue' sub-tab and /web/accounts/overdue route, 'n accounts over 60 days' link) must be removed or reduced to a plain flat outstanding list; dashboard tests (store/dashboard.test.ts, lookbackHistory.test.ts) and the catalogue component recreations (apps/demo/catalogue/mobile/recreations.tsx) reference outstandingAccpayInvoicesFor and will change. New navigation from list rows to Booking detail is needed on both mobile and web with Back handling (lookbackState router state). Dependency: US-08.3.1 (payable pair) defines 'unpaid payables', so what counts as outstanding should be checked against the payable/disbursement state, not only payer receipts. OQ-123 is unresolved: at most keep a hook; no build required until answered. Demo script impact: S3 style payment/balances beat (03-demo-script.md ~lines 698, 753-767, 904) and the URL table entry 'Accounts, outstanding /web/accounts/overdue' (line 71) mention Overdue/ageing and would change wording; the Beat 3 statement that a paid invoice 'remains under Overdue because no longer outstanding' needs rewording.

## US-12.1.4 · Anaesthetist identity, contact and bank details · Contradicts

[US-12.1.4](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.1.4.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.1.4) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: admin, domain, store, seed · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Admins hold each anaesthetist's contact, registration number, HPI CPN and bank account details on the profile (not Xero only); a record saved without an HPI CPN or without bank details is refused.
- **Prototype today:** Add and Edit forms take registration number, name, phone, email, unit value, GST period, start date and 'HPI CPN (optional)'. HPI CPN is normalised and checked for uniqueness only when typed; blank is accepted. No bank field in the type, forms, store actions, seed or record page. Record page shows 'HPI CPN not set' as a valid state. Profile history sheet (audit) exists.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - No bank account details field (type, add and edit sheets, record page, seed, audit field labels)
  - HPI CPN is optional (type comment 'where available', label '(optional)', addAnaesthetist/editAnaesthetist accept blank, 'HPI CPN not set' branch); must become mandatory and refuse the save
  - No refusal when bank details are missing
  - Seed anaesthetists need bank details (all 13 already have an HPI CPN); PERSIST_VERSION bump (currently 25)
  - Bank details visible to office only (already true: no anaesthetist profile screen) and history on change (HistorySheet exists, needs the new field label)

Corrected the reviewer's seed point: every seeded anaesthetist already has an HPI CPN (cast.ts:50-63), so the seed only needs bank details. Confirmed PERSIST_VERSION is 25 and a seed shape change needs a bump. Noted the existing audit history sheet covers the history need once a field label is added.

## US-12.2.1 · Outstanding balances list · Contradicts

[US-12.2.1](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.2.1.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.2.1) · status Agreed (4) · surfaces: mobile, web, store, domain, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each anaesthetist sees a flat list of their own unpaid payables (~100 rows), oldest first, with no rollup, no ageing buckets and no age chips; each line opens its Booking (Booking need not be shown on the line). AA accepted this 'for now' (OQ-59): no Overdue view.
- **Prototype today:** Mobile Balances 'Outstanding' list: one card per ACCPAY invoice (patient, outstanding, invoice no, counterparty) sorted by raisedAtISO, with an AgeChip 'Nd' (warning tint over 60 days) and ACC chip, plus a total card. Web Accounts > Overdue table: one row per invoice with Current / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+ bucket columns, bucket totals row, ACC column, none clickable. Web Dashboard has a 'Receivables aging' panel with four bucket bars and 'n accounts over 60 days' link to Overdue. Rows have no navigation to a Booking.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Age chips on mobile rows (AgeChip, days > 60 warning) must go
  - Web 'Overdue' tab/view and its AGING_COLS bucket columns and bucket totals must go; replace with a flat list, oldest first, no age wording ('Overdue' naming contradicts the no-Overdue-view ruling)
  - Dashboard 'Receivables aging' panel (four buckets, 'accounts over 60 days' count and link) must go or become a plain outstanding total
  - AccpayInvoiceRow.bucket/agingDays and receivablesAgingFor ageing buckets are domain concepts the story rejects (the selector and its Vitest/dashboard tests change)
  - Acceptance criterion 'Back to the Booking': no row on mobile or web opens its Booking (rows carry bookingId via the case but are not links); need row tap/click to Booking detail (/mobile booking route, /web booking route) with correct Back
  - Outstanding is defined as invoice total minus payer receipts, not as the unpaid payable (US-08.3.1 pair: payable paid out to the anaesthetist); a part-paid or paid-but-not-disbursed invoice may be listed differently from 'unpaid payables'
  - No guarantee of ~100 rows in seed; volume/scroll not exercised (minor)
  - Demo guide beat S? Balances/Overdue (03-demo-script.md lines 58, 71, 698, 753-767) names Overdue and ageing and must be reworded
  - Mockup screenshots web-outstanding.png and mobile-outstanding.png on the story show the target; recaptured screens needed
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:76-86 (list), :134-147 (AgeChip), :97-98`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:88-170 (OverdueTable with buckets, tfoot totals)`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/DashboardScreen.tsx receivables aging panel (:188-225 per map)`, `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:1025-1096 (accpayRowForCase bucket/agingDays, receivablesAgingFor)`, `searched: bookingId navigation in BalancesScreen/AccountsScreen -> none`, `docs/demo-guide/03-demo-script.md:71,767`

## US-12.2.2 · GST report · Contradicts

[US-12.2.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/US-12.2.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=US-12.2.2) · status Verify (3) · surfaces: web, mobile, domain, store, seed, demo-guide · confidence high

- **Requirement:** Each month the anaesthetist gets a list of the payments AA made to them in the month with the GST component of each (not invoices sent), something to download, email or print so it suits the web app. BCTI treatment still open (OQ-29, OQ-47).
- **Prototype today:** Web Accounts > GST activity: period selector (default from the anaesthetist gstPeriod), rolling window, rows of date received, invoice, payer, amount received, GST component, period totals. Mobile Balances > 'GST this month': receipts this month with GST. Both come from gstActivityFor over billing.receipts.
- **Missing or wrong:**
  - Rows are receipts (r.atISO, grossAmount), not disbursements; billing.disbursements (id, accPayId, amount, atISO, payablesRunId) and case.disbursedAtISO exist and should drive the list (US-10.1.2)
  - Bi-monthly / six-monthly options and the gstPeriod master field contradict a monthly report
  - No download, email or print (searched web apps for print\|download\|mailto\|csv: none)
  - Labels 'Amount received' / 'Payer' / 'No payments received' should read as payments passed to the anaesthetist
  - BCTI link not shown (open in OQ-29, OQ-47; status Verify, so low priority)
- **Demo trigger:** Existing 'Simulate payment and payout' on the Xero pair view (Demo > Xero) records the disbursement that should populate the month's report; add 'Download CSV', 'Print' and 'Email me this report' buttons on the web GST activity tab (CSV/print produce a file or print view of the rows; email shows a sent confirmation toast).
- **Evidence:** `aa-prototype/src/store/selectors.ts:975-1005`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/web/screens/AccountsScreen.tsx:384-470`, `aa-prototype/src/apps/mobile/screens/BalancesScreen.tsx:40-45,113-135`, `aa-prototype/src/domain/types.ts:1361-1367 (Disbursement), :237 (GstPeriod)`

## FT-12.2 · Reporting for anaesthetists · Partial

[FT-12.2](../../../../requirements-board/requirements/stories/FT-12.2.md) · [board](http://localhost:5180/#/board?item=FT-12.2) · status Proposed (1) · surfaces: mobile, web, store · confidence medium

- **Requirement:** Anaesthetists get two reports of their own: a flat list of what is owed to them and a monthly GST report of payments AA passed on, both read from the Billing/Invoice Engine's own ledger, not from Xero. OQ-123 asks whether practice reporting (e.g. modifier use) is wanted or only hooks kept.
- **Prototype today:** Mobile BalancesScreen has Outstanding / GST this month toggle (calendar month to date); web AccountsScreen has Overdue, Payments, AA fees and a GST report tab (rolling months, not tax periods). - **Missing or wrong:**
  - Outstanding list still carries ageing buckets and age chips (contradicts US-12.2.1), so the 'flat list' report is not as now written
  - GST report is a rolling-month window, not tax-period or 'monthly report of payments AA passed to them' strictly; gstPeriod only sets default (per prototype map)
  - No hook or demo placeholder for reporting on billing practice (OQ-123, unresolved; hooks-only reading is not shown)
  - Sibling stories US-12.2.2 and US-12.2.3 are outside this grading but share the feature
