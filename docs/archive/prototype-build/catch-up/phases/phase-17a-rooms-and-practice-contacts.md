## Goal

Ben's specialist list (3 November) is the master list of surgeons and rooms FT-13.6 waited for. It shows that most practices give one shared inbox, 16 give no email and some no phone, that at one ENT practice each surgeon has their own PA and inbox, and that four surgeons sit at two practices. Built Phase 17 cannot hold any of that: `cleanRoomFields` refuses a blank email (`surgeonActions.ts:115`), `SurgeonRoom.contactEmail` is a required string (`types.ts:281`), `Surgeon.roomId` is one required id, and the directory seed writes an invented `reception@` address where the list gives none and takes the smaller practice for the four.

1. **A room may hold no email and no phone** (US-13.6.1's note, DM-60). Both become optional, absent when blank (as `Hospital.contactEmail` already is, `types.ts:252`). Every view says so plainly ("No email held"); the directory seed loses its invented placeholders.
2. **A surgeon belongs to one or more rooms** (FT-13.6, US-13.6.1, US-13.6.2; OQ-128 answered, D75; built as answered, no label). `Surgeon.roomId` becomes an ordered list `roomIds`, one or more, **the first the main room**, so a single-room read keeps one meaning. It runs through the type, the surgeon form (a multi-select needing at least one room), the profile (a card per room), the rooms view (a surgeon listed under each of their rooms), audit field labels, the derived room-to-surgeons lookup, every reader of `roomId` (about 10 non-test files) and the seed, where the four two-practice surgeons hold both rooms.
3. **Practice manager and PA contacts** (US-13.6.5, OQ-127 default, D74, labelled provisional): a **contacts list on the room**, each `RoomContact { id, name, email, role: 'PRACTICE_MANAGER' | 'PA', forSurgeonId? }`, `forSurgeonId` naming one surgeon of that room. That one optional field is the owner, so moving contacts to the surgeon later is a small change; the room list and `forSurgeonId` are read only through the pure helper. A surgeon's profile shows, for each of their rooms, the room's contacts and those named for them. No surgeon email field. The office adds, edits and removes contacts by hand (Ben does not hold them); a few are seeded on the fictional list's pattern. Office only, audited through `mutate()`.
4. **One helper for the address the office's emails use** (`src/domain/roomContacts.ts`), for 35's update email and 40's NHI request: the room's contact email, empty when none is held. For a surgeon at several rooms, which room's contacts apply is OQ-127 part 5 (D74's extension, provisional, in this one helper): the **main room's** email by default, with the surgeon's other rooms returned so 35's and 40's drafts offer them for the office to switch to. Contacts never change the To.
5. **Specialties are kept, not built** (US-13.6.6, D3, built by 18a): each surgeon keeps one or more specialties through the `roomId` to `roomIds` migration, the form, the profile and the seed edit, and a seed test proves every surgeon still holds one. Nothing new is built for them.
