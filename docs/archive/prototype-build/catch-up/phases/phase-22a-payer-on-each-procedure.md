## Goal

**Each Procedure holds its payer** (US-11.2.2, DM-11): the patient, or another payer with a `BP####` id, name, relationship, email, NZ Post postal address and phone, in 21's `BookingPayer` shape, required on every Procedure. `Booking.payer` goes. **Identity, never matching:** a payer copied to another Procedure keeps its BP id and is the same payer; a payer typed in separately is a new id even with the same name and email. Hana Park stays BP0001 on Grace Park's Procedures and the Whitcombe Family Trust BP0003, so Xero's `billableParty:BP0001` contact holds.

**One writer with an offer.** `setProcedurePayer(api, actor, procedureId, input, alsoApplyTo)` replaces `setBookingPayer`. Changing one Procedure's payer offers the same change on the Booking's other Procedures that hold the same payer, each ticked by default and never forced (US-11.2.2 "Change offered to the others"). **A Procedure added later** (an extra procedure, or a post-op addendum's Procedure while the addendum is still its own Booking, until 38b) takes the payer of the Booking's payer-billed Procedures when they all share one, else the patient ("Procedure added later").

**Who is billed per Procedure** (US-08.2.1, D17): `billablePartyForProcedure` reads the Procedure's payer. Invoices already group by counterparty ref, so Procedures sharing a payer share one invoice and a separately typed payer gets their own, sent once per payer (US-08.4.2). A Procedure whose Contract bills its holder never invoices its payer. **Prepayment, today's raise only** (US-06.3.1, D6, D23): one prepayment invoice per payer, and 21's lock becomes per payer: a payer is locked on the Procedures their own raised prepayment invoice covers, while a payer whose own is not raised can still change. 27 builds the approval lifecycle on this.

**The contact rule and the child warning per Procedure** (US-11.2.3, US-11.2.4, US-13.7.1, D4, D70): asked on each Procedure billed to its payer, never on a holder-billed one; the child-payer rule raises one finding per Procedure and the to-do list folds them to one row per Booking. **A split share can name another person** (US-08.2.3, DM-28, D18) with their own contact and BP id; the under-18 check reads only a share billed to the patient.

**One place to see and change it** (US-11.2.2, US-08.2.3): the "Paid by" line of each Procedure's Billing card (22's `PaidByRow`) shows the payer's name and relationship with the "Email or postal address needed" and "Under 18" pills, only where its Contract bills the payer; a holder-billed Procedure shows its holder as today; with no Contract it reads "Payer: not billed yet". One **Paid by sheet** (bottom sheet on the phone, Dialog on web and Admin): who pays at the top, then the offer, then the split. The Booking-level Payer section goes in all three apps; the Insurer or funder section and its approval number stay on the Booking, never in Paid by (US-11.4.3, D28). Review shows each Procedure's payer.

**One place each.** Who is billed stays in `aa-prototype/src/domain/billing/whoIsBilled.ts`; the copy rule, the offer set and payer identity go in one new pure `domain/billing/procedurePayer.ts` beside it; `setProcedurePayer` in `store/payerActions.ts` is the only writer. The S3 figures do not move.

## Adversarial review (after build)

The standard pass (PROGRESS convention 18): quality, bugs and plan adherence, plus a **money-integrity** lens. Steer the reviewers at:
- **Identity, never matching.** Find any path that groups, resolves or locks a payer by name or email; any copy that loses a BP id; any rename that keeps one (21's rule); two Procedures on one Booking holding one BP id with different details.
- **The offer.** Only `payerOfferFor` Procedures can be applied to; unticking leaves them untouched; nothing applies without the user's tick; the provisional label appears once.
- **The copy rule.** Every creation path fills the payer; `addProcedure` and the addendum use `payerForNewProcedure`; no Procedure exists without a payer (a seed test).
- **Parity.** The fixture passes with no declared difference; S3 unchanged; Xero contact `billableParty:BP0001` unchanged.
- **Prepayment.** One invoice per payer, never two for one; the lock is per payer; a split prepaid Procedure still refused; a later raise invoices only new payers.
- **Warnings.** A holder-billed Procedure raises no contact ask or child warning; one to-do row per Booking; Clear clears every Procedure's warning; the sample restores each Procedure.
- **No Booking-level payer.** The grep gate returns nothing; the insurer or funder never shows in Paid by and never decides who is billed.
- **Private copies.** A new private copy of a shared component, or a sheet built outside `src/shared/surface`, is a finding.
