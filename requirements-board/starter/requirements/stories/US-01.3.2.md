The board's owner sets up the project's vocabulary, and how its requirements are written.

- **Components** (Settings): the areas of your system, each with a three-letter code for the card glyph. Leave the list empty to accept any.
- **Statuses** (Settings): keep these or define your own, with help text that names who signs requirements off.
- **Writing voice** (Settings): how requirements are written, kept in `requirements/VOICE.md`. Claude Code follows it whenever it writes or rewords a card, so edit it to the way your team writes.
- **Skills**: each skill in `requirements-board/agent/skills/` has a "This repo" section. Fill in who signs off, any folders that are out of bounds, and where your app and build plan live. `requirements-board/ADOPTING.md` has the checklist.
