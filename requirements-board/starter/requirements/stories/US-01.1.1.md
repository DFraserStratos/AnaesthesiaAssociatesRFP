As a new user, I can click any card to open its sheet and read the whole requirement.

Cards show only the title and the start of the description. The sheet holds the rest: acceptance criteria, notes, open questions, sources and the artifacts behind it. Edit any field there and it saves to the card's file.

## Acceptance criteria

- Clicking a card opens its sheet beside the board.
- The lineage pills at the top of the sheet lead back to the feature and the epic.
