The diagrams, mockups, transcripts and documents behind a requirement are attached as artifacts, and a requirement can point at a spot in one.

This is so a reader can see the picture or the words a requirement came from, not just a summary of them.

`/add-artifact` registers a file you already have; `/svg-diagram` draws a new diagram in the board's house style. Either way it appears on the Artifacts tab with named **regions**. This card links to the [side inputs region](AR-01#side-tools) of the loop diagram: open it from the sheet and the region is highlighted.
