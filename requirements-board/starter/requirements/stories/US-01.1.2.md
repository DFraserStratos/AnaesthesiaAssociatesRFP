A card's status pill shows how far its requirement has got: proposed, waiting on a question, to verify, confirmed or retired.

This card is **Open** because a question affects it: [who signs requirements off](OQ-01). Answer the question on the Outstanding items tab, then move this card on. Rename, recolour or add statuses in Settings; cards store only the number.
