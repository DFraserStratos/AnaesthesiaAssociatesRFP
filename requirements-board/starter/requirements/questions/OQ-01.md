An example question, to show how outstanding items work. Questions hold anything not yet decided; the cards they affect stay **Open** until they are answered.

Who on the client's side signs requirements off, so a card can move to Confirmed? Write the answer on the Outstanding items tab, then run `/update-requirements` to carry it into the cards (and into the skills' "This repo" sections).
