How a team iterates its requirements with the Requirements Board and the Claude Code skills, as a loop of six steps round the catalogue: evidence (a meeting, transcript, email or decision), `/update-requirements` (files a note, answers the questions the evidence settles, updates epics, features and stories, writes a change log), review on the board (statuses, outstanding questions, swimlanes), commit to git, `/update-build-plan <commit>` (re-grades the changed requirements against the app and updates the build plan), and build, then round again at the next meeting. Side inputs: `transcript-reconciler` merges duplicate transcripts of a meeting; `add-artifact` and `svg-diagram` add the diagrams and documents requirements link to. Now and then, `link-requirements` links items to each other and `consistency-review` catches drift across the whole catalogue.
