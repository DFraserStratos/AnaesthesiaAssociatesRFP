Donald's decisions of 17 November, made while reviewing the prototype's Booking screens: the payer moves from the Booking to each Procedure, following Greg's technical design v6. Changing one Procedure's payer offers the change to the others billing the same payer; a Procedure added later copies the payer; invoices and prepayment invoices are one per payer; a split can name another person; and the payer shows in each Procedure's "Paid by" only where its Contract bills the payer. Not yet confirmed by AA or Greg.
