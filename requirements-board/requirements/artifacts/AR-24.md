The billing and invoice flow from Booking to settled payment, in four stages. At booking setup the office gives each Procedure one Contract (holder, Contract and line; the RVG code is optional, and the procedure is the text as received), and the engine checks whether a prepayment is required and outstanding, which never blocks the procedure. If it is, the prepayment invoice is generated when an admin approves it: its pair is a receivable from the payer on the Booking and a payable to the prepay holding account, mirrored to Xero; it goes out with a prepayment letter, is paid into the holding account within AA's trust accounting, is reconciled, and the requirement is re-checked. A move to another anaesthetist changes nothing in the ledger. After the procedure the anaesthetist records the billing data, may change the Contract until the List is AUTHORISED, and may change the price, rate or discount only where the Contract's flag allows it; they mark each Booking complete and submit the List. The office reviews and corrects it, the anaesthetist can still change it, and the last change stands. Authorising locks the whole List and hands it to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit; after that, corrections are additional invoices or credits, never edits. The engine applies the multi-procedure rule, calculates each fee, deducts any prepayment (a prepaid Procedure's anaesthetist is paid by a second pair from the holding account, its trigger still to confirm), creates a linked receivable and payable in the internal ledger, generates one invoice per billable party per Booking and sends the pair to Xero (ACCREC, draft ACCPAY). The engine sends the invoice; a payment detected in Xero is reconciled to the ledger and the payable is authorised for exactly the amount received, any remaining balance staying outstanding. The weekly payables run and the approval of a period's BCTIs are not yet confirmed (OQ-47).
