The billing and invoice flow from Booking to settled payment, in four stages. At booking setup the office gives each Procedure one Contract (holder, Contract and line; the RVG code is optional, and the procedure is the text as received), and the engine checks whether a prepayment is required and outstanding, which never blocks the procedure. If it is, the prepayment invoice is generated when an admin approves it (an optional step AA can switch off): its pair is a receivable from the payer on the Booking and a payable to the AA Holding account, mirrored to Xero; it goes out with a prepayment letter, is paid and held in the AA Holding account (the money sits in AA's trust account), is reconciled, and the requirement is re-checked. A move to another anaesthetist changes nothing in the ledger. Cancelled before the procedure, a prepayment is credited if unpaid or refunded from the AA Holding account if paid, raised automatically and paid from the system (OQ-115). After the procedure the anaesthetist records the billing data, may change the Contract until the List is AUTHORISED, and may change the price, rate or discount where the Contract's flag allows it, but never a fixed price (OQ-117) and never on a prepaid Contract, which is not editable (OQ-96); they mark each Booking complete and submit the List. The office reviews and corrects it, the anaesthetist can still change it, and the last change stands. Authorising locks the whole List and hands it to the Billing/Invoice Engine as one unit; after that, corrections are additional invoices or credits, never edits. The engine applies the multi-procedure rule, calculates each fee and leaves prepaid Procedures off the final invoice, mixed Bookings included: nothing is deducted, and the final invoice covers only the Procedures that are not prepaid (OQ-116). Each prepaid Procedure's prepayment is instead released from the AA Holding account to whoever did the List by a journal transfer, not a payment, its trigger still open (OQ-125); any difference on a prepaid Procedure is a second invoice or credit raised by hand (OQ-96). The engine then creates a linked receivable and payable in the internal ledger, generates one invoice per billable party per Booking and sends the pair to Xero (ACCREC, draft ACCPAY). The engine sends the invoice (shown so for explanation: whether our system or Xero sends invoices is for the Stratos dev team to decide at build); a payment detected in Xero is reconciled to the ledger and the payable is authorised for exactly the amount received, any remaining balance staying outstanding. The weekly payables run and the approval of a period's BCTIs are not yet confirmed (OQ-47).
