The billing and invoice flow from Booking to settled payment, in four stages. At booking setup each Procedure gets one Contract, and the engine checks whether a prepayment is required and outstanding (it never blocks the procedure); if so it raises a prepayment invoice, which the office approves and the engine sends, paid into AA's trust account and reconciled, then re-checked. After the procedure the anaesthetist records the billing data, may adjust the price where the Contract allows, marks each Booking complete and submits the List; the office reviews and authorises it, locking the Bookings, Procedures and Contracts. The Billing/Invoice Engine then applies the multi-procedure rule, calculates each fee, deducts any prepayment, creates a linked receivable and payable in the internal ledger, generates one invoice per billable party per Booking and sends the pair to Xero (ACCREC, draft ACCPAY). The engine emails the invoice; a payment detected in Xero is reconciled to the ledger and the payable is authorised for exactly the amount received, any remaining balance staying outstanding.
