Where the money goes for a procedure billed after it is done, against a prepaid one, side by side, with a running example ($100 not prepaid, paid to Dr Nick; $200 prepaid, done by Dr Ben). Not prepaid: once the List is authorised, an invoice pair (a receivable from the billable party, a payable to the anaesthetist who did the List); payment into AA's trust bank account clears the receivable; the payable is authorised for what was received and paid in the payables run. Prepaid: once an admin approves the prepayment invoice, a pair whose payable is to the AA Holding account; payment into the same trust bank account; after the procedure, a journal transfer (not a payment) moves the amount from the AA Holding account to whoever did the List, who is then paid in the payables run. A cancelled prepaid Booking is credited if unpaid, refunded from the AA Holding account if paid. The right-hand panel shows where it all sits, as Greg drew it on 28 October: one trust bank account; the debtors and creditors control accounts holding totals; and their sub-ledgers (patients and payers; each anaesthetist plus the AA Holding account, a creditor, not a bank or ledger account). Still open: which event releases the prepayment (OQ-125), and the payables run and buyer-created tax invoice timing (OQ-47, OQ-29).
