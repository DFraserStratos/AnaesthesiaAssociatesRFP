The entity relationship diagram of the contract pricing model, version 6: the RVG group (master data), contract lines, contracts and contract holders (contract data), and the booking procedure that is priced under one contract and line. It is a design proposal and may change. The design it illustrates is [AR-29](AR-29).

Historical: this is the v6 design's ERD, replaced on 5 November 2026 by the flat Contract model, drawn as [AR-61](AR-61). Its Contract lines, Contract versions and holder-level billing no longer describe the model.

Retired on 5 November 2026: it no longer describes the design, which is now the flat Contract model ([ERD](AR-61), domain model). Kept as a record of what was proposed; requirements no longer link to it (Donald, Notes 2026-11-05 · Donald contract model decisions #27).
