How the office decides where a Procedure's invoice goes, as Ben drew it on 30 October 2026. The first
answer is the patient or a facility. Under the patient, the question is whether it is prepaid. Under a
facility, it is the facility's Contract and its funding tag (SXAP, HNZ or ACC), MacMurray's procedure
type, or one of the special contracts. Each path ends in what happens next: a prepayment invoice to the
patient, an agreed fee pre-filled, or the Booking sent to the anaesthetist to fill in the RVG.

The [step rail](US-04.3.8) follows this tree: where the invoice goes, then the payment type or the
funding tag, then the Contract.

Redrawn from Ben's Word document (an ASCII tree, `docs/discovery-reference/Invoice_Decision_Tree.docx`)
left to right, with the same branches and outcomes. Wording brought into line with the catalogue: "Card"
is now "Booking", "St Georges" is "St George's", "Is it a prepaid account?" is "Is it prepaid?", "Send
prepaid account to patient" is "Send the prepayment invoice to the patient", and "contract" is
capitalised as "Contract". Under each facility the tree drew the outcome beneath its last funding tag;
here it follows all of that facility's tags, as the tree means. The colours of the outcomes are added.
