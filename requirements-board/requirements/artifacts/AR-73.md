Donald's decisions of 26 November on Bookings with no Contract: a missing Contract is common and often the anaesthetist's to settle; what makes it matter is that the List cannot be submitted without one; the office reads the Procedures at intake and looks for prepaid ones; some Contract information is not the same as none; the Admin Day rail's work card gains a Contracts tab, one row per List, while the To-do list keeps warnings only; and a combined Lists page as a later idea.
