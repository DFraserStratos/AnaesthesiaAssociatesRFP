The three terms that decide who gets an invoice, and how they connect: the contract holder (who the arrangement is with), the billable party (who receives the invoice) and the payer on the Booking (prefilled from the patient, changeable to a parent or guardian). Each Procedure's Contract decides: when its holder is the billable party, the holder's own billable party record is billed; otherwise the payer is. The office's steps at booking setup, and six typical arrangements with who is billed under each.
