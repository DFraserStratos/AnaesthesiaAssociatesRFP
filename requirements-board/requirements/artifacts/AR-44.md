The three terms that decide who gets an invoice, and how they connect (revised 5 November to the flat Contract model): the Party that holds a Contract, the billable party (who receives the invoice) and the payer on the Booking (prefilled from the patient, changeable to a parent, guardian or other payer). Each Contract's own setting decides: when it bills its holder, the holder's Party billing record is billed; otherwise the payer is. The office's step rail at booking setup (where the invoice goes, then the Payment type or funding tag, then the Contract or Other price), and eight example Contracts showing their Party, billable party and invoice destination, with No contract · RVG first.
