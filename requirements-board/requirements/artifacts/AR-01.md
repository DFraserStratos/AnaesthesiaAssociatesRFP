How a Procedure gets its price, in four steps: what goes in (the Contract and line, an optional RVG code, time and modifier units, the anaesthetist's own unit value and the procedure date); the values on the Procedure, filled in from the Contract line and the RVG code and then changeable by whoever is permitted, the last change standing, and reset when the Contract changes; the price, which is the Procedure's own price where it holds one and otherwise (base + time + modifier units) x rate x (1 - discount), with its checks; and who is billed, with the invoice and the snapshot taken when the List is authorised. A running example (St George's, RVG less 10%) is carried through, and seven worked examples price the same H3 anaesthetic on different Contracts.
