How a Procedure gets its price on the flat Contract model, in four steps (revised 5 November 2026 to the flat Contract model, from Donald's decisions and the contract model proposal, AR-58): what goes in (the Contract, one per priced item, with No contract · RVG always offered first; the RVG code, time and modifier units, needed on an RVG price only; the anaesthetist's own unit value; the procedure date, which picks the price range in force on a fixed price); the values on the Procedure, which holds its units and any typed price while rate and discount come from the pricing kind, who may type a price, how an untyped price follows a new price range or an edited rate or discount, and how changing the Contract clears a typed price; the price, which is the price the Procedure holds (the snapshot, the prepaid amount once invoiced, or a typed price) and otherwise comes from the Contract's pricing kind (RVG at the anaesthetist's rate, RVG at the Contract's rate, RVG less a discount, fixed price from its price ranges, open price), with "Needs a price" and the multi-procedure units rule; and who is billed, the Contract billing its holder or the payer and so giving its invoice destination (Patient, Hospital or Special), with the invoice and the snapshot taken when the List is authorised. A running example (St George's · HNZ, RVG less 10%) is carried through, and nine worked examples price the same H3 anaesthetic on different Contracts, among them a backdated price range (OQ-131) and an amount the office types on Prepaid · Fixed price (Ben's "just make it two grand").
