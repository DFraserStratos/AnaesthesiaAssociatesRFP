A snapshot, taken on 5 November, of Donald's design for the flat Contract model: every priced item is one Contract with its own settings, held by a Party, with pricing kinds, dated price ranges for fixed prices, specialties, funding tags, open-price fallbacks and a derived invoice destination driving the step rail. It replaces the Contract header and lines, versions, line keys and the technical design v4 and v6 tables. Its appendix holds Donald's decisions D1 to D11, which the model is written from; section 11.1 holds the assumptions made where the decisions leave a gap.
