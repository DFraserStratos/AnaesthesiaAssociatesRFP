The entity relationship diagram of the flat Contract model decided on 5 November 2026: a party holds Contracts, each Contract one priced item with its own settings (bills its holder or the payer, an optional funding tag, Payment type Standard or Prepaid, one of five pricing kinds, price ranges for a fixed price only, specialties, linked surgeons and at most one linked anaesthetist, Active or Disabled), and each Procedure on a Booking is priced under exactly one Contract. The invoice destination and pricing kind are drawn as boxes for reading, though the destination is worked out from the Contract's settings and never stored. It is drawn from the ER diagram in the domain model (§2) and the [Contract model proposal](AR-58#3-er-diagram) section 3, the domain model winning where they differ; field names are for orientation only, entities shown without fields are defined elsewhere in the domain model, and the ledger, modifiers and Slots are left off. It replaces Greg's v6 ERD (AR-30), kept as history.
