How a Procedure gets its Contract and who pays, revised 5 November to the flat Contract model: the procedure as text on the Booking (no procedure master list), its invoice description (the rooms', then the hospital's, then the Contract's, an admin's edit always winning, OQ-130), and what each Procedure gets (exactly one Contract, an RVG code, a price; "Needs an invoice destination" when none). Contracts as a Party, a Contract's undated settings and its pricing kind, with examples coloured by invoice destination: No contract · RVG and the open-price fallbacks (AA), Merivale's fixed fees with a backdated price range, an anaesthetist's prepaid Contract, Christchurch Eye Surgery's SXAP items, and NIB billed directly. Then the office's step rail: the Contracts offered on the List (specialty filter, no "show all"), step 1 where the invoice goes, step 2 Payment type or funding tag, step 3 pick the Contract or Other price, then the anaesthetist's RVG detail; the insurer or funder only suggests a path (OQ-93).
