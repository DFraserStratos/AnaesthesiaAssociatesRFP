How a Procedure gets its Contract and who pays: the procedure as text on the Booking (no procedure master list) with its invoice description; Contracts as a holder, a header and lines, with No contract (RVG) as AA's one standing Contract and examples for a hospital, an insurer, an anaesthetist's prepay list and a surgeon's rooms; and the office's choice at booking setup, from the filtered list to the Contract, its line, the optional RVG code, who pays and prepayment. Open matters are marked: the invoice description (OQ-103), whether insurance narrows the list (OQ-93) and how a surgeon arrangement is found (OQ-105).
