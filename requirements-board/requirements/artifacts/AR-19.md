The AA process at mid level, with a note on each step: the Scheduling Engine keeps the rolling schedule, Bookings come in from hospital systems, surgeons' rooms, anaesthetists and the AA office, the anaesthetist does the work and submits the List in the Anaesthetist App, the office reviews, corrects and authorises it in the Admin App, the Billing/Invoice Engine finds who pays, calculates the charges and generates the invoices, then the invoice goes to the billable party while Xero tracks the receivable and payable through to payment into AA and payment to the anaesthetist.
