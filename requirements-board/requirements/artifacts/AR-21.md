How booking information reaches the Scheduling Engine after MVP. Hospital rows from St George's and Southern Cross (and later other hospitals and surgeons' PDF Lists) wait on the Admin App's matching screen; when an admin opens one, the system proposes a Booking or List with the same surgeon, hospital, day and session, and patient NHI, and the admin can also search by hand. No match lets the admin create the Booking on an existing List, a new List or a Draft List, or reject the row, and the engine adds the new Booking; a match shows the field-level differences for the admin to approve, and the engine applies the modification, reschedule or cancellation. AA office and anaesthetist entries go straight in. The engine records it in the append-only history and shows the latest Booking, and further changes loop back through the matching screen until the anaesthetist submits the List, after which no changes are allowed and the latest Booking details are ready for the procedure.
