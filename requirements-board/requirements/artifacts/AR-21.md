How booking information reaches the Scheduling Engine. Hospital rows from St George's and Southern Cross arrive on the Admin App's matching screen on their own, and the surgeons' rooms' PDF Lists are read from the office's mailbox (other hospitals later); AA only reads from these sources. Everything waits in one feed grouped by List, with rows needing an NHI and PDFs that can't be read kept in it. The system finds the List with the same surgeon, hospital, day and session, then its Bookings by NHI, and shows each key, the List's earlier versions and its Slot. No match: the admin places the List in the named anaesthetist's free Slot (suggested), another free Slot, an existing List or a Draft List, or rejects it, with stubs for rows that have no NHI, and the engine adds the List and its Bookings. A match: the admin reviews the List's differences one Booking per row, leaves out any change, and applies the rest; a Booking missing from a re-sent PDF is offered as a removal, and Lists that match exactly are confirmed together. The engine applies the modification or cancellation (a move to another day arrives as a removal and a new Booking), records it in the append-only history and shows the latest Booking. AA office and anaesthetist entries go straight in. Further changes loop back through the matching screen until the office authorises the List; what happens to a change after that is not yet decided.
