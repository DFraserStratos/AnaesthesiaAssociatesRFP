# Requirements update, 2026-11-04: Donald's working decisions

Donald answered the open questions most likely to change the prototype, so it can be updated on the
current understanding of the requirements and smaller changes made later. They came out of a review of
every item not yet agreed with Greg: none of the 120 items or 29 open questions would change the
product's direction, and the 32 that would materially change one feature came down to eight
decisions. These are Donald's decisions for the build; Greg and AA have not seen them, so every item
whose substance changed went to Verify, never Agreed.

The main changes:

- **Stubs:** a booking without an NHI shows as a stub on its List (FT-02.6 is now "Stubs on a List").
- **Both intake sources** in the first release: the hospital downloads and the rooms' PDF lists
  (US-02.2.1 leaves Future Work). Both integrated hospitals are assumed synced.
- **Events** are billed in the next run, never held for the Procedure's invoice.
- **A prepaid Contract changed** after the prepayment is sent or paid: the prepayment is refunded.
- **A surgeon can be at more than one room.**
- **Payments to anaesthetists are made in Xero**; the system tracks them.
- **The approval number** is shown to the anaesthetist when there is one.
- **The step rail** (contract-picker-mock) is the working direction for the Contract picker: a guess.
- **Contract upkeep** is a new question for Vanessa (OQ-131); US-04.2.10 and US-04.2.14 go back to Open.
