Are CS250, CS260 and CS70 the right codes for ACC pre-operative assessments? The RFP names them; they have not been confirmed. For AA's accountant.

What hangs on the answer: which codes the preconfigured ACC [pre-operative assessment](US-05.5.2) events carry.

**Already settled:**

- An ACC pre-operative assessment is recorded as a [pre-op event](US-03.7.1) against the real Procedure: its own element attached to the Procedure, not an added procedure, shown on the invoice as its own line item (OQ-63).
- It follows the generic event and pricing rules, not necessarily a fixed-fee Contract. Some ACC pre-op events may be preconfigured; otherwise the event is free text.

**Background:**

- Originally asked: confirm the three codes, and that each assessment is billed separately as a flat-fee line, with ACC otherwise following the holder's Contract.
- 1 October: recorded as pre-op events against the real Procedure; how events are modelled and invoiced went to OQ-63. See "Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #6 #34 #46 #54"
- Donald, on the board: "Our new feature for pre op / post op events will cover this requirement. But yes, these events are recorded and displayed as distinct line items."
