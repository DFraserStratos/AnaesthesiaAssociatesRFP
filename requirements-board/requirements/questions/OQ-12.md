Confirm ACC pre-operative assessments (CS250, CS260, CS70) are billed separately as flat-fee lines and that ACC otherwise follows the holder's Contract.

Meeting 2026-10-01: these assessments are recorded as pre-op events against the real Procedure. What is left for AA's accountant is confirming the codes (CS250, CS260, CS70) and that the assessment is a fixed-fee Contract. How events are modelled, and whether they show on an invoice as line items or procedures, is OQ-63.

## Answer

Our new feature for pre op / post op events will cover this requirement.

But yes, these events are recorded and displayed as distinct line items

Transcript adds: an ACC pre-operative assessment is a fixed-price service ACC pays for when the anaesthetist sees the patient beforehand. It is held as a fixed-fee Contract and is never the primary procedure. AA does little of it, but it must be supported. It is recorded as a pre-op event against the real procedure (a time or a fixed fee, no other modifiers), and a Contract can replace a recorded time with a fixed fee. Not settled: whether these show on an invoice as line items or as procedures. Left Open for AA's accountant to confirm. See Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #6 #34 #46 #54.
