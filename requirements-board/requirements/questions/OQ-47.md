Does AA's accountant, Michael, agree to a weekly cycle for paying anaesthetists from the trust account, and on which day of the week is the payment made?

The proposal, as Greg last set it out (10 November):

- an accounting week runs Monday to Sunday, named by its ISO week number (for example "week 46")
- the week is reconciled against the daily bank downloads from the Monday, with the office sorting out problems
- payment is made on Thursday (Wednesday was also floated), from a payment schedule sent to Michael
- nothing is carried forward: each week reconciles clean
- before each payment run, the accountant or Vanessa shows that the critical numbers balance; if they don't, the run stops

Today the run is Tuesday to Tuesday and banked on Wednesday, so the anaesthetists are always a day behind. Nobody knows why Wednesday was chosen; Ben can change it.

Related points for the accountant:

- On a part payment (a payment plan), is the anaesthetist's payable [released for exactly the amount received](US-10.2.1)?
- Is [approving the period's BCTIs for payment](US-10.2.6) a separate step before the run, or the same as releasing the payable?

What hangs on the answer: when each week's payments go out, and when the period's BCTIs are approved.

Recommendation: adopt the weekly cycle if Ben agrees, because it gives the office time to reconcile before paying. This is a proposal, not a confirmed requirement.

**Already settled:**

- The 20th of the month is a separate monthly run for non-trust payments, handled by Michael: the service-fee invoices (the monthly [fee invoice run](US-10.3.1), OQ-60) and rent, which is outside the system.
- Once AA has generated a period's BCTIs, it approves them for payment before they are paid.
- Ben, Vanessa and Greg said the weekly cycle "makes sense" (7 October), so the prototype and requirements are built to it as a proposal.

Shown as not yet decided on [the billing and invoice flow](AR-24#payables-approval-undecided), and as proposed, to confirm, on [the billing, Xero and payment lifecycle](AR-25#payment-cycle).
