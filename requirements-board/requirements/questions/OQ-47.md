AA's payment run today is Tuesday to Tuesday and banked on Wednesday, so the anaesthetists are always a day behind. Outgoing money is on the 20th of the month and is handled by Michael, the accountant. Nobody knows why Wednesday was chosen; Ben can change it.

Greg proposed a weekly accounting cycle:

- an accounting week named by its ISO week number (for example "week 46") as the unit
- a Friday close: snapshot the ledger and reconcile it against the daily bank downloads
- a working day on Monday for the office to sort out problems
- a payment schedule to Michael for Tuesday
- payment anomalies handled in the office and rolled into the cycle

Is the payment day fixed at Wednesday, or does AA move to a weekly cycle like this? How does the 20th-of-the-month payment fit with it?

Recommendation: adopt Greg's weekly cycle if Ben agrees, because it gives the office a day to reconcile. This is a proposal, not a confirmed requirement.

Meeting 2026-10-01: Greg says trust-account payments to anaesthetists go out weekly, and the 20th is a separate monthly cycle for non-trust payments such as the service-fee invoices and rent. Rent is outside the system; the service-fee invoices are the monthly fee-invoice run (OQ-60). Greg does not know why the 20th is in the question. Also new: once AA generates a period's BCTIs, it approves them for payment before they are paid. The weekly cycle is still to confirm with Ben.
