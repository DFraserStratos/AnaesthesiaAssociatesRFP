The [age modifiers](US-05.1.5) (A1, A2) are applied automatically from the patient's age and locked. The modifiers file leaves open the exact band boundaries (exactly 1, 2, 70, 80), whether A1 or A2 also apply on the age-split base codes H6b and P2, and whether ASE stacks with an ASA code.

Which age is used (on the procedure date) and how do the boundaries work?

Also to confirm: the ASA grade no longer pre-fills modifier units, and defaults on a procedure no longer seed its modifiers. The directors said the system applies only the modifiers that cannot be removed (age, and a modifier already included in a code's base units) and "All else are optional", but did not name the ASA grade, which the guide said would stop pre-filling only "if the directors agree with Ben" (Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #3). Does AA agree that neither pre-fills, so the ASA grade is just one of the optional modifiers the anaesthetist chooses?

Recommendation: Use the patient's age on the procedure date, inclusive lower bounds as printed, and do not add an age modifier on base codes already split by age; confirm with Vanessa. Take no ASA or procedure-default pre-fill as settled unless AA says otherwise.
