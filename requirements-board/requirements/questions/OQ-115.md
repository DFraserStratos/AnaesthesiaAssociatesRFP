**Already settled:** the ledger side is the same either way: the holding-account pair is reversed ([US-06.5.2](US-06.5.2)). All payments come to AA, and a prepayment is held in the AA Holding account until the procedure is done (Donald, 20 October).

**Still to answer, for Greg:**

1. **When a prepayment is cancelled, is the cash refund to the payer paid out from the new system, or through Xero's refund process?** [US-06.5.1](US-06.5.1) and [US-06.5.2](US-06.5.2) say from the system. On 2 and 16 October cash refunds were said to happen outside the system, in Xero, shown only as an event on the Procedure.

Update 2026-10-28: payments out of the holding account are made from the system, and cancelling raises the credit or refund. Reading [US-06.5.1](US-06.5.1) ("payments out of it are made from the system, not by hand outside it"), Greg: "That's important. Otherwise, the balance breaks... You end up with a reconciliation imbalance." On cancellation, Greg: "scenario one is they haven't paid, so you raise a credit note and it nullifies the transaction. Scenario two, they've paid you refund. That should be as part of the booking cancellations process. So if you press cancel, it should automatically do this"; the refund is "just a normal refund transaction". Still open: whether the cash refund itself is paid through Xero's refund process. See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #8 #64 #65"

## Answer

From the system (Greg and Donald, 28 October). Payments out of the AA Holding account are made from the system, not by hand outside it. Greg: "That's important. Otherwise, the balance breaks... You end up with a reconciliation imbalance." Pressing cancel on a prepaid Booking raises the credit note or the refund automatically. Greg: "scenario one is they haven't paid, so you raise a credit note and it nullifies the transaction. Scenario two, they've paid you refund. That should be as part of the booking cancellations process. So if you press cancel, it should automatically do this." The refund is "just a normal refund transaction."

This overtakes, for a cancelled prepayment, the 2 and 16 October line that refunds happen outside the system and show only as an event on the Procedure. Whether the cash refund goes through Xero's refund process was not discussed (point 8); how the system's refund reaches Xero is a build matter. See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #8 #64 #65"
