Every Contract needs AA's own unique identifier, with holder codes kept for reference, and combinations are Contracts too, so there will be "thousands". What coding scheme does AA use for Contracts, and how is the bucket kept navigable when picking one (procedure, then hospital, then search by code)? Donald: "Give me a shot to make it navigable."

Recommendation: a short structured AA code per Contract and a picker filtered by procedure and hospital with code search.

## Answer

Lets go with your Recommendation for now

The meeting adds: Greg agreed, "It's to be designed... We need to design a contract", and noted it to think about over the weekend. The code format is not set. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #7)

The meeting adds: Greg read the AA identifier story without change ("OK. So far so good") and asked for a composite search, "like a modern search where you can type it in and figure out where it is", with the holder's own codes searchable. Finding a Contract: the Contract list filters by active or all and by contract holder; Greg: navigation starts two ways, the raw RVG tree or the contract holder, which "provides you with the natural filters of what contracts are available"; the client meeting has the procedure picked first (by body section and subgroup, or by RVG code), then the Contract list filtered. An anaesthetist sees only their own first-party Contracts, not everyone's. The draft technical design gives a Contract no AA code beyond its name, and leaves the candidate list context rules open. (See "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #4 #24 #27 #52", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #27", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #23 #27")

The meeting adds: the Contract list shows Contracts, not their lines ("you've got a filter of contracts, not contract lines"): No contract (RVG) first, then the hospital's Contracts, then surgeons' and anaesthetists' arrangements; picking one navigates down to its lines, where at the last level "you don't have a choice but to scroll". Greg expects about a dozen on a hospital, to check with Vanessa; Donald expects the list may be large without an intermediate list; both agreed to test it. Contract lines get a system line key and the holder's optional reference code, and a line can carry a default RVG code so the list can be searched by RVG. (See "Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #33 #34", "Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #37 #40", "Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #44")

Update 2026-11-05 (Donald, for the build; not yet confirmed by AA or Greg): overtakes the answers above. There are no Contract lines, so the Contract is the thing picked and searched; nothing navigates down to lines, and there is no line key. Donald: "Every Contract is a single record with its own settings: no parent schedule or price list, no contract lines. A holder's schedule document becomes many Contracts." Each Contract keeps AA's own code, carried forward as its unique key, beside the holder reference code, a setting that is searchable but not unique (our reading). The code format is still not set: the prototype seeds codes such as `CES-SXAP-004`, a seed reading, not AA's scheme (AR-58 section 8.2). Finding a Contract among thousands is now the step rail: only Active Contracts that fit the List (its hospital, or linked to its surgeon or anaesthetist, or an unlinked insurer or funder) and whose specialties include the List surgeon's are offered, with no "show all", then one search by typing. Ben, of an eye schedule: "you would just go, 'CAT', and then cataract comes up." What the search covers (the name, holder reference code, invoice description, AA code and default RVG code) is our reading (AR-58 section 5.4). The recommendation stands.
