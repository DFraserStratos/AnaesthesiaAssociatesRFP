A surgeon can be held, and loaded at [cutover](US-13.4.3), without an HPI CPN, and AA's admin staff fill the numbers in later (OQ-129). Two smaller points are left open.

1. **Does the HPI CPN ever become required?** For example, before the surgeon's first Booking can be saved, or never, with a warning only.
2. **Can a row with no title or surname load?** Ben's specialist list has some rows like this.

What hangs on it: whether the [surgeon profile](US-13.6.2) and the cutover load refuse, warn about or accept a surgeon with missing details.
