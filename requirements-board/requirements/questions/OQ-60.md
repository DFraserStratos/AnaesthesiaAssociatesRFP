What makes up AA's monthly fee to each anaesthetist, and how is it charged and paid? For AA's accountant.

1. **What fixed charges make up the monthly fee?** Several items may make it up; Greg does not know the schedule.
2. **Does the per-invoice charge count only invoices that were paid?** That would stop a Booking moved from one anaesthetist to another being charged twice. Greg says it should.
3. **Is the fee always a separate receivable paid by the anaesthetist, never netted against their payables?** Greg says always separate, under trust law, paid into a separate bank account.

What hangs on the answer: what the monthly [AA fee invoices](US-10.3.1) count and charge, and the [AA fee settings](US-10.3.3) the office keeps.

Recommendation: count each BCTI once, against the anaesthetist who did the procedure, and keep the fee a separate receivable.

**Already settled:** AA's monthly fee is fixed charges plus a charge per BCTI (ACCPAY) issued to the anaesthetist (OQ-02).
