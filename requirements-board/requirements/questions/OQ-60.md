OQ-02 settled the shape of AA's monthly fee: fixed charges plus a charge per BCTI (ACCPAY) issued to the anaesthetist. Still to settle: (1) the schedule of fixed charges (several items may make it up); (2) does the per-invoice charge count only invoices that were paid, so a Booking moved from one anaesthetist to another is not charged twice; (3) does the fee net against the anaesthetist's payables, or is it always a separate receivable paid by the anaesthetist?

Recommendation: count each BCTI once, against the anaesthetist who did the procedure, and keep the fee a separate receivable.

## Meeting update

Discussed with Greg on 2026-10-02 but not answered on the board, so still Open. Part 3: the fee never nets against payables. Greg: "it absolutely doesn't. Under trust law it mustn't. So it's always a separate invoice and it's paid into a separate bank account." Part 2: the per-invoice charge should count only invoices that were paid. Greg: "It should. I don't know what it does, but that's definitely what it should do." Part 1, the schedule of fixed charges, is still unknown ("No, I don't know"). Parts 2 and 3 are Greg's view, still to confirm with AA's accountant. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #1)
