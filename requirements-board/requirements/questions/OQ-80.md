**Already settled:** when a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist, the new anaesthetist keeps the agreed amount; OQ-70's answer also has the payable half of the draft pair updated to them (but see part 3).

**Still to answer, for Greg:**

1. **When is the pair created?** When the prepayment invoice is generated, when an admin approves it, or when the money is received.
2. **At which points is it amended?** Candidates: a move to another anaesthetist, a cancellation, the final invoice.
3. **Is the payable still updated on a move, given the directors' later rule that the system should have no logic to detect these flows?** The 2026-10-07 expansion of the directors' answer does not say (see the update below).

Recommendation: create the pair when the prepayment invoice is generated, as the catalogue already says, and amend only the payable on any move before the procedure.

Shown, as answered, on [the billing and invoice flow](AR-24#prepayment-pair).

## Answer

1. **Created:** the pair is created when the prepayment invoice is generated, and the prepayment invoice is generated when an admin approves it (the office chooses the prepayment Contract, then approves the invoice where outgoing invoices are approved). "When the money is received" is not an option: no money arrives before the invoice is sent.
2. **Its other side is a prepay holding account, not the anaesthetist.** The pair is a receivable from the patient and a payable to a prepay holding account, which AA treats "just like an anaesthetist"; its balance is all the deposits received for procedures not yet done. Prepayment becomes two steps: collect the money into the holding account, then, once the procedure is done and the Booking goes to billing, a second pair moves it from the holding account to whichever anaesthetist did the List, in place of another invoice to the patient.
3. **A move changes nothing in the ledger.** When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist the payable does not move, because it is the holding account; the anaesthetist who does the List is paid at the second step. Nothing is recalculated: the price prepaid when the Booking was created and the prepayment invoice went out is the price. The system may send the patient an informational email that their anaesthetist has changed and they need do nothing; its wording is AA's choice.

This replaces OQ-70's answer that the payable half of the draft pair is updated to the new anaesthetist.

Two things are left open. Greg named the second step's trigger as the Booking being "confirmed and submitted"; in the catalogue a List goes to billing when the office authorises it, which is taken as the trigger here. And a prepaid price override, together with a move and the holding account, still needs working through; Greg "might draw that out" (OQ-96). Amending the pair on a cancellation or at the final invoice was not discussed.

Greg: "now we don't pay the money to the anaesthetist, we hold it. And it makes all the curly problems go away." "We need to set up a prepay account that has the same sort of accounting status as an anaesthetist." "If a booking is moved, the payable doesn't move because the payable is no longer the anaesthetist, the payable is the holding account."
