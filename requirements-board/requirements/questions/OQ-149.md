The [Ledger tab](US-13.2.1) lets the office place a receipt that matches no invoice, or mark it refunded, in the system, and counts it as a failed ledger check. [Bulk remittance](US-10.2.4) leaves the items Xero's remittance add-on cannot match in Xero's bank reconciliation for the office to handle there, and does not say whether such a receipt is parked on the bank reconciliation or in a holding account in Xero.

Is a receipt nobody can place placed in the system, on the Ledger tab, and then pushed to Xero; handled only in Xero, with the system reading the result; or both?

What hangs on it: the Ledger tab's place and refund actions, the receipts-to-place figure and count, and the unresolved items in the [reconciliation status](US-13.2.4).
