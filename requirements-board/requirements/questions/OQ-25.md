Q&A #2 lists 'RVG Default Contract Pre-paid' as a category; Q&A #5 says prepaid is a property of the procedure choice, not the contract. Keep the category (and if so, does it trigger prepayment on its own) or drop it and rely on the anaesthetist's prepaid RVG settings?

## Answer

At this stage, we expect that Prepaid is a property of the procedure from the anaesthetist profile, not of the contract

Superseded on 16 October, with the next paragraph: see the last paragraph.

The meeting adds: the prepaid amount now comes from a Contract. The directors answered that each anaesthetist has their own fixed-price (first-party) contracts for prepaid work, and the guide keeps as agreed that "Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or whole RVG groups, they want prepaid", so the answer above stands for which procedures are prepaid. On 7 October Greg argued the trigger belongs on the Contract ("a flag in the contract master for is pre payable", "the flag belongs with the contract, not with the anaesthetist"); Donald held it is "a decision of the anaesthetist on the anaesthetist profile, not on the contract", then accepted Greg's "I would set up two contracts for that" in the sense that each anaesthetist needs a first-party contract of their own. Final position: prepayment comes from selecting the anaesthetist's first-party fixed-price Contract; whether a pre-payable flag also sits on the Contract is not settled. (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #4 #9", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #34 #35", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #15")

The meeting adds, and moves the answer above: prepaid is now a setting on the Contract, not on the anaesthetist's profile. Donald, 16 October: "we no longer have that concept" of RVG codes treated as prepaid, and "There needs to be some human in the loop that places [it] on the contract to then trigger the prepayment." Greg: "There's no association between RVG codes and prepay." The v6 design: selecting a Contract with is_prepay set starts the prepay process. Still no Pre-paid category: it is a flag on any Contract. (See "Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #20 #22", "Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #4 #28")
