Greg: most insurer and hospital Contracts are still RVG (Southern Cross holds several), "there's nothing underneath them... it falls through to the default values", billed to their holder. In the draft design a Contract is [offered](US-04.3.2) only when it has a line for the procedure or its group, and group lines are dormant (no group prices).

How is a holder's plain RVG Contract with no special prices offered for every procedure: a line per procedure, a line per group, or a Contract with no lines that applies to every procedure?

Recommendation: Allow a Contract with no lines to apply to every procedure at RVG pricing (the anaesthetist's unit value), offered when its holder fits the Booking's context, the same way [No contract (RVG)](US-04.1.1) is offered by rule.

## Answer

A Contract with no lines, offered by the Booking's context. The v6 diagram: "A contract may hold no lines." Selection lists the default Contract first, then every Contract valid on the procedure date that fits the booking's context (the hospital, the surgeon or rooms, anaesthetist-specific lists), with no test that it has a line for the procedure. See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #2.

Donald and Greg, 16 October: such a Contract defines how a procedure is paid for and who pays, and the office can apply it "even if it's got no lines on it". See Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #5. Greg, 14 October: "a lot of the contracts are RVG contracts", and all they require is that the holder's reference, such as SXAP, goes on the invoice; Donald: such a Contract need only say "it's an RVG contract for Southern Cross. And this is who you send the invoice to". See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #2.

With the procedure master gone, "for every procedure" no longer needs a rule: Contracts are no longer filtered by procedure at all. This matches the recommendation.
