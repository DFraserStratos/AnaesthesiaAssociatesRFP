The office reads the Procedures as they arrive and picks out the prepaid ones, since only it can put a Procedure on a prepaid Contract and the [prepayment](FT-06.2) needs time before the day. Deciding a List at [intake](US-02.1.2) does not ask for a Contract on each row, so the office marks prepaid ones from the [Contracts tab](US-04.3.12) or the Booking, after intake.

Should intake offer a quick way to mark a Procedure prepaid, or choose its Contract, while the List is being decided, perhaps suggested from the heuristics the office uses; or does the office set them afterwards, from the Contracts tab?

What hangs on it: the intake workbench, how soon a prepayment request can go out, and how the Contracts tab orders its Lists.
