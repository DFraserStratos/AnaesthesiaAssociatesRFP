The Booking holds the [insurer approval number](US-11.4.3), and a Contract asks the Booking for no other reference (OQ-136). Two smaller points were left open.

1. **Is the approval number printed on the invoice to the holder** when the Booking holds one? Hospitals sometimes ask for it.
2. **Does the Booking also hold an ACC claim number,** printed on an ACC invoice?

What hangs on it: what [invoice documents](US-08.4.1) carry for a holder, and whether the Booking asks for one more field. Neither has been raised by AA as a need.
