**Already settled:** when the office moves a List, the colleagues offered are ordered by admin-only tier and preferred pairing ([availability finder](US-01.4.2)). An anaesthetist moving their own List or Booking sees no pairing warnings.

**Still to answer, for Donald:**

1. **When an anaesthetist moves their own List or a single Booking, are the colleagues offered also ordered by tier and preferred pairing, or by availability alone?** [US-01.3.6](US-01.3.6) and [US-01.4.5](US-01.4.5) say availability alone. Ordering by tier would reveal nothing directly, but it would use admin-only data.

## Answer

No they are not. This is only something the admin team can see.

Update 2026-10-29: so an anaesthetist moving their own List or a single Booking sees the colleagues offered by availability alone, never ordered by tier or preferred pairing. Tiers and preference lists order the list for the admin team only ([availability finder](US-01.4.2)).
