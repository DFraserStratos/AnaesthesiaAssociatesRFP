## Answer

Answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Greg). Donald: "Xero is assumed to handle all payments. Our system tracks them." The anaesthetist's payment is made in Xero's payables run, and the system detects and tracks it; the system does not make the payment itself. This replaces the line that trust payments are made from the new system. See "Notes 2026-11-04 · Donald working decisions #5"
