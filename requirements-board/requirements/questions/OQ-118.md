**Already settled:** the NHI is mandatory. Today's rule (19 October, OQ-49): a booking that arrives without an NHI [waits at intake](US-11.1.5) and creates no Booking.

**Still to answer, for Vanessa and/or Ben:**

1. **How often does a booking arrive without an NHI today, and from where?**
2. **Should the anaesthetist see a stub on their List until the office resolves it?** What hangs on it: whether the List can be incomplete while a booking waits at intake, or whether the system needs a placeholder Booking held on an identifier other than the NHI.

Raised on 28 October. Greg asked what today's rule means operationally, since the List is then incomplete. He would keep the booking in an error queue and show a stub on the List ("There is a booking here, but there's a problem with it"), seen by the anaesthetist and the office, held on an identifier other than the NHI. Donald recalled Ben and Vanessa saying upstream systems would not send a patient without one.
