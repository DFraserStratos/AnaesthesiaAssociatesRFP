What GST agency treatment applies to the invoices AA raises, and what must each document carry? For AA's accountant.

1. **Are receivable invoices issued in the anaesthetist's name, with AA acting as their agent?** This is the proposed treatment; not yet confirmed.
2. **Is the payable (ACCPAY) the buyer-created tax invoice?** Greg says it is: the receivable and the payable are "a matching pair". Not yet the accountant's view.
3. **How often is a buyer-created tax invoice raised?** One per procedure, one per payment, or one per weekly payment run.
4. **What is it called now?** IRD renamed the buyer-created tax invoice about 18 months ago; the new name is not known.
5. **What must appear on each document?**

What hangs on the answer: the wording and details on the [buyer-created tax invoice](US-09.1.4) and on the [invoice issued in the anaesthetist's name](US-08.4.5), and how many buyer-created tax invoices the system generates.

**Already settled:**

- All prices are held excluding GST, with GST added at the bottom of the invoice.
- GST treatment is set on the Contract, not chosen per Booking.
- AA raises buyer-created tax invoices on the anaesthetist's behalf, so it can pay out money it holds for them.
- For their GST return, each anaesthetist needs a monthly list of payments received showing the GST component, not a list of invoices sent, because some invoices are paid in part.
