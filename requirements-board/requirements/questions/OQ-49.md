The agreed direction is that the NHI is required and a missing NHI becomes a [visible exception](US-11.1.5). When the NHI is missing, can the Booking still be created in a provisional (NHI pending) state, or can it not be created at all until the NHI is known? Some surgeons' rooms' PDFs omit the NHI, so a strict block could stop a Booking that the office needs to enter.

Recommendation: allow the Booking as provisional, listed on the problem list, and block authorising its List until the NHI is added.

Meeting 2026-10-01: the lean (Donald and Greg) is to let the Booking, its Procedures and Contracts proceed with the NHI pending, flagged as an anomaly to fix, and to block authorising the List until the NHI is added; the prototype assumes this until AA answers. A procedure never goes ahead without an NHI in the room, but theatre-list PDFs sometimes omit it. Proposed: patients get the system's own unique ID, with the NHI as a second unique index added later, which sits against "Patient record keyed on NHI". When the NHI is later found, a second record for the same person collides: options are a merge screen (a big lift), a "do not change" flag on genuinely identical records, or accepting some duplicates; Greg also asks whether duplicates matter once the operation is done. The NHI can be refreshed from the central register.

The question for AA: is it more painful to block all work on a Booking without an NHI, or to allow it and handle duplicates later?

Update 2026-10-07: partly answered. Vanessa: "I think it's probably reasonable to say we need an NHI. If there isn't an NHI, it creates confusion and duplicate patient records. So yes, make NHI mandatory." The room: this "makes NHI the patient identifier throughout the system"; in the public sector a patient without one is given a temporary NHI, and even a self-funding patient from overseas will have one if they are in the hospital records. Still open: whether "mandatory" means a Booking cannot be created without an NHI, or can be held pending with later steps blocked (the recommendation above), given that some surgeons' rooms' PDFs omit it; and how the NHI as the patient identifier sits with the proposal of the system's own patient ID with the NHI as a second index. (See "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #7 #34")
