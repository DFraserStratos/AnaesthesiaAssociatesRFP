Donald's decision D10 puts "overdue alerts" outside the contract model, for the requirements update. In the meeting of 5 November, the overdue alerts were the patient balance alerts ([alert on booking a patient with unpaid bills](US-11.3.2), OQ-33, OQ-74): mild under 90 days, strong over 90, and mild for a credit balance. Vanessa asked for overdue stages to be visible, and Ben pictured "an alert box here where you could have other alerts". Separately, an [outstanding prepayment](US-06.3.2) alerts the office as the procedure date gets closer.

Does "overdue alerts" also cover the outstanding prepayment alert, so that both follow one set of rules and show together? Or does the prepayment alert stay a separate warning?

What hangs on it: whether US-06.3.2 stays in the first release as written, or moves with the overdue alerts.

1. **Two rules, shown together.** The prepayment alert stays as written, and shows beside the balance alert on the Booking.
2. **One set of overdue alerts** that includes the outstanding prepayment, built together.

Recommendation: option 1. The balance alert is about the patient's past invoices; the prepayment alert is about this Booking's own money. Raised from section 11.2, point 3, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58).

## Answer

Option 1: two rules, shown together. The outstanding prepayment alert stays a separate warning, with its own rules as [US-06.3.2](US-06.3.2) describes them, and shows beside the patient balance alert ([US-11.3.2](US-11.3.2)) on the Booking. The balance alert is about the patient's past invoices; the prepayment alert is about this Booking's own money. D10's "overdue alerts" means the patient balance alert only. Nothing moves between releases. Decided by Donald on 18 November 2026, following the recommendation.
