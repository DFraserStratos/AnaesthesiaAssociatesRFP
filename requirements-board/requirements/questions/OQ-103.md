Every RVG group now has a general procedure, which a Procedure takes when it is reached from the RVG codes tab and priced on No contract (RVG) ([procedure master](US-05.1.6)). The invoice prints the procedure's own wording, never the source text ([source wording](US-03.1.9)), so a Procedure on a general procedure would print the general procedure's name.

What does a general procedure's name say on the invoice: the RVG group's published description (for example "Moderate Procedures"), a plainer name AA writes for each group, or something else? And should the office be prompted to pick a more specific procedure at review where one exists? Also, when a holder's plain RVG Contract (one with no lines, OQ-98) is picked from the RVG codes tab, which procedure does the Procedure take?

Recommendation: Name each general procedure by its section, tier and code in plain words AA can edit (for example "Head, moderate procedure (H3)"), since sections and sub-headings alone repeat across codes; a plain RVG Contract picked from the RVG codes tab sets the group's general procedure, as No contract (RVG) does; and flag Procedures on a general procedure at office review so the office can pick a specific one where it exists, without blocking.
