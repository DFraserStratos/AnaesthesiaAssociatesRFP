**Already settled:** the invoice prints its own wording for each Procedure, never the source text ([source wording](US-03.1.9)). Agreed in principle on 16 October: the Booking adds an invoice description, copied from the Contract line's description if there is one (Ben's preference), otherwise from the Booking Procedure description.

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg:**

1. **What does the invoice say when only an RVG code was picked?** That is No contract (RVG), or a hospital's plain RVG Contract. Donald and Greg agree a patient should not receive a group name like "H1 minor procedures"; Greg leaves the wording to the developers.
2. **Which description do the anaesthetists want in each case?** They have not thought it through. Donald's idea, which Greg backed: when a hospital is billed, use the hospital's own description.
3. **Can the office edit the invoice description at review?** Proposed by us, not discussed.

Recommendation: the invoice prints the Procedure's invoice description. It starts as the Contract line's description where the line has one, otherwise as a copy of the Booking's description, and the office can edit it at review.

History, superseded on 16 October: on 8 October every RVG group had a general procedure, taken by a Procedure reached from the RVG codes tab and priced on No contract (RVG), and the question was what that general procedure's name should say on the invoice. The recommendation to name each general procedure by section, tier and code, and to flag such Procedures at office review, is withdrawn: the procedure master and its general procedures fall away (OQ-88).
