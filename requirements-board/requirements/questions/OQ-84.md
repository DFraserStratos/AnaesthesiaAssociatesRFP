Greg: when a full List is moved, "You're left with your slot, right? So we need to give that slot a default status." Not answered. US-01.4.1's Technical discussion (RFP response) says the vacated Slot returns to available, or is marked unavailable if more appropriate; the prototype asks the anaesthetist what to mark their Slot as when they reassign a List (Greg: "Because I asked that question this morning").

## Answer

One path for everyone: whoever moves a List away from a Slot, the anaesthetist or the office, and whether to another anaesthetist or back to the office, is asked what the vacated Slot becomes, free or unavailable, as the prototype already does.

Donald: "if you choose to transfer a list to somebody else or transfer it to the office, the app needs to ask you what does that slot become?" Greg: "Agreed." On the prototype's "Return to office and then mark as unavailable or free", Greg: "This is very nice. We like that." On the office reassigning: "Yes." Greg: "One path is the answer."
