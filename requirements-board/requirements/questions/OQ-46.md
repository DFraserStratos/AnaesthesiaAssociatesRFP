Vanessa's main need is telling hospitals when the anaesthetist on a List changes (cover). That is a change to a List (US-01.4.1), not to a Booking. Should [the draft email button](US-02.3.3), and the [explicit save](US-02.3.2), also cover List changes such as reassignment? Who is the To address in each case: the hospital, the surgeon's room, or both? Are there other changes hospitals or admin teams want to hear about (time, patient, cancellation)?

Recommendation: offer the button after any saved change to a Booking and after a List reassignment; To is the hospital contact for cover changes and the surgeon's room for Booking changes, editable by the admin.

## Answer

I agree.

Transcript adds: Greg thinks a prompt after every save would annoy users, and that mainly the surgeon's rooms want to hear, not the hospitals. Donald's alternative: the system remembers the last set of changes, and a draft email button creates an editable mail draft (subject and the list of changes) when pressed, rather than a prompt after each save. After Greg's remark Donald read out the recommendation (the hospital contact for cover changes) and said he was happy with it, and Greg agreed, so the recipients stand as recommended, with Greg's hedged view noted. Still to define: a prompt or an on-demand button. See Notes 2026-10-01 · AA meeting with Greg #22 #60.
