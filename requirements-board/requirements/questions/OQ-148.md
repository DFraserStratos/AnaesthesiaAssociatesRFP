The system keeps no record of surgeon groups. A group that holds Contracts and is billed for them is a [Party](US-04.1.5), and the surgeons who work for it are the ones linked on its Contracts. Each [surgeon](US-13.6.2) also holds their practice name.

Does AA's office need any other list of which surgeons practise together, for example to find every surgeon of a group, beyond the practice name and the surgeons linked on a group's Contracts?

What hangs on it: whether surgeon groups stay out of the system, as they are now, or come back as a list the office keeps.
