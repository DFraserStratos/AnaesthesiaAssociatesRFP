OQ-58's answer raises the prepayment invoice "only if the patient is the billable party"; in the meeting Donald said "we say patient as a generic term", meaning the person paying for the patient (for example a guardian). Does a prepayment apply when the billable party is a guardian, and when it is an insurer or hospital?

Recommendation: any person paying for the patient, never an organisation.

## Answer

That's right, it's any person paying for the patient, never an organisation.

The meeting adds: Greg: "prepayments are always patient-direct... there's no hospital involved in this process." (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #14)
