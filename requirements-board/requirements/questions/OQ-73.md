Who can be asked to prepay? OQ-58 raises a prepayment invoice only when the patient pays, and "patient" there is a generic term for the person paying for the patient. Does a prepayment apply when that is a parent or guardian, and when the one billed is an organisation such as an insurer or hospital?

What hangs on the answer: who receives a [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1), and when [a Booking needs prepayment](US-06.2.1).

Recommendation: any person paying for the patient, never an organisation.

## Answer

The one who prepays is always the payer on the Booking: the patient, or the parent, guardian or other person named in their place, never an organisation. A prepaid Contract always bills the payer. Its holder can be any Party, such as an anaesthetist with their own price list, but it never bills its holder.

To confirm with Ben, Vanessa or Greg: that no Contract that bills its holder is ever needed as a prepaid Contract (the [contract model proposal](AR-58#assumptions), section 11.2, point 2). If one is, reopen this question.

Our reading of Donald's decisions, the proposal's assumption A2, "A prepaid Contract must bill the payer": each Contract says whether it bills its holder or the payer (D2); a prepaid Contract "appears only in the Prepaid flow" (D4); and Prepaid is the payment type under Patient, where Contracts that bill the payer go (D7), so a holder-billed prepaid Contract is one "which D7 could never offer". Any holder's Contract can still be prepaid, but an organisation, such as Greg's DHB, is never the one billed for it. See "Notes 2026-11-05 · Donald contract model decisions #2 #4 #7 #17 #20"
