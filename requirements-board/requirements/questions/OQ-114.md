**Already settled:** the selected Contract decides who is billed: the holder's billable party when the holder pays AA itself (its "is billed" setting), otherwise the payer named on the Booking ([whether a holder is billed](US-04.1.5)).

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg:**

1. **What does the insurer's "accepts direct claims" flag do now?** Either (a) it is the same thing as the holder's "is billed" setting, so it is dropped; (b) it is a default that pre-fills "is billed" on a new insurer Contract; or (c) it is information only, for the office.

The insurer feature and its insurer master list, which held this flag, were retired on 28 October (see the update below); [contract holders](US-04.1.5) now carry how invoices are delivered.

Update 2026-10-28: moving away from an insurer flag. Greg, reading the insurer master list story (since retired): insurers are "just an instance of contract holders"; "I would rather see all of this headed as contract holder." What is needed instead is "a flag on billable, on contract holder or billable party... which says whether it generates an email invoice or whether it generates some other output." So the "accepts direct claims" flag gives way to an output flag on the holder or billable party. Still open: what the other outputs are, since how a direct claim reaches an insurer (an email into a portal, or an API) is not known. Donald moved that story back to Proposed, and said most of the insurer feature could go: "We don't have a master insurance list." See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #3 #40"
