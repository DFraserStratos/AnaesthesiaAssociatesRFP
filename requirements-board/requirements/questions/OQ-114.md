**Already settled:** the selected Contract decides who is billed: its holder, when the Contract is set to bill its holder, otherwise the payer named on the Booking. (Reworded 5 November to the flat Contract model, where "bills its holder or the payer" is a setting on each Contract rather than an "is billed" setting on the holder; see the [Party](US-04.1.5) record.)

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg:**

1. **What does the insurer's "accepts direct claims" flag do now?** Either (a) it is the same thing as the holder's "is billed" setting, so it is dropped; (b) it is a default that pre-fills "is billed" on a new insurer Contract; or (c) it is information only, for the office.

The insurer feature and its insurer master list, which held this flag, were retired on 28 October (see the 2026-10-28 update below); [Parties](US-04.1.5) now carry how invoices are delivered.

Update 2026-10-23: nib is the only insurer AA bills directly (Donald; Greg: "Yes."). Its invoices go to a portal (Greg: "one instance of a contract holder that we want to send stuff to a portal"); whether by email into it or an integration is not known (Greg: "I don't know how the portal works"; still unknown on 28 October). Greg first floated delivery as a property of the billable party: "Is that a characteristic of the bill[able party]?", which the 28 October update takes further. See "Notes 2026-10-23 · AA meeting with Greg #10"

Update 2026-10-28: moving away from an insurer flag. Greg, reading the insurer master list story (since retired): insurers are "just an instance of contract holders"; "I would rather see all of this headed as contract holder." What is needed instead is "a flag on billable, on contract holder or billable party... which says whether it generates an email invoice or whether it generates some other output." So the "accepts direct claims" flag gives way to an output flag on the holder or billable party. Still open: what the other outputs are, since how a direct claim reaches an insurer (an email into a portal, or an API) is not known. Donald moved that story back to Proposed, and said most of the insurer feature could go: "We don't have a master insurance list." See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #3 #40"

Update 2026-11-05 (Donald, for the build; not yet confirmed by AA or Greg): partly answered. How invoices are delivered is held on the [Party](US-04.1.5), the shared record for every holder. Donald: "The holder is a shared Party (a hospital, insurer or funder, surgeon or surgeon group, anaesthetist, or AA) holding the billing name and details and how invoices are delivered. Each Contract says whether it bills its holder or the payer on the Booking." So whether a holder is billed is the Contract's own setting, insurers are Parties like any other, and the proposal drops the insurer's "accepts direct claims" flag (the [contract model proposal](AR-58), section 2.7), which settles question 1 as (a) in effect; dropping the flag is the proposal's model detail, not one of Donald's decisions. Our reading, not Donald's words: delivery is by email or post for now (assumption A32), and a Party's billing name and delivery method are required only once it holds a Contract that bills it (A43). Still open: what the other outputs are, such as nib's portal or an insurer's API, which waits on knowing how a direct claim reaches an insurer. See "Notes 2026-11-05 · Donald contract model decisions #2 #17"
