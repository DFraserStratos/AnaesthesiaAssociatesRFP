What ways can a [Party](US-04.1.5) have its invoices delivered, besides email and post? The case we know of is nib, the only insurer AA bills directly: its invoices go into a portal, and nobody yet knows whether a claim reaches that portal by email or through an integration (an API).

The answer decides the options for a Party's delivery method and whether the Billing/Invoice Engine needs anything beyond producing an emailed or posted invoice, such as a claim file or an insurer integration. Until it is answered, delivery is by email or post.

**Who to ask:** Greg, or nib through AA, about how nib's portal receives claims.

**Already settled:**

- How invoices are delivered is held on the Party, the shared record for every Contract holder. Insurers are Parties like any other; there is no insurer master list.
- Each Contract says whether it bills its holder or the Procedure's payer.
- The insurer's old "accepts direct claims" flag is dropped: whether a holder is billed is the Contract's own setting.

**Background:**

- Greg, 23 October: nib is "one instance of a contract holder that we want to send stuff to a portal"; "I don't know how the portal works". Still unknown on 28 October. See "Notes 2026-10-23 · AA meeting with Greg #10"
- Greg, 28 October: insurers are "just an instance of contract holders"; what is needed is "a flag on billable, on contract holder or billable party... which says whether it generates an email invoice or whether it generates some other output." See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #3 #40"
- Donald, 5 November (for the build; not yet confirmed by AA or Greg): "The holder is a shared Party ... holding the billing name and details and how invoices are delivered. Each Contract says whether it bills its holder or the payer on the Booking." Dropping the flag is the [contract model proposal](AR-58)'s model detail (section 2.7), not one of Donald's decisions. Our readings: delivery is by email or post for now (A32), and a Party's billing name and delivery method are required only once it holds a Contract that bills it (A43). See "Notes 2026-11-05 · Donald contract model decisions #2 #17"
