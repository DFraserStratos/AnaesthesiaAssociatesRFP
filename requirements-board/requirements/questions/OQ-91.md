The directors answered that each anaesthetist "will create and manage their own fixed price contracts" (first-party Contracts). On 7 October Greg said the office should stay in control of Contracts in version one, with anaesthetists lodging their prices with the office like any other holder, and the client meeting room (Ben, Vanessa, Greg) said the office should manage the [prepayment amounts](US-06.2.2) and an anaesthetist should "just send an email". Donald also mentioned an approval step by the admin team without saying what it approves.

Who [creates and edits these Contracts](US-04.1.2) in the first release, and is there an approval step?

Recommendation: The office maintains first-party Contracts in version one; each anaesthetist can see their own price list read-only in the app and emails changes. Anaesthetist self-service editing is later work. No separate approval step beyond the office being the editor.

## Answer

The office (the admin team) creates and maintains every first-party Contract, in version one and generally. Donald, 8 October: "First party contracts will always be created by the administrative team ... And it is the anesthetists who would supply those price lists to the admin team to set up within the system."

Each anaesthetist owns their prices: they decide them and supply their price list, and the office enters it as the anaesthetist's own [first-party Contract](US-04.2.14), and enters any later changes the same way. This is how the directors' "anaesthetist will create and manage their own fixed price contracts" is read: the anaesthetist decides the prices, the office keeps the Contract. Anaesthetists do not create or edit Contracts in the app. The decision names no separate approval step; the office is the only editor. (See Typed decision 2026-10-08 · Donald (product owner))
