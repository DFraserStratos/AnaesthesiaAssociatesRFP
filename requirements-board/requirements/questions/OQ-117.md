**Still to answer, for AA:**

1. **Does AA have fixed-price Contracts that allow a price override?** Any Contract can be flagged to allow a price override or not. Greg: "I don't know for sure" whether such Contracts exist. What hangs on it: whether the system needs anything for a fixed price that is changed, and whether [the office's price change](US-05.4.2) can still be called "the only way to change a fixed price" (Greg: "it's not universally true").

Raised on 28 October. Donald asked for this to be investigated: a fixed-price Contract that is allowed a price override, where the override means more or less is charged, "We want to investigate whether or not this is a use case that impacts AA." He first framed it with the system automatically raising an invoice for the difference.

Update 2026-10-28: partly answered later the same day.

- Never on a prepaid Contract: prepaid Contracts are not editable and cannot allow a price override; a rare difference is a second invoice or credit raised by hand (OQ-96).
- Outside prepayment the case is simple. Donald: "it's not a prepayment and somebody decides to override the price... the thing that's on the invoice is just the new price that was set." Nothing extra is invoiced or credited.
- Where it may arise, Greg: an anaesthetist's own price list holds "fixed prices, but they're editable. So when he does his timesheet, he can change it"; a "lecitus" (as heard; perhaps a locum, an uncertain reading) "might have a fixed price list" but "can change" it; a surgeon's fixed price list may carry "a proviso that says... if we end up spending hours more on this, then you can charge more".
- Still open: whether such Contracts exist at AA.

See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #1 #2 #21 #75"

## Answer

If something is a fixed fee, it doesn't allow a price override.

Update 2026-10-29: so the anaesthetist's price override is never allowed on a fixed fee, whatever the Contract's flag, so the system needs nothing for an anaesthetist's change to a fixed price. The flag still governs the price, rate and discount on Procedures not priced at a fixed fee. This does not touch [the office's price change](US-05.4.2), which can still change any price, a fixed fee included.

Update 2026-10-29: Donald confirmed that "a fixed fee doesn't allow a price override" covers every fixed price, the anaesthetist's [own price list](US-04.2.14) included. On a Procedure priced from their own list, to charge differently the anaesthetist switches Contract or asks the office; the office can still change any price. This overtakes Greg's 28 October reading that an own price list holds "fixed prices, but they're editable... when he does his timesheet, he can change it".
