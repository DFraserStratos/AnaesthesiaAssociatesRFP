An anaesthetist [made inactive](US-12.1.6) can no longer sign in after their last day. Payments for work they did before that day can still arrive afterwards, and the remittances and invoices for that work are normally seen in the anaesthetist apps.

How does an anaesthetist who has left see those remittances and invoices: by email from the office, by a read-only sign-in kept for a while, or some other way?

What hangs on it: whether sign-in stops entirely on the last day, and what the office sends a departed anaesthetist when a payment for their work arrives.
