A Booking can mix Procedures priced from the RVG with Procedures on a fixed or open price (for example a surgeon's fixed-fee facelift with an extra procedure priced from the RVG). The [multi-procedure rule](US-05.3.1) puts base units only on the [primary Procedure](US-03.2.1). When the primary is on a fixed or open price, its base units are not billed, so the anaesthetic's base units are billed nowhere.

The choices:

1. **Keep the rule as it is.** Base units stay on the primary only. The RVG-priced Procedures carry time and modifier units only, and the fixed price is read as covering the anaesthetic's base.
2. **The first RVG-priced Procedure takes the base units** when the primary is on a fixed or open price.
3. **Mark a different Procedure as primary.** Whoever completes the Booking marks the RVG-priced one as primary, and the rule is unchanged.

Raised by Donald from the contract model rework of 5 November (section 11.2, point 5, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58)). Until it is answered, the prototype keeps option 1: base units go only on the marked primary, so on such a Booking none are billed.

The meeting of 5 November did not discuss where the base units go, though Ben asked for an anaesthetist's RVG detail to be recorded under his fixed price, the case this question turns on.
