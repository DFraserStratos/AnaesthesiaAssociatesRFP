A patient prepays on Monday for a Thursday procedure. On Tuesday the office loads a new version of the Contract with a new fixed price, effective before Thursday. Under the versioning rules, the Procedure would be relinked to the new version's line and priced from it ([effective dates](US-04.2.10)), which changes a price the patient has already paid.

Greg: "There needs to be a way to not alter the price of a prepay arrangement... when you update the schedule it will point to the new price. So you don't want it to do that if it's prepay." He also said the price "should really be for the price that's been paid, even if the anaesthetist has changed their price", but "we might want to check this with the anaesthetists". Ben has said fixed prices are "based on what the price is on the day [of] the procedure". The v6 technical design says "The management of prepay and surgeon fixed price contracts is not fully resolved in this scenario".

Conditions to put to Ben and Vanessa (Donald asked for these to be fronted up):

1. The anaesthetist changes their own prepay price list after a patient has prepaid.
2. A third party's price list changes a fixed price, for example a surgeon's rooms' arrangement or a plastic surgery price list, after the patient has prepaid or been quoted.
3. Which date governs: the prepayment date, the booking date or the procedure date.
4. Whether the answer differs for a prepaid procedure and for a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement that is not prepaid.

Recommendation: a prepaid Procedure keeps the price that was prepaid; a new Contract version does not relink it, and the office is shown it on the version's review dashboard. Any difference follows whatever OQ-96 settles for a prepaid procedure.
