**Still to answer, for Ben and Vanessa:**

1. **When a fixed price changes after the patient has prepaid or been quoted, does the Procedure keep the old price or move to the new one?** Under the current versioning rules it moves to the new Contract version's price. Greg wants a prepaid price left alone but would check with the anaesthetists; Ben has said fixed prices follow the price on the day of the procedure.

Conditions to put to Ben and Vanessa (Donald asked for these to be fronted up):

1. The anaesthetist changes their own prepay price list after a patient has prepaid.
2. A third party's price list changes a fixed price, for example a surgeon's rooms' arrangement or a plastic surgery price list, after the patient has prepaid or been quoted.
3. Which date governs: the prepayment date, the booking date or the procedure date.
4. Whether the answer differs for a prepaid procedure and for a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement that is not prepaid.

Recommendation: a prepaid Procedure keeps the price that was prepaid; a new Contract version does not relink it, and the office is shown it on the version's review dashboard. Any difference follows whatever OQ-96 settles for a prepaid procedure.

Example: a patient prepays on Monday for a Thursday procedure. On Tuesday the office loads a new version of the Contract with a new fixed price, effective before Thursday. Under the versioning rules, the Procedure would be relinked to the new version's line and priced from it ([effective dates](US-04.2.10)), which changes a price the patient has already paid.

Greg: "There needs to be a way to not alter the price of a prepay arrangement... when you update the schedule it will point to the new price. So you don't want it to do that if it's prepay." He also said the price "should really be for the price that's been paid, even if the anaesthetist has changed their price", but "we might want to check this with the anaesthetists". Ben has said fixed prices are "based on what the price is on the day [of] the procedure". The v6 technical design says "The management of prepay and surgeon fixed price contracts is not fully resolved in this scenario".

Update 2026-10-20 (Donald): keep this open for Vanessa to confirm. Until then the build assumes the recommendation: a prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice, and a new Contract version does not relink it.

Update 2026-10-27: settled for a prepaid Procedure, as recommended; the rest is still open. Donald's example: a patient prepays on Monday, on Tuesday "all the anaesthetists and AA resubmit all the new fixed prices they want", and the procedure is on Wednesday. Ben: "You would honour the money that's been prepaid, because that's the quote that the patient's been given." This covers condition 1 (and a change by AA), and for a prepaid Procedure answers condition 3: the prepaid quote governs. Not raised separately: a third party's price list (condition 2). Not discussed: a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement that is not prepaid (condition 4). Ben did not comment on Donald's restatement that a non-prepaid fixed price is "priced on the day that the procedure is actually done". Vanessa, whom Donald wanted to confirm this, was not asked. ## Answer

Settled (Donald, 29 October, on Ben's 27 October answer). A prepaid Procedure keeps the price that was prepaid, whatever changes the price list before the procedure: the anaesthetist's own prepay list, AA, or a third party's list. A new Contract version does not relink it, and the office is shown it on the version's review dashboard. The prepaid quote governs, not the booking or procedure date. Ben: "You would honour the money that's been prepaid, because that's the quote that the patient's been given." A fixed price that is not prepaid is priced on the day the procedure is done, as Ben has said. Any difference on a prepaid Procedure is handled by hand (OQ-96). A Contract changed away from prepaid after the prepayment is a separate case (OQ-119). See "Notes 2026-10-27 · AA meeting with Ben and Vanessa #1".
