Until now a Contract could name references its holder needs on its invoices, such as an insurer member number or a claim reference, for the office to supply ([Contract pricing terms](US-04.2.2); Required booking inputs, US-04.2.7, was retired on 7 October). Donald's Contract settings (D4) have no such setting, so for now the Booking keeps only the [insurer approval number](US-11.4.3) (our reading, AR-58 A21).

Should holder-required booking inputs (a member number, a claim reference, a purchase order) be dropped for good, or added later as a Contract setting that makes the Booking ask for them?

What hangs on it: whether a holder that needs such a reference on its invoice can be billed without a manual note.

1. **Drop them for good.** The Booking keeps the insurer approval number only.
2. **Add a Contract setting later** that names the references the Booking must collect, printed on the invoice.

No recommendation from the evidence. Raised from section 11.2, point 6, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58).
