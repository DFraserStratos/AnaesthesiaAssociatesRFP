Does any hospital, insurer or other Contract holder need a number on AA's invoice that changes with each Booking, such as a claim reference or a purchase order number, other than the insurer approval number?

Two references can reach a holder's invoice today. The Contract's [holder reference code](US-04.2.18) is the holder's own code for the item, the same on every Booking. The Booking holds the [insurer approval number](US-11.4.3). A Contract cannot ask the Booking for anything else.

What hangs on it: whether a holder that needs such a number can be billed without a manual note on the invoice, and whether the Booking has to ask for references that depend on its Contract.

1. **No other number.** The Booking keeps the insurer approval number only. Hospitals sometimes ask for it on the invoice, so it could also be printed on the invoice to the holder when the Booking holds one.
2. **Some holders need one.** A Contract names the references its holder needs. A Booking on that Contract asks for them, and they are printed on the holder's invoice. Name the holders and the numbers.

The insurer member number is not one of these numbers. Greg ruled it out on 23 October: the holder's reference is the holder reference code, and the patient's insurance details stay on the Booking. Raised from section 11.2, point 6, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58). The proposal assumes no other number for now (A21).

## Answer

No other number. A Contract does not ask the Booking for references of its own, and the Booking holds the [insurer approval number](US-11.4.3) only. The holder's own code for the item is the Contract's [holder reference code](US-04.2.18). If a holder turns up that needs a per-Booking number such as a claim reference or a purchase order, reopen this question: a Contract setting can be added then without changing anything built.
