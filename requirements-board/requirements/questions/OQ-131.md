How does the office keep a Contract's prices and dates current, day to day? The catalogue holds a Contract as a series of [dated versions](US-04.2.10), each a header with optional lines. The realistic alternatives are:

1. **Whole-Contract dated versions**, as the catalogue has it now.
2. **A date on each line**, so one price can change without a new version of the whole Contract.
3. **Editing in place**, with an audit trail of what changed and when.

What hangs on it: the Contract record, the screens the office uses to keep Contracts up to date, and how a Procedure's price is found for its date. It does not change how a Contract is picked for a Procedure (Notes 2026-11-04 · Donald working decisions #9).

Left open on 23 October. Donald: "this is kind of a bigger conversation to be had with Vanessa and such"; "I certainly want to understand a little bit more about how it looks like day-to-day from their side." US-04.2.10 and US-04.2.14 were left open then, though they still read Agreed.

## Answer

Answered 5 November (Donald, for the build; not yet confirmed by AA or Greg). None of the three options as asked: there are no Contract versions and no lines. Each priced item is its own Contract (D1), edited in place with every change audited, and only a fixed price is dated, through its **price ranges**. Donald: "At least one per fixed-price Contract. Each has a start date, which may be in the past, and a price; it ends the day before the next one starts, so ranges never overlap or leave a gap. The range in force on the procedure date gives the price." The other settings carry no date: "Every change is audited. Changing a setting from a date means a new Contract." A Contract's rate or discount is undated too, so a change to it is an audited edit (D5).

So option 3, editing in place with an audit trail, holds for everything but a fixed price, and a fixed price changes by adding a price range from a date. Because the start date may be in the past, the office can enter a supplier's new price list late and backdate it. A Procedure's price follows the range in force on its procedure date, unless a person's edit, a prepayment invoice or AUTHORISED holds it (D9).

In the meeting, Vanessa on a new price list today: it "gets emailed to us and then we manually do it". Donald's expectation: "you could go into the system, find each one of these line items (breast reduction simple, breast reduction complex) and just update the prices... back in the settings." Vanessa: "That's what I'd love to do." Ben and Vanessa agreed to editing item by item; dates were not discussed, and the price ranges and one record per priced item are Donald's (D1, D6). A brand-new procedure "needs a new line" (Ben), which is now a new Contract of its own. Whether the office wants a list of the Procedures a backdated range affects is still open (section 11.2, point 1, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58)).
