How does the office keep a Contract's prices and dates current, day to day? The catalogue holds a Contract as a series of [dated versions](US-04.2.10), each a header with optional lines. The realistic alternatives are:

1. **Whole-Contract dated versions**, as the catalogue has it now.
2. **A date on each line**, so one price can change without a new version of the whole Contract.
3. **Editing in place**, with an audit trail of what changed and when.

What hangs on it: the Contract record, the screens the office uses to keep Contracts up to date, and how a Procedure's price is found for its date. It does not change how a Contract is picked for a Procedure (Notes 2026-11-04 · Donald working decisions #9).
