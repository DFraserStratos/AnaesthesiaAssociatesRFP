1. **Is the Contract schedule upload in the first release, or does it follow after?** The requirement exists, but the v6 MVP baseline is a developer-run import then office screens, with the upload used for ongoing maintenance. Donald and Nick hold that schedules change rarely and can be keyed by hand; Greg disagreed, but now calls the upload a proposal the developers may find too difficult or expensive.

Recommendation: First release: developer-run seed load plus office screens, with a second-person check done on screen; the schedule upload follows soon after, once the format is agreed with Vanessa.

Background: Greg asked for a requirement that the system supports uploading a Contract schedule, for Contracts only, so the office can transpose a holder's price list into one approved format and have a second person check it; [the requirement now exists](US-04.2.13). Donald and Nick hold that schedules change rarely and can be keyed by hand, and Donald questioned whether it is needed on day one; Greg disagreed. The draft design suggests a [developer-run import](US-13.4.3) then office screens as the MVP baseline.

See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #9.
