## Answer

Answered 5 November (Donald, for the build; not yet confirmed by AA or Greg): no. A Contract schedule upload is not in the first release; it is Future Work, and so is an export of Contracts. Donald's D10 puts "bulk upload and export" outside the contract model ("These are for the requirements update, not this model"); the [contract model proposal](AR-58) (sections 9.1, 9.3 and 10) reads them as Future Work and gives the reason: "Vanessa maintains prices by finding each Contract and editing it on screen. A developer seed loader remains (it is not a user feature)." So the first release keeps the [developer-run load at cutover](US-13.4.3) and the office's screens, with every change audited (D4). With no header, lines or versions there is nothing left to stage, so staging Contract changes for review and submitting them together goes with the upload.

The meeting, the same day: Vanessa, shown Donald's expectation of finding each item "back in the settings" and updating its price: "That's what I'd love to do." Ben and Vanessa did not ask for an upload. Donald reported Greg's wish to export Contracts, edit them in a spreadsheet and bring them back in: "Greg feels quite passionately about the idea... And I think some version of that is currently in scope." D10, later that day, takes it out of the contract model and the proposal reads it as Future Work, so the sources differ and Greg has not heard it. Vanessa's "a screen, like a spreadsheet" may be a wish for a grid on screen rather than a file; it was not explored. AA expects to clean up the procedure list itself and remove the items it does not use.

Not settled: Ben and Vanessa's wish for a safe copy of the contracts outside the system. Ben: "It's whether or not we have a source of truth that sits outside of your system as well... it needs to be kept somewhere safe so that we don't lose it." Vanessa: "I think we do need it." Ben: "something in your system and something outside of your system... in case, I don't know, a cyber attack and your whole thing goes down." Donald pushed back: the hosting services "do their own backups", and spreadsheet copies are "creating yourself another job that may inevitably fail in the future... your backup is always just going to be a snapshot in time." Ben: "As long as there's a safe, secure source of truth that sits somewhere." Ben softened but did not drop the wish. Donald, 5 November: the wish is covered by the requirement to export the data to a spreadsheet, so it raises no question of its own; it is a backup and continuity matter more than an upload.
