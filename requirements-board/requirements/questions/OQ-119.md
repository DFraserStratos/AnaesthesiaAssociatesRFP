**Still to answer, for Vanessa and Ben:**

1. **If a prepaid Procedure's Contract is changed to one not flagged as prepaid after the prepayment invoice is sent or paid, what happens?** For example, the job becomes an insurance job. What hangs on it: what the [re-check when the Booking changes](US-06.3.5) does with the prepayment invoice, and whether it follows the [refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2).

What was said on 28 October:

- Paid: Donald proposed a full refund, then an invoice after the procedure under the new Contract. Greg preferred a refund to a credit: "Theoretically you could issue them a credit, but it just creates more work, I think, than just doing a refund and just starting again. You can raise it with Ben." Not agreed.
- Sent but unpaid: not settled. Donald asked whether the invoice is voided or a credit note raised, and what happens if the patient pays it anyway; Greg: "If you've sent them an invoice, you can't actually stop them sending you money."

Greg, 28 October, would rather see the Booking cancelled and rebooked, and has never seen a prepayment converted to an insurance job. Raised alongside OQ-104, but not part of it.

See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #56"

## Answer

Answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Vanessa or Ben). Donald: "prepaids are assumed to be refunded." When a prepaid Procedure's Contract is changed to one not flagged as prepaid after the prepayment invoice is sent or paid, the change is handled like a cancellation of the prepayment: a paid prepayment is refunded in full, and the Procedure is invoiced under its new Contract after the procedure. This is Donald's 28 October proposal, which Greg preferred to a credit. The sent-but-unpaid case was not stated separately and is read as following the [refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2).
