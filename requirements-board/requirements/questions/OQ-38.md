From [the 29 September meeting transcript](AR-03#two-modifiers), on the prepayment estimate:

> **Greg:** What are the modifiers?
>
> **Vanessa:** We always add two.
>
> **Donald:** Plus two modifier units?
>
> **Vanessa:** Two modifiers. No matter what, we always add two, just because something can go wrong.
>
> **Greg:** So it's base plus time plus two.
>
> **Vanessa:** Yep. Anything can happen.

AA always adds two modifiers to a prepayment estimate, no matter what, "because something can go wrong". Is this literally two modifier units added to [the estimate](US-06.2.4) only, as a contingency buffer (the final invoice then uses the modifiers actually recorded), or two specific modifier codes recorded on the Procedure, or does it also feed the final calculation? Is it always two, or should it vary by anaesthetist or Procedure?

Recommendation: an estimate-only buffer of two units, held as a setting, shown in the estimate and not recorded as modifiers on the Procedure.

## Answer

The question no longer arises: the prepayment is no longer an estimate, so no contingency modifier units are added. Donald, at the 7 October client meeting: "What Nick, Ron and Ben said last night is that, going forward, if an anaesthetist wants a procedure to be prepaid, they need to define a fixed prepaid amount that is selected and used for that prepayment, rather than an estimate based on BTM." The room: "No - every anaesthetist always has a defined amount: this is how much the prepayment is. I think that's reasonable." Donald: "So that's question 38."

The directors' answers agree: the amount comes from the anaesthetist's own fixed-price (first-party) contract, and the system adds no modifiers on its own, other than the locked age modifier and modifiers already included in the base units. Where the amounts are kept and who maintains them is on OQ-04. The estimate story the question names (US-06.2.4) is retired; the live rule is [set the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), with [no automatic invoice or credit after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1). (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #3 #4", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #3 #14 #17 #18")
