**Already settled:** the Contract defined rate is an override unit rate in place of the anaesthetist's, for the whole Procedure, not a separate billing line; a fixed discount is a contract-level percentage discount.  The anaesthetist adds their own price or discount only where the Contract lets them change its pricing.

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg:**

1. **Is the optional time band dropped from [Contract lines](US-04.2.4)?** The story no longer lists one, and neither the v6 contract line nor the draft technical design has one, but dropping it has not been confirmed.

Recommendation: keep Contract lines able to carry an optional time band, with the matched line's fee as the price.

## History

Original recommendation: treat the Contract defined rate as the agreed contract rate per unit, and keep schedule lines able to carry time bands and add-ons, with the matched line's fee as the price.

On 2026-10-02 two pricing stories were reworded:

- The Contract defined rate (US-05.2.6) is now a unit rate the Contract specifies, an alternative to the anaesthetist's own unit value, not an hourly rate x time.
- Fixed fee pricing (US-05.2.5) now takes the fixed fee as the whole price, not "the matched schedule line... including any time band or add-on".

The original three questions:

1. US-05.2.1 and US-04.2.2 already have a Contract basis of RVG units x an agreed contract rate per unit (or a discount). Is the Contract defined rate that same basis (so the two collapse into one), or something different?
2. The old other-billing-lines story (since retired and merged into events, FT-03.7; it was retitled "UNIT X RATE") still captured it as a billing line outside the BTM calculation. Is the Contract defined rate a pricing basis for the whole Procedure, or a separate billing line?
3. Fixed fee schedule lines (US-04.2.4) still carry time bands, add-on flags and quantity rules. Do these still pick or add to the fixed fee, or are they dropped?

Update 2026-10-07: questions 1 and 2 answered in effect, question 3 partly.

- Questions 1 and 2: the directors said "Some contracts DO have a fixed rate contract." and "Some contracts do have a fixed discount contract. (Price override)". Donald: the fixed rate "will obviously still use the RVG BTM units, but it would be multiplying it by a fixed rate as opposed to the anaesthetist rate"; the fixed discount is "a pre-applied price override that the anaesthetist could not edit", for example 10%. So the Contract defined rate is a unit rate in place of the anaesthetist's, for the whole Procedure, not a separate billing line. On 7 October the custom unit rate and the pre-applied discount became two settings in US-04.2.2.
- Question 3: of the schedule line extras, the add-on flag and the quantity rule were dropped (Greg: "No"), and the optional RVG mapping went because every line has an RVG code. "An optional time band" is still in the text; whether it was meant to go too is not clear. Greg: schedule lines are generic Contract lines, and the story mixes Contract and Contract line. The draft technical design has no time band, add-on or quantity rule on a contract line.

Still open: whether the time band stays, and whether the anaesthetist can add a discount or price of their own on top of a fixed discount contract. (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #6 #11", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #5 #28 #30 #31", "Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #24 #38")

Update 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16: whether the anaesthetist can add their own discount is answered; the time band is not.

- Rate and discount are live values on a Contract line ("An override unit rate in place of the anaesthetist's default rate"; "A contract-level percentage discount"), no longer dormant. See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #3 #26.
- Whether the anaesthetist can add a price or discount of their own on a fixed discount Contract follows from one setting per Contract, whether it lets the anaesthetist change its pricing: only where it does. See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #3 #27. Hospital fixed-price Contracts take no anaesthetist discount at all (Ben, via Greg: "if you don't want the rate, don't do the procedure"). See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #23.
- A standard RVG price less a fixed discount (a hospital at "standard RVG pricing minus 20%", a DHB Contract at a discount) is just another Contract line. See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #36.
- The v6 contract line has no time band, add-on or quantity rule.
- A line's RVG code is now an optional default, and fixed-price lines need none, which corrects the 7 October note that every line has an RVG code. See Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #40.

Still open: confirming the time band is dropped from [Contract lines](US-04.2.4).

## Answer

As far as we understand, contracts / contract line don't have any concept of a "TIME BAND" Time is only an optional recording from the anaesthetist.

The meeting adds: the rest of the "already settled" text was read and Donald found it true, though Greg found the rate wording "a bit scary" and the definition of fixed "a little rigid, but we can worry about that another day"; the add-on flag and quantity rule line was deleted because both were dropped. Greg's remaining doubt, whether the anaesthetist may change a price, rate or discount a Contract specifies, is settled as a flag on each Contract set by AA, not a global rule (Greg: "The flag on the contract might be the best way because then it's not global... I'm happy now"). See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #9 #20".
