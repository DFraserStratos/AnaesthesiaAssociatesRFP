When the office [replaces a Contract from a date](US-04.1.8), a replacement priced at a rate or a discount is offered from the day it is saved, because only a fixed price's ranges carry dates. The old Contract stays offered until the office disables it, so between the two days both are offered on the same Lists, and a to-do on the date reminds the office to disable the old one.

Should a Contract be able to be disabled from a future date instead, so the old rate stops being offered on the day of the change?

What hangs on it: whether a Contract carries a date of its own (today it has none: it is Active or Disabled), and whether a Procedure booked before the change can be put on the new rate by mistake.
