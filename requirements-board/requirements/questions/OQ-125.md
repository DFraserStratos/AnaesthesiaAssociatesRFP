Which event releases a prepayment from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) to the anaesthetist who did the procedure? The catalogue uses the office authorising the List; Greg named the booking being "confirmed and submitted". For Donald and Greg.

What hangs on the answer: when the prepayment moves out of the holding account, and so [what happens after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1).

**Already settled:** the money is released once the procedure is done (Donald and Greg, 28 October).

**Background:** the trigger was first held at Verify, in a Settlement after the procedure feature that has since been retired. On 28 October Greg and Donald agreed the release follows the finished procedure, without restating the event that triggers it. See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #51 #100"
