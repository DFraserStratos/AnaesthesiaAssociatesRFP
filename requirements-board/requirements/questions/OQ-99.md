At the client meeting AA asked that, when the office cannot confidently match the room's text to a procedure or [RVG code](US-03.3.1), it can leave it blank for the anaesthetist, as long as the billable party and billing approach are settled first ([booking setup](US-04.3.4)). Greg wants every booked procedure assigned to a procedure, even on a fixed price, and the [Contract list](US-04.3.2) is narrowed by the picked procedure.

With the procedure blank, how is the Contract chosen (holder first, or No contract (RVG) only), and what must be filled in before the anaesthetist can [mark the Booking complete](US-03.6.1)?

Recommendation: Allow the procedure to be blank at setup, with the Contract chosen by holder (or No contract (RVG)); require a procedure on every Procedure before the anaesthetist can mark the Booking complete, and show unmatched Bookings to the office.

Update 2026-10-08: the picker's RVG codes tab bears on this. The office can now pick an RVG code without a specific procedure; with No contract (RVG) the Procedure takes the group's general procedure, and with a Contract line it takes that line's procedure ([Contract list from an RVG code](US-04.3.2)). Whether that replaces leaving the procedure blank is not decided. (See "Notes 2026-10-08 · Procedure picker and source text #4 #5")

## Answer

There is no procedure to match any more (the procedure master goes, OQ-88). At setup the office's job is to identify who pays: it picks the contract holder, then the Contract, then the line where the Contract has lines (Greg: "the primary job of the office is to identify the billable party... this has got nothing to do with the RVG code"). The RVG code is optional for the office: "if you can find it, fine; if you don't know, don't worry". The v6 design lets the office leave the line or RVG entry blank "for the anaesthetist to assign at a later time". See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #19 #32; Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #39; Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #7 #31.

On completing the Booking, the RVG code is optional (good practice on an RVG Contract, since it sets the base units), time recording depends on the Contract (not needed on a fixed price), and the holder's reference is not the anaesthetist's job. See Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #57.

That each Procedure needs its Contract before the anaesthetist marks the Booking complete is the catalogue's standing rule ([booking setup](US-04.3.4)), not a point the meetings made. The v6 design says the contract line "Must be not null for billing" and leaves open what must be filled in before completion and whether the contract itself may be blank; it is still to be confirmed with US-03.6.1, which is at Verify. See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #7.

Where the anaesthetist cannot complete a Procedure, the design asks for a way to forward it to the office to resolve (Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #37).
