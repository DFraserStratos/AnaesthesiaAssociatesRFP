The catalogue recommends one room per surgeon, and that was never settled. Ben's specialist list puts 4 surgeons at two practices.

Can a surgeon belong to more than one room? If yes, which room's contacts apply to a given Booking? What hangs on it: the room record, the surgeon profile, and who the office emails about a Booking.

## Answer

Answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Vanessa). Donald: "Our system doesn't need to assume a surgeon at one room, we can be more flexible." A surgeon can belong to more than one surgeons' room. Which room's contacts apply to a given Booking was not answered; it is carried in OQ-127. See "Notes 2026-11-04 · Donald working decisions #4"

18 November (Donald): superseded. There is no surgeons' room record for now, so a surgeon is not attached to rooms at all. A surgeon who works from two practices holds an office contact for each on their profile, and the practice name is plain text there. See OQ-127, answers 1, 2 and 5.
