**Proposed so far (Verify):** for an automated reschedule from an integration, a clash means the change is accepted and the List becomes a Draft List, and a single Booking can move onto the same surgeon's List in the new session, whoever its anaesthetist.

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg (Future Work, not needed for MVP):**

1. **Where does a single Booking go when it moves to a session where the surgeon has no List?** Not discussed; the only stated condition for an automatic move is an existing List for the same surgeon.
2. **What happens when a whole List moves into a session where the same surgeon already has a List?** The Lists do not merge. Greg: "If you're moving the list, I think it goes to draft because there are some ugly combinations there."
3. **How is a move recognised at all?** Surgeons send snapshots of their List rather than individual changes, so the system has to tell a reschedule from a cancellation plus a new Booking.

Greg thought the rules "too hard... I think we're just like draft"; Donald: "I do think there's a set of rules that would allow it to happen automatically." This is Future Work, so it need not be settled before MVP.

## Answer

Greg's view, which Donald accepted ("I think we answered all of this"): a List does not move; it is defined by its day and session, and Bookings sit on it. Within a List, a change of procedure time is common and is an ordinary modification. When a Booking or a whole List moves to another day, the rooms in practice cancel and re-send: the Bookings disappear from the old List's snapshot and arrive again, as new Bookings, on the List of the new day, possibly reshuffled among several Lists.

So the system handles all three cases the simple way:

1. **A single Booking moving to a session where the surgeon has no List** (not discussed directly; follows from the rule) is a cancellation of the old Booking and a new Booking that arrives through the usual intake and matching for its new day.
2. **A whole List moving** is the same: its Bookings are cancelled, the old List is emptied and "goes back to be a slot" (whether that makes it a Draft List was left open), and the Bookings arrive again on the new day. Since the List itself never moves, the question of merging two Lists does not arise.
3. **A move is not recognised as a move.** The system detects Bookings that are no longer on a re-sent List snapshot and treats them as removed; the new ones arrive as new Bookings. The patient is the same (same NHI); the Booking is new.

Greg: "Our system is concerned, the bookings move, the list doesn't move." "We just delete the booking and the new ones just arrive because they might not move the list in toto." "We just delete it and wait for new news." Donald asked for the question to be updated. Greg raised the consequence: removals reach AA only as a Booking missing from the next PDF, "so we're going to have to do some sort of match and merge to detect removals."
