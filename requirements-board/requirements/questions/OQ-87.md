For an automated reschedule from an integration, two rules were proposed in the room (Verify): a clash means the change is accepted and the List becomes a Draft List, and a single Booking can move onto the same surgeon's List in the new session, whoever its anaesthetist. Three cases are not settled:

1. A single Booking moved to a session where the surgeon has no List.
2. A whole List moved into a session where the same surgeon already has a List. The Lists do not merge; Greg: "If you're moving the list, I think it goes to draft because there are some ugly combinations there."
3. How a move is recognised at all, given surgeons send snapshots of their List rather than individual changes: how does the system tell a reschedule from a cancellation plus a new Booking?

Greg thought the rules "too hard... I think we're just like draft"; Donald: "I do think there's a set of rules that would allow it to happen automatically." This is Future Work, so it need not be settled before MVP.
