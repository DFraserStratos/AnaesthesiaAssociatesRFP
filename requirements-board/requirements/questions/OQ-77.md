## Answer

Before the combined invoice is sent, a split is a normal admin edit. Until the office authorises the List and hands it to the Billing/Invoice Engine, the admin simply edits the Booking's Procedures (and their Contracts and prices) to split the work; no credit or rebill is needed. After the invoice has gone out, the split is the credit and rebill already settled (OQ-72): credit the original, raise one additional invoice per component, with the rebuilt lines equal to the credited invoice and the credit reversing the linked payable.

Greg: "the first process is it's gone out, so you roll it back and redo it. But of course, if it hasn't gone out, you just... the admin just edits it... So that should just say that's handled by a normal admin edit." Donald: "if it's not authorised and sent to the billing engine yet, you would just edit it." Donald asked for the question to be marked complete. See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #2".
