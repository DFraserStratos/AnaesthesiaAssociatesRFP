OQ-72 settled the split asked for after the combined invoice is sent: credit the original through the credit note option, then raise one additional invoice per component. Still open: (1) must the rebilled components come to the same amount as the credited invoice? Greg: it "must come to the same amount" (L715), but the catalogue says the components need not add up to the bundle price, because the bundle can carry a discount. (2) Greg also said the credit "should automatically include a reversal" (L715): does that mean the credit reverses the linked payable to the anaesthetist, as a full credit and rebill does? (3) what happens when a split is asked for before the combined invoice is sent? That case was not discussed.

Recommendation: (1) keep no forced total, since a bundle discount means the parts can differ; (2) yes, the credit reverses the linked payable, as in a full credit and rebill; (3) use the same credit and rebill once the combined invoice is raised, so there is one flow.

Update 2026-10-07: parts 1 and 2 answered, part 3 partly.

- Part 1, against the recommendation: the rebuilt lines must equal the credited invoice. Vanessa: "the rebuilt lines always have to equal what the original invoice was." The split amounts are not the old individual prices; Vanessa had told Donald "We would just figure out the right way to split it", so it "needs to be just a free form way".
- Part 2, yes, on Donald's word; nobody in the room responded to it before the next topic. Donald: "Greg also noted that the credit should automatically include the corresponding reversal of the linked payable to the anaesthetist; that makes sense. I think that answers question 77."
- Part 3 was not raised with AA. With Greg the same morning: "There's a good chance that this would get caught before it was invoiced, so you wouldn't have to raise the credit... What you actually want to do is override the fixed price and split it", though "It sort of contravenes [the] cannot change price law. But that's fine. Maybe we should just leave things open and they can adjust the price of the first procedure and add one or two more." Donald floated showing a pending invoice that can be cancelled and replaced with free-form invoices. Greg suggested checking with Vanessa whether an insurer's split is routine and predictable. Left for now as "just a generic invoice", to tackle later.

Still open: part 3, how a split asked for before the combined invoice is sent is handled. (See "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #3 #11", "Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #9 #37")
