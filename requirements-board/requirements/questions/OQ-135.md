[Disabling a Contract](US-04.1.2) hides it from the step rail. A Procedure already on it keeps it and bills as normal (our reading, AR-58 A14).

Should a Disabled Contract on a Procedure not yet authorised raise a flag at [office review](US-07.2.2), so the office can decide whether to move it?

What hangs on it: whether disabling a Contract AA no longer uses can quietly bill Bookings already set up on it.

1. **No flag.** The Procedure bills as normal.
2. **A review flag, never a block.** The office sees it at review and decides.

No recommendation from the evidence. Raised from section 11.2, point 4, of the [contract model proposal](AR-58).
