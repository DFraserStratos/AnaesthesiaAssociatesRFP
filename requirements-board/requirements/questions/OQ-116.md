**Already settled:** a prepaid Procedure's price is its issued prepayment invoice, and nothing more is calculated for it: no balance invoice or credit ([US-06.4.1](US-06.4.1)).

**Still to answer, for Donald and Greg:**

1. **On a Booking that mixes prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures, does the final invoice deduct the prepayment, or are the prepaid Procedures simply left off it?** If left off, [AR-24](AR-24)'s "remaining amount, less any prepayment" step and [US-08.2.2](US-08.2.2)'s "Net prepayments" come out, and AR-24 needs redrawing. If deducted, the step stays, but only for mixed Bookings.

Update 2026-10-28: points to leaving prepaid Procedures off. Donald: "prepayments are all or nothing. So there wouldn't be a situation where a line item was partially prepaid"; Greg: "there shouldn't be any remaining amount." Donald: on the final invoice the patient "wouldn't see a line that shows your prepayment... it doesn't exist anymore 'cause it's already been paid." Greg: in the normal case "there's no invoice issued to the customer patient because they've already paid and it goes through that holding account transfer process." A Booking mixing prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures was not named. See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #7"

## Answer

Prepaid Procedures are simply left off

Update 2026-10-29: so the final invoice deducts no prepayment. Prepaid Procedures are not on it, and a Booking that mixes prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures is invoiced for its not-prepaid Procedures only ([prepaid Procedures left off the final invoice](US-08.2.2)). AR-24 was redrawn on 29 October to match.
