Ben wants the name and email of each practice manager and PA, but does not hold them. In his list, most practices give one shared inbox, but at one ENT practice each surgeon has their own PA and inbox.

1. **Where does a contact belong?** To the room, to one surgeon, or either.
2. **Which address do the office's emails use?** Today a [booking update email](US-02.3.3) and a [missing NHI request](US-11.1.5) go to the room's contact email. Should they go to the surgeon's own PA where there is one?
3. **Where will the contacts come from?** Ben says "I don't have this information."
4. **A surgeon's own email.** In some shared practices a surgeon has an email of their own. Does it sit on the [surgeon profile](US-13.6.2)?
5. **A surgeon at more than one room.** A surgeon can now belong to more than one room (OQ-128, answered 4 November). When they do, which room's contacts apply to a given Booking?

## Answer

1. I think each surgeon should have a personal contact fields and an office contact fields. (Name, Email and phone.)

   18 November: no surgeons' room record for now. Contacts are held only on the surgeon's profile: one personal contact, and one or more office contacts. The form always shows one set of office contact fields; a plus button adds another set, and a minus button on any extra set removes it. The profile also holds the practice name as plain text, so Ben's list loads and the office can see which practice a surgeon works from.

2. I don't know at this stage. Likely the office contact.

   18 November: each List keeps where it came from, including the email address its PDF arrived from. The office's emails about a Booking go back to that address. Where the List has none (it came from the hospital feed or was made by hand), they go to the surgeon's first office contact, with any others offered in the draft for the office to switch to.

3. AA Admins will fill it in.

4. Yes.

5. 18 November: no longer arises. With no room record, there is no "which room" to choose. A surgeon who works from two practices holds an office contact for each (answer 1). The office's emails go back to the address the List came from, otherwise to the surgeon's office contact (answer 2). Never send to every office contact: the missing NHI request carries patient details. The rooms were never needed for the app to work: Lists arrive with their sender known, and the surgeon is identified by their HPI number, not their practice.
