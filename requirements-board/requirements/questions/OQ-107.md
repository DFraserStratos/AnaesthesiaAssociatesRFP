**Already settled:** each Contract says whether the anaesthetist may change its pricing: typically not on a hospital's fixed-price Contract or a surgeon's fixed fee (v6), and Ben has said no discounts on a hospital's fixed price ("if you don't want the rate, don't do the procedure"). Only the office changes a fixed price without changing the Contract.

**Still to answer, for Donald:**

1. **On a Contract that does not let them change the price, can the anaesthetist still change it at their own discretion?** Or must they change the Contract, or ask the office? On 16 October Donald and Greg seemed to agree "they do it at their own discretion", but it is not clear which they meant.

Recommendation: no. On such a Contract the anaesthetist changes the Contract (which may change who is billed), or asks the office, which can change any price.

On 16 October, reading FT-03.5, Donald summed up his model ("the price is always the price on the contract. And if the contract is a fixed price then nobody can change it"), then, after Greg's "Exactly": "And if they want to change it, they do. And if anybody complains, it'll inevitably become an admin team problem. So they do it at their own discretion." Greg: "Yeah, exactly." The transcript does not make clear whether this means the anaesthetist can change a fixed price, or that they change the Contract.

Update 2026-10-19: Greg asked the same thing ("if a contract specifies a price, a rate, or a discount... is the anaesthetist allowed to change it?"). Donald: "It can just be a setting that is applied by AA. So you in theory could have a price on a contract, and mark it as not being able to have a price override and therefore it sort of becomes fixed. Or... we trust you, anaesthetist, to do what you need to do." Greg: "The flag on the contract might be the best way because then it's not global... I'm happy now." This supports the recommendation: on a Contract that does not allow it, the anaesthetist cannot change the price. See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #20".

## Answer

No. Whether the anaesthetist may change a Contract's price, rate or discount is a flag on each Contract, set by AA, not a global rule. On a Contract whose flag does not allow it, the anaesthetist cannot change the price: they change the Contract (which may change who is billed) or ask the office. On a Contract that allows it, they may. (Donald confirmed this reading after the meeting, 2026-10-19.) See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #20".
