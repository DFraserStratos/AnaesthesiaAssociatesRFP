OQ-65's answer adds a shared notification pool to the Admin App, separate from the to-do list. The first source is an anaesthetist moving their own List. Not settled: (1) what else posts to it; (2) whether notices expire or are kept (Greg: "how do they expire? Just let them scroll"); (3) whether a notice can be marked actioned or unactioned (Donald), when by definition it needs no action; (4) how it sits beside the to-do list on the dashboard.

Recommendation: List moves only for now; keep every notice, newest first, with no expiry and no actioned state; revisit with Vanessa once it is in use.
