**Already settled:** the Admin App gets one notification pool shared by the whole admin team (not per user), separate from the to-do list, for things that happened but need no action (OQ-65; Vanessa agreed 2026-10-07). Its first and only confirmed source is an anaesthetist moving their own List.

**Still to answer, for Vanessa:**

## Answer

1. For now, Lists and Bookings will have a option to alert the office to review / action them with a note.

2. Never expire, must be marked as resolved manually, then are hidden.

3. Two states, Active or resolved. (tick box sorta thing)

4. This is a design question for claude to resolve for now.

The meeting adds: Greg: "It's a tick box thing... I need a way to make them go away, whether they're to-dos or notifications. I think we don't want notifications that all just magically disappear." Donald, on part 3: "I don't like the idea of one person reading a notification but not actioning it and then having it look like it's been read by somebody else. So I feel like it just needs to be active or resolved." The same tick applies to to-dos. See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #3".

Part 4, resolved by Claude as asked, for AA to check: the pool is a panel on the Admin App dashboard beside the to-do list, listing active notices newest first with a count of active ones. Ticking a notice resolves it for the whole team and hides it; who resolved it and when are kept, and a "show resolved" filter brings resolved notices back. A flag raised from a List or Booking carries its note and opens that List or Booking.
