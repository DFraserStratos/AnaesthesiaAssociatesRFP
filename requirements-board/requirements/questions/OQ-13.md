What format does each hospital's download arrive in, and what fields does it carry? For someone at Stratos Tech who understands the data; who to ask is not yet named.

1. **What format does each hospital send?** St George's and Southern Cross already feed the current system: its matching screen imports their files through an integration (OQ-109). The files' format is not known. Two hospitals on HL7 may not send the same shape (Greg: HL7 provides for that mapping), and PDFs must still be ingested even once HL7 or FHIR is in place.
2. **What fields does each download carry?** It is not comprehensive: the Contract and patient details sometimes come through and sometimes do not, so the theatre list is still needed for the insurer or Contract.

What hangs on the answer: the specification of the matching and creating screen that [hospital bookings are imported](US-02.1.1) through, [a later automatic sync from St George's and Southern Cross](US-02.1.5), and [feeds from the other providers](US-14.6.1).

**Already settled:**

- The hospital integration sends one shot of data, the day before: tomorrow's theatre list, as a PDF and as a direct data integration. It holds the Contracts and dates of birth more fully than the rooms' lists.
- Hospitals also send updated schedules by free-text email, two weeks to a month ahead.
- The first version has no automated intake. Downloads land in a matching and creating screen, where staff see the patient details and what is missing, then save a draft List or assign an anaesthetist. A later update for the same booking, matched by date, surgeon, location and slot, arrives in the same screen for staff to approve. Automated approvals come once the matching is trusted.
- Integrations are time and materials.
