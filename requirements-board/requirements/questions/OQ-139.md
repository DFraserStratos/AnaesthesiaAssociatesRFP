In Solutions Plus's day view, many half-days read "CPH" (Christchurch Public Hospital) with a Public colour, beside private Lists that show the surgeon and hospital ("H Waller STG"). AA does not book or bill public work, and Greg has called public hospital one of several kinds of unavailable that "all have the same effect... it's for information". So AA seems to know only that the anaesthetist is at the public hospital, not what that work is.

1. **What should a public Slot show?** Just "Public" (or "CPH"), or something more, such as the hospital, the theatre or the kind of session?
2. **Who enters it, and where does it come from?** The anaesthetist, the office from a public roster, or some other feed?
3. **Is it a status of its own or a kind of unavailable?** That is, does the office need to tell public work apart from other unavailability at a glance (its own colour), or is a note on an Unavailable Slot enough?
4. **Is it ever available for cover?** For example, a public session the anaesthetist might give up for private work.

What hangs on it: whether Public stays in the [Slot status list](US-01.2.2) as its own value with a colour, what text a public Slot carries on the [day view](US-13.1.1), and whether anaesthetists [setting their availability](US-01.2.1) pick it from their app.

Recommendation: treat public as a kind of unavailable that shows "Public" (with an optional note) and carries no List detail, unless Vanessa says the office uses more.
