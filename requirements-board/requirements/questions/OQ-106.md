The v6 documents propose loading frequently performed procedures as lines on the default No contract (RVG), so the office or anaesthetist can "choose this pre-defined procedure rather than just specifying the RVG Base code", with their descriptions printed on the invoice; the executive summary lists it under an area "still under active design discussion". The documents do not say whether these lines carry prices; the executive summary says the no-contract arrangement carries no practice-wide prices ("AA does not set prices for its members").

Against it: Greg relayed on 14 October that Nick and Ben had dismissed a list of favourite procedures in the default Contract ("we're spending a lot of time and effort doing something that we don't really think is necessary"), and on 16 October Donald wants to build "without that intermediate list". Greg still expects a strong pattern between surgeons' booking text and RVG codes that history could later pre-allocate.

Should No contract (RVG) carry routine procedure lines in the first release, and would they carry any price?

Recommendation: not in the first release. Keep No contract (RVG) without lines, and revisit once booking history shows which wordings recur.
