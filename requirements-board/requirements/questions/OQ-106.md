**Still to answer, for Donald:**

1. **Should the first release load routine procedures as lines on No contract (RVG)?** The v6 documents propose it, so the office or anaesthetist can pick a pre-defined procedure instead of an RVG Base code; Nick and Ben (via Greg) dismissed the idea and Donald wants to build without it. If the lines go in, whether they carry any price is also open, since the documents do not say.

Recommendation: not in the first release. Keep No contract (RVG) without lines, and revisit once booking history shows which wordings recur.

## Background

The v6 documents propose loading frequently performed procedures as lines on the default No contract (RVG), so the office or anaesthetist can "choose this pre-defined procedure rather than just specifying the RVG Base code", with their descriptions printed on the invoice; the executive summary lists it under an area "still under active design discussion". The documents do not say whether these lines carry prices; the executive summary says the no-contract arrangement carries no practice-wide prices ("AA does not set prices for its members").

Against it: Greg relayed on 14 October that Nick and Ben had dismissed a list of favourite procedures in the default Contract ("we're spending a lot of time and effort doing something that we don't really think is necessary"), and on 16 October Donald wants to build "without that intermediate list". Greg still expects a strong pattern between surgeons' booking text and RVG codes that history could later pre-allocate.

## Answer

Not in the first release. No contract (RVG) carries no lines of routine procedures. Greg reported on 14 October that Nick and Ben had dismissed a list of what Ben called favourite procedures in the default Contract: "we're spending a lot of time and effort doing something that we don't really think is necessary... So they've dismissed it." On 16 October Donald: "I do really wanna basically at this stage build out the system without that intermediate list"; Greg: "Yes." The intermediate list is the same idea: pre-defined procedures between the surgeon's booking text and the RVG codes, which the v6 documents hold as lines on the default Contract.

Greg still expects a strong pattern between surgeons' booking text and RVG codes, which booking history could later be used to pre-allocate: an idea for later, not a requirement now. See "Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #17", "Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #37"
