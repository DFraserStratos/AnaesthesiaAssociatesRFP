Three readings were heard: credit the whole prepayment and re-prepay at the new rate (OQ-21, which Ben disagreed with); the new anaesthetist keeps the agreed amount and wears or benefits from the difference (Ben, OQ-03's answer); the draft payable pair is repointed to the new anaesthetist. Confirm with Ben that the agreed amount stands and the anaesthetist who does it is paid.

Recommendation: that reading, with the payable repointed.

## Answer

the new anaesthetist keeps the agreed amount and wears or benefits from the difference. This is true.

the draft payable pair is updated to the new anaesthetist.

The meeting adds: only one half of the pair (presumably the payable, the anaesthetist's side) is updated ("repointed" is the wrong word). Greg: the payable pair must be amended "because the trust account in Xero always has to balance", which raises when in the lifecycle the pair is created and amended (not answered). Donald treats Ben's confirmation as assumed. (See Notes 2026-10-02 · AA meeting with Greg #11 #47)

The meeting adds: Donald's 7 October expansion confirms the answer: "there is essentially an honour system where the new anaesthetist also does not try and claim any more for the procedure than what was already prepaid. So that there is stability for the patient", and "the system should not try and have any logic to detect any of these flows that would re-trigger any sort of calculations for invoices." Not said: whether the update of the payable to the new anaesthetist, which is system action on the move, survives that (OQ-80). (See "Notes 2026-10-06 · AA directors meeting #10")
