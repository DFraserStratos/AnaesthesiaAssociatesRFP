Anaesthetists usually find their own cover at short notice, and ring the office only if they cannot. [The swap request](US-01.4.3) has the office confirm before it takes effect. Should an anaesthetist be able to hand a List to a colleague without the office confirming it, for short-notice cover, with the office only notified? Or must the office confirm each one?

Recommendation: direct, with the office notified and the availability conflict cleared.

## Answer

An anaesthetist can in their app, select a list, and start a move action.

Then, they can pick to move to AA office (Becomes a draft list) or they can see the availability of other anaesthetists and PUSH the booking into one of their free slots. The second anaesthetist does NOT need to accept. 

This is a high trust system.
