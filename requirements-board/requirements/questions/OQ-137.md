Our RFP response offers BAU support Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm NZT, with no weekend or on-call cover by default. On 5 November Donald told Ben and Vanessa, from memory ("I believe"), that the support team is "currently eight o'clock to five thirty". Vanessa starts at 8:00am, before anyone else, and AA's current vendor starts at 8:30 or 9:00.

1. **Which start time does Stratos offer, 8:00am or 8:30am?**
2. **Does AA need cover from 8:00am, when its office opens?**
3. **How are outages outside these hours handled?** Donald: "we do have an incident response process, and we can talk about that in the future."

What hangs on it: the [help desk requirement](US-15.0.8), and the support-hours delivery constraint on [inbound changes accepted until the List is authorised](US-02.5.4).

Recommendation: confirm Stratos' current support hours internally, and offer cover from 8:00am if it can be staffed, since that is when AA's office starts. Settle outage response during scoping.

## Answer

Stratos support hours are **8:30am to 5:00pm NZT, Monday to Friday, excluding New Zealand public holidays**. These are Stratos' standard business hours for supported bespoke systems. The 8:00am start Donald gave at the 5 November meeting, and the 5:30pm end in our RFP response, were misspoken.

Support runs on two separate channels with different rules.

### 1. AA as Stratos' client: product support and development

This is AA's office dealing with Stratos about the product: incidents, fixes, changes and new work.

**Response times, for tickets raised during business hours**

| Priority | Response |
|---|---|
| P1: system down with widespread business impact | 1 hour |
| P2: high-priority issue significantly affecting business operations, no viable workaround | Same business day |
| P3: standard issue or request with limited business impact, or a reasonable workaround is available | 3 business days |
| P4: low-priority issue, cosmetic defect, minor enhancement, or planned task with no operational urgency | Scheduled at a mutually agreed time |

**Outside business hours**

| When | Cover |
|---|---|
| After hours | Best effort, not guaranteed. |
| Public holidays | Call-out only, with AA's approval. |
| Christmas and New Year (about two weeks) | Limited, best-effort cover. |

### 2. General user support: anaesthetists and other users

This is the help line for the people who use the apps day to day (anaesthetists, AA office staff and other users) for problems such as a forgotten password, being unable to sign in, or something not working as expected.

- **How:** a support request form or a support phone line, both shown in the apps.
- **Hours:** 8:30am to 5:00pm NZT, Monday to Friday, excluding New Zealand public holidays.
- **After hours:** none, except for a security breach or the platform being offline or unavailable.

| Priority | Response |
|---|---|
| P1: system down with widespread business impact | 1 hour |
| P2: high-priority issue significantly affecting business operations, no viable workaround | Same business day |
| P3: standard issue or request with limited business impact, or a reasonable workaround is available | 4 hours |
| P4: low-priority issue, cosmetic defect, minor enhancement, or planned task with no operational urgency | Scheduled at a mutually agreed time |

Not settled by this answer: whether AA needs cover from 8:00am, when its office opens (question 2).
