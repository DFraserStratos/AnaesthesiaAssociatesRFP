Ben would like "an electronic address book somewhere, perhaps incorporated into the new software". The surgeon and surgeons' room master data already holds each surgeon, their room and the room's contacts.

1. **Is the surgeon master data the address book?** Or does AA want something more, such as searching by specialty or sharing contacts outside the office?
2. **Does it cover other contacts?** For example hospital and insurer contacts, as well as surgeons. Every row of Ben's list also has a street address, which the room record does not hold today.
3. **Who keeps it current?** The office alone, or the rooms as well. Ben expects the list to hold inaccuracies, so someone has to check it before it is loaded and keep it right afterwards.

What hangs on the answer: whether address book screens are part of the system, and what reference data the [cutover load](US-13.4.3) must carry.
