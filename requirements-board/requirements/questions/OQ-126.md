Ben would like "an electronic address book somewhere, perhaps incorporated into the new software". The surgeon master data already holds each surgeon, their practice name and their [personal and office contacts](US-13.6.5), and every [surgeon](US-13.6.2) carries one or more broad specialties.

1. **Is the surgeon master data the address book?** Or does AA want something more, such as searching or browsing surgeons by specialty, or sharing contacts outside the office?
2. **Does it cover other contacts?** For example hospital and insurer contacts, as well as surgeons. Every row of Ben's list also has a street address, which the surgeon record does not hold.
3. **Who keeps it current?** The office alone, or the rooms as well. Ben expects the list to hold inaccuracies, so someone has to check it before it is loaded and keep it right afterwards.

What hangs on the answer: whether address book screens are part of the system, and what reference data the [cutover load](US-13.4.3) must carry.
