A Contract held and billed by a hospital sits under that hospital on the [step rail](US-04.3.8) only when it carries a [funding tag](US-04.2.17). A hospital's Contract with no tag, such as St George's plain RVG Contract, sits under Special instead.

Is Special where the office would look for it, or should a hospital's untagged Contracts sit under the hospital too, for example as a "No tag" choice beside SXAP, HNZ and ACC?

What hangs on it: where step 1 sends the office for a hospital's own plain RVG work, and how many choices step 2 shows under each hospital.
