The [No contract backstops](US-04.3.9) give each hospital and funding tag an RVG backstop (for example "Forte · SXAP · RVG") as well as the Fixed price one. The domain model's built-in Contracts list only the Fixed price one for each place an invoice can go.

Which is right: does every hospital and tag, and every Special holder, get both an RVG and a Fixed price backstop, or only the Fixed price one?

What hangs on it: the "No contract" choices at step 3 of the step rail, and the backstops the system makes for each place an invoice can go.
