**Still to answer, for Ben:**

1. **Can the anaesthetist change the price on a prepaid procedure, or is any difference handled only by an ad hoc invoice or credit note raised by hand?** The directors said nothing further is calculated on a prepaid procedure, so by hand only. The v6 design instead makes the prepaid amount an editable fixed price: raised, the difference is billed as an additional invoice; lowered, a refund process is invoked. On 14 October Donald and Greg left both readings standing.

Recommendation: On a prepaid procedure the price stays the prepaid amount and the price field is locked; any difference is handled only by an ad hoc invoice or credit note raised by hand.

## History

The 5 October guide lets the anaesthetist [change the price](US-03.5.1) on their own price list Contracts, and a prepaid procedure is priced on one. But the directors said [nothing further is calculated](US-06.4.1) on a prepaid or fixed-price procedure: when the work differs, the anaesthetist raises an ad hoc invoice or credit note by hand, and the guide's 'raise the price and have the difference invoiced' was not adopted. If the anaesthetist types a different price on a prepaid procedure, [netting the prepayment](US-08.2.2) would leave a balance the system would then invoice or credit.

Update 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16: not settled, and the documents now differ from the directors.

- 14 October, reading the arrangements table: the anaesthetist's prepaid price is "technically... an estimate, but it's the anaesthetist's price"; Donald: "The anaesthetist can change it in the room", Greg: "Yes." On a prepaid facelift where half the work is done or extra is done, Donald: "I wouldn't think you would handle that via the price override. It would be through the additional invoices." Greg: "That's what happens." Both readings stand. See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #5.
- The v6 design: "The prepaid amount behaves as an editable fixed price"; raised, the difference is billed as an additional invoice; lowered, "a refund process is invoked for the difference"; its changes table now reads "A refund may be issued". This is in tension with the directors' answer recorded in US-06.4.1 (nothing raised automatically, by hand only). See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #6 #13.
- A separate case, a Contract schedule change between prepayment and the procedure, is raised as its own question, OQ-104.

Update 2026-10-19: not settled, and wider. Prepayments are now held in a prepay holding account until the procedure is done (OQ-80). Greg: prepaid values are "technically... always an estimate"; "Ben said there's a strong [unclear] that they don't change prepaid values" (the key word is lost in the recording), yet they may go "both up and down", as in the document Greg updated and Ben checked; this concerns only a few anaesthetists. A change of anaesthetist, the holding account and an override amount "could all happen on the same procedure. So we may need to redraft that a bit so there's some clarity." Greg: "I might draw that out because it is a bit subtle." Donald: prepaid Contracts "practically speaking... should just be a fixed cost". See "Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #15".

## Answer

No. Prepaid Contracts are set up as not editable: a prepaid Contract cannot also carry the setting that allows a price override, so the price stays the prepaid amount. Any difference, such as a facelift that becomes a facelift plus liposuction on the day, is handled by a second invoice or a credit raised by hand; nothing is raised automatically. Decided on 28 October, when Donald and Greg put it to Ben by phone.

- Ben: "in my mind, in that scenario, you'd raise a second invoice"; "Automation is important, but there will always be the exceptional cases... not everything needs to be automated because we're talking about a rare event here. So a rare event can have some manual input." On informed financial consent: "my personal philosophy is that you just have to wear that", otherwise "discuss with the patient and say, look, I'm going to give you an extra invoice for X."
- Greg, after the call: "prepaid contracts must be set up as not editable. That locks all this down"; "in order for this to operate correctly the contract must be non-editable otherwise you're going to break the system." Donald: "Yes, prepaid contracts can't also have the setting of being allowed to have a price override. Cool, I like that."
- The AA office can still change any price, whatever the Contract's rules. Donald: "only AA admin staff can do some sort of free text price change on any contract, regardless of the rules of the contract"; Greg: "Yeah, they can. Agreed."

This matches the recommendation. Greg had argued for the system invoicing or crediting the difference automatically; Ben's answer settles it as by hand. See "Notes 2026-10-28 · AA meeting with Greg #1 #59 #60 #61"
