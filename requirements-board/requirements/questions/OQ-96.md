The background below describes the 5 October guide and the directors' answer. The 14 and 16 October update at the end changes the picture, and the question is still open.

The guide lets the anaesthetist [change the price](US-03.5.1) on their own price list Contracts, and a prepaid procedure is priced on one. But the directors said [nothing further is calculated](US-06.4.1) on a prepaid or fixed-price procedure: when the work differs, the anaesthetist raises an ad hoc invoice or credit note by hand, and the guide's 'raise the price and have the difference invoiced' was not adopted. If the anaesthetist types a different price on a prepaid procedure, [netting the prepayment](US-08.2.2) would leave a balance the system would then invoice or credit.

Is the price on a prepaid procedure editable, or is the only route an ad hoc invoice or credit note?

Recommendation: On a prepaid procedure the price stays the prepaid amount and the price field is locked; any difference is handled only by an ad hoc invoice or credit note raised by hand.

Update 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16: not settled, and the documents now differ from the directors.

- 14 October, reading the arrangements table: the anaesthetist's prepaid price is "technically... an estimate, but it's the anaesthetist's price"; Donald: "The anaesthetist can change it in the room", Greg: "Yes." On a prepaid facelift where half the work is done or extra is done, Donald: "I wouldn't think you would handle that via the price override. It would be through the additional invoices." Greg: "That's what happens." Both readings stand. See Notes 2026-10-14 · AA meeting with Greg #5.
- The v6 design: "The prepaid amount behaves as an editable fixed price"; raised, the difference is billed as an additional invoice; lowered, "a refund process is invoked for the difference"; its changes table now reads "A refund may be issued". This is in tension with the directors' answer recorded in US-06.4.1 (nothing raised automatically, by hand only). See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #6 #13.
- A separate case, a Contract schedule change between prepayment and the procedure, is raised as its own question, OQ-104.
