Prepayment is triggered by the anaesthetist's prepaid setting (procedures or whole RVG groups), and [the amount](US-06.2.2) now comes from their own first-party fixed-price Contract.

What happens when a Booking has a procedure in the prepaid set but the anaesthetist's Contract has no line or price for it (for example a whole group is marked prepaid)?

Recommendation: Flag it to the office as a warning and hold the [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) until a price is added to the anaesthetist's Contract; never fall back to an estimate.

## Answer

No longer arises. Prepayment is now triggered by the Contract, not by a prepaid list on the anaesthetist's profile: "The admin managing the booking process, selects a Contract that specifies is_prepay = true", and "The Contract Line will always specify an (estimated) fixed price." So a prepaid procedure always has a price on the line the office picks. See Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #4 #28.

Donald and Greg, 16 October: "all this prepaid procedure stuff isn't automated anymore... There needs to be some human in the loop that places [it] on the contract to then trigger the prepayment." Greg: "if a booking's made using that contract, then it does invoke a prepay procedure." The anaesthetist's prepaid RVG codes go from the profile: "There's no association between RVG codes and prepay." See Notes 2026-10-16 · AA meeting with Greg #20 #22.
