## Observations on the list

Donald's reading of the spreadsheet on 3 November. These are facts about the data, not decisions.

5. **Shape.** 137 specialists at 40 practices, across 18 specialties (orthopaedics 25, ophthalmology
   18, ENT 14, colorectal 14, gynaecology 11, plastics 10, and smaller groups). Columns: Practice,
   Specialty, Specialist, Role, Phone, PA Contact, Email, Address, Source URL. The Source URL column is
   empty.
6. **Emails are mostly shared inboxes.** 89 of the 137 rows have an email, and most of those are a
   practice's shared inbox (info@, admin@, reception@, enquiries@). 16 practices have no email at all.
7. **Few PA names, and some PAs belong to one surgeon.** Only 10 rows name a PA. At one ENT practice,
   each surgeon has their own PA and their own inbox, so one practice holds several contacts. In some
   shared practices, a surgeon also has an email of their own practice.
8. **Surgeons at two practices.** 4 surgeons are listed at two practices.
9. **No identifiers.** The list carries no NZ medical registration numbers and no HPI CPNs.
10. **Incomplete names.** 13 rows have no title (Dr, Mr, Prof), and two of them give a first name only, with the
    specialty in brackets in place of a surname.
