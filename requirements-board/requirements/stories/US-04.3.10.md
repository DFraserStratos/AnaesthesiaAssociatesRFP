When the [step rail](US-04.3.8) opens, it marks one path "Suggested" from the [Booking's funder](US-11.4.3):

- with no funder (self-funded), Patient › Standard
- where the funder itself holds a Contract offered on the List, that holder under Special (NIB)
- otherwise, the List's hospital under the [funding tag](US-04.2.17) that names that funder (Southern Cross Health Society suggests SXAP)

This is so the funder already on the Booking speeds up the office's choice without overruling it, since the Contract, not the insurance, decides who is billed (OQ-93).

Where none of these fits, nothing is marked. A suggestion never narrows the choice: every other path stays open.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Suggested.** Given a Booking at Forte whose funder is Southern Cross Health Society, then Forte › SXAP is marked Suggested.
- **Funder's own Contracts.** Given a Booking whose funder is NIB and a NIB Contract offered on the List, then Special › NIB is marked Suggested.
- **No funder.** Given no funder on the Booking, then Patient › Standard is marked Suggested.
- **No match.** Given a funder that holds no Contract on the List and names no tag at the List's hospital, then nothing is marked Suggested.
- **Never narrows.** Given any suggestion, then every other path is still offered with all its Contracts.

## Notes

Donald, D7: "The booking's funder only suggests a path". OQ-93 stands: insurance never narrows the [Contracts offered](US-04.3.2).

Our readings (contract model proposal): a funding tag names its insurer or funder, and the Booking's funder is a Party (A25); the order of the tests, and Patient › Standard for a Booking with no funder (L687-691).
