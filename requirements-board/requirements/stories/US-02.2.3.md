The surgeons' rooms re-send the whole List as a snapshot (a PDF) on every change and never send a deletion. The system compares each re-sent List with what it holds and finds the Bookings that are no longer on it. The office sees them in the intake review and matching step as removals to confirm as cancellations ([record a cancellation](US-02.5.3)).

This is so a cancelled patient, for example one whose insurance was declined, does not stay on the List just because no message said so.
