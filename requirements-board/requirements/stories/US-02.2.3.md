The surgeons' rooms re-send the whole List as a snapshot (a PDF) on every change and never send a deletion. When a re-sent PDF is merged as the [next version](US-02.2.1) of a List AA holds, the system finds the Bookings on the List that are missing from it, and the matching screen offers each as a removal for the office to confirm as a cancellation ([record a cancellation](US-02.5.3)).

This is so a cancelled patient, for example one whose insurance was declined, does not stay on the List just because no message said so.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Offered as a removal.** Given a List with three Bookings, when a re-sent PDF for it lists only two, then the third is offered on the matching screen as a removal.
- **Confirmed by the office.** Given a removal offered for a Booking, when the office confirms it, then the Booking is recorded as cancelled; when the office leaves it out, then the Booking is unchanged.
