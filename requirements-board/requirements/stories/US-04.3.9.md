Every invoice destination has two backstop Contracts the system makes and keeps, so a procedure can always be priced even when no Contract of the destination's own covers it:

- **No contract · RVG**, priced from RVG units at the anaesthetist's own rate. Under Patient › Standard it is [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.1) itself; each hospital and [funding tag](US-04.2.17) and each Special holder has its own, billing that hospital or holder (for example "Forte · SXAP · RVG"). Patient › Prepaid has none, as a prepaid price is never RVG.
- **No contract · Fixed price**, an open price whose amount is typed on the Procedure: "No contract · Fixed price" and "Prepaid · Fixed price" held by AA and billing the payer, one per hospital and funding tag (for example "Forte · SXAP · Fixed price") billing the hospital, one per Special holder (for example "NIB · Fixed price") billing that holder.

At step 3 of the [step rail](US-04.3.8) every list of Contracts starts with a "No contract" heading holding the destination's two backstops, shown as "No contract · RVG" and "No contract · Fixed price" whoever holds them, above the destination's own Contracts. Choosing No contract · RVG asks for the RVG code; choosing No contract · Fixed price asks for the amount. Under Patient › Standard, where No contract · RVG is the only Contract, it is picked as the answer is given and "RVG code | Fixed price" switches to the typed amount.

This is so every destination always has an answer, even for a procedure no price list covers yet.

- The system makes a destination's backstops when its first Active Contract exists; AA's Patient ones are there from the start.
- A Special holder's backstops are offered once one of that holder's Contracts is on offer.
- A backstop is never disabled or deleted and carries no specialties or links.
- The office may rename a backstop and edit its invoice description and [AA code](US-04.1.4), nothing else, and cannot create one itself.
- The office may type the amount on any Fixed price backstop; the anaesthetist only on No contract · Fixed price under the patient, so a hospital or insurer is never billed a figure the anaesthetist typed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Two per destination.** Given Forte with an Active SXAP Contract, then there is exactly one Forte · SXAP · RVG and one Forte · SXAP · Fixed price.
- **Listed first.** Given Forte › SXAP at step 3, when the office opens the list of Contracts, then "No contract" heads it with No contract · RVG and No contract · Fixed price.
- **RVG backstop.** Given Forte › SXAP, when the office chooses No contract · RVG, then the Procedure is priced from its RVG code at the anaesthetist's rate and invoiced to Forte.
- **Fixed price backstop.** Given Forte › SXAP, when the office chooses No contract · Fixed price, then the Procedure is on Forte · SXAP · Fixed price and needs an amount.
- **Anaesthetist.** Given a Procedure on a hospital Fixed price backstop, then the anaesthetist cannot type its amount.
- **Kept.** A backstop cannot be disabled or deleted.
