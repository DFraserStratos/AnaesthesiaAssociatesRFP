Every invoice destination has exactly one open-price fallback, a Contract the system makes and keeps:

- "No contract · Fixed price" and "Prepaid · Fixed price", held by AA and billing the payer
- one per hospital and [funding tag](US-04.2.17) (for example "Forte · SXAP · Fixed price"), billing the hospital
- one per Special holder (for example "NIB · Fixed price"), billing that holder

A fallback has no price of its own: at step 3 of the [step rail](US-04.3.8) the "Other price" toggle picks the destination's fallback, and the amount is typed on the Procedure.

This is so every destination always has an answer, even for a procedure no price list covers yet.

- The system makes a fallback when the first Active Contract for its destination exists; AA's two Patient fallbacks are there from the start.
- A Special holder's fallback is offered once one of that holder's Contracts is picked.
- A fallback is never disabled or deleted, carries no specialties or links, and is never listed as a row.
- The office may rename a fallback and edit its invoice description and [AA code](US-04.1.4), nothing else, and cannot create an open-price Contract itself.
- The office may type the amount on any fallback; the anaesthetist only on No contract · Fixed price, so a hospital or insurer is never billed a figure the anaesthetist typed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One per destination.** Given Forte with an Active SXAP Contract, then there is exactly one Forte · SXAP · Fixed price.
- **Other price.** Given Forte › SXAP at step 3, when the office flips Other price, then the Procedure is on Forte · SXAP · Fixed price and needs an amount.
- **Reachable.** Given a hospital tag the specialty filter has emptied, then step 3 fills in its fallback.
- **Anaesthetist.** Given a Procedure on a hospital fallback, then the anaesthetist cannot type its amount.
- **Kept.** A fallback cannot be disabled or deleted.
