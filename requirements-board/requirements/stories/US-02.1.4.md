Rows the office has not yet matched to a Booking, or accepted as new, stay in a queue for manual intervention.

An incoming booking (hospital download, surgeon PDF or feed) that has no NHI also waits at intake until the office adds the NHI, shown on Intake beside the unmatched rows as a row [waiting for an NHI](US-11.1.5). While it waits, a [stub](US-02.6.1) shows in its place on its List, seen by the anaesthetist and the office. A Booking [cannot be saved without an NHI](US-11.1.4), and there is no "NHI pending" Booking.

This is so nothing from a hospital download is ever silently dropped.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No NHI.** Given an incoming booking has no NHI, then it waits at intake and no Booking is created until the office adds the NHI.
- **Stub on the List.** Given an incoming booking with no NHI waiting at intake, then a stub shows in its place on its List until the office resolves it.
