Everything that has arrived and is waiting for the office shows in one feed on the matching screen, grouped by the List each row belongs to. A hospital's file is a heading over the Lists it holds; a rooms' PDF is one List. The office can filter the feed by source (each hospital, or the rooms' PDFs), to rows that need an NHI, and to what has been decided.

This is so nothing that arrives is ever silently dropped, and the office works through it all in one place.

- **Nothing dropped.** A row stays in the feed until the office decides it. A PDF that cannot be made into a List still lands in the feed, with the reason.
- **Needs NHI.** An incoming booking with no NHI waits in the same feed, under the Needs NHI filter, until the office adds the NHI ([rows waiting for an NHI](US-11.1.5)). While it waits, a [stub](US-02.6.1) shows in its place on its List, seen by the anaesthetist and the office. A Booking [cannot be saved without an NHI](US-11.1.4), and there is no "NHI pending" Booking.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Grouped by List.** Given a hospital file holding three Lists, then the feed shows the file as a heading over its three Lists, each with its rows.
- **By source.** Given arrivals from a hospital and from a rooms' PDF, when the office filters to the rooms' PDFs, then only the PDF's List shows.
- **Needs NHI and Decided.** When the office filters to Needs NHI, then only rows waiting for an NHI show; when it filters to Decided, then only rows already decided show.
- **Unreadable PDF.** Given a rooms' PDF that cannot be made into a List, then it shows in the feed with the reason, and is not dropped.
- **No NHI.** Given an incoming booking has no NHI, then it waits in the feed under Needs NHI and no Booking is created until the office adds the NHI.
- **Stub on the List.** Given an incoming booking with no NHI waiting at intake, then a stub shows in its place on its List until the office resolves it.
