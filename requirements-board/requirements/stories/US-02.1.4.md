Rows that can't be matched automatically stay in a queue for manual intervention.

An incoming booking (hospital download, surgeon PDF or feed) that has no NHI also waits here, on the matching screen, until the office adds the NHI. A Booking cannot be saved without an NHI, and there is no "NHI pending" Booking.

This is so nothing from a hospital download is ever silently dropped.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No NHI.** Given an incoming booking has no NHI, then it stays in the queue and no Booking is created until the office adds the NHI.
