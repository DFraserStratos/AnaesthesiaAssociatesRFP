The office gives each Contract none or more specialties from AA's [specialty list](US-13.4.4). The [step rail](US-04.3.8) offers a Contract only where its specialties include the List's, or it has none.

This is so the office sees only the Contracts that fit the surgeon, which keeps long hospital schedules short.

- None means every specialty.
- A List's specialty is its [surgeon's](US-13.6.6), so it is known even on a Draft List. Where a surgeon holds several, any overlap matches.
- A Contract for work done in more than one specialty carries each: a breast reduction by a plastic or a general surgeon carries both.
- A hidden Contract stays hidden: there is no "show all".

## Acceptance criteria

- **Hidden.** Given a List for ophthalmologist Anna Whitcomb, then a Contract whose only specialty is plastic surgery is not offered.
- **Two specialties.** Given a breast reduction Contract with plastic surgery and general surgery, then it is offered on a plastic surgeon's and a general surgeon's Lists.
- **None.** Given a Contract with no specialties, then the specialty never hides it.
- **No show all.** There is no control to show the hidden Contracts.
