The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, then approves the Contract selected for each Procedure or corrects it. The review covers:

- each Procedure and its Contract, shown as [the text as received, then the Contract, then the RVG code](US-03.1.9)
- the insurer or funder on the Booking, which only suggests a path on the [step rail](US-04.3.8) and never narrows the choice
- the insurer and insurance approval number on the Booking ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)); SXAP is a funding tag on the hospital's Contracts, not a statement that cover is approved, and the selected Contract decides who is billed
- [each Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2), shown in its "Paid by" line where its Contract bills the payer: the patient by default, which the office or anaesthetist can change to a parent or guardian
- other references, addresses and invoice email addresses
- any Procedure the anaesthetist [flagged for the office to check](US-03.6.3)
- review flags: a Contract the anaesthetist changed; a price typed on a Contract that is not an open price, shown beside the computed price; a Contract that no longer fits the List [after a move](US-04.3.11); a Contract that is Disabled, on a Procedure dated on or after the day it was [disabled](US-04.1.2)
- any Procedure that still "Needs an invoice destination" ([step rail](US-04.3.8)) or ["Needs a price"](US-05.2.8)
- anything missed or filled in incorrectly, such as an operation not completed properly

This is so mistakes in the anaesthetist's Contract choice and the billing details are caught before the List is authorised. AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice and relies on this review.

The review warns, without blocking, on each Procedure billed to the payer where a patient under 18 is left as that Procedure's payer ([warn when a child is the payer](US-11.2.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Payer shown.** Given a Booking with a Procedure whose Contract bills the payer, when the admin reviews it, then [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2) is shown in its "Paid by" line and can be changed.
- **Child warning per Procedure.** Given a Booking with two Procedures billed to the payer, each leaving a patient under 18 as its payer, when the admin reviews it, then each Procedure shows the warning and the admin can still approve.
- **Review flags.** Given an anaesthetist changed a Procedure's Contract, or a price was typed on a Contract that is not an open price, when the admin reviews the Booking, then the Procedure is flagged, with a typed price shown beside the computed price.
- **Disabled Contract.** Given a Contract disabled on 1 December, when the admin reviews a Booking dated 3 December with a Procedure on that Contract, then the Procedure is flagged, nothing is blocked, and the admin approves it or moves it to another Contract; a Procedure on the same Contract in a Booking dated 28 November is not flagged.
