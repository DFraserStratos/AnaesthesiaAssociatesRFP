The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, then approves the Contract selected for each Procedure or corrects it. The review covers:

- each Procedure and its Contract, shown as [the text as received, then the Contract, then the RVG code](US-03.1.9)
- the insurer or funder on the Booking, which only suggests a path on the [step rail](US-04.3.8) and never narrows the choice
- the insurer and insurance approval number on the Booking ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)); SXAP is a funding tag on the hospital's Contracts, not a statement that cover is approved, and the selected Contract decides who is billed
- [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), who is invoiced when the Contract bills the patient: the patient by default, which the office or anaesthetist can change to a parent or guardian
- other references, addresses and invoice email addresses
- any Procedure the anaesthetist [flagged for the office to check](US-03.6.3)
- review flags: a Contract the anaesthetist changed; a price typed on a Contract that is not an open price, shown beside the computed price; a Contract that no longer fits the List [after a move](US-04.3.11)
- any Procedure that still "Needs an invoice destination" ([step rail](US-04.3.8)) or ["Needs a price"](US-05.2.8)
- anything missed or filled in incorrectly, such as an operation not completed properly

This is so mistakes in the anaesthetist's Contract choice and the billing details are caught before the List is authorised. AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice and relies on this review.

The review warns, without blocking, of a patient under 18 who is the payer on the Booking for a Procedure billed to that payer ([warn when a child is the payer on the Booking](US-11.2.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Payer shown.** Given a Booking with a Procedure whose Contract bills the patient, when the admin reviews it, then [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is shown and can be changed.
- **Review flags.** Given an anaesthetist changed a Procedure's Contract, or a price was typed on a Contract that is not an open price, when the admin reviews the Booking, then the Procedure is flagged, with a typed price shown beside the computed price.
