The admin reviews each Booking on a submitted List, covering:

- its Procedures and the Contract selected for each one, each shown as [source wording, then procedure and RVG code, then Contract](US-03.1.9)
- whether a Procedure needs an insurer's Contract, and the insurance status and insurer details that came with the Booking
- [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), who is invoiced when the Contract bills the patient: the patient by default, which the office or anaesthetist can change to a parent or guardian
- other references
- addresses and invoice email addresses
- anything missed or filled in incorrectly, such as an operation not completed properly

The admin then approves the Contract selections made earlier, or corrects them. The review raises two warnings, neither a block:

- a patient under 18 who is the payer on the Booking for a Procedure billed to that payer (see [warn when a child is the payer on the Booking](US-11.2.4))
- a Booking that says an insurer will pay, but with no insurer's Contract chosen for any of its Procedures; pre-approval often falls through on the day, when the patient pays and the anaesthetist changes the Contract to No contract (RVG)

This is so mistakes in the anaesthetist's Contract choice and the billing details are caught before the List is authorised. AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice and relies on this review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking whose insurance status says an insurer will pay, when no Procedure on it has an insurer's Contract, then the office review shows a warning that the admin can clear, and the List can still be authorised.
- Given a Booking with a Procedure whose Contract bills the patient, when the admin reviews it, then [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is shown and can be changed.

## Notes

Approval happens here, at the SUBMITTED review after the anaesthetist submits, not before the procedure (OQ-26; Q&A #4).

The Booking may show the patient's insurance status (for example SXAP), which guides the Contract choice, while each Procedure's Contract decides who is billed, so one Booking can have Procedures billed to different parties (OQ-55; Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #11). Greg: "the booking does hold the funding source" (Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #6). Whether and where the insurance status is stored is OQ-93. The insured-but-no-insurer-Contract warning is the draft design's "Warning at office review, not a block, because pre-approval regularly falls through on the day" (Notes 2026-10-07 · Pricing model documents #34). The order for who pays (the insurer named on the hospital's theatre list, the hospital applying to the insurer as agent) is still to be drawn out with Vanessa (Notes 2026-10-01 #29 #55). The under-18 check is a mild warning that the admin can clear, not a block (OQ-54; Notes 2026-10-01 #28 #47).
