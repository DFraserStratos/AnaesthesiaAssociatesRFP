For every procedure invoice, the Billing/Invoice Engine creates a receivable from the billable party and a matching payable to the anaesthetist; for a [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) the payable is to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) instead, where AA holds the money until the procedure is done. The two records are linked to each other and to the Booking and Procedures they came from.

This is so the two sides of the same transaction are always handled together: neither can be created, paid or reversed without the other moving with it.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Payments are collected into AA's account, and amounts due to anaesthetists are tracked separately through linked receivable and payable records.
- The [ACCREC and ACCPAY](US-09.1.1) are expected to be treated as a unit that completes or backs out together, since a pair created only on the ACCPAY side is money owed to an anaesthetist with no receivable behind it.

Greg and Sam's proposed posting design: in Xero the invoice and its bill post through a clearing account (a current asset) rather than a sales or expense account. The invoice debits the payer and credits the clearing account; the bill credits the anaesthetist (or the AA Holding account) and debits it. The clearing account should always be zero, so money left in it is the quickest sign that a pair is out of step. Its full working is to be set out in the process maps Greg and Sam are drawing; the [trust account diagram](AR-63#clearing) shows it.
