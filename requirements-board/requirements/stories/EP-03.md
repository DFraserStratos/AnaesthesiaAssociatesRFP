The anaesthetist:

- sees their Lists and Bookings
- reads each procedure as received
- sees the Contract on each Procedure and can change it, and the RVG code, until the List is authorised
- records the clinical billing data
- adds pre-op and post-op [events](FT-03.7) to a Procedure, including after it is invoiced
- changes the price where the Contract lets them
- marks Bookings complete

This is so every Booking carries a complete billing record before it goes to the office for [review and authorisation](EP-07).
