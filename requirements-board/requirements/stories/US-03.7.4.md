AA bills, through the system, a pre-op consult held in AA's own room.

This is so that charge is raised and tracked in the system like everything else AA bills.

What is billed, to whom, by whom, at what price and how it is recorded is still open (OQ-138). It is not the [ACC pre-op assessment](US-05.5.2), which is a pre-op event on a Procedure (OQ-12).

## Technical discussion

Whether it is an event on a Procedure, an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), a Booking of its own, or a charge to the anaesthetist for the room depends on the answer; it sits under events until then.

## Notes

Vanessa, 5 November, after confirming that anaesthetists' pain work is not billed through AA: "The only thing that we've billed through here is if the room gets used for pre-op consults. So we do have to have that added in here." Ben: "We can talk about that later if you'd like."
