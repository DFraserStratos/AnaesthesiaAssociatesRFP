A billable [event](US-03.7.1) is its own line item, priced from its recorded time or its fixed fee. The Procedure's Contract can replace a recorded time with a fixed fee: the anaesthetist may see the patient for a time, but only the fee is charged.

- An event recorded before the Procedure's invoice is approved travels with the Procedure and, where possible, is a line on its invoice.
- An event recorded after the invoice is raised generates a [separate invoice](US-08.6.1), traceable to the Procedure, in the next run.

Every event goes through the same standard review step: one process for everything.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a billable event with the Procedure's billable party, recorded before the Procedure's invoice is approved, then it is a line on that invoice.
- Given a billable event recorded after the invoice was raised, then a separate invoice is raised in the next run, traceable to the Procedure.
- Given a Contract with a fixed fee for the event, then the fee is charged instead of the time.
- Given an event not yet through the review step, then it is not invoiced.
- Given an event whose invoice tick box is cleared, then it is not invoiced.

## Notes

Anaesthetists may submit at once and wash up post-ops in the next round (Donald; Greg: "They're very do it now"). The rule covers both that and holding back.

"If possible" (OQ-63): our reading is that an event billed to a party other than the Procedure's cannot be a line on the Procedure's invoice, so it gets its own invoice.
