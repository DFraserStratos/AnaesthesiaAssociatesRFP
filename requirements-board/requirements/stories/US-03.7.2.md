A billing [event](US-03.7.1) is a free-form way to invoice on a Procedure and its Contract: a charge with a free-text description and an amount, or a credit. It is raised on the Procedure and sits alongside the [additional invoice](US-08.6.3). It is its own line item, attached to the Procedure.

- Recorded before the Procedure's invoice is approved: it goes on the same invoice where it can.
- Recorded after the invoice has gone: it is invoiced separately in the next run, [traceable to the Procedure](US-08.6.1).

Every event goes through the same standard review step: one process for everything.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a billing event with a description and an amount, then it is invoiced as its own line.
- Given a credit event, then it reduces what is owed and is traceable to the Procedure.
- Given a billable event with the Procedure's billable party, recorded before the Procedure's invoice is approved, then it is a line on that invoice.
- Given a billable event recorded after the invoice was raised, then a separate invoice is raised in the next run, traceable to the Procedure.
- Given an event not yet through the review step, then it is not invoiced.
- Given an event whose invoice tick box is cleared, then it is not invoiced.
