A billing [event](US-03.7.1) is a free-form way to invoice on a Procedure and its Contract: a charge with a free-text description and an amount, or a credit. It is raised on the Procedure and sits alongside the [additional invoice](US-08.6.3). It is its own line item, attached to the Procedure.

An event marked for invoicing is billed in the next billing run and is never held for the Procedure's own invoice. Where it can go on the same invoice as other lines for the same billable party in that run, it does; otherwise it is an invoice of its own, [traceable to the Procedure](US-08.6.1). There are no business rules by kind of event.

Every event goes through the same standard review step: one process for everything.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a billing event with a description and an amount, then it is invoiced as its own line.
- Given a credit event, then it reduces what is owed and is traceable to the Procedure.
- **Next run.** Given a billable event through the review step, when the next billing run is made, then the event is billed in it, whether or not the Procedure's own invoice is in that run.
- **Bundled where possible.** Given a billable event and other lines for the same billable party in the same run, then the event is a line on that invoice.
- **On its own.** Given a billable event with no other line for its billable party in the run, then it is an invoice of its own, traceable to the Procedure.
- Given an event not yet through the review step, then it is not invoiced.
- Given an event whose invoice tick box is cleared, then it is not invoiced.

## Notes

Anaesthetists may submit at once and wash up post-ops in the next round (Donald; Greg: "They're very do it now"). The rule covers both that and holding back.

An event billed to a party other than the Procedure's is never a line on the Procedure's invoice: it joins other lines for its own billable party in the run, or is an invoice of its own (OQ-121).

A credit event is a credit raised through the [credit note option](US-08.6.5), which also reverses the linked payable to the anaesthetist; it shows in the Procedure's [events](US-03.7.3).
