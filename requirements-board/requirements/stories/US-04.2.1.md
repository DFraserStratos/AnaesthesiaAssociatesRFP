Every Contract belongs to one [contract holder](US-04.1.5):

- an insurer
- a hospital
- a surgeon or a surgeon's rooms
- an anaesthetist, for their own price list

AA itself holds [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3).

The Contract decides who is billed:

- its holder, when the holder pays AA itself: an insurer accepting direct claims, or a hospital
- the [payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), when the holder only sets the price (a surgeon's fixed-fee arrangement, an anaesthetist's own list), and for No contract (RVG)

There can be as many Contracts as AA needs (OQ-67).

Where a Contract applies is the context that narrows the [Contract list](US-04.3.2) for every user, admins and anaesthetists alike:

- the List's hospital
- the surgeon or surgeon's rooms
- the anaesthetist or surgeon a Contract belongs to: such a Contract is offered only on that person's Bookings

This is so each Contract both prices the procedure and settles who receives the invoice, and every user sees only the Contracts relevant to the Booking.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Contract whose holder pays AA, when it is billed, then the holder's billable party is invoiced.
- Given a Contract whose holder only sets the price, or No contract (RVG), when it is billed, then the payer named on the Booking is invoiced.
- Given a Contract that belongs to an anaesthetist, then it is offered only on its own anaesthetist's Bookings.
- Given a List at one hospital, then another hospital's Contracts are not offered.

## Technical discussion

Greg wants the billable party held as a reference to a billable party record (one table of hospitals, surgeons, insurers and patients or guardians), with a setting saying whether the holder is the billable party, yes or no. No invoice email is stored on the Contract. The technical design v6 holds this on the contract holder: whether it is the billable party, a link to its billable party record, and an optional practitioner ([holder fields](AR-29#contract-holder-fields), [billable party](AR-29#billable-party)).

## Notes

Merges the former US-04.2.9 (Organisational and individual Contracts), now Retired. An individual anaesthetist's own arrangement is now a Contract that belongs to them. The office creates and maintains anaesthetists' own Contracts, from the price list each anaesthetist supplies (OQ-91, answered 8 October).

There are no hospital, insurer or procedure default Contracts. Most insurer and hospital Contracts are plain RVG Contracts billed to their holder.

The insurer and approval number are stored on the Booking ([US-11.4.3](US-11.4.3)); whether insurance narrows the Contract list is open (OQ-93).

How a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement is found for a Booking is OQ-105.
