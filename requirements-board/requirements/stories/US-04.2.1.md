Every Contract is held by one [Party](US-04.1.5), says whether it bills its holder or the [Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2), and says where it is offered. Where it is offered narrows the [Contract list](US-04.3.2) for every user, admins and anaesthetists alike.

This is so each Contract settles who receives the invoice, and every user sees only the Contracts relevant to the Booking.

Who is billed:

- one holder can hold Contracts of both kinds
- a Contract held by AA or an anaesthetist always bills the payer, and so does a prepaid Contract (OQ-73)

Where it is offered: a Contract can be linked to any number of surgeons and at most one anaesthetist, and is offered on their Lists.

- a hospital's Contract carries no links and is offered only on that hospital's Lists
- a Contract held by a surgeon, surgeon group or anaesthetist needs at least one link
- an unlinked Contract held by AA, an insurer or a funder is offered on every List
- in every case its [specialties](US-04.2.16) must match

There can be as many Contracts as AA needs.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Bills its holder.** Given a Contract set to bill its holder, when it is billed, then the holder Party is invoiced.
- **Bills the payer.** Given a Contract set to bill the payer, or [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3), when it is billed, then the payer on the Procedure is invoiced.
- **Anaesthetist's own.** Given a Contract that belongs to an anaesthetist, then it is offered only on its own anaesthetist's Bookings.
- **Other hospital.** Given a List at one hospital, then another hospital's Contracts are not offered.
- **Linked surgeons.** Given a Contract linked to surgeons S and T, then it is offered on a Booking with either surgeon, and not on another surgeon's Booking.
- **Unlinked insurer.** Given NIB's unlinked Contract with no specialties, then it is offered on every List.
- **Link required.** Given a surgeon-held Contract with no linked surgeon, when it is saved, then it is refused.

## Technical discussion

Greg wants the billable party held as a reference to a billable party record (one table of hospitals, surgeons, insurers and patients or guardians), with a setting saying whether the holder is the billable party, yes or no. No invoice email is stored on the Contract.

Bills holder or payer is a setting of each Contract (D2), not of the holder as in Greg's technical design v6.
