The office can flag a Procedure to confirm its price with the hospital. While the flag is open, the patient is not invoiced for that Procedure, and the flag shows at [office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so a patient is not invoiced and then refunded when a hospital has brought in a contract that AA does not hold yet.

The flag is for a Procedure the hospital says is under contract but AA has no Contract for. Until the hospital answers, the Procedure is priced on the hospital's [open-price fallback](US-04.3.9). When the hospital answers, the office moves the Procedure to the right Contract and clears the flag.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Flag.** Given a Procedure on a hospital's open-price fallback, when the office flags it to confirm with the hospital, then the flag shows at office review.
- **Held.** Given an open flag, when the List is invoiced, then the patient is not invoiced for that Procedure.
- **Fixed price confirmed.** Given the hospital confirms a fixed price, then the office creates a fixed-price Contract held by the hospital with its [funding tag](US-04.2.17), moves the Procedure to it and clears the flag.
- **No fixed price.** Given the hospital confirms there is no fixed price, then the office moves the Procedure to [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3) or the hospital's RVG Contract and clears the flag.
