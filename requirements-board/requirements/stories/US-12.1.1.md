Each anaesthetist sets their own [dollar value per unit](US-05.2.1), their unit rate: the Unit as specified in the RVG. The system stores it per anaesthetist, never as a price list shared across anaesthetists.

A later change to the unit rate never alters an [issued invoice](US-08.4.4).

Greg, 28 October: "Anaesthetist rate and unit value, that's actually the same thing. It's just the anaesthetist unit rate."
