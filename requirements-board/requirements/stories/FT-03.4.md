The office [sets the Contract on each Procedure](US-04.3.4) when the Booking is set up, on the [step rail](US-04.3.8). The anaesthetist sees it and can change it, with the RVG code and, where the Contract allows, the price, until the List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), not only until they [submit it](US-07.1.1); the office approves the choice at [review](US-07.2.2) (OQ-26).

This is so the Contract, which decides who is billed and how the price is worked out, is set early by the office and can still be corrected by the anaesthetist who did the work.

Neither the office nor the anaesthetist outranks the other: the last change stands, and every change is audited and seen at review.
