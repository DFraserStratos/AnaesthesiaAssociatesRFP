Admin normally [sets the Contract when the Booking is set up](US-04.3.4). It is visible to the anaesthetist, who may change it; admin then approves the selection during the [SUBMITTED review](US-07.2.2), after the anaesthetist [submits the List](US-07.1.1) (OQ-26). The anaesthetist can therefore change the Contract freely until they submit.

The anaesthetist can change the procedure as well as the Contract until they submit, with no reason needed. The office sees the change at review.
