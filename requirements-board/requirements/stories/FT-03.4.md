Admin [sets the Contract when the Booking is set up](US-04.3.4): the holder, the Contract and, where the Contract has lines, the line. Setting the Contract is mandatory, and a Booking without one stays on the admin list; the line or RVG code may be left blank for the anaesthetist (OQ-99). It is visible to the anaesthetist, who may change it; admin then approves the selection during the [SUBMITTED review](US-07.2.2) (OQ-26).

The anaesthetist can change the Contract, and the RVG code, until the List is AUTHORISED, not only until they [submit it](US-07.1.1): once it is authorised it is handed to the Billing/Invoice Engine. Submitting still matters: it moves the List out of the anaesthetist's way and tells the office it has work to do.

Neither the office nor the anaesthetist outranks the other: the last change stands, and every change is audited and seen at review. The procedure itself is no longer something either of them picks: it is the text that came in ([kept as received](US-02.5.7)).
