Payments to anaesthetists run on a weekly cycle:

- each accounting week is named by its ISO week number (for example "week 46")
- on Friday the ledger is closed, snapshotted and reconciled against the bank downloads
- Monday is a working day for the office to sort out problems before payment
- on Tuesday the payment schedule of [released payables](US-10.2.1) goes to the accountant for payment
- payment anomalies are handled in the office and rolled into the next cycle

This gives AA 52 cycles a year, each identifiable by its week number, so it is easy to say which payment happened when, and a maintenance day to make sure everything is right before payment.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a payment, when the office looks it up, then it is shown against the week number of the cycle it was paid in.
- Given an anomaly found during the Monday check, when it cannot be fixed before Tuesday, then it is held out of this cycle's payment schedule and rolled into the next cycle.
