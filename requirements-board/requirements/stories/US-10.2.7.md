Payments to anaesthetists run on a weekly cycle:

- each accounting week is named by its ISO week number (for example "week 46")
- on Friday the ledger is closed, snapshotted and reconciled against the bank downloads
- Monday is a working day for the office to sort out problems before payment
- on Tuesday the payment schedule of [released payables](US-10.2.1) goes to the accountant for payment
- payment anomalies are handled in the office and rolled into the next cycle

This is so AA has 52 cycles a year, each identifiable by its week number, so it is easy to say which payment happened when, and a maintenance day to make sure everything is right before payment.

The office runs the cycle from the Payments tab of the [Finances section](US-13.3.3): the week's steps, what is waiting for the run, Close the week and the steps after it, the events awaiting the run, and every earlier payment run. Each payment run has its own page under the Payments tab, with each anaesthetist's [remittance advice](US-10.2.6) under it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Week number.** Given a payment, when the office looks it up, then it is shown against the week number of the cycle it was paid in.
- **Anomaly rolled over.** Given an anomaly found during the Monday check, when it cannot be fixed before Tuesday, then it is held out of this cycle's payment schedule and rolled into the next cycle.
- **Run page.** Given a past payment run on the Payments tab, when an admin opens it, then its page shows the run, its week number and each anaesthetist's remittance advice.
