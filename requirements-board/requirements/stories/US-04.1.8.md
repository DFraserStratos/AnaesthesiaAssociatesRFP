When a Contract's rate, discount or another setting must change only from a date, the office replaces it from that date in one action. The action creates a new Contract copied from the old one, with any setting changed, the settings fixed at creation included. It lists the Procedures dated on or after that date still on the old Contract, and moves the ones the office ticks to the new one. The office then either leaves the old Contract active, with a to-do on that date reminding it to disable it, or disables it straight away.

This is so a dated change is one step for the office, with nothing to forget, while every Procedure that moves is the office's own choice.

- Nothing moves by itself: only the Procedures the office ticks move, each as an audited change to that Procedure. A Procedure on an [authorised List](US-07.3.1) is locked and is not listed.
- When the office disables the old Contract after the date, the disable lists the Procedures dated from the date of the change still on it, not only those from that day on.
- Only a fixed price follows the calendar, through its [price ranges](US-04.2.15). A replacement priced any other way is offered from the day it is saved, and the action says so; until the old Contract is disabled, both are offered.
- A fixed price changing from a date needs no replacement: the office [adds a price range](US-04.2.15).
- A system Contract cannot be replaced.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Copied.** Given Unisports Ortho at $93.47 per unit, when the office replaces it from 1 January at $97.00 per unit, then a new Contract with a new AA code holds every other setting of the old one.
- **Listed and ticked.** Given three Procedures dated on or after 1 January on the old Contract, then the action lists all three, and only the ones ticked move to the new Contract.
- **Locked left out.** Given a Procedure on the old Contract on an authorised List, then it is not listed and does not move.
- **Old one left active.** Given the office leaves the old Contract active, then on 1 January a to-do reminds the office to disable it, and the old Contract stays offered until it does.
- **Late disable.** Given the office disables the old Contract on 8 January, and a Procedure dated 5 January was put on it, then the disable lists that Procedure.
- **Old one disabled.** Given the office chooses to disable it now, then the old Contract is disabled as it is replaced, and Procedures already on it keep it.
- **Not a fixed price.** Given a replacement priced at a rate, then the action says the new Contract is offered from today, not from 1 January.

## Technical discussion

Whether a Contract could be disabled from a future date, so the old rate stops being offered on the day of the change, is OQ-144. Without it, the to-do on the date stands in.
