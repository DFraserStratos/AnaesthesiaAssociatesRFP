The anaesthetist can change the Contract on a Procedure until the List is AUTHORISED, choosing from the Contracts [already filtered](US-04.3.2) to that Booking. No explanation is needed; [every change is audited](US-02.5.5) and [flagged for office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so the anaesthetist can bill for what actually happened. The common case, which happens regularly: the hospital cannot get insurance pre-approval on the day, so the anaesthetist changes the Contract to [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3) and the patient is billed.

Changing the Contract clears any price typed under the old one. Recorded RVG detail is kept, and the RVG code fills from the new Contract's default only when it is empty.

The anaesthetist cannot move a Procedure onto or off a prepaid Contract; only the office can.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No reason needed.** Given a List not yet authorised, when the anaesthetist changes a Procedure's Contract, then it saves without an explanation.
- **Reset.** Given a changed Contract, then a typed price is cleared and the recorded RVG detail is kept.
- **Audited and flagged.** Every change is audited and flagged at office review.
- **Pre-approval falls through.** Given a Procedure on an insurer's Contract, when the anaesthetist changes it to No contract · RVG, then the patient, or [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), is billed.
- **Prepaid.** Given a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, then the anaesthetist cannot change its Contract; and when they change any other Procedure's Contract, no prepaid Contract is offered.
