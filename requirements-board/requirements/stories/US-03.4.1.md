The anaesthetist can change the Contract on a Procedure until the List is AUTHORISED, choosing from the Contracts [already filtered](US-04.3.2) to the Contracts relevant to that booking. No explanation is needed.

Changing the Contract resets the Procedure's price, rate and discount to the new Contract's: anything entered under the old Contract is cleared. Here "Contract" means the Contract and its line.

[Every change is audited](US-02.5.5), and [flagged for office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so the anaesthetist can bill for what actually happened. The common case: the hospital cannot get insurance pre-approval on the day and the patient pays, so the anaesthetist changes the Contract to [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3) and the patient is billed. This happens regularly.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No reason needed.** Given a List not yet authorised, when the anaesthetist changes a Procedure's Contract, then it saves without an explanation.
- **Reset.** Given a changed Contract, then the price, rate and discount are the new Contract's, and anything entered before is cleared.
- **Audited and flagged.** Every change is audited and flagged at office review.
- **Pre-approval falls through.** Given a Procedure on an insurer's Contract, when the anaesthetist changes it to No contract (RVG), then the patient, or [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2), is billed.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 lets the anaesthetist change the contract, the RVG code, and the price, rate or discount where permitted, with no reason; the change is audited and flagged at office review, and on any change the pricing values refresh ([contract selection](AR-29#contract-selection)). The v6 text says "before the list is submitted"; the catalogue follows Donald and Greg on 16 October (until AUTHORISED). The diagram AR-24, redrawn on 19 October, shows the window until AUTHORISED.
