The system prices RVG units at each [anaesthetist's own dollar value per unit](US-12.1.1) by default, never a price list shared across anaesthetists. [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3) carries no practice-wide prices.

This applies wherever the Procedure's Contract does not set a fixed fee or its own rate.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Fixed rate.** When a Procedure's Contract sets a [fixed rate](US-05.2.6), the system uses it instead of the anaesthetist's own unit value.
- **Fixed discount.** When a Procedure's Contract sets a [fixed discount](US-05.4.3), the system applies it to the price calculated at the anaesthetist's own unit value.

## Notes

Merges the former US-05.2.4 (Contract rate or discount), now Retired. Greg (2026-10-02): "there's no provision for any grouping or clustering of that, it's just one to one... it's not only a default, it's mandated"; Donald read it as applying to default Contracts and those that don't set their own rate. The directors confirmed on 6 October that some Contracts have a fixed rate, used in place of the anaesthetist's, which bears out Donald's reading (OQ-89).

Greg, 28 October: the Commerce Act claim was too bold. Anaesthetists may not collude with each other, but AA does accept price offers from vendors, such as ACC's negotiated rates; those are Contracts like any other, and the anaesthetist chooses whether to work under them.
