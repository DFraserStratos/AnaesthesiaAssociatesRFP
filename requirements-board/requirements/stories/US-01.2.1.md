From their app, an anaesthetist sets the availability of each of their Slots. A Slot is free by default, and the anaesthetist changes it, for example to on holiday, unavailable or public.

This is so the office sees the anaesthetist's availability immediately.

Public marks a session the anaesthetist works at the public hospital. It works like Unavailable: the Slot is not open for bookings and carries no List detail (no surgeon, hospital, theatre or kind of session). The anaesthetist can add a short note to it, such as "CPH". An anaesthetist who gives up a public session for private work changes the Slot back to Free.

The office can also set a Slot's status for the anaesthetist.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Public.** Given an anaesthetist sets their Tuesday AM Slot to Public with the note "CPH", then the Slot is not open for bookings and the office sees "Public" with the note.
- **Back to Free.** Given a Public Slot, when the anaesthetist changes it to Free, then it is open for bookings again.
- **Office sets it.** Given an admin, when they set an anaesthetist's Slot to Public, then the anaesthetist sees it in their app.
