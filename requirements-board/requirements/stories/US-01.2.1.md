From their app, an anaesthetist sets the availability of each of their Slots. A Slot is free by default, and the anaesthetist changes it, for example to on holiday or unavailable.

This is so the office sees the anaesthetist's availability immediately.

## Notes

Owner decision for now (OQ-64): start from the values already in use (free by default, on holiday, unavailable, the OQ-17 examples), with colours chosen for now, held as a user-maintained list ([Slot status master data](US-01.2.2)) so they can change without code. The final values and their colours are to be settled with AA's users, who have "a lot of redundant statuses" (Greg). The RFP lists private, public, pre-op, holiday, unavailable and free as candidates.

Greg: there are several versions of unavailable (unavailable, public hospital, annual leave), "all of which have the same effect... it's for information".
