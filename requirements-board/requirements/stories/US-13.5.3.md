Anaesthetists sign in to their app with an account login. The working assumption is that anaesthetists get a PWA first, which needs an account login.

- Stratos' default identity provider is Auth0, which handles passwords and MFA.
- Higher Auth0 tiers allow single sign-on with, for example, a Google account.
- Biometric sign-in (Face ID, fingerprint) is available to a native app but probably not to a standard PWA.

The platforms to support are defined first, then the sign-in experience.

This is so it is clear what an anaesthetist meets when they open the app on their phone.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an anaesthetist with an account, when they open the PWA, then they sign in with their account login.

## Notes

Greg asked "what's the experience going to be when the anaesthetist brings up the app on the phone?" Nothing was decided beyond an account login for the PWA. Platform, biometrics, MFA, single sign-on and identity provider are OQ-83. The feature's Technical discussion holds the RFP response proposal (Auth0 or Microsoft Entra, MFA for admins, self-service password reset, logged sign-in attempts, social login on mobile); it is not confirmed.
