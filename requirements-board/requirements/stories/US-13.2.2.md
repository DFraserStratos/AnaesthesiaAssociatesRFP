Admins see a patient's [outstanding balance](US-11.3.1) from their profile, the same balance described under Patients and billable parties (EP-11).

This is attached to the patient, even if it's a guardian who is going to pay.

## Notes

Greg: balances are always shown as attributable to the patient, whoever the billable party is ("a patient-centric view"), and the same holds for an insurance claim. The description names only a guardian.
