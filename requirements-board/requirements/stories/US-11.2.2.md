Every Booking names a payer: the person invoiced whenever a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder ([No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), or [a holder that only sets the price](US-04.2.1)).

- The payer's name, email and postal address are filled in from the patient's details automatically.
- The office or the anaesthetist can change them to someone else who pays: a parent, a guardian, or another party such as a benefactor or a family trust.

The payer must have an email or a postal address ([payer contact required](US-11.2.3)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a new Booking, then its payer is filled in with the patient's name, email and postal address.
- Given a Booking for a child under No contract (RVG), when the office or the anaesthetist changes the payer to a guardian's name and email, then the invoice goes to the guardian and the price is unchanged.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, then the payer on the Booking is not invoiced for it.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 holds the patient's contact details on the booking procedure as the default billable party, with optional payer contact details and an "alternate payer" flag, set by the office ([booking procedure fields](AR-29#booking-procedure-fields)). v6 shows these fields as set by the admin team only, while the catalogue keeps the anaesthetist able to change the payer (OQ-54); this difference is unresolved.

## Notes
