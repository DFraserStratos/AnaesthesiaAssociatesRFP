Every Booking names a payer: the person invoiced whenever a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder ([No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), or [any Contract set to bill the payer](US-04.2.1)).

- The payer's name, email and postal address are filled in from the patient's details automatically.
- The office or the anaesthetist can change them to someone else who pays: a parent, a guardian, or another party such as a benefactor or a family trust.

The payer must have an email or a postal address ([payer contact required](US-11.2.3)).

This is so the invoice reaches whoever pays in the patient's place, such as a child's parent.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a new Booking, then its payer is filled in with the patient's name, email and postal address.
- Given a Booking for a child under No contract · RVG, when the office or the anaesthetist changes the payer to a guardian's name and email, then the invoice goes to the guardian and the price is unchanged.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract that bills its holder, then the payer on the Booking is not invoiced for it.

## Technical discussion

Greg's technical design v6 holds the patient's contact details on the booking procedure as the default billable party, with optional payer contact details and an "alternate payer" flag. v6 has only the admin team setting these fields, while the catalogue keeps the anaesthetist able to change the payer (OQ-54); this difference is unresolved.

## Notes

This replaces the earlier reading that the payer is set through the Contract, by entering the name and email when picking a default Contract (OQ-67). The payer, a guardian included, is now held on the Booking (OQ-54). Greg (1 October): a guardian's details last for the life of the debt and are then archived.

The screenshots show the prototype's earlier per-Booking payer override, which is close to this; check them against the payer on the Booking when they are next captured.

Greg, 28 October, would hold this as a "different payer" tick, using the patient's details otherwise, rather than copying every patient's details onto the Booking (v6 has an alternate payer flag). Some patients have no email "and they want hard mail": how posted invoices are produced is OQ-122.
