Every Booking names a payer: the person invoiced whenever a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder ([No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), or a holder that only sets the price).

- The payer's name and email are filled in from the patient's details automatically.
- The office or the anaesthetist can change them to a parent, guardian or other person who pays.

A hospital or insurer taking the invoice is not a payer on the Booking: it is a Contract whose [holder](US-04.2.1) pays AA, for example Christchurch Eye taking the invoice instead of the patient.

This covers cases where the person responsible for payment is not the patient themselves, such as a guardian paying for a child.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a new Booking, then its payer is filled in with the patient's name and email.
- Given a Booking for a child under No contract (RVG), when the office or the anaesthetist changes the payer to a guardian's name and email, then the invoice goes to the guardian and the price is unchanged.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, then the payer on the Booking is not invoiced for it.

## Technical discussion

The [draft design](AR-29#p6) holds the payer's name and email on the booking procedure, prefilled from the patient and editable by the office or the anaesthetist. Greg (7 October) wants every billable party, patients and guardians included alongside hospitals and surgeons, held in one reference table that records point to, with no invoice email embedded in the Contract. Neither is settled: the design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
