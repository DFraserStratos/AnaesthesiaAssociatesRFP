Each Procedure names a payer: the person invoiced whenever that Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder ([No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), or [any Contract set to bill the payer](US-04.2.1)). The payer is filled in from the patient, and the office or the anaesthetist can name someone else who pays instead, so two Procedures on one Booking can bill two different people.

This is so the invoice reaches whoever pays in the patient's place, such as a child's parent, and one place on each Procedure answers who pays.

- A new Procedure's payer is the patient, with the patient's name, email and postal address filled in automatically.
- A "different payer" tick names someone else who pays: a parent, a guardian, or another party such as a benefactor or a family trust.
- Changing one Procedure's payer offers the same change on the Booking's other Procedures that bill the same payer, each ticked by default. The user can untick any, and is never made to change them.
- A Procedure added to the Booking later, such as an extra procedure or a post-op addendum, takes the payer of the Booking's Procedures that bill the payer when they all share one; otherwise it starts from the patient.
- A Procedure whose Contract bills its holder never invoices its payer.

The payer is shown and changed in the "Paid by" line of the Procedure's billing details, and only where its Contract bills the payer: the payer's name, their relationship to the patient, and any warning ("Email or postal address needed", "Under 18"). The email and postal address show where the payer is changed. Before a Contract is chosen, the Procedure's payer reads as "not billed yet". There is no separate payer section for the Booking as a whole.

The payer must have an email or a postal address ([payer contact required](US-11.2.3)), and a patient under 18 left as the payer is [warned](US-11.2.4).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Filled from the patient.** Given a new Procedure, then its payer is the patient, with the patient's name, email and postal address.
- **Guardian pays.** Given a Procedure for a child under No contract · RVG, when the office or the anaesthetist changes its payer to a guardian's name and email, then the invoice goes to the guardian and the price is unchanged.
- **Change offered to the others.** Given a Booking with two Procedures that bill the patient as payer, when the payer on one is changed, then the system offers the same change on the other, ticked; when the user unticks it, then the other Procedure keeps the patient as its payer.
- **Procedure added later.** Given a Booking whose Procedures that bill the payer all name the same guardian, when a Procedure is added, then its payer is the guardian; given they name different payers, then the new Procedure's payer is the patient.
- **Holder billed.** Given a Procedure under a Contract that bills its holder, then its payer is not invoiced for it and its "Paid by" line does not show the payer.
- **Not billed yet.** Given a Procedure with no Contract chosen, then its payer reads as "not billed yet".

## Technical discussion

Greg's technical design v6 held the payer fields on BOOKING_PROCEDURE, as the catalogue now does: the patient's contact details as the default billable party, with optional payer contact details and an "alternate payer" flag. v6 has only the admin team setting these fields, while the catalogue keeps the anaesthetist able to change the payer (OQ-54); this difference is unresolved.

A payer copied to another Procedure through the offered change is the same payer, so those Procedures [share one invoice](US-08.2.1); a payer typed in separately is a different payer, never matched on name or email.
