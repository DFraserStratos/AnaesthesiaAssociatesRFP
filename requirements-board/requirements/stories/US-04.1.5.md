The office keeps a Party record for everyone who holds Contracts. Each records the short name shown on the [step rail](US-04.3.8), the billing name printed on its invoices, its email or postal address, and how its invoices are delivered: email or post for now (other outputs are OQ-114).

This is so one record holds the billing details of everyone AA invoices other than [a Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2).

The Parties are kept on their own tab of the Contracts section, grouped by kind, including Parties that hold no Contracts, since an insurer or funder is also chosen as a Booking's funder. Each Party has a page showing its details, edited in place, and its Contracts counted by [funding tag](US-04.2.17), each count opening the Contract list at those Contracts.

A Party is one of:

- AA (exactly one)
- a hospital, pointing at its hospital master record
- an insurer (NIB, Southern Cross Health Society)
- a funder (ACC, Health NZ, NZDF, Veterans Affairs)
- a surgeon or surgeon group
- an anaesthetist, for their own price list

Its billing name and delivery details are required only once it holds a Contract that bills its holder. Whether a Party is billed, and which surgeons or anaesthetist a Contract is offered for, are set on each Contract ([who is billed](US-04.2.1)), not on the Party. AA's rooms is never a Party, and the patient, a guardian or anyone else who pays for a Procedure is held as that Procedure's payer, not as a Party.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Billing details when billed.** Given a Party holding a Contract that bills its holder, when it is saved without a billing name or delivery method, then it is refused.
- **Contact for delivery.** Given delivery by email, an email is required; by post, a postal address.
- **Hospital Party.** Given a hospital Party, then it names its hospital.
- **One AA.** There is exactly one AA Party.
- **Grouped by kind.** Given the Parties tab, then Parties show under their kind, and an insurer that holds no Contracts is still listed.
- **Contracts by tag.** Given Christchurch Eye Surgery holds 56 SXAP, 62 HNZ and 26 ACC Contracts, then its page shows those three counts, and opening SXAP lists its 56 SXAP Contracts.

## Technical discussion

Insurer and funder as two kinds of Party is our reading (AR-58 A42); the separate insurer records and the old holder organisation records fold into Party (AR-58 A24).
