The office keeps a Party record for everyone who holds Contracts. Each records the short name shown on the [step rail](US-04.3.8), the billing name printed on its invoices, its email or postal address, and how its invoices are delivered: email or post for now (other outputs are OQ-114).

This is so one record holds the billing details of everyone AA invoices other than the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2).

A Party is one of:

- AA (exactly one)
- a hospital, pointing at its hospital master record
- an insurer (NIB, Southern Cross Health Society)
- a funder (ACC, Health NZ, NZDF, Veterans Affairs)
- a surgeon or surgeon group
- an anaesthetist, for their own price list

Its billing name and delivery details are required only once it holds a Contract that bills its holder. Whether a Party is billed, and which surgeons or anaesthetist a Contract is offered for, are set on each Contract ([who is billed](US-04.2.1)), not on the Party. AA's rooms is never a Party.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Billing details when billed.** Given a Party holding a Contract that bills its holder, when it is saved without a billing name or delivery method, then it is refused.
- **Contact for delivery.** Given delivery by email, an email is required; by post, a postal address.
- **Hospital Party.** Given a hospital Party, then it names its hospital.
- **One AA.** There is exactly one AA Party.

## Technical discussion

Insurer and funder as two kinds of Party is our reading (AR-58 A42); the separate insurer records and the old holder organisation records fold into Party (AR-58 A24).
