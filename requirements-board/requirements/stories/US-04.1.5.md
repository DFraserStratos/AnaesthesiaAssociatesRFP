The office keeps a record for every contract holder, the party a Contract belongs to:

- an insurer
- a hospital
- a surgeon (a holder may be named for the surgeon's rooms, but its Contracts are applied to individual surgeons, one or more)
- an anaesthetist, for their own price list

AA itself holds [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3).

For each holder it records:

- whether AA invoices the holder: if so, its billable party record (required; one billable party may serve more than one holder, which is unusual); if not, the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is billed
- optionally, the practitioners its Contracts belong to, by practitioner ID: one anaesthetist, or one or more surgeons, so they are [offered only on those people's Bookings](US-04.3.2)
- how its invoices are delivered: an emailed invoice or some other output, such as a portal or an API for an insurer (what the other outputs are is OQ-114)

A holder can hold many Contracts.

A surgeon's fixed-price arrangement is one Contract linked to every surgeon it covers, not one Contract per surgeon.

This is because staff identify the holder early when narrowing the Contract list, and the holder's record decides whether the holder or the payer on the Booking is invoiced.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a holder that pays AA, when it is saved, then it must have a billable party.
- Given a holder linked to an anaesthetist, then its Contracts are offered only on that anaesthetist's Bookings.
- Given a holder linked to a surgeon, then its Contracts are offered only on that surgeon's Bookings.
- Given a surgeon's arrangement that covers surgeons S and T, when the office sets it up, then it is one Contract linked to both surgeons by practitioner ID, offered on Bookings with either of them and on no other surgeon's.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 calls this the contract holder ([fields](AR-29#contract-holder-fields), [ERD](AR-30#contract-holder)): whether it is the billable party, a link to the billable party record, and an optional practitioner (anaesthetist or surgeon). It holds one practitioner; linking one Contract to several surgeons goes beyond it.

## Notes

Greg, 14 October, said a hospital holder is always the billable party and an anaesthetist or a surgeon's rooms never is. This is not kept as a rule: whether a holder is billed is set on each holder. OQ-105 answered 28 October: a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement is held against the individual surgeon, not the rooms, and the office picks it from the Contract list filtered by practitioner ID. Holding Contracts by rooms is not a requirement. Donald, 29 October: one Contract can be linked to many surgeons; there is not one Contract per surgeon.

Greg, 28 October: insurers are "just an instance of contract holders"; there is no separate insurer master list or accepts direct claims flag, and most of the insurer feature goes. A contract holder that nominates a billable party uses that party's details for the invoice.
