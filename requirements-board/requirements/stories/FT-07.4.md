Once the office has authorised a List, the List drops off the anaesthetist's main view and its invoices show as lines in their [outstanding balances](US-12.2.1). The anaesthetist can still find the List afterwards by [search](US-03.1.6).

This is so the main view holds the work still in progress, while the anaesthetist can still go back to finished work when an invoice is queried months later.
