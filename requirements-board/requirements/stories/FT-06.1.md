Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or [whole RVG groups](US-05.1.3), require prepayment, as a setting on their own profile.

This is because it's their own patients and their own practice that decide which procedures get paid for upfront.

## Notes

The trigger stays on the anaesthetist's profile, which the pricing guide lists as already agreed: "Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or whole RVG groups, they want prepaid." On 7 October Greg argued for a pre-payable flag on the Contract instead ("the flag belongs with the contract, not with the anaesthetist"). In the end prepayment comes from selecting the [anaesthetist's own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14), and a flag on the Contract is not adopted (OQ-25).
