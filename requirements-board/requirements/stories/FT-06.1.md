Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or [whole RVG groups](US-05.1.3), require prepayment, as a setting on their own profile.

This is because it's their own patients and their own practice that decide which procedures get paid for upfront.

## Notes

Until 16 October the trigger was the anaesthetist's profile, which the pricing guide listed as already agreed: "Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or whole RVG groups, they want prepaid." On 7 October Greg argued for a pre-payable flag on the Contract instead ("the flag belongs with the contract, not with the anaesthetist"). At that point prepayment came from selecting the anaesthetist's own price list Contract, and a flag on the Contract was not adopted (OQ-25).

Retired 16 October: prepayment is no longer a setting on the anaesthetist's profile. Donald: "we no longer have that concept"; Greg: "There's no association between RVG codes and prepay." The trigger is a Contract flagged as prepaid ([prepayment trigger](FT-06.2)).

The children US-06.1.1 and US-06.1.2 retire with it.
