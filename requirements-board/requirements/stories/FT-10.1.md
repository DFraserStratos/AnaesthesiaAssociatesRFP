Hospital contract holders used to pay some anaesthetists directly, with a remittance advice sent to AA to reconcile afterwards. That direct-payment pathway is retired.

Every payment now follows one path: the billable party pays AA, and AA disburses to the anaesthetist. This means the system only ever has one payment destination to model, not two.
