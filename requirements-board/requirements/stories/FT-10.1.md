Every payment follows one path: the billable party pays AA, and AA disburses to the anaesthetist. No hospital pays an anaesthetist directly.

This is so the system has only one payment destination to model, not two.

## Notes

This retires the direct-payment pathway, where some hospitals paid anaesthetists directly and sent AA a remittance advice to reconcile afterwards.
