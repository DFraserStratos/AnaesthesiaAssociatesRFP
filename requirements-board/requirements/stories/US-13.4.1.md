Admins maintain all of the system's reference data, in one place:

- hospitals, each with a contact email for booking updates
- [surgeons](US-13.6.2) and surgeon groups
- [surgeons' rooms](US-13.6.1)
- [insurers](US-11.4.1)
- anaesthetists
- [Slot availability statuses](US-01.2.2)
- [recurring bookings](US-01.3.2)
- the master (public holiday) calendar, and [each hospital's own holiday calendar](US-01.5.1)
- RVG codes and groups
- modifier codes
- [contract holders](US-04.1.5)
- Contracts

This is master data: the rest of the system refers to it by reference rather than copying it, so a change here is reflected everywhere it's used. Billing is the exception: a Contract change is a new dated version, and a Procedure keeps the pricing stored when its List was authorised, so a later change never alters an issued invoice.
