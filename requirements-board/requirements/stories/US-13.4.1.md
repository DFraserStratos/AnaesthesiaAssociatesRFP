Admins maintain all of the system's reference data, in one place:

- hospitals, each with a contact email for booking updates
- [surgeons](US-13.6.2) and surgeon groups
- [Parties](US-04.1.5), insurers and funders among them
- anaesthetists
- [Slot availability statuses](US-01.2.2)
- [recurring bookings](US-01.3.2)
- the master (public holiday) calendar, and [each hospital's own holiday calendar](US-01.5.1)
- RVG codes and groups
- modifier codes
- [specialties](US-13.4.4)
- [funding tags](US-13.4.5)
- Contracts, with their [price ranges](US-04.2.15)

This is so a change made here is reflected everywhere it is used: this is master data, and the rest of the system refers to it rather than copying it.

Billing is the exception: a Contract change is an audited edit, a fixed price changes through a price range, and a Procedure keeps the pricing stored when its List was authorised, so a later change never alters an issued invoice.
