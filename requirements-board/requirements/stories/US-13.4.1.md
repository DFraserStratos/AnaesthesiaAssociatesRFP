Admins maintain the system's reference data in the Admin app, in three places. Master data holds:

- hospitals, each with a contact email for booking updates, and [each hospital's own holiday calendar](US-01.5.1)
- the master (public holiday) calendar
- [Slot availability statuses](US-01.2.2)
- [recurring bookings](US-01.3.2)
- RVG codes and groups, and the RVG time rule
- modifier codes

The Contracts section holds, each on its own tab:

- Contracts, with their [price ranges](US-04.2.15)
- [Parties](US-04.1.5), insurers and funders among them
- [funding tags](US-13.4.5)

The People section holds, each on its own tab:

- anaesthetists
- [surgeons](US-13.6.2)
- [specialties](US-13.4.4)
- [patients](US-11.1.1), whose records come from Bookings rather than being added by hand

This is so a change made in any of them is reflected everywhere it is used: this is master data, and the rest of the system refers to it rather than copying it.

Each list in the People section can be searched, filtered and paged, and each anaesthetist, surgeon, specialty and patient has its own page, keyed by an identifier that never changes, where its details are changed one card at a time. Each saved card is its own audited change.

Billing is the exception: a Contract change is an audited edit, a fixed price changes through a price range, and a Procedure keeps the pricing stored when its List was authorised, so a later change never alters an issued invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Three places.** Given an admin opens the Admin app, then hospitals and their holidays are under Master data, Contracts, Parties and funding tags under Contracts, and anaesthetists, surgeons, specialties and patients under People.
- **No surgeon group record.** Given an admin looks through the reference data, then there is no surgeon group list to keep; a group that holds Contracts is kept as a Party.
- **Find a person.** Given the Surgeons list, when an admin searches by name and filters by specialty, then only the matching surgeons are shown, a page at a time.
- **One card, one change.** Given an admin edits one card on a surgeon's page and saves, then only that card's details change, and the audit records that one change.
