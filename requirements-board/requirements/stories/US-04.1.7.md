Every Contract has its own page in the Contracts section of the Admin app, where the office reads it and changes it. The page shows, each as its own card:

- its price: the [price ranges](US-04.2.15) of a fixed price, or the rate or discount of an RVG Contract
- where it is offered: the step rail path it appears under, its linked surgeons or anaesthetist, its [specialties](US-04.2.16) and [funding tag](US-04.2.17)
- who it bills: its holder or the Procedure's payer and, when it bills its holder, the holder [Party](US-04.1.5)'s billing name and delivery, which are changed on the Party
- its name, AA code, holder reference, invoice description and default RVG code
- what was fixed when it was created: its holder, pricing kind and prepaid setting

This is so everything about one Contract, and everyone it touches, is on one screen the office can link to and keep open while it works.

Each card is edited and saved on its own, and each save is one [audited](US-04.1.3) change; a card that would break a Contract rule is refused with the rule's reason. The page also lists the Procedures on the Contract (those upcoming and not yet billed, and those authorised at their locked price), its [event fees](US-03.7.2), and its history. A setting fixed at creation can only change by [replacing the Contract from a date](US-04.1.8).

A system Contract, such as [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3) or a [backstop](US-04.3.9), has the same page: only its name, AA code and invoice description can change, and it cannot be disabled or replaced.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One card at a time.** Given the office edits where a Contract is offered and saves, then only those settings change, and the audit records that one change.
- **Refused in place.** Given a Contract that bills its holder, when the office saves "who it bills" for a holder Party with no billing details, then the card refuses it with the reason, and nothing changes.
- **Party details read-only.** Given a Contract that bills Christchurch Eye Surgery, then its page shows the Party's billing name and email, and changing them opens the Party.
- **Who it touches.** Given three upcoming Procedures and one authorised Procedure on a Contract, then its page lists all four, the authorised one at its locked price.
- **Fixed settings.** Given a saved Contract, then its holder, pricing kind and prepaid setting show but cannot be edited on the page.
- **System Contract.** Given No contract · RVG, then only its name, AA code and invoice description can be edited, and it offers no Disable.
