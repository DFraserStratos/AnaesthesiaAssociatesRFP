Insurers are held as master data, each flagged as accepting direct claims from AA or not.

The selected Contract decides [who is billed](FT-11.2): the holder's billable party when the holder pays AA itself, such as an insurer billed directly under its own Contract, otherwise the payer named on the Booking, usually the patient, who forwards the invoice to the insurer for reimbursement.

The Booking records the insurance approval number, and the v6 design also names the insurer there ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)).
