Insurers are held as master data, each flagged as accepting direct claims from AA or not.

This flag decides which of two flows applies to an insured patient's procedure: billed directly to the insurer under its own Contract, or invoiced to the patient to forward for reimbursement themselves.

The Booking records the insurance approval number, and the v6 design also names the insurer there ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)).
