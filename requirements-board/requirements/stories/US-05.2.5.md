When a Procedure's Contract is a fixed price, the Billing/Invoice Engine prices it at the [price range](US-04.2.15) in force on the procedure date, not from BTM units. On an [open-price fallback](US-04.3.9) the price is the amount typed on the Procedure. No times or modifiers are asked for on either; an RVG code may be kept for reference.

This is so a price agreed with a holder is charged as agreed, whatever work is recorded.

The anaesthetist sees a fixed price read-only and can never override it: to depart from it they [change the Contract](US-03.4.1) or ask the office. Only [the office's price change](US-05.4.2) changes a fixed price on a Procedure.

Nothing further is calculated. If the recorded BTM would come to more or less than the fixed price, no invoice or credit is raised automatically; where the work was very different, the anaesthetist or the office may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

A fixed price with no range in force, or an open price with no amount, shows "Needs a price" ([price follows the Contract](US-05.2.8)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Fixed price used.** Given a Procedure on a fixed-price Contract, then the price is the range in force on its date.
- **Open price.** Given a Procedure on No contract · Fixed price with $650 typed, then the price is $650.
- **No anaesthetist override.** Given a Procedure priced at a fixed price, then the anaesthetist cannot change its price.
- **Office change.** Given a Procedure priced at a fixed price, then the office can change its price, and the changed price is the price invoiced.
- **Nothing further.** Given recorded BTM units that would come to more or less than the fixed price, then no invoice or credit is raised automatically.

## Notes

Simplified 2026-10-02 from "prices it from the matched schedule line instead of RVG units, including any time band or add-on the line carries". A Contract has no time band, add-on flag or quantity rule; a schedule priced by time band is one Contract per band (OQ-89). Add-ons and quantity rules were dropped on 7 October, and on 19 October, as far as Donald and Greg understood, there was no time band either. Time is only an optional recording by the anaesthetist.

Donald, 7 October: "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract" (OQ-61). The RVG suggests that a "top up" on a fixed fee, using the RVG modifiers as a guide, is agreed with the holder before the service.

OQ-117 answered (Donald, 29 October): "If something is a fixed fee, it doesn't allow a price override." This narrows the per-Contract flag (OQ-107, 19 October) for fixed prices: the anaesthetist's price override is never allowed on one, prepaid or not. On a prepaid Procedure any difference is a second invoice or credit raised by hand (OQ-96). Outside prepayment, a price the office changes is simply the price invoiced (Donald, 28 October).
