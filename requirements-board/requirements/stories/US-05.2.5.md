When a Procedure's Contract is a [fixed fee](US-04.2.4), the system uses the fixed fee as the total price for the procedure, not the BTM units.

Base, time and modifier units are still recorded against the Procedure even though they aren't used for pricing, so the underlying activity is never lost.

On a fixed-price Contract the system calculates nothing further. If the recorded BTM would come to more or less than the fixed price, no additional invoice or credit is raised automatically. Where the work was very different, the anaesthetist or the office may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

## Notes

Simplified 2026-10-02 from "prices it from the matched schedule line instead of RVG units, including any time band or add-on the line carries". Of the extras on [fixed fee schedule lines](US-04.2.4), add-ons and quantity rules were dropped on 7 October; whether the time band stays is still open (OQ-89).

Donald, 7 October: "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract" (OQ-61). The RVG suggests that a "top up" on a fixed fee, using the RVG modifiers as a guide, is agreed with the Contract holder before the service.
