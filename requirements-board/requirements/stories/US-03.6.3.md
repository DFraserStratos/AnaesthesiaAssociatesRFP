When the anaesthetist is unsure about a Booking (with its Procedures) or a List, or cannot complete it, they can alert the office to review or act on it, with a note. The alert posts an active notice to the [shared notification pool](FT-13.8). It stays there until someone in the office ticks it resolved.

This is so a new or unsure anaesthetist can ask for a second look, especially if the office review one day stops. It is a way to ask for help: what it does to the rule that a List cannot be [submitted](US-07.1.1) while a Booking is incomplete is not decided.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking or List, when the anaesthetist asks the office to check it and adds a note, then an active notice with that note appears in the shared notification pool.
- Given that notice, then it stays active until someone in the office ticks it resolved.
