When the anaesthetist is unsure about a Procedure, or cannot complete it, they can ask the office to check or resolve it, for example with a tick box or a forward-to-admin action. The request reaches the admin team (for example through the [shared notification pool](FT-13.8)).

This is so a new or unsure anaesthetist can ask for a second look, especially if the office review one day stops. It is a way to ask for help: what it does to the rule that a List cannot be [submitted](US-07.1.1) while a Booking is incomplete is not decided.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure, when the anaesthetist asks the office to check it, then the admin team sees the request.
