The system treats an ACC-funded Procedure as an ordinary Procedure, priced by the Contract of a hospital or other [holder](US-04.2.1) that carries the ACC [funding tag](US-04.2.17), by the standard base, time and modifier calculation where the Contract is priced from RVG units. ACC is a funding tag, not a kind of Contract.

This is so ACC work needs no pricing rules of its own.

ACC is never billed directly. It is who the holder ultimately claims from, which the Billing/Invoice Engine does not see.
