The system treats an ACC-funded Procedure as an ordinary Procedure, priced by the hospital or other [contract holder](US-04.2.1)'s ACC Contract using the standard base, time and modifier calculation.

ACC is never billed directly. It is simply who the contract holder ultimately claims from, which is invisible to the Billing/Invoice Engine.
