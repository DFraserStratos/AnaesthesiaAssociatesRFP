Patient identity checked against the NHI FHIR API, anaesthetists referenced by their [HPI CPN](US-12.1.4), and [ethnicity coded to the NZHIS Level 4 set](US-11.1.1).

These are the identity standards Health NZ mandates for new health data systems, so the integration needs to speak them from the outset.
