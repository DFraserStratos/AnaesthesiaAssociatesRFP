For each [ledger pair](US-08.3.1), the engine creates an ACCREC to the billable party and a DRAFT ACCPAY to the anaesthetist in the same transaction, then stores both returned Xero IDs on the ledger record.

This is so the two records are treated as one unit: the engine can always find both sides of a pair by its own ID, rather than one side going missing or being created without the other.

Each invoice in the pair also carries the engine's own [unique invoice number](US-08.4.3), in Xero's InvoiceNumber field, and the internal case reference, in Xero's Reference field.

InvoiceNumber is the field hospitals and insurers quote back on remittance advices, so it is what an automated remittance matching process keys its lookups on. Reference is for internal tracing and staff-facing display only, since Xero does not enforce uniqueness on it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Invoice identifiers.** The engine puts its own [unique invoice number](US-08.4.3) in Xero's InvoiceNumber field, and the internal case reference in Xero's Reference field. InvoiceNumber is what an automated remittance matching process keys its lookups on. Reference is for internal tracing and staff-facing display only, since Xero does not enforce uniqueness on it.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the expected handling is to treat the ACCREC and ACCPAY as a unit that completes or backs out together, so a pair is not left on one side only (for example an ACCPAY with no receivable behind it). The specific policy is to be confirmed during Scoping and Design.
