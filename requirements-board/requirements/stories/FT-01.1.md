The Scheduling Engine pre-generates the Schedule, Day, Anaesthetist and Slot structure four months ahead, on a rolling basis.

This is a fixed canvas: every active anaesthetist has a place on it before any booking exists.

Four months in the current practice, but the system should be flexible enough to have any number of months.

## Notes

Greg: four months is Vanessa's normal practice, stretched over Christmas (Donald heard Christmas given as the reason for four months; this is in doubt). Changing the length is rare: shortening it drops the future days, and extending it fills them in. Very long horizons (say 36 months) may hit performance limits, but "we don't have to worry about it for now".
