On the Invoices tab of the [Finances section](US-13.3.3), an admin finds every invoice and prepayment invoice AA raises on a payer, and the credit notes against them. The list is searched by [invoice number](US-08.4.3), the payable's number, case reference, patient, payer or anaesthetist.

This is so the office can answer any question about an invoice from one list, whatever detail it is given.

- **What each row shows.** Its kind (invoice, prepayment invoice or credit note), payer, patient and Booking, anaesthetist, date raised, total, what is still owed, its state (Unpaid, Part paid or Paid, with the age of an unpaid one) and [how it was delivered](US-08.4.2).
- **Opens on Unpaid.** The tab opens filtered to unpaid invoices; the other states, and all of them, are a filter away.
- **Awaiting approval.** One of the filters lists the prepayment invoices awaiting approval, where each is [approved and sent](US-06.3.1). The switch that turns the approval step on or off sits beside that list.
- **Own pages.** Each invoice and each credit note opens on its own page under the Invoices tab.
- **Not AA's fees.** AA's fee invoices to the anaesthetists are not on this tab; they are on the [AA fees tab](US-10.3.1).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Opens on Unpaid.** Given an admin opens the Invoices tab, then it lists the unpaid invoices first, and choosing All lists paid ones too.
- **Search by payable number.** Given an invoice whose payable is AA-2026-0002-P, when an admin searches for that number, then the invoice is found.
- **Search by patient.** Given a patient with two invoices, when an admin searches for the patient's name, then both are listed.
- **Age of an unpaid invoice.** Given an invoice raised 40 days ago and not paid, then its row shows it as Unpaid with its age, and what is still owed.
- **Part paid.** Given a $1,000 invoice with $400 received, then its row shows Part paid and $600 still owed.
- **Awaiting approval.** Given a prepayment invoice awaiting approval, when an admin chooses Awaiting approval and approves it, then it is generated and sent and leaves that filter.
- **No AA fee invoices.** Given a month's AA fee invoices have been raised, then none of them is listed on the Invoices tab.
- **Own page.** Given an invoice and a credit note against it, when an admin opens each from the list, then each opens on its own page under Invoices.
