Whenever a Booking's Procedures, Contract or payer change before the procedure takes place, the Billing/Invoice Engine re-checks the [prepayment requirement](US-06.2.1). For example, if the Procedure's Contract changes to one not flagged as prepaid, the requirement is cleared.

A move is not one of these changes. The system has no logic that detects a List or a Booking moving to another anaesthetist, and a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation. The money sits in the [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1), so nothing in the ledger changes, and the new anaesthetist claims no more than was prepaid ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

This is so a Booking that no longer needs prepayment, for example because its Contract changed, has that cleared automatically rather than left stale on the Booking.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Booking, when its Procedure's Contract changes to one not flagged as prepaid before the procedure, then the prepayment requirement is cleared.
- Given a prepaid Booking, when it or its List moves to another anaesthetist, then no prepayment or invoice calculation is re-triggered.

## Notes

No RFP passage supports this. It comes from a diagram Donald created and Greg (a subject matter expert) checked. Until AA says otherwise, treat it as more correct than the RFP (OQ-35).

The Contract example is our reading of moving the trigger to the Contract; an already-raised prepayment invoice is not covered (OQ-80 settled when the invoice and its pair are created, not this case). A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new Contract version does not relink it (Donald, 19 October). This is the build assumption until Vanessa confirms it (OQ-104).

The 2026-10-01 meeting had prepayments re-checked whenever a List or a procedure is moved. Donald's 7 October words replace that: "the system should not try and have any logic to detect any of these flows that would re-trigger any sort of calculations for invoices. It is simply not something the system supports." On a move the payable is not updated: it is the holding account (OQ-80, answered 19 October).
