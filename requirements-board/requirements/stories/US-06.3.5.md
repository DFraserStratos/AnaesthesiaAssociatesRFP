The Billing/Invoice Engine re-checks the [prepayment requirement](US-06.2.1) whenever a Booking's Procedures, Contract or payer change before the procedure takes place.

This is so a Booking asks for prepayment only while it still needs one.

If the prepayment invoice has already been sent or paid when the Contract changes to one not flagged as prepaid, the change is handled as a cancellation of the prepayment ([Contract changed after a prepayment is sent or paid](US-06.3.7)).

A move is not one of these changes. The system has no logic that detects a List or a Booking moving to another anaesthetist, and a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation. A moved Booking keeps every Procedure's Contract ([a moved Booking keeps its Contracts](US-04.3.11)).

The money sits in the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), so nothing in the ledger changes, and the new anaesthetist claims the prepaid amount ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Contract changed.** Given a prepaid Booking whose prepayment invoice has not yet been generated, when its Procedure's Contract changes to one not flagged as prepaid before the procedure, then the prepayment requirement is cleared.
- **Move.** Given a prepaid Booking, when it or its List moves to another anaesthetist, then no prepayment or invoice calculation is re-triggered.
- **Sent or paid.** Given a prepaid Booking whose prepayment invoice has been sent or paid, when its Procedure's Contract changes to one not flagged as prepaid, then the prepayment is handled as a cancellation ([Contract changed after a prepayment is sent or paid](US-06.3.7)).

## Notes

No RFP passage supports this. It comes from a diagram Donald created and Greg (a subject matter expert) checked. Until AA says otherwise, treat it as more correct than the RFP (OQ-35).

A Contract changed after the prepayment invoice is sent or paid is its own story ([Contract changed after a prepayment is sent or paid](US-06.3.7)): handled like a cancellation of the prepayment, refunded if paid (OQ-119, answered 4 November for the build); Greg would rather see such a Booking cancelled and rebooked: "I'd rather see that deleted and rebooked, really." A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new price range does not move it once its prepayment invoice is raised (Donald, 19 October; D9). Ben confirmed this for a prepaid Procedure on 27 October (OQ-104).

The 2026-10-01 meeting had prepayments re-checked whenever a List or a procedure is moved. Donald's 7 October words replace that: "the system should not try and have any logic to detect any of these flows that would re-trigger any sort of calculations for invoices. It is simply not something the system supports." On a move the payable is not updated: it is the holding account (OQ-80, answered 19 October).
