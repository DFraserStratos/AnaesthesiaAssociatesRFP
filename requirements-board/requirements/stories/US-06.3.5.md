Whenever a Booking's Procedures, Contract or payer change before the procedure takes place, the Billing/Invoice Engine re-checks the [prepayment requirement](US-06.2.1) and amount. For example, if the payer becomes an insurer, prepayment no longer applies and the requirement is cleared.

A move is not one of these changes. The system has no logic that detects a List or a Booking moving to another anaesthetist, and a move re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation. An honour system applies instead: the new anaesthetist claims no more than was prepaid ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

This is so a Booking that no longer needs prepayment, for example because its payer changed, has that cleared automatically rather than left stale on the Booking.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Booking, when its payer changes to an insurer before the procedure, then the prepayment requirement is cleared.
- Given a prepaid Booking, when it or its List moves to another anaesthetist, then no prepayment or invoice calculation is re-triggered.
