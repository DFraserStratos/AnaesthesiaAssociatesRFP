A Procedure is priced by its Contract and the values held on the Procedure. Choosing the Contract (and its line) and the RVG code fills those values in ([starting units](US-03.3.1)); whoever is permitted can then change them, and the last change stands.

- If the Procedure holds a price (a fixed price from its Contract line, [a prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), or a price entered by someone permitted), that is the price, and BTM units are kept for reference if recorded.
- Otherwise the price is (base + time + modifier units) x rate x (1 - discount), where:
  - the rate is the Contract's rate where it sets one, otherwise [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-12.1.1)
  - the discount is the one held on the Procedure (from the Contract, or entered where permitted), held as a fraction (20% is 0.2) and shown as its own line on the invoice

Prices are worked out with the [Contract version in force on the procedure date](US-04.2.10), except a prepaid Procedure, which keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice. A price of 0 gives a no-charge invoice, and BTM is still recorded if entered.

This is so every Procedure is priced the same way whichever Contract it is on, and the units behind the price are never lost.

## Technical discussion

In the technical design v6 the resolver calculates the price from the values in the booking procedure (base, time, modifiers, price, rate, discount), at the procedure date; it checks the Contract version is valid on that date and, if needed, refreshes values from the revised Contract line ([resolver](AR-29#resolver)); this refresh does not apply to a prepaid Procedure. Time entry rejects a price or discount entry on a Contract that is not editable, and submission is rejected when no price can be worked out; where the Contract is not valid on the procedure date, the original values are used and a warning is flagged ([validation](AR-29#price-validation)).

The pricing documents also set out an ordered sequence of price steps; it is not adopted for the build (Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #11).
