The Billing/Invoice Engine prices each Procedure by its Contract's [pricing kind](US-04.2.2) and the values held on the Procedure:

- a [fixed price](US-05.2.5): the [price range](US-04.2.15) in force on the procedure date
- an [open price](US-04.3.9): the amount typed on the Procedure
- RVG units: (base + time + modifier units) x the rate, [the Contract's](US-05.2.6) or [the anaesthetist's own](US-12.1.1), less any [Contract discount](US-05.4.3) shown as its own invoice line

A typed price, a prepaid amount and AUTHORISED hold the price ([price follows the Contract](US-05.2.8)). A price of 0 gives a no-charge invoice.

This is so every Procedure is priced the same way whichever Contract it is on, and the units behind the price are never lost.

## Technical discussion

The price function is [AR-58 section 5.7](AR-58#L515-567); the resolver in Greg's technical design v6 is historical.

The pricing documents also set out an ordered sequence of price steps; it is not adopted for the build (Notes 2026-10-16 · Pricing model documents v6 #11).
