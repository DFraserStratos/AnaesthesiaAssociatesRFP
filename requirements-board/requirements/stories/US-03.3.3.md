The anaesthetist records the start time, when they took exclusive clinical care of the patient, and the finish time. [Time units](US-05.2.2) are calculated from those two timestamps.

## Notes

Greg, 16 October: "It should say finish... They hand it over to intensive care or post-op care, but that's not what they're recording." The RFP's screenshots say finish, and the current app has start and finish buttons. Time recording is optional where the Contract fixes the price ([mark a Booking complete](US-03.6.1)).
