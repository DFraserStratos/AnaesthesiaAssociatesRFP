On the Ledger tab of [the Finances section](US-13.3.3), admins see whether the books are square, in one sentence that names what is wrong, with four figures:

- owed to AA: [receivables outstanding](US-08.3.1)
- owed to the anaesthetists: payables due
- [the AA Holding account](US-06.5.1)
- receipts to place

The list beneath shows, as the admin chooses, the anaesthetists, the invoice pairs, or the AA Holding account's entries. Each anaesthetist's row shows what has been billed for them, paid into AA, [disbursed](US-10.1.2), what AA owes them and what they owe AA. It opens that anaesthetist's account page, [their own ledger position](US-08.3.5): what AA owes them and what they owe AA, their invoice pairs, payment runs and credit trails, with a way back to the Ledger.

A receipt that matches no invoice is placed, or marked refunded, here.

This is so the office has one place to check whether the ledger is in balance, both overall and for any one anaesthetist.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Square or not.** Given the ledger is out of balance, when an admin opens the Ledger tab, then one sentence says the books are not square and names what is wrong; given it balances, the sentence says the books are square.
- **Four figures.** Given the Ledger tab, then it shows owed to AA, owed to the anaesthetists, the AA Holding account and receipts to place.
- **Anaesthetist account page.** Given Dr Souter's row in the anaesthetists list, when an admin opens it, then her page shows what AA owes her and what she owes AA, her invoice pairs, payment runs and credit trails, and leads back to the Ledger.
- **Who owes whom.** Given an anaesthetist out of balance, then their row in the anaesthetists list shows what AA owes them and what they owe AA.
- **Holding account entries.** Given the list set to the AA Holding account, then each of its entries is listed.
- **Receipt to place.** Given a receipt that matches no invoice, when an admin places it against an invoice or marks it refunded, then it no longer counts among the receipts to place.
