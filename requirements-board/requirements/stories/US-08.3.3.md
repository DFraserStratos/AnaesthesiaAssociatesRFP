The Billing/Invoice Engine keeps every patient's billing history in its own ledger, even after that patient's [Xero contact has been archived](US-09.3.3) or purged.

This is because Xero contacts are archived periodically to keep Xero performant, while the ledger must still be able to answer for a patient's full history at any time.
