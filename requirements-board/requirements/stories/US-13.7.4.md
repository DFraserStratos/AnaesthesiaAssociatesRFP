On a settings page in the Admin App, AA sets the thresholds for the [warning routine](US-13.7.1), chooses which warnings are active, and switches off admin check steps as trust in the system builds.

This is so AA can tune the warnings and checks without a change to the system.

The switch for [the prepayment invoice approval step](US-06.3.1) is the exception: it sits beside the prepayment invoices awaiting approval on the Invoices tab of [the Finances section](US-13.3.3), not on this page.
