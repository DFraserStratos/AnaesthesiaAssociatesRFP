Admin staff can also record positive preferences: surgeons and anaesthetists who want to work together. These sit alongside the private [not-preferred pairings](US-13.6.3).

When matching an anaesthetist to a List, admin see both kinds of preference. When admin assign or move a List, the available anaesthetists are ordered by [priority tier](US-01.3.6) and preferred pairing. This only orders the list; nothing is assigned automatically.

Preferred pairings are private to admin staff, like the not-preferred pairings: anaesthetists never see them.

This is so admin can favour pairings that work well, not only avoid ones that do not.
