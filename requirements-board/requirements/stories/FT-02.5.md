Every inbound change to a Booking is classified before it's applied. It is one of:

- a new Booking
- a modification (including a time change within the same List)
- a cancellation

A Booking that moves to another day is not a separate type: it arrives as a cancellation and a new Booking ([US-02.5.2](US-02.5.2)).

Whichever it is, the change is recorded with its source and history ([US-02.5.5](US-02.5.5)). Applying these changes automatically is Future Work; in the first release the office reviews and approves them.
