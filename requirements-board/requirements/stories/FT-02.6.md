When an incoming booking cannot become a Booking, for example because it has [no NHI](US-11.1.4), a stub shows in its place on its List until the office resolves it from intake. Anaesthetists and the office both see the stub, clearly marked as a booking with a problem.

This is so a List with a booking that could not be posted never looks complete, and the problem is chased rather than left until the hospital list arrives the day before.

A stub sits against the 19 October ruling (OQ-49) that an incoming booking with no NHI waits at intake, on the matching screen only. This feature stays open until OQ-118, raised for Vanessa and Ben on 28 October, is answered.
