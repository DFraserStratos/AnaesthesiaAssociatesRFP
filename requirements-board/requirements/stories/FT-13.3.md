Admins monitor the billing flow as it moves from List, through the Billing Engine, to Xero, seeing what has completed and what has failed. They do it in the Admin App's Finances section, where the office keeps all its money work.

This is so the office can see where any Booking's billing stands and step in where it has stopped.

## Notes

Putting this monitoring in the Admin App, rather than a separate Billing Engine console, is our own proposal; the RFP left open where it should live.
