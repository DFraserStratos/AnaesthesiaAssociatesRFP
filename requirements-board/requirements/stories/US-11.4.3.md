An insured Booking holds the insurer and the insurance approval number as fields on the Booking. The insurer says whether the patient has insurance at all; the approval number says the claim has been accepted.

The surgeon's rooms are expected to send them with the list, but often do not. They show on the [matching screen](FT-02.1) when the booking comes in, and on the Booking, where the office can change them. The anaesthetist may also change them on the day of the procedure, when they may first learn of an approval.

An insured Booking with no approval number raises a warning; it never blocks. Without approval on the day, the patient is billed.

A code such as SXAP says the hospital is arranging the insurer billing, not that the cover is approved. The Contract still decides who is billed.

This is because being insured is not the same as having the claim approved, and the approval number is the missing piece the holder needs.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Recorded on the Booking.** Given an insured Booking, the office can record and change the insurer and the approval number.
- **Missing approval number.** Given an insured Booking with no approval number, then a warning shows.
- **Not required to complete.** Given a Booking, the anaesthetist can mark it complete without an approval number.
- **Changed on the day.** Given an insured Booking, the anaesthetist can change the approval number.
