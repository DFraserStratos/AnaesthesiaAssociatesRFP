A Booking names its insurer or funder as a [Party](US-04.1.5), and an insured Booking holds the insurance approval number. They show on the [matching screen](FT-02.1) when the booking comes in, and the office can change them on the Booking. The anaesthetist can change them on the day, and sees the approval number when the Booking holds one.

This is so AA knows whether an insured patient's claim is approved: being insured is not the same as having the claim approved, and the approval number is the missing piece the holder needs.

The surgeon's rooms are expected to send both with the list, but often do not, and the anaesthetist may first learn of an approval on the day.

- A Booking whose funder is an insurer (NIB, Southern Cross Health Society) and that has no approval number [raises a warning](US-13.7.1) to the office and to the anaesthetist viewing the Booking. It never blocks. A funder such as ACC or Health NZ raises none.
- It is the only insurance warning: an insurer on a Booking under a Contract that bills the payer raises none.
- Without approval on the day, the patient is billed.
- The insurer or funder only [suggests a path](US-04.3.10) on the step rail and never narrows the [Contracts offered](US-04.3.2). The Contract still decides who is billed.
- SXAP is a [funding tag](US-04.2.17) on the hospital's Contracts, not a statement that cover is approved.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Recorded on the Booking.** Given a Booking, the office can record and change its insurer or funder and the approval number.
- **Missing approval number.** Given a Booking whose funder is an insurer and that has no approval number, then a warning shows.
- **Funder.** Given a Booking funded by ACC with no approval number, then no warning is raised.
- **Who sees it.** Given an insured Booking with no approval number, then the warning shows to the office and to the anaesthetist viewing the Booking.
- **No other insurance warning.** Given a Booking that names an insurer and has an approval number, when a Contract that bills the payer is chosen, then no insurance warning is raised.
- **Not required to complete.** Given a Booking, the anaesthetist can [mark it complete](US-03.6.1) without an approval number.
- **Changed on the day.** Given an insured Booking, the anaesthetist can change the approval number.
- **Anaesthetist sees the number.** Given a Booking that holds an approval number, when the anaesthetist views the Booking, then the number itself is shown.
