The [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) function has a credit note option. An admin, or the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, credits the original invoice in full: a credit note is never for part of an invoice. Any party can receive the credit, the original billable party included. Anything still owed is then raised afresh as a new additional invoice.

The credit automatically includes the reversal of the [linked payable](US-08.3.1) to the anaesthetist, as in [credit note and re-issue](US-08.6.2). The credit and any new invoices are recorded as [events on the Procedure](FT-03.7).

This is so the office can credit and rebill an invoice, for example when an insurer asks for a combined Procedure to be split out, without editing the original. It is also so the anaesthetist can credit a patient by hand, for example when only half the prepaid work was done: the prepayment invoice is credited in full, and the work done is invoiced afresh.

A credit note is not a refund. Refunding a credit that results happens outside the system, except for a [cancelled prepayment](US-06.5.2), whose refund the system raises itself, automatically on cancel; the payment is made in Xero, as all payments are.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Credit to a chosen party.** Given an invoiced Procedure, when the admin, or the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, uses the credit note option, then a credit against the original invoice is raised to the party they pick.
- **Payable reversed.** Given a credit, then it includes the reversal of the linked payable to the anaesthetist.
- **Recorded as an event.** Given a credit, then it shows as an event on the Procedure.
- **After a fixed price.** Given a prepaid or other fixed-price Procedure whose work turned out less than expected, then no credit note exists until the anaesthetist or an admin creates one by hand with this option.
- **No refund.** The credit note option does not pay a refund.
- **Always in full.** Given an invoice, a prepayment invoice included, when a credit note is raised against it, then it credits the whole invoice; a credit for part of an invoice cannot be raised.
- **Fresh invoice.** Given a prepayment invoice credited in full because only part of the work was done, when the anaesthetist or an admin raises a new additional invoice for the work done, then it is a separate invoice with its own number, traceable to the Procedure.
