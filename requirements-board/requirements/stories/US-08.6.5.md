The [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) function has a credit note option. An admin, or the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, credits the original invoice, and any party can receive the credit, the original billable party included. New additional invoices are then raised where needed.

The credit automatically includes the reversal of the [linked payable](US-08.3.1) to the anaesthetist, as in [credit note and re-issue](US-08.6.2). The credit and any new invoices are recorded as [events on the Procedure](FT-03.7).

This is so the office can credit and rebill an invoice, for example when an insurer asks for a combined Procedure to be split out, without editing the original (OQ-72). It is also so the anaesthetist can credit a patient by hand, for example when only half the prepaid work was done.

A credit note is not a refund ("One's cash, one's not"). Refunding a credit that results happens outside the system.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Credit to a chosen party.** Given an invoiced Procedure, when the admin, or the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, uses the credit note option, then a credit against the original invoice is raised to the party they pick.
- **Payable reversed.** Given a credit, then it includes the reversal of the linked payable to the anaesthetist.
- **Recorded as an event.** Given a credit, then it shows as an event on the Procedure.
- **After a fixed price.** Given a prepaid or other fixed-price Procedure whose work turned out less than expected, then no credit note exists until the anaesthetist or an admin creates one by hand with this option.
- **No refund.** The credit note option does not pay a refund.
