Each anaesthetist has a profile, held by the office: their identity (registration number and HPI CPN), contact details and bank details, and their dollar value per unit, the only setting on it that drives their billing. Their preferred and not-preferred pairings with surgeons are held there too, seen only by admin staff ([preferred pairings](US-13.6.4), [not-preferred pairings](US-13.6.3)).

Anaesthetists also have two reporting needs of their own: a list of the balances still owing to them, and a monthly GST report, a list of the payments AA has passed to them in the month with the GST component of each, which they send to their accountant for their GST return.

## Notes

Prepaid procedures are no longer on the profile: the anaesthetist tells the office the prices they want, and the office sets up a Contract (EP-06). The not-preferred and preferred pairings are stored on the surgeon and anaesthetist profiles, seen only by admin staff ([not-preferred pairings](US-13.6.3), [preferred pairings](US-13.6.4)).
