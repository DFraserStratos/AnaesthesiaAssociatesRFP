Each anaesthetist has a profile: their dollar value per unit, as specified in the RVG, which is the only setting that drives their billing, and their contact and bank details.

Anaesthetists also have two reporting needs of their own: a list of the balances still owing to them, and a monthly GST report, a list of the payments AA has passed to them in the month with the GST component of each, for their GST return.
