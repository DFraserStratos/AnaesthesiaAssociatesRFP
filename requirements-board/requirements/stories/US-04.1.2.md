Admins create Contracts, edit their settings, and disable a Contract AA no longer uses, in the Contracts section of the Admin app. Each Contract is Active or Disabled. A Disabled Contract is hidden: it is no longer offered on the [step rail](US-04.3.8), and the Contract list shows it only when asked to. It is never deleted.

This is so the office keeps each holder's price list current by finding an item on screen and changing it, and so a Contract that has ended stays on record without getting in the way.

The Contract list is searched by name, holder reference code or AA code, shows Active Contracts unless asked for Disabled ones or all, and can be grouped by:

- holder
- kind of holder (hospitals, insurers, funders, surgeon groups, surgeons, anaesthetists)
- funding tag
- surgeon, where a Contract linked to two surgeons shows under both
- where the invoice goes
- pricing kind

An index of the groups and their counts sits beside the list. Opening a row opens [the Contract's page](US-04.1.7), where its settings are changed.

How a change is made:

- a fixed price changes by adding a [price range](US-04.2.15), which may start in the past
- a rate, a discount or another setting is edited in place and applies straight away
- a setting that must change only from a date, such as a rate from 1 January, means [replacing the Contract from that date](US-04.1.8)
- a new item on a holder's price list is a new Contract

A new Contract is entered on its own page, in the order its rules depend on: its holder and who it bills, then its pricing (the pricing kind, the rate or first price range, prepaid, and whether the anaesthetist may change the price), then where it is offered, then its name, codes and invoice wording. The page shows where the Contract will appear on the step rail, and lists the holder's existing Contracts with a similar name as a hint, never a block.

Disabling asks the office to confirm, and lists the Procedures dated from today still on the Contract. Nothing moves by itself: the office decides each one. A Procedure already on a Contract keeps it when the Contract is disabled. One not yet authorised and dated on or after the day the Contract was disabled is flagged at [office review](US-07.2.2), so the office keeps it or moves it; one dated before then bills as normal without a flag.

A Contract's holder, prepaid setting and [pricing kind](US-04.2.2) cannot change once it is created. Every change is [audited](US-04.1.3).

The office creates every Contract, including the items of each anaesthetist's [own price list](US-04.2.14) from the list the anaesthetist supplies. Anaesthetists never create or edit Contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Active filter.** The Contract list shows Active Contracts by default, and Disabled or all Contracts when asked.
- **Grouped.** Given the Contract list grouped by funding tag, then each tag, and No tag, is a group with its count in the index, and picking SXAP lists only the SXAP Contracts, under their holders.
- **Two surgeons.** Given a Contract linked to Ms Lim and Mr Okafor, when the list is grouped by surgeon, then it shows under both.
- **No dates.** Given a Contract, then it has no start, end or review date of its own (a fixed price's dates sit on its price ranges); its status is Active or Disabled.
- **New contract order.** Given the office starts a new Contract held by an anaesthetist, then who it bills is fixed to the payer before its pricing is chosen.
- **Similar name.** Given Merivale holds "Abdominoplasty, simple", when the office starts "Abdominoplasty with liposuction" for Merivale, then the page shows the existing Contract as similar, and the new one can still be created.
- **Disable confirmed.** Given an Active Contract with a Procedure dated next week on it, when the office chooses Disable, then it is asked to confirm and shown that Procedure; once confirmed, the Contract is no longer offered on any List, it is not deleted, and the Procedure keeps it.
- **Disabled flag.** Given a Disabled Contract, then a Procedure on it, not yet authorised and dated on or after the day it was disabled, is flagged at office review.
- **Fixed after creation.** Given a saved Contract, its holder, prepaid setting and pricing kind cannot be changed.

## Technical discussion

The day a Contract was disabled comes from the [Contract audit](US-04.1.3): a Contract has no dates of its own.

Whether a Contract could be disabled from a future date is OQ-144.
