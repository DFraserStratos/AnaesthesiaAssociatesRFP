Admins create, edit, version and retire Contracts, including each anaesthetist's [own price list Contract](US-04.2.14), which the office enters from the price list the anaesthetist supplies. Anaesthetists do not create or edit Contracts in the app. Each Contract has a start date and, if needed, an end date, and a review date. A price review creates a [new dated version](US-04.2.10) of the Contract rather than changing the old one.

The office maintains a Contract on one screen: the header (name, dates and settings) with its lines below. In the v6 design, changes are staged, reviewed and submitted as a new dated version ([stage and submit Contract changes](US-04.1.6)); whether the review and upload are in the first release is still open (OQ-100).

This is so fee schedule updates, for example a new rate taking effect on 1 April, can be applied cleanly.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Contract list filters by active Contracts or all Contracts.
- The Contract list filters by [contract holder](US-04.1.5), since staff are always interested in one holder's Contracts.
- Given a Contract, then it has a start date, an optional end date and a review date.
