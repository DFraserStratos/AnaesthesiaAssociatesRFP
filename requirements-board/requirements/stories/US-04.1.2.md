Admins create Contracts, edit their settings, and disable a Contract AA no longer uses. Each Contract is Active or Disabled. A Disabled Contract is hidden: it is no longer offered on the [step rail](US-04.3.8), and the Contract list shows it only when asked to. It is never deleted.

This is so the office keeps each holder's price list current by finding an item on screen and changing it, and so a Contract that has ended stays on record without getting in the way.

How a change is made:

- a fixed price changes by adding a [price range](US-04.2.15), which may start in the past
- a rate, a discount or another setting is edited in place and applies straight away
- a setting that must change only from a date, such as a rate from 1 January, means a new Contract, and the old one is disabled once it is no longer needed
- a new item on a holder's price list is a new Contract

A Procedure already on a Contract keeps it when the Contract is disabled, and bills as normal. A Contract's prepaid setting and [pricing kind](US-04.2.2) cannot change once it is created. Every change is [audited](US-04.1.3).

The office creates every Contract, including the items of each anaesthetist's [own price list](US-04.2.14) from the list the anaesthetist supplies. Anaesthetists never create or edit Contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Active filter.** The Contract list shows Active Contracts by default, and all Contracts when asked.
- **Holder filter.** The Contract list filters by holder [Party](US-04.1.5), since staff are usually working on one holder's Contracts.
- **No dates.** Given a Contract, then it has no start, end or review date of its own (a fixed price's dates sit on its price ranges); its status is Active or Disabled.
- **Disable.** Given an Active Contract, when the office disables it, then it is no longer offered on any List, it is not deleted, and a Procedure already on it keeps it.
- **Dated change.** Given a rate that changes from 1 January, then the office creates a new Contract for it.
- **Fixed after creation.** Given a saved Contract, its prepaid setting and pricing kind cannot be changed.
