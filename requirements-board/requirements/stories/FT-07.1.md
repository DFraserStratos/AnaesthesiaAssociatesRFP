A List is ACTIVE once it has all five of its pairing: one anaesthetist, one surgeon, one hospital, one day and one session (AM or PM). It gets there when an admin [assigns a Draft List](US-01.6.3) to an anaesthetist, or is set up with its anaesthetist from the start, for example from a [recurring booking](US-01.3.2).

While a List is ACTIVE, the anaesthetist [completes its Bookings](US-03.6.1), then submits the List for office review.

## Notes

This state was called DRAFT until 7 October 2026. DRAFT now means a [Draft List](FT-01.6), a List with no anaesthetist yet.
