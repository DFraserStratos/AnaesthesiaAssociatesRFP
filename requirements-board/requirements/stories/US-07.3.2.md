The system rejects any attempt to edit an AUTHORISED List's Bookings or Procedures.

This keeps the List exactly as it was when it was [handed to the Billing/Invoice Engine](US-08.1.1), so the record it works from never changes underneath it.

## Notes

After authorisation, corrections are new records, never edits: the office raises [ad hoc invoices](US-08.6.3) or credits on the Procedure. Greg (19 October): "it becomes like a list of changes really, rather than changing the original."
