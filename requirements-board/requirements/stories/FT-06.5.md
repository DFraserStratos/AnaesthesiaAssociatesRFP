Prepayment money is not paid to the anaesthetist. It is held in a [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1), within AA's trust accounting, and moved to the anaesthetist who did the List only once the procedure is done. It is handled centrally by the office rather than by the anaesthetist.

- A cancelled Booking's prepayment is always refunded in full from the holding account.
- A Booking that [moves to another anaesthetist](US-01.4.7) keeps its prepayment: the payable is the holding account, so it does not move, and nothing is recalculated. The price prepaid is the price, so the patient sees a stable amount (OQ-70, OQ-80).

Today, if the anaesthetist has already been paid, they refund the patient themselves or pay the replacement anaesthetist directly, with a lot of back and forth.
