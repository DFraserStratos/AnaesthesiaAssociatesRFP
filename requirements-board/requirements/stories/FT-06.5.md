Prepayment money is not paid to the anaesthetist. It is credited to a payables account called the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), within AA's trust accounting, and transferred automatically to whoever did the procedure once it is done: the office does not handle it by hand.

- A cancelled Booking's prepayment is reversed as part of the cancellation: the invoice is credited if unpaid, and refunded in full from the holding account if paid ([cancellation of prepayment procedures](US-06.5.2)).
- A Booking that [moves to another anaesthetist](US-01.4.7) keeps its prepayment price: the payable is the holding account, so it does not move, and nothing is recalculated. The price prepaid is the price, so the patient sees a stable amount (OQ-70, OQ-80).
- After a prepaid procedure nothing further is calculated or raised automatically ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).
