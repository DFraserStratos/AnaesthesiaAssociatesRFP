AA starts on the new system with a clean cut rather than a full migration of Solutions Plus. Before go-live AA decides which data is useful, and only that is loaded, cleaned and checked. Nothing comes across just because it exists in Solutions Plus.

This is because Solutions Plus data is not migration-ready: it will not export, its unit values are not reliable, and it holds junk entries.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given data in Solutions Plus that AA has not approved, when go-live data is loaded, then it is not loaded.
- Given approved data, when it is loaded, then it comes from the [controlled spreadsheets](US-13.4.3).
