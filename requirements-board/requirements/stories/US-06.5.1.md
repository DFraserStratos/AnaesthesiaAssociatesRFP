A prepayment is not paid to the anaesthetist. It is held in a prepay holding account, which the internal ledger treats like an anaesthetist's account: the other side of the [prepayment invoice's pair](US-06.3.1) is the holding account instead of an anaesthetist. Its balance is all the deposits received for procedures not yet done. The holding account sits within AA's trust accounting.

Prepayment is two steps. First the money is collected into the holding account. Then, once the procedure is done and the office authorises the List ([settlement](FT-06.4)), a second pair moves the money from the holding account to whichever anaesthetist did the List, in place of another invoice to the patient. A cancelled prepayment is refunded to the patient from the holding account ([refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2)), and payments out of it are made from the system, not by hand outside it.

This is so refunds and prepayments are reconciled in one place, instead of loosely across AA's books and each anaesthetist's, and so a prepaid Booking that changes anaesthetist leaves nothing to amend.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a paid prepayment, when the procedure has not happened, then nothing is paid to the anaesthetist.
- Given a prepayment invoice, then its pair is a receivable from the payer named on the Booking and a payable to the prepay holding account.
- Given the holding account, then its balance is the total of the prepayments received for procedures not yet done.
