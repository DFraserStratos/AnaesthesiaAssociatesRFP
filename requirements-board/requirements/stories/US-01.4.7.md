In their app, an anaesthetist can move a single Booking from one of their Lists to another anaesthetist, as well as [move a whole List](US-01.4.3) (Greg: "They need to be able to move at the list level or at the booking level").

The anaesthetist finds the receiver in one of two ways: by scrolling the colleagues who are free in that Slot, or by typing in a search box that filters the names as each letter is typed, like the RVG code picker.

The Booking can go only to a colleague who is free in that Slot. The move creates a List in the receiver's Slot holding the Booking. A Booking is never moved into another anaesthetist's existing List. This is the rule for now and may change if it does not work in practice. A receiver who is on leave or unavailable does not show as free: they first [set their own Slot to available](US-01.2.1), which is their declaration that they accept responsibility for the work. The office does not assign work to someone marked unavailable without them knowing.

The mover can, if they want, notify the admin team (a notice in the [shared pool](FT-13.8)) or raise the update email. Neither is required.

The mover is never warned of another anaesthetist's not-preferred pairing with the surgeon: it is sensitive, and the anaesthetists are professionals.

This is so an anaesthetist can hand on one case and keep the rest, for example to a colleague who agrees to do one procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking on their List, when the anaesthetist moves it to a colleague free in that Slot, then it leaves their List and is on a new List in the colleague's Slot.
- Given a colleague who already holds a List in that Slot, then the Booking is not moved into it, and they are not offered as a receiver.
- Given a colleague marked unavailable, then they are not offered as a receiver until they set themselves available.
- Given the mover types letters in the search box, then the names filter as each letter is typed.
- Given a move, then the mover may notify the admin team or raise the update email, and is not required to.
- Given a colleague the surgeon does not prefer, then the mover sees no warning.
