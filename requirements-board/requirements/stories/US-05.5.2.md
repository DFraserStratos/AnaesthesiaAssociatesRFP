An ACC pre-operative assessment is a fixed-price service ACC pays for when the anaesthetist sees the patient before the procedure. The anaesthetist records it against the real Procedure, as a [pre-op event](US-03.7.1). It is an event, not an added procedure: its own element attached to the real Procedure.

It follows the generic event and pricing rules, not necessarily a fixed-fee Contract: some ACC pre-op events may be preconfigured, otherwise the event is free text.

AA does little of this work, but practices in other regions do, so it must be supported.

The three codes are to be confirmed: CS250, CS260 or CS70.

## Notes

The screenshots show the earlier model, a flat fee billing line.

OQ-12 part 2 answered 28 October: the RVG guide lists only the codes and gives no prices, so no fixed-fee Contract is required. Whether CS250, CS260 and CS70 are the right codes is still OQ-12. How and when an event is billed (a line on the Procedure's invoice or an invoice of its own) is still to ask Vanessa (OQ-121).
