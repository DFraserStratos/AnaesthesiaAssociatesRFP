Bookings reach the Scheduling Engine from four sources: the hospitals, the surgeons' rooms (their List PDFs), admin entry, and an anaesthetist's own ad hoc entry. Lists and changes from the hospitals and the rooms arrive at one [matching screen](FT-02.1), where the office checks them before they reach the schedule, and every change to a Booking keeps a record of where it came from.

This is so the schedule follows what the rooms and hospitals send, and the office stays in control of what reaches it.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change is validated against the system's internal data model rather than the incoming message, so there is one consistent validation gate however a booking change arrived.
