Bookings reach the Scheduling Engine from four sources: the hospitals (imported however each hospital provides them), the surgeons' rooms (their List PDFs, read and ingested: [surgeon PDF list ingest](FT-02.2)), admin entry, and an anaesthetist's own ad hoc entry. The first release builds both intake pathways, the hospital booking downloads and the rooms' PDF lists, each landing in the same matching screen.

Every change to a Booking, whether it's a new Booking, a modification or a cancellation, is recorded with its source in the Booking's [change history](US-02.5.5). A Booking that moves to another day arrives as a cancellation and a new Booking ([US-02.5.2](US-02.5.2)).

In the first release nothing is applied automatically. Incoming data, from a hospital or a rooms' PDF, lands on a review and merge screen: the office accepts rows that match nothing as new Bookings, and approves or rejects the differences shown for a matched Booking. Applying changes automatically is Future Work ([FT-02.5](FT-02.5)).

An incoming booking that cannot become a Booking, such as one with no NHI, shows as a stub on its List until the office resolves it ([stubs on a List](FT-02.6)).

Inbound changes, through the review and merge step, are accepted until the office authorises the List, the same editing window as the anaesthetist's and the office's own changes ([US-02.5.4](US-02.5.4)).

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change is validated against the system's internal data model rather than the incoming message, so there is one consistent validation gate however a booking change arrived.
