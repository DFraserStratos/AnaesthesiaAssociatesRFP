Bookings reach the Scheduling Engine from four sources: the hospitals (imported however each hospital provides them), the surgeons' rooms (their List PDFs; reading these automatically is [Future Work](US-02.2.1)), admin entry, and an anaesthetist's own ad hoc entry.

Every change to a Booking, whether it's a new Booking, a modification or a cancellation, is recorded with its source in the Booking's [change history](US-02.5.5). A Booking that moves to another day arrives as a cancellation and a new Booking ([US-02.5.2](US-02.5.2)).

In the first release nothing is applied automatically. Hospital data lands on a review and merge screen: the office accepts rows that match nothing as new Bookings, and approves or rejects the differences shown for a matched Booking. Applying changes automatically is Future Work ([FT-02.5](FT-02.5)).

Inbound changes, through the review and merge step, are accepted until the office authorises the List, the same editing window as the anaesthetist's and the office's own changes ([US-02.5.4](US-02.5.4)).

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change is validated against the system's internal data model rather than the incoming message, so there is one consistent validation gate however a booking change arrived.
