A List leaves the anaesthetist's [main view](US-07.4.2) once the office has finalised it and sent it through to invoicing, with no more questions on it. It does not leave when the anaesthetist submits it, and not when payment is received.

Its invoices then appear as lines in the anaesthetist's [outstanding balances](US-12.2.1). The List stays findable afterwards, through the archive or search and the [calendar of past work](US-03.1.6).

This is so the main view holds only work still in progress, while the anaesthetist can still look back at what they entered when an old invoice is queried.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a submitted List the office has not yet finalised, then it still shows on the anaesthetist's main view.
- Given a List the office has finalised and sent through to invoicing, then it no longer shows on the main view, and its invoices show in the anaesthetist's outstanding balances.
- Given a List that has left the main view, when the anaesthetist searches for it or opens its day, then they can still open it and its Bookings.

## Notes

From the 7 October client meeting (OQ-31; Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #2 #41): "once the office staff have finalised it and sent it through to invoicing", with "the ability to go back and look at old invoices". The room's words are close to, not the same as, the RFP's "once its invoices are generated", so the exact trigger is to verify with AA. AA (2026-09-29): it is the anaesthetists' call, though they need to see their own work; a List should move from unbilled to billed and processed, not simply vanish.
