What the anaesthetist records on a Procedure follows its Contract's [pricing kind](US-04.2.2). On a Contract priced from RVG units, the RVG code, the start and finish times and the modifiers step are required; choosing no modifier is a valid answer. On a fixed or open price the form collapses: an RVG code may be picked for reference only, and no times or modifiers are asked for.

This is so the anaesthetist fills in only what prices the Procedure, and can still keep the RVG basis for a price that is queried.

An anaesthetist who records RVG detail for their own reference but charges their own price has two routes to the same invoice:

- stay on [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3), record the RVG code, times and modifiers, and [override the price](US-03.5.1), the RVG detail staying recorded and visible
- or choose [No contract · Fixed price](US-04.3.9), type the price and optionally pick the RVG code for reference

## Acceptance criteria

- **RVG kind.** Given a Procedure on a Contract priced from RVG units, such as No contract · RVG, then it cannot be completed without an RVG code, times and a completed modifiers step.
- **Collapsed.** Given a Procedure on a fixed-price Contract, then no times or modifiers are asked for, and an RVG code can be picked but is not required.
- **Kept for reference.** Given RVG detail recorded and the price overridden, then the detail stays on the Procedure and is shown.

Donald's gut was an optional RVG code with no time or modifiers.
