Once the incoming data shapes are understood, the system matches incoming hospital rows to Lists and Bookings itself, and puts updates to a matched Booking, such as changed times, onto an admin queue for approval; whether that queue can later be cleared automatically for routine changes is not yet decided. [Anything it cannot match](US-02.1.4), or that looks wrong (a double booking, a patient who has disappeared or appeared), still goes to an admin for review.

This is so the office's daily check of the hospital sheets shrinks to the approvals and exceptions.

## Notes

Today's process, which the first release keeps, is a [manual matching review](US-02.1.2). In the future state the hospital's data may define the Contract, as AA answered in OQ-22 (Notes 2026-09-29 #7); the detail, such as a procedure not on the selected Contract, was left as future work without agreeing it (Notes 2026-10-07 · AA meeting with Greg #57 #58).
