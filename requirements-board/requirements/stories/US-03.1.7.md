The anaesthetist searches by a patient's NHI or name and gets the matching Bookings, and from a Booking its Procedures.

This is so they can find a patient's past work without knowing the day it happened.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an NHI, when the anaesthetist searches for it, then that patient's Bookings show.
- Given part of a name, when the anaesthetist searches for it, then the matching patients' Bookings show.

## Notes

Greg mentioned the health "three-way match" (name, date of birth, NHI) for identity; not taken further. Which apps besides the anaesthetist's offer this search is not stated.
