Each anaesthetist's profile holds their own list of [prepaid procedures and RVG groups](US-06.1.1), as part of the prepayment flow (see EP-06). The [prepaid amount for each procedure](US-06.2.2) comes from the anaesthetist's own fixed-price [Contract](US-04.2.1), a first-party Contract that applies to their own bookings only. The anaesthetist supplies their price list and the office sets up and maintains the Contract; the anaesthetist does not edit it in the app (OQ-91).

A Procedure on this list [must be prepaid](US-06.2.1) before the anaesthetist carries it out.

## Notes

Retired 16 October: prepaid procedures are no longer on the profile; the anaesthetist tells the office which procedures and prices, and the office sets up a Contract flagged as prepaid (FT-06.2). Greg: prepaids are "not really" part of the profile.
