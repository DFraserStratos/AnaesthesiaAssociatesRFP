Instead of [entering a missing Booking manually](US-02.4.1), the anaesthetist can photograph the physical hospital or surgeon booking card.

The system reads the photo and pre-fills the Booking, so the anaesthetist only needs to check it and confirm, rather than type everything in by hand. The Booking then flows back to the Scheduling Engine like any other.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Photo capture.** Given a photo of a hospital or surgeon booking card, when the anaesthetist takes it in the app, then the system pre-fills a new Booking on their List from it, for them to check and confirm.
