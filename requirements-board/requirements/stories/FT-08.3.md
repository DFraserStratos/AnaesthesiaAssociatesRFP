Every procedure invoice the Billing/Invoice Engine raises creates a linked pair in its own ledger: a receivable from the billable party and a payable to the anaesthetist. For a [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) the money still comes to AA, but the payable is to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) instead: AA holds it there, not released to the anaesthetist until the procedure is done, when a second pair moves it to them ([settlement](US-06.4.1)). The monthly [AA fee invoice](FT-10.3) is AA charging the anaesthetist, so it has no payable. The ledger tracks these per patient and per anaesthetist, and keeps that history independent of what happens to the [matching contact in Xero](FT-09.3).
