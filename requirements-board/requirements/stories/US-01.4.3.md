In their app, an anaesthetist selects one of their Lists and starts a move, then either:

- moves it to the AA office, where it becomes a [Draft List](FT-01.6), or
- uses the [availability view](US-01.4.2) to push it into a free Slot of a colleague

To move a single Booking rather than the whole List, see [moving a single Booking](US-01.4.7).

The colleague does not need to accept, and the office does not confirm the move first: this is a high-trust system. An anaesthetist who withdraws from a List also returns it to a Draft List, so it can be assigned to someone else.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an anaesthetist moves a List to the office or a colleague, then a notification posts to the Admin App's [shared notification pool](FT-13.8).
- Given a List pushed into a colleague's free Slot, then the colleague sees it in their app with a notice.
- Given an anaesthetist moves a List to the office or a colleague, then they are asked what their vacated Slot becomes, free or unavailable.

## Notes

Who is told of the move (OQ-65): the office is notified through the Admin App's shared notification pool, and can send the cover-change [update email](US-02.3.3) to the hospital from the on-demand button, picking the move from the change history (OQ-69). The colleague sees the List appear in their app with a notice.

The Slot left behind when a whole List moves (OQ-84): whoever moves a List, the anaesthetist or the office, and to a colleague or the office, is asked what the vacated Slot becomes, free or unavailable. One path for everyone; Greg: "One path is the answer."
