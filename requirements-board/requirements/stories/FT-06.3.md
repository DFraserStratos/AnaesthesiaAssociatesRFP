Prepayment invoices go through the same [internal ledger](FT-08.3) and [Xero pairing](US-09.1.1) as any other invoice, each creating its own [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1), with one difference: the payable credits the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not the anaesthetist. The anaesthetist is paid later by a transfer out of the holding account.

## Notes

Greg, 28 October: "It's not the same as any other invoice because it doesn't credit the anaesthetist, it credits the holding account."
