On a Booking, the user presses a button against a Procedure's [Contract line](US-04.2.4) to split the invoiced fee from that Contract between several billable parties. The Billing/Invoice Engine divides the line item and invoices each party its share as a separate invoice, for example the insurer for its portion and the patient for the gap. Each party's share is a typed value in $ or % (for example $48 or 48%). Splitting is a user action on the Booking: there is no setting on the Contract or the Procedure, and no default from the Contract.

This lets a Procedure that is only partly covered still be billed correctly on both sides, without the patient being charged for the covered portion or the insurer being charged for the gap.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure on a Booking, when the user presses the split button on its Contract line, then they can divide the invoiced fee between several billable parties.
- Given shares typed as a $ amount or a %, when billed, then each party is invoiced its share on its own invoice.
- Given a Contract, then it carries no full payment or split setting, and a Booking never defaults a split from it.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: AA is asked to advise whether cover split between an insurer and the patient is a real case. AA's answer (OQ-23) confirmed it is. On 2026-10-05 Donald ruled that the split is a user action on the Booking, not a Contract setting (the former payment setting, US-04.2.12, is retired). The basis follows OQ-68, where AA left the choice to us ("You pick what's best. We can edit after."): a typed value in $ or %.

## Notes

Split basis is our pick under OQ-68, to pressure test with AA, which can change it later. Whether % shares must total 100, and what the line items say, are not settled. Adding an extra invoice on top of a Contract line is the separate [additional invoice](US-08.6.3).
