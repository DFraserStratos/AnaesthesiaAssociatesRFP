The Billing/Invoice Engine can regenerate any past invoice exactly, from the Procedure data and Contract version that were [locked in at the time](US-04.3.5).

This is so an invoice raised under an older Contract version can always be reproduced later, even after the Contract has since changed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: [full change history](US-02.5.5) and invoice reproducibility are required across roughly 28,000 invoices a year. Azure SQL temporal tables at the data layer act as the audit log, so no write path can bypass it and point-in-time reconstruction is a direct query rather than a log replay. Trigger-based audit tables were considered workable but more bespoke and weaker at point-in-time reconstruction; a domain-event log may still be used where the reason for a change matters, but not as the primary history mechanism. The invoice itself stays reproducible because the reference data it depended on is copied into the billing record at AUTHORISED (see US-04.3.5).

## Notes

Clarified 2026-10-02: reproducing an invoice means keeping what it was generated from, its "recipe", so the invoice as raised can be produced again even after, for example, the rate or the patient's address has changed. It is not a mechanism to go back to another point in time and generate a new invoice from the data as it then stood. How it is kept is a design matter (Donald: "I don't know exactly how we're going to implement this").
