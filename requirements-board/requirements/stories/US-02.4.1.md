When the hospital's list differs from what's in the app, the anaesthetist can add the missing Booking directly to their List by entering it manually.

They can type the procedure as written on the hospital's list into an optional "as given" field, kept as its [source wording](US-02.5.7).

This Booking flows back to the Scheduling Engine like any other.

## Notes

Merges the former US-02.4.2 (Photo capture of the physical card), now Retired. On 2026-10-02 photo capture was split out again into its own story, [add a Booking from a photo of the booking card](US-02.4.4), which is Future Work: Greg expects one of the two "to get done and the other one's not".

In theatre the anaesthetist must still be able to create a Booking on the fly, even if surgeons' rooms are one day able to add Lists themselves (a wish raised in the meeting, not a decision).
