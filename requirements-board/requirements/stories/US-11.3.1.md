Admins find patients on the Patients tab of the [People section](US-13.4.1) of the Admin app. The list can be searched and is paged, and has three filters:

- **Coming up**, the default: patients with a Booking coming up.
- **Needs follow-up**: every patient [needing the office](US-11.3.3), with the reason on each row.
- **All**: every patient.

A row opens the [patient's page](US-11.1.1), which shows all their invoices, each paid, part paid or unpaid, across every anaesthetist the patient has been booked with, with the balance.

This gives AA a single view of a patient's billing history, since each anaesthetist otherwise bills separately, and puts the patients the office is about to see first.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Coming up by default.** Given the office opens the Patients tab, then the Coming up filter is selected and lists the patients with a Booking coming up.
- **Search and paging.** Given a long list, when the office searches by name or NHI, then only matching patients show, a page at a time.
- **Invoices across anaesthetists.** Given a patient billed by two anaesthetists, when the office opens the patient's page, then both anaesthetists' invoices show, each paid, part paid or unpaid, with the balance.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the [system of record](US-08.3.2), with a dedicated Xero instance as an accounts-receivable and banking add-on only. Because the response follows RFP Appendix 2 and keeps the NHI out of Xero, looking a patient up by NHI happens in the billing system rather than directly in Xero. This is settled: there is no patient data in Xero, only a unique ID that links each transaction back to its invoice in our system (OQ-30).
