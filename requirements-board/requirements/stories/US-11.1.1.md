The system keeps exactly one patient record per NHI, holding contact details and, where supplied, demographics and [ethnicity coded to NZHIS Level 4](FT-14.4). This information can be supplied by the hospital, the surgeon, or AA itself.

When a patient returns, the system matches them to this existing record by their NHI, even when their name or address has changed since their last visit, rather than creating a new one.

This is so every clinical episode for the same patient links back to a single record, whoever supplied it, and the same patient is never duplicated just because their details were recorded differently at check-in.

Demographics and ethnicity are held and shown as information when supplied, and are never required.

Every patient record has an NHI from the moment it is created, with no blank. A booking that arrives without one waits at intake and creates no patient record (see [patient without NHI](US-11.1.4)).

Each patient has a page in the Admin app, opened from the [Patients tab](US-11.3.1) and keyed by the NHI, which never changes. The page follows the same pattern as every other record's page:

- The NHI sits beside the patient's name. Any internal identifier the system keeps is never shown.
- The patient's details sit in the side panel, with their own Edit. Saving them is one audited change.
- The main column holds the patient's [invoices](US-11.3.1), alerts and [follow-up log](US-11.3.3).

When a returning patient's details arrive and differ from the record, they are held for the office and never written over it. The patient's page shows the differences, and the office keeps the record as it is or uses the new details.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Dedupe on NHI.** When a returning patient is entered, the system matches them to their existing record by NHI, even when their name or address has changed since their last visit, so no duplicate record is created.
- **Page keyed by NHI.** Given a patient opened from the Patients tab, then their page shows the NHI beside their name and no internal identifier.
- **Details edited in the side panel.** Given the office edits the patient's details in the side panel and saves, then the record changes and the change is audited.
- **Details that differ.** Given a returning patient's details arrive and differ from the record, then the record is unchanged, the differences show on the patient's page, and the office can keep the record or use the new details.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the NHI is treated as the most restricted element in the system and is held in the core platform only, with lookups against the [NHI FHIR API via the Health NZ Digital Services Hub](US-14.4.1). It is not used as a billing key and [does not cross into Xero](US-15.0.6).

The response follows RFP Appendix 2 (NHI never leaves the core system). This is settled: there is no patient data in Xero, so the NHI is never held there, and Xero holds only a unique ID that links each transaction back to its invoice in our system (OQ-30).

NHI is the patient identifier throughout the system, billing and ledger records included, and the dedupe key; any internal patient ID is only an implementation detail. The NHI never reaches Xero, which holds only a unique ID linking back to the invoice (OQ-30).
