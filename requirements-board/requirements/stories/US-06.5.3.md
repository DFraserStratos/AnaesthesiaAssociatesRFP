When a prepaid Booking is cancelled and the procedure is later rebooked as a new Booking with a different anaesthetist, that new Booking starts its own prepayment: the system raises a new prepayment for the new anaesthetist, at the new anaesthetist's own fixed price for the procedure, from [their own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14).

The original prepayment is refunded ([refund on cancellation](US-06.5.2)), not moved between anaesthetists, so the patient may see two different amounts. A Booking that moves to another anaesthetist without being cancelled keeps its prepayment instead ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

This is so each new Booking is prepaid at its own anaesthetist's price.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a cancelled and refunded prepayment, when the procedure is rebooked as a new Booking under an anaesthetist with a different fixed price for it, then a new prepayment is raised at their price.
