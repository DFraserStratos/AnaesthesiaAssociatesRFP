Admins hold each anaesthetist's master record: their contact details, registration number, HPI CPN (Common Person Number) where available, and bank account details.

This is so the anaesthetist's identity can be referenced consistently across the system, in line with the NZ Health Provider Index standard (the HPI CPN is the practitioner's single HPI identifier), and so AA has what it needs to pay them.

The bank account details are used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Bank details.** Admins hold each anaesthetist's bank account details, used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid. Bank details are held in the system, on the anaesthetist's profile (OQ-14), not in Xero only.

## Notes

Merges the former US-12.1.5 (Bank details for disbursement), now Retired. US-12.1.5 was Open pending OQ-14 (whether anaesthetist bank accounts live in the system, in Xero only, or both). OQ-14 is now answered: bank details live in the system, on the anaesthetist's profile.

The HPI and the CPN are one identifier, not two fields: Health NZ writes it as "HPI CPN (Common Person Number)", and AA agreed to call it the HPI CPN (OQ-52).
