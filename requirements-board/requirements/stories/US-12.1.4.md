Each anaesthetist has a page in the People section of the Admin app, reached from the Anaesthetists list and keyed by their registration number, which never changes. The office holds the anaesthetist's master record there, on cards:

- their name, registration number and HPI CPN (Common Person Number)
- their contact details
- their bank account details
- their [dollar value per unit](US-12.1.1) and their [offer order](US-01.3.6), on one card

This is so the anaesthetist's identity can be referenced consistently across the system, in line with the NZ Health Provider Index standard (the HPI CPN is the practitioner's single HPI identifier), so AA has what it needs to pay them, and so everything about one anaesthetist is on one screen.

Each card is edited and saved on its own, with Edit and Save, and each save is one audited change. Only the office sees or sets the offer order.

A side panel shows what the anaesthetist is on: their [preferred](US-13.6.4) and [not-preferred](US-13.6.3) pairings with surgeons, the Contracts linked to them, and their next Lists, a few of each with a way to see all. The office also [makes the anaesthetist inactive](US-12.1.6) from the page.

The Anaesthetists list is searched, filtered (by offer order among others) and paged.

The bank account details are used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Bank details.** Admins hold each anaesthetist's bank account details, used for the payables run: the disbursement of funds from AA's account to the anaesthetist once their receivable is paid. Bank details are held in the system, on the anaesthetist's page (OQ-14), not in Xero only.
- **HPI CPN and bank details mandatory.** Given an anaesthetist's master record, when it is saved without an HPI CPN or without bank details, then it is refused.
- **One card at a time.** Given the office edits the contact card, when it saves, then only the contact details change, and the change is one entry in the audit.
- **Rate and offer order together.** Given an anaesthetist on Standard, when the office chooses Edit on the rate and offer order card, picks Second and saves, then their offer order is Offer second; picking one without Save changes nothing.
- **Side panel.** Given an anaesthetist with eight upcoming Lists, when the office opens their page, then the side panel shows the next few Lists and a way to see all eight, alongside their pairings and the Contracts linked to them.
