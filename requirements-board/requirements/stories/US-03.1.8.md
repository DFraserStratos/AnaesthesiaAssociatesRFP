When a Booking has been prepaid, the anaesthetist sees its prepaid amount, both when they open the Booking and when they record their units. The amount is the Procedure's price when its prepayment invoice was raised, read-only from then on ([set the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2)).

This is so anaesthetists can see what has been prepaid, as the directors asked.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Shown.** Given a prepaid Booking, when the anaesthetist opens it or records their units, then they see the prepaid amount.

## Notes

The directors, 6 October: "Anaesthetists want to view the amount that has been pre-paid on a booking." This settles the guide's open point in favour of showing it, over Greg's view that "clinical work should be kept apart from billing work".

Read again on 16 October and confirmed.
