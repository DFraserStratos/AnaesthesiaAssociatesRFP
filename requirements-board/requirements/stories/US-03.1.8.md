When a Booking has been prepaid, the anaesthetist sees the [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2) on it, both when they open the Booking and when they record their units.

This is because the directors want anaesthetists to see what has been prepaid: "Anaesthetists want to view the amount that has been pre-paid on a booking."

## Acceptance criteria

- **Shown.** Given a prepaid Booking, when the anaesthetist opens it or records their units, then they see the prepaid amount.

## Notes

This settles the guide's open point in favour of showing it, over Greg's view that "clinical work should be kept apart from billing work".
