An admin can view and edit an anaesthetist's prepaid settings on their behalf.

The prepaid amounts themselves sit on the anaesthetist's [own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14), which the office always creates and maintains from the price list the anaesthetist supplies (OQ-91).

Retired 16 October with FT-06.1. The office keeps each anaesthetist's price list Contract ([own price list](US-04.2.14)), as before (OQ-91).
