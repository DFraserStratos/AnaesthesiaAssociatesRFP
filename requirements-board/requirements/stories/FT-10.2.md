Once a receivable is paid, its matching payable is authorised so it can be included in the next payables run.

This is so the anaesthetist is paid only what AA has received for their work.

The payment to the anaesthetist is made in Xero's payables run; the system [records and tracks it](US-09.2.4), and does not make the payment itself (OQ-111).
