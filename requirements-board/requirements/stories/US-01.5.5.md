When an anaesthetist marks an AM or PM Slot unavailable while it holds a List, the app asks what happens to the List:

- return it to the office, where it becomes a [Draft List](FT-01.6) with its hospital, surgeon and Bookings, for the office to resolve, or
- assign it to an available colleague, as when they [move their own List](US-01.4.3)

This is so a List is never left with an anaesthetist who will not be there.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Slot holding a List, when the anaesthetist marks it unavailable, then they are offered "return to the office" or "assign to another anaesthetist".
- Given "return to the office", then the List keeps its hospital, surgeon and Bookings and becomes a Draft List.
- Given "assign", then the List moves into an available colleague's Slot.

## Notes

Settled by OQ-64. Greg: the List "reverts to a draft list"; Donald: "return to office or assign to an available anaesthetist". Handling several Lists at once (a series of days off) was not discussed. Applying the [notifications](US-01.4.3) of moving their own List, and [no preference warning](US-01.4.5), to "assign" is an assumed reading.
