A Contract is a dated set of terms held by one contract holder: a header (its name, dates and settings) and lines. A line sets the terms for a group of procedures (for example Cataracts) and carries only what it sets:

- a fixed price
- [a rate](US-05.2.6)
- [a discount](US-05.4.3)
- base or modifier units
- a default RVG code (agreed in principle; Ben has not yet been asked)

A Contract may have no lines at all, such as a hospital's plain RVG Contract.

AA deals with many different pricing arrangements across hospitals, surgeons, surgeon groups and insurers, so the Contract needs one consistent, reusable structure that covers all of them.

See domain-model.md for the narrative model.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 and its ERD ([data model](AR-29#data-model), [ERD](AR-30)) give the reference shape:

- RVG groups as published
- a contract holder holds many contracts
- a contract, kept as dated versions, holds its lines
- a booking procedure is priced under one contract and at most one of its lines

Greg likes the settings as a "neat little... switches table" but doubts it is flexible in the long term; he leaves the wording of the RVG calculation to the developers. Where the documents differ internally (for example whether a booking procedure's contract is required), the catalogue follows the requirement stories.
