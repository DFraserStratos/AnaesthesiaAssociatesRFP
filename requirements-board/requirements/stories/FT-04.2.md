A Contract is one priced item held by a [Party](US-04.1.5). Its settings say who it bills, where it is offered, how it is priced and how it appears on an invoice.

This is so every arrangement AA holds, from a hospital schedule to one anaesthetist's prepay item, has one consistent shape.

No setting is dated: a change is an audited edit, and a setting that must change from a date means a new Contract. Only a fixed price is dated, through its price ranges.

## Technical discussion

The flat model is AR-58 sections 2 and 5; Greg's technical design v6 and its ERD are historical.
