A Contract is a dated set of terms held by one contract holder. Its lines each set the terms for one procedure; no Contract sets one price for a whole [RVG group](US-05.1.1).

A line carries only what it sets:

- a fixed price
- [a contract unit rate](US-05.2.6)
- [a discount](US-05.4.3)
- base or modifier units that differ from the procedure's

Everything else shows through from the procedure and its RVG group.

AA deals with many different pricing arrangements across hospitals, surgeons, surgeon groups and insurers, so the Contract needs one consistent, reusable structure that covers all of them.

See domain-model.md for the narrative model.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design v4 and its ERD ([data model](AR-29#p3), [ERD](AR-30)) give the reference shape:

- a contract holder holds many contracts
- a contract holds its contract lines, each setting terms for one procedure
- each procedure belongs to one RVG group
- each booking procedure (one procedure on one Booking) is priced under one contract

They are a draft for Greg's review and may change before the catch-up build. Greg is to review the technical design and update his database model.
