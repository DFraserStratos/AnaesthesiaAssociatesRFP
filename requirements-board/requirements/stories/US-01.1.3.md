When an admin adds a new anaesthetist, the Scheduling Engine creates their Slots from their start date across the horizon (four months in current practice).

This is so the canvas is ready to accept bookings from day one, with no gap while Slots catch up.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: when an anaesthetist joins, their forward canvas is generated automatically, so coverage exists before any booking activity begins.

## Notes

Once created, the Slots can be edited freely.
