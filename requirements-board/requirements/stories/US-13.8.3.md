An admin resolves a notice by ticking it. It is then hidden for the whole team. A notice has two states only, active and resolved, and never expires or is archived by itself. The same tick [clears a to-do](US-13.7.2).

The system keeps who resolved a notice and when.

This is so a notice stays in front of the office until someone has dealt with it, and one person's reading of a notice never makes it look dealt with to everyone else. Greg: "It's a tick box thing."

## Acceptance criteria

- **Resolve.** Given an active notice, when an admin ticks it, then it is hidden for every admin.
- **Kept.** Given a resolved notice, then who resolved it and when are recorded.
- **No expiry.** Given a notice nobody has ticked, then it stays active however long it has been there.

## Technical discussion

Claude's proposal, for AA to check: the dashboard panel has a "show resolved" filter that brings resolved notices back, showing who resolved each and when. Hidden means filtered out of the default view, not deleted.
