When the surgeon's office cancels the request behind a Draft List, or no anaesthetist can be found, an admin removes the Draft List or edits its date.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a cancelled request, when the admin removes the Draft List, then it no longer shows and the removal is recorded in the [audit trail](US-13.5.2).
- Given a Draft List, when the admin edits its date, then it shows on the new day.

## Notes

Closing it with a recorded reason (the OQ-44 recommendation) was not discussed.
