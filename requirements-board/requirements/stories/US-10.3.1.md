An admin raises every anaesthetist's AA fee invoice for the month in one run: one action generates each fee invoice for the period, from the [fee settings](US-10.3.3), and tracks each one in the ledger.

The admin runs it from the AA fees tab of the [Finances section](US-13.3.3), which lists AA's fee invoices to the anaesthetists, opening on the unpaid ones, with the month's run and its preview beside them. AA fee invoices are not on the [Invoices tab](US-08.4.7).

This invoice is kept separate from procedure receivables and payables, since it is AA charging the anaesthetist, not a payment for a procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Fee total.** Given a month with 40 [BCTIs](US-09.1.4) for an anaesthetist, fixed charges of $500 and a charge of $5 per invoice, when the monthly run is made, then their fee invoice is $700.
- **Preview before the run.** Given the month's run has not been made, when an admin previews it, then each anaesthetist's fee invoice and its total are shown and nothing is raised until the run is made.
- **Opens on unpaid.** Given fee invoices from earlier months, some paid, when an admin opens the AA fees tab, then the unpaid fee invoices are listed first.
