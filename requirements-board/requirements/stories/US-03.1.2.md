The anaesthetist can see which Contract applies to each Procedure, both before the session and after it: its name and [holder](US-04.1.5), who is invoiced, and its pricing basis with the figure, shown with the procedure text as received ([descriptions and Contract shown together](US-03.1.9)).

This is so they know how a Procedure will be billed ahead of time, and can check what actually applied once it is recorded.
