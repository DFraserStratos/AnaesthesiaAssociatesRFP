The anaesthetist can see which Contract applies to each Procedure, both before the session and after it: its name and holder, who is invoiced, and its pricing basis with the figure, shown under the source wording and the procedure ([the three-part stack](US-03.1.9)).

This is so they know how a Procedure will be billed ahead of time, and can check what actually applied once it is recorded.
