The anaesthetist can type a Procedure's price on a Contract priced from RVG units whose [setting allows it](US-04.2.2). The RVG detail stays recorded and the computed price is kept beside it, so the full calculation is never lost.

This is so an anaesthetist can charge their own price where the Contract allows it, without losing the work recorded behind it.

The anaesthetist can never type a price on a [fixed price](US-05.2.5) (OQ-117) or a prepaid Contract (OQ-96). On a Contract that does not allow it, the price stands until the anaesthetist changes the Contract or the office changes the price. On [No contract · Fixed price](US-04.3.9) the anaesthetist types the amount.

No reason is needed; the change is [audited](US-13.5.2) and flagged at review beside the computed price. A $0 price gives a no-charge invoice.

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal A49): a typed price is flagged at review beside the computed price.

Whether the anaesthetist can change a Contract's price is the flag on each Contract, set by AA (OQ-107, answered 19 October), except that a fixed price never allows the anaesthetist's price override (OQ-117, answered 29 October).

Ben, 5 November: one anaesthetist records his RVG codes, time and modifiers for his own reference but charges his own price: "I've got that recorded, but blow that, I'm just going to do my fixed price." Donald's D9, later that day: "Where the Contract allows it, the anaesthetist can override an RVG price with the RVG detail kept."
