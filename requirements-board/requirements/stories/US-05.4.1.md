An anaesthetist adjustment, a typed price or a percentage discount, applies only on [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3) and [first-party Contracts](US-04.1.1). It never applies:

- on a third-party Contract, whose agreed price stands
- to change or remove a [fixed discount](US-05.4.3) already set by the Contract (whether the anaesthetist can add their own on top is OQ-89)

Where allowed, the system applies a percentage discount, or replaces the whole total with a fixed final price. Either way, it records the fee as it stood before the adjustment, so the full calculation is never lost.

A 100% discount or a $0 price gives a no-charge invoice.
