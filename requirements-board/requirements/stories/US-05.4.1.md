An anaesthetist adjustment, a changed price, rate or percentage discount, applies where the Contract lets the anaesthetist change their pricing ([typical arrangements](US-04.1.1)), and not to a [discount such a Contract fixes](US-05.4.3). The Contract carries this as a flag set by AA, not a global rule. On a Contract that does not let them, the agreed price stands until the anaesthetist changes the Contract or the office changes the price.

Where allowed, the system applies a changed rate, a percentage discount, or replaces the whole total with a fixed final price. Either way, it records the fee as it stood before the adjustment, so the full calculation is never lost.

A 100% discount or a $0 price gives a no-charge invoice.

No reason is needed; the change is audited and flagged at review.

## Notes

Whether the anaesthetist can change a Contract's price is the flag on each Contract, set by AA (OQ-107, answered 19 October).
