The system consumes FHIR R4 Appointment, Patient and Practitioner resources directly from hospitals that support FHIR.

Where a hospital sends HL7 v2 instead, the system translates it into the same [internal booking model](US-14.2.1) as a FHIR message, so downstream processing is consistent regardless of source format.
