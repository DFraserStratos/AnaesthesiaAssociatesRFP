An admin can create an out-of-hours List: a List after the PM session, for any specialty, created on the day if need be. Like any List it has an anaesthetist, surgeon, hospital and day, and it has its own start time. The anaesthetist sees it on their day after their AM and PM Lists, and its Bookings are recorded and billed as on any other List.

This is so emergency and late cases are booked and billed through Lists like everything else.

## Acceptance criteria

- **After the PM session.** Given an emergency case at 6pm, when an admin creates an out-of-hours List for an anaesthetist that day, then it shows on the anaesthetist's day after the PM session, with its own start time.
- **Same day.** Given an emergency case raised this evening, when an admin creates an out-of-hours List for today, then it is saved and shows on the anaesthetist's day.

## Technical discussion

Accepted, but not yet designed. It sits beside the [two Slots per anaesthetist per day](US-01.1.1), which are AM and PM only. Still to design:

- whether it is a third kind of Slot or a List outside the Slots (a List's pairing names its session, AM or PM), and whether an anaesthetist can have more than one in an evening
- its default times ([Slot default times](US-01.1.4))
- who may create one when the office is closed, and whether the anaesthetist can
- how the [availability finder](US-01.4.2) treats the evening, since anaesthetists set availability for AM and PM only.
