An admin can open an out-of-hours Slot for an anaesthetist on a day: a third Slot, after the PM session, that holds an out-of-hours List for emergency and late cases in any specialty. The office can open one for any day from today, today included, to the end of the schedule. Like the AM and PM Slots it holds at most one List, and it has default times (starting at 18:00) that the Slot can override.

This is so emergency and late cases are booked and billed through Lists like everything else.

- **Opened by the office only.** The Scheduling Engine does not create out-of-hours Slots: a day has none for an anaesthetist until the office opens one, and never more than one. Recurring bookings never land on one. The anaesthetist does not open one.
- **A session like AM and PM.** A newly opened out-of-hours Slot is free. The anaesthetist can mark it available or unavailable, as for their [AM and PM sessions](US-01.2.1), and the [availability finder](US-01.4.2) offers it when it is free. Marking it unavailable while it holds a List follows the [same rule as any session](US-01.5.5): the List goes back to the office or to a colleague.
- **Any List can go in.** From the open Slot the office assigns a new List (surgeon and hospital), or assigns a [Draft List](US-01.6.1). A Draft List can be for a day's out-of-hours session, so an evening request is logged before anyone is found to do it.
- **Shown and billed like any List.** The anaesthetist sees the out-of-hours Slot on their day after their AM and PM sessions. Its Bookings are captured, submitted, authorised and billed as on any other List.
- **Closing it.** The office can close an out-of-hours Slot that holds no List; it then disappears from the anaesthetist's day.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Opened by the office.** Given an emergency case at 6pm, when an admin opens an out-of-hours Slot for an anaesthetist that day, then it shows on the anaesthetist's day after the PM session, free, starting at 18:00 by default.
- **Same day.** Given an emergency case raised this evening, when an admin opens an out-of-hours Slot for today and assigns a List to it, then the List is saved and shows on the anaesthetist's day.
- **None until opened.** Given an anaesthetist with no out-of-hours Slot on a day, then their day shows only the AM and PM sessions, and the anaesthetist has no way to open one.
- **One a day.** Given an anaesthetist already has an out-of-hours Slot on a day, then the office cannot open a second one for that day.
- **Own times.** Given an open out-of-hours Slot, when an admin gives it its own start time, then it keeps that time while the default stays 18:00.
- **Availability.** Given an open, free out-of-hours Slot, when the availability finder is used for that day, then the anaesthetist is offered as free for the out-of-hours session; when the anaesthetist marks it unavailable, then they are not.
- **Draft List for the evening.** Given a Draft List created for a day's out-of-hours session, when an admin assigns it to an anaesthetist with an open, free out-of-hours Slot that day, then it becomes an active List in that Slot.
- **Marked unavailable while booked.** Given an out-of-hours Slot holding a List, when the anaesthetist marks it unavailable, then they must return the List to the office or give it to a colleague, as for any session.
- **Close an empty one.** Given an open out-of-hours Slot holding no List, when an admin closes it, then it no longer shows on the anaesthetist's day; one holding a List cannot be closed.

## Technical discussion

The out-of-hours session is a third session value beside AM and PM, so a List's pairing (anaesthetist, surgeon, hospital, day, session) names it like any other. Its default times sit with the AM and PM defaults in [Slot default times](US-01.1.4). Still open: who may open one when the office is closed (an evening call after hours).
