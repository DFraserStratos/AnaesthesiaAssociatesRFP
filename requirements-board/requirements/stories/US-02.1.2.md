For each List that arrives on the matching screen, from a hospital or a rooms' PDF, the office decides what happens to it. It decides a whole List at a time: the List's changes are applied together, and any one Booking's change can be left out.

This is so the schedule reflects what the rooms and hospitals sent, and one bad row never holds up the rest of its List.

When the List matches one AA already holds, its changes go to that List and its Bookings, after the office has seen [why it matched and what changes](US-02.1.3). When it matches none, the office can:

- add its Bookings to an existing List
- place it as a new List in an anaesthetist's Slot: the Slot of the anaesthetist the PDF or hospital List names is suggested when it is free
- place it in another anaesthetist's free Slot
- make it a [Draft List](US-01.6.1) when there is nobody to place it with
- reject it

A row with [no NHI](US-11.1.4) cannot become a Booking: it waits in the [feed](US-02.1.4) under Needs NHI until the office adds the NHI.

Each row shows its procedure text exactly as received, so the office can read it while deciding ([kept as received](US-02.5.7)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **As received.** Given an arriving row, then its procedure text shows exactly as it was sent.
- **A whole List.** Given an arriving List with changes to three of its Bookings, when the office leaves one change out and applies the List, then the other two changes are applied and the one left out is not.
- **Named anaesthetist suggested.** Given an arriving List AA does not hold, whose PDF or hospital List names an anaesthetist whose Slot for that day and session is free, then that anaesthetist's Slot is suggested for it.
- **Placed elsewhere.** Given an arriving List AA does not hold, then the office can instead place it in another anaesthetist's free Slot, or make it a Draft List.
- **Draft List from intake.** Given an arriving List with no anaesthetist to place it with, when the office makes it a Draft List, then a Draft List holding its Bookings is created for its hospital, surgeon, day and session.
- **Reject.** Given the office rejects an arriving List, then nothing is created or changed on the schedule.
