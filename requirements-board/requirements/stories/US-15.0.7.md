When the office changes a Booking or a List, or a change to one arrives from a hospital, the anaesthetist sees the change in their app without signing out and back in. At the latest it shows when they go back to the List and open the Booking again, or refresh the page.

The change need not appear live on a screen the anaesthetist already has open, as it would in a shared document.

This is so anaesthetists always work from the current Booking. Today they must sign out and back in to see an office change, which frustrates them.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No sign-out.** Given the office changes a Booking's time, when the anaesthetist goes back to their List and opens the Booking again, then the new time shows, without signing out.
- **Refresh.** Given a Booking open on the anaesthetist's phone, when they refresh, then any change made since shows.

## Technical discussion

The mechanism is open: pull to refresh (Donald), a refresh button (Ben), or the app checking for updates in the background, say every minute (Donald: "maybe I should pause before I commit to anything"). Live updates on a screen already open are not required.
