An admin can split a combined cosmetic Procedure, such as an abdominoplasty, breast lift and liposuction, into one free-form [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) per component, attached to the Procedure, when a billable party asks for it: for example, the patient receives the invoice and Southern Cross asks for it split three ways.

This is so each party can account for its share in the pieces it needs.

Such a combination is usually prepaid or fixed price, billed as one Procedure on a [fixed-price Contract of its own](US-04.2.11), with no parent procedures.

How a split is made:

- Before the List is authorised, a split is a normal admin edit of the Booking's Procedures, Contracts and prices, with no credit or rebill.
- After the combined invoice is sent, the usual case, a split is a credit and rebill (OQ-72): first a credit note against the original from the [credit note option](US-08.6.5), to any party including the original billable party, which reverses the [linked payable](US-08.3.1) to the anaesthetist; then one additional invoice per component.
- The rebuilt invoices add up to the credited combined invoice. The office splits the total freely across them, not by the components' old individual prices.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One per component.** An admin can create several additional invoices from one combined Procedure, each with its own amount.
- **Before sending.** Given a combined Procedure whose List is not yet authorised, when a split is asked for, then the admin edits the Booking's Procedures, Contracts and prices, and no credit is raised.
- **Same total.** Given a credited combined invoice, then the components rebilled from it must add up to the credited amount; each component's amount is entered freely, not taken from its individual price.
- **Payable reversed.** Given the credit against the combined invoice, then it reverses the linked payable to the anaesthetist.
- **Traceable.** Every split invoice links back to the Procedure.
