Admins keep AA's list of broad surgical specialties on the Specialties tab of the People section in the Admin app. There are no subspecialties: general surgery, not upper GI. [Surgeons](US-13.6.6) and [Contracts](US-04.2.16) take their specialties from it. Every change is audited.

This is so one shared list narrows the Contracts offered for each surgeon.

Each specialty has its own page, which shows the surgeons who practise it and the Contracts for it. The specialty is renamed or removed there.

Until AA's specialist spreadsheet gives the list, it starts as Ophthalmology, General surgery, Plastic surgery, ENT, Gynaecology, Orthopaedics, Urology, Gastroenterology and Cardiothoracic surgery. Anaesthetists have no specialty.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Broad only.** Given the list, then each specialty is one broad name with no subspecialty under it: general surgery, never upper GI.
- **Shared.** Given a specialty is renamed, then every surgeon and Contract that holds it shows the new name.
- **Specialty page.** Given Ophthalmology is held by three surgeons and two Contracts, when an admin opens its page, then it lists those three surgeons and two Contracts.
