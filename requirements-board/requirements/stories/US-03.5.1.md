What the anaesthetist can change on the price depends on the Procedure's Contract:

- **No contract (RVG), and [the anaesthetist's own first-party Contracts](US-04.2.14):** they may type their own price or apply a percentage discount, with a reason. A 100% discount or a price of $0 gives a no-charge invoice.
- **A third-party Contract** (an insurer, a hospital or a surgeon's rooms): the price is shown read-only and no discount field is offered. To depart from the agreed price the anaesthetist [changes the Contract](US-03.4.1), which also changes who is billed.
- **A Contract with a [fixed discount](US-04.2.2):** its discount is shown, locked, where the price change would be.

Before an entered price or discount can be saved, the anaesthetist must record a reason for it.

This is because AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, who have the final say over their own pricing, while a price agreed with a third party stands. The reason means the office, and the audit trail, always show why a fee was changed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Own pricing.** Given No contract (RVG) or one of the anaesthetist's own first-party Contracts, the anaesthetist can type their own price or enter a percentage discount.
- **Third-party price.** Given a third-party Contract, the price shows read-only and no discount field is offered.
- **Fixed discount.** Given a Contract with a fixed discount, the discount shows where the price change would be, and the anaesthetist cannot edit it.
- **Reason required.** The anaesthetist must record a reason with any entered price or discount before it can be saved.

## Technical discussion

[The draft technical design](AR-29#p6) derives the rule from the Contract: a price or discount may be entered on No contract (RVG) or on a Contract whose holder is first party, and the billing engine rejects one entered on any other Contract. A discount is a percentage only; a flat change is made by typing a price. The office can still [override any price at review](US-05.4.2), with a reason. The design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build; it has no case yet for a fixed discount Contract.
