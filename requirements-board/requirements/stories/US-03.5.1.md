What the anaesthetist can change on the price depends on a flag on the Procedure's Contract, set by AA. It is a per-Contract setting, not a global rule:

- **A Contract that lets the anaesthetist change its pricing** (No contract (RVG), a hospital's RVG Contract, [their own price list](US-04.2.14); on a prepaid Procedure, whether they can is OQ-96): they may change the price, the rate or the discount. A 100% discount or a price of $0 gives a no-charge invoice.
- **A Contract that does not** (a hospital's fixed-price Contract, a surgeon's fixed-fee arrangement): the price is shown read-only and no discount of their own is offered. To depart from it the anaesthetist [changes the Contract](US-03.4.1), which may also change who is billed, or asks the office, which can [change any price](US-05.4.2).
- **A discount the Contract fixes** ([fixed discount](US-05.4.3)): shown where the price change would be; whether the anaesthetist can edit it follows the Contract's setting.

No reason is needed: the change is audited and flagged at office review. The only explanation the system asks for is on a claimed [modifier](US-03.3.4), because insurers ask for it.

This is because AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, while a price agreed with a hospital or surgeon stands.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Own pricing.** Given a Contract that lets the anaesthetist change its pricing, then they can change the price, rate or discount.
- **Fixed price.** Given a Contract that does not, then the price shows read-only and no discount field is offered.
- **Fixed discount.** Given a Contract that does not let the anaesthetist change its pricing, and has a fixed discount, the discount shows where the price change would be, and the anaesthetist cannot edit it.
- **No reason.** Given an entered price or discount, then it saves without a reason and is flagged at office review.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 sets this per Contract ("is_editable"): typically false for hospital fixed-price Contracts and true for Contracts using RVG values. Time entry prohibits a price or discount entry on a Contract that is not editable, and the adjustment reason is optional ([contract behaviour](AR-29#contract-behaviour)). The office can change any price ([office price change](US-05.4.2)).
