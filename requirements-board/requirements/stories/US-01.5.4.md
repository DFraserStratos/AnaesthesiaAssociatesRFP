The Conflicts tab of [the Lists section](US-13.1.3) shows every List at a hospital that is [closed that day](US-01.5.2), across every date, in one coloured view. Each row shows the reason, the anaesthetist and the hospital, with the List's Bookings still in place.

This is so the office sees at a glance which Lists need covering, instead of running a report and printing, annotating and reprinting it.

- **A conflict is a hospital closure.** Nothing else is a conflict. A not-preferred pairing is ["not preferred"](US-01.3.5), never a conflict.
- **Unavailability is never a conflict.** A List never stays with an anaesthetist who has said they are unavailable, short-notice sickness included. It goes back to the office as a [Draft List](FT-01.6), worked from the [Draft Lists tab](US-01.6.2):
  - when the anaesthetist marks a booked Slot unavailable and returns the List to the office, rather than assigning it to a colleague ([return or assign](US-01.5.5))
  - when a surgeon's [recurring booking](US-01.3.2) lands on a Slot already marked unavailable
- **Resolved from the row.** From a conflict, an admin can [reassign the List](US-01.4.1) to cover it, or clear the conflict with an optional note. The conflict leaves the tab once the List is covered or the closure no longer applies.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Listed.** Given a booked List whose hospital is closed, when an admin opens the Conflicts tab, then the List appears as a conflict and its Bookings are still in place.
- **Every date.** Given Lists at closed hospitals on two different days, then both show on the Conflicts tab.
- **Reassigned.** Given a conflict, when the List is reassigned to an available anaesthetist, then the conflict clears.
- **Not a conflict.** Given a recurring booking that lands on a Slot marked unavailable, then it shows as a Draft List, not as a conflict on the anaesthetist.
- **Not preferred is not a conflict.** Given a List whose anaesthetist and surgeon are a not-preferred pairing, at an open hospital, then it does not appear on the Conflicts tab.
