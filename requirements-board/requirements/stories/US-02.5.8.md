An admin can open a Booking's change history from the Booking itself: every change made to the Booking and its Procedures, each showing when it was made, who made it and what changed. Who is the named user, or the hospital system or integration the change came from.

The history sits one step away from the Booking, behind a history button, not on the face of the page.

This is so that, with several people in the office editing Bookings and invoices, AA can always tell who changed what, and when.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Who and when.** Given a Booking changed by two office users, when an admin opens its history, then each change shows the user who made it, the date and time, and what changed.
- **From the hospital.** Given a Booking updated from the hospital's list, then the history entry names the hospital's list as its source, and shows the procedure text it replaced ([procedure text](US-02.5.7)).
- **On the Booking.** The history opens from the Booking itself.

## Technical discussion

The history is already kept, append-only, by the [change history](US-02.5.5); this story is the screen that shows it. Our reading: each office user signs in as themselves ([role-based access](US-13.5.1)), so every entry names a person, not a shared office login.
