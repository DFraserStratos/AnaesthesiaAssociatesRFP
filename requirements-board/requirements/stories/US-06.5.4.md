When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist before the procedure, the system changes nothing in the ledger, recalculates nothing and does not bill the patient again. The new anaesthetist claims the prepaid amount, and is paid it from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) once the procedure is done ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

This is so the patient's amount stays stable and nothing in the ledger needs amending.

The payable does not move, because it is the holding account, not the anaesthetist. The price is the one on the prepayment invoice that went out, and the new anaesthetist wears or benefits from any difference in their own price.

The system may send the patient an informational email saying their anaesthetist has changed, the payment stays the same and they need do nothing. The wording is AA's choice. No email is required, since the patient did not know who the anaesthetist would be in the first place.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Not billed again.** Given a prepaid Booking for anaesthetist A, when it is moved to anaesthetist B before the procedure, then no new prepayment invoice is raised and the patient is not billed again.
- **Ledger unchanged.** Given that move, when it is saved, then the prepayment's pair is unchanged: the receivable from the payer and the payable to the holding account stay as they were, and no price is recalculated.
- **New anaesthetist paid.** Given the procedure is done by B, then B is paid the prepaid amount from the holding account at the second step.
- **Optional email.** Given that move, when the office chooses, then the patient can be sent an informational email that the anaesthetist has changed and they need do nothing.

Donald, 19 October: "Whatever was the prepaid price for the first anaesthetist when the booking was created and the prepaid invoice went out, that's just the price."

OQ-96 answered 28 October: a prepaid Contract is not editable, so the new anaesthetist cannot change the price. A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new price range does not move it (Donald, 19 October).
