When a prepaid Booking moves to another anaesthetist before the procedure, nothing changes in the ledger and the patient is not billed again. The payable does not move, because it is the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not the anaesthetist. Nothing is recalculated: the price prepaid when the Booking was created and the prepayment invoice went out is the price. The new anaesthetist wears or benefits from any difference in their own price, and claims the prepaid amount: the deal is the deal.

Whichever anaesthetist does the List is paid from the holding account once the procedure is done ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

The system may send the patient an informational email saying their anaesthetist has changed, the payment stays the same and they need do nothing. The wording is AA's choice. The patient did not know who the anaesthetist would be in the first place, so no email is required.

This keeps the amount stable for the patient and leaves nothing to amend in the ledger.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Booking for anaesthetist A, when it is moved to anaesthetist B before the procedure, then no new prepayment invoice is raised and the patient is not billed again.
- Given that move, when it is saved, then the prepayment's pair is unchanged: the receivable from the payer and the payable to the holding account stay as they were, and no price is recalculated.
- Given the procedure is done by B, then B is paid the prepaid amount from the holding account at the second step.
- Given that move, when the office chooses, then the patient can be sent an informational email that the anaesthetist has changed and they need do nothing.
