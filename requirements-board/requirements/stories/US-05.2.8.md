The Billing/Invoice Engine works out an unedited Procedure's price from its Contract whenever it is needed, rather than storing it, so the price follows the Contract: the [price range](US-04.2.15) in force on the procedure date, the Contract's rate or discount as they stand, and the [anaesthetist's own unit value](US-12.1.1).

This is so a price change reaches every Procedure not yet settled, without touching one that a person, a prepayment or authorisation has fixed.

Three things hold the price:

- a price a person typed
- a [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), once its prepayment invoice is raised
- AUTHORISED, which freezes everything in the [snapshot](US-04.3.5)

Changing the Contract clears a typed price.

A Procedure with no price (an [open price](US-04.3.9) with no amount, or a fixed price with no range in force) shows "Needs a price". It blocks the anaesthetist's complete, though the Procedure can still be sent to the office, and blocks AUTHORISED. No automatic difference invoice or credit ever follows.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Follows.** Given a $500 range from 1 January and a Booking for 10 December, when a $550 range from 1 December is added, then the Procedure is invoiced $550.
- **Typed price sticks.** Given the office typed $520 earlier, then it stays $520.
- **Prepaid holds.** Given its prepayment invoice was raised at $500, then it stays $500 with no difference invoice.
- **Authorised holds.** Given the List is AUTHORISED, then a later range changes nothing.
- **Rate edit.** Given an edited Contract rate, then every un-authorised, unedited, un-prepaid Procedure on it is priced at the new rate.
- **Needs a price.** Given an open-price Procedure with no amount, then the List cannot be AUTHORISED.
