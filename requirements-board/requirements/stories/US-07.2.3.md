The admin corrects a Booking directly, without sending it back to the anaesthetist. There is no 'returned' state.

Until the List is authorised the anaesthetist can also [still change it](US-07.2.1); neither side has authority over the other, and the last change stands. In practice the office is usually the last to touch it.
