Where the Contract chosen for an insured patient's procedure does not bill its holder, for example [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3) when the patient pays and claims the money back, the system invoices the payer named on the Booking, usually the patient ([who is billed](FT-11.2)).

The patient then forwards the invoice to their insurer to claim reimbursement themselves.
