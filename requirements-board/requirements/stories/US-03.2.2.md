Anyone with edit rights, an admin, the anaesthetist, or an inbound source such as a hospital or surgeon feed, can set or change which Procedure is primary, for as long as the Booking stays editable.

## Notes

AA staff normally choose the primary when creating or importing a Booking, from the order on the rooms' sheet (Vanessa). Donald wants the primary shown first in the Booking. The first procedure done may not be the primary; an [ACC pre-op assessment](US-05.5.2) never is.
