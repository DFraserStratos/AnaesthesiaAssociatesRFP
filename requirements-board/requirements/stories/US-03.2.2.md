Anyone with edit rights, an admin, the anaesthetist, or an inbound source such as a hospital or surgeon feed, can set or change which Procedure is primary, for as long as the Booking [stays editable](US-07.3.2).

## Notes

AA staff normally choose the primary when creating or importing a Booking, from the order on the rooms' sheet (Vanessa). Donald wants the primary shown first in the Booking. The first procedure done may not be the primary; an [ACC pre-op assessment](US-05.5.2) never is.

Greg, 16 October: "Is it just the first one in the list? Because that's how a user would think about it." Donald, relaying Vanessa: the most expensive is always the primary, as the rooms send the big thing first. Read with [add additional Procedures](US-03.2.3) and agreed, no change.
