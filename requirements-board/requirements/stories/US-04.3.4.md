Admins set each Procedure's Contract at booking setup, ahead of the day of the procedure, on the [step rail](US-04.3.8): first where the invoice goes, then the Contract. Setting it is mandatory: a Procedure without one shows "Needs an invoice destination" and its Booking stays on the admin's list until it is sorted. Picking a prepaid Contract starts the [prepayment](FT-06.2).

This is so office staff can see and check who pays well before the procedure happens.

The office's job ends at the Contract. It may enter the RVG code if it knows it; otherwise it leaves it for the anaesthetist. A Contract priced from the RVG goes to the anaesthetist for the RVG code, times and modifiers. A fixed-price Contract needs none, and the office can pick it by typing, though nothing forces it to at setup.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Needs an invoice destination.** Given a Procedure with no Contract, then it shows "Needs an invoice destination" and its Booking stays on the admin's list.
- **RVG to the anaesthetist.** Given the office sets a Contract priced from the RVG and leaves the RVG code blank, then the Booking shows the anaesthetist the RVG code to fill in.
