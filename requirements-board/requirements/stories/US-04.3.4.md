Admins set each Procedure's Contract at booking setup or intake, ahead of the day of the procedure, on the [step rail](US-04.3.8): first where the invoice goes, then the Contract. The office sets as much as it knows, and above all picks out the Procedures that are prepaid, since picking a prepaid Contract starts the [prepayment](FT-06.2) and only the office can make that choice.

This is so office staff can see and check who pays well before the procedure happens.

The office does not always know the whole Contract, such as which insurer pays or the RVG code. It may stop part way, with where the invoice goes chosen but no Contract yet, or leave the Contract for the anaesthetist, who [chooses it on the Booking](US-03.4.1). A Procedure with no Contract shows "Needs an invoice destination", and its List waits on the office's [Contracts tab](US-04.3.12) until one is set. A Booking cannot be completed without a Contract on every Procedure, so its List cannot be submitted until then.

The office's job ends at the Contract. It may enter the RVG code if it knows it; otherwise it leaves it for the anaesthetist. A Contract priced from the RVG goes to the anaesthetist for the [RVG code, times and modifiers](US-03.3.9). A fixed-price Contract needs none, and the office can pick it by typing, though nothing forces it to at setup.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Needs an invoice destination.** Given a Procedure with no Contract, then it shows "Needs an invoice destination" and its List is on the office's Contracts tab.
- **Left for the anaesthetist.** Given the office leaves a Procedure's Contract unset, when the anaesthetist chooses one on the Booking, then the Procedure is set and the List leaves the Contracts tab once all its Procedures are set.
- **Part way.** Given the office chooses where the invoice goes but no Contract, then the answer is kept on the Procedure and the Procedure still needs an invoice destination.
- **Not completed without one.** Given a Booking with a Procedure that has no Contract, when anyone marks it complete, then it is refused because the Procedure needs an invoice destination.
- **RVG to the anaesthetist.** Given the office sets a Contract priced from the RVG and leaves the RVG code blank, then the Booking shows the anaesthetist the RVG code to fill in.
