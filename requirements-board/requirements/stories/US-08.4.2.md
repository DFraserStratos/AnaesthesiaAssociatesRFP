Where the Contract bills the payer, the Billing/Invoice Engine emails the invoice to the invoice email captured on the Booking, which may belong to [a guardian](US-11.2.2) rather than the patient. An invoice to a holder goes by the delivery method on its [Party](US-04.1.5): email or post for now; other outputs are OQ-114.

This covers cases such as a child patient, where the person who should receive the invoice is not the patient themselves.
