Where the Contract bills the payer, the Billing/Invoice Engine emails the invoice to the email of [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2), which may belong to a guardian rather than the patient. Procedures on one Booking that bill the same payer go on one invoice, sent once; a payer typed in separately on another Procedure receives their own. An invoice to a holder goes by the delivery method on its [Party](US-04.1.5): email or post for now; other outputs are OQ-114.

This covers cases such as a child patient, where the person who should receive the invoice is not the patient themselves.
