The Billing/Invoice Engine emails the invoice to the invoice email captured on the Booking, which may belong to [a guardian](US-11.2.2) rather than the patient. Where the Contract instead specifies [portal upload](US-04.2.8), the engine queues the invoice for that instead.

This covers cases such as a child patient, where the person who should receive the invoice is not the patient themselves.
