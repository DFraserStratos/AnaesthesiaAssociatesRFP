Once a List is SUBMITTED, the anaesthetist can still see it and change its Bookings until the office authorises it. Their changes are [audited](US-02.5.5) and the office sees them at review; the last change stands.

It stays visible to them, less prominent than active work, scrolling up from today on their main view, until it is authorised.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a submitted List, when the anaesthetist changes a Booking on it, then the change saves and the office sees it at review.
- Given an authorised List, then the anaesthetist cannot change it.
