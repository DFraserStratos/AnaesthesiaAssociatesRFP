The anaesthetist finds past work mainly by [search](US-03.1.7):

- **Patient name**, the primary lookup, with a loose search: type Smith, get all the Smiths.
- **NHI**, which works too, though the person asking may not have it, so the name comes first.
- **Date**, a useful cue as well, for example when someone rings about a patient seen on a given day. The anaesthetist jumps to that day and drills into its Lists, Bookings and Procedures, as the [drill-down](US-03.1.1) does. Scrolling back through a calendar beyond a few days is of limited value.

Submitted Lists stay on the main view, scrolling up from today, until they are authorised. Authorised Lists are found through search, elsewhere in the app.

This is so they can look back at past procedures, for example to add a [post-op event](US-03.7.1), or to answer a query about an invoice from three months ago: "what did I enter?"

## Acceptance criteria

- **Name.** Given part of a patient's name, when the anaesthetist searches, then the matching patients' Bookings show, including invoiced ones, and the anaesthetist sees what they entered.
- **NHI.** Given an NHI, when the anaesthetist searches, then that patient's Bookings show.
- **Date.** Given a date, when the anaesthetist picks it, then that day's Lists show and can be drilled into.
