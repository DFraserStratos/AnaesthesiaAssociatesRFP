On a Booking, an admin or the anaesthetist presses a button against a Procedure's Contract line to add an additional invoice on top of it. It is a user action, not a Contract setting:

- an admin can do this on any Procedure, from the Admin App;
- the anaesthetist can do this on their own Procedure, from the Anaesthetist App, for example when a prepaid procedure took far longer than expected.

The invoice links to, and is traceable back to, the original Procedure and its invoice, and it is recorded as an event on the Procedure. It can be sent to any billable party, not only the original invoice's.

It is a free-form invoice: the user sets each line's description, quantity and amount. No Contract pricing or unit rules apply, so the anaesthetist or AA staff can bill whatever the case needs. It is not to be confused with [splitting the Contract's invoiced fee](US-08.2.3) between billable parties. Whoever raises it, it goes through the same standard review step as every event (OQ-63) and is invoiced in the next run. It is audited like any user action.

Each additional invoice gets its own unique invoice number, its own [receivable and payable pair](US-08.3.1), its own Xero pair and its own payable to the anaesthetist. The original Booking is not unlocked or changed ([immutable after AUTHORISED](US-07.3.2)).

This is so the anaesthetist or the office can bill anything that turns up after invoicing without editing the original. It covers:

- post-op charges after the original invoice was sent
- a combined Procedure split at a billable party's request
- extra work after a prepaid or any other fixed-price procedure: the system raises no balance invoice and recalculates nothing ([no automatic invoice or credit](US-06.4.1)); the anaesthetist or the office raises one by hand if they choose

## Acceptance criteria

- **Button.** A Procedure on an invoiced Booking shows a button to create an additional invoice, in the Admin App and, on the anaesthetist's own Procedure, in the Anaesthetist App.
- **Linked.** The additional invoice shows and links to the original Procedure and its invoice, and the original shows its additional invoices.
- **Own pair.** It creates its own ledger pair, Xero pair and anaesthetist payable.
- **Review step.** Given an additional invoice raised by an admin or by the anaesthetist, then it is issued only after the standard review step.
- **Any billable party.** The user can pick any billable party for it.
- **After a fixed price.** Given a prepaid or other fixed-price Procedure whose work turned out more than expected, then no additional invoice exists until the anaesthetist or an admin creates one by hand with this button.
- **Not a correction.** Correcting a wrong invoice uses [credit in full, then rebill](US-08.6.2), not an additional invoice.

## Notes

Solutions Plus does this with its 'next account' (another invoice number attached back to the original); we keep the idea, not the numbering. Pricing settled by OQ-45: free form. OQ-72 confirms "Desc" means description, not discount, and that it can go to any billable party. Ben (2026-10-06) said anaesthetists "can / must create any ad-hoc invoices or credit notes as needed", and Donald (7 October) that the anaesthetist "can optionally choose to generate any ad hoc invoices or credit notes", "hooking into the already established and defined ability to create additional invoices on a procedure" (OQ-61, OQ-76): so the anaesthetist raises them too, through this same button.
