**Not paid.** When a Booking whose prepayment invoice has not been paid is cancelled ([record a cancellation](US-02.5.3)), the system raises a credit note that cancels the prepayment invoice, as part of the cancellation.

**Paid.** When a Booking with a paid prepayment is cancelled before the procedure, the system always refunds the original prepayment in full: the money is held in the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) until the procedure, so it has not reached the anaesthetist, and it is refunded to the patient from there. The refund is recorded in the ledger as a credit against the prepayment invoice that reverses its receivable and its payable to the holding account (nothing is reversed against the anaesthetist, who was never paid), so the trail stays visible.

Both happen automatically when the Booking is cancelled. If the patient pays after the credit note, the money is sent back to them; who refunds it, and whether from the system as for a cancelled prepayment, is not yet stated.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a paid prepayment on a Booking, when the Booking is cancelled before the procedure, then the full prepayment is refunded, whichever anaesthetist held it.
- Given the refund, when the ledger is viewed, then the credit is linked to the original prepayment invoice.
- Given an unpaid prepayment invoice on a Booking, when the Booking is cancelled, then a credit note is raised against it automatically.
- Given a payment received after that credit note, then it is refunded.
