When a Booking with a paid prepayment is cancelled before the procedure ([record a cancellation](US-02.5.3)), the system always refunds the original prepayment in full: the money is held in the [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1) until the procedure, so it has not reached the anaesthetist, and it is refunded to the patient from there. The refund is recorded in the ledger as a credit against the prepayment invoice that reverses its receivable and its payable to the holding account (nothing is reversed against the anaesthetist, who was never paid), so the trail stays visible.

The office handles the refund; the anaesthetist is no longer left to refund the patient or pay a replacement anaesthetist directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a paid prepayment on a Booking, when the Booking is cancelled before the procedure, then the full prepayment is refunded, whichever anaesthetist held it.
- Given the refund, when the ledger is viewed, then the credit is linked to the original prepayment invoice.
