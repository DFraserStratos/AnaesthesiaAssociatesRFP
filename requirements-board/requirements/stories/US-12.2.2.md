Each month, each anaesthetist gets a list of the payments AA has passed to them in the month, with the [GST component](US-05.2.7) of each, so they know their income and its GST for their GST return.

It lists [payments](US-10.1.2), not invoices sent, because an invoice may be paid in part: some patients pay off a bill on a repayment plan, for example $500 a month. Anything still outstanding falls into a later month.

It is something to download, email or print, so it suits the [web app](US-03.1.4).
