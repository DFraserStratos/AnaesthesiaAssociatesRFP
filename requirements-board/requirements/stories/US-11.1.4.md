The NHI is mandatory: every patient record has one, and there is no blank NHI (Donald and Greg, 16 October). Some Bookings still arrive from feeds and theatre lists without one. What happens to such a Booking until its NHI arrives, held pending with later steps blocked or not created, is OQ-49.

The earlier working assumption, a Booking going ahead with the NHI pending and authorising blocked, is on hold until OQ-49 is answered.

This is because Bookings arrive from feeds and theatre lists that sometimes omit the NHI, and "you don't want things rejected out of feeds" (Greg). A procedure never goes ahead without an NHI in the room (Donald), so an NHI exists even when the office does not have it yet. A patient without one is an exception that [has to be resolved](US-11.1.5).

## Notes

What happens to a duplicate patient record when the NHI is later found (for example a second record under a married name), and whether duplicates matter once the patient has had their operation, are part of OQ-49.

On 7 October Vanessa said "make NHI mandatory"; the room added that this makes the NHI the patient identifier throughout the system. In the public health sector a patient without one is assigned a temporary NHI, and even a self-funding patient from overseas has one if they are in the hospital's records. Whether "mandatory" means a Booking cannot be created without an NHI, or can be held pending with later steps blocked, was not said and is still OQ-49.
