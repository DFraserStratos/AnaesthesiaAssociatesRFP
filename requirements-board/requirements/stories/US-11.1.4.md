The NHI is mandatory: every patient record has one, and there is no blank NHI. A Booking cannot be saved without an NHI.

A booking that arrives without one, from a feed or a surgeon's PDF, is not a Booking yet. It waits at intake, shown with the [rows waiting for an NHI](US-11.1.5) beside the [unmatched rows](US-02.1.4), until the office adds the NHI, and then it is matched and becomes a Booking. While it waits, a [stub](FT-02.6) stands in its place on its List, seen by the anaesthetist and the office; a stub is not a Booking.

This is because Bookings arrive from feeds and theatre lists that sometimes omit the NHI, and a row from a feed is never rejected: the intake row is kept, not lost. A procedure never goes ahead without an NHI in the room, so the NHI exists even when the office does not have it yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking being created or edited, when it has no NHI, then it cannot be saved.
- Given an incoming booking with no NHI, then it waits at intake, shown with the rows waiting for an NHI, and no Booking or patient record is made from it.
- Given the office adds the NHI to a waiting row, then it can be matched and becomes a Booking.
- Given an incoming booking with no NHI waiting at intake, then a stub shows in its place on its List until the office resolves it.

## Notes

On 7 October Vanessa said "make NHI mandatory"; the room added that this makes the NHI the patient identifier throughout the system. In the public health sector a patient without one is assigned a temporary NHI, and even a self-funding patient from overseas has one if they are in the hospital's records. On 19 October Greg: "It's mandatory now, so that's that" (OQ-49); Donald then settled the incoming case as above (the NHI mandatory with no blank, Donald and Greg, 16 and 19 October). Greg: "you don't want things rejected out of feeds"; Donald: a procedure never goes ahead without an NHI in the room. The earlier idea of a Booking going ahead with the NHI pending and authorising blocked was dropped.

What happens to a duplicate patient record when an NHI is found later no longer arises, because no patient record is made without an NHI.

Greg, 28 October, agrees the rule but asked what happens operationally: "you can't just not accept something... especially if it's a hospital feed". His model is an error queue plus a stub on the List, so the office and the anaesthetist see there is a Booking with a problem, with the record held on an identifier other than the NHI. Donald found this story "a bit muddled" and asked for a question for Vanessa and/or Ben (OQ-118), and a new feature for the stubs under EP-02 ([stubs on a List](FT-02.6)). OQ-118 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Vanessa or Ben): "Yes, we will have stubs." The rule here stands: no Booking or patient record is made without an NHI.

Donald, 25 November (for the build; not yet seen by AA): the rows waiting for an NHI are shown on their own tab on Intake, beside the unmatched rows. He chose "A tab on Intake".
