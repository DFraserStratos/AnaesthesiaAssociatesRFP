The app's main screen shows the anaesthetist's Lists starting at today:

- upcoming work
- any earlier Lists not yet invoiced, which they can scroll back to

Invoiced Lists are not shown there; the anaesthetist finds them through a deeper [archive or search](US-03.1.6) elsewhere in the app.

This is so anaesthetists see the work still in progress without being cluttered by finished work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a past List not yet invoiced, then it shows on the main view, reached by scrolling back from today.
- Given an upcoming List, then it shows on the main view.
- Given an invoiced List, then it does not show on the main view, but the anaesthetist finds it by search.

## Notes

Donald's proposal at the 7 October client meeting, which the room moved on from without objection (Notes 2026-10-07 · AA client meeting #16): "Maybe it starts you on whatever today is... and you can scroll back to see other lists that haven't been invoiced." It meets the room's wish to "go back and look at old invoices" (OQ-31). When a List counts as invoiced is [the List's exit point](US-07.4.1). The [calendar of past work](US-03.1.6) is another way back to old Lists.
