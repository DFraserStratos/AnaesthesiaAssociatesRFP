The office can give a Contract one funding tag from AA's [funding tag list](US-13.4.5), such as SXAP, HNZ or ACC. Together with who the Contract bills, the tag decides where it sits on the [step rail](US-04.3.8).

This is so a hospital's work under each funder is offered together, and a hospital's future fixed prices for a funder's patients have a place.

- A Contract held by a hospital that bills its holder sits under that hospital, under its tag at step 2.
- A hospital-billed Contract with no tag sits under Special, and so does a Contract that bills any other holder, whatever its tag.
- A Contract that bills the payer sits under Patient, whatever its tag.

ACC and SXAP are funding tags, not kinds of Contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Under the hospital.** Given a CES-held SXAP item that bills CES, then it is offered under Christchurch Eye Surgery › SXAP.
- **Untagged.** Given STG Bariatric, held and billed by St George's with no tag, then it is under Special.
- **Billed directly.** Given a surgeon group's Contract billed to the group with an ACC tag, then it is under Special.
