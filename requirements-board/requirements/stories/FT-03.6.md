The app validates that the minimum billing data is present before a Booking can be marked complete. It shows what is missing on each Booking, and a List cannot be [submitted](US-07.1.1) while any Booking is incomplete ([incomplete Bookings listed](US-03.6.2)). The anaesthetist can also [ask the office to check a Booking or its List](US-03.6.3).
