Some charges (events) are dated after the List containing them has already been invoiced, for example:

- a ward or HDU review
- a nerve catheter day
- a pain consult

Separately, an already-invoiced Booking can turn out to need correcting.

Both cases have to be handled without unlocking the original, [already-immutable](US-07.3.2) Booking.

Late charges are billed either as an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), raised by an admin or by the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, or added by the anaesthetist as a [post-op event](FT-03.7). Both are events on the Procedure (OQ-63), and every event goes through the same standard review step. Every event marked for invoicing, late or not, is billed in the next run, on the same invoice as other lines for the same billable party where it can be, otherwise on its own; none is held for the Procedure's own invoice (OQ-121).

A wrong invoice is credited in full, then rebilled. A split asked for after invoicing uses the [credit note option](US-08.6.5) against the original, then new additional invoices (OQ-72).
