Some charges (events) are dated after the List containing them has already been invoiced, for example:

- a ward or HDU review
- a nerve catheter day
- a pain consult

Separately, an already-invoiced Booking can turn out to need correcting.

Both cases have to be handled without unlocking the original, [already-immutable](US-07.3.2) Booking.

Late charges are billed either as an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3), raised by an admin or by the anaesthetist on their own Procedure, or added by the anaesthetist as a [post-op event](FT-03.7). Both are events on the Procedure (OQ-63), and every event goes through the same standard review step:

- an event raised before the Procedure's invoice is approved travels with that invoice;
- an event raised after it, which includes every late event, is invoiced in the next run.

A wrong invoice is credited in full, then rebilled. A split asked for after invoicing uses the [credit note option](US-08.6.5) against the original, then new additional invoices (OQ-72).
