Surgeons' rooms email the office their List as a PDF as soon as a booking is assigned to a day and an anaesthetist, and re-send the whole List as a PDF whenever anything on it changes, right up to the procedure day. Each PDF is a snapshot that does not say what changed. The system reads them, lets AA staff review and merge the changes into the Bookings, and accept them.

This pathway is in the first release alongside the [hospital download](FT-02.1), each landing in the same matching screen (OQ-108).

## Notes

PDF ingest is still needed after the hospitals' [HL7 v2 or FHIR feeds](EP-14) arrive: the hospital download is not comprehensive, so the theatre list is still needed for the insurer or Contract.

OQ-108 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Ben or Vanessa): "let's make the prototype with the assumption that we have both hospital integrations, as well as PDF scanning / ingestion." This replaces the 20 October working rule (hospital download first, this pathway later). The hospital download's formats and fields are OQ-13.

Ben has told Vanessa not to forward the rooms' List PDFs to anaesthetists: "if they're doing their job properly, everything they need to know is on their phone", and the PDFs are "just noise" (Greg, 16 October). No decision was recorded on whether the system changes this.
