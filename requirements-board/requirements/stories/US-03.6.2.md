The anaesthetist can see which Bookings on a List are still incomplete, and exactly what is missing from each one.

## Notes

On 16 October Greg liked the prototype blocking submit and naming what is missing ("that's actually quite nice"), and asked whether the missing time in the example is pre-op or post-op; that was not settled. Greg also said the time should be called finish, not handover (see [start and finish times](US-03.3.3)). Listing what is missing stands; whether the missing time in the example is pre-op or post-op is not settled.
