The Scheduling Engine requires every Procedure to reference exactly one Contract before its Booking can be [marked complete](US-03.6.1).

This is so each Procedure always has pricing rules and an invoice recipient attached, and nothing is left unresolved when billing runs.

## Technical discussion

A Procedure without a Contract shows "Needs an invoice destination" ([step rail](US-04.3.8)).

## Notes

Greg agreed on 7 October ("That's true"). One Contract per Procedure, with no line under it, is the contract model proposal's flat model (section 2.7).
