The Scheduling Engine requires every Procedure to reference exactly one Contract before its Booking can be [marked complete](US-03.6.1).

This guarantees each Procedure always has pricing rules and an invoice recipient already attached, so nothing is left unresolved when billing runs.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 gives each booking procedure one contract and at most one of its lines; the line "must be not null for billing" where the Contract has lines ([booking procedure fields](AR-29#booking-procedure-fields)).
