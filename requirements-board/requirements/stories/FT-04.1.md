The Contract catalogue is where the office keeps every contract holder and every Contract, [a dated set of terms](FT-04.2) (a header with its name, dates and settings, and the lines under it), and creates, edits, versions and retires them.

Contract screens are needed from the first release, so AA's admin staff can define the rules for every arrangement AA holds.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: contract and billing reference data is named as part of the go-live migration set, to be identified with AA. Data is loaded by extract, transform and load scripts, checked and validated ahead of time, and a small group of AA users validates the contract, rate and billing reference data. The extract can be re-run to pick up data added to the old system in the interim.

## Notes

The catalogue list filters by active or all Contracts, and by contract holder (see the create, edit and retire story).

The v6 design's baseline for the first release: a developer-run import, then office screens to create, edit and retire records, with ongoing changes made on a header and lines screen per Contract and staged for review.
