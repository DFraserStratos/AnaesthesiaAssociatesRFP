The office keeps the Contract catalogue in its own section of the Admin app: the [Parties](US-04.1.5) that hold Contracts, and every Contract, one record per priced item, each with a page of its own. It creates them, edits them in place, and disables a Contract it no longer uses; every change is audited.

This is so AA's office can keep every arrangement and price current itself, from the first release.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: contract and billing reference data is named as part of the go-live migration set, to be identified with AA. Data is loaded by extract, transform and load scripts, checked and validated ahead of time, and a small group of AA users validates the contract, rate and billing reference data. The extract can be re-run to pick up data added to the old system in the interim.

## Notes

First release: a developer-run load at cutover ([reference data loaded at cutover](US-13.4.3)), then the office's screens; [bulk upload and export](US-04.2.13) are Future Work (OQ-100).
