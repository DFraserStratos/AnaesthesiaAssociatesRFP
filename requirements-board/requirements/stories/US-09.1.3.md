A [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) creates the same [ACCREC and draft ACCPAY pair](US-09.1.1) as any other invoice, with one difference: the payable side is to the [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1), not to the anaesthetist. The ACCREC is the receivable from the patient.

The later transfer to the anaesthetist is a second pair, from the holding account to whichever anaesthetist did the List, created when the procedure is done and the List goes to billing ([settlement](FT-06.4)).

This is so prepayments run through the same ledger and Xero mechanism as invoices raised at List authorisation, with no separate case to handle, and so the trust account in Xero balances without any amendment when a Booking changes anaesthetist.
