A [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) creates the same [ACCREC and draft ACCPAY pair](US-09.1.1) as any other invoice.

This is so prepayments run through the same ledger and Xero mechanism as invoices raised at List authorisation, with no separate case to handle.
