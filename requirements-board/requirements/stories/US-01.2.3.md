The Scheduling Engine never derives a Slot's status from its booking activity. Once a List is put in the Slot, the List shows in place of the status.

This means a status such as 'on leave Tuesday PM' stays meaningful even when that Slot holds no List or bookings.

## Notes

OQ-64 settled that Slots are boxes with a status and Lists go into them, so the List shows in place of the status, represented much as in the prototype today. How it is stored is for the developers.
