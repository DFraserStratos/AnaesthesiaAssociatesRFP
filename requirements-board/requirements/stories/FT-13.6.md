Surgeons and surgeons' rooms are master data, not free-text names. Each surgeon belongs to a room and has a profile page. Admin keep private preferences between surgeons and anaesthetists, both who someone will not work with and who they prefer to work with.

This is so the system can hold each surgeon's identifying numbers, know who to contact for a room, and inform the matching of anaesthetists and surgeons.

Vanessa will supply the master list of rooms and surgeons; reference data is loaded from controlled spreadsheets rather than migrated (see [reference data from controlled spreadsheets](US-13.4.3)).
