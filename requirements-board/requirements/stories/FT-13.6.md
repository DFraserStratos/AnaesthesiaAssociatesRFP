Surgeons and surgeons' rooms are master data, not free-text names. Each surgeon belongs to one or more rooms and has a profile page. Admin keep private preferences between surgeons and anaesthetists, both who someone will not work with and who they prefer to work with. Where [practice managers' and PAs' contacts](US-13.6.5) belong, and which room's contacts apply to a Booking when a surgeon belongs to more than one, are open (OQ-127).

This is so the system can hold each surgeon's identifying numbers, know who to contact for a room, and inform the matching of anaesthetists and surgeons. Ben would like an electronic address book of surgical specialists, perhaps in the new system (OQ-126).

Ben shared the master list of rooms and surgeons with Vanessa on 3 November; reference data is loaded from controlled spreadsheets rather than migrated (see [reference data from controlled spreadsheets](US-13.4.3)).
