Surgeons and surgeons' rooms are master data, not free-text names. Each surgeon belongs to one or more rooms, holds one or more specialties and has a profile page. Admins keep private preferences between surgeons and anaesthetists, both who someone will not work with and who they prefer to work with.

This is so the system can hold each surgeon's identifying numbers, know who to contact for a room, and inform the matching of anaesthetists and surgeons.
