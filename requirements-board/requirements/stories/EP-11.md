Every patient has one record in the system, keyed on their NHI (mandatory, with no blank), with their contact details.

Each Procedure names a payer, billed whenever its Contract [does not bill its holder](US-04.2.1). The payer defaults to the patient but can be someone else, such as a guardian, a benefactor or a family trust. A Booking names its insurer or funder, a [Party](US-04.1.5) like any other holder, and an insured Booking holds the approval number.

Admins can see a patient's outstanding balances across all anaesthetists, and are alerted when a patient who owes AA money or holds a credit is booked again; anaesthetists see the alert on their List.

This is so each patient is held once, every invoice reaches whoever actually pays, and AA can follow up a balance before the next procedure.
