Every patient has one record in the system, keyed on their NHI, with their contact details.

Each Booking names a payer, billed whenever a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder. The payer defaults to the patient but can differ, for example a guardian paying and receiving the invoice for a child.

Admins can see a patient's outstanding balances across all anaesthetists, and are alerted when a patient with bills unpaid past the alert threshold is booked again.
