Every patient has one record in the system, keyed on their NHI, with their contact details.

Each Booking names a payer, billed whenever a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder. The payer defaults to the patient but can differ, for example a guardian paying and receiving the invoice for a child.

The NHI is mandatory, with no blank. Insurers are held as master data with an accepts direct claims flag, and the office records the approval number on an insured Booking. The selected Contract decides who is billed: the holder's billable party when the holder pays AA itself, otherwise the payer named on the Booking.

Admins can see a patient's outstanding balances across all anaesthetists, and are alerted when a patient who owes AA money (a mild alert under the alert threshold, a strong one over it) or holds a credit balance (a mild alert) is booked again.
