The system keeps every [version of a Contract](US-04.2.10), not just the latest one.

Every create, update and retirement of an RVG group, a contract holder or a Contract is audited (who, what, when), and each submitted Contract change also records the lines added, altered and removed, with their values before and after. Nothing is deleted: a line left out of a new version stays on the earlier one.

This is so an invoice raised under an older version [can always be reproduced](US-08.4.4), even after the Contract has since changed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change to key scheduling and billing data (who changed what, and when) is retained using Azure SQL system-versioned (temporal) tables, giving a full history rather than a single last-modified stamp. Applied to Contracts, this would keep earlier Contract versions available.
