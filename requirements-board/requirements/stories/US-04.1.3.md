Every create, edit and disable of a Party, a Contract, a [price range](US-04.2.15), a [specialty](US-04.2.16), a [funding tag](US-04.2.17) or an RVG group is audited: who, what, when, and the values before and after. Nothing is deleted: a Contract is disabled, not removed, and a corrected price range keeps its earlier value in the audit.

This is so any change to a price or setting can be traced, and an invoice can always be [reproduced](US-08.4.4) from the [pricing snapshot](US-04.3.5) taken at AUTHORISED, whatever has changed on its Contract since.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: every change to key scheduling and billing data (who changed what, and when) is retained using Azure SQL system-versioned (temporal) tables, giving a full history rather than a single last-modified stamp. Applied to Contracts, this keeps every earlier value of each setting and price range.
