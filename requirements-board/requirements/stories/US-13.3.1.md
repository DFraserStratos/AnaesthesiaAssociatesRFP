On the Billing tab of [the Finances section](US-13.3.3), admins see the billing of every [AUTHORISED](FT-07.3) List across the practice in one table, grouped by anaesthetist. Under each anaesthetist, each List is a heading carrying its date, session, hospital, status, counts (sent, in Xero, paid in) and billing run. The rows beneath it are that List's Bookings, each showing its status, its invoices, whether it was [sent to the billable party](US-08.4.2), whether it was [mirrored to Xero](US-09.1.1), whether it has been paid in, and whether it has been disbursed.

Where a Booking's billing has failed, the reason shows under the patient's name, with its fix beside it: retry, fix the payer, or [resolve it by hand](US-13.3.2).

This is so the office sees what each Booking needs and fixes it where it sees it, since [billing fails one Booking at a time](US-08.5.2).

- **Problems first.** The tab opens on Problems, where each List shows only the Bookings that need something, with a way to show the rest of its Bookings.
- **Filters.** Problems, In progress, Done and All, with a search and a filter for one anaesthetist.
- **Lists awaiting approval.** The tab shows the Lists awaiting approval as a short list whose rows open the Review queue, where a List is [authorised](US-07.3.1). The Billing tab never approves a List itself.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Problems first.** Given a List with one failed Booking and four billed ones, when an admin opens the Billing tab, then the List shows under its anaesthetist with only the failed Booking.
- **Reason and fix beside the Booking.** Given a Booking whose payer has no billing email, then its row shows the reason under the patient's name and a fix-the-payer action beside it.
- **Show the rest.** Given a List showing only its problem Booking, when the admin chooses to show the rest, then all its Bookings show.
- **Filters and search.** Given the Billing tab, when an admin chooses Done and one anaesthetist, then only that anaesthetist's Lists whose billing is complete show; a search narrows the table to the Lists and Bookings that match.
- **List heading.** Given an authorised List, then its heading shows its date, session, hospital, status, counts sent, in Xero and paid in, and its billing run.
- **Approval only in Review.** Given a List awaiting approval, when the admin chooses it on the Billing tab, then the Review queue opens on it; the Billing tab offers no approve action.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: AA staff have clear visibility of Xero sync status for each record. Failed items are surfaced for review rather than silently dropped.
