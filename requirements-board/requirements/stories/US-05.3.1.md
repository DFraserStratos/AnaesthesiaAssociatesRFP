The Billing/Invoice Engine applies one rule to every Booking with more than one Procedure:

- **Base units** go only on the [primary Procedure](US-03.2.1). The system never allows them on an additional Procedure, and enforces this rather than only hinting on screen: the field is not editable there.
- **Time units** go on every Procedure, calculated from its own [recorded start and finish times](US-03.3.3).
- **Modifier units** sit wholly on the primary while the Booking's total is 4 or fewer. Above 4, the system divides them equally across the Procedures, and any remainder goes to the primary.

This is so every Booking with several Procedures is priced the same way, by the rule AA agreed.

The rule adjusts only Procedures priced from RVG units, and the modifier split is shared among them. On a mixed Booking whose primary is on a fixed or open price, no base units are billed until OQ-132 is answered.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Time units.** The system calculates time units for each Procedure in a Booking from that Procedure's own [recorded start and finish times](US-03.3.3), whether it is the primary Procedure or an additional one.
- **Modifier split.** When a Booking's total modifier units add up to 4 or fewer, they all sit on the primary Procedure. When the total is more than 4, the system divides the modifier units equally across every Procedure in the Booking priced from RVG units, and allocates any remainder to the primary.
- **Prepaid primary.** Given a Booking whose primary Procedure is prepaid and [left off the final invoice](US-08.2.2), then the rule still ranks all the Booking's Procedures: a not-prepaid additional Procedure is priced as an additional Procedure, with no base units and its share of any split modifier units, as if the primary were on the invoice.

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal A19): the rule adjusts only Procedures priced from RVG units, and the modifier split is shared among them.

Merges the former US-05.3.2 (Time units on every Procedure) and US-05.3.3 (Modifier split above four units), both now Retired.

On 7 October the equal split was agreed; whether it applies across different Contracts was not raised then, and the 19 October answer (OQ-90) makes it the one system-wide rule with no per-Contract override. v6 applies the multi-procedure rules to adjust the units of calculated (RVG-priced) procedures. Greg: "there should be no base units on the secondary procedure."

Which Procedure is primary was not discussed on 19 October. It was settled on 16 October: the primary is the first Procedure, typically the most expensive (Vanessa: "always the most expensive one would be the primary"), and anyone with edit rights sets it.

Donald, 29 October: when the primary Procedure is prepaid and left off the final invoice, the not-prepaid additional Procedure still takes the reduction for an additional Procedure; the ranking is across all the Booking's Procedures, and leaving the prepaid one off does not change its price (Typed decision 2026-10-29 · Donald (product owner)).
