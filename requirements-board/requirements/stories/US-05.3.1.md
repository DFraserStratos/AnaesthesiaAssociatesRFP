The system never allows base units on an additional Procedure within a Booking; only the [primary Procedure](US-03.2.1) carries them.

This is enforced structurally, not just as a UI hint: the base units field is not editable on an additional Procedure.

**Time units** are different: every Procedure in the Booking earns its own, calculated from that Procedure's own [recorded start and finish times](US-03.3.3), whether it is the primary Procedure or an additional one.

**Modifier units** sit wholly on the primary Procedure while the Booking's total is 4 or fewer. Once the total exceeds 4, the system divides the modifier units equally across every Procedure in the Booking, allocating any remainder to the primary.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Time units.** The system calculates time units for each Procedure in a Booking from that Procedure's own [recorded start and finish times](US-03.3.3), whether it is the primary Procedure or an additional one.
- **Modifier split.** When a Booking's total modifier units add up to 4 or fewer, they all sit on the primary Procedure. When the total is more than 4, the system divides the modifier units equally across every Procedure in the Booking, and allocates any remainder to the primary.
- **Prepaid primary.** Given a Booking whose primary Procedure is prepaid and [left off the final invoice](US-08.2.2), then the rule still ranks all the Booking's Procedures: a not-prepaid additional Procedure is priced as an additional Procedure, with no base units and its share of any split modifier units, as if the primary were on the invoice.

## Notes

Merges the former US-05.3.2 (Time units on every Procedure) and US-05.3.3 (Modifier split above four units), both now Retired.

Worked example in domain-model.md: 7 modifier units over 3 procedures = 3 / 2 / 2.

On 7 October the equal split was agreed; whether it applies across different Contracts was not raised then, and the 19 October answer (OQ-90) makes it the one system-wide rule with no per-Contract override. v6 applies the multi-procedure rules to adjust the units of calculated (RVG-priced) procedures. OQ-15 is answered: the split was agreed with Ben, Vanessa and Greg on 7 October. AA confirmed the whole rule on 19 October (OQ-90): base units on the primary Procedure only, time units on every Procedure from its own times, modifier units on the primary when they total four or fewer and split equally above four. Greg: "there should be no base units on the secondary procedure."

Which Procedure is primary was not discussed on 19 October. It was settled on 16 October: the primary is the first Procedure, typically the most expensive (Vanessa: "always the most expensive one would be the primary"), and anyone with edit rights sets it.

Donald, 29 October: when the primary Procedure is prepaid and left off the final invoice, the not-prepaid additional Procedure still takes the reduction for an additional Procedure; the ranking is across all the Booking's Procedures, and leaving the prepaid one off does not change its price (Typed decision 2026-10-29 · Donald (product owner)).
