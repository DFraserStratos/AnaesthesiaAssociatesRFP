Availability is a status held on the half-day Slot: an anaesthetist's AM and PM Slots on the same day can carry different statuses, independent of whether either Slot holds a List or bookings.

The anaesthetist keeps the status up from their own calendar ([anaesthetist availability calendar](US-01.5.3)), so the Slot status and the calendar are one mechanism, not two to reconcile. Once a List is put in a Slot, the List shows in place of the status.

## Notes

OQ-64 settled that a Slot is a box with a status and a List goes into it, the List then showing in place of the status. The status values are a user-maintained list ([Slot status master data](US-01.2.2)), not a fixed set. The final values are still to be defined with AA's users, who have "a lot of redundant statuses".
