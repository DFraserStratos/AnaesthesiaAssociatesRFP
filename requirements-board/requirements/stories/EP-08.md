When a List reaches AUTHORISED, the Billing/Invoice Engine reads each [locked Procedure and its Contract](US-04.3.5), applies the [multi-procedure rules](FT-05.3), calculates the final amount, and deducts any [prepayment already taken](US-06.4.1). It then creates linked receivable and payable records in its own ledger, generates and sends the invoices, and [mirrors the result to Xero](FT-09.1).

This internal ledger, not Xero, is the system of record: it is where AA and anaesthetists see the true position on what is owed and what has been paid.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the Billing Engine candidate architecture is adopted as the starting point and verified during discovery.

Delivery constraint: after go-live, a one-month parallel run has AA recording bookings, billing and invoicing in both the legacy and new systems. AA reconciles the financial outputs, including billing calculations, invoice totals and revenue by anaesthetist, hospital and contract; any variance is raised to Stratos, investigated with Hypercare priority and corrected or explained before the parallel run closes and the legacy system is retired.
