Some Contracts set their own unit rate. The Billing/Invoice Engine prices a Procedure on one at its BTM units x the Contract's rate, instead of [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1). The rate applies to the whole Procedure, not to a separate billing line.

This is so a holder's agreed unit rate is honoured while the price still reflects the work recorded.

The rate is undated: an edit is audited and reaches every Procedure whose price is not yet held ([price follows the Contract](US-05.2.8)). A rate that must change from a date means a new Contract. Neither the anaesthetist nor the office changes the rate on a Procedure; the office [changes the price](US-05.4.2) instead.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Contract rate used.** Given a Procedure with 10 BTM units on a Contract with a fixed rate of $X per unit, when it is priced, then the price is 10 x $X, whatever the anaesthetist's own unit value.
- **Rate fixed for the anaesthetist.** Given a Procedure on such a Contract, then the rate cannot be changed on the Procedure.
