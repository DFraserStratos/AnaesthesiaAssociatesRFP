Some Contracts set a fixed rate. The system still prices the Procedure from its BTM units, multiplied by the Contract's rate instead of [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1).

- The rate applies to the whole Procedure, not to a separate billing line.
- The anaesthetist can change the rate only where the Contract lets them change its pricing.

This is so a Contract holder's agreed unit rate is honoured while the price still reflects the work recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Contract rate used.** Given a Procedure with 10 BTM units on a Contract with a fixed rate of $X per unit, when it is priced, then the price is 10 x $X, whatever the anaesthetist's own unit value.
- **Rate fixed for the anaesthetist.** Given a Contract with a fixed rate that does not let the anaesthetist change its pricing, when they record the Procedure, then they cannot change the rate.
