The office makes a Procedure prepaid by choosing Patient › Payment type: Prepaid on the [step rail](US-04.3.8) and picking a prepaid Contract. A prepaid Contract bills [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2) (OQ-73) and is priced at a fixed price above zero, or at an amount the office types on [Prepaid · Fixed price](US-04.3.9), never from RVG units. The prepaid amount is the Procedure's price when the prepayment invoice is raised.

This is so a person decides which Procedures are prepaid, and whoever pays for each prepaid Procedure pays a known amount before the procedure.

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal A2): a prepaid Contract always bills the payer; OQ-73 awaits AA's confirmation.
