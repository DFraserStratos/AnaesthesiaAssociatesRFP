When the office [sets up a Booking](US-04.3.4) and picks a Contract flagged as prepaid for one of its Procedures, the Booking needs prepayment. Such a Contract typically does not bill its holder, so the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) prepays. The amount is the fixed price on the Contract line picked, which must carry a price above zero.

## Notes

Donald, 16 October: "There needs to be some human in the loop that places [it] on the contract to then trigger the prepayment." A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new Contract version does not relink it (Donald, 19 October). Ben confirmed this for a prepaid Procedure on 27 October (OQ-104).

Greg: "if a booking's made using that contract, then it does invoke a prepay procedure. But it's not the procedure to set it up, it's the procedure to bill the customer."

Greg, 28 October: a prepaid Contract can be an anaesthetist's own price list or a surgeon's, identified in the Contract by practitioner ID, "because we can use this exact same mechanism for surgeon's... arrangements".
