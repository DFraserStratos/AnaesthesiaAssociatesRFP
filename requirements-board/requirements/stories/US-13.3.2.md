Where a Booking's billing has failed, an admin resolves it beside the Booking on the Billing tab, where [each List's Bookings are listed](US-13.3.1): retry it, fix the payer, or mark it resolved.

Hand-offs to Xero that are waiting or failing are on the Xero tab of [the Finances section](US-13.3.3), each with its attempts, next try, last error and Retry now. The invoices changed directly in Xero, caught by [the daily reconciliation poll](US-09.2.2), are listed there too, and each is resolved on its own. The Xero tab opens on what needs action.

This is so the office can step in by hand on anything the automated billing flow could not process on its own.

- **Stuck at Xero shows twice.** A Booking whose hand-off to Xero is waiting or failing shows on the Xero tab, and on the Billing tab as a problem under its List.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Fix beside the Booking.** Given a Booking that failed because its payer has no billing email, when an admin fixes the payer from its row on the Billing tab and retries, then the Booking bills and leaves the Problems view.
- **Retry now.** Given a hand-off to Xero that is failing, when an admin chooses Retry now on the Xero tab and it succeeds, then it leaves the Xero tab's list and the Booking shows as in Xero.
- **Stuck at Xero shows twice.** Given a Booking waiting for Xero, then it shows on the Xero tab with its attempts, next try and last error, and on the Billing tab under its List.
- **Changed in Xero.** Given an invoice voided or edited directly in Xero, when the daily poll finds it, then it is listed on the Xero tab until an admin resolves it.
