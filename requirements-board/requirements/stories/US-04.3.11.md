When a Booking moves to another List, every Procedure keeps its Contract: nothing is picked again. A Contract that would not be [offered on the new List](US-04.3.2) is flagged at [office review](US-07.2.2).

This is so a move never silently changes who is billed, and the office sees any Contract that no longer fits.

A price that is not held works out again on the new procedure date, so a fixed price with no price range in force on that date shows "[Needs a price](US-05.2.8)". A prepaid amount, a typed price and an [AUTHORISED snapshot](US-04.3.5) do not change.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Kept.** Given a Booking moved from one surgeon's List to another's, then each Procedure keeps its Contract.
- **Flagged.** Given the kept Contract is linked only to the first surgeon, then it is flagged at office review.
- **Re-priced.** Given an unedited fixed-price Procedure moved to a date in a later price range, then it is priced from that range.
- **Prepaid.** Given a prepaid Procedure whose prepayment invoice is raised, then a move keeps its prepaid amount.
