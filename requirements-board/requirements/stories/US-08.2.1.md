The Billing/Invoice Engine groups a Booking's Procedures by the [billable party](FT-11.2) each one has, which its [Contract](US-04.2.1) defines, then generates one invoice per group.

This is why a single Booking can produce several invoices: each group of Procedures billed to a different party gets its own invoice.

## Notes

Settled by OQ-67 for now: the billable party is always defined by the Contract, with as many Contracts as AA needs. Greg floated a different model in the meeting (no default Contract, the hospital holding the Contract always pays); OQ-78 is now answered against it: No contract (RVG) is the one default Contract, and it bills the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2).
