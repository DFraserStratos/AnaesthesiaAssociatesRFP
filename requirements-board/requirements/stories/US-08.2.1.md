The Billing/Invoice Engine groups a Booking's Procedures by the [billable party](FT-11.2) each one has, which its [Contract](US-04.2.1) defines, then generates one invoice per group.

This is why a single Booking can produce several invoices: each group of Procedures billed to a different party gets its own invoice.

Where a Contract bills the payer, the billable party is [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2):

- Procedures that bill the same payer (the patient, or a payer copied to them when another Procedure's payer was changed) share one invoice.
- A payer typed in separately on a Procedure is a different payer, with an invoice of its own. Payers are never matched on name or email.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One invoice per party.** Given a Booking with two Procedures billed to the same holder, when its invoices are generated, then they share one invoice.
- **Same payer.** Given two Procedures billed to the payer, both left as the patient, when the invoices are generated, then they share one invoice to the patient.
- **Payer typed separately.** Given two Procedures billed to the payer, each with a payer typed in separately with the same name and email, when the invoices are generated, then each payer receives their own invoice.
