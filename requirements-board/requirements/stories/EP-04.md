A Contract is one priced item: it says how a Procedure on a Booking is priced and who is invoiced for it. It is not necessarily a legal contract between AA and anyone.

Each Contract is held by a [Party](US-04.1.5), [bills its holder or the Procedure's payer](US-04.2.1), is priced by one of five [pricing kinds](US-04.2.2), and is Active or Disabled. Every Procedure has [exactly one Contract](US-04.3.1), picked on the [step rail](US-04.3.8).

This is so every Procedure knows, from one choice, how it is priced and who gets the invoice.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: whether the hospital route is final or can fall back to the Billable Party (for example on dispute or non-payment) is treated as a commercial and relationship decision before a technical one. Re-routing an issued invoice also affects the matching ACCPAY and the anaesthetist's outstanding balance view, so Stratos wants to understand what AA does today before proposing what the system should do.

Note: the response still frames this as the hospital billing route; in this catalogue picking the Contract replaces separate route resolution. AA has since answered the fallback question (OQ-19): if a hospital or other holder disputes an invoice because of an error on AA's side, it is fixed by [credit note and re-issue](US-08.6.2); otherwise it is treated as an outstanding invoice. No fallback to the patient is defined.
