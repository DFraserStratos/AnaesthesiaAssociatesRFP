What the anaesthetist can change on the price depends on the Procedure's Contract:

- on No contract (RVG) or their own first-party Contracts, they can type their own price or apply a percentage discount, with a reason
- on a third-party Contract the agreed price stands; to depart from it they [change the Contract](US-03.4.1)
- on a Contract with a [fixed discount](US-04.2.2), the discount is shown locked

Base, time and modifier (BTM) units are still recorded in full.
