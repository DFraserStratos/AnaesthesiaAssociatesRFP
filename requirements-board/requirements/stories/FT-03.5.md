Whether the anaesthetist can change a Procedure's price, rate or discount is set on its Contract: each Contract carries a flag, set by AA, that says whether the anaesthetist may change its pricing.

- Typically they can on [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3), on a hospital's RVG Contract and on their own price list (on a prepaid Procedure, whether they can is OQ-96).
- Typically they cannot on a hospital's fixed-price Contract or a surgeon's fixed-fee arrangement. There the price is shown, no discount of their own can be added, and to depart from it they [change the Contract](US-03.4.1).
- A discount the Contract fixes ([fixed discount](US-05.4.3)) is shown.

The office can change any price, rate or discount at any time ([office price change](US-05.4.2)); the last change stands. No reason is needed for any of these: each change is audited and seen at review. Base, time and modifier (BTM) units are still recorded where entered.

## Notes

Hospital fixed prices take no anaesthetist discount: Ben, via Greg, "if you don't want the rate, don't do the procedure" (14 October).

On 16 October Greg and Donald talked about whether an anaesthetist could still depart from a price at their own discretion. On 19 October it was settled as the flag on each Contract, set by AA, not a global rule (OQ-107): Greg, "The flag on the contract might be the best way because then it's not global." On a Contract that does not allow it, the anaesthetist changes the Contract or asks the office, which can change any price.

The diagram [Billing route](AR-24#anaesthetist-adjustment) shows the per-Contract flag and no reason needed (redrawn 19 October).
