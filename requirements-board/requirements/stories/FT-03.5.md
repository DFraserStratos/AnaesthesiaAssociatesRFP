The anaesthetist can [type their own price](US-03.5.1) on a Procedure where its Contract allows it: a setting AA sets per Contract, only on a Contract priced from RVG units, with the RVG detail kept beside the typed price. Where it does not, they [change the Contract](US-03.4.1) or ask the office, which can [change any price](US-05.4.2).

This is so the anaesthetist can charge their own price where AA allows it, without losing the RVG basis behind it.

## Notes

Hospital fixed prices take no anaesthetist discount: Ben, via Greg, "if you don't want the rate, don't do the procedure" (14 October).

On 16 October Greg and Donald talked about whether an anaesthetist could still depart from a price at their own discretion. On 19 October it was settled as the flag on each Contract, set by AA, not a global rule (OQ-107): Greg, "The flag on the contract might be the best way because then it's not global." On a Contract that does not allow it, the anaesthetist changes the Contract or asks the office, which can change any price.

The diagram [Billing / Invoice Flow](AR-24#anaesthetist-adjustment) shows the per-Contract flag and no reason needed (redrawn 19 October).
