When an anaesthetist [moves their own List](US-01.4.3) to the office or into a colleague's free Slot, including when they hand a List on after [marking its Slot unavailable](US-01.5.5), a notification posts to the [shared pool](US-13.8.1). It says which List moved and to whom. The notice stays active until an office admin [ticks it resolved](US-13.8.3). The office can then send the cover-change [update email](US-02.3.3).

This is so the office knows about every List move made without them, and can tell the parties.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Moved to a colleague.** Given an anaesthetist moves a List to a colleague, when the move is saved, then a notification naming both anaesthetists posts to the pool.
- **Returned to the office.** Given an anaesthetist returns a List to the office, when the move is saved, then a notification posts to the pool and the List shows as a [Draft List](FT-01.6).

## Notes

Reading this story in the 2026-10-02 review, Greg said the anaesthetist must be able to move a single Booking as well as a whole List (see [moving a single Booking](US-01.4.7)). A single Booking move offers the same notice as an option: the mover may notify the admin team, but it is not required (OQ-85; Notes 2026-10-19 · AA meeting with Greg #7).
