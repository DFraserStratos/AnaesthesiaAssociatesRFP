As the anaesthetist records a Procedure, their app shows what its price comes to, worked out from its Contract and what they have entered so far. The figure changes as they add time or modifiers. It shows for every Contract, RVG-priced ones included, and is read-only unless the Contract lets them [change the price](US-03.5.1).

The price is visible but not prominent: it sits quietly on the Procedure, not in the anaesthetist's face.

This is so anaesthetists see what they are charging as they choose modifiers. Hospitals query modifier claims with the office daily, and seeing the price rise may lead anaesthetists to claim only the modifiers that apply.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Updates as entered.** Given an RVG-priced Procedure, when the anaesthetist adds a modifier, then the price shown rises to include it.
- **Fixed price.** Given a Procedure on a fixed-price Contract, then the app shows the fixed price, read-only.
- **Not in the way.** The price shows on the Procedure without a prompt, pop-up or confirmation.

## Technical discussion

Where it sits and how prominent it is are open beyond "not in your face".
