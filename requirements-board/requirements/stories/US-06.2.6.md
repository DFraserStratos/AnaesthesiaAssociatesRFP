The office can prepay a Procedure at an amount the anaesthetist asks for when no prepaid Contract has the price. On the [step rail](US-04.3.8) it chooses Patient › Payment type: Prepaid, flips the ["Other price" toggle](US-04.3.9), which picks Prepaid · Fixed price, and types the amount, which must be above zero.

This is so an anaesthetist can ask for a prepayment on work that is not on any price list, without the office setting up a Contract.

The typed amount becomes the [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2) when the [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) is raised, and is read-only from then on. Only the office moves a Procedure onto or off a prepaid Contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Typed amount.** Given a spine case with no prepaid Contract, when the office picks Prepaid · Fixed price and types $2,000, then when the prepayment invoice is raised it is addressed to the payer for $2,000 plus GST.
- **Above zero.** Given the office types 0 on Prepaid · Fixed price, then the prepayment invoice cannot be raised.
- **Read-only after.** Given the prepayment invoice is raised, then nobody can change the amount: a difference is a credit or an additional invoice.

## Notes

Our readings (contract model proposal): the amount is read-only once the prepayment invoice is raised (A16); only the office moves a Procedure onto or off a prepaid Contract (A17).
