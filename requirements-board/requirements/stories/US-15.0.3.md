Data is entered once and stays current everywhere it appears. Hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and other [reference data](US-13.4.1) are referenced by identity rather than copied into place, so a change made in one place shows up wherever that data is used.

Billing is the one deliberate exception. Reference data is [locked once a Procedure is authorised](US-04.3.5), so an issued invoice can never be rewritten by a change made elsewhere afterwards.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and similar [reference data](US-13.4.1) are referenced into the schedule by identity, not copied into it, so a change made once is reflected everywhere. The one deliberate exception is the billing model, where reference data is [frozen at authorisation](US-04.3.5) so an issued invoice cannot be rewritten by later drift. Exactly which fields are frozen is to be determined in discovery.
