The system looks up and validates patients against the NHI FHIR API, via the Health NZ Digital Services Hub, and references anaesthetists by their [HPI CPN](US-12.1.4).

This is in scope for the first release: the NHI lookup is a simple API, and the office needs a solid NHI to avoid duplicate patients (see [missing NHI problem list](US-11.1.5)).

The NHI is used only for that purpose, nothing more, because a system holding health information carries specific obligations under the Health Information Privacy Code 2020.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: NHI is used only for its intended purpose, with lookups performed against the NHI FHIR API via the Health NZ Digital Services Hub. NHI lookups via the Hub are included in [dual-format NHI](US-11.1.2) regression testing, alongside inbound HL7 v2 and FHIR paths.

## Notes

The NHI could be refreshed from the central NHI register. Hospitals currently get NHI updates twice a day.
