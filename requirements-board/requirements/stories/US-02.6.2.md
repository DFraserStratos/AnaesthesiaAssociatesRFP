Anaesthetists and the office both see a stub on the List, clearly marked as a booking with a problem that the office must resolve.

This is so whoever looks at the List knows a booking is missing from it, and the problem is chased.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Clearly marked.** Given a List holds a stub, when an anaesthetist or the office views the List, then the stub is clearly marked as a booking with a problem that the office must resolve.

## Technical discussion

What the stub shows (patient name, Procedure, the reason it could not be posted) is to be worked out with intake.
