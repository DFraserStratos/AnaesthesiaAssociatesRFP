Admins maintain a master list of surgeons' rooms. Each room holds its name, contact details including a contact email, and the surgeons who belong to it. A surgeon can belong to more than one room.

This is so a surgeon is always tied to a room, and so the office has one place to find the room's email. That email is the To address for a [drafted booking update email](US-02.3.3), and the room is who the office emails for a [missing NHI](US-11.1.5). Which room's contacts apply to a Booking for a surgeon at more than one room is open (OQ-127).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Room record.** A room has a name, contact email and phone, and a list of surgeons.
- **Surgeons belong to rooms.** A surgeon is linked to one or more rooms; each room's contacts are shown on the surgeon's profile.
- **More than one room.** Given a surgeon at two practices, then the one surgeon record is linked to both rooms.

## Notes

The room no longer sends estimated durations for prepayment: a prepaid procedure is now a fixed amount, not an estimate (OQ-38).

The initial list comes from Vanessa. OQ-128 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Vanessa): "Our system doesn't need to assume a surgeon at one room, we can be more flexible." Ben's list of 3 November puts 4 surgeons at two practices.

In that list most practices give one shared inbox (info@, admin@), 16 give no email, and at one ENT practice each surgeon has their own PA and inbox. Rooms in the list may therefore have no email (and some no phone) when loaded; a [drafted booking update email](US-02.3.3) leaves its To address empty where none is held. Whether a room's contacts include its [practice managers and PAs](US-13.6.5), or those belong to the surgeon, is OQ-127.

Greg's picture of the room record: it holds the office's contact details ("a short contact list") and, as children, its surgeons; or a more generic relationship, an actor with a role of admin or surgeon. Donald: "Whichever the relationship may look like."

Retired 18 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by AA): the system keeps no surgeons' room record for now. Donald: "the room information is, and Surgeon's contact information is supplementary to the functions of the app... for now we don't have a room, and we record this information just against the surgeon's profile." A surgeon's contacts are on their [profile](US-13.6.2) as [surgeon contacts](US-13.6.5), the practice name is plain text there, and the office's emails go back to [where the List came from](US-02.2.4).
