Admins can review any future day in the [same day view](US-13.1.1), as far ahead as Slots have been created. Reviewing tomorrow's Lists is the usual case, as part of the office's normal practice.

This review carries no tracked state in the system: there is no gate or step for the office to complete, so nothing blocks a List's progress if the check is skipped.

## Notes

Generalised from "tomorrow" in the 2026-10-02 review: neither Donald nor Greg could see why it said tomorrow, and Donald asked for a general requirement to review all future days up to where Slots are created. He called it 13.1.3; this story is meant.
