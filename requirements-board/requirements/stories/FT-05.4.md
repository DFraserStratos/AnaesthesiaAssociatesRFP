The anaesthetist, on RVG Contracts whose [setting lets them](US-04.2.2), and [the office](US-05.4.2), on any Contract, can change a Procedure's price. No reason is needed, every change is [audited](US-13.5.2) and flagged at [office review](US-07.2.2), the last change stands, and BTM units are kept as recorded. A prepaid Procedure's price is read-only once its prepayment invoice is raised.

This is so whoever is working on a Procedure can put its price right, and the office sees every change before the List is authorised.
