The anaesthetist, on [Contracts that let them](US-04.2.2), and [the office](US-05.4.2), on any Contract, can change a Procedure's price, rate or discount. Each is a change to the values on the Procedure: no reason is needed, every change is audited and flagged at office review, and the last change stands. BTM units are kept as recorded.
