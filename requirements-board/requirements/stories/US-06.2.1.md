The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Procedure as needing prepayment whenever its Contract is [prepaid](US-04.2.14), and the Booking as needing prepayment if any of its Procedures does. Each prepaid Procedure's prepayment is invoiced to [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2) (OQ-73), so prepaid Procedures that name different payers are invoiced to each payer separately ([one prepayment invoice per payer](US-06.3.1)).

This is so prepayment follows from the Contract a person picked, and from nothing else.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Prepaid Contract.** Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when it is checked, then prepayment is flagged.
- **No prepaid Contract.** Given a Booking whose Procedures are all on Contracts not flagged as prepaid, when it is checked, then no prepayment is flagged.
- **Per Procedure.** Given a Booking with one Procedure on a prepaid Contract and others on Contracts not flagged as prepaid, then only that Procedure needs prepayment.
