The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Booking as requiring prepayment whenever any of its Procedures is on a Contract flagged as [prepaid](US-04.2.14). The prepayment is invoiced to whoever the Contract bills ([who is billed](FT-11.2)). In practice that is the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2), a person, since a prepaid Contract typically does not bill its holder, but the system does not forbid a Contract that bills its holder from being set up as prepaid (OQ-73).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when it is checked, then prepayment is flagged.
- Given a Booking whose Procedures are all on Contracts not flagged as prepaid, when it is checked, then no prepayment is flagged.
- Given a Booking with one Procedure on a prepaid Contract and others on Contracts not flagged as prepaid, then only that Procedure needs prepayment.
