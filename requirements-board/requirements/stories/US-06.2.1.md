The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Booking as requiring prepayment whenever any of its Procedures, or that Procedure's [RVG group](US-05.1.1), is in the [anaesthetist's prepaid set](US-06.1.1), and only where the [payer](US-11.2.2) is a person paying for the patient (the patient, or for example a guardian), never an organisation such as an insurer or hospital.

This is checked across the whole Booking, not just its primary Procedure, since the typical case is a hospital Booking with an additional, elective cosmetic Procedure added on.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with a Procedure in the prepaid set whose payer is an insurer or hospital, when it is checked, then no prepayment is flagged.
- Given a Booking with a Procedure whose RVG group is in the prepaid set and whose payer is a person, when it is checked, then prepayment is flagged.

## Notes

The trigger is the anaesthetist's prepaid setting, not a flag on the Contract, and there is no [Pre-paid Contract category](US-04.1.1) (OQ-25). The amount comes from the [anaesthetist's own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14) (OQ-04). On 7 October Greg argued for a pre-payable flag on the Contract ("that would be the trigger"); it was not adopted. What happens when a prepaid procedure has no price in that Contract is OQ-92.

The patient-only limit comes from the OQ-58 answer. OQ-73 answered: "patient" means any person paying for the patient, never an organisation. Greg: "prepayments are always patient-direct... there's no hospital involved in this process."
