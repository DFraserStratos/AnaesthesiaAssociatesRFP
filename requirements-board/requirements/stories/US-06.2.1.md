The Billing/Invoice Engine flags a Booking as requiring prepayment whenever any of its Procedures is on a Contract flagged as [prepaid](US-04.2.14). Prepayment is always from a person paying for the patient (OQ-73); the typical prepaid Contract does not bill its holder, so the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) prepays. This is checked across the whole Booking, not just its primary Procedure, since the typical case is a hospital Booking with an additional, elective cosmetic Procedure added on.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when it is checked, then prepayment is flagged.
- Given a Booking whose Procedures are all on Contracts not flagged as prepaid, when it is checked, then no prepayment is flagged.

## Notes

The trigger moved from the anaesthetist's profile to the Contract on 16 October (OQ-25, OQ-92). OQ-73: prepayment is always from a person.
