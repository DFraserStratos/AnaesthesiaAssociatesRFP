When a List becomes [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine processes it as a whole: it works through each Booking on the List, and each Procedure within it, reading [the Contract already locked to that Procedure](US-04.3.5).

The engine does not work out who pays. The pricing rules, the price and who is billed all come from that locked Contract: the holder Party when the Contract bills its holder, otherwise [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2). Procedures on the Booking that bill the same payer share [one invoice](US-08.2.1); a payer typed in separately is a different payer with an invoice of its own. The Contract was chosen earlier, at [booking setup](US-04.3.4) and [office review](US-07.2.2), and can be changed until the List is AUTHORISED.

This is so every Procedure is priced and billed from data fixed at authorisation, not recalculated against Contract terms or a payer that may since have changed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Contract already resolved.** Given an AUTHORISED List, when the engine processes it, then each Procedure's pricing rules, price and billable party come from [the Contract already locked to it](US-04.3.5) and, where that Contract bills the payer, the Procedure's payer, and the engine works out no route or payer of its own.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: authorisation passes the completed List to the Billing Engine, which applies procedure, contract and billing rules, determines the appropriate payer, and generates the required invoices.

Note: the response describes the engine as determining the payer at this point, whereas this story (per Q&A #2) takes the recipient from the locked Contract on each Procedure (and the Procedure's payer where the Contract bills the payer), with no route resolution in the engine.
