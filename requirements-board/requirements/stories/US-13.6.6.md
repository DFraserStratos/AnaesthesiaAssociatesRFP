Every surgeon holds a list of specialties from AA's [specialty list](US-13.4.4): usually one, at least one required. The surgeon's specialties decide which [Contracts are offered](US-04.2.16) on their Lists: a Contract with no specialty is offered for every surgeon. An admin adds, changes and removes a surgeon's specialties on the surgeon's [profile](US-13.6.2), with their other details.

This is so the specialty comes from the surgeon, not guessed from the procedure text.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Required.** Given a surgeon with no specialty, when saved, then it is refused.
- **Several.** Given a surgeon with two specialties, then Contracts with either are offered on their Lists.
- **Edit on the profile.** Given a surgeon's profile, when an admin adds a specialty and saves, then the surgeon's Lists offer Contracts with that specialty.
