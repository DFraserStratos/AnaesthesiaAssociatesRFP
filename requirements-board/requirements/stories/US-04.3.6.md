In the future state the hospital defines the Contract: when its inbound data (the daily sheet, later an integration) says which Contract a Procedure is under, the system sets that Contract on the Procedure, and the office still reviews it at the [SUBMITTED review](US-07.2.2).

Hospitals know their own Contracts and send sheets every day saying which applies. Today the integration sometimes carries the Contract and sometimes does not, so the office adds it by hand ([admin sets Contracts](US-04.3.4)). Insurance comes from the sheets or the surgeons' secretaries and is entered separately.

This is so the Contract comes from the party that knows it, with less re-keying by the office.
