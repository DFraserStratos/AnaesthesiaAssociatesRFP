The office brings each hospital's bookings into the system and matches them to Lists and Bookings on a matching screen. Incoming data goes through review and merge: the office sees what has changed, actions anything needed and accepts it.

This is one of the two intake pathways, alongside the [surgeon PDF pathway](FT-02.2), so hospital bookings arrive through a checking step the office controls.

## Notes

Today AA staff take bookings from the hospitals' daily sheets and downloads and match them to Lists and Bookings on a matching screen. That matching screen imports the files from St George's and Southern Cross through an integration; everything from the other providers is entered by hand.

Greg, from Vanessa, says the hospital sends one updated theatre list a day, for the next day's bookings, and does not send repeated updates; the v6 executive summary describes it as a PDF and as a direct data integration (format still OQ-13). It can carry a few things the surgeon's rooms do not know.

Both intake pathways land in this matching screen in the first release (OQ-108). The first release keeps matching a manual admin review, with hospital bookings imported into the screen and no automatic sync. Automatic sync, more hospital feeds and automatic matching are [Future Work](FT-14.6).

Greg and Donald agreed the first version has no automated decisions: downloads from "probably just the same two hospitals" land in the matching and creating screen, where staff see the patient details and what is missing, then save a [Draft List](US-01.6.1) or assign an anaesthetist. Automated approvals come once the matching is trusted. The cadence is settled for now (one list a day, the day before); the format and fields per hospital are still OQ-13.

OQ-108 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Ben or Vanessa): both intake sources are built in the first release, replacing the 20 October rule that this pathway was the priority. OQ-109 answered 18 November (Donald, for the build; not yet discussed with AA): both accounts of today hold, since the matching screen imports hospital files through an integration, and there is no automatic syncing in the first release. Ben named Forte as one of the two on 27 October; the catalogue keeps St George's and Southern Cross.
