Today AA staff take bookings from the hospitals' daily sheets and downloads and match them to Lists and Bookings on a matching screen. Only St George's and Southern Cross are integrated with the current system; everything from the other providers is entered by hand.

Greg, from Vanessa, says the hospital sends one updated theatre list a day, for the next day's bookings, and does not send repeated updates; the v6 executive summary describes it as a PDF and as a direct data integration (format still OQ-13). It can carry a few things the surgeon's rooms do not know.

This manual download and match is the real pathway the system supports, and for now it is treated as the priority intake pathway. If the surgeons' rooms turn out to start most Bookings (OQ-108), the [surgeon PDF pathway](FT-02.2) can be raised later.

The first release keeps matching a manual admin review, and adds an automatic sync from St George's and Southern Cross into the screen. More hospital feeds and automatic matching are Future Work ([feeds from the other Christchurch providers](US-14.6.1)).

Incoming data, whether a download, an integration or a PDF, goes through a review and merge step: the office sees what has changed, actions anything needed and accepts it.

## Notes

Greg and Donald agreed the first version has no automated decisions: downloads from "probably just the same two hospitals" land in the matching and creating screen, where staff see the patient details and what is missing, then save a [Draft List](US-01.6.1) or assign an anaesthetist. Automated approvals come once the matching is trusted. The cadence is settled for now (one list a day, the day before); the format and fields per hospital are still OQ-13.
