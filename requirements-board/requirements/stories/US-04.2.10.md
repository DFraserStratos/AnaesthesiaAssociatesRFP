Each Contract has a start date and, if needed, an end date, and all its lines share those dates. A price review creates a new dated version of the Contract, linked to the one it replaces: its lines are copied and then changed, and the old version ends the day before the new one starts.

The price used is the one in force on the date of the procedure. Bookings priced under the old version keep the old price. Admins can enter a new version ahead of its start date, so a change a hospital announces with a few weeks' notice is already in place when it takes effect.

Hospitals and funders send an annual schedule and then email additions during the year, especially as outsourced DHB Lists change, usually with an effective date. The same procedure can have different prices under different funding arrangements. Each arrangement is its own Contract, offered through the [filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2).

This is so a price review applies cleanly from its date, and older prices stay for [invoices that have to be reproduced](US-08.4.4).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Contract version starting 1 April and the next version starting 1 July, when a procedure done on 15 June is priced, then the 1 April version's price is used.
- Given a new version entered ahead of its start date, when admins view the Contract before that date, then both the current and the upcoming version are visible.
- Given a new version, then the previous version ends the day before it starts, and the two never overlap.
- Given a Booking priced under the old version, when the new version starts, then the Booking keeps the old price.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design holds validity on the Contract only, never on its lines, and links each price-review version to the one it replaces ([AR-29 p9](AR-29#p9)). Greg's view on 7 October was the same: "the contract would be dated, but the lines wouldn't". It is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
