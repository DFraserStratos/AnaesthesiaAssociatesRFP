Each Contract is kept as a series of dated versions, and every change, a correction or a renewal, creates a new version from a date the office chooses. That date has no default and is never in the past: nothing is backdated and nothing is deleted. On submit ([stage and submit Contract changes](US-04.1.6)) the previous version ends the day before, so versions never overlap and never leave a gap. A Contract ends without a successor only when the office gives its current version an end date.

The procedure date decides which version prices a Procedure, even when it is invoiced later. When a new version takes effect:

- un-invoiced Procedures dated on or after its start move to the matching line of the new version (matched by the line's system key), except a prepaid Procedure, which keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice
- any with no matching line are listed for the office to resolve
- Procedures dated before it, and invoiced ones, are untouched

A Contract can have only one future-dated version at a time. Until it starts it can be corrected freely, its start date moved (the current version's end date moves with it) or withdrawn, in which case the current version becomes open-ended again and any Procedures already relinked return to the matching current lines. Once a version is in effect, an error is corrected with a new version from a chosen date, and any Procedures in between by the [office changing their price](US-05.4.2).

Hospitals and funders send an annual schedule and then email additions during the year, especially as outsourced DHB Lists change, usually with an effective date. The same procedure can have different prices under different funding arrangements. Each arrangement is its own Contract, offered through the [filtered Contract list](US-04.3.2).

This is so a price review applies cleanly from its date, and older prices stay for [invoices that have to be reproduced](US-08.4.4).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Contract version starting 1 April and the next version starting 1 July, when a procedure done on 15 June is priced, then the 1 April version's price is used.
- Given a new version entered ahead of its start date, when admins view the Contract before that date, then both the current and the upcoming version are visible.
- Given a new version, then the previous version ends the day before it starts, and the two never overlap.
- Given an un-invoiced Procedure dated after a new version's start, when the version is submitted, then it is priced from the matching line of the new version, unless it is prepaid, when it keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice.
- Given an Effective From in the past, the version cannot be submitted.
- Given a Contract with a future-dated version, a second one cannot be submitted.

## Notes

OQ-48 is answered: the date of the procedure decides the price (Greg, and AA at the client meeting on 7 October). The Contract's start, end and review dates are set when it is [created or edited](US-04.1.2).

The single future-dated version, free correction or withdrawal before the start, and relinking are Greg's v6 proposal, not yet reviewed with AA. The v6 text does not say how relinking applies to a Procedure already locked when its List became authorised ([lock at authorisation](US-04.3.5)).

A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new Contract version does not relink it (Donald, 19 October). Ben confirmed this for a prepaid Procedure on 27 October: "You would honour the money that's been prepaid, because that's the quote that the patient's been given" (OQ-104). A non-prepaid fixed price follows the procedure date (Donald's restatement; Ben did not comment).
