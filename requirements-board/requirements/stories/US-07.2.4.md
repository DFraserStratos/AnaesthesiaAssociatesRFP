The To authorise tab of [the Lists section](US-13.1.3) lists every submitted List waiting for the office, oldest submitted first. Each row opens the List's review page, where the admin [reviews its Bookings](US-07.2.2) and [authorises it](US-07.3.1); the review page goes back to the tab.

This is so the office works through submitted Lists in the order they arrived, and starts on the ones that need a closer look.

- **Search and date.** A search narrows the tab to the Lists that match, and a date filter to the Lists on chosen dates.
- **Needs a look.** A filter shows only the Lists that need a closer look: a Procedure with a review flag, a change the anaesthetist [made after submitting](US-07.2.1), or a Procedure that ["Needs a price"](US-05.2.8).
- **The only place to authorise.** A List is authorised on its review page and nowhere else. The [Billing tab](US-13.3.1) shows the Lists awaiting approval, and its rows open this tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Oldest first.** Given a List submitted on Monday and one submitted on Tuesday, then Monday's List is above Tuesday's.
- **Needs a look.** Given one submitted List with a Procedure that needs a price and one with nothing to check, when the admin chooses Needs a look, then only the first shows.
- **Changed since submitted.** Given the anaesthetist changes a Booking on a submitted List, then the List shows under Needs a look.
- **Back to the tab.** Given an admin opens a List from the tab, when they go back from its review page, then the To authorise tab shows again.
- **Leaves when authorised.** Given an admin authorises a List on its review page, then the List leaves the tab.
