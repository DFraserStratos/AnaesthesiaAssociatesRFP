The Admin app keeps the work the office still has to do on Lists in one Lists section: one side-nav entry with four tabs, each its own page with its own address, its own table and filters, and a one-line sub saying what it holds:

- Draft Lists: [every List with no anaesthetist yet](US-01.6.2)
- Conflicts: [Lists at a hospital that is closed that day](US-01.5.4)
- Contracts to set: [Lists whose Procedures still need a Contract](US-04.3.12)
- To authorise: [submitted Lists waiting for the office to authorise them](US-07.2.4)

This is so the office finds every List that needs something in one place, laid out like the People, Contracts and Finances sections, with each job keeping the columns and actions it needs.

- **A tab's number counts what needs doing**, as in [the Finances section](US-13.3.3), and is hidden at zero.
- **One badge on the Lists entry.** It counts each List once, however many tabs it shows on: a Draft List at a closed hospital shows on Draft Lists and on Conflicts, and counts once. Which Lists the badge counts is open (OQ-151).
- **Records under their tab.** A List's review page opens under To authorise, with a way back to the tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Four tabs.** Given an admin chooses Lists in the side nav, then the Draft Lists, Conflicts, Contracts to set and To authorise tabs show, each with its own table, filters and one-line sub.
- **Own address.** Given an admin is on the Conflicts tab, when they reload the page or open its address in a new tab, then the Conflicts tab opens.
- **Hidden at zero.** Given no Lists at a closed hospital, then the Conflicts tab shows no number.
- **Counted once.** Given a Draft List at a closed hospital and nothing else needing work, then the Draft Lists and Conflicts tabs each show 1 and the Lists entry's badge shows 1.

## Technical discussion

Every earlier address of the Draft Lists, Conflicts and Review queue pages still opens the same content, now on its tab.
