The reference data every price starts from:

- the NZSA RVG 2021 groups as published, organised under body sections, plus AA's own groups
- the procedure list beneath them
- the modifier list

The office maintains them, and every change is recorded.

This is so every procedure has a published starting point for its base units, and users find a procedure by body area, then subgroup, then procedure, or browse the RVG codes by body area.
