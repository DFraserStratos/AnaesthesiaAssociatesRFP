Admins can record follow-up actions on a patient (a call, a note, a reminder with a due date) and re-send invoices for a patient's outstanding items. Each is kept in the patient's follow-up log, with who, when and the invoice it concerns, where there is one.

The Needs follow-up filter of the [Patients list](US-11.3.1) is one list of every patient needing the office, for any of these reasons:

- a [balance warning](US-11.3.2): an amount owing or a credit;
- a follow-up that has come due;
- details received that differ from the [patient's record](US-11.1.1).

Each row gives the reason, and a patient with more than one reason appears once. The row opens the patient's page, where the action is taken; once nothing is left to do, the patient leaves the list. The number of patients on it is the attention count on the [People section](US-13.4.1)'s entry in the side nav.

Anyone, office or anaesthetist, can write a free-text note on a List or a Booking, and everyone who opens it sees the note, so a later reader sees what was done or left behind.

This is so an admin's follow-up on an unpaid invoice is tracked in the system, not just remembered, and every patient waiting on the office is found in one place.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Follow-up logged.** Given the office records a call, a reminder or a re-send for a patient, then it shows in the patient's follow-up log with who, when and any invoice it concerns.
- **One list with a reason.** Given patients with a balance warning, a follow-up due or details that differ, when the office opens Needs follow-up, then each appears once with its reason on the row.
- **Action on the patient's page.** Given a row on Needs follow-up, when the office opens it, then the patient's page opens, where the follow-up is taken.
- **Leaves when done.** Given the only reason a patient was on Needs follow-up is resolved, then the patient leaves the list and the attention count drops by one.
- **Shared note.** Given a note on a Booking, when another user opens it, then the note is shown.
