The anaesthetist can attach files or photos to a Booking, or to a List as a whole.

## Notes

Retired 16 October. Donald found attachments on a List "quite unusual"; Greg: "I don't recall any requirement for that". Holding reference information is a generic need, not tied to a List, and Greg, reporting Ben, says he does not want the rooms' PDFs kept on a Booking (Greg: "I'm comfortable removing this feature"). The photo use case it was close to (a patient added to today's List that the anaesthetist does not know about) was not settled; see [Add a Booking from a photo](US-02.4.4).
