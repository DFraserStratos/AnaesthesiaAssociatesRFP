The building blocks every price starts from: the NZSA RVG groups (as published, plus AA's own) and the modifier list. On them sit the pure calculation rules:

- base, time and modifier units
- the multi-procedure split across a Booking
- [the pricing values a Contract can set](US-04.2.2)
- changes to a Procedure's price by the anaesthetist and the office

All of it is deterministic and testable.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Open policy decisions, such as a Contract resolving rates differently or an override that carries a reason (as proposed then; no reason is now required for a price change, FT-05.4), are treated as rules added within the existing domain model, proven by automated test coverage, and resolved during Scoping and Design.
- Automated testing is the safety net that confirms nothing broke when fixing bugs, adding features or responding to regulatory updates; it covers core business logic and critical workflows, supported by targeted manual end-to-end testing.
- The Test environment checks billing and invoicing calculations in detail.
- At cutover a small group of AA users validates contract, rate and billing reference data and the billing calculation logic, including tiered time and split billing, and a sample set of invoices is calculated end to end and reconciled against expected values.
