The Billing/Invoice Engine generates the invoice document itself, in one of two very similar layouts: the holder's layout [when the Contract bills its holder](US-04.2.1), the patient's when it bills the payer. The holder's layout carries the BTM breakdown and a couple more fields so a hospital can cross-match; the patient's does not. Each line prints the Procedure's [invoice description](US-08.4.6), any discount as its own line, and the Contract's [holder reference code](US-04.2.18) where it has one.

This is because the two audiences need slightly different information on the page: a holder's invoice carries extra detail that a patient's does not.

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal, A31), not one of Donald's decisions: the layout follows whether the Contract bills its holder or the payer, not the kind of holder.
