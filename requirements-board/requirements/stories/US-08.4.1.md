The Billing/Invoice Engine generates the invoice document itself, in one of two very similar layouts: one for hospitals and other holders, one for patients. The hospital's carries the BTM breakdown and a couple more fields so the hospital can cross-match; the patient's does not. Each line prints the Procedure's [invoice description](US-08.4.6), any discount as its own line, and the holder's reference where the Contract names one.

This is because the two audiences need slightly different information on the page: a contract holder's invoice carries extra detail that a patient's does not.
