When an ACCPAY is paid in Xero's [payables run](US-10.2.7), whether the engine learns this from a webhook or the daily poll, it records the disbursement against the [ledger payable](US-08.3.1).

The payment itself is made in Xero: Xero handles all payments to anaesthetists, and the system records and tracks them; it does not make the payment (OQ-111).

This is so 'paid into the AA account' and 'disbursed to the anaesthetist' are [tracked as two separate states](US-10.1.2). Every payment now passes through AA on its way to the anaesthetist, so disbursement needs its own explicit tracked step rather than being assumed to happen automatically.

## Notes

OQ-111 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Greg): "Xero is assumed to handle all payments. Our system tracks them." This replaces the line on OQ-111 that payments are made from the new system.
