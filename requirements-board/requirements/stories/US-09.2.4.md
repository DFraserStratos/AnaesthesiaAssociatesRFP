When an ACCPAY is paid in Xero's payables run, whether the engine learns this from a webhook or the daily poll, it records the disbursement against the [ledger payable](US-08.3.1).

This is so 'paid into the AA account' and 'disbursed to the anaesthetist' are [tracked as two separate states](US-10.1.2). Every payment now passes through AA on its way to the anaesthetist, so disbursement needs its own explicit tracked step rather than being assumed to happen automatically.
