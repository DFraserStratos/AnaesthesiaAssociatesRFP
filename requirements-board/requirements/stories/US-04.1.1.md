The office sets up every arrangement AA has as Contracts with the same settings: its holder, [who it bills](US-04.2.1), its [funding tag](US-04.2.17) and its [pricing kind](US-04.2.2). There are no kinds or categories of Contract: where a Contract's invoice goes (Patient, the List's hospital, or Special) follows from its settings.

This is so Contracts stay as simple as the business: who holds the arrangement and what it sets, not categories.

These arrangements cover most work:

| Arrangement | Destination | Who is billed | Pricing kind | Anaesthetist may override | Prepaid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3) | Patient › Standard | the Procedure's payer (the patient, or someone else such as a guardian) | RVG at the anaesthetist's rate | yes | no |
| No contract · Fixed price, and Prepaid · Fixed price | Patient (Standard, or Prepaid) | the Procedure's payer | open price | no | no, or yes |
| [An anaesthetist's prepay item](US-04.2.14) | Patient › Prepaid | the Procedure's payer | fixed price | no (a prepaid Contract is not editable) | yes |
| A surgeon's fixed fee, such as Merivale's | Patient › Standard | the Procedure's payer | fixed price | no | no |
| A hospital schedule item, such as a CES SXAP item | the hospital › SXAP | the hospital | fixed price | no | no |
| A hospital's plain RVG Contract, such as Forte's SXAP Contract | the hospital › SXAP | the hospital | RVG at the anaesthetist's rate | per Contract | no |
| An insurer or funder billed directly, such as NIB, or a surgeon group, such as Unisports | Special | the holder | RVG at the anaesthetist's or the Contract's rate | per Contract | no |

Rules across them:

- Any pricing kind can sit under any destination, for example RVG less the Contract's discount, except that Prepaid holds no Contract priced from RVG units.
- Only a Contract priced from RVG units can let the anaesthetist override its price, set by AA on each Contract: never a fixed price, and so never a prepaid Contract. The office can still change any price ([office price change](US-05.4.2)).
- A hospital can hold several Contracts for the same work, for example one for ACC work and one for an insurer's.
- Most insurer and hospital Contracts, for example a hospital's SXAP Contract for Southern Cross work, are plain RVG Contracts with no special prices, billed to their holder.

## Notes

ACC is a [funding tag](US-05.5.1), not a kind of Contract (RFP). Prepaid is a setting on a Contract, fixed for its life, not a category (OQ-25). No price override on a fixed price is OQ-117, and none on a prepaid Contract, which is not editable, is OQ-96.
