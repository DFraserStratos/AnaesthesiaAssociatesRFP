Each Procedure holds its procedure text exactly as received. It starts as the surgeon's rooms' description (from their PDF, email or booking). When the hospital's theatre list or feed arrives, usually the day before, the hospital's description replaces it, with no check against the rooms' text; a later update with different wording replaces it again in the same way.

This is so the office and the anaesthetist read one current text: the hospital's is usually clearer than the rooms' short codes ("WLE MM", "RTK", "blephs"), and is what is on its theatre list.

Nobody edits the text by hand. Each replaced text is kept in the Booking's [change history](US-02.5.5) with its source system and time, so the rooms' original can always be seen there. A Booking entered by hand keeps what the admin or anaesthetist typed or was told, in the same way.

The text is the procedure: there is no procedure master to match it to. The anaesthetist reads it to choose the Contract and the RVG code, and it is shown [with the Contract and RVG choice](US-03.1.9). The [invoice description](US-08.4.6) is held separately.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Verbatim.** Given a Booking from any channel, then its procedure text is stored exactly as received, character for character.
- **Hospital's replaces the rooms'.** Given a Procedure holding the rooms' text, when the hospital's list for it is applied with different wording, then the hospital's text replaces the rooms' on the Procedure.
- **Original in the history.** Given the hospital's text has replaced the rooms', then the Booking's change history shows the rooms' text with its source and time.
- **Entered by hand.** Given a Booking entered by hand, then the procedure text typed or told is kept as its procedure text, and is shown with the Contract and RVG choice.

## Technical discussion

In the first release the hospital's list reaches a Booking through the office's review and merge on the [matching screen](FT-02.1); the text is replaced when that change is applied, with no separate check of the wording. A late hospital text does not replace an admin's edited invoice description or a Contract's invoice description (OQ-130).
