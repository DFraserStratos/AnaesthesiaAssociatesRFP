Each Procedure keeps the procedure text exactly as the originating system sent it, from every intake channel: a hospital download or feed (HL7 or FHIR), a surgeon's PDF list, or an email. The text is never edited or overwritten. A later update that brings different wording, such as a hospital list that spells out what the rooms abbreviated, is added beside it with the system it came from and when it arrived.

When an admin or anaesthetist enters a Booking by hand, an optional "as given" field holds the procedure as it was told or written to them.

This is because the wording that arrives is short codes and snippets in endless variety ("WLE MM", "RTK", "blephs"), which someone who knows the work can read, and the person matching it to a procedure and Contract, or checking that match later, needs the original. It is shown [above the procedure and the Contract](US-03.1.9).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Verbatim.** Given a Booking from any channel, then its Procedure's source text is stored exactly as received, character for character.
- **Never overwritten.** Given a later update with different wording for the same Procedure, then the earlier text is kept and the new text is added with its source system and time.
- **As given.** Given a Booking entered by hand, then the "as given" field is optional, and when filled it is kept as the source text.
