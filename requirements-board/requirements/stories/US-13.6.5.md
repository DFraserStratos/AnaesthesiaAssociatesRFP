Admin staff record the practice managers and personal assistants (PAs) who handle surgeons' bookings, each with a name and an email address.

This is so the office can reach the practice manager or PA, not only a [room's](US-13.6.1) shared inbox.

Whether a contact belongs to the room, to one surgeon, or either, and where the contacts come from, is open (OQ-127).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Named contact.** A practice manager or PA contact has a name and an email address.
- **Shown with the surgeon.** Given a surgeon with a practice manager or PA contact, when admin staff open the surgeon's [profile](US-13.6.2), then the contact's name and email are shown.
