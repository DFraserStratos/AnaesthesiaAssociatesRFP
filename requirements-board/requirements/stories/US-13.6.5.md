Admin staff record how to reach each surgeon and their office, on the surgeon's [profile](US-13.6.2):

- one **personal contact**: the surgeon's own name, email and phone
- one or more **office contacts**: a practice manager, PA or shared office inbox, each with a name, email and phone

On the profile form, one set of office contact fields is always shown. A plus button adds another set, and a minus button on any extra set removes it completely. A surgeon who works from two practices holds an office contact for each.

This is so the office can reach the surgeon, or the person who handles their bookings, from one place. The office's emails about a Booking use these contacts where the List gives no address of its own (see [where a List came from](US-02.2.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **One set always shown.** Given a surgeon with no office contact, when admin staff edit their profile, then one empty set of office contact fields (name, email, phone) is shown.
- **Add a set.** Given the profile form, when admin staff press the plus button, then another, empty set of office contact fields is added.
- **Remove a set.** Given a surgeon with two or more office contacts, when admin staff press the minus button on an extra set, then that set and everything in it is removed. When only one set is left, it has no minus button.
- **Personal contact.** Given a surgeon, then their profile holds exactly one personal contact with a name, email and phone.
- **Shown with the surgeon.** Given a surgeon with contacts, when admin staff open their profile, then the personal contact and every office contact are shown with their name, email and phone.
- **Kept by admin staff.** Given a surgeon's profile, when admin staff change a contact's name, email or phone and save, then the profile shows the change.

## Technical discussion

Ben's list gives an email for most practices but none for 16 and no phone for some, so a contact may be saved with some fields blank. Whether a contact needs at least one field filled, and whether an office contact carries a label such as the practice it belongs to, are build choices. Whether the [cutover load](US-13.4.3) fills office contacts from the practice emails and PA names the list does hold is open. Whether a surgeon at two practices has a PA at each, or one PA for both, is still to check with AA.
