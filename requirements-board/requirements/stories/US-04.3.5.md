When a List becomes [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine copies each Procedure's Contract settings as they stand, with the [price range](US-04.2.15) in force, the units, the unit rate used, the price and the billable party, onto that Procedure, locking it in.

This is so later edits to the Contract or the RVG groups never change an invoice already raised: each Procedure keeps exactly what applied when it was authorised.

Until AUTHORISED, a Procedure's price [follows its Contract](US-05.2.8) unless it is held. The snapshot cannot be written without a price, so a Procedure that still shows "Needs a price" blocks AUTHORISED.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and similar reference data are referenced by identity into the schedule (changed once, reflected everywhere), with one exception in the billing model: at AUTHORISED the engine copies the reference data the calculation depended on (contract terms, rates, RVG values, the anaesthetist's unit value, party details) into the billing record. The exact fields to snapshot are to be determined in discovery.

Since Contracts are edited in place, the [pricing snapshot](AR-58#L181-196) holds a copy of the Contract's settings rather than pointing at a version, with the values on the Procedure, the recorded BTM units, the rate and discount used, the price, and the payer or billable party details.
