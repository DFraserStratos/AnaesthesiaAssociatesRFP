When a List becomes [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine snapshots the version of each Procedure's selected Contract onto that Procedure, locking it in, together with the price, rate and discount on the Procedure.

This means later edits to the Contract can never change an invoice that has already been raised from it. Each Procedure keeps exactly what applied at the moment it was authorised.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: hospitals, surgeons, statuses, rates and similar reference data are referenced by identity into the schedule (changed once, reflected everywhere), with one exception in the billing model: at AUTHORISED the engine copies the reference data the calculation depended on (contract terms, rates, RVG values, the anaesthetist's unit value, party details) into the billing record. An issued invoice therefore stays tied to what was true when it was raised, and later reference-data changes cannot rewrite it. The exact fields to snapshot are to be determined in discovery.

The technical design v6 ([pricing snapshot](AR-29#pricing-snapshot)) has the snapshot hold the Contract version (each version is its own record), the values on the Procedure, the recorded BTM units, the rate and discount used, the price, and the payer or billable party details. Later changes to the RVG groups or Contracts never alter issued billing.
