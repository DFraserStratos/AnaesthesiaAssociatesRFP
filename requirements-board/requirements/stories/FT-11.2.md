Each Procedure's Contract decides who is billed:

- If the Contract is set to [bill its holder](US-04.2.1), the invoice goes to the holder [Party](US-04.1.5), such as an insurer or a hospital.
- Otherwise it goes to the payer named on the Booking: [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3), a surgeon's fixed fee, [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), and every prepaid Contract (OQ-73).

The [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is filled in from the patient automatically, and the office or the anaesthetist can change it to someone else who pays, such as a parent, a guardian, a benefactor or a family trust.

This is so the Contract alone decides who is billed, while the name and contact details of whoever pays in the patient's place are recorded on the Booking.

An [event](FT-03.7) on a Procedure can have its own billable party, by default the Procedure's ([event billable party](US-03.7.1)).

## Technical discussion

[AA acts as agent](US-08.4.5) for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice of Contract and relies on [office review](US-07.2.2) to catch mistakes ([ERD](AR-61#who-is-billed)).
