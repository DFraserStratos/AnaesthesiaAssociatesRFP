Each Procedure's Contract decides who is billed:

- If the Contract's [holder](US-04.2.1) pays AA itself, such as an insurer that accepts direct claims or a hospital, the invoice goes to the holder's billable party.
- Otherwise the invoice goes to the payer named on the Booking. This covers a holder that only sets the price (a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), and [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3).

The [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is filled in from the patient automatically, and the office or the anaesthetist can change it to a parent or guardian.

This is so the Contract alone decides who is billed, while the name and email of whoever pays in the patient's place are recorded on the Booking.

An [event](FT-03.7) on a Procedure can have its own billable party, by default the Procedure's ([event billable party](US-03.7.1)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, when it is invoiced, then the invoice goes to the holder's billable party.
- Given a Procedure under No contract (RVG), an anaesthetist's own Contract or a Contract whose holder only sets the price, when it is invoiced, then the invoice goes to the payer on the Booking.
- Given a new Booking, then its payer is the patient until the office or the anaesthetist changes it.

## Technical discussion

In the technical design v6 the contract holder says whether it is the billable party and links to its billable party record; otherwise the patient, or another nominated payer captured on the booking, is billed. AA acts as agent for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice of Contract and relies on office review to catch mistakes ([billable party](AR-29#billable-party), [ERD](AR-30#contract-holder)).

## Notes

This replaces the earlier reading that picking a default Contract asks for the payer's name and email, and that there is no separate payer on the Booking (OQ-67, OQ-54). The executive summary keeps a default Contract, [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3), which bills the payer on the Booking, rather than Greg's floated "no default contract" model (OQ-78).

The executive summary also has the Booking showing an insurance indication (for example SXAP) that guides which Contract is chosen; the Contract still decides who is billed. The insurance noted on the Booking guides the office; it never decides who is billed (OQ-93, partly answered, still open).
