Each Procedure's Contract decides who is billed:

- If the Contract's [holder](US-04.2.1) pays AA itself, such as an insurer that accepts direct claims or a hospital that has agreed to pay, the invoice goes to the holder's billable party.
- Otherwise the invoice goes to the payer named on the Booking. This covers a holder that only sets the price (a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement), [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14), and [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3).

The [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is filled in from the patient automatically, and the office or the anaesthetist can change it to a parent or guardian.

This is so the Contract alone decides who is billed, while the name and email of whoever pays in the patient's place are recorded on the Booking.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, when it is invoiced, then the invoice goes to the holder's billable party.
- Given a Procedure under No contract (RVG), an anaesthetist's own Contract or a Contract whose holder only sets the price, when it is invoiced, then the invoice goes to the payer on the Booking.
- Given a new Booking, then its payer is the patient until the office or the anaesthetist changes it.

## Technical discussion

In the draft design ([AR-29 p8](AR-29#p8), ERD AR-30) the holder carries whether it is billed and, if so, a reference to its billable party record; otherwise the payer on the booking is billed. Greg wants every billable party (hospitals, surgeons, patients and guardians) held in one reference table that a Contract points to, with no invoice email on the Contract itself. The design is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.

## Notes

This replaces the earlier reading that picking a default Contract asks for the payer's name and email, and that there is no separate payer on the Booking (OQ-67, OQ-54). The guide keeps a default Contract, No contract (RVG), which bills the payer on the Booking, rather than Greg's floated "no default contract" model (OQ-78).

The guide also has the Booking showing an insurance indication (for example SXAP) that guides which Contract is chosen; the Contract still decides who is billed. Whether and where that indication is stored is OQ-93.
