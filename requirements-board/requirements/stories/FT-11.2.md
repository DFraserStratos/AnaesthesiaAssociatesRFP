Each Procedure's Contract decides who is billed for it, its billable party: either the Contract's holder, such as an insurer or a hospital, or the Procedure's payer, who is the patient or someone who pays in their place, such as a parent, a guardian or a family trust.

This is so the Contract alone decides who is billed, while the name and contact details of whoever pays in the patient's place are recorded where the fee is charged: on the Procedure.

## Technical discussion

[AA acts as agent](US-08.4.5) for independent anaesthetists, so the system accepts the anaesthetist's choice of Contract and relies on [office review](US-07.2.2) to catch mistakes ([ERD](AR-61#who-is-billed)).
