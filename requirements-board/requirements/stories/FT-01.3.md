A Slot with no List attached has no surgeon and no hospital. A List sits in one Slot and, once assigned, belongs to exactly one anaesthetist, one surgeon, one hospital, one day and one session: with all five it is an [active List](FT-07.1). A [Draft List](FT-01.6) has its surgeon, hospital, day and session, but no anaesthetist yet.
