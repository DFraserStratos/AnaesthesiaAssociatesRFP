The office gives every Contract one of five pricing kinds when it creates it:

- **RVG at the anaesthetist's unit rate**: BTM units x [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-12.1.1)
- **RVG at the Contract's own rate** ([Contract rate](US-05.2.6))
- **RVG less the Contract's discount**, shown as its own invoice line ([Contract discount](US-05.4.3))
- **fixed price**, through [price ranges](US-04.2.15)
- **open price**, typed on the Procedure, used only by the system's [open-price fallbacks](US-04.3.9)

This is so every Procedure that selects a given Contract is billed the same way.

Rate and discount are undated: a change is an audited edit. Prices are held ex GST. A Contract states no base or modifier units of its own: base units live on the RVG group (OQ-62).

AA sets on each Contract priced from RVG units whether the anaesthetist may override its price, with the RVG detail kept ([anaesthetist adjustment](US-03.5.1)). The setting covers the price only, not a rate or discount, and a fixed-price or prepaid Contract never carries it (OQ-117, OQ-96). Without it the anaesthetist cannot change the price: they [change the Contract](US-03.4.1), which may change who is billed, or ask the office. The office can change any price ([office price change](US-05.4.2)), and the last change stands.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Fixed price.** Given a fixed-price Contract, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is that of the range in force on the procedure date, no times or modifiers are asked for, and any RVG code recorded is kept for reference only ([RVG detail](US-03.3.9)).
- **Fixed rate.** Given a Contract priced at its own rate, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is BTM units x the Contract's rate instead of the anaesthetist's own.
- **Fixed discount.** Given a Contract priced at RVG less a discount, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is BTM units x the anaesthetist's own unit value less the discount, shown where the price is changed.
- **Default.** Given a Contract priced at the anaesthetist's unit rate, or No contract · RVG, then the price is BTM units x the anaesthetist's own unit value.
- **Open price.** Given an open-price fallback, the Procedure has no price until one is typed ([Needs a price](US-05.2.8)).
- **Editable setting.** Given an RVG-priced Contract whose setting lets the anaesthetist override its price, they can change the price, with no reason needed, and the RVG detail is kept. Given one that does not, the price is read-only and they cannot apply a discount of their own.
- **Fixed price never overridden.** Given a fixed-price or prepaid Contract, then it cannot carry the override setting, and the anaesthetist cannot change its price.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: insurer terms tend to be schedule-based (a maximum payable per procedure) rather than a discount applied to a counterparty, so the question is the shape of the rate lookup. Stratos leans toward extending how the Contract model resolves rates rather than building a parallel structure alongside it. AA to advise how likely direct insurer billing is to grow (see OQ-20).

The kinds and their fields are set out in [the proposal's pricing kinds](AR-58#L114-122) (AR-58 section 2.3).

## Notes

Our readings (contract model proposal): open price is used only by the system's fallbacks (A3); the kind is set when the Contract is created (A36); the override setting covers the price only, not a rate or discount (A12); a Contract has no base or modifier units of its own (A22, following Donald's D5, re-answering OQ-62 on 5 November).

The five pricing kinds, open price among them, are Donald's D5, not yet confirmed by AA or Greg. Open price answers Ben's prepaid example of a price not on any list, where "Vanessa just put in 2 grand".

Merges the former US-04.2.3 (Base unit override) and US-04.2.6 (Adjustment and override permissions), both now Retired. US-04.2.6's office override permission is not carried over: the office can always change the price, whatever the Contract says (OQ-16).

Greg floated, as an idea only, making Contracts children of a master code so that a rare change to an RVG unit value routes to every Contract under it (Donald: "it could have multiple parents"). Not adopted.

The billing flow draft's note also lists billable party, invoice email and pre-paid amount as defined by the Contract. Who is billed is set on the Contract ([who is billed](US-04.2.1)); the payer and their email are on the Booking; the prepaid amount comes from a prepaid Contract, such as an item on an anaesthetist's [own price list](US-04.2.14).

The override is a per-Contract setting, not a global rule (OQ-107, answered 19 October). Greg found the definition of fixed "a little rigid, but we can worry about that another day". A prepaid Contract is never editable (Greg, 28 October; OQ-96). OQ-117 answered (Donald, 29 October): "If something is a fixed fee, it doesn't allow a price override." The office's price change is not affected.

A Contract no longer names references its holder needs the Booking to collect, such as an insurer member number or a claim reference: until OQ-136 is answered, the Booking keeps only the [insurer approval number](US-11.4.3) (our reading, AR-58 A21). The holder's own code for the item is the Contract's [holder reference code](US-04.2.18).
