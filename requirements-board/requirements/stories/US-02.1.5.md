Rows from St George's and Southern Cross arrive in the [matching screen's feed](US-02.1.4) by themselves, as each hospital sends them. There is no import button and no sync button.

This is so the office never pulls from these two hospitals by hand, and keeps the checking step it relies on (who is on the List, double bookings, changed times, patients appearing and disappearing).

The integration only brings data in and never writes back to the hospital. Nothing is applied to a List or Booking until the office decides.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Arrives by itself.** Given St George's sends its theatre list, then its rows appear in the matching screen's feed without anyone importing or syncing them.
- **No silent apply.** Given rows have arrived, then every List they belong to still waits for the office's [decision](US-02.1.2), and nothing is applied until the office decides.
- **Inbound only.** Given the office applies a change from a hospital, then nothing is sent back to that hospital's system.

## Technical discussion

How each hospital delivers its data is to be confirmed (OQ-13). The cadence is one theatre list a day for the next day's bookings, with no repeated updates (Vanessa, via Greg, 16 October). Vanessa holds a few old emails about the current integrations. This story covers St George's and Southern Cross; the other providers are Future Work ([feeds from the other Christchurch providers](US-14.6.1)).
