At the end of each month AA raises one fee invoice to each anaesthetist, which the anaesthetist pays. The fee is made up of fixed charges plus a charge per [BCTI (ACCPAY invoice)](US-09.1.4) issued to that anaesthetist in the month, for example $500 + $5 x 40.

This invoice is distinct from any procedure's receivable and payable: it is AA charging the anaesthetist, the one transaction where money flows that way.

## Notes

What the per-invoice fee counts, and how the fee is paid, is OQ-60, still open for AA's accountant. Greg's view, to be confirmed with the accountant: the fee never nets against the anaesthetist's payables; under trust law it is always a separate invoice, paid into a separate bank account. The per-invoice charge counts only invoices that were paid, so a Booking moved between anaesthetists is not charged twice.

The schedule of fixed charges is still not known; Greg does not know it.
