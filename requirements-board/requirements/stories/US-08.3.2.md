The Billing/Invoice Engine's internal ledger, not Xero, is the source of truth for the [balances shown to anaesthetists](US-12.2.1) and admins.

This is because Xero sits behind the ledger as an accounts receivable and banking service only; it is not AA's system of record for what is owed.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response: the billing engine is the system of record for what is owed. [A dedicated Xero instance](US-09.4.1) sits behind it as an accounts receivable and banking add-on only, not the system of record and not AA's general ledger.
