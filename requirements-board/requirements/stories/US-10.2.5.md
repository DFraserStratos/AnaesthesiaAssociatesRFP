A negative invoice to an anaesthetist, from a refund or a [credit after invoicing](US-08.6.2), is netted against their positive payables in the [next payment run](US-10.2.7), for example 19 positive invoices and one negative.

The anaesthetist sees the run washed up in their remittance advice. Netting this way means the anaesthetist bears the refund.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given 19 positive payables and one negative invoice in a payment run, when the run is paid, then the payment to the anaesthetist is the net total.
- Given a negative invoice in a payment run, when the remittance advice is produced, then it shows the negative invoice netted against the positive ones.

## Notes

When there is no later positive payment to net against, for example a refund after the month's last payment run with no later work, AA settles it with the anaesthetist outside the system (OQ-71). Nothing is built for this case. [A cancelled prepayment](US-06.5.2) is not this case: that money is still in trust and the anaesthetist has not been paid.
