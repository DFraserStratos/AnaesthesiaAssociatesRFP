Everything on the mobile app need not also be on the web app, nor the other way round. Parity is a nice to have, not a requirement: each app is used for what it does best.

- The web app suits work that is painful on a phone, for example downloading PDFs, monthly reconciliations, the [GST report](US-12.2.2) and anything to email or print.

## Notes

Greg, 16 October: parity "sort of undermines the argument about whether you need a web app", and some people are natural phone users and some prefer the web: "You'll need to run that past them directly." Donald left it at Verify.
