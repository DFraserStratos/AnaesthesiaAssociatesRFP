A List whose only line is a [stub](US-02.6.1) never reads as complete. It can be neither [submitted as completed](US-07.1.1) nor [authorised](US-07.3.1) until the office resolves or rejects the stub, and the reason is shown to whoever tries.

This is so a List is never closed off holding nothing but a booking that could not be posted.

A List that also holds Bookings is never held up by its stub: it can be submitted and authorised as usual.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Only a stub.** Given a List whose only line is a stub, then it does not read as complete, and it can be neither submitted as completed nor authorised; the reason is shown.
- **Stub resolved or rejected.** Given the office resolves or rejects that stub, then the List can be submitted and authorised as usual.
- **Not held up.** Given a List with two Bookings and a stub, then the stub does not stop it being submitted or authorised.
