Admins keep AA's list of funding tags, such as SXAP, HNZ and ACC, in the order the [step rail](US-04.3.8) shows them. A Contract may carry [one funding tag](US-04.2.17). Every change is audited.

This is so a hospital's work is grouped by who funds it, with one list AA controls.

A tag may name the insurer or funder whose work arrives under it (Southern Cross Health Society for SXAP, ACC for ACC, Health NZ for HNZ), which lets the Booking's funder [suggest a path](US-04.3.10) when the office picks a Contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Order.** Given SXAP is listed before HNZ, then step 2 of the step rail shows a hospital's SXAP before its HNZ.
- **Funder named.** Given SXAP names Southern Cross Health Society, then a Booking at a hospital with SXAP Contracts, funded by Southern Cross Health Society, has that hospital's SXAP path marked Suggested.

## Notes

Donald's D4, 5 November: a Contract's settings include a "funding tag (optional, from an AA list: SXAP, HNZ, ACC, ...)". ACC and SXAP become funding tags (see [ACC through the holder's Contract](US-05.5.1) and the [office review of Contracts](US-07.2.2)).

Our reading (contract model proposal, A25): a tag names its funder, and the Booking's funder suggests the tag.
