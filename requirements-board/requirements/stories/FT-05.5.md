ACC-funded work is billed through a [holder](US-04.2.1)'s Contract carrying the ACC [funding tag](US-04.2.17), and priced like any other Procedure on that Contract: by the [standard base, time and modifier calculation](FT-05.2) where the Contract is priced from RVG units, which not every ACC Contract is. There is no separate ACC calculation path.

This is so ACC needs no pricing rules of its own.
