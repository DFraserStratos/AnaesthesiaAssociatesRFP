When a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder, the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) is billed, and that is the patient unless the office or the anaesthetist has changed it. This applies to:

- [No contract (RVG)](US-04.3.3)
- [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14)
- a surgeon's fixed-price arrangement, where the surgeon only sets the price and the patient pays
- insured patients who pay AA and claim the money back from their insurer themselves, priced under No contract (RVG)

This is the common case, since most patients pay for their own procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure under No contract (RVG) and an unchanged payer, when it is invoiced, then the patient is billed.
- Given a Procedure under a surgeon's fixed-price Contract, when it is invoiced, then the payer on the Booking is billed at the Contract's price.
- Given an insured patient who will claim the money back themselves, when No contract (RVG) is chosen, then the patient is billed, not the insurer.
