When a Procedure's Contract does not bill its holder, [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2) is billed, and that is the patient unless the office or the anaesthetist has changed it. This applies to:

- [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3)
- [an anaesthetist's own Contract](US-04.2.14)
- [a surgeon's fixed-price Contract](US-04.2.1), which bills the payer
- [No contract · Fixed price](US-04.3.9), and every prepaid Contract
- insured patients who pay AA and claim the money back from their insurer themselves, priced under No contract · RVG

This is so a Procedure that no holder pays for is still billed to someone: the patient, or whoever pays in their place. It is the common case.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure under No contract · RVG and an unchanged payer, when it is invoiced, then the patient is billed.
- Given a Procedure under a surgeon's fixed-price Contract, when it is invoiced, then the Procedure's payer is billed at the Contract's price.
- Given an insured patient who will claim the money back themselves, when No contract · RVG is chosen, then the patient is billed, not the insurer.
