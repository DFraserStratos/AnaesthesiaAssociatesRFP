When a [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1) is sent, it goes with a prepayment letter filled in from a standard template, instead of a letter written by hand. There is at least one such template; it need not be many. Admins maintain it.

The template states the prepaid amount (a price from a prepaid Contract, or one the office typed) as the price payable before the procedure. It may say that if the procedure turns out very different from what was planned, the anaesthetist may raise a further invoice or a credit. The letter is filled in from the Booking (patient, procedure, prepaid amount, anaesthetist) and goes out with the prepayment invoice.

This is because the office writes every prepayment letter by hand today.
