When a Booking's total modifier units add up to 4 or fewer, they all sit on the primary Procedure.

When the total is more than 4, the system divides the modifier units equally across every Procedure in the Booking, and allocates any remainder to the primary.

## Notes

Worked example in domain-model.md: 7 modifier units over 3 procedures = 3 / 2 / 2.

Retired: merged into US-05.3.1 (Multi-procedure BTM rule).
