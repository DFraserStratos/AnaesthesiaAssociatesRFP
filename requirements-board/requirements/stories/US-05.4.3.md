Some Contracts carry a fixed discount, for example a hospital at standard RVG pricing less 20%, or a DHB Contract at a discount. The system prices the Procedure as usual, BTM units x [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1), less the Contract's discount, shown as its own invoice line.

- Where the Contract does not let the anaesthetist change its pricing, they see the discount applied and cannot edit or remove it.
- The office can [change the price](US-05.4.2) at any time.

This is so a discount agreed with a Contract holder is applied every time, without the anaesthetist having to remember it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Discount applied.** Given a Contract with a fixed discount of 10% and a calculated price of $1,000, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is $900.
- **Shown and fixed.** Given that Procedure on a Contract that does not let the anaesthetist change its pricing, when they open its price, then the 10% discount shows as applied and cannot be edited or removed.

## Technical discussion

The technical design v6 holds the discount as a live percentage on the Contract line ([contract line fields](AR-29#contract-line-fields)).
