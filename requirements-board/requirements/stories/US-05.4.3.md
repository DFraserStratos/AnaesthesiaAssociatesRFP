Some Contracts carry a fixed discount, for example a hospital at standard RVG pricing less 20%, or a DHB Contract at a discount. The Billing/Invoice Engine prices the Procedure as usual, BTM units x [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1), less the Contract's discount, shown as its own invoice line.

This is so a discount agreed with a holder is applied every time, without the anaesthetist having to remember it.

The discount is undated: a change is an audited edit. The anaesthetist sees it applied, cannot edit or remove it, and has no discount of their own. The office can [change the price](US-05.4.2) at any time.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Discount applied.** Given a Contract with a fixed discount of 10% and a calculated price of $1,000, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is $900.
- **Shown and fixed.** Given that Procedure, when the anaesthetist opens its price, then the 10% discount shows as applied and cannot be edited or removed.

## Technical discussion

The discount is a setting of the Contract (AR-58 section 2.3).

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal A12): the anaesthetist cannot add a discount of their own, since there are no per-Procedure rate or discount edits; a different price is a [price change](US-05.4.2) instead. The discount is undated (D5).

And it might be a discount of 10%." On 7 October the custom unit rate and the pre-applied discount became separate settings in [Contract pricing](US-04.2.2).
