The office makes an anaesthetist inactive from [their page](US-12.1.4), choosing their last day and giving a reason, when they leave the practice or stop taking Lists for a long time. Until the last day nothing changes. After it the anaesthetist is inactive:

- they have no [Slots](US-01.1.1)
- they are not offered or picked for any List, in assignment or in the availability finder
- they can no longer [sign in](US-13.5.3) to their app
- the Contracts held by their own Party and linked to them are [disabled](US-04.1.2)

Everything already recorded about them, their Lists, Bookings, invoices and payments included, stays readable from their page.

This is so the schedule stops offering someone who no longer works for AA, and their own Contracts stop being offered on Lists, without losing any of their history.

- Making them inactive is refused while they hold a List after the last day. The office is shown those Lists, to reassign them first.
- The office can clear the last day at any time before it comes, and nothing has changed.
- After the last day, the office can make the anaesthetist active again. They have Slots again from that day, and can sign in again. The office is offered their disabled Contracts, each ticked and showing its price, and the ticked ones are made active again.
- Making inactive, clearing the last day and making active again are each an audited change.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Last day chosen.** Given Dr Sarah Beaumont with no List after 31 January, when the office makes her inactive with 31 January as her last day and a reason, then until 31 January she keeps her Slots, is offered for Lists and can sign in.
- **Refused while holding Lists.** Given Dr Beaumont holds a List on 3 February, when the office chooses 31 January as her last day, then it is refused and the office is shown the 3 February List to reassign.
- **After the last day.** Given her last day was 31 January, then from 1 February she has no Slots, is not offered in assignment or the [availability finder](US-01.4.2), and cannot sign in.
- **Her Contracts disabled.** Given Dr Beaumont's Rhinoplasty Contract at $1,350.00, prepaid, held by her own Party and linked to her, then after her last day it is [Disabled](US-04.1.2) and no longer offered on any List.
- **History readable.** Given Dr Beaumont is inactive, when the office opens her page, then her past Lists, Bookings, invoices and payments are all there.
- **Cleared in time.** Given a last day of 31 January, when the office clears it on 20 January, then nothing about her changes.
- **Active again.** Given Dr Beaumont inactive since 1 February, when the office makes her active again on 1 March, then she has Slots from 1 March and can sign in, and the office is offered Rhinoplasty, $1,350.00, ticked; once confirmed, the Contract is Active again.

## Technical discussion

How an anaesthetist who has left sees remittances and invoices for work done before their last day, now that they can no longer sign in, is OQ-147.

## Notes

Donald, 25 November (for the build; not yet discussed with AA): an anaesthetist is made inactive "from a chosen date", with no sign-in after it, and their own Contracts are disabled "with her"; making them active again restores them ("Yes, restore").

"Inactive" is the word for an anaesthetist who has left or stopped working; "Retire" stays the word for surgeons, hospitals and modifiers.
