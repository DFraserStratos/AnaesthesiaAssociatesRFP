The anaesthetist sees the patient's NHI on each Booking in their app. The NHI is mandatory: a Booking's patient always has one.

This is so the anaesthetist can look the patient up in Health Connect. The NHI stays inside the clinical and ledger systems and is never sent to Xero, in any field ([privacy and data minimisation](US-15.0.6), [contact identification without NHI](US-09.3.1)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with an NHI, when the anaesthetist opens it, then the NHI is shown.

## Notes

Donald and Greg, 16 October: the NHI is mandatory, "so there can be no support of a blank"; Donald asked for the catalogue to stop treating the NHI as blankable. AA confirmed again on 19 October: "It's mandatory now, so that's that" (OQ-49).
