The anaesthetist sees the patient's NHI on each Booking in their app. Where the [NHI is still missing](US-11.1.5), the Booking shows that clearly instead of a blank.

This is so the anaesthetist can look the patient up in Health Connect. The NHI stays inside the clinical and ledger systems, as [privacy and data minimisation](US-15.0.6) requires.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with an NHI, when the anaesthetist opens it, then the NHI is shown.
- Given a Booking with no NHI, when the anaesthetist opens it, then it shows 'NHI missing'.
