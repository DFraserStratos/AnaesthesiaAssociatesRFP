Anaesthetists can copy a Booking's skeleton, its patient, List and references, without copying its procedure details.

This is so an anaesthetist can quickly add another Booking, capturing only the new procedure.

## Notes

Retired 2026-10-02 and to be removed from the prototype. Greg: "I think it's redundant because it was probably an early response to the fact that they couldn't add a procedure to a booking." Copying a whole Booking was a workaround (Vanessa's "three-numbers, six-numbers" Bookings, each holding a single procedure); another procedure for the same patient is added to the Booking itself.
