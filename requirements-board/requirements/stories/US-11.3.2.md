When a Booking is created or matched to a patient who has a balance with AA, the system [alerts AA staff](US-13.7.1), and the anaesthetist sees the alert on the Booking in their List (the mild alert as a colour code).

- For an amount owing, the alert is mild while the unpaid invoice is younger than the alert threshold, and strong once it is older.
- The threshold's days count from the invoice date. It applies only to amounts owing and starts at 90 days. Until AA asks for a [settings page](US-13.7.4), the threshold is fixed.
- A credit (+) balance raises a mild alert.
- Mild versus strong is a yellow versus a red warning.
- Staff can always wave the alert through: it never blocks the Booking.

This is so AA can follow up on the outstanding balance before the next procedure goes ahead.

Patients are allowed 90 days to pay and AA raises around 100 invoices a day, so a younger unpaid invoice is not yet a problem. Some patients come back with invoices six months to two years old.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient with an invoice unpaid longer than the threshold (start 90 days), when a Booking is created or matched to them, then AA staff get a strong alert.
- Given a patient whose only unpaid invoice is 30 days old, when a Booking is created or matched, then AA staff get a mild alert.
- Given a patient with a credit balance, when a Booking is created or matched, then AA staff get a mild alert.
- Given an invoice dated 100 days ago and unpaid, when a Booking is created or matched to the patient, then AA staff get a strong alert.
- Given either alert, when staff go ahead with the Booking, then it is saved.
- Given a patient with an unpaid invoice, when the anaesthetist opens their List, then the Booking shows the alert.
