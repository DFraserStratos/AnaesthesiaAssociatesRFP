Admins can change a Procedure's price at any time, on any Contract, including a [fixed price](US-05.2.5). No reason is needed, the change is [audited](US-13.5.2), and the last change stands.

This is so the office can put a price right before the invoice goes out, including a fixed price the anaesthetist cannot override (OQ-117).

The office's change is not the anaesthetist's price override: it ignores the Contract's [override setting](US-04.2.2), which exists only on RVG Contracts. The office normally touches a Booking last, at [review](FT-07.2), but has no special authority over the anaesthetist.

Once a prepaid Procedure's prepayment invoice is raised, its price is read-only for the office too; a difference is a credit or an additional invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Any Contract.** Given a Procedure on a fixed price, or on a Contract whose override setting does not let the anaesthetist change its pricing, then the office can change its price, with no reason needed.
- **Audited.** Given the office changes a price, then the change is audited and the last change stands.
- **Prepaid after invoice.** Given a prepaid Procedure whose prepayment invoice is raised, then the office cannot change its price.

## Notes

Our readings (contract model proposal): once a prepaid Procedure's invoice is raised its price is read-only for the office too (A16), which narrows what Greg agreed on 28 October; the office changes the price only, since rate and discount are Contract settings (A12); a typed price on a Contract other than an open price is flagged at review beside the computed price (A49).

OQ-16: the office can always change a price. On 14 October Greg suggested "locked" should read "fixed", and "price" means price, rate or discount ("price with a small p"). On 16 October Donald: the documents were "mostly documenting that, sequence-wise, probably the office was the last person to touch it, but they don't have any special authority"; Greg: "Yeah, that's changed that."

Whether the anaesthetist can change a Contract's price is a flag on each Contract, set by AA (OQ-107, answered 19 October); on a Contract that does not allow it, the anaesthetist asks the office.

Donald and Greg, 23 October, agreed the office can change any price, a fixed price included ("even if it's a fixed price... because they need to be able to"). Greg: "Lock that down if it gets too loose later. In the first instance, that'll be the only way they can fix problems before the invoice goes out."

Greg, 28 October, agreed: only AA admin staff can change any price on any Contract regardless of the Contract's rules, prepaid Contracts included; any difference on a prepaid Procedure is a second invoice or credit raised by hand (OQ-96). He questioned "the only way to change a fixed price" while a fixed-price Contract might allow an override ("it's not universally true"). OQ-117 answered (Donald, 29 October): "If something is a fixed fee, it doesn't allow a price override", so the statement holds.
