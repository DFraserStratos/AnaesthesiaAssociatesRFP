Admins can change a Procedure's price, rate or discount at any time, on any Contract, including a [fixed price](US-05.2.5): it is the only way to change a fixed price without changing the Contract. It is a change to the values on the Procedure like any other: no reason is needed, it is audited and the last change stands. The office normally touches a Booking last, at [review](FT-07.2), but it has no special authority over the anaesthetist.

## Notes

OQ-16: the office can always change a price. On 14 October Greg suggested "locked" should read "fixed", and "price" means price, rate or discount ("price with a small p"). On 16 October Donald: the documents were "mostly documenting that, sequence-wise, probably the office was the last person to touch it, but they don't have any special authority"; Greg: "Yeah, that's changed that."

Whether the anaesthetist can also depart from a fixed price at their own discretion is OQ-107.
