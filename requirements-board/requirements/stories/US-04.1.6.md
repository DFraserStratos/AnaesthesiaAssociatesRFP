The office maintains one Contract at a time on a screen with its header (name, dates and settings) and its lines below. Edits on screen, and [uploads](US-04.2.13), are not applied straight away: they are staged and shown on a review dashboard for that Contract. The office reviews them, sets the date the change takes effect, and submits. On submit the system creates the [new dated version](US-04.2.10) and records what changed. A one-line fix follows the same path.

The dashboard shows:

- header changes, before and after
- counts of lines added, altered, removed and unchanged
- each altered line's changed values, highlighting any that become blank
- errors that block submit, for example duplicate codes, a missing or past effective date, or a second future-dated version
- warnings that do not block it, such as Bookings affected by altered or removed lines

Submit applies everything or nothing.

This is so every Contract change is checked before it can affect a price, and the history of each version is clear.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an edit on screen, then it is not applied until submitted.
- Given a staged change with no effective date, or one in the past, then submit is blocked.
- Given a staged change affecting un-invoiced Bookings, then the dashboard warns but submit is allowed.
- Given a submit, then every staged change is applied or none is.
