The Billing/Invoice Engine tracks dollars in (receipts from billable parties) and dollars out ([disbursements to anaesthetists](US-10.1.2)).

This is so AA can see the [ledger is in balance overall](US-13.2.1), and drill into the position for any one anaesthetist or patient.
