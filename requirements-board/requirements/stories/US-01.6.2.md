Admins see every Draft List in the Admin App, each clearly shown as a List with no anaesthetist yet, in two places:

- the Draft Lists tab of [the Lists section](US-13.1.3), which holds every Draft List across all dates, soonest first, under a group row for each day
- a band above the grid on the [one-day dashboard](US-13.1.1), holding the Draft Lists for the day in view

Each shows its day, session, hospital and surgeon, and how long it has been waiting for an anaesthetist.

This is so an unassigned List cannot be mistaken for an active one, and so nothing waits unnoticed for someone to be found.

- **Search and filters.** The Draft Lists tab has a search, and filters by date, by hospital and by why the List has no anaesthetist (for example a surgeon's request, a recurring booking on an unavailable day, or a List returned to the office).
- **Paging.** The tab pages through its Lists, so a long run of Draft Lists stays quick to read.

## Acceptance criteria

- **One visible place.** The Draft Lists tab lists all Draft Lists that still need an anaesthetist, on every date.
- **Flagged.** Every Draft List is labelled as unassigned wherever it appears, and looks different from an active List.
- **On the day view.** A Draft List also appears in the band on the one-day dashboard for its day, and not on other days.
- **Sorting.** On the Draft Lists tab, Draft Lists are sorted soonest first, under a group row for each day.
- **Filtered.** Given Draft Lists at two hospitals, when the admin filters by one hospital, then only that hospital's Draft Lists show.
