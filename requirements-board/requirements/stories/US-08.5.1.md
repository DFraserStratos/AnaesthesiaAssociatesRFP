For each Booking on a List, the Billing/Invoice Engine records whether processing succeeded and, if it did not, why. The List shows a roll-up of its Bookings: billing fails per Booking, never for the List as a whole ([per-Booking failure](US-08.5.2)).

This gives the office something concrete to act on when a failure needs resolving, rather than an invoice simply failing to appear.
