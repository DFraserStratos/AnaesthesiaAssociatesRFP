The system models exactly one payment path for every procedure: the billable party pays AA, and AA disburses the money on to the anaesthetist.

This is so the system never has to model two different payment destinations for a procedure; there is only ever the one route.

## Notes

10 November, with AA's accountant: every receipt goes through the one trust account. Today some money is paid straight into another AA bank account; Michael: "it'll all have to go through the one account"; Greg: "It has to go through the one account if you want to be able to reconcile it." Ben: AA did this for a while, but the banking overwhelmed the office, so they went back "until we get the software"; "it's been socialised", and the anaesthetists who complained about "a couple of days delay" got over it.
