On a Booking, the office sees the total price of all its Procedures together, and on each Procedure its own price and any [events](FT-03.7) recorded on it, such as pre-op or post-op work, with the means to [change any price](US-05.4.2) from there.

This is so the office can see at a glance what a Booking comes to, and correct it in one place.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Total.** Given a Booking with two Procedures priced $1,200 and $400, then the Booking page shows a total of $1,600.
- **Events shown.** Given a Procedure with a post-op event, then the event and its amount show within that Procedure.

## Technical discussion

Donald's expectation, not a design ("I don't know exactly what they look like"). Open: whether events billed on an invoice of their own count in the Booking's total, and whether the total shows including GST.

Vanessa, 5 November: "if we were to amend an invoice, will that obviously automatically change for the anaesthetist to see? I don't know exactly what they look like, but I know that's something you need."

What "automatically add notes" means for a hospital was not explored.
