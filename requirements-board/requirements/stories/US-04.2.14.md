The office sets up each anaesthetist's own price list from the list the anaesthetist supplies (for example a spreadsheet of 25 procedures). Each item is a fixed-price Contract of its own (for example Dr B. Smith, Face lift, $3,200), held by the anaesthetist's [Party](US-04.1.5) and linked to them. A new item is a new Contract; a price change is a new [price range](US-04.2.15).

This is so each anaesthetist can charge their own prices, since AA cannot set a common price for its members. It is also where a [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2) comes from.

These Contracts:

- are offered only on that anaesthetist's own Lists
- bill [the Procedure's payer](US-11.2.2)
- never let the anaesthetist override the price, prepay list or not (OQ-117, OQ-96); to depart from one, the anaesthetist changes the Contract or asks the office, which can change any price ([office price change](US-05.4.2))
- when on the anaesthetist's prepay list, are prepaid: they appear only under Patient › Payment type: Prepaid on the step rail, start the [prepayment](FT-06.2), and every price range is above zero
- may carry a [default RVG code](US-04.2.18) for reference

The anaesthetist decides the prices and sends any change to the office, which enters it. Anaesthetists never create or edit Contracts in the app.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Offered and priced.** Given Dr B. Smith's own Face lift Contract at $3,200, when one of Dr Smith's Bookings for a face-lift is set up, then the Contract is offered and prices it at $3,200.
- **Own Lists only.** Given another anaesthetist's Booking, then Dr Smith's own Contracts are not offered.
- **Bills the payer.** Given the Contract is chosen, when the Procedure is billed, then the payer on the Procedure is invoiced.
- **No override.** Given the Contract is chosen, then the anaesthetist cannot change its fixed price; they change the Contract or ask the office, which can.
- **Office only.** Given an anaesthetist in the app, then they cannot create or edit a Contract, their own included.
- **Prepaid price.** Given a prepaid Contract, when the office saves a price range at zero or with no price, then it is refused.
