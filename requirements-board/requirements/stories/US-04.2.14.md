An anaesthetist who wants fixed prices for their own work, for example prepaid cosmetic procedures, has their own Contract: a price list with a line and a fixed price for each procedure they price this way, for example Dr B. Smith, Face lift, $3,200. The anaesthetist tells the office which procedures and prices they want (for example from a spreadsheet of 25 procedures), and the office sets it up.

These Contracts:

- are offered only on that anaesthetist's own Bookings
- are created and kept by the office, from the price list the anaesthetist supplies
- have a holder that is not billed, so they bill [the payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2)
- let the anaesthetist [change the price](US-05.4.1) where AA has set the Contract editable (on a prepaid Procedure, whether they can is OQ-96)
- when one is the anaesthetist's prepay list, are flagged as prepaid, which starts the [prepayment](FT-06.2) when the office picks it

Lines are fixed prices and need no RVG reference, though one can be given as a default (agreed in principle; Ben has not yet been asked).

This is where a [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2) comes from.

Each anaesthetist sets their own prices, because AA cannot set a common price for its members. The anaesthetist decides the prices and supplies their price list, and any change to it, to the office; the office enters it. Anaesthetists do not create or edit Contracts in the app.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given Dr B. Smith's own Contract with a Face lift line at $3,200, when one of Dr Smith's Bookings for a face-lift is set up, then the Contract is offered and prices it at $3,200.
- Given another anaesthetist's Booking, then Dr Smith's own Contract is not offered.
- Given the Contract is chosen, when the Procedure is billed, then the payer named on the Booking is invoiced.
- Given the Contract is chosen and AA has set it editable, then the anaesthetist can change the price, with no reason needed; if it is not editable, they change the Contract or ask the office; on a prepaid Procedure, whether they can is OQ-96.
- Given an anaesthetist in the app, then they cannot create or edit a Contract, their own included.
