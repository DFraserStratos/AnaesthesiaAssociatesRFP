On each Procedure the anaesthetist reads the procedure as received and [the Contract the office set](US-04.3.4), and records:

- the RVG code, where the Contract prices from RVG units
- the [start and finish times](US-03.3.3), from which time units are worked out
- base, time and modifier units, where they apply (not required on a fixed price)
- any modifiers that apply, each with a short explanation; modifiers are optional
- any [events](FT-03.7)

The ASA score is not a separate item: it is one of the optional modifiers.

The anaesthetist does not record the procedure: it comes through from the rooms or the hospital. The received texts are never edited ([kept as received](US-02.5.7)); where it matters, the wording the invoice prints can be adjusted ([invoice description](US-08.4.6)), with history kept.
