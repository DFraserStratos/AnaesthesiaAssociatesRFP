On each Procedure the anaesthetist reads the procedure text and [the Contract the office set](US-04.3.4), and records what that Contract needs to price it: on a Contract priced from RVG units, the RVG code, start and finish times and modifiers, with a short explanation for each modifier claimed; on a fixed or open price, at most an RVG code for reference ([RVG detail follows the pricing kind](US-03.3.9)). They also record any [events](FT-03.7).

This is so each Procedure carries what its invoice needs, and no more.

The anaesthetist does not record the procedure itself: its text comes from the rooms or the hospital ([procedure text](US-02.5.7)), and the wording the invoice prints is its [invoice description](US-08.4.6).
