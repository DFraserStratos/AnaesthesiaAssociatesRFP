Admins set default start and end times for AM and PM Slots. Each Slot can override these defaults individually.

A booking that runs all day simply uses both the AM and PM Slot, rather than needing a separate all-day type.

## Notes

A Slot keeps its standard default shape on screen, even when its List holds only one Booking. Greg: a Slot's real time runs from the first procedure's start to the last one's end, but Donald: "respecting the standard sort of shape is probably best for everyone"; Greg: "I agree. Even if there's just one booking in it."
