The Admin app keeps the office's money work in one Finances section: one side-nav entry with six tabs, each its own page with its own address:

- Billing: [the billing status of each List and Booking](US-13.3.1)
- Invoices: [every invoice, prepayment invoice and credit note](US-08.4.7) AA raises on a payer
- Payments: [the weekly payment cycle](US-10.2.7) and its payment runs, and the [events on Procedures](US-03.7.2) awaiting approval or waiting for the billing run
- Ledger: [the ledger's balance and each anaesthetist's account](US-13.2.1)
- Xero: [the hand-offs to Xero and invoices changed there](US-13.3.2)
- AA fees: [AA's monthly fee invoices to the anaesthetists](US-10.3.1)

Finances opens on the Billing tab. It has no summary page: whether the books are square shows on [the admin dashboard](US-13.2.4).

This is so the office finds all its money work in one place and starts on what needs doing.

- **A tab's number counts what needs doing**, not what the tab lists:
  - Billing: Lists with a billing problem
  - Invoices: prepayment invoices awaiting approval
  - Ledger: ledger checks that failed, a receipt to place among them
  - Xero: hand-offs that raised an alert, and invoices changed directly in Xero
  - Payments and AA fees: no number until something there is due

  The [Lists section](US-13.1.3) counts the same way; the tabs of the Contracts and People sections count what they list.
- **One badge on the Finances entry.** It counts billing problems (on the Billing and Xero tabs) and ledger checks together, and shows as a warning.
- **Records on their own pages.** Each record opens on its own page under its tab, following the record page pattern of the Contract and People pages: an invoice and a credit note under Invoices, a payment run and its remittance advice under Payments, an anaesthetist's account under Ledger.
- **What stays elsewhere.** Lists are [authorised](US-07.3.1) on [the To authorise tab](US-07.2.4) of the Lists section. Credit notes and additional invoices are started from the Procedure or the invoice. A patient's balance and follow-up stay on their page in People.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Opens on Billing.** Given an admin chooses Finances in the side nav, then the Billing tab shows.
- **Own address.** Given an admin is on the Ledger tab, when they reload the page or open its address in a new tab, then the Ledger tab opens.
- **Counts what needs doing.** Given 40 unpaid invoices and 2 prepayment invoices awaiting approval, then the Invoices tab shows 2.
- **No number when nothing is due.** Given nothing in the payment cycle is due, then the Payments tab shows no number.
- **One badge.** Given one billing problem and two failed ledger checks, then the Finances entry shows one badge reading 3, as a warning; once the billing problem is resolved it reads 2.
- **Record page.** Given an invoice on the Invoices tab, when an admin opens it, then it shows on its own page under Invoices, with a way back to the tab.
- **Events approved on Payments.** Given a credit raised on a Procedure and awaiting approval, then it shows on the Payments tab, where the admin approves it for the next billing run.
