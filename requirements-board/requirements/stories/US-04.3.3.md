The system keeps one Contract, No contract · RVG, offered on every List for standard RVG pricing: BTM units x [the anaesthetist's own unit value](US-05.2.1), billed to the [payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) (the patient, or a parent or guardian). On the [step rail](US-04.3.8) it is pinned first under Patient › Payment type: Standard.

This is so a Procedure is never left without pricing rules to fall back on when nobody else has an arrangement.

- It is held by AA and has no price of its own.
- The system finds it by its role, never its name. It is stored as a Contract like any other, so every Booking is priced the same way; users never need to know.
- The anaesthetist can [override its price](US-03.5.1), with the RVG detail kept.
- Picking an RVG code on it is optional for the office; the anaesthetist picks one to set the base units.
- Insured patients who claim the money back themselves are billed under it.

Beside it, every invoice destination has a system-made [open-price fallback](US-04.3.9).

## Acceptance criteria

- **First under Standard.** Given any List, when Patient › Standard is chosen, then No contract · RVG is first and is never filtered out by a search.
- **Price.** Given No contract · RVG, when the Procedure is priced, then the price is BTM units x the anaesthetist's own unit value.
- **Billing.** Given No contract · RVG, when the Procedure is billed, then the payer named on the Booking is invoiced.
- **Only one.** There is exactly one No contract · RVG, held by AA; it has no price of its own.
