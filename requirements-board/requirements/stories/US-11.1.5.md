An incoming booking with no NHI waits on the [unmatched queue](US-02.1.4) until the office adds the NHI (see [patient without NHI](US-11.1.4)). This story is the office's view of those waiting rows: it shows the patient as received, the List or date, and the surgeon's rooms to chase, and marks the ones whose procedure date is close. No Booking and no patient record exists for such a row, so a missing NHI can never produce a duplicate patient.

The office emails the surgeon's rooms to get the NHI. Some rooms' PDFs leave it out, so it may only arrive with the daily hospital or theatre list. When it arrives, the office adds it, the row is matched to the patient's [single record](US-11.1.1) and becomes a Booking.

This is because the NHI is the patient's unique identifier, and duplicate patients are a recurring problem in Solutions Plus when it is missing. The anaesthetist also needs the NHI to look the patient up in Health Connect ([anaesthetist sees the NHI](US-03.1.5)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an incoming booking with no NHI, when it reaches intake, then it appears as waiting for an NHI, with its surgeon's rooms shown.
- Given an admin adds the NHI, when the row is matched, then it leaves the waiting view and no second patient record exists.
- Given the NHI is still missing, when the List date approaches, then the row stays visible and is marked as urgent.

## Notes
