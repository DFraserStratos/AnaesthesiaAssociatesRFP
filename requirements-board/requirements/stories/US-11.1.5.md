An incoming booking with no NHI waits on the [unmatched queue](US-02.1.4) until the office adds the NHI (see [patient without NHI](US-11.1.4)). Meanwhile a [stub](US-02.6.1) shows in its place on its List, seen by the anaesthetist and the office, until the office resolves it. This story is the office's view of those waiting rows: it shows the patient as received, the List or date, and who to chase for the NHI, and marks the ones whose procedure date is close. No Booking and no patient record exists for such a row, so a missing NHI can never produce a duplicate patient.

To get the NHI, the office emails the address the List [came from](US-02.2.4), or, where the List holds none, the surgeon's first [office contact](US-13.6.5), with any others offered to switch to. The request goes to one address only, because it carries the patient's details. Some rooms' PDFs leave the NHI out, so it may only arrive with the daily hospital or theatre list. When it arrives, the office adds it, the row is matched to the patient's [single record](US-11.1.1) and becomes a Booking.

This is because the NHI is the patient's unique identifier, and duplicate patients are a recurring problem in Solutions Plus when it is missing. The anaesthetist also needs the NHI to look the patient up in Health Connect ([anaesthetist sees the NHI](US-03.1.5)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Waiting for an NHI.** Given an incoming booking with no NHI, when it reaches intake, then it appears as waiting for an NHI, with the address to chase shown: the List's sender address, otherwise the surgeon's first office contact.
- **One address.** Given a surgeon with more than one office contact, when the office requests the NHI, then the request goes to one address, never to every contact.
- **NHI added.** Given an admin adds the NHI, when the row is matched, then it leaves the waiting view and no second patient record exists.
- **Urgent when close.** Given the NHI is still missing, when the List date approaches, then the row stays visible and is marked as urgent.
- **Stub on the List.** Given the row is waiting for an NHI, then its List shows a stub in its place until the office resolves it.
