An incoming booking with no NHI waits at intake until its NHI is added (see [patient without NHI](US-11.1.4)). Meanwhile a [stub](US-02.6.1) shows in its place on its List, seen by the anaesthetist and the office, until the office resolves it. No Booking and no patient record exists for such a row, so a missing NHI can never produce a duplicate patient.

The rows waiting for an NHI show in the [matching screen's feed](US-02.1.4) (Intake) in the Admin app, among the other arrivals, under a Needs NHI filter; they have no view of their own. Intake's attention count includes each of them once. Each row shows the patient as received, the List or date, and who to chase for the NHI, and the ones whose procedure date is close are marked as urgent.

To get the NHI, the office emails the address the List [came from](US-02.2.4), or, where the List holds none, the surgeon's first [office contact](US-13.6.5), with any others offered to switch to. The request goes to one address only, because it carries the patient's details.

When the NHI comes back, the office adds it from the row, the one way to add an NHI by hand. Some rooms' PDFs leave the NHI out, so it may only come with a later arrival for the List, usually the hospital's: that update shows the NHI, and applying it fills it in. Either way, the row is matched to the patient's [single record](US-11.1.1), becomes a Booking, and its stub leaves the List.

This is because the NHI is the patient's unique identifier, and duplicate patients are a recurring problem in Solutions Plus when it is missing. The anaesthetist also needs the NHI to look the patient up in Health Connect ([anaesthetist sees the NHI](US-03.1.5)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Waiting for an NHI.** Given an incoming booking with no NHI, when it reaches intake, then it appears in the matching screen's feed under the Needs NHI filter, with the address to chase shown: the List's sender address, otherwise the surgeon's first office contact.
- **Counted on Intake.** Given a row waiting for an NHI, then Intake's attention count includes it once, and the count drops by one when the NHI is added.
- **One address.** Given a surgeon with more than one office contact, when the office requests the NHI, then the request goes to one address, never to every contact.
- **NHI added.** Given an admin adds the NHI, when the row is matched, then it leaves the Needs NHI filter and no second patient record exists.
- **NHI from a later arrival.** Given a row waiting for an NHI, when a later arrival for its List carries the patient's NHI, then the proposed update shows the NHI, and applying it makes the row a Booking on the patient's single record and removes its stub.
- **Urgent when close.** Given the NHI is still missing, when the List date approaches, then the row stays visible and is marked as urgent.
- **Stub on the List.** Given the row is waiting for an NHI, then its List shows a stub in its place until the office resolves it.
