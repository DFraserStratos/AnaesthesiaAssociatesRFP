A missing NHI is a visible exception that has to be resolved: the system requires an NHI for every Booking's patient, so a patient without one is not quietly accepted and is never given a duplicate patient record. It appears on a problem list in the Admin App, showing the patient, the List and the surgeon's rooms, until the NHI is added. Whether the Booking exists meanwhile is OQ-49.

The office emails the surgeon's rooms to get the NHI. Some rooms' PDFs leave it out, so it may only arrive with the daily hospital or theatre list. When it arrives, the exception clears and the patient is matched to their [single record](US-11.1.1).

This is because the NHI is the patient's unique identifier, and duplicate patients are a recurring problem in Solutions Plus when it is missing. The anaesthetist also needs the NHI to look the patient up in Health Connect ([anaesthetist sees the NHI](US-03.1.5)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient with no NHI, when a Booking is created or imported for them, then the missing NHI appears on the problem list.
- Given an admin adds the NHI, when the patient is matched, then the exception clears and no second patient record exists.
- Given the NHI is still missing, when the List date approaches, then the entry stays visible to admins.

## Notes

Overseas patients are pre-billed and normally already have an NHI (Vanessa).

The working assumption until OQ-49 is answered is in [patient without NHI](US-11.1.4). How a duplicate record is handled when the NHI is found later, and whether duplicates matter once the operation is done, are part of OQ-49.
