Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, must be paid for before they go ahead. Prepayment is triggered by the Contract: when the office sets up the Booking and picks a Contract flagged as prepaid, normally [the anaesthetist's own price list](US-04.2.14), the system starts the prepayment (a letter and an invoice). The anaesthetist tells the office which procedures they want prepaid and at what price, and the office sets up that Contract; nothing on the anaesthetist's profile triggers it.

The prepaid amount is the fixed price on that Contract's line, which always carries one, and the full amount is prepaid: there is no part payment or deposit. The office approves the prepayment invoice where outgoing invoices are approved, and it is then sent. The anaesthetist [sees the prepaid amount on the Booking](US-03.1.8).

A prepayment is not paid to the anaesthetist. It is held in a [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1), within AA's trust accounting, until the procedure is done. Prepayment is then two steps: the money is collected into the holding account, and once the procedure is done and the office authorises the List, a second pair moves it from the holding account to whichever anaesthetist did the List, in place of another invoice to the patient. If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment is refunded in full.

By default the prepaid amount is the final price, and the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only. The system calculates nothing further and raises no invoice or credit automatically, in either direction. This is the build position until Ben answers OQ-96 and OQ-97. Where the work turns out very different, for example only half done or much longer than expected, the anaesthetist or the office may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

If the Booking moves to another anaesthetist, nothing changes in the ledger, because the money sits in the holding account, and nothing is recalculated: the price prepaid when the invoice went out is the price ([prepaid Booking moved](US-06.5.4)).
