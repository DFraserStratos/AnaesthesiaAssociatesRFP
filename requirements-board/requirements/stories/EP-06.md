Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, must be paid for before they go ahead. Each anaesthetist chooses which procedures, or [whole RVG groups](US-05.1.3), they want prepaid, as a setting on their own profile.

The prepaid amount is not an estimate. It is the fixed price for that procedure in the [anaesthetist's own first-party Contract](US-04.2.14), and the full amount is prepaid: there is no part payment or deposit. AA invoices it at booking setup.

Prepayment money is held in AA's trust account until the procedure is done. If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment is refunded in full.

By default the prepaid amount is the final price, and the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only. The system calculates nothing further and raises no invoice or credit automatically, in either direction. Where the work turns out very different, for example only half done or much longer than expected, the anaesthetist may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

If the Booking moves to another anaesthetist, an honour system applies: the new anaesthetist claims no more than was prepaid. The system has no logic that detects the move or re-triggers any calculation (OQ-70).
