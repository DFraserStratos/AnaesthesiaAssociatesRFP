Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, must be paid for before they go ahead. Prepayment is triggered by the Contract: when the office sets up the Booking and picks a Contract flagged as prepaid, normally [the anaesthetist's own price list](US-04.2.14), the system starts the prepayment (a letter and an invoice). The anaesthetist tells the office which procedures they want prepaid and at what price, and the office sets up that Contract; nothing on the anaesthetist's profile triggers it.

The prepaid amount is the fixed price on that Contract's line, which always carries one, and the full amount is prepaid: there is no part payment or deposit. AA invoices it at booking setup. The anaesthetist [sees the prepaid amount on the Booking](US-03.1.8).

Prepayment money is held in AA's trust account until the procedure is done. If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment is refunded in full.

By default the prepaid amount is the final price, and the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only. The system calculates nothing further and raises no invoice or credit automatically, in either direction. Where the work turns out very different, for example only half done or much longer than expected, the anaesthetist or the office may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

If the Booking moves to another anaesthetist, an honour system applies: the new anaesthetist claims no more than was prepaid. The system has no logic that detects the move or re-triggers any calculation (OQ-70).
