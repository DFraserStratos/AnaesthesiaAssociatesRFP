Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, must be paid for before they go ahead. Prepayment is triggered by the Contract: when the office sets up the Booking and picks a Contract flagged as prepaid, normally [the anaesthetist's own price list](US-04.2.14), the system starts the prepayment (a letter and an invoice). The anaesthetist tells the office which procedures they want prepaid and at what price, and the office sets up that Contract; nothing on the anaesthetist's profile triggers it.

The prepaid amount is the fixed price on that Contract's line, which must carry a price above zero, and the full amount is prepaid: there is no part payment or deposit. By default an admin approves the prepayment invoice where outgoing invoices are approved, and it is then sent; this approval is an optional step that AA can switch off, so prepayment invoices go out without anyone approving each one. The anaesthetist [sees the prepaid amount on the Booking](US-03.1.8).

A prepayment is not paid to the anaesthetist. It is credited to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), an accounts payable account, within AA's trust accounting, until the procedure is done. Prepayment is then two steps: the money is collected into the holding account, and once the procedure is done and the office authorises the List, it is transferred from the holding account to whichever anaesthetist did the List. If the Booking is cancelled, the prepayment invoice is credited if unpaid, or refunded in full if paid.

By default the prepaid amount is the final price, and the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only. The system calculates nothing further and raises no invoice or credit automatically, in either direction. Where the work turns out very different, for example only half done or much longer than expected, the anaesthetist or the office may raise an [ad hoc invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand.

A prepaid Contract is not editable by the anaesthetist: it cannot also allow a price override, so the prepaid amount stays the price (OQ-96). The office can still change any price ([office price change](US-05.4.2)). Ben, 28 October: a rare difference is handled by hand, "a rare event can have some manual input".

If the Booking moves to another anaesthetist, nothing changes in the ledger, because the money sits in the holding account, and nothing is recalculated: the price prepaid when the invoice went out is the price ([prepaid Booking moved](US-06.5.4)).

## Notes

OQ-80 answered 2026-10-19: the prepayment invoice and its pair are generated when an admin approves it, the pair's other side is the AA Holding account, and a move changes nothing in the ledger. It replaces OQ-70's answer that the payable half is updated to the new anaesthetist. The second step is triggered by the office authorising the List, since the engine acts only then (Donald, 19 October; Greg had said "confirmed and submitted", [nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

The v6 design treats the prepaid amount as an editable price and refunds a lowered one, which OQ-96's answer of 28 October rejects. A prepaid Procedure keeps the price on its issued prepayment invoice when the Contract's price changes before the procedure: a new Contract version does not relink it (Donald, 19 October). Ben confirmed this for a prepaid Procedure on 27 October: "You would honour the money that's been prepaid, because that's the quote that the patient's been given" (OQ-104).
