Some Procedures, typically cosmetic, plastics or dental work, are paid for before they go ahead. The office makes a Procedure prepaid by picking a prepaid Contract, normally an item on [the anaesthetist's own price list](US-04.2.14), or by typing an amount for one Booking ([a prepaid amount from outside any price list](US-06.2.6)). The system then invoices the payer, holds the money in the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) until the procedure is done, and transfers it to the anaesthetist who did the List.

This is so the patient pays a known amount before the work, and the money is held in trust until it is earned.

The epic covers:

- what makes a Procedure prepaid and sets its amount ([prepayment trigger and amount](FT-06.2))
- raising, sending and tracking the prepayment invoice and letter ([prepayment invoicing and tracking](FT-06.3))
- the holding account, cancellations, moves to another anaesthetist, and the transfer after the procedure ([trust account and cancellation refunds](FT-06.5))

The prepaid amount is the price: the system raises no further invoice or credit by itself, and a person raises one by hand where the work turned out very different ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

## Notes

OQ-80 answered 19 October: the prepayment invoice and its pair are generated when an admin approves it, the pair's other side is the AA Holding account, and a move changes nothing in the ledger. It replaces OQ-70's answer that the payable half is updated to the new anaesthetist. The transfer is triggered by the office authorising the List, since the engine acts only then (Donald, 19 October; Greg had said "confirmed and submitted").

OQ-96 answered 28 October: a prepaid Contract cannot allow a price override, and a rare difference is handled by hand (Ben: "a rare event can have some manual input"). Greg's technical design v6 treated the prepaid amount as an editable price and refunded a lowered one; that is rejected. Our reading (contract model proposal A16): once the prepayment invoice is raised the price is read-only for the office too.

A new price range does not move a prepaid Procedure once its prepayment invoice is raised (Donald, 19 October). Ben, 27 October: "You would honour the money that's been prepaid, because that's the quote that the patient's been given" (OQ-104).
