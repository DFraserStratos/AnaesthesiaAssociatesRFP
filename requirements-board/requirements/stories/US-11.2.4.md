When a patient under 18 is left as [the payer](US-11.2.2) on a Procedure billed to its payer, the system gives AA staff a [mild warning](US-13.7.1). There is no warning when someone else is billed: a parent already set as payer, or a holder whose Contract bills it.

The warning does not block saving the Booking or authorising the List. It shows on each such Procedure, as "Under 18" in its "Paid by" line. The [dashboard to-do list](US-13.7.2) holds one warning per Booking, however many of its Procedures raise it, and an admin can clear it there. It reminds the office to check the payer's details: a parent's or guardian's name and [email or postal address](US-11.2.3), which are held on the Procedure. There is no separate guardian record.

This is because an invoice for a child normally goes to a parent or guardian, and today the office catches this by hand at review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient under 18 left as the payer on a Procedure billed to its payer, then a mild warning is raised.
- Given a patient under 18 whose Procedure is under a Contract that bills its holder, then no warning is raised.
- Given a Booking with two Procedures that both bill a patient under 18 as payer, then the warning shows on each Procedure and the to-do list holds one warning for the Booking.
- Given the warning, when an admin clears it, then it leaves the to-do list.
