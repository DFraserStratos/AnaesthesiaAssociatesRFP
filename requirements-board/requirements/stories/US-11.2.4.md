When a patient under 18 is the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2) for a Procedure billed to that payer, the system gives AA staff a [mild warning](US-13.7.1). There is no warning when someone else is billed: a parent already set as payer, or a holder whose Contract bills it.

The warning does not block saving the Booking or authorising the List. It shows on the [dashboard to-do list](US-13.7.2), where an admin can clear it, and reminds the office to check the payer's details: a parent's or guardian's name and [email or postal address](US-11.2.3), which are held on the Booking. There is no separate guardian record.

This is because an invoice for a child normally goes to a parent or guardian, and today the office catches this by hand at review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a patient under 18 left as the payer on a Booking billed to the payer, then a mild warning is raised.
- Given a patient under 18 whose Procedure is under a Contract that bills its holder, then no warning is raised.
- Given the warning, when an admin clears it, then it leaves the to-do list.
