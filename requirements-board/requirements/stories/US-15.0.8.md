Anaesthetists, AA office staff and other users of the apps can get help from Stratos' support line when something is not working as expected, such as a forgotten password or being unable to sign in. They raise a request through a support request form or a support phone line, both shown in the apps, and each request is logged and tracked.

This is so the people who use the apps every day have someone to turn to.

- Hours: 8:30am to 5:00pm NZT, Monday to Friday, excluding New Zealand public holidays.
- After hours: no cover, except for a security breach or the platform being offline or unavailable.

Response times, for requests raised in hours:

| Priority | Response |
|---|---|
| P1: system down with widespread business impact | 1 hour |
| P2: high-priority issue significantly affecting business operations, with no viable workaround | Same business day |
| P3: standard issue or request with limited business impact, or a reasonable workaround | 4 hours |
| P4: low-priority issue, cosmetic defect, minor enhancement or planned task | Scheduled at a mutually agreed time |

## Acceptance criteria

- **Reachable from the apps.** Given any of the three apps, then the support request form and the support phone line can be found from it.
- **Logged.** Given a request through the form or the phone line, then it is logged as a ticket and tracked to its close.
- **Hours.** A request raised in hours gets a response within its priority's time; after hours only a security breach or the platform being offline or unavailable is answered.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response, now replaced by Stratos' standard support terms (this story and [product support for AA](US-15.0.9)):

- Support hours: BAU support Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm NZT, with no weekend or on-call cover by default; given late bookings up to the start of a session, coverage is to be discussed during scoping.
- Who raises tickets: AA administrators are normally the first point of contact, escalating to Stratos where needed. As an option, anaesthetists and office users raise tickets with the Stratos support desk themselves, with AA administrators still seeing every ticket; AA can start administrator-led and move to direct support later.
- Channels: tickets are raised through a support portal or by email or phone, and tracked there with their status, history and SLA audit trail.
- Response targets where an SLA is in place: P1 1 hour, P2 same business day, P3 3 business days, P4 at an agreed time. Without an SLA, requests are handled on a best-effort basis.

The help line answers an outage after hours; how Stratos' incident response process handles it is to be discussed later.
