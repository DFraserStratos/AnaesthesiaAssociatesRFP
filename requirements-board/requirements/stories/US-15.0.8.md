AA's office staff can reach Stratos' help desk by phone or by email when something in the system is not working, a password problem included, and get help within the support hours. Each request is logged as a ticket and tracked.

A request for something the system does not yet do goes to the product owner or the development team, to plan as a new feature.

This is so the office has someone to turn to when the system it relies on for bookings and billing is not working.

The support hours are not settled (OQ-137): AA's office starts at 8:00am. How an outage is handled, through Stratos' incident response process, is to be discussed later.

## Technical discussion

Proposed in our RFP response:

- Support hours: BAU support Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm NZT, with no weekend or on-call cover by default; given late bookings up to the start of a session, coverage is to be discussed during scoping.
- Who raises tickets: AA administrators are normally the first point of contact, escalating to Stratos where needed. As an option, anaesthetists and office users raise tickets with the Stratos support desk themselves, with AA administrators still seeing every ticket; AA can start administrator-led and move to direct support later.
- Channels: tickets are raised through a support portal or by email or phone, and tracked there with their status, history and SLA audit trail.
- Response targets where an SLA is in place: P1 1 hour, P2 same business day, P3 3 business days, P4 at an agreed time. Without an SLA, requests are handled on a best-effort basis.
