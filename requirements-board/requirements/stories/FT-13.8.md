The Admin App holds one pool of notifications shared by the whole AA admin team, not one per user, for things that have happened and need no action. It is separate from the [to-do list](FT-13.7) of warnings that need action. Anyone logged on sees it: Greg, "If you're logged on the screen, you can see it."

A notice is in one of two states: active or resolved. Anyone in the office ticks a notice to resolve it, and it is then hidden for the whole team. Nothing expires by itself and nothing is archived automatically; there is no separate "read" state. The same tick [clears a to-do](US-13.7.2).

Notices come from:

- an anaesthetist [moving their own List](US-13.8.2)
- an alert raised from a List or Booking, with a note, asking the office to review or act on it ([ask the office to check a Procedure](US-03.6.3))
- optionally, a single Booking move, when the mover chooses to notify the admin team (see [moving a single Booking](US-01.4.7))

This is so the office knows what has changed without each change becoming a task, and so no notice quietly disappears before someone has dealt with it. Donald: "I don't like the idea of one person reading a notification but not actioning it and then having it look like it's been read by somebody else."
