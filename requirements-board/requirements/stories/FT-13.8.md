The Admin App holds one pool of notifications shared by the whole AA admin team, not one per user, for things that have happened and need no action. It is separate from the [to-do list](FT-13.7) of warnings that need action. Anyone logged on sees it: Greg, "If you're logged on the screen, you can see it."

The first source of notifications is an anaesthetist moving their own List. Several may move each morning, and Vanessa wants to know.

This is so the office knows what has changed without each change becoming a task.

Not settled: what else posts to the pool, whether notifications expire (Greg: "Just let them scroll"), what actioned versus unactioned means for a notice that needs no action, and how it sits beside the to-do list on the dashboard (OQ-79).
