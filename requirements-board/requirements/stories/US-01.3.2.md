Admins maintain recurring bookings: the standing intersection of a hospital, an anaesthetist and a surgeon, on a day of the week and an AM or PM session, painted onto Lists.

A recurring booking creates its List against the anaesthetist, surgeon, hospital, day and session across the horizon (four months in current practice), before any Bookings exist, so a List can exist with no Bookings.

This is so roughly 80% of List assignments populate automatically from the pattern, rather than being assigned by hand each time.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a recurring booking, when Lists are generated, then each List has its anaesthetist, surgeon, hospital, day and session and holds no Bookings yet.
- Given a recurring booking whose Slot the anaesthetist has already marked unavailable, when Lists are generated, then its List is created as a [Draft List](FT-01.6) with no anaesthetist, for the office to resolve.

## Notes

"Recurring booking" replaces "template" and "permanent booking" (and this catalogue's earlier "Permanent List"). A recurring booking is a pattern of Lists, not a Booking for one patient.

The anaesthetist's calendar is painted before recurring bookings (OQ-81). Donald doubted "most" in the title ("Not sure about the most"); the title is unchanged for now.

The surgeon and the anaesthetist each keep their own one-off and recurring blockouts, and "the shared view is the list". Whether a recurring arrangement can be edited from both ends, or one end is read only, is not settled.
