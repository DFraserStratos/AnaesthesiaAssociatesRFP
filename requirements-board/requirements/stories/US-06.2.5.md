An admin records the estimated duration of a Procedure on the Booking when the surgeon's rooms give one, by email, phone or in their PDF list ([surgeon PDF ingest](US-02.2.1)).

The [prepayment estimate](US-06.2.4) uses it to work out time units. It is an estimate only: once the procedure is done, the anaesthetist's recorded [start and handover times](US-03.3.3) decide the final time units.

This is so the estimate has a time figure to work from before the procedure has happened.

## Notes

Retired 2026-10-07: it existed only to feed the prepayment estimate, which no longer exists. The prepaid amount is the anaesthetist's own fixed price, set by US-06.2.2 (Set the prepaid amount).
