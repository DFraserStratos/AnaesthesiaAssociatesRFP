Every surgeon has a profile page in the Admin App, held on the surgeon master list. It records:

- the surgeon's name and the [room](US-13.6.1) they belong to
- their New Zealand medical registration number
- their HPI CPN (Common Person Number), the practitioner's unique identifier on the HPI (Health Provider Index); other numbers on the profile are for information
- private [not-preferred](US-13.6.3) and [preferred](US-13.6.4) pairings with anaesthetists, in either direction, seen only by admin staff

This is so a surgeon is one identified record rather than free text, the same way an anaesthetist has [a profile](US-12.1.4). Solutions Plus already keeps these fields on its surgeon profile page.

## Notes

HPI identifies practitioners and facilities; it is not the patient's NHI. The HPI number and the CPN are one identifier for an individual practitioner: Health NZ writes it as "HPI CPN (Common Person Number)", so the catalogue calls it the HPI CPN (OQ-52). The HPI CPN is the surgeon's unique index, the equivalent of the NHI for a patient.
