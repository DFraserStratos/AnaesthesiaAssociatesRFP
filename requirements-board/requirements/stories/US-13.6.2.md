Every surgeon has a profile page in the Admin App, held on the surgeon master list. It records:

- the surgeon's name and the [room or rooms](US-13.6.1) they belong to (a surgeon can belong to more than one)
- their New Zealand medical registration number
- their HPI CPN (Common Person Number), the practitioner's unique identifier on the HPI (Health Provider Index); other numbers on the profile are for information
- their [specialties](US-13.6.6), usually one, at least one
- private [not-preferred](US-13.6.3) and [preferred](US-13.6.4) pairings with anaesthetists, in either direction, seen only by admin staff

This is so a surgeon is one identified record rather than free text, the same way an anaesthetist has [a profile](US-12.1.4).

## Notes

Solutions Plus already keeps these fields on its surgeon profile page.

HPI identifies practitioners and facilities; it is not the patient's NHI. The HPI number and the CPN are one identifier for an individual practitioner: Health NZ writes it as "HPI CPN (Common Person Number)", so the catalogue calls it the HPI CPN (OQ-52). The HPI CPN is the surgeon's unique index, the equivalent of the NHI for a patient.

Ben's specialist list of 3 November carries no HPI CPNs or registration numbers, so where they come from, and whether a surgeon can be loaded without one, is OQ-129.

The specialty is now required ([surgeon specialties](US-13.6.6), D3); whether the address book is searched by specialty is still OQ-126. Some surgeons in a shared practice have an email of their own; whether that sits on the profile is OQ-127.

OQ-128 answered 4 November (Donald, for the build; not yet seen by Vanessa): a surgeon can belong to more than one room; which room's contacts apply to a given Booking is OQ-127.
