Every surgeon has a profile page in the Admin App, held on the surgeon master list. It records:

- the surgeon's name, and the name of the practice they work from, as plain text
- their New Zealand medical registration number
- their HPI CPN (Common Person Number), the practitioner's unique identifier on the HPI (Health Provider Index), which admin staff fill in; a surgeon can be saved without one until it is known. Other numbers on the profile are for information
- their [specialties](US-13.6.6), usually one, at least one
- their [personal and office contacts](US-13.6.5)
- private [not-preferred](US-13.6.3) and [preferred](US-13.6.4) pairings with anaesthetists, in either direction, seen only by admin staff

This is so a surgeon is one identified record rather than free text, the same way an anaesthetist has [a profile](US-12.1.4).

The surgeon master list is AA's address book of surgeons: there is no separate address book. It holds surgeons and how to reach them, not hospital or insurer contacts, and AA's admin staff keep it current.

Once recorded, a surgeon's HPI CPN identifies them, not their practice: no two surgeons share one. The practice name is for the office to read; nothing else in the system depends on it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Practice name.** Given a surgeon with a practice name, when admin staff open their profile, then the practice name is shown as typed, and admin staff can change it as text.
- **Contacts on the profile.** Given a surgeon with a personal contact and office contacts, when admin staff open their profile, then each contact is shown with its name, email and phone.
- **No HPI CPN yet.** Given a surgeon with no HPI CPN, when admin staff save the profile, then it is saved, and the HPI CPN can be added later.
- **Unique HPI CPN.** Given a surgeon holding an HPI CPN, when admin staff save another surgeon with the same one, then it is refused.

## Notes

Solutions Plus already keeps these fields on its surgeon profile page.

HPI identifies practitioners and facilities; it is not the patient's NHI. The HPI number and the CPN are one identifier for an individual practitioner: Health NZ writes it as "HPI CPN (Common Person Number)", so the catalogue calls it the HPI CPN (OQ-52). The HPI CPN is the surgeon's unique index, the equivalent of the NHI for a patient.

Ben's specialist list of 3 November carries no HPI CPNs or registration numbers. OQ-129 answered 18 November (Donald, for the build): AA admin staff fill them in, and a surgeon can be loaded without one. Whether it becomes required at some point, such as before the surgeon's first Booking, is OQ-143.

The specialty is required ([surgeon specialties](US-13.6.6), D3); OQ-126 answered 18 November (Donald): the address book is the surgeon master data, it holds "just people", and AA's admin team keep it current; no search by specialty is asked for. Our reading of "just people": surgeons and their contacts, not the hospital and insurer contacts the question offered.

Donald, 18 November (for the build; not yet seen by AA): no surgeons' room record for now, and the practice name is plain text on the surgeon (OQ-127; Notes 2026-11-18 · Donald surgeon contacts no rooms #6 #8). This replaces the 4 November reading that a surgeon belongs to one or more rooms (OQ-128). Ben's list puts 4 surgeons at two practices; such a surgeon holds an office contact for each.
