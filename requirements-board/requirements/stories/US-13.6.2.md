Every surgeon has their own page in the People section of the Admin app, reached from the Surgeons list and keyed by an identifier that never changes. The page shows, each on a card:

- the surgeon's name, and the name of the practice they work from, as plain text
- their New Zealand medical registration number
- their HPI CPN (Common Person Number), the practitioner's unique identifier on the HPI (Health Provider Index), which admin staff fill in; a surgeon can be saved without one until it is known. Other numbers on the page are for information
- their [specialties](US-13.6.6), usually one, at least one
- their [personal and office contacts](US-13.6.5)

Each card is edited and saved on its own, and each save is one audited change.

A side panel shows what the surgeon is on: their private [not-preferred](US-13.6.3) and [preferred](US-13.6.4) pairings with anaesthetists, in either direction, seen only by admin staff; the Contracts linked to them; and their next Lists. Each shows a few, with a way to see all.

This is so a surgeon is one identified record rather than free text, the same way an anaesthetist has [a page](US-12.1.4), and everything about them is on one screen the office can link to.

The Surgeons list can be searched by name, practice or HPI CPN, filtered by specialty and by Active or Retired, and is shown a page at a time.

The surgeon list is AA's address book of surgeons: there is no separate address book. It holds surgeons and how to reach them, not hospital or insurer contacts, and AA's admin staff keep it current.

Once recorded, a surgeon's HPI CPN identifies them, not their practice: no two surgeons share one. The practice name is for the office to read; nothing else in the system depends on it. A surgeon belongs to no group: the surgeons who work for a group that holds Contracts are those linked on its Contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Practice name.** Given a surgeon with a practice name, when admin staff open their page, then the practice name is shown as typed, and admin staff can change it as text.
- **Contacts on the page.** Given a surgeon with a personal contact and office contacts, when admin staff open their page, then each contact is shown with its name, email and phone.
- **No HPI CPN yet.** Given a surgeon with no HPI CPN, when admin staff save the page, then it is saved, and the HPI CPN can be added later.
- **Unique HPI CPN.** Given a surgeon holding an HPI CPN, when admin staff save another surgeon with the same one, then it is refused.
- **One card at a time.** Given admin staff edit a surgeon's contacts and save, then only the contacts change, and the audit records that one change.
- **Side panel.** Given a surgeon with four pairings, two linked Contracts and six upcoming Lists, when admin staff open their page, then the side panel shows a few of each, and each can be opened in full.
- **Find a surgeon.** Given the Surgeons list, when admin staff search by HPI CPN, or filter by Ophthalmology and Active, then only the matching surgeons are shown.

## Notes

Solutions Plus already keeps these fields on its surgeon profile page.

HPI identifies practitioners and facilities; it is not the patient's NHI. The HPI number and the CPN are one identifier for an individual practitioner: Health NZ writes it as "HPI CPN (Common Person Number)", so the catalogue calls it the HPI CPN (OQ-52). The HPI CPN is the surgeon's unique index, the equivalent of the NHI for a patient.
