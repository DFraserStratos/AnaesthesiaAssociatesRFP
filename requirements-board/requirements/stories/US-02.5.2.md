A List is defined by its day and session, and it never moves. A change of procedure time within the same List is an ordinary modification ([modify a Booking](US-02.5.1)).

When a Booking or a whole List moves to another day, the surgeons' rooms in practice cancel and re-send. The Bookings disappear from the old List's snapshot, and the Scheduling Engine handles them as removals and cancellations ([detect removed Bookings](US-02.2.3)). They arrive again as new Bookings on the new day's List or Lists, possibly reshuffled among several, through the usual intake and matching. The patient is the same (same NHI); the Booking is new. The emptied List goes back to being a Slot; whether that leaves a Draft List was left open. There is no clash or Draft List rule for moves.

This is so the schedule follows the surgeons' rooms without the system trying to guess that a cancelled Booking and a new one are the same procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Moved to another day.** Given a Booking, or a whole List, moves to another day, when the rooms re-send, then the old Bookings are recorded as cancellations and the Bookings arrive as new Bookings on the new day's List or Lists, through the usual intake and matching.
- **Same List, new time.** Given a procedure time changes within the same List, then it is applied as an ordinary modification.
- **History kept.** Given a cancellation or a new Booking arrives from an integration or a PDF, then its change history records that source.

## Technical discussion

- Surgeons' rooms send a copy of their List, a snapshot, not individual changes, so the system does not recognise a move as a move. It sees Bookings missing from a re-sent List and new Bookings appearing.
- Because a List never moves, two Lists never need to merge.
