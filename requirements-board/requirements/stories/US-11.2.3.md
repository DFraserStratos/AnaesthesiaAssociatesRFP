Where a Procedure is billed to the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2), the system requires the payer's email.

This is because no contract holder, such as a hospital or insurer, takes the invoice instead.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure whose Contract does not bill its holder, when the payer on the Booking has no email, then the system asks for one.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, then no payer email is required for it.

## Notes

When the email is enforced is not restated by the new sources. The former US-04.2.7 (Required booking inputs), now Retired, blocked the Booking from being [marked complete](US-03.6.1) until a required input was present; that stays the working rule here: a missing payer email blocks marking the Booking complete.
