Where a Procedure is billed to the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2), the system requires the payer's email or postal address: at least one must be filled in. A postal address follows the NZ Post address standard. A Contract's billable party is not affected: it has its own nominated email.

This is because no contract holder, such as a hospital or insurer, takes the invoice instead, and some patients have no email and want invoices by post.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure whose Contract does not bill its holder, when the payer on the Booking has neither an email nor a postal address, then the system asks for one.
- Given a payer with a postal address and no email, then no email is asked for.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract whose holder pays AA, then no payer email or postal address is required for it.

## Notes

When the email is enforced is not restated by the new sources. The former US-04.2.7 (Required booking inputs), now Retired, blocked the Booking from being [marked complete](US-03.6.1) until a required input was present; where a missing payer email or postal address is enforced (completion, office review or invoicing) is not stated by the sources. US-03.6.1 does not currently list it among its completion checks.
