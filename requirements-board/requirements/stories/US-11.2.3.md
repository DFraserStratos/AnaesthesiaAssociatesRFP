Where a Procedure is billed to the [payer on the Booking](US-11.2.2), the system requires the payer's email or postal address: at least one must be filled in. A postal address follows the NZ Post address standard. A holder billed by its Contract is not affected: its delivery details are on its [Party](US-04.1.5).

This is because no holder, such as a hospital or insurer, takes the invoice instead, and some patients have no email and want invoices by post.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure whose Contract does not bill its holder, when the payer on the Booking has neither an email nor a postal address, then the system asks for one.
- Given a payer with a postal address and no email, then no email is asked for.
- Given a Procedure under a Contract that bills its holder, then no payer email or postal address is required for it.

## Notes

Where a missing payer email or postal address is enforced (completion, office review or invoicing) is not stated by the sources, and [marking complete](US-03.6.1) does not list it among its checks. The former US-04.2.7 (Required booking inputs), now Retired, blocked completion until a required input was present.

Greg, 28 October: "There will be some patients who need an address because they don't have an email"; "one of them has to be filled", "using the NZ Post standard... address format".
