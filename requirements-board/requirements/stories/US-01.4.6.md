Whoever submits a List is the anaesthetist who did every procedure on it. If another anaesthetist does a Booking, the Booking is moved to that anaesthetist's List (before the procedure), even if it is the only Booking on it. An anaesthetist never does a procedure on another anaesthetist's List.

The payable, and any prepayment payee, follow the Booking ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given anaesthetist B did a Booking on anaesthetist A's List, then the Booking ends up on a List of B's and A's List no longer holds it.
- Given the move, then the Booking's payable is to B.
- Given the move, then prepayments are [re-checked](US-06.3.5).

## Notes

The word "timesheet" is not used; say a completed or submitted Booking. The agreed prepayment stands, and only one half of its draft pair, presumably the payable, is updated to the anaesthetist who does the procedure (OQ-70). When in the lifecycle the pair is created and amended is not settled yet (OQ-80).
