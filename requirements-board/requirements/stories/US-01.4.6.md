Whoever submits a List is the anaesthetist who did every procedure on it. If another anaesthetist does a Booking, the Booking is [moved to that anaesthetist's List](US-01.4.7) (before the procedure), even if it is the only Booking on it. An anaesthetist never does a procedure on another anaesthetist's List.

For an unprepaid Booking the payable follows the Booking to the anaesthetist who does it. For a prepaid Booking the payable is the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) and does not move; whichever anaesthetist did the List is paid from it at settlement. The system re-triggers no prepayment or invoice calculation on the move, and nothing is recalculated ([prepaid Booking moved to another anaesthetist](US-06.5.4)).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given anaesthetist B did a Booking on anaesthetist A's List, then the Booking ends up on a List of B's and A's List no longer holds it.
- Given the move of an unprepaid Booking, then the Booking's payable is to B.
- Given the move of a prepaid Booking, then its payable stays the AA Holding account, and B is paid from it at settlement.
- Given the move, then no prepayment or invoice calculation is [re-triggered](US-06.3.5) and the new anaesthetist claims the prepaid amount ([prepaid Booking moved](US-06.5.4)).

## Notes

The word "timesheet" is not used; say a completed or submitted Booking. The agreed prepayment stands: its price is the price at the time the Booking was created and the prepayment invoice went out. Its payable is the AA Holding account, not an anaesthetist, so a move changes nothing in the ledger (OQ-80, which replaced OQ-70's answer that the payable half was updated to the new anaesthetist).
