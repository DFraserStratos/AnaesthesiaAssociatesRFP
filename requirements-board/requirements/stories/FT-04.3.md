Every Procedure has exactly one Contract. The office picks it at booking setup on a [step rail](US-04.3.8): first where the invoice goes, then the Contract, from the Active Contracts that [fit the List](US-04.3.2), or the destination's [open-price fallback](US-04.3.9). The anaesthetist sees the Contract and [may change it](US-03.4.1).

This is so every Procedure always has a Contract to work from, and the choice offered stays short and relevant.
