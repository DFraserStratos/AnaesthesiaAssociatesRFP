Every Procedure has exactly one Contract. At booking setup the office identifies who pays by choosing the [contract holder](US-04.1.5), then the Contract, then, where the Contract has lines, the line that fits. The anaesthetist sees it and [may change it](US-03.4.1).

The Contract list shows Contracts, not their lines:

- "No contract (RVG)" always first
- then the hospital's Contracts
- then any surgeons' and anaesthetists' arrangements

It is limited to those valid on the procedure date whose holder fits the Booking: the List's hospital, and Contracts that belong to the Booking's anaesthetist or are linked to its surgeon. Picking a Contract leads down to its lines.

The RVG code can come before or after. Where it is picked first, it narrows the lines to those carrying it as their [default RVG code](US-04.2.4); where a fixed-price Contract is picked first, no RVG code is needed. The order and navigation are to be designed and tested with AA.

This is so every Procedure always has a Contract to work from, whichever route the user takes to it, and the choice offered stays short and relevant.

Greg asked for this to be written in terms of contract holders. The picker will be a centrepiece of the system and gets its design and testing with AA later (Greg: the current booking screen is "awful"). The directors' concern is misallocated RVG groups and billable parties, and new anaesthetists sending everything back to the office, so it must be easy.

A holder's plain RVG Contract has no lines and is offered by context (OQ-98, answered).
