Admins set how a Contract's invoices look and how they are delivered:

- [the invoice layout](US-08.4.1): hospital or holder, or patient. The two are very similar; the hospital's carries the BTM breakdown and a couple more fields so the hospital can cross-match, the patient's does not.
- the delivery method: email, or another output such as a portal upload, for a [holder](US-04.1.5) that takes its invoices that way (OQ-114)
- [the GST treatment](US-05.2.7)
- the holder's reference for the Contract (for example SXAP), printed on the invoice so the holder knows which of its contracts to file it under

This is so every invoice raised under a Contract is laid out, delivered and taxed the same way, without office staff choosing these settings booking by booking.

## Notes

Read with Greg on 7 October, who agreed these are set on the Contract (moved to Confirmed on the board). All prices are held excluding GST, and GST is added at the bottom of the invoice.

Greg, 28 October: the delivery method would be held as a flag on the contract holder or billable party, saying "whether it generates an email invoice or whether it generates some other output"; where it sits is with OQ-114.
