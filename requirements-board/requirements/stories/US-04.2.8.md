How a Contract's invoices look and how they are delivered follow from the Contract and its Party; the office does not choose them per Contract:

- The [invoice layout](US-08.4.1) follows whether the Contract bills its holder (the holder's layout, with the BTM breakdown and a couple more fields to cross-match) or the payer (the patient's layout).
- How a holder's invoices are delivered is set on its [Party](US-04.1.5): email or post for now, other outputs open (OQ-114).
- Every price is held ex GST ([GST](US-05.2.7)).
- The Contract's [holder reference code](US-04.2.18) is printed on the holder's invoice.

This is so every invoice raised under a Contract is laid out, delivered and taxed the same way, without office staff choosing these settings booking by booking.
