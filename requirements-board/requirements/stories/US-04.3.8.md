At booking setup the office decides where each Procedure's invoice goes before it picks the Contract, following [the invoice decision tree](AR-62), from the [Contracts offered on the List](US-04.3.2):

1. **Where does the invoice go?** The patient, the List's hospital, or a special contract.
2. **Then narrow it down.** For the patient: is it prepaid or standard? For the hospital: which of its funding tags applies (SXAP, HNZ or ACC)? For a special contract: which one?
3. **Then the Contract.** It decides what happens next.

This is so the office answers first what it knows, who pays, and the Contracts it chooses from stay few.

What happens next follows from the chosen Contract's [pricing kind](US-04.2.2):

- **Prepaid:** the patient is sent the prepayment invoice.
- **A fixed price:** the agreed fee is filled in, and the anaesthetist is not asked for the RVG.
- **Priced from RVG units**, [No contract · RVG](US-04.3.3) included: the Booking goes to the anaesthetist to fill in the RVG code, times and modifiers.
- **No Contract fits:** the destination's [open-price fallback](US-04.3.9), where the amount is typed in.

The office's part ends at the Contract.

**The anaesthetist sees the same flow.** At every point in it, the anaesthetist sees what the office sees: the answers given so far, the choices still open and the Contracts on offer. Whoever comes to the Procedure next carries on from where it was left, so the anaesthetist can pick up a choice the office has not finished and finish it, or [change the Contract](US-03.4.1) once it is chosen. Prepaid is the one exception: only the office puts a Procedure on, or takes it off, a prepaid Contract.

This is so a Procedure is never stuck waiting on the office: the person who knows the answer can give it.

Until a Contract is chosen, the answers given so far are kept on the Procedure. Once it is chosen, the destination is worked out from the Contract's own settings and never stored: a Contract that bills the payer goes to the patient; one that bills the List's hospital and has a funding tag goes to the hospital; anything else is a special contract, grouped by holder.

- Under the patient, the payment type is always asked: Standard first, then Prepaid, which is shown but cannot be chosen when it holds no Contract.
- Elsewhere, where only one answer is possible, it is filled in; where no Contract is left to choose, the fallback is.
- The hospital is offered whenever it has tagged Contracts, even if the specialty filter leaves it none for this surgeon.
- A Contract priced from RVG units is never prepaid.
- A Procedure with no Contract shows "Needs an invoice destination" on its List and on the office's list of them; the Booking's Billing card shows the rail at its first open step instead.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Needs an invoice destination.** Given a Procedure with no Contract, then its List row and the office's list show "Needs an invoice destination", never "Needs a contract".
- **Derived destination.** Given a List at Christchurch Eye Surgery, then a surgeon's fixed fee billed to the payer (Merivale) sits under the patient, a CES SXAP item under Christchurch Eye Surgery › SXAP, and NIB's Contract under special contracts.
- **Fixed price filled in.** Given a List at Christchurch Eye Surgery, when the office chooses a CES SXAP Contract at a fixed price, then the agreed fee is the Procedure's price and the anaesthetist is not asked for an RVG code.
- **RVG to the anaesthetist.** Given a List at St George's, when the office chooses a St George's HNZ Contract priced from RVG units, then the Booking asks the anaesthetist for the RVG code, times and modifiers.
- **No RVG when prepaid.** Given the patient and prepaid, then no Contract priced from RVG units is offered.
- **Fills itself in.** Given a List at Christchurch Hospital, which has only HNZ Contracts, when the office chooses the hospital, then HNZ is filled in.
- **Picked up by the anaesthetist.** Given the office has chosen the patient and Standard but no Contract, when the anaesthetist opens the Procedure, then they see those answers and the same Contracts the office would see, and choosing one finishes it.
- **Mirrored at every step.** Given the office has answered only "Where does the invoice go?", or nothing at all, then the anaesthetist sees the flow at that same point and can carry on from it.
- **Payment type always asked.** Given a List with no prepaid Contract, when the office chooses the patient, then the rail stops at the payment type with Standard first and Prepaid shown but not choosable.
- **Prepaid stays with the office.** Given the patient is chosen, then the anaesthetist sees Prepaid marked "Set by the office" and cannot choose it.

## Notes

1. "Where does the invoice go?": Patient, the List's hospital, or Special, each showing how many Contracts it holds.

3. The Contract, typed to search, under a "No contract" heading that always holds the destination's [two backstops](US-04.3.9), No contract · RVG and No contract · Fixed price, above its own Contracts.
