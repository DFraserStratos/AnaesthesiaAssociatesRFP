The anaesthetist can mark a Booking complete once every Procedure in it has:

- a Contract
- on a Contract priced from RVG units, the RVG code, [its times](US-03.3.3) and a completed [modifiers step](US-03.3.4), with a short explanation for any modifier claimed
- a price, not "Needs a price": a Procedure's price follows its Contract until a person types one ([price follows the Contract](US-05.2.8))

This is so a Booking reaches the office with what it needs to be priced.

The holder's references are the office's to pick up. The insurance approval number is not needed to complete: the anaesthetist sees it where the Booking holds one, and may change it on the day ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)). Where the anaesthetist cannot complete a Procedure, they can [send it to the office to resolve](US-03.6.3).
