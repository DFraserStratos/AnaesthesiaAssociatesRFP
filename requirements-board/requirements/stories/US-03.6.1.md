Before an anaesthetist can mark a Booking complete, every Procedure in it must have:

- a Contract
- [its times recorded](US-03.3.3), where the Contract prices from time (not on a fixed price)
- a short explanation for any [modifier claimed](US-03.3.4)

Only once all of this is present can the anaesthetist mark the Booking complete.

The RVG code is optional: on an RVG Contract it is good practice, since it sets the base units. The holder's references, such as the insurance approval number, are not the anaesthetist's job: the office picks them up ([insurer and approval number](US-11.4.3)). Where the anaesthetist cannot complete a Procedure, they can [send it to the office to resolve](US-03.6.3).
