Before the Billing/Invoice Engine works out the remaining amount for a Booking, it deducts any [prepayment already invoiced](US-06.3.1) against that Booking.

For a prepaid Procedure the price is [the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2) by default, so once the prepayment is deducted nothing is left to bill:

- the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only;
- the system never invoices or credits a difference by itself ([no automatic invoice or credit](US-06.4.1));
- any further invoice or credit is raised by hand, by the anaesthetist or the office.

This is so a patient who has already paid a prepayment (the prepaid amount, the anaesthetist's own fixed price) is not billed for the full fee again.

## Notes

The prepaid amount is the [anaesthetist's own fixed-price Contract](US-04.2.14) (directors, 2026-10-06), not an estimate. Whether the price on a prepaid Procedure can be changed at all, which would leave a difference after the deduction, is OQ-96.
