When the Billing/Invoice Engine generates the invoices for an AUTHORISED List, it leaves every prepaid Procedure off them. A prepaid Procedure has already been invoiced in full on its [prepayment invoice](US-06.3.1), so nothing is deducted from the final invoice and the patient sees no prepayment line on it.

On a Booking that mixes prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures, the final invoice carries only the not-prepaid Procedures, priced as usual. A Booking whose Procedures are all prepaid has no final invoice.

Leaving a prepaid Procedure off does not change the price of the others. The [multi-procedure rule](US-05.3.1) ranks all the Booking's Procedures, prepaid or not, so where the primary Procedure is prepaid, a not-prepaid additional Procedure on the final invoice is still priced as an additional Procedure.

For a prepaid Procedure the price is [the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), the price on its issued prepayment invoice:

- the BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only;
- the system never invoices or credits a difference by itself ([no automatic invoice or credit](US-06.4.1));
- any further invoice or credit is raised by hand, by the anaesthetist or the office;
- the anaesthetist's payment for it comes from the transfer out of the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not from a new patient invoice.

This is so a patient who has already paid a prepayment is not billed for that Procedure again, and the final invoice shows only what is still owed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Left off.** Given a Booking with a prepaid Procedure, when its List is AUTHORISED and invoices are generated, then the prepaid Procedure is on no invoice and no prepayment is deducted from any invoice.
- **Mixed Booking.** Given a Booking with one prepaid and one not-prepaid Procedure, when invoices are generated, then the final invoice carries only the not-prepaid Procedure, priced as usual.
- **All prepaid.** Given a Booking whose Procedures are all prepaid, when invoices are generated, then no final invoice is raised for it.
- **Prepaid primary.** Given a Booking whose primary Procedure is prepaid and whose additional Procedure is not, when invoices are generated, then the final invoice carries the additional Procedure priced as an additional Procedure under the [multi-procedure rule](US-05.3.1), at the same price it would have had with the primary on the invoice.
