On the matching screen, the Lists in an arrival that match what AA already holds with no differences are shown as unchanged, and the office can confirm them all in one click. Confirming them changes nothing on the schedule.

This is so the office spends its time on the Lists that changed, not on re-checking the ones that did not.

A person still confirms them: unchanged Lists are never confirmed automatically. A List with any difference is [decided on its own](US-02.1.2).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Shown as unchanged.** Given a hospital file whose Lists match AA's with no differences for four Lists and one difference for a fifth, then the four are shown as unchanged and the fifth is not.
- **One click.** Given those four unchanged Lists, when the office confirms them, then all four are marked decided in one action, and no List or Booking changes.
- **Not automatic.** Given unchanged Lists have arrived, then they stay waiting in the feed until a person confirms them.
