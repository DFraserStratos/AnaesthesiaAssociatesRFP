The system applies the RVG age [modifier](US-05.1.5) from the patient's age on the procedure date (the date of the Booking's List), and shows it already selected. It is not locked: the anaesthetist can remove it.

This is because it comes from the patient, not from a clinical choice.

The bands follow the RVG, each lower bound included:

- under 1: A2
- 1, up to but not including 2: A1
- 2, up to but not including 70: none
- 70, up to but not including 80: A1
- 80 and over: A2

No age modifier is added on a base code the RVG already splits by age, such as H6b or P2.

## Acceptance criteria

- **In a band.** Given a patient in an RVG age band on the procedure date, when the anaesthetist opens the Procedure, then the matching age modifier is already selected, and the anaesthetist can remove it.
- **Outside the bands.** Given a patient outside the RVG age bands, then no age modifier is added.
- **Boundaries.** Given a patient who turns 2 on the procedure date, then no age modifier is added; given one who turns 80 that day, then A2 is selected; given one who turns 1 or 70 that day, then A1 is selected.
- **Age-split codes.** Given a Procedure whose base code is split by age (H6b, P2), then no age modifier is added, whatever the patient's age.

## Notes

OQ-95 answered 18 November (Donald, for the build, taking the recommendation; still to confirm with Vanessa): the age on the procedure date, inclusive lower bounds as printed, and no age modifier on base codes already split by age. At exactly 80, the RVG prints A1 as "70-80" and A2 as "> 80"; reading 80 as the inclusive lower bound of A2 is our reading of "inclusive lower bounds as printed", and the boundary is part of what Vanessa confirms.

The RVG itself leaves the modifying units to the anaesthetist ("at the discretion of the individual anaesthetist"), so pre-applying it is AA's rule, from the directors' answer of 6 October ("Some RVGs will include modifiers which can't be removed (Age)").

On 16 October Donald moved this back to Verify to talk to Ben. Greg: "I would challenge whether it should be locked", "everything's permissive"; Donald: a lock "could potentially cause trouble in some random configuration". Whether the age modifier is applied and locked at all then became part of OQ-95. On 20 October Donald decided: the age modifier is pre-applied but not locked, and no modifier is locked.

Ben, 27 October: "I think age modifier, that's an easy coding thing, right? That's an easy one." This keeps the age modifier pre-applied.
