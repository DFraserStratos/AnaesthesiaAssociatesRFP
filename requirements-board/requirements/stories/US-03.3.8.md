The system applies the RVG age [modifier](US-05.1.5) (A1 or A2) from the patient's date of birth on the procedure date, and, if it is applied and locked at all (OQ-95), shows it selected and locked: the anaesthetist cannot remove it.

This is because it comes from the patient, not from a clinical choice.

## Acceptance criteria

- **In a band.** Given a patient in an RVG age band on the procedure date, when the anaesthetist opens the Procedure, then the matching age modifier is selected and locked (subject to OQ-95).
- **Outside the bands.** Given a patient outside the RVG age bands, then no age modifier is added.

## Notes

The exact band boundaries, whether the age is taken on the procedure date, and whether an age modifier also applies on the base codes already split by age (H6b, P2), are to confirm (OQ-95).

The RVG itself leaves the modifying units to the anaesthetist ("at the discretion of the individual anaesthetist"), so this lock is AA's rule, from the directors' answer of 6 October ("Some RVGs will include modifiers which can't be removed (Age)").

On 16 October Donald moved this back to Verify to talk to Ben. Greg: "I would challenge whether it should be locked", "everything's permissive"; Donald: a lock "could potentially cause trouble in some random configuration". Whether the age modifier is applied and locked at all is now part of OQ-95.
