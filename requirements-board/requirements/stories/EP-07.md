A List moves through four states: DRAFT, ACTIVE, SUBMITTED and AUTHORISED.

A List is DRAFT while it has no anaesthetist: it is a [Draft List](FT-01.6), with its surgeon, hospital, day and session known, waiting for the office to assign it. It is [ACTIVE](FT-07.1) once it has all five of its pairing: an anaesthetist, a surgeon, a hospital, a day and a session. A List set up with its anaesthetist starts ACTIVE, and an ACTIVE List that goes back to the office (moved or withdrawn by its anaesthetist, or caught by a reschedule clash) becomes a Draft List again.

While a List is ACTIVE, the anaesthetist can edit its Bookings. Submitting the List removes the anaesthetist's edit access and sends it to the office for review. Authorising the List locks its Bookings, Procedures and [selected Contracts](US-04.3.5), and [hands the whole List to the Billing/Invoice Engine](US-08.1.1).
