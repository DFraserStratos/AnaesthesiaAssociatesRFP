When the office picks a [prepaid Contract](FT-06.2) for a Procedure on a Booking, the Billing/Invoice Engine generates the prepayment invoice automatically at booking setup. It is not sent until an admin approves it; it then goes out with a [prepayment letter](US-06.3.6).

Generating it creates a [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1) in the internal ledger, and the [matching pair of draft records in Xero](US-09.1.3). A later List submit does not invoice it again.

The invoice's amount is the fixed price on the prepaid Contract's line. Its payer is [the person named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) (the patient, or for example a guardian); it is raised only where the payer is a person paying for the patient, never an organisation (OQ-73).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract and whose payer is a person paying for the patient, when the Booking is set up, then a prepayment invoice is generated.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its amount is the fixed price on the prepaid Contract's line, and it is addressed to the person named on the Booking as payer.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, when no admin has approved it, then it is not sent.
- Given a prepayment invoice already generated, when the List is submitted, then the prepayment is not invoiced again.

## Technical discussion

Technical design v6 starts prepayment when the office picks a Contract with is_prepay = true ([prepayment](AR-29#prepayment)).
