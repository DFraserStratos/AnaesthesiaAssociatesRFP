When a Procedure on a Booking matches the [anaesthetist's prepaid list](US-06.1.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine generates the prepayment invoice automatically at booking setup. It is not sent until an admin approves it; it then goes out with a [prepayment letter](US-06.3.6).

Generating it creates a [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1) in the internal ledger, and the [matching pair of draft records in Xero](US-09.1.3). A later List submit does not invoice it again.

The invoice's amount is the fixed price for the procedure in the anaesthetist's own first-party Contract. Its payer is the person named on the Booking (the patient, or for example a guardian); it is raised only where the payer is a person paying for the patient, never an organisation (OQ-73).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking whose Procedure matches the anaesthetist's prepaid list and whose payer is a person paying for the patient, when the Booking is set up, then a prepayment invoice is generated.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its amount is the fixed price on the anaesthetist's own first-party Contract line for the procedure, and it is addressed to the person named on the Booking as payer.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, when no admin has approved it, then it is not sent.
- Given a prepayment invoice already generated, when the List is submitted, then the prepayment is not invoiced again.

## Technical discussion

The draft technical design puts the link from a Procedure to its prepayment on the Procedure (the booking procedure), rather than on the Booking as the catalogue has held it ([design §10](AR-29#p9)). It is a draft and may change before the catch-up build.
