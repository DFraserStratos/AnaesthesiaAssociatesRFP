When the office picks a [prepaid Contract](FT-06.2) for a Procedure on a Booking, an admin approves the prepayment invoice on the screen where outgoing invoices are approved, and the system generates it and sends it with a [prepayment letter](US-06.3.6). AA can switch this approval off, so prepayment invoices go out without anyone approving each one.

This is so a prepaid patient is invoiced as soon as the Booking is set up, with a person checking each invoice only where AA wants that.

The invoice:

- is for the Procedure's price when it is raised (the price range in force, or the amount typed on Prepaid · Fixed price), which becomes its [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2)
- is addressed to [the payer named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) (the patient, or for example a guardian), since a prepaid Contract always bills the payer (OQ-73)
- creates a [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1) in the internal ledger, a receivable from the payer and a payable to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not to the anaesthetist, and the [matching pair of draft records in Xero](US-09.1.3)

A later List submit does not invoice it again. Once the procedure is done, the money is transferred from the holding account to the anaesthetist who did it by a second pair: a journal transfer, not a new invoice or payment ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Approved and sent.** Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when an admin approves the prepayment invoice, then it is generated and sent.
- **Amount and payer.** Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its amount is the Procedure's price when raised, and it is addressed to the payer named on the Booking.
- **Waits for approval.** Given a Booking set up on a prepaid Contract and the approval step on, when no admin has approved the prepayment invoice, then it is not generated or sent.
- **Approval off.** Given AA has switched the approval step off, then a prepayment invoice is generated and sent without an admin approving it.
- **Pair.** Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its pair is a receivable from the payer named on the Booking and a payable to the AA Holding account.
- **Not invoiced again.** Given a prepayment invoice already generated, when the List is submitted, then the prepayment is not invoiced again.
