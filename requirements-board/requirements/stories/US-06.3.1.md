When the office picks a [prepaid Contract](FT-06.2) for a Procedure on a Booking, the office sets the Booking up with that Contract, and the prepayment invoice is generated when an admin approves it, on the screen where outgoing invoices are approved. That approval is an optional step: AA can switch it off, so prepayment invoices go out without anyone approving each one. Once generated it goes out with a [prepayment letter](US-06.3.6).

Generating it creates a [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1) in the internal ledger: a receivable from the payer named on the Booking (the patient, or for example a guardian) and a payable to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not to the anaesthetist. It also creates the [matching pair of draft records in Xero](US-09.1.3). A later List submit does not invoice it again; once the procedure is done the money is transferred from the holding account to the anaesthetist who did it, by a second pair: a journal transfer, not a new invoice or payment ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

The invoice's amount is the fixed price on the prepaid Contract's line. Its payer is whoever the Contract bills: normally [the person named on the Booking](US-11.2.2) (the patient, or for example a guardian). In practice the payer is a person, but the system does not forbid a Contract that bills its holder from being prepaid, in which case the holder's billable party is invoiced (OQ-73).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when an admin approves the prepayment invoice, then it is generated and sent.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its amount is the fixed price on the prepaid Contract's line, and it is addressed to whoever the Contract bills, normally the person named on the Booking as payer.
- Given a Booking set up on a prepaid Contract and the approval step on, when no admin has approved the prepayment invoice, then it is not generated or sent.
- Given AA has switched the approval step off, then a prepayment invoice is generated and sent without an admin approving it.
- Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its pair is a receivable from the payer named on the Booking and a payable to the AA Holding account.
- Given a prepayment invoice already generated, when the List is submitted, then the prepayment is not invoiced again.
