When the office picks a [prepaid Contract](FT-06.2) for a Procedure on a Booking, an admin approves the prepayment invoice on the screen where outgoing invoices are approved, and the system generates it and sends it with a [prepayment letter](US-06.3.6). A Booking raises one prepayment invoice per payer: prepaid Procedures that bill the same payer share one, and prepaid Procedures that name different payers each give their payer their own. AA can switch this approval off, so prepayment invoices go out without anyone approving each one.

This is so a prepaid patient is invoiced as soon as the Booking is set up, with a person checking each invoice only where AA wants that.

The invoice:

- is for each of its Procedures' price when it is raised (the price range in force, or the amount typed on Prepaid · Fixed price), which becomes that Procedure's [prepaid amount](US-06.2.2)
- is addressed to [the Procedures' payer](US-11.2.2) (the patient, or for example a guardian), since a prepaid Contract always bills the payer (OQ-73)
- creates a [linked receivable and payable](US-08.3.1) in the internal ledger, a receivable from that payer and a payable to the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), not to the anaesthetist, and the [matching pair of draft records in Xero](US-09.1.3)

Once a payer's prepayment invoice is raised, that payer is locked on the Procedures it covers; a payer whose own prepayment invoice is not yet raised can still be changed. A later List submit does not invoice it again. Once the procedure is done, the money is transferred from the holding account to the anaesthetist who did it by a second pair: a journal transfer, not a new invoice or payment ([nothing further after a prepaid procedure](US-06.4.1)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Approved and sent.** Given a Booking with a Procedure on a prepaid Contract, when an admin approves the prepayment invoice, then it is generated and sent.
- **Amount and payer.** Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its amount is its Procedures' price when raised, and it is addressed to their payer.
- **One per payer.** Given a Booking with two prepaid Procedures that name different payers, when the prepayment invoices are raised, then each payer receives their own prepayment invoice.
- **Payer locked.** Given a payer's prepayment invoice has been raised, then that payer can no longer be changed on the Procedures it covers, while a payer whose own prepayment invoice is not yet raised still can.
- **Waits for approval.** Given a Booking set up on a prepaid Contract and the approval step on, when no admin has approved the prepayment invoice, then it is not generated or sent.
- **Approval off.** Given AA has [switched the approval step off](US-13.7.4), then a prepayment invoice is generated and sent without an admin approving it.
- **Pair.** Given a generated prepayment invoice, then its pair is a receivable from its payer and a payable to the AA Holding account.
- **Not invoiced again.** Given a prepayment invoice already generated, when the List is submitted, then the prepayment is not invoiced again.
