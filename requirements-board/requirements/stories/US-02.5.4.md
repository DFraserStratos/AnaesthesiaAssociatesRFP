The Scheduling Engine accepts inbound Booking changes, including automated updates from an integration, right up to the start of the operating session and beyond, until the office authorises the List.

This is so a late booking is still supported, however close to the session it arrives.

Inbound changes follow the same rule as the anaesthetist's and the office's own edits: they can be accepted until the office [authorises the List](US-07.4.1), even after the anaesthetist has [submitted it](US-07.1.1) ([the anaesthetist can still edit a submitted List](US-07.2.1)). There is one editing window for every source.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Open until authorised.** Given a List the office has not yet authorised, submitted or not, when an inbound change to one of its Bookings arrives, then it is accepted: applied, or for a change from the hospital's list or the rooms' PDFs sent to the office's review and merge first (see [automatic sync](US-02.1.5) and [surgeon PDF lists](FT-02.2)).
- **Same rule after submit.** Given a List the anaesthetist has submitted but the office has not authorised, when an inbound change to one of its Bookings arrives, then the office can still review and accept it, for example a corrected name spelling or email address.
- **Locked once authorised.** Given a List the office has authorised, when an inbound change to one of its Bookings arrives, then it is not applied. How such a change is then handled is not yet decided.
- **Anaesthetist edits.** Given a submitted List the office has not yet authorised, then the anaesthetist can still change its Bookings ([US-07.2.1](US-07.2.1)).

## Technical discussion

Delivery constraint from our RFP response: BAU support is Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm NZT, with no weekend or on-call support by default. Given the requirement to support late bookings up to the start of an operating session, coverage expectations are to be discussed during scoping.
