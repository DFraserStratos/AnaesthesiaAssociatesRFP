The Scheduling Engine accepts inbound Booking changes, including automated updates from an integration, right up to the start of the operating session and beyond, until the anaesthetist submits the List.

This is so a late booking is still supported, however close to the session it arrives.

Inbound changes are accepted until the anaesthetist [submits the List](US-07.1.1). The anaesthetist themselves can still change the List's Bookings after submitting, until the office authorises it ([the anaesthetist can still edit a submitted List](US-07.2.1)).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Open until submitted.** Given a List the anaesthetist has not yet submitted, when an inbound change to one of its Bookings arrives, then it is accepted: applied, or for a change from the hospital's list or the rooms' PDFs sent to the office's review and merge first (see [automatic sync](US-02.1.5) and [surgeon PDF lists](FT-02.2)).
- **Locked once submitted.** Given a List the anaesthetist has submitted, when an automated change to one of its Bookings arrives from an integration, then it is not applied automatically. How such a change is then handled is not yet decided.
- **Anaesthetist edits.** Given a submitted List the office has not yet authorised, then the anaesthetist can still change its Bookings ([US-07.2.1](US-07.2.1)).

## Technical discussion

Delivery constraint from our RFP response: BAU support is Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm NZT, with no weekend or on-call support by default. Given the requirement to support late bookings up to the start of an operating session, coverage expectations are to be discussed during scoping.
