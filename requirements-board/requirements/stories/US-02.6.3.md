The office resolves [a stub](US-02.6.1) from intake, in the [feed](US-02.1.4), for example by adding the missing NHI, the one way to add an NHI by hand. A later arrival for the List, usually the hospital's, can also carry the missing NHI: the update shows it, and applying it resolves the stub. Either way, when the incoming booking becomes a Booking, the Booking takes the stub's place on the List.

This is so the List is complete again once the problem is fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Resolved from intake.** Given the office resolves the incoming booking at intake (for example by adding the NHI), when it becomes a Booking, then the Booking replaces the stub on the List.
- **NHI from a later arrival.** Given a stub waiting for an NHI, when the hospital's List for the same List arrives carrying that patient's NHI, then the update on the matching screen shows the NHI, and applying it turns the stub into the Booking.
