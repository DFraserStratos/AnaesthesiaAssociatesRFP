The office resolves a stub from intake, in the [unmatched queue](US-02.1.4), for example by adding the missing NHI. When the incoming booking becomes a Booking, the Booking takes the stub's place on the List.

This is so the List is complete again once the problem is fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Resolved from intake.** Given the office resolves the incoming booking at intake (for example by adding the NHI), when it becomes a Booking, then the Booking replaces the stub on the List.
