Once a List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), the Billing/Invoice Engine prices a prepaid Procedure at [the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), the price on its issued prepayment invoice, and does nothing further for it apart from transferring that amount from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1) to the anaesthetist who did it. The BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only: nothing is calculated against them.

This is so a prepaid patient has a stable amount, and is never billed or credited without a person deciding to.

No invoice or credit note is raised automatically, whether the work turned out more or less than expected, and there is no threshold. Where one is needed, for example because only half the work was done or the procedure took far longer, the anaesthetist or the office raises an [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand, as for any procedure. A credit note credits the whole prepayment invoice, never part of it; anything still owed, such as the half of the work that was done, is then raised by hand as a new additional invoice.

The price cannot change: a prepaid Contract cannot carry the setting that allows a price override, and once the prepayment invoice is raised the price is read-only for the office too.

The anaesthetist's payment is the transfer, not a new invoice to the patient. The prepaid Procedure is [left off the final invoice](US-08.2.2), including on a Booking that mixes prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Prepaid amount.** Given a prepaid Procedure, when the List is AUTHORISED, then its price is the prepaid amount and the recorded BTM units do not change it.
- **No extra invoice.** Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to more than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no invoice is created for the difference.
- **No automatic credit.** Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to less than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no credit note or refund is created for the difference.
- **No threshold.** Given a prepaid Procedure, then no threshold applies: nothing is raised however large the difference.
- **By hand.** Given a prepaid Procedure, when the anaesthetist or the office chooses, then they can raise an additional invoice or a credit note by hand, and it is created and traceable to the Procedure.
- **No override setting.** Given a prepaid Contract, when AA tries to set it to allow a price override, then it is refused.
- **Credit in full.** Given a prepaid Procedure where only part of the work was done, when the anaesthetist or the office credits it by hand, then the whole prepayment invoice is credited and a credit for part of it cannot be raised; any amount still owed is raised by hand as a new additional invoice.

## Technical discussion

How a full credit of a prepayment invoice unwinds the AA Holding account transfer, and how the new additional invoice for the work done is paired with its payable to the anaesthetist, is to be worked out in the build.

## Notes

Our reading (contract model proposal A16): once the prepayment invoice is raised the price is read-only for the office too, and any difference is a credit or additional invoice raised by hand.

OQ-61 answered by Ben on 2026-10-06: "By default, if the outcome of the procedure is that the pre-paid amount is too low or too high, then the system will not automatically invoice. The system will allow users to manually create them if needed." On 7 October he added that "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract", and that when a prepaid procedure is only half done or takes far longer, "the anaesthetist can optionally choose to generate any ad hoc invoices or credit notes as they need to." OQ-76 answered. This replaces the earlier rules that any positive remaining balance is invoiced automatically and that an overpayment is never refunded (OQ-03).
