Once a List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), a prepaid Procedure is priced at [the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), the price on the prepaid Contract's line (normally the anaesthetist's own price list). The BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only: nothing is calculated against them.

No invoice or credit note is raised automatically, whether the work turned out to be more or less than expected, and there is no threshold. Where one is needed, for example because only half the work was done or the procedure took far longer, the anaesthetist or the office raises an ad hoc [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand, using the abilities the system already has for any procedure.

The anaesthetist's payment for a prepaid Procedure is not a new invoice to the patient: it is a transfer from the [prepay holding account](US-06.5.1) ([settlement](FT-06.4)).

This is so a prepaid patient has a stable amount, and is never billed or credited without a person deciding to (OQ-61).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Procedure, when the List is AUTHORISED, then its price is the prepaid amount and the recorded BTM units do not change it.
- Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to more than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no invoice is created for the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to less than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no credit note or refund is created for the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure, then no threshold applies: nothing is raised however large the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure, when the anaesthetist or the office chooses, then they can raise an additional invoice or a credit note by hand, and it is created and traceable to the Procedure.

## Notes

OQ-61 answered by Ben on 2026-10-06: "By default, if the outcome of the procedure is that the pre-paid amount is too low or too high, then the system will not automatically invoice. Anaesthetists can / must create any ad-hoc invoices or credit notes as needed." Donald clarified: "There is no automatic credit or additional invoice. The system will allow users to manually create them if needed." On 7 October he added that "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract", and that when a prepaid procedure is only half done or takes far longer, "the anaesthetist can optionally choose to generate any ad hoc invoices or credit notes as they need to." OQ-76 answered. This replaces the earlier rules that any positive remaining balance is invoiced automatically and that an overpayment is never refunded (OQ-03).

Merges the former US-06.4.2 (No automatic credit when prepaid exceeds final), now Retired; this story now covers both directions.

Whether the anaesthetist may type a different price on a prepaid procedure is OQ-96, which on 19 October widened: a price override, a move and the holding account could all happen on the same procedure, and Greg may draw that out. This is mentioned here, not settled. Whether a credit note can be for part of the prepayment is OQ-97.

The v6 design differs: it treats the prepaid amount as an editable price, bills a raised price's difference as an additional invoice, and invokes a refund for a lowered one; its changes table now says "A refund may be issued". The directors' answer above stands for the build until Ben answers OQ-96 and OQ-97. On 14 October Donald and Greg agreed half-done or extra work is handled "through the additional invoices", not the price.
