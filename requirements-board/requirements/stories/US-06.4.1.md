Once a List is [AUTHORISED](US-07.3.1), a prepaid Procedure is priced at [the prepaid amount](US-06.2.2), the price on its issued prepayment invoice, taken from the prepaid Contract's line (normally the anaesthetist's own price list). The BTM units recorded on the day are for reference only: nothing is calculated against them.

Once the procedure is done, nothing further occurs for a prepaid Procedure apart from the transfer of the prepaid amount from the AA Holding account to the anaesthetist who did it.

A prepaid Contract is not editable: it cannot also carry the setting that allows a price override, so the anaesthetist cannot change the prepaid price (OQ-96). The office can still change any price ([office can change any price](US-05.4.2)).

No invoice or credit note is raised automatically, whether the work turned out to be more or less than expected, and there is no threshold. Where one is needed, for example because only half the work was done or the procedure took far longer, the anaesthetist or the office raises an ad hoc [additional invoice](US-08.6.3) or [credit note](US-08.6.5) by hand, using the abilities the system already has for any procedure. This is AA's choice: Ben, 28 October, on a facelift that becomes a facelift plus liposuction: "you'd raise a second invoice"; "a rare event can have some manual input."

The anaesthetist's payment for a prepaid Procedure is not a new invoice to the patient: it is a transfer from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), made once the procedure is done and the office authorises the List. The prepaid Procedure is [left off the final invoice](US-08.2.2), including on a Booking that mixes prepaid and not-prepaid Procedures (OQ-116).

This is so a prepaid patient has a stable amount, and is never billed or credited without a person deciding to (OQ-61).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a prepaid Procedure, when the List is AUTHORISED, then its price is the prepaid amount and the recorded BTM units do not change it.
- Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to more than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no invoice is created for the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure whose recorded BTM units would come to less than the prepaid amount, when the List is AUTHORISED, then no credit note or refund is created for the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure, then no threshold applies: nothing is raised however large the difference.
- Given a prepaid Procedure, when the anaesthetist or the office chooses, then they can raise an additional invoice or a credit note by hand, and it is created and traceable to the Procedure.
- Given a prepaid Contract, when AA tries to set it to allow a price override, then it is refused.

## Notes

OQ-61 answered by Ben on 2026-10-06: "By default, if the outcome of the procedure is that the pre-paid amount is too low or too high, then the system will not automatically invoice. Anaesthetists can / must create any ad-hoc invoices or credit notes as needed." Donald clarified: "There is no automatic credit or additional invoice. The system will allow users to manually create them if needed." On 7 October he added that "the system should not try and calculate anything additional on a prepaid or on a fixed cost contract", and that when a prepaid procedure is only half done or takes far longer, "the anaesthetist can optionally choose to generate any ad hoc invoices or credit notes as they need to." OQ-76 answered. This replaces the earlier rules that any positive remaining balance is invoiced automatically and that an overpayment is never refunded (OQ-03).

Merges the former US-06.4.2 (No automatic credit when prepaid exceeds final), now Retired; this story now covers both directions.

OQ-96 answered 28 October: prepaid Contracts are not editable and any difference is a second invoice or credit raised by hand. Greg had argued for an automatic difference invoice or credit; Ben chose by hand. Greg: "prepaid contracts must be set up as not editable. That locks all this down." Donald: "prepaid contracts can't also have the setting of being allowed to have a price override." Whether a credit can be for part of a prepayment is still OQ-97.

The v6 design differs: it treats the prepaid amount as an editable price, bills a raised price's difference as an additional invoice, and invokes a refund for a lowered one; its changes table now says "A refund may be issued". OQ-96's answer rejects that design for prepaid Contracts. On 14 October Donald and Greg agreed half-done or extra work is handled "through the additional invoices", not the price.

Moved 28 October from the former FT-06.4 (Settlement after the procedure), now Retired. The transfer's trigger: Greg named it as the booking being "confirmed and submitted"; the catalogue hands a List to billing when the office authorises it, so that is used here, still to confirm with AA (OQ-125). On 28 October Greg and Donald agreed the release follows the finished procedure, without restating the event that triggers it.

Not adopted: the pricing guide's proposal that the anaesthetist raises the price and "the difference is invoiced to the patient as an extra invoice", nor the idea floated on 7 October that the system works out the difference after the procedure with a later invoice to increase or decrease the final amount.
