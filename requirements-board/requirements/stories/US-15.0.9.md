AA's office deals with Stratos about the product itself: incidents, fixes, changes and new work. Each ticket is logged and tracked, and a request for something the system does not yet do goes to the product owner or the development team, to plan as a new feature.

This is so AA has an agreed route, and agreed response times, for keeping the system it relies on for bookings and billing working and growing.

- Hours: 8:30am to 5:00pm NZT, Monday to Friday, excluding New Zealand public holidays.

Response times, for tickets raised in business hours:

| Priority | Response |
|---|---|
| P1: system down with widespread business impact | 1 hour |
| P2: high-priority issue significantly affecting business operations, with no viable workaround | Same business day |
| P3: standard issue or request with limited business impact, or a reasonable workaround | 3 business days |
| P4: low-priority issue, cosmetic defect, minor enhancement or planned task with no operational urgency | Scheduled at a mutually agreed time |

Outside business hours:

| When | Cover |
|---|---|
| After hours | Best effort, not guaranteed |
| Public holidays | Call-out only, with AA's approval |
| Christmas and New Year (about two weeks) | Limited, best-effort cover |

## Acceptance criteria

- **Response time.** Given a P1 ticket raised at 10:00am on a weekday, then Stratos responds by 11:00am.
- **After hours.** Given a ticket raised at 7:00pm on a weekday, then it is handled on a best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response time.
- **Public holiday.** Given a ticket on a New Zealand public holiday, then a call-out happens only with AA's approval.
