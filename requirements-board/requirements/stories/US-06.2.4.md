The Billing/Invoice Engine calculates the prepayment estimate automatically for a Booking that needs prepayment, so the office no longer works it out by hand:

- base units for the Procedure, from the Procedure's Contract: one of its [default RVG Contracts](US-04.4.2) unless another applies (OQ-62)
- time units from the [estimated duration](US-06.2.5), using the standard [tiered time rule](US-05.2.2) (part intervals always rounded up)
- two modifier units added as a contingency, because something can go wrong (OQ-38)
- the total multiplied by the anaesthetist's own unit value ([per-unit pricing rate](US-05.2.1)), for base and time alike; it is never an AA rate

This replaces the office's local duration table, and the practice of choosing six or seven time units for 90 minutes depending on whether the anaesthetist's rate is high or low.

This is so a prepayment request takes moments instead of a hand calculation each time, and two anaesthetists differ only by their own rate.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a Procedure with 10 base units and an estimated duration of 90 minutes, when the estimate is calculated, then it is (10 base + 6 time units + 2 contingency modifier units) x the anaesthetist's unit value.
- Given two anaesthetists with different unit values, when the same Procedure and duration are estimated, then the units are identical and only the dollar amount differs.
- The number of contingency modifier units should be a setting, two to start, not hard-coded.
