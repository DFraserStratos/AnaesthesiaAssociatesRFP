The system keeps the full base, time and modifier record on a Contract priced from RVG units, including RVG at the [Contract's rate](US-05.2.6), even when the price is overridden down to zero. On a fixed or open price no times or modifiers are asked for ([RVG detail follows the pricing kind](US-03.3.9)).

This is so the work performed is still recorded, even when nothing is charged for it.
