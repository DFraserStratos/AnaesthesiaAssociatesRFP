The system keeps the full base, time and modifier record even when the anaesthetist applies a 100% discount, bringing the fee to zero. BTM is not mandatory when the Contract [sets a fixed price](US-05.2.5); it still is on a [fixed-rate Contract](US-05.2.6), which prices from the units.

This is so the work performed is still recorded, even when nothing is charged for it.
