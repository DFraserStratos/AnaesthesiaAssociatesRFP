A prepaid Procedure's Contract may be changed to one not flagged as prepaid after its prepayment invoice has been sent, or after it has been paid. The system must decide what happens to the invoice and to money already collected, even if this is rare in practice.

What was said on 28 October, not agreed:

- if the prepayment is paid, Donald proposed refunding it in full from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1), then invoicing after the procedure under the new Contract; Greg preferred a refund to a credit, which "just creates more work", and said to raise it with Ben;
- if it is sent but not paid, nothing was settled: Donald asked whether the invoice is voided or a credit note raised, and what happens if the patient pays it anyway.

How AA expects both to be handled is open (OQ-119).

## Notes

Donald, 28 October: "Even if it doesn't exist in real life, the system has to decide what it does in that condition." Greg: "I'd rather see that deleted and rebooked, really"; "If you've sent them an invoice, you can't actually stop them sending you money"; "we haven't talked about credits."

Donald also asked how the draft payable changes; on a prepayment the payable is the AA Holding account, so what changes is part of the question.
