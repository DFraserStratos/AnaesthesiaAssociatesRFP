When a prepaid Procedure's Contract is changed to one not flagged as prepaid after its prepayment invoice has been sent or paid, the Billing/Invoice Engine handles the change as a cancellation of the prepayment ([cancellation of prepayment procedures](US-06.5.2)):

- paid: the prepayment is refunded in full from the [AA Holding account](US-06.5.1)
- sent but not paid: a credit note cancels the prepayment invoice, as for a cancelled Booking, and a payment that still arrives after it is refunded

The Procedure is then invoiced under its new Contract after the procedure.

This is so the change is handled the same way as a cancellation: Greg preferred a refund to a credit, which "just creates more work".

## Acceptance criteria

- **Paid.** Given a paid prepayment, when the Procedure's Contract is changed to one not flagged as prepaid, then the prepayment is refunded in full and the Procedure is invoiced under the new Contract after the procedure.
- **Sent, not paid.** Given a sent, unpaid prepayment invoice, when the Procedure's Contract is changed to one not flagged as prepaid, then a credit note cancels the prepayment invoice and the Procedure is invoiced under the new Contract after the procedure.
