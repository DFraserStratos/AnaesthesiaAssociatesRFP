The office prices a fixed-price Contract through one or more price ranges, each a start date and a price ex GST. The range in force on the procedure date (the date of the Booking's List) gives the price.

This is so the office can enter a holder's new prices when they arrive, even late, and backdate them, while each Procedure keeps the price of its own date.

- A range ends the day before the next one starts, so ranges never overlap or leave a gap; the last runs on with no end.
- A start date may be in the past.
- Changing a price from a date is adding a range with that start. Correcting a mistake is editing or deleting a range, never the last one. Every change is [audited](US-04.1.3).
- A Procedure's price [follows the range in force](US-05.2.8) until something holds it.
- A fixed-price Contract with no range in force on a List's date [is not offered on that List](US-04.3.2).
- Only a fixed price has ranges: a rate or discount is undated.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Range in force.** Given ranges from 1 April and 1 July, when a Procedure dated 15 June is priced, then the 1 April price is used.
- **Implied end.** Given a range from 1 April, when a range from 1 July is added, then the April range ends on 30 June.
- **Backdated.** Given Merivale's Face lift at $3,100 from 1 June, when on 5 November the office adds $3,200 from 1 October, then an un-authorised, unedited Procedure dated 15 October is priced $3,200, one dated 20 September stays $3,100, and an authorised one is untouched.
- **Unique starts.** Two ranges on one Contract cannot share a start date.
- **Never the last.** The only range on a Contract cannot be deleted.
- **Not yet in force.** Given a List dated before a Contract's first range, then that Contract is not offered.

## Technical discussion

Ranges sit inside the Contract's pricing ([price range](AR-58#L124-133) and [price range rules](AR-58#L504-513), AR-58 sections 2.4 and 5.6).
