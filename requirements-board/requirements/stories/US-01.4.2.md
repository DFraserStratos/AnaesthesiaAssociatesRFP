Admins and anaesthetists can see AM and PM availability across every anaesthetist for a given day.

The anaesthetists who are free are listed in order of [tier](US-01.3.6) and [preferred pairing](US-13.6.4) with the surgeon (both admin-only: anaesthetists never see tiers or pairings). This sequences the list; it does not assign anyone automatically.

When the finder is used to move a Booking or a List, a search box also filters the names as letters are typed ([moving a single Booking](US-01.4.7)).

This is so a locum can be found quickly to cover illness.
