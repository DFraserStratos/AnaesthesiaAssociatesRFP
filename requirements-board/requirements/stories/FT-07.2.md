Once a List is submitted, the office reviews and corrects it. The anaesthetist can still see it, and can still change it until it is authorised, for example on realising the next day that something was wrong. Submitting makes the List less prominent for the anaesthetist and tells the office it has work to do.

## Notes

Greg's original aim was that a booking should not disappear from a sharp-eyed anaesthetist's view on submit, before it shows on their receivables; "if they get a query, they're gonna ring Vanessa". The state names are to check with Vanessa and the directors.
