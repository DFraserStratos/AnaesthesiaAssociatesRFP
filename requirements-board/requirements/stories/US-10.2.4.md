Bulk hospital payments that cover multiple invoices at once are matched by Xero's own bank reconciliation and its remittance add-on, not by the Billing/Invoice Engine.

Any item the remittance add-on cannot match automatically is left as an unmatched item in Xero's bank reconciliation, for office staff to handle there.

## Technical discussion

The remittance add-on reads hospitals' and insurers' remittance advices from an inbox of their own, matches them to the bank lines by reference and amount, and leaves the office the unmatched lines; for a receipt with no reference it lists every unreconciled transaction of that amount. Where a receipt nobody can place is parked, left open on the bank reconciliation or moved to a holding account in Xero, is not settled.
