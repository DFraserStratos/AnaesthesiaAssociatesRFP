Within one Booking, the [primary Procedure](US-03.2.1) and any additional Procedures earn units differently:

- Base units: only the primary Procedure carries them.
- Time units: every Procedure carries its own, calculated from its own [recorded times](US-03.3.3).
- Modifier units: all sit on the primary Procedure when the total is 4 units or fewer. Above 4, they are split equally across every Procedure in the Booking, with any remainder going to the primary.

## Notes

The modifier split was agreed with Ben, Vanessa and Greg on 7 October (OQ-15 answered): "basically what Vanessa does in the office normally".

AA confirmed on 19 October that this default stands (OQ-90): it is the one system-wide rule. Greg: "procedure 2 has its own time recording... there should be no base units on the secondary procedure." Pricing each Procedure alone for base and time units is "not correct".

The v6 design applies the rule to adjust the units of RVG-priced procedures.

Which Procedure is primary was not discussed on 19 October, so it is not settled by that answer.
