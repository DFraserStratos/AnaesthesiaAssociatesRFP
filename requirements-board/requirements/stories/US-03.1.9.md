On the booking screens, each Procedure shows its:

1. **Procedure text**: the hospital's once it arrives, otherwise the rooms' ([procedure text](US-02.5.7))
2. **Contract**: its name and [holder](US-04.1.5), who is invoiced, and its pricing basis with the figure, for example "Fixed $2,400", "RVG less 10%", "RVG" or "Other price $2,000"
3. **RVG code**, where the Contract prices from RVG units, or where one was picked for reference on a fixed or open price

It also shows the **invoice description** that will print ([invoice description](US-08.4.6)).

This is so whoever chooses or checks the Contract and RVG code sees the procedure text beside them. The text is not plain English but short codes and snippets, such as "WLE MM" or "RTK", that someone who knows the work can read.

The office picks the Contract first, on the [step rail](US-04.3.8), where typing an RVG code finds Contracts with that [default RVG code](US-04.2.18). A Contract priced from RVG units goes to the anaesthetist for the RVG code ([Contract selection](FT-04.3)).

The apps need not show this the same way: a larger screen can break a Booking into more fields than the anaesthetist sees on a phone.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Parts shown.** Given a Booking from any source, then each Procedure shows its text as received, its Contract, and its RVG code where the Contract prices from RVG units.
- **Both apps.** Both apps show the received text, Contract and RVG code, though not necessarily laid out the same way.
- **Contract summary.** Given a Procedure on a fixed-price Contract held by Southern Cross, then the Contract part shows the Contract's name, Southern Cross as holder, who is invoiced, and the fixed price.
- **Not matched yet.** Given a Procedure whose RVG code is not chosen yet, then the received text and the Contract still show, with the empty parts marked as still to choose. A Booking with no Contract shows "Needs an invoice destination" and its List waits on the office's [Contracts tab](US-04.3.12) until one is set.
- **Hospital's wording replaces.** Given a later hospital update with fuller wording for the same procedure, then the Procedure shows the hospital's wording, and the rooms' text stays in the change history.
